

I use home objects to teach Celine materials, shapes, and dimensions together. In the kitchen, I ask: "Is this cup plastic, glass, or stainless steel?" "Is this plate round or rectangular?"

Then we add dimension words: long, wide, tall, thin, and thick. I let her describe one object with at least two features: "This box is rectangular and wide." "This spoon is metal and shiny."

During toy time, we sort flat and solid shapes. Circle and square for 2D, cube and cylinder for 3D. She touches the object while saying the word, so memory is stronger.

Safety language is part of this lesson too. Glass can break, metal edges can be sharp, and heavy items need two hands.

For me, this topic is excellent for practical English. It combines science observation, daily safety, and sentence building in one routine.
