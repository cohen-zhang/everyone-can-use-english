Celine, today is your tenth birthday.

First, I want to read a praise letter your teacher wrote about you.

Since school started, you’ve stayed focused in class. You listen carefully and think things through. You also speak up and share your ideas. As my little helper, you jump in with a warm heart. You’re responsible, and you keep things organized. That deserves real praise. I’m so proud of you! Keep it up—and keep working hard.

Your teacher wrote that she’s proud of you. I am too.

Getting this praise letter from your teacher makes me happier than seeing a better grade.

You’re in fourth grade now. You’re growing up so fast.

Can I call you my little kid sister? Okay, I know you don’t like being called that.

You’re so polite to people. Whenever we come into our community, you say hi to the security guards. You’re friendlier than I am!

You’re curious, and you love asking all kinds of funny, unexpected questions. I love your curiosity. Keep wondering, “Why?”

I really admire how brave you are. Once I took you to a dance competition. You got up on stage and danced alongside a whole group of people. You showed what you could do in front of the dance teachers and the cameras. That was so cool!

You like looking pretty, and you love sleeping in.

You make funny faces whenever we take pictures.

You finish your homework really quickly.

I think your classmates see you as a good friend. Your watch is always ringing because your friends are always looking for you and asking you to play. You cheered your friend up. You’re a good friend.

To Mom and me, you’re a good friend too. You’re our travel buddy, our after-dinner walking buddy, our photo buddy, and our food buddy.

You’re not just a great helper at school. You help out at home, too. When you get home from school, you start the washing machine, and when the laundry’s done, you help hang up the clothes. You help us wash vegetables, and you’ve gone downstairs to buy soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and even pork for us.

Thank you for helping us with all those things. You make our family feel like we have one more dependable teammate.

I love playing games with you and shooting hoops at your school. We go hiking together, walk by the river in the morning, and ride our bikes up the nearby hills.

When I take you to school or to your classes on my electric scooter, you wrap your arms tightly around my waist.

When I go away on a business trip, you always walk me to the gate of our community and watch me get into the car.

I hope you keep growing a little every day—in height, of course, but even more in your mind and in all the things you learn.

You’re so good at looking after your friends.

And I hope you’ll take good care of yourself, too. Eat well and give your body the good things it needs—milk, eggs, and meat. And remember to drink plenty of water. Do you remember what we said? When you take a drink, imagine the little people inside your body cheering?

I’m proud of you!

That’s all for now. Have a wonderful time today.

Happy birthday. I love you.
