
Organizing a Meeting via Feishu

I. Pre - meeting Arrangements

1. Scheduling the Meeting via Calendar
    1. As an organizer, I first logged into my Feishu account. Then, I accessed the calendar feature. I selected the appropriate date and time slot for the meeting. I filled in the details such as the meeting title, which clearly indicated the main purpose of the meeting, for example, "Project Progress Review Meeting". I also set the duration of the meeting, making sure to allocate enough time for all the topics to be discussed.
2. Creating the Meeting Group
    1. After scheduling the meeting in the calendar, I created a meeting group. I clicked on the option to create a new group specifically for this meeting. I named the group after the meeting title, which made it easy for participants to identify. In the group settings, I added relevant information about the meeting, such as the agenda and any pre - reading materials that the participants might need.
3. Inviting Participants
    1. I then started to invite participants to the meeting. I used the "add members" function in the meeting group. I searched for the names or email addresses of the colleagues who needed to attend the meeting. I sent out the invitations, which included the meeting time, link to the meeting (which was automatically generated by Feishu), and a brief note about the importance of their attendance.

II. During - the - meeting Processes

1. Initiating the Video Meeting
    1. At the scheduled time, I, as the organizer, clicked on the "start video meeting" button in the meeting group. Feishu quickly launched the video conferencing interface. I waited for the participants to join. As they joined, I could see their video feeds and names on the screen.
2. Discussing the Meeting Topics
    1. Once all the participants had joined, I began to lead the discussion according to the pre - set agenda. I presented the first topic, for example, "Project A's Current Progress". I shared my screen to show relevant data and reports. The participants actively participated in the discussion. They asked questions, provided their own insights, and proposed solutions to the problems we were facing in Project A. We then moved on to the next topics one by one, ensuring that each topic was thoroughly discussed.
3. Recording the Meeting Process
    1. During the meeting, Feishu has a built - in recording function. I clicked on the "record" button to start recording the entire meeting process. This included all the conversations, presentations, and discussions. The recording would be useful for those who could not attend the meeting and also for future reference.

III. Post - meeting Actions

1. Forming the Online Meeting Master Document
    1. After the meeting, I created an online meeting master document in Feishu Docs. I titled it with the meeting name and date. I first added a summary section, which briefly outlined the key points and decisions made during the meeting. Then, I went through the recording and my own notes to add detailed content for each topic discussed. I also added the action items, clearly stating who was responsible for each task, the deadline, and the expected outcome. I shared this document with all the meeting participants in the meeting group so that they could review and add any additional information if needed.
