# 吃 / Eat

> 来源：《摩登家庭》第一季  
> 适合场景：与 不多/Celine 讨论吃饭相关话题

---

## 一、一日三餐 / Meals

### 早餐 / Breakfast
- **Kids, breakfast!** — 孩子们，吃早饭了！
- **Okay, your brother's lunch is packed.** — 好了，你弟弟的午餐装好了。
- **Remember, uh, breakfast for dinner last week?** — 还记得，上周用早餐当晚餐吗？
- **We're making you breakfast.** — 我们给你做早餐呢。
- **Sorry, sorry, sorry. Daddy needed snacks.** — 抱歉，我来晚了，老爹去买点心了。

### 午餐 / Lunch
- **Can I have $40 for lunch?** — 能给我40块钱吃午饭吗？
- **Forty dollars?** — 40块？
- **You're buying your own lunch.** — 你的午餐自己买。

### 晚餐 / Dinner
- **we are actually all supposed to have Sunday dinner tonight** — 其实我们今晚要搞周日聚餐
- **And then- Oh, we gotta leave for that dinner thing at 5:00.** — 然后我们五点就得去赴宴。
- **So, we were just headed out to dinner.** — 我们正打算出去吃晚餐。
- **Why don't you come over to our place for dinner tonight?** — 不如你今晚到我们家来共进晚餐吧？
- **Hang on. Dinner's ready in five minutes.** — 别玩了，晚饭马上就好。
- **Steaks will be ready in five minutes.** — 牛排五分钟后就好。

## 二、吃具体东西 / Eating Specific Foods

### 主食类
- **French toaster sticks** — 法式吐司条
- **Those pancakes smell great.** — 嗯，薄饼闻起来不错哟。
- **scone** — 松饼
- **No butter. Nonfat.** — 零黄油，无脂肪。

### 零食/点心
- **Look at that baby with those cream puffs.** — 你看那个拿着泡芙的宝宝。
- **You can never have too many pies** — 派再多也不嫌多
- **I gotta lay off these cannolis.** — 我不该再吃煎饼卷了。
- **snacks** — 点心、零食

### 水果
- **fruit** — 水果
- **grab a snow cone** — 去吃个冰霜卷吧

### 肉类/海鲜
- **I brought dinner- lobsters!** — 我带晚餐了——龙虾！
- **They got a chef there that can flip a shrimp into his own hat.** — 那有个厨师可以把虾翻进自己的帽子里。
- **Steaks will be ready in five minutes.** — 牛排五分钟后就好。
- **turkey** — 火鸡
- **She stole a turkey at Thanksgiving for her family** — 害的她在感恩节偷了只火鸡给家里

### 饮料
- **What are you drinking coffee for anyway?** — 你为什么要喝咖啡？
- **It's my culture. I'm Colombian.** — 这是我的文化习俗，我是哥伦比亚人。
- **wine** — 葡萄酒
- **We're gonna drink some wine, eat some good food.** — 去喝点小酒，吃些美食。
- **And I have some nice wine, a good Cuban cigar for Jay.** — 还带了瓶好酒，给杰的一盒上等雪茄。
- **virgin mai tais** — "处女红"鸡尾酒（无酒精）

### 快餐/外出就餐
- **we'll order a whole bunch of pizzas** — 我们可以订购许多匹萨
- **cheeseburger** — 芝士汉堡
- **I want the biggest cheeseburger you got.** — 给我来一个你们这里最大的芝士汉堡。
- **Instead of salad, I want fries.** — 把沙拉换成薯条。
- **Instead of fruit, I want chili fries.** — 把水果换成辣薯条。
- **hot dogs** — 热狗
- **on their hot dogs** — 在他们的热狗上
- **sloppy Jays / sloppy joes** — 炒牛肉酱三明治
- **I make my famous sloppy Jays** — 我做的炒牛肉"杰"（我做的炒牛肉酱）

### 甜品/冰淇淋
- **take them out for ice cream** — 带他们出去吃冰激凌
- **Manny likes ice cream.** — 曼尼喜欢吃冰激凌。
- **shaved ice** — 刨冰
- **I'm gonna go get some shaved ice** — 我去吃点刨冰

### 其他具体食物
- **chicken enchiladas** — 鸡肉玉米卷
- **grilled-cheese sandwich and tomato soup** — 烤起司三明治和番茄浓汤
- **pickles** — 腌菜/泡菜
- **Here. Would you care for a sack of pickles?** — 给你，请收下这包腌菜。
- **Do you think I really loved home pickle-making?** — 你以为我真的喜欢做腌菜吗？
- **We were picklers** — 我们是腌菜工
- **lettuce dryer** — 生菜烘干机
- **she gave me a piece of exercise equipment and a lettuce dryer.** — 她送了我一台健身器材和生菜烘干机。
- **cupcakes** — 纸杯蛋糕
- **she needs 40 cupcakes for her school fund-raiser** — 她要为学校的募捐会准备40个纸杯蛋糕
- **I will take the cupcakes.** — 我来做纸杯蛋糕。
- **yogurt maker** — 酸奶机
- **chocolates** — 巧克力
- **Maybe you'd like a chocolate.** — 来颗巧克力吧。
- **bread for the ducks** — 喂鸭子的面包
- **Did you pack the bread for the ducks?** — 喂鸭子的面包带了吗？
- **kibble** — 狗食/干粮
- **Big bowl of kibble.** — 一大碗狗食。

### 其他动词/表达
- **Let's see how these bad boys taste.** — 看看味道怎样。
- **It's like he's feeding a baby bird.** — 就像他在喂幼鸟一样。
- **I bake for 'em.** — 我烤蛋糕给他们。
- **if you bake her a cake** — 那你给她烤个蛋糕
- **I'm cooking** — 我在做饭
- **probably because you were cooking and your hands are warm.** — 可能是因为你在做饭，手温高。
- **go to a luau** — 去吃夏威夷野猪宴
- **I smoke sausages.** — 我自家熏制了香肠。
- **Ate like crap** — 总是吃垃圾食品

## 三、相关表达 / Related Expressions

- **What do you think?** — 味道如何？
- **Now, that is a scone.** — 绝对正宗的松饼啊。
- **Oh, so delicious.** — 哦，太好吃了。
- **I gotta jump in the shower. I'm gonna be late.** — 嗯，我得去洗澡了，要迟了。（饭前准备）
- **Can we just please get the diapers and get out of here?** — 我们能不能只拿尿布，买单闪人？（超市购物）
- **This is Costco.** — 这是好市多超市哎。
- **Which is where we buy diapers.** — 就是买尿布的地方啊。
