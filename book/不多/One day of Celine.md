# One day of Celine

这是 **不多** 第 76 篇学习记录。上一篇谈「摆脱哑巴英语」时提到：未必有一本完全合你口的口语书，真正合适的往往要**自己写**出来；想整理「自己的口语书」，只能靠自己从简单的开始。本篇尝试把 **不多** 最近学的单词揉进对话里。

---

## Good morning `亲子`

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Daddy, wakey wakey! Rise and shine.** | 爸爸，起床啦！太阳晒屁股啦！ |
| **Five more minutes! Honey, Daddy is too sleepy!** | 再睡五分钟！宝贝，爸爸太困了！ |
| **Your breakfast is getting cold. Come on! We’re waiting on you!** | 早饭要凉了，快点！我们在等你呢！ |
| **Just let me sleep, please!** | 让我再睡会儿吧，求你了！ |
| **Let’s get dressed! Put on your clothes and shoes!** | 穿衣服穿鞋啦！ |
| **OK! OK! Morning, Celine :) How’d you sleep?** | 好好好！早啊 Celine :) 睡得好吗？ |
| **Slept like a dog!** | 睡得很香！（习惯说法：*sleep like a log*；口语里也很常说 *slept like a baby*。） |
| **Hmm, that’s good. What are we having?** | 嗯，不错。咱们吃什么？ |
| **Are you hungry?** | 你饿吗？ |
| **So hungry I could eat a horse!** | 饿得能吃下一头马！（夸张说法：饿坏了。） |

## Getting ready (out for a walk) `亲子`

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Daddy, let’s go for a walk, OK?** | 爸爸，去散步好不好？ |
| **Come on, let’s get in the stroller.** | 来，坐进婴儿车。 |
| **Are you ready?** | 准备好了吗？ |
| **Ready!** | 好啦！ |
| **Let’s go out and play. Look at the tree. Smell the beautiful flowers.** | 出去玩吧。看看树，闻闻漂亮的花。 |
| **Look! The bird and butterfly are flying! Let’s chase the butterfly!** | 看！小鸟和蝴蝶在飞！我们去追蝴蝶！ |
| **Be careful!** | 小心！ |

*原稿里「Are you ready? / Ready!」两行都写作 Celine；按情景更常是爸爸问、孩子答，背诵时也可一人分饰两角。*

## Have a meal `亲子`

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Let’s put on your bib. Eat delicious food with a beautiful spoon.** | 戴上围兜，用漂亮勺子吃好吃的。 |
| **What do we eat today?** | 今天吃什么？ |
| **Grandma cooked the potatoes you love today, and your favorite egg, broccoli, carrot, and milk.** | 奶奶今天做了你爱吃的土豆，还有你喜欢的鸡蛋、西兰花、胡萝卜和牛奶。 |
| **Is it yummy? Do you want more?** | 好吃吗？还要吗？ |
| **Yummy, more please!** | 好吃，还要！ |

## Sleeping time `亲子`

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Let’s wash your face and change your diaper.** | 洗脸、换尿布啦。 |
| **I haven’t had my milk yet.** | 我还没喝奶呢。（更口语也可以说 *I haven’t drunk my milk yet*。） |
| **Oh, sorry, I forgot! Here is your bottle.** | 哎呀对不起，我忘了！奶瓶在这儿。 |
| **OK, close your eyes. Good night!** | 好了，闭上眼睛。晚安！ |
