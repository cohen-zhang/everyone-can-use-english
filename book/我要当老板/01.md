# 我要当老板 · 01

## 选剧原因（关键词）

**family**、**father**、**love**、**business**

---

## 人物

- **Alex** — 男主角；辞职前在电台公司做栏目策划。
- **Rooni** — 妻子，律师。
- **Ben** — 儿子。
- **Soraya** — 女儿。

---

## 带女儿见投资人

Alex 带女儿见投资人（女儿需照顾；台词里会提到多家公司与名人，仅作剧情背景）。

**投资人开门见山：**

> Look, I’ve invested in Twitter, Uber, Instagram. Why should I invest in your podcast company?

**Alex（紧张）：**

> Well, first of all, it’s not just any podcast company. It’s the Cadillac of podcast companies. Do people still use “Cadillac” as a reference? Sorry, it’s, uh… the Maybach, the Khaleesi, the Leonardo DiCaprio.

**投资人问 Soraya：**

> Wow, is your dad always this nervous?

**女儿：**

> It’s ’cause you’re rice.

*听感/字幕常被写成 **rich**（「因为你很有钱」），与童言无忌的喜剧效果有关。*

**Alex（尴尬）：**

> Soraya! I’m sorry, again, about her. We had a little childcare dilemma.

……一顿操作后，投资人对准备不足的 Alex 有些失望，最后抛出一个带**虚拟语气**的假设：

> Look, Alex, listen, if I were calling an Uber and said it was gonna be here in two minutes and that’s all the time you had… who are you and what are you doing? Go.

---

## 倒叙 · One month ago

镜头转场，旁白倒述：

> I decided, at 37, with a wife and two kids, now would be a good time to quit my job and start my own business. Who am I and what am I doing? My name is Alex Schuman, and I’m ruining my life.

*原文台词有 “One mouth ago”，一般为 “One month ago” 之误听/笔误。*

---

## 早餐 · 家庭时间

还未辞职的 Alex 与家人早餐；接完工作电话后：

> But Mommy is right… no more work talk. This is morning family time.

顺便亲了妻子一下。

**儿子：**

> Oh, I’m gonna puke up my breakfast.

**Alex：**

> All right, what’s up? I’m sorry, you have all my attention. Go.

---

## 儿子的魔术

儿子表演 Magic 失败，对搭档（一只仓鼠）抱怨：

> What the heck, Todd?

*What the heck* ≈ 「见鬼了 / 搞什么」。

---

## 会议上辞职

栏目说明会上 Alex 激情讲 Idea，被老板嘲讽并 **reject** 之后，当场决定辞职：

> And I don’t know why, but in that moment, something in me just snapped.

*snap* — 这里可理解为情绪/理智「突然断了」、一时冲动。

> You know what, you know what… I-I-I-I can’t do this anymore, okay? I-I can’t do one more feel-good piece about it… I-I don’t want to cheer people up. I want to engage their minds and open their eyes to what’s happening in the world. And I know I’m not the only one who feels this way, okay? I’m gonna go somewhere I can tell those stories. Now, who’s coming with me?

……当然，没有人和他一起走。

---

## 和妻子吃饭 · 创业

辞职后约妻子吃饭，提出做播客公司。

**妻子：**

> You quit your job?!

**Alex：**

> Yes. But what if I… and this is one of the reasons why I wanted to take you to dinner tonight… start my own podcast company.

（妻子笑。）

**Alex：**

> Why are you laughing?

**妻子：**

> Because you’re kidding. You’re not kidding? Oh, God.

**Alex：**

> Rooni, I’ve worked my whole life for other people. I want to do something I can be proud of. I want something that’s mine.

**妻子（玩笑接龙）：**

> Buy a boat. / We can’t afford a boat. / Buy a kayak. / I would love a kayak.

**妻子：**

> Alex, you’re an amazing storyteller, but… please don’t take this the wrong way… you’re not good at anything else.

**Alex：**

> I know. That’s why I’m gonna ask Eddie to be my partner.

---

## 「改变世界」

**Alex：**

> Rooni, this is my moment. This industry’s about to explode, and if it does, it could change the whole world. Let’s be a part of that. I want to change the world. Don’t you?

**妻子（被说服）：**

> Damn it. You know I’m a sucker for “change the world,” Alex.

**Alex：**

> I didn’t want to play the change-the-world card, but you left me with no other option.

**Rooni：**

> Fine, go change the world! Love you!
