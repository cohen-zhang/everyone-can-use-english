## 可数 vs 不可数（先定「能不能数」）

| 类型 | 画面 | 例 | 简中 |
|------|------|-----|------|
| **可数 countable** | 能一个个数 | **two apples**, **three kids**, **ten pairs of pants** | 两个苹果；三个孩子；十条裤子 |
| **不可数 uncountable** | 物质/抽象，不数「个」 | **rice**, **water**, **advice**, **traffic** | 米饭；水；建议；交通 |
| **容器/部分词当桥梁** | 不可数 + 容器/部分 | **a cup of rice**, **a piece of advice**, **a bottle of water** | 一杯米；一条建议；一瓶水 |

**诚实标注：** **bread** 日常常说 **a loaf of bread**（一条）或 **a slice of bread**（一片）；**paper** 当「纸」不可数，**a piece of paper** 才是一片。**hair** 指发型总量通常不可数；**a hair** 才是一根。

**与介词 of 联动：** 见 [[learning-notes/grammar-lab/介词#anchor-of|介词 · of · 部分—整体]] — **a piece of cake** / **a cup of tea** 是同一套结构。

## 容器量词 Partitive（「一容器 / 一份」）

结构：**a / an / two + 量词 + of + 名词**

## 模糊数量词（不精确，但极口语）

| 词 | 大约意思 | 例句 | 简中 | 来源 |
|----|----------|------|------|------|
| **a couple of** | 两三个（时间/人/物） | **A couple of other people** were on break. / Manny's father is taking him for **a couple of days** to Disneyland. / I could have lost all of it in **a couple of days**. | 还有两三个人在休息。/ 曼尼爸带他去迪斯尼玩几天。/ 我本可能在几天内亏光。 | Jeff E06 · Modern Family S01E02 · [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/investing/stock-and-commodity-broker-notes|经纪访谈]] |
| **a few** | 几个（比 some 略少） | **a few bananas** / **a few of the stories** / **Just a few hours** with **a couple of adults** | 几根香蕉 / 几则新闻 / 和几个大人待几个小时 | Jeff E07 · Jeff E03 · Modern Family S01E08 |
| **several** | 好几个 | **Several adults** use white lies to keep family peace. | 好几个大人为保和气说善意的谎言。 | Modern Family S01E19 |
| **a bunch of** | 一堆（口语；可略带贬义） | **A bunch of immature boys** / **a bunch of dorks** / **a bunch of necklaces** / **Got your panties in a bunch**（习语：小题大做） | 一群幼稚男生 / 一群呆瓜 / 一堆兽牙项链 / （习语）别小题大做 | Modern Family S01E16 · S01E07 |
| **a dozen / two-dozen** | 一打 / 两打（12 / 24） | He's opened up **a dozen** (accounts). / **at least two-dozen emails** I need to go through. / **a half-dozen ties** | 他开了一大堆（账户）。/ 至少二十四封邮件要处理。/ 半打领带 | Modern Family S01E20 · Jeff E06 · Jeff E04 |
| **a lot of / lots of** | 很多 | Peppa and George are having **a lot of fun**. / There were **lots of rough materials** lying around. / **What a lot of stuff!** | 玩得很开心。/ 很多粗材料散在地上。/ 东西真多！ | Peppa S01 · [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/vocab-story/2025.02.13 A Day in the City|City story]] · Peppa S01E48 |
| **plenty of** | 足够多 | **Trust me. I had plenty of fun in my time.** / **plenty of time before it rains** | 相信我，我年轻时快活够了。/ 下雨前时间还充裕。 | Modern Family S01E17 · Peppa S01E32 |
| **half a day** | 半天 | I will take **half a day off** on the afternoon of April 3. | 4 月 3 日下午请假半天。 | [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/personal-matters|个人事务]] |
| **one … and one …** | 各一（点餐） | **Can we get one vegetable dish and one meat dish?** / **Pick two apples and one pomelo, please.** | 点一个素菜一个荤菜好吗？/ 请挑两个苹果和一个柚子。 | 亲子食物词表 |

**习语提醒：** **a piece of cake** = 小菜一碟（不是「一块蛋糕」）；**Want a piece of this?** = 想跟我比划？（冲突口语，见 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/spoken-insults-name-calling-phrases|骂人口语]]）。**Everybody wants a slice.** = 人人都想分一杯羹（Modern Family S01E17）。

<a id="anchor-day-story"></a>

## 一天四段场景短文（跟读 · 量词密集）

*约 3 分钟 · ~320 words · 例句多来自 Jeff 一日与深圳家场景*

### A. 早餐 · Breakfast

**阿泽** pours **a bowl of cereal**, adds **a glass of skim milk**, and takes **a spoon** from the silverware tray. The coffeemaker needs **a piece of paper** or **a piece of plastic**—the filter—before **a cup of coffee** drips clean. **Celine** asks for **a slice of toast** with jam; **不多** wants **a little more** soup later, not chips before lunch. **Add a pinch of salt** to the eggs; **a splash of dark soy sauce** waits for tonight's braise. When the bread runs out, there's still **a banana** and **a bottle of water** on the table within arm's reach.

**简中：** 阿泽倒**一碗麦片**、加**一杯脱脂奶**，从餐具托盘拿**一把勺**。咖啡机要先垫**一张滤纸**（或**一片塑料滤片**）才滴得出干净**一杯咖啡**。**Celine** 要**一片果酱吐司**；**不多**说午饭后才多喝**一点汤**，饭前别吃薯片。鸡蛋**撒一小撮盐**；今晚红烧等着**一点老抽**。**面包**没了，桌上伸手还有**一根香蕉**和**一瓶水**。

---

### B. 穿衣 · Getting dressed

In the closet: about thirty dress shirts, **ten pairs of pants**, **a half-dozen ties**, and **a pair of socks** pulled out fresh. **A pair of khakis** beats color-guessing before the stand-up. Keys and phone go in the front pocket of **a pair of pants**; the wallet rides the back. **These running shoes** are done—time for **a new pair**.

**简中：** 衣柜里大约三十件衬衫、**十条裤子**、**半打领带**；新换**一双袜子**。**一条卡其裤**比瞎配色靠谱。**一条裤子**前袋放钥匙手机，后袋放钱包。跑鞋报废了，该买**一双新的**。

---

### C. 上班 · At the desk

Inbox: **a stack of memos** and **at least two-dozen emails**. **A couple of coworkers** are on break, grabbing **a piece of food** from the vending machine and reading notices on **a big piece of** bulletin board. Spot wallet holds USDT and **a little BTC**; I'll convert **a small slice** later—not the whole stack. **Don't FOMO** into **a green candle** at the top.

**简中：** 收件箱**一摞备忘录**、**至少二十四封邮件**。**两三个同事**休息，从售货机买**一份零食**，看**公告板**上的通知。现货钱包有 USDT 和**少量 BTC**；待会儿只闪兑**一小笔**——别动整仓。别在顶部阳线 **FOMO** 追高。

---

### D. 超市回家 · Market run

Fridge empty—stop for **a bag of apples**, **a few bananas**, **some pasta**, **tomato sauce**, and **a cooked chicken**. **Pick two apples and one pomelo**, **Celine**; wash all fruit first. **One cup** from the **rice dispenser** tonight. Rinse the crisper; leave **a box of baking soda** overnight. Open the trunk and **load everything up**.

**简中：** 冰箱空了——买**一袋苹果**、**几根香蕉**、**一些意面**、**意面酱**和**一只烤鸡**。**Celine**，挑**两个苹果一个柚子**；回家先洗。**今晚米桶按一杯出米**。冲洗果蔬抽屉，**放一盒小苏打过夜**。打开后备箱**全部装车**。

## 学习策略（一句话）

1. **不可数名词**先想「用什么容器/部分词」— **a cup of rice**, not *a rice*.
2. **pair** 永远当整体：**a pair of pants** = 一条裤子；**two pairs** 才数两条。
3. **couple / few / several** 别死抠数字；听语气：**a couple of days** ≈ 两三天。
4. 习语 **piece / slice / bunch** 单独收录 Anki，别按字面译。
