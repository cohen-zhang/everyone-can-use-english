## 本页在做什么

用**同一幅画面**，示范两件事：

1. **扩写句子**：从最短「核心句」一层层加上宾语、状语、补语，信息变多、句子变长，意思不跑偏。  
2. **成分换序**：还是**同一件事**，换主语起点、把地点/时间放句首、用存现句、被动句等，练习**英语语序**，而不是中文逐词翻译。

每条英文下附**简中**；表格里标注 **S**（主语）、**V**（谓语）、**O**（宾语）、**A**（状语）、**C**（补语）。

## 成分速查（对照中文「主谓宾状补」）

释义压缩自 [Cambridge Dictionary · English Grammar Today](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/) 对 *subject / verb / predicate / object / adverbial / complement* 的说明；**IPA 为美式**，可查 [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/)。

| 中文 | 英文术语 | IPA（美） | 词典释义（英） | 简例 | 简中 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主语 | **Subject (S)** | /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt/ 或 /ˈsʌbdʒekt/ | The word or group of words that tells you **who or what** performs the action, or **what the sentence is about**. | **Celine** reads. | 谁 / 什么在做 |
| 谓语 | **Verb (V)** | /vɜːrb/ | A word or group of words that describes an **action**, **state**, or **experience**. | Celine **reads**. | 动作或状态 |
| 谓语（整段） | **Predicate** | /ˈpredɪkət/ | The part of a clause that **includes the verb** and what follows it, telling what the subject **does or is**. | Celine **reads a book in the park**. | 谓语部分 = 动词 + 宾语/补语/状语等 |
| 宾语 | **Object (O)** | /ˈɑːbdʒekt/ 或 /ˈɑːbdʒɪkt/ | A noun, noun phrase, or pronoun that is **affected by the action** of a verb, or that follows a preposition. | Celine reads **a book**. | 动作承受者 |
| 状语 | **Adverbial (A)** | /ədˈvɜːrbiəl/ | A word, phrase, or clause that adds information about **when, where, how, or why** something happens. | Celine reads **in the park**. | 时间、地点、方式、原因等 |
| 补语 | **Complement (C)** | /ˈkɑːmplɪment/ | A word or group of words that **completes the meaning** of a verb, adjective, or other expression. | The book is **interesting**. / Mom keeps Celine **happy**. | 补充说明主语或宾语 |
| 主语补语 | **Subject complement** | /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt ˈkɑːmplɪment/ | A word or group of words that follows a **linking verb** and **describes the subject**. | The book is **interesting**. / Mom is **happy**. | 系动词后，说明主语 |
| 宾语补语 | **Object complement** | /ˈɑːbdʒɪkt ˈkɑːmplɪment/ | A word or group of words that follows an **object** and **describes or renames** it. | Mom keeps Celine **happy**. / I made Mom **smile**. | 宾语后，说明宾语 |

> **定语（modifier / attribute）：** 英语语法书常不单列「定语」成分；形容词修饰多写在名词前（*a **red** apple*）或放在名词后从句里（*the book **that Celine reads***）。Cambridge 用 *modifier* / *adjective phrase* 描述这类功能，见 [Modifiers](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/modifiers-and-other-parts-of-speech)。

## 八大词类速查（Parts of Speech）

**词类**问的是：这个词在句子里**扮演什么角色**（名、动、形、副……）。与上一节**句子成分**对照：**词类**是词的「身份牌」；**成分**是词在某一具体句里的「岗位」（例如同一个 **noun** 可以是 **Subject** 或 **Object**）。

释义压缩自 [Cambridge Dictionary · English Grammar Today](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/parts-of-speech) 对八大词类的说明；**IPA 为美式**。亲子分篮玩法见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/games-and-activities/parenting-category-classification-game#21-词类--parts-of-speech|分类归类游戏 · §21 词类]]（与此表 **相向互链**）。

| 词类（EN） | 中文 | IPA（美） | 词典释义（英） | 回答什么问题 | 亲子例词 | 亲子句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **noun** | 名词 | /naʊn/ | A word that refers to a **person, place, thing, event, substance, or quality**. | 谁？什么？哪里？ | dog, Celine, school, happiness | **Celine** has a **dog**. — **Celine** 有一只 **dog**。 |
| **verb** | 动词 | /vɜːrb/ | A word or phrase that describes an **action**, **state**, or **experience**. | 做什么？什么状态？ | run, eat, is, have | **Celine runs** fast. — **Celine 跑**得快。 |
| **adjective** | 形容词 | /ˈædʒɪktɪv/ | A word that **describes a noun or pronoun**. | 什么样的？ | big, happy, red, tired | A **big** bear. — 一只**大**熊。 |
| **adverb** | 副词 | /ˈædvɜːrb/ | A word that **describes or gives more information about** a verb, adjective, adverb, or phrase. | 怎样地？何时？何地？（常修饰动词） | quickly, very, here, often | Run **quickly**. — **快**跑。 |
| **pronoun** | 代词 | /ˈproʊnaʊn/ | A word that is used **instead of a noun or noun phrase**. | 代替名词 | I, you, she, it, this, who | **She** is happy. — **她**很开心。 |
| **preposition** | 介词 | /ˌprepəˈzɪʃn/ | A word used **before a noun, noun phrase, or pronoun**, connecting it to another word. | 在……上／里／旁（表关系） | in, on, under, with, to | **In** the room. — **在**房间里。 |
| **conjunction** | 连词 | /kənˈdʒʌŋkʃn/ | A word that **connects words, phrases, or clauses**. | 连接词或句 | and, but, because, or | **And** then we eat. — **然后**我们吃。 |
| **interjection** | 感叹词 | /ˌɪntərˈdʒekʃn/ | A short sound, word, or phrase spoken suddenly to express **strong emotion**. | 突然的情绪 | oh, wow, hey, ouch | **Wow**, a rainbow! — **哇**，彩虹！ |

> **词类 ↔ 成分（一眼对照）：** **noun / pronoun** → 常当 **S** 或 **O**；**verb** → **V**（或 **Predicate** 核心）；**adjective** → 常修饰名词（定语功能）或作 **Subject complement**；**adverb** → 常作 **Adverbial**；**preposition** → 与名词构成介词短语，多作 **Adverbial**；**conjunction** → 连接分句，本身通常不算单一成分。**interjection** 多独立成句，如 *Wow!*

## 示范 1 · 公园读书（亲子 · 扩写）

**画面：** 傍晚，**Celine** 在小区公园长椅上看绘本。

### 1.1 由短到长（扩写阶梯）

| 层级 | 英文 | 简中 | 新增成分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ① 核心 | **Celine reads.** | 不多在看。 | S + V |
| ② +宾 | **Celine reads a picture book.** | 不多在看一本绘本。 | + O |
| ③ +地点状 | **Celine reads a picture book on a park bench.** | 不多在公园长椅上看绘本。 | + A（地点） |
| ④ +时间状 | **Every evening, Celine reads a picture book on a park bench.** | 每天傍晚，不多在公园长椅上看绘本。 | + A（时间，句首） |
| ⑤ +方式状 | **Every evening, Celine reads a picture book quietly on a park bench.** | 每天傍晚，不多在公园长椅上静静看绘本。 | + A（方式） |
| ⑥ +原因状 | **Every evening, Celine reads a picture book quietly on a park bench to wind down before bed.** | ……睡前放松一下。 | + A（目的） |
| ⑦ +主语补 | **Every evening, the picture book keeps Celine calm on the park bench before bed.** | 每天傍晚，绘本让不多在睡前安静下来。（换句式） | 换 V + 宾补 |

**朗读提示：** 念完 ① 再叠 ②，一层一层加，嘴跟得上，就是在练「扩写」。

### 1.2 同一画面 · 成分换序

**中文同一事实：** 不多在公园长椅上看绘本。

| 焦点 | 英文 | 成分（简注） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **主语开头（常规）** | **Celine** reads a picture book **on a park bench**. | S + V + O + A |
| **时间放句首** | **Every evening**, **Celine** reads a picture book on a park bench. | A + S + V + O + A |
| **地点放句首** | **On a park bench in the park**, **Celine** is reading a picture book. | A + S + V + O |
| **宾语 / 物作主语** | **A picture book** sits in **Celine's** hands on the park bench. | S（物）+ V + A |
| **存现句** | **There is** a girl reading a picture book **on a park bench**. | 存现 + 分词短语 |
| **倒装强调地点** | **On the bench** sits **Celine**, reading her picture book. | A + V + S |
| **宾语话题化** | **The picture book** — **Celine** reads it **every evening** on that bench. | O 前置 + S + V + A |
| **被动（书被读）** | **A picture book is read** by Celine on the park bench every evening. | O → S + 被动 + A |

## 示范 2 · 母亲节送花（生活 · 扩写 + 换序）

**画面：** **泽哥**把一束花送给妈妈。

### 2.1 扩写阶梯

| 层级 | 英文 | 简中 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① | **I give flowers.** | 我送花。 |
| ② | **I give Mom flowers.** | 我送妈妈花。 |
| ③ | **I give Mom a bouquet of flowers.** | 我送妈妈一束花。 |
| ④ | **On Mother's Day, I give Mom a bouquet of flowers.** | 母亲节那天，我送妈妈一束花。 |
| ⑤ | **On Mother's Day, I give Mom a bouquet of flowers at the breakfast table.** | ……在早餐桌旁。 |
| ⑥ | **On Mother's Day, I give Mom a bouquet of flowers at the breakfast table because she always takes care of us.** | ……因为她一直照顾我们。 |
| ⑦ | **Mom looks happy with the bouquet I gave her on Mother's Day.** | 妈妈拿着花，看起来很开心。（换主语与句式） |

### 2.2 同一画面 · 成分换序

| 焦点 | 英文 | 成分（简注） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **主谓宾双宾** | **I** give **Mom** **a bouquet** on Mother's Day. | S + V + O（人）+ O（物） |
| **时间句首** | **On Mother's Day**, **I** give Mom a bouquet. | A + S + V + O |
| **地点句首** | **At the breakfast table**, **I** hand Mom a bouquet. | A + S + V + O |
| **花作主语** | **A bouquet of flowers** waits on the table for Mom. | S（物）+ V + A |
| **使役 + 宾补** | **I** made **Mom** **smile** with a bouquet on Mother's Day. | S + V + O + C（宾补） |
| **系表 + 主补** | **Mom** is **happy** with the bouquet on the table. | S + V + C（主补） |
| **被动** | **A bouquet** was given **to Mom** on Mother's Day. | S + 被动 + A |

## 示范 3 · 修缺陷（工作 · 短例）

**画面：** 研发修了一个线上缺陷。

### 3.1 扩写（四层即可）

| 层级 | 英文 | 简中 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① | **We fixed a bug.** | 我们修了一个 bug。 |
| ② | **We fixed a production bug.** | 我们修了一个线上 bug。 |
| ③ | **Yesterday, we fixed a production bug in the payment module.** | 昨天我们修了支付模块的一个线上 bug。 |
| ④ | **Yesterday, we fixed a production bug in the payment module before the release review.** | ……在发布评审之前。 |

### 3.2 换序（同一事实）

| 焦点 | 英文 |
| --- | --- |
| 常规 | **We** fixed **a production bug** **in the payment module** yesterday. |
| 时间前置 | **Yesterday**, **we** fixed a production bug in the payment module. |
| 模块前置 | **In the payment module**, **we** fixed a production bug yesterday. |
| Bug 作主语 | **The production bug** in the payment module **was fixed** yesterday. |
| 存现 | **There was** a production bug in payments; **we fixed it** yesterday. |

## 扩写口诀（自用）

1. **先 S + V**（谁干什么）  
2. **再加 O**（干什么 / 对谁）  
3. **再加 A**（何时、何地、怎样、为何）— 时间、地点常可放句首  
4. **需要时换 C**（*keep / make / find* + 补语，或系动词 + 形容词）  
5. **单句尽量 ≤ 20 词**；太长就拆成两句（与教练 Prompt 一致）
