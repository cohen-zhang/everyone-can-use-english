
pre-position 定义位置和空间的关系的词

1. on 

词源：
on 不是在……上面；
原始画面：更侧重贴在二维平台上；用贴纸比喻

on the street


in the car 是三维里面，包裹感


## 时间 

at 7 o'clock：时间轴上的精确点
on Monday 纸面
in Apir/Evening 容器


## 抽象
on duty 

go on 继续



2. at  戳一个点

符号：@
不要翻译成“在”


laugh at someone
yell at 
loot at 

3. with 
不是“和”
词源：对抗
原始画面：合二为一

withstand 抵抗/ withhold 扣留 / withdraw 撤退
write with a pen/ cut with a knife
He fights with his brother;  VS fight alongside him /together with him
A girl with a long hair

Filed with joy
come with me 
I need to talk to you ;  VS 
I need to talk with you 


4. Of 
表示：“属”； 而不是“的”
画面：掉落的树叶 ；
The Color of sky
A piece of cake 
A cup of tea
United states of America

made of wood 木头制造
made from grapes 葡萄洒

VS “‘s” 
Jone's car
The president's decision

Of course: 轨道
Proud of you 因你而骄傲 
Smell of rose
Born of noble blood
