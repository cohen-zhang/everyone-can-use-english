## 朗读串联记忆 · Read-aloud chain

*约 1 分钟 · 先英后对照简中*

### A. 分句场景链

- **She lost her short-term memory.** — 她失去了短期记忆。
- **She has no memory that she ever met you.** — 她完全不记得曾经见过你。
- **Every day is different, but basically this is what happens.** — 每天都不一样，但基本上就是这样。
- **Why couldn't I have met you one day before the accident?** — 我为什么不能在事故前一天遇见你。
- **To make me fall in love with you every day.** — 让我每天都爱上你。
- **She remembers me!** — 她记得我！

### B. 一段串联

**She lost her short-term memory. She has no memory that she ever met you. Every day is different, but basically this is what happens. Why couldn't I have met you one day before the accident? To make me fall in love with you every day. She remembers me!**

**简中：** 她失去了短期记忆。她完全不记得曾经见过你。每天都不一样，但基本上就是这样。我为什么不能在事故前一天遇见你。让我每天都爱上你。她记得我！
