## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 还要辅助轮吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Are you coming?** | 你来吗？ | **Are you coming?** | 别人已经骑上山 |
| **Do you think you can ride without them?** | 你觉得没有辅助轮你能骑吗？ | **Can you ride without them?** | 她想拆掉辅助轮 |
| **Are you sure?** | 你确定吗？ | **Are you sure?** | 她说自己可以 |
| **Would you like some help?** | 你想要人帮一下吗？ | **Would you like some help?** | 她骑不稳 |

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *Peppa and George are riding their bicycles.* | *You are riding your bicycle.* |
| **have to** | *We have to be very careful not to bump into it.* | *You have to be careful.* |
| **wish + 过去式** | *Peppa wishes she did not have stabilizers.* | *I wish I did not need them.* |
| **现在完成进行时** | *You've been cycling on your own for ages.* | *You have been riding on your own.* |
| **will** | *I promise I will.* | *I promise I will look.* |
| **there will be** | *There will be enough pumpkin pie for everyone.* | *There will be enough for everyone.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Watch out.** — 小心。
- **Don't let go.** — 别松手。
- **Keep pedaling.** — 继续蹬。
- **Look where you are going.** — 看清楚往哪儿走。
