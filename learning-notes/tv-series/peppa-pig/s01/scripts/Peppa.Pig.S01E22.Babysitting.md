## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 该睡觉了

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Are you awake?** | 你醒着吗？ | **Are you still awake?** | 该睡了，看她是不是还醒着 |
| **What strange noises.** | 什么奇怪的声音。 | **What was that noise?** | 楼上或隔壁有响动 |
| **I wonder if we should check upstairs.** | 要不要上楼看看。 | **Should we check upstairs?** | 不确定孩子是不是已经睡着 |

### 还想玩

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Can we watch TV with you?** | 我们可以跟你一起看电视吗？ | **Can I watch with you?** | 她想再看一会儿 |
| **Can you play that game where you throw us up and catch us?** | 你能玩把我们抛起来再接住的游戏吗？ | **Can you throw the ball and I catch it?** | 睡前还想玩一轮 |
| **Hello? Anyone here?** | 有人在吗？ | **Hello? Anyone here?** | 进门时家里静悄悄 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be going to** | *Granny Pig and Grandpa Pig are going to baby-sit.* | *Grandma is going to stay with you.* |
| **一般过去时** | *They just fell asleep when Mummy Pig told them to.* | *You fell asleep before the story ended.* |
| **一般现在时** | *Are you awake?* | *Are you still awake?* |
| **should** | *You should be in bed asleep.* | *You should be in bed.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Into your beds!** — 上床。
- **Now go to sleep quickly.** — 快点睡。
- **Shhh!** — 嘘。
