## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 你喜欢吃吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Would you like tomatoes?** | 你要不要西红柿？ | **Would you like some rice?** — 你要不要来点饭？ | 盛饭的时候 |
| **Do you like tomatoes?** | 你喜欢西红柿吗？ | **Do you like carrots?** — 你喜欢胡萝卜吗？ | 她皱眉 |
| **Do you like cucumber?** | 你喜欢黄瓜吗？ | **Do you like this green one?** — 你喜欢这个绿色的吗？ | 她还没尝 |
| **What vegetable do you like?** | 你喜欢哪种菜？ | **What food do you like today?** — 你今天喜欢吃什么？ | 她什么都摇头 |

### 吃饱了吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Don't you like the salad?** | 你不喜欢这份沙拉吗？ | **Don't you like your soup?** — 你不喜欢这碗汤吗？ | 她把菜拨到一边 |
| **Would you like some more?** | 你还要一点吗？ | **Would you like a little more rice?** — 你还要一点饭吗？ | 碗快空了 |
| **Are you too full to eat any more?** | 你是不是太饱，吃不下了？ | **Are you too full for another bite?** — 你是不是太饱，一口都吃不下了？ | 她说吃饱了，又看见蛋糕 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **would like** | *Would you like to pick some vegetables for lunch?* | *Would you like to pick an apple?* — 你要不要去摘一个苹果？ |
| **一般现在时** | *George does not like tomatoes.* | *You do not like this soup.* — 你不喜欢这碗汤。 |
| **have to** | *First, we have to wash them.* | *First, we have to wash the fruit.* — 我们得先洗水果。 |
| **现在完成时** | *George has eaten his pizza.* | *You have eaten your rice.* — 你把饭吃完了。 |
| **won't** | *Then you won't want any of this chocolate cake.* | *Then you will not want any cake.* — 那你就不要蛋糕了。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Let's choose some vegetables for lunch.** — 我们选点菜做午饭。
- **Tuck in, everyone.** — 大家开动。
- **Just try a little bit.** — 就尝一小口。
- **Try a piece of lettuce.** — 尝一片生菜。

## 特色 · don't like / enough

这一集 George 什么菜都不喜欢，Peppa 说的是 **does not like**。洗菜洗到 **enough**，人吃到 **too full**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **does not like** 不喜欢西红柿 | *George does not like tomatoes.* | *You do not like tomatoes today.* — 你今天不喜欢西红柿。 |
| **does not like** 不喜欢生菜 | *George does not like lettuce.* | *You do not like the green leaves.* — 你不喜欢这些绿叶子。 |
| **does not like** 不喜欢黄瓜 | *George does not like cucumbers.* | *Cucumber is not your favorite.* — 黄瓜不是你最喜欢的。 |
| **Don't you like** 你不喜欢吗 | *Don't you like the salad?* | *Don't you like your rice?* — 你不喜欢这碗饭吗？ |
| **enough** 够了 | *Maybe that's enough washing.* | *That is enough washing.* — 洗得够了。 |
| **too full** 太饱 | *George is too full to eat any more.* | *You are too full to eat another bite.* — 你太饱了，一口都吃不下了。 |
| **won't want** 那就不要了 | *Then you won't want any of this chocolate cake.* | *Then you will not want this cookie.* — 那你就不要这块饼干了。 |
