## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be going to** | *Peppa is going to her first ballet lesson.* | *You are going to your first lesson.* |
| **will** | *I'll pick you up later.* | *I will pick you up later.* |
| **might** | *What noise do you think a swan might make?* | *What noise might it make?* |
| **used to** | *Mummy Pig and I used to be quite good at ballet.* | *We used to do ballet.* |
| **一般过去时** | *It was easy for me.* | *It was easy.* |
| **will** | *You will find it very hard.* | *You will find it hard.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Run and join your friends.** — 跑去和朋友们一起。
- **Raise your arms.** — 举起手臂。
- **Copy what I do.** — 照我这样做。
