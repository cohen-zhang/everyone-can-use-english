**S1-15 daddy loses his glasses**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态** 两节是这一集的跟读版。

### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa Pig.

Daddy Loses his Glasses!

### Scene · Daddy Pig Wears Glasses（爸爸的眼镜不见了 · 段1/3）
Daddy Pig Wears glasses.

He needs to wear glasses to see clearly.

When Daddy Pig Wears his glasses, everything Looks fine.

But when Daddy Pig takes his glasses off...

...he can't see things clearly.

Everything Looks a bit soft and fuzzy.

So, it is very important that Daddy Pig knows Where his glasses are.

Sometimes, Daddy Pig Loses his glasses.

Peppa, George. Have you seen Daddy Pig's glasses?

He can't find them anywhere.

No, Mummy.

Peppa and George do not know Where Daddy Pig's glasses are.

Oh, dear. Daddy Pig cannot see a thing Without them and it makes him very grumpy.

Without his glasses on, Daddy Pig cannot read his newspaper.

This is ridiculous!

I can't see anything!

Somebody must have put my glasses somewhere.

Do you remember Where you Last put them, Daddy Pig?

### Scene · When I Don't Wear Them（爸爸的眼镜不见了 · 段2/3）


When I don't wear them, I always put them in my pocket.

But they aren't there now.

Daddy! Can We help find your glasses?

Good idea, Peppa.

If you find them, Daddy will stop being so grumpy.

I'm not grumpy.

Peppa and George are Looking for Daddy's glasses.

Peppa looks under the newspaper.

But Daddy Pig's glasses are not there.

Oh.

George looks on top of the television.

But Daddy Pig's glasses are not there.

Oh.

Let's Look upstairs in Mummy and Daddy's bedroom!

Peppa and George are Looking in Mummy and Daddy Pig's bedroom.

George... be careful not to knock anything over.

Argh!

It's not funny.

Peppa looks under the pillows.

But Daddy Pig's glasses are not there.

Oh.

### Scene · George Looks in（爸爸的眼镜不见了 · 段3/3）


George looks in Daddy's sIippers.

But the glasses are not there, either.

Let's Look in the bathroom!

Peppa and George are Looking in the bathroom.

The glasses are not in the bath.

Oh.

The glasses are not in the toilet.

Oh.

Hmm ! It's too difficult.

Peppa and George cannot find Daddy Pig's glasses anywhere.

We've Looked everywhere.

But We can't find Daddy's glasses.

Oh, dear. Now, What can We do?

I suppose I'll just have to Learn to do Without them...

If I move slowly, I won't bump into things.

There they are!

Daddy's glasses!

Daddy Pig. You Were sitting on them all the time!

Oh...

Silly Daddy!

I don't know how they got there.

I Wonder how!

Well, you may have been a bit silly, Daddy Pig, but at Least you're not grumpy anymore!

I Was not grumpy.


### Closing · Outro（片尾）
I'm Peppa Pig. This is my little brother, George.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 眼镜呢

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Have you seen Daddy Pig's glasses?** | 你们看见爸爸的眼镜了吗？ | **Have you seen my glasses?** | 东西不见了，先问她 |
| **Do you remember where you last put them?** | 你记得最后放在哪儿了吗？ | **Where did you last put them?** | 她说没看见 |
| **Can we help find your glasses?** | 我们可以帮你找眼镜吗？ | **Can I help you look?** | 她想一起找 |

### 还是没有

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **We've looked everywhere.** | 我们到处都找了。 | **Where else can we look?** | 几处都没有 |
| **Now, what can we do?** | 现在怎么办？ | **What can we do?** | 准备放弃的时候 |
| **I wonder how!** | 我想知道怎么会这样！ | **How did they get there?** | 东西出现在意想不到的地方 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *Daddy Pig wears glasses.* | *You wear glasses.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Have you seen Daddy Pig's glasses?* | *Have you seen them?* |
| **现在完成时** | *We've looked everywhere.* | *We have looked in the bedroom.* |
| **过去进行时** | *You were sitting on them all the time.* | *You were sitting on them.* |
| **may have** | *You may have been a bit silly.* | *You may have put them there.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Be careful not to knock anything over.** — 小心，别碰倒东西。
- **Move slowly.** — 慢慢走。
