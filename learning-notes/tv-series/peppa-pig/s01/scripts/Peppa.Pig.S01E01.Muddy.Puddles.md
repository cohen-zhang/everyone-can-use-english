### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa pig.

Muddy Puddles.

### Scene · It Is Raining Today（泥坑 · 段1/3）
It is raining today.

So, Peppa and George Cannot play outside.

Daddy, it's stopped raining.

Can we go out to play?

Alright, run along you two.

Peppa loves jumping in muddy puddles.

I love muddy puddles.

Peppa. If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.

Sorry, Mummy.

George likes to jump in muddy puddles, too.

George. If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.

Peppa likes to look after her little brother, George.

George, Let's find some more puddles.

Peppa and George are having a lot of fun.

Peppa has found a little puddle.

George has found a big puddle.

### Scene · Look George There's（泥坑 · 段2/3）


Look, George. There's a really big puddle.

George wants to jump into the big puddle first.

Stop, George.

I must check if it's safe for you.

Good. It is safe for you.

Sorry, George. It's only mud.

Peppa and George love jumping in muddy puddles.

Come on, George.

Let's go and show Daddy.

Goodness me.

Daddy. Daddy.

Guess what we've been doing.

Let me think...

Have you been watching television?

No. No. Daddy.

Have you just had a bath?

No. No.

I know. You've been jumping in muddy puddles.

### Scene · Jumping in Muddy Puddles（泥坑 · 段3/3）



Yes. Yes. Daddy. We've been jumping in muddy puddles.

Ho. Ho. And look at the mess you're in.

Oooh...

Oh, well, it's only mud.

Let's clean up quickly before Mummy sees the mess.

Daddy, when we've cleaned up, will you and Mummy Come and play, too?

Yes, we can all play in the garden.

Peppa and George are wearing their boots.

Mummy and Daddy are wearing their boots.

Peppa loves jumping up and down in muddy puddles.

Everyone loves jumping up and down in muddy puddles.

Oh, Daddy pig, Look at the mess you're in.

It's only mud.


### Closing · Outro（片尾）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *It is raining today.* | *It is raining.* |
| **现在完成进行时** | *We've been jumping in muddy puddles.* | *You've been jumping in puddles.* |
| **must** | *You must wear your boots.* | *You must wear your boots.* |
| **will** | *Will you and Mummy come and play, too?* | *Will you come and play?* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Run along.** — 去玩吧。
- **Stop.** — 停下。
- **Let's clean up quickly.** — 我们快点收拾。

## 特色 · If you jump, you must

这一集跳泥坑，主句是 **if** 加 **must**：要是跳，就必须穿靴子。George 也喜欢跳，所以同一句又对他说了一遍。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **if** 要是 | *If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.* | *If you play in the rain, you must wear a coat.* — 你要是在雨里玩，就必须穿外套。 |
| **must** 必须 | *If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.* | *You must wear a coat in the rain.* — 下雨你必须穿外套。 |
| **too** 也 | *George likes to jump in muddy puddles, too.* | *You like puddles, too.* — 你也喜欢水坑。 |
| **if ... must** 对 George 再说一遍 | *George. If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.* | *If Celine jumps, she must wear boots.* — 不多要是跳，就必须穿靴子。 |

## 亲子讲解 · muddy、look after、puddle

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### muddy

*Do you know the word muddy?* — 你知道 muddy 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: muddy.* — 跟我说：muddy。

*Muddy means covered in mud.* — muddy 就是沾满了泥。

*There's a difference between mud and muddy.* — mud 和 muddy 不一样。

*Mud is the dirt. Muddy means it has mud on it.* — mud 是泥。muddy 是上面有泥。

*For example, the path is muddy.* — 比方说，这条小路全是泥。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### look after

*Please look after your brother.* — 请照看一下弟弟。

*Do you understand this sentence?* — 这句话你听懂了吗？

*Do you know what "look after" means?* — 你知道 look after 是什么意思吗？

*Look after means to watch someone and keep them safe.* — look after 是照看，让人安全。

*Notice that we say look after, not look for.* — 注意说 look after，不说 look for。

*Look for means you are trying to find something.* — look for 是在找东西。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### puddle

*Do you know the word puddle?* — 你知道 puddle 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: puddle.* — 跟我说：puddle。

*A puddle is a little pool of water on the ground.* — puddle 是地上的一小滩水。

*We call a muddy one a muddy puddle.* — 沾了泥的，我们叫 muddy puddle。

*For example, don't step in that puddle.* — 比方说，别踩进那个水坑。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
