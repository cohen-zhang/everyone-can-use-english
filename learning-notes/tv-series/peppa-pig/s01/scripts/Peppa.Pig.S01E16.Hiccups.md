### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa Pig.

Hiccups!

### Scene · Peppa George Mummy and Daddy（打嗝 · 段1/3）
Peppa, George, Mummy and Daddy are having breakfast.

It is a Lovely, sunny day, and Peppa and George can't Wait to go into the garden to play.

Mummy, please, Can We go out to play?

You can go out to play When you finish your breakfast.

George! Don't drink your juice too quickly!

George drank his juice too quickly, and now he has hiccups.

Can We go out to play now, Mummy?

Not while George has hiccups.

But George's hiccups have gone, Mummy.

Haven't they, George?

Oh, Alright, you can go out and play.

But Look after George.

Peppa and George love playing in the garden.

George. I'm going to throw the ball, and then you have to catch it.

George, please stop hiccupping!

### Scene · George Now You（打嗝 · 段2/3）


George! Now you throw the ball to me, and I have to catch it.

George!

Oh, dear, George's hiccups are spoiiing the game.

George... I know how to cure hiccups!

You have to do What I say.

Rub the top of your head and rub your tummy!

Good! Now your hiccups have gone!

Peppa's cure for hiccups doesn't seem to have Worked.

George! You have done it all Wrong!

I know a better Way to cure hiccups!

First, jump up and down three times... on one Leg!

Now... Wave your arms up and down and stick your tongue out!

... And now...

close your eyes, and turn around quickly, three times!

### Scene · Three Times（打嗝 · 段3/3）



Peppa's new cure hasn't Worked, either.

George still has hiccups.

I know!

To cure hiccups, the iII piggy must be given a shock!

George, I'm going to scare you.

But you must remember, it's only a game and it will stop your hiccups.

Remember, this is just pretend scaring.

Waaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaah!

Peppa! You mustn't play so roughly with George!

He's only Little.

Sorry, Mummy.

I Was just trying to stop George's hiccups.

George's hiccups have gone!

Here comes Daddy Pig, with two beakers of juice.

George! If you drink too quickly, you will get hiccups again!

I'm not as Little as George.

I can drink juice as quickly as I want to!

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **when + 一般现在时** | *You can go out when you finish your breakfast.* | *You can go out when you finish.* |
| **现在完成时** | *George's hiccups have gone.* | *Your hiccups have gone.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Peppa's cure doesn't seem to have worked.* | *It doesn't seem to have worked.* |
| **will** | *If you drink too quickly, you will get hiccups again.* | *You will get hiccups again.* |
| **mustn't** | *You mustn't play so roughly with George.* | *You mustn't play so roughly.* |
| **过去进行时** | *I was just trying to stop George's hiccups.* | *I was trying to help.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Don't drink too quickly.** — 别喝太快。
- **Rub your tummy.** — 搓搓肚子。
- **Stick your tongue out.** — 把舌头伸出来。
- **Close your eyes.** — 闭上眼。

## 特色 · Too quickly and again

这一集乔治喝太快，打了嗝。后面又说，喝太快会 **again**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **too quickly** 喝得太快 | *George drank his juice too quickly, and now he has hiccups.* | *You drank your milk too quickly.* — 你牛奶喝得太快了。 |
| **too quickly** 别喝太快 | *Don't drink your juice too quickly!* | *Do not drink your milk too quickly.* — 别把牛奶喝得太快。 |
| **again** 会再来 | *If you drink too quickly, you will get hiccups again.* | *If you gulp it, the hiccups come again.* — 你要是大口灌，打嗝还会再来。 |
| **as quickly as** 想多快就多快 | *I can drink juice as quickly as I want to.* | *You may walk as quickly as you want.* — 你想走多快就走多快。 |
| **quickly** 快点转 | *Close your eyes, and turn around quickly, three times!* | *Turn around quickly, then sit down.* — 快点转身，然后坐下。 |

## 亲子讲解 · hiccup、spoil、wave

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### hiccup

*Do you know the word hiccup?* — 你知道 hiccup 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: hiccup.* — 跟我说：hiccup。

*A hiccup is the little jump in your throat.* — hiccup 是嗓子里那一下嗝。

*We usually say hiccups when they keep coming.* — 一下又一下，常说 hiccups。

*For example, drink slowly if you have hiccups.* — 比方说，打嗝的话就慢慢喝。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### spoil

*Do you know the word spoil?* — 你知道 spoil 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: spoil.* — 跟我说：spoil。

*Spoil means to ruin a game or a plan.* — spoil 是把游戏或计划搅乱。

*We say the rain spoiled the picnic.* — 我们说 the rain spoiled the picnic。

*It does not mean the food went bad, in this line.* — 这一集不是说食物坏了。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### wave

*Do you know the word wave?* — 你知道 wave 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: wave.* — 跟我说：wave。

*Here, wave means move your arms.* — 这里的 wave 是挥手臂，不是海浪。

*There's a difference between wave your arms and a wave in the sea.* — 挥手和海里的浪不是同一个 wave。

*For example, wave your arms slowly.* — 比方说，慢慢挥手臂。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
