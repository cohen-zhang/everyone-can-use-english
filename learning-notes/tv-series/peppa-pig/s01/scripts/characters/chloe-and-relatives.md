## Short Introduction（短文）

Chloe is Peppa and George's older cousin. Aunty Pig brings her to Peppa's house, so Chloe is a useful character for talking about family visits and relatives. Chloe is confident because she is a bit older than Peppa and George. She likes grown-up games and talks about proper rules, baby rules, and little children. At first, Peppa wants to act big like Chloe, but both girls still love jumping in muddy puddles in the end. Chloe helps children learn that being older can mean knowing more games, but it does not mean you must stop having fun.

Chloe 是 Peppa 和 George 年纪更大的 cousin。Aunty Pig 带她来 Peppa 家，所以 Chloe 很适合用来讲亲戚来访和家庭称呼。Chloe 因为比 Peppa 和 George 大一点，所以很自信。她喜欢“大孩子”的游戏，也会说正式规则、宝宝规则和小孩子。刚开始，Peppa 想像 Chloe 一样表现得很大，但最后两个女孩还是都喜欢跳泥坑。Chloe 可以帮助孩子理解：长大可以表示会更多游戏，但不表示不能继续开心玩。

## Relative Words for Parents

In English, “cousin” can mean 表姐、堂姐、表哥、堂哥、表妹、堂妹、表弟、堂弟.  
英语里的 “cousin” 可以覆盖中文里很多种“表/堂兄弟姐妹”。

| English | 中文 | Parent-Child Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cousin | 表/堂兄弟姐妹 | Chloe is Peppa's cousin. — Chloe 是 Peppa 的 cousin。 |
| older cousin | 年纪更大的表/堂兄姐 | Chloe is Peppa's older cousin. — Chloe 是 Peppa 年纪更大的 cousin。 |
| aunt / aunty | 阿姨、姑姑、伯母、婶婶等 | Aunty Pig brings Chloe to visit. — Aunty Pig 带 Chloe 来拜访。 |
| grandparents | 祖父母/外祖父母 | Granny Pig and Grandpa Pig are Peppa's grandparents. — 猪奶奶和猪爷爷是 Peppa 的 grandparents。 |
| family visit | 亲戚来访 | Chloe comes for a family visit. — Chloe 来做客。 |

Parent note: for a young child, you can keep “cousin” in English and add the Chinese relationship later.  
家长提示：对小朋友可以先记 “cousin”，中文里具体表/堂关系以后再细分。

## Catchphrases and Useful Lines

| English | 中文 | When to Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hello, Peppa! Hello, George! | 你好，Peppa！你好，George！ | 亲戚见面 |

## Funny Things

| Scene | Why It Is Funny | Parent Prompt |

| Peppa says Chloe is a big girl like her. | Peppa wants to feel grown-up too. | Does Peppa want to be big? — Peppa 想当大孩子吗？ |

## Mini Talk for 阿泽 and Celine

阿泽 can say: “Celine, Chloe is older, but she still likes muddy puddles. Being older does not mean you must stop having fun.”  
阿泽可以说：“Celine，Chloe 年纪更大，但她还是喜欢泥坑。长大不代表不能开心玩。”

Celine can answer: “Big girls can jump too!”  
Celine 可以回答：“大女孩也可以跳！”
