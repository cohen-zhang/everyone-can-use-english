# George Pig

**索引：** [[README|Peppa Pig S01 · Family Character Guides]]

George Pig is Peppa's little brother.  
George Pig 是 Peppa 的弟弟。

He is small, sweet, and very attached to Mr. Dinosaur.  
他年纪小、很可爱，而且非常离不开恐龙先生。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

George Pig is Peppa's little brother. He is small, gentle, and a little shy, but he loves joining Peppa's games. George's favorite toy is Mr. Dinosaur, and he likes making dinosaur sounds like “Grrrrr!” When Mr. Dinosaur is lost, George feels very sad, so Peppa and Daddy help him look for it. George also likes muddy puddles, chocolate cake, and pancakes with syrup. He does not always like vegetables, but Grandpa Pig can make salad fun by turning it into a dinosaur. George is funny because his short words and little actions show big feelings.

George Pig 是 Peppa 的弟弟。他年纪小、温和、有一点害羞，但他很喜欢加入 Peppa 的游戏。George 最爱的玩具是恐龙先生，他喜欢发出 “Grrrrr!” 这样的恐龙声音。恐龙先生不见时，George 很伤心，所以 Peppa 和爸爸帮他一起找。George 也喜欢泥坑、巧克力蛋糕和加糖浆的煎饼。他不总是喜欢蔬菜，但猪爷爷能把沙拉变成恐龙，让蔬菜变有趣。George 很好笑，因为他短短的话和小小的动作里有很大的情绪。

## Mini Talk for 阿泽 and Celine

阿泽 can say: “Celine, George is small. When he loses Mr. Dinosaur, he feels very sad.”  
阿泽可以说：“Celine，George 还小。恐龙先生不见了，他会很难过。”

Celine can answer: “Don't worry, George. We will find him.”  
Celine 可以回答：“别担心，George。我们会找到他的。”
