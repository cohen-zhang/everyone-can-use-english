# Granny Pig

**索引：** [[README|Peppa Pig S01 · Family Character Guides]]

Granny Pig is Peppa and George's grandmother.  
猪奶奶是 Peppa 和 George 的奶奶。

She is warm, welcoming, and good at making family time feel special.  
她温暖、好客，也很会让家庭时光变得特别。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Granny Pig is Peppa and George's granny. She welcomes the children warmly and often calls them “my little ones.” At her house, Peppa and George meet Polly Parrot, eat chocolate cake, and learn that parrots can copy people's words. Granny Pig is gentle at the table and caring at bedtime, but she can also be playful when she babysits. She likes gardening programs and can make a nice salad from Grandpa Pig's vegetables. Granny Pig makes family visits feel cozy because she gives the children food, surprises, kind words, and time together.

猪奶奶是 Peppa 和 George 的奶奶。她热情欢迎孩子们，常常叫他们 “my little ones”。在她家里，Peppa 和 George 见到了鹦鹉 Polly，吃了巧克力蛋糕，也知道了鹦鹉会模仿别人说话。猪奶奶在餐桌上温和有礼，睡前也很会照顾孩子，但当她帮忙看孩子时也会陪他们玩。她喜欢园艺节目，也会用猪爷爷种的蔬菜做美味沙拉。猪奶奶让亲戚拜访变得很温馨，因为她给孩子们食物、惊喜、温柔的话和陪伴。
