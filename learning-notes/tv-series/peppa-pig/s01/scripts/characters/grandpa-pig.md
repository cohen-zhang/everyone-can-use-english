# Grandpa Pig

**索引：** [[README|Peppa Pig S01 · Family Character Guides]]

Grandpa Pig is Peppa and George's grandfather.  
猪爷爷是 Peppa 和 George 的爷爷。

He loves gardens, boats, and acting like a cheerful captain.  
他喜欢花园、船，也喜欢像开心的船长一样发号施令。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Grandpa Pig is Peppa and George's grandpa. He is warm, energetic, and proud of the things he loves. In the garden, he grows tomatoes, lettuce, and cucumber, and he helps George try vegetables by making them look like a dinosaur. On his boat, Grandpa Pig becomes “Captain Grandpa,” and everyone can answer, “Aye-aye, Captain!” He also has a very best friend called Granddad Dog, but the two grown-ups sometimes argue and race like little children. Grandpa Pig is funny because he is confident and playful, but he also knows how to say sorry and be kind again.

猪爷爷是 Peppa 和 George 的爷爷。他温暖、有活力，也为自己喜欢的东西感到自豪。在花园里，他种番茄、生菜和黄瓜，还把蔬菜做得像恐龙，帮助 George 尝一尝。到了船上，猪爷爷就变成 “Captain Grandpa”，大家可以回答 “Aye-aye, Captain!” 他还有一个最好的朋友 Granddad Dog，但两个大人有时会像小孩子一样争论和比赛。猪爷爷很好笑，因为他自信又爱玩，但他也知道怎样道歉，重新变得友善。
