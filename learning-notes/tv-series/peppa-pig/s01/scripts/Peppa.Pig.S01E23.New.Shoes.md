### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig. This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa pig.

New Shoes!

### Scene · Peppa and George Have Been（新鞋子 · 段1/3）
Peppa and George have been playing in the garden.

Peppa, where are your shoes?

Oh, I've Lost them.

Well, I'm sure We can find them.

Maybe We should try the garden?

Yes!

Everyone is Looking for Peppa's shoes.

Mummy Pig is Looking in the fIowerbed.

Peppa's shoes are not there.

Oh!

Daddy Pig is Looking in the Wheeibarrow.

Peppa's shoes are not there.

Oh!

Peppa and George look in the fIowerpots.

Peppa's shoes are not there, either.

Oh! Oh!

We've Looked everywhere, but We can't find Peppa's shoes!

Peppa's shoes are Lost.

Now I haven't got any shoes to wear.

Poor Peppa. Your shoes Were getting a bit old...

We'll buy you a new pair!

Can my new shoes be red, Mummy?

Of course, they can.

George! I'm going to have new red shoes!

Mummy Pig and Peppa are at Miss Rabbit's shoe shop.

### Scene · Hello Miss Rabbit（新鞋子 · 段2/3）


Hello, Miss Rabbit.

Hello, Mummy Pig.

We Would Like to buy some new shoes for Peppa, please.

Red Shoes!

Oh I'm sure We can find you some Lovely new shoes, Peppa!

Red ones, please.

Of course, red ones.

Wow! New red shoes!

George and Daddy Pig are playing draughts.

Oh, Well done, George.

Daddy! Daddy!

Look at my new shoes!

They are red!

I say! They are red.

Mummy, do you Like my new shoes?

Yes, Peppa, they make you Look very smart.

George, do you Like my new shoes?

Everyone likes Peppa's new shoes.

I like my new shoes so much.

I don't want to ever take them off.

It is bath time.

Peppa wants to keep her new shoes on, even for her bath.

Peppa is in her pajamas.

She still has her new shoes on.

Peppa even wants to wear her new shoes in bed!

Peppa, are you sure you don't want to take your shoes off?

### Scene · I Don't Want to Ever Take（新鞋子 · 段3/3）


I don't want to ever take my new shoes off, Mummy.

Very Well. Goodnight, Peppa and George.

Goodnight, Mummy!

Goodnight, Daddy Goodnight, my Little piggies!

It has been raining all night and now the garden is very Wet.

Mummy Pig is Wearing her boots.

Daddy Pig is Wearing his boots.

George is Wearing his boots.

Peppa is still Wearing her new red shoes.

George is going to play in the Wet grass.

Oh, dear. Peppa loves playing in the Wet grass, but she doesn't want to get her new shoes Wet.

I can't play in the Wet grass, George.

Oh.

George loves jumping in muddy puddles.

Peppa loves jumping in puddles, but she doesn't want to get her new shoes muddy.

Oh.

Mummy and Daddy love jumping up and down in muddy puddles!

Everyone likes jumping in muddy puddles!

Where is Peppa going?

Peppa is putting on her boots!

Wheeeeee!

If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots!

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 找鞋子

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Where are your shoes?** | 你的鞋呢？ | **Where are your shoes?** | 出门前找不到鞋 |
| **Where is Peppa going?** | 佩奇去哪儿了？ | **Where are you going?** | 她突然跑去拿另一双鞋 |

### 新鞋要不要脱

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Can my new shoes be red?** | 我的新鞋可以是红色的吗？ | **Can my new shoes be red?** | 买鞋时选颜色 |
| **Do you like my new shoes?** | 你喜欢我的新鞋吗？ | **Do you like my new shoes?** | 穿上新鞋给家里人看 |
| **Are you sure you don't want to take your shoes off?** | 你确定不把鞋脱掉吗？ | **Are you sure?** | 洗澡、上床还不肯脱 |
| **If you jump in muddy puddles, you must wear your boots.** | 要是跳泥坑，就必须穿靴子。 | **If you jump in puddles, wear your boots.** | 雨天出门前说规则 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在完成时** | *We've looked everywhere, but we can't find Peppa's shoes.* | *We've looked everywhere.* |
| **be going to** | *I'm going to have new red shoes.* | *You are going to have new shoes.* |
| **一般过去时** | *Your shoes were getting a bit old.* | *Those shoes were getting old.* |
| **一般现在时** | *Peppa loves jumping in puddles.* | *You love jumping.* |
| **must** | *You must wear your boots.* | *You must wear your boots.* 原句就能用。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Take your shoes off.** — 把鞋脱掉。
- **Wear your boots.** — 穿上靴子。
