## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *They are going for a drive.* | *We are going for a drive.* |
| **will** | *It will take all day.* | *This will take all day.* |
| **将来完成时** | *When you come back, I will have fixed your car.* | *I will have finished by then.* |
| **现在完成时** | *We've only borrowed it for today.* | *We have only borrowed it.* |
| **be going to** | *I think it's going to rain.* | *It is going to rain.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Daddy Pig has forgotten which button closes the roof.* | *I have forgotten which one.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Let's go.** — 我们走吧。
- **Keep the change.** — 不用找零了。

## 比较 · Like … better

这一集真正用来比较的，是 *like ... better*：新车喜欢过，旧车更喜欢。旁边几句是在说喜欢哪一辆。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 形式 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **like ... better** 更喜欢 | *I liked the new car but I like our old car better.* | *I like our red bike better.* — 我更喜欢我们的红自行车。 |
| **like** 喜欢这辆 | *Peppa and her family like the new blue car.* | *We like the blue car for today.* — 我们今天喜欢这辆蓝车。 |
| **love** 很喜欢 | *I love our car.* | *I love riding in our old car.* — 我喜欢坐我们的旧车。 |
| **likes me** 它喜欢我 | *I don't think this car likes me.* | *I do not think this button likes me.* — 我觉得这个按钮不喜欢我。 |
| **likes us** 它也喜欢我们 | *I think our old car likes us, too.* | *I think this old chair likes us, too.* — 我觉得这把旧椅子也喜欢我们。 |
