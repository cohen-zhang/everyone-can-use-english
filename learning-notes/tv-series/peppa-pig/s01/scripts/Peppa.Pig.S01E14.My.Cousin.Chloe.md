**My cousin Chloe**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态**、**比较** 是这一集的跟读版。

N: Peppa and George are playing in the garden.

P: To me, George! You threw the ball too hard, George! So the rule say, I win! Now it’s my turn. I win again.

M: Peppa, George. Today your big cousin Chloe is coming to visit!

P: Yippee! Cousin Chloe! George, Chloe is a big girl like me. So don’t be sad if she finds you too little to play with.

D: I’m sure Chloe will play with both of you.

P: Chloe is Here!

N: Aunty Pig has brought Chloe to spend the day at Peppa’s house. Chloe is Peppa and George’s cousin. Chloe is a bit older than Peppa and George P: Hello, Chloe!

C: Hello, Peppa! Hello, George!

D: See you later.

C: do you want to play a game?

P: Yes! Let’s play “catch” N: Peppa loves playing “Catch” C: Do you still play “catch” ? That’s a game for little children.

P: oh.. we only play “catch” because George likes it.

C: Ok, let’s play it for George. Do you play with the proper rules or the baby rules?

P: Proper rules!

C: I’ll start, Peppa! You’re “it”. Catch me if you can!

N: Peppa is “it”, she has to chase Chloe and George.

C: can’t catch me!

P: George! I’m going to easily catch you! You’re so little! That’s not fair, you are helping George.

C: that’s because he’s little. Do you want me help you?

P: No! I don’t need help, I’m a big girl like you.

C: Come on then, Peppa! Try and catch us. Can’t catch us! Can’t catch us!

P: This is a silly game. Can we play something else?

C: ok, I know a really good game for big children. It’s called “ Sly Fox”!

P: “sly fox”, I want to play “sly fox”, what is it?

C: One person is the “sly fox”, and the others creep up on them.

P: Me! Me! I want to be the “sly fox”!

N: Peppa is the “sly fox”, while her back is turned, the others creep up on her. But if Peppa turns and sees someone move, they have to go back to the start!

P: George, I saw you move, back to the start!

C: Got you! I win!

P: You moved before I was ready!

C: The rule say I can move when I like. Don’t they, George?

N: Now is Chloe’s turn to be the “Sly fox.” Peppa and George must stay very still.

P: Whoaao..

C: Peppa, I saw you move! Back to the start.

P: It’s not fair, you looked too long!

C: the rule say, I can look as long as I want. Back to the start.

N: George is the winner.

P: Can we play a different game?

C: Yes. But as long as it’s a grown-up game.

P: I know. Let’s play my favorite game! It’s very grown-up.

C: George, do you know what Peppa’s favorite game is?

N: Peppa’s favorite game is jumping up and down in muddy puddles!

P: George, if you jump in puddles, you must wear your boots. I’ve brought some boots for you too, Chloe.

C: I’m too grown-up to jump in muddy puddles.

P: oh, so am I.

N: George loves jumping in muddy puddles. Secretly, Peppa would love to jump in the puddle. But she wants to look grown-up.

D: I hear there’s some puddle jumping going on!

N: Mummy pig and Dad pig love jumping in muddy puddles!

C: It does look fun.

P: Yes, it does.

C: Maybe there’s a rule that says, big girls can jump in muddy puddles.

P: Yes, that’s a good rule.

C: Race you!

P: Race you!

N: Peppa and Chloe love jumping up and down in muddy puddles. Everyone loves jumping up and down in muddy puddles, even grown-up girls.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 玩哪个

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Do you want to play a game?** | 你想玩游戏吗？ | **Do you want to play a game?** | 客人进门 |
| **Do you still play catch?** | 你们还玩抓人吗？ | **Do you still play that?** | 她觉得那个游戏太小 |
| **Do you play with the proper rules?** | 你们用正式规则玩吗？ | **Proper rules, or baby rules?** | 先讲好规则 |
| **Can we play something else?** | 我们能玩点别的吗？ | **Can we play something else?** | 她玩腻了 |

### 什么叫狡猾的狐狸

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *Your big cousin Chloe is coming to visit.* | *Your cousin is coming.* |
| **will** | *I'm sure Chloe will play with both of you.* | *She will play with both of you.* |
| **have to** | *She has to chase Chloe and George.* | *You have to chase them.* |
| **would** | *Secretly, Peppa would love to jump in the puddle.* | *I would love to play.* |
| **as long as** | *As long as it's a grown-up game.* | *As long as we take turns.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Back to the start.** — 回到起点。
- **Stay still.** — 别动。
- **Catch me if you can.** — 来抓我啊。

## 比较 · Older than and a bit

这一集在比谁大。**a bit older than** 只说了一次，后面用 **too little**、**big** 和 **too grown-up** 接着说。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 形式 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a bit older than** 大一点点 | *Chloe is a bit older than Peppa and George.* | *She is a bit older than you.* — 她比你大一点点。 |
| **too little** 太小 | *Don't be sad if she finds you too little to play with.* | *You are not too little to play.* — 你没有小到不能一起玩。 |
| **so little** 这么小 | *You're so little!* | *This coat is so little.* — 这件外套太小了。 |
| **big** 大孩子 | *I'm a big girl like you.* | *I am a big kid like you.* — 我和你一样是大孩子。 |
| **too grown-up** 大得不能 | *I'm too grown-up to jump in muddy puddles.* | *I am not too grown-up to jump.* — 我没有大到不能跳。 |
