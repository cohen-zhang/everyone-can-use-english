## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 可以进来吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Who is it?** | 是谁？ | **Who is at the door?** — 门口是谁？ | 她在毯子堡垒里 |
| **Can he come in?** | 他可以进来吗？ | **Can I come in now?** — 我现在可以进来吗？ | 她不让人进 |
| **Have you room for any more visitors?** | 还容得下客人吗？ | **Is there room for me, too?** — 还有地方给我吗？ | 堡垒看起来很小 |
| **What are the secret words?** | 暗号是什么？ | **What do I have to say?** — 我得说什么？ | 她要一句口令 |

### 还要不要出来

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Would you like to go in the tree house, too?** | 你也想进树屋吗？ | **Would you like to come in, too?** — 你也想进来吗？ | 还有人在外面 |
| **Can I have different secret words, please?** | 我可以换一句暗号吗？ | **Can we use a new secret word?** — 我们可以换一个新暗号吗？ | 她不想说那一句 |
| **Are you sure you don't want to visit me?** | 你确定不想来看我吗？ | **Are you sure you don't want a cookie?** — 你确定不要饼干吗？ | 她说再也不出来 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **must** | *Before you come in the house, you must take off your muddy boots.* | *You must take your shoes off at the door.* — 你必须在门口脱鞋。 |
| **would like** | *Would you like to go in the tree house, too?* | *Would you like to come into the fort?* — 你想进堡垒吗？ |
| **can** | *You can come in now.* | *You may step inside.* — 你可以走进来了。 |
| **have to** | *You have to say the secret words.* | *You have to ask before you come in.* — 进来之前你得先问一声。 |
| **as long as** | *As long as he takes off his muddy boots.* | *You can come in, as long as your hands are clean.* — 你可以进来，只要手是干净的。 |
| **if** | *If you want to come in, you have to say the secret words.* | *If you want a cookie, you have to ask.* — 你要是想吃饼干，就得先问。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **In you go.** — 进去吧。
- **Take your boots off.** — 把靴子脱了。
- **Say the secret words.** — 说暗号。

## 特色 · Can he come in / have to ask

这一集进树屋要先问是谁，再说暗号，才可以进来。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who is it** 是谁 | *Who is it?* | *Who is knocking?* — 谁在敲门？ |
| **Can he come in** 可以进来吗 | *Can he come in?* | *Can I come in and sit with you?* — 我可以进来，坐在你旁边吗？ |
| **room** 还有地方吗 | *Have you room for any more visitors?* | *Is there room for one more?* — 还容得下一个人吗？ |
| **have to** 必须先说 | *You have to say the secret words. Then you can come into our house.* | *You have to ask, and then you can come in.* — 你得先问，然后才能进来。 |
| **as long as** 只要 | *As long as he takes off his muddy boots.* | *You can come in, as long as you take your shoes off.* — 你可以进来，只要先脱鞋。 |
| **must** 必须脱 | *Before you come in the house, you must take off your muddy boots.* | *You must wipe your feet first.* — 你必须先蹭蹭脚。 |
| **if** 想进来就得 | *If you want to come in, you have to say the secret words.* | *If you want to come in, you have to knock.* — 你要是想进来，就得先敲门。 |

## 亲子讲解 · come in、hurray

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### come in

*Would you like to come in?* — 你想进来吗？

*Do you understand this sentence?* — 这句话你听懂了吗？

*Do you know what "come in" means?* — 你知道 come in 是什么意思吗？

*Come in means step into the room or the house.* — come in 是走进来。

*We say come in when someone is at the door.* — 人在门口时说 come in。

*Notice that we say come in, not come inside only.* — 口语里常说 come in。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### hurray

*Do you know the word hurray?* — 你知道 hurray 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: hurray.* — 跟我说：hurray。

*Hurray means you are glad.* — hurray 是高兴时喊的好哇。

*Notice that we say huh-RAY, not HUH-ray.* — 重音在后面，读 huh-RAY。

*We say hurray when the game can start.* — 游戏可以开始时喊这一声。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
