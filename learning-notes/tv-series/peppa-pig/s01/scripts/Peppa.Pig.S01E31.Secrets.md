**S1-31 secrets**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态**、**must not / won't** 是这一集的跟读版。

### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa pig.

Secrets.

### Scene · Mummy Pig Has Made（秘密 · 段1/3）
Mummy Pig has made a special box for Peppa.

Peppa, this box is just for you!

Thank you, Mummy!

It's a secret box for you to keep secret things in.

What things can I put in it, Mummy?

Only you can decide that, Peppa!

It's your secret box!

I know What I Can put in it!

Good.

But don't tell me.

I won't tell you or George or Daddy!

It's a secret!!

George, this is my secret box!

It's empty. I have to find some things to put inside.

George wants to help Peppa.

No, George, only I must know What's in the box.

You have to Wait outside While I choose.

Peppa is going to choose some secret things to put in the box.

Now, my secret box is full!

George, you must not Look inside!

George Would Like to know What is inside Peppa's secret box.

If you Like, you can try to guess.

Dine-Saw!

George thinks Peppa has put a dinosaur in the box!

Oh, George!

You always say ''dinosaur'' for everything!

### Scene · Anyway a Dinosaur（秘密 · 段2/3）


anyway, a dinosaur is too big to fit in the box.

George cannot guess What is in Peppa's secret box.

Daddy Pig is cleaning a picture.

Daddy! This is my secret box!

Mummy made it for me!

It's very nice, Peppa!

What's inside?

It's a secret!

George tried to guess, but he didn't get it right.

Can I have a try?

Yes.

Hmmm. Have you put my glasses inside?

No!!! Your glasses are on your head!

So they are!

Silly Daddy! You must have a proper guess.

Ok. Have you put Mummy's shoes in the box?

Nope! And that's all your guesses. Used up!

Peppa likes secrets!

Mummy! No one knows What is in my box!

Oh. What has George got?

I made a box for George, too!

And he's filled it with secret things!

And only George knows What's inside.

Oh.

Peppa Would Like to know What is inside George's box.

It's George's toy dinosaur, of course!

No!

George's toy dinosaur is not in George's box.

It's too difficult!

### Scene · I'Ll Never Guess（秘密 · 段3/3）


I'll never guess!

Peppa does not Like secrets as much as she used to.

Maybe, if you showed George one thing from your box, then he could show you one thing from his.

Ok. But We must do it at the same time.

That's a good idea.

On the count of three, you each show What's in your boxes.

Ready?

Yes.

One... two... three!

George has a drum and Peppa has a trumpet.

Lovely!

Yes, it sounds very nice.

Do you have anymore secret things?

George has a custard doughnut!

Oh.

But Peppa's box is empty.

I've got nothing else!

Luckily, I have!

Come on. Tuck in.

George, Peppa, Mummy Pig and Daddy Pig all Like custard doughnuts!

I don't have a special box.

But I know a very good place to hide my custard doughnut!

Can you guess where?

Um. Are you going to hide it in the fridge?

No! somewhere much more special!

My tummy!

Mmmmmm! Delicious!

I'm going to hide mine in my tummy!

Me, too!


### Closing · Outro（片尾）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在完成时** | *Mummy Pig has made a special box.* | *I have made you a box.* |
| **won't** | *I won't tell you.* | *I won't tell.* |
| **have to** | *I have to find some things to put inside.* | *I have to find something.* |
| **must** | *Only I must know what's in the box.* | *Only you must know.* |
| **used to** | *Peppa does not like secrets as much as she used to.* | *You don't like it as much as you used to.* |
| **be going to** | *I'm going to hide mine in my tummy.* | *I am going to eat mine.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Don't tell me.** — 别告诉我。
- **Wait outside.** — 在外面等。
- **Tuck in.** — 开动吃。

## 特色 · Must not and won't tell

这一集守秘密，Peppa 说 **must not**（不许看）和 **won't tell**（不会告诉你）。旁边几句 **must** 也是「必须」。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **won't tell** 不会告诉 | *I won't tell you or George or Daddy.* | *I won't tell anyone your secret.* — 你的秘密我谁都不告诉。 |
| **must not** 不许 | *You must not look inside.* | *You must not peek in there.* — 你不许往里面偷看。 |
| **must** 必须。只有我可以知道 | *Only I must know what's in the box.* | *Only you must know the hiding place.* — 只有你可以知道藏的地方。 |
| **must** 必须好好猜 | *You must have a proper guess.* | *You must guess for real.* — 你必须认真猜。 |
| **must** 必须同时做 | *We must do it at the same time.* | *We must start together.* — 我们必须一起开始。 |
