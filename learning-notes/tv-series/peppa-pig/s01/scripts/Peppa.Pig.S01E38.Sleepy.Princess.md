## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 要不要听故事

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Can I have a story?** | 可以给我讲个故事吗？ | **Do you want a story tonight?** — 今晚要不要听故事？ | 灯关了，她还不睡 |
| **Do you want a story?** | 你想听故事吗？ | **Do you want one short story?** — 你想听一个短故事吗？ | 先问她要不要 |
| **Is it a good story?** | 这个故事好听吗？ | **Is this a good story?** — 这个故事好听吗？ | 她怀疑太无聊 |
| **Is there a little princess in the story?** | 故事里有小公主吗？ | **Is there a little cat in the story?** — 故事里有小猫吗？ | 她要先知道角色 |

### 故事里怎么样

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Why was she called that?** | 她为什么叫这个名字？ | **Why was the cat called that?** — 那只猫为什么叫这个名字？ | 她打断故事 |
| **Was the Sleepy Princess pretty?** | 贪睡公主漂亮吗？ | **Was the little cat pretty?** — 那只小猫好看吗？ | 她追问长什么样 |
| **Are you sleepy?** | 你困了吗？ | **Are you sleepy yet?** — 你现在困了吗？ | 故事讲到一半 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *It is night time.* | *It is bedtime.* — 该睡觉了。 |
| **be going to** | *Peppa and George are going to bed.* | *You are going to bed now.* — 你现在要去睡觉了。 |
| **will** | *I will tell you just one story.* | *I will tell you one short story.* — 我给你讲一个短故事。 |
| **must** | *You must both promise to go to sleep.* | *You must promise to close your eyes.* — 你必须答应把眼睛闭上。 |
| **if** | *If you keep talking, Daddy Pig can't start the story.* | *If you keep talking, I can't start.* — 你要是一直说，我就开不了头。 |
| **一般过去时** | *She was called the Sleepy Princess.* | *Our bear was called the sleepy bear.* — 我们的小熊叫贪睡熊。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Say "yes".** — 说 yes。
- **Goodnight.** — 晚安。
- **Wake up.** — 醒醒。
- **Let's carry you to bed.** — 我们抱你去床上。

## 特色 · Once upon a time

这一集 Daddy 讲童话。开头是 **Once upon a time**，故事里的人用 **was / were**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Once upon a time** 从前 | *Once upon a time, in a castle, there lived a little princess.* | *Once upon a time, there was a little cat.* — 从前，有一只小猫。 |
| **was called** 名叫 | *And she was called the Sleepy Princess.* | *She was called the sleepy cat.* — 她叫贪睡小猫。 |
| **was** 她很漂亮 | *Yes, she was very pretty.* | *The cat was very small.* — 那只猫很小。 |
| **were** 他们都在 | *They were all living there as well.* | *They were all in the little house.* — 他们都在那间小屋里。 |
| **wasn't / was** 不是龙 | *It wasn't a dragon. It was a dinosaur.* | *It was not a tiger. It was a cat.* — 那不是老虎，是一只猫。 |
| **was so** 困得睡着了 | *The Sleepy Princess was so sleepy, she had fallen fast asleep.* | *You were so sleepy, you fell asleep.* — 你太困了，已经睡着了。 |
