## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *It is night time.* | *It is bedtime.* — 该睡觉了。 |
| **be going to** | *Peppa and George are going to bed.* | *You are going to bed now.* — 你现在要去睡觉了。 |
| **will** | *I will tell you just one story.* | *I will tell you one short story.* — 我给你讲一个短故事。 |
| **must** | *You must both promise to go to sleep.* | *You must promise to close your eyes.* — 你必须答应把眼睛闭上。 |
| **if** | *If you keep talking, Daddy Pig can't start the story.* | *If you keep talking, I can't start.* — 你要是一直说，我就开不了头。 |
| **一般过去时** | *She was called the Sleepy Princess.* | *Our bear was called the sleepy bear.* — 我们的小熊叫贪睡熊。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Say "yes".** — 说 yes。
- **Wake up.** — 醒醒。
- **Let's carry you to bed.** — 我们抱你去床上。

## 口语 · A promise is a promise

Daddy 让两人先答应：*You must both promise to go to sleep.* Peppa 和 George 回答 *We promise!* 想把「答应了就算数」说得更硬，用重复句型 **A promise is a promise.**（一诺千金 / 说到做到）。这句本集没有直接说，是从 promise 延伸的常用口头禅，收在 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/spoken-catchphrases-reduplicatives-adverbs|口头禅 / 叠词 / 口语副词 · X is X 句型]]；同一类还有 *Rules are rules.*、*Enough is enough.*

| 句型 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **promise** 答应 | *We promise!* | *I promise!* — 我答应！ |
| **must promise** 必须答应 | *You must both promise to go to sleep.* | *You must promise to brush your teeth.* — 你得答应刷牙。 |
| **A promise is a promise** 一诺千金 | 本集没说 | *A promise is a promise.* — 一诺千金，说到做到。 |

*promise* 做名词是「承诺」，做动词是「答应」。*We promise!* 里是动词。

## 用法 · anyway 与 sun set（日出日落）

### anyway

台词 *Anyway, there was also a dragon!* 里 **anyway** 放在句首，意思是「总之、反正」。Daddy 讲故事被 Peppa 打断，用 anyway 把话拉回故事主线。口语里也常听到 *Anyways*，但规范写法是 **anyway**。

| 用法 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **anyway** 句首 · 拉回正题 | *Anyway, there was also a dragon!* | *Anyway, let's finish the story.* — 总之，我们把故事讲完吧。 |
| **anyway** 句尾 · 结束话题 | 本集没说 | *Thanks anyway.* — 还是谢谢你。 |

家长讲解套式：

*Do you know the word anyway?* — 你知道 anyway 吗？

*Anyway means we go back to the main story.* — anyway 是把话拉回正题。

*Notice that we say anyway at the start of a sentence.* — 注意 anyway 常放在句首。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### sun set / sunrise（日升日落）

台词 *You see, the sun set.* 是「太阳下山了」。这里 *the sun* 是主语，*set* 是动词（落下），set 的过去式还是 **set**。写成名词时合为一个词 **sunset**（日落）；对说的是 **sunrise**（日出）。

| 说法 | English | 简中 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 本集原句 | *You see, the sun set.* | 你看，太阳下山了。 |
| 名词 · 日落 | *The sunset is beautiful.* | 晚霞很美。 |
| 名词 · 日出 | *We watched the sunrise.* | 我们看了日出。 |
| 口语 · 升 / 落 | *The sun comes up. The sun goes down.* | 太阳升起来。太阳落下去。 |

接着台词 *Then the stars and moon came out.* 星星和月亮出来了，正是日落后。

## 词义 · Princess 与 Queen（艾莎公主 / 冰雪女王）

本集 Daddy 把家人讲成 *the small Prince, Queen Mummy and King Daddy*，正好是一组 **prince / princess / king / queen**。**princess** /ˈprɪnsɛs/ 是「公主」（国王的女儿），**queen** /kwin/ 是「女王 / 王后」。两个词不是一回事：princess 是女儿这一辈，queen 是掌权的君主或国王的妻子。

《冰雪奇缘》（Frozen）正好把这两个词讲清楚：**Elsa** 一开始是 **Princess Elsa**（公主），加冕后成为 **Queen Elsa**（女王），也就是常说的 **the Snow Queen**（冰雪女王）；妹妹 **Anna** 一直是 **Princess Anna**。所以同一家人里，姐姐是 queen，妹妹是 princess。

| 词 | IPA (GA) | 简中 | Frozen 里是谁 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **princess** | /ˈprɪnsɛs/ | 公主 | Anna、加冕前的 Elsa | *Anna is a princess.* — 安娜是公主。 |
| **prince** | /prɪns/ | 王子 | 本集 the small Prince | *The little prince is sleepy.* — 小王子困了。 |
| **queen** | /kwin/ | 女王 / 王后 | 加冕后的 Elsa、Queen Mummy | *Elsa becomes the queen.* — 艾莎成为女王。 |
| **king** | /kɪŋ/ | 国王 | King Daddy | *The king tells a story.* — 国王讲故事。 |

- 构词：prince 加 **-ess** 变 princess；king 没有这种形式，对应词直接是 **queen**。
- *Queen Elsa*、*Princess Anna* 这类说法，头衔放在名字前面、首字母大写。
- 口语里 *the Snow Queen* 就指冰雪女王（Elsa）；主题曲见 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/let-it-go-idina-menzel|Let It Go · 冰雪奇缘]]。

*Elsa is a princess. Then she becomes a queen.* — 艾莎是公主，后来成了女王。
*Notice that we say Princess Anna, but Queen Elsa.* — 注意说 Princess Anna，而 Queen Elsa。
*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

## 特色 · Once upon a time

这一集 Daddy 讲童话。开头是 **Once upon a time**，故事里的人用 **was / were**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Once upon a time** 从前 | *Once upon a time, in a castle, there lived a little princess.* | *Once upon a time, there was a little cat.* — 从前，有一只小猫。 |
| **was called** 名叫 | *And she was called the Sleepy Princess.* | *She was called the sleepy cat.* — 她叫贪睡小猫。 |
| **was** 她很漂亮 | *Yes, she was very pretty.* | *The cat was very small.* — 那只猫很小。 |
| **were** 他们都在 | *They were all living there as well.* | *They were all in the little house.* — 他们都在那间小屋里。 |
| **wasn't / was** 不是龙 | *It wasn't a dragon. It was a dinosaur.* | *It was not a tiger. It was a cat.* — 那不是老虎，是一只猫。 |
| **was so** 困得睡着了 | *The Sleepy Princess was so sleepy, she had fallen fast asleep.* | *You were so sleepy, you fell asleep.* — 你太困了，已经睡着了。 |

## 亲子讲解 · sleepy、once upon a time

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### sleepy

*Do you know the word sleepy?* — 你知道 sleepy 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: sleepy.* — 跟我说：sleepy。

*Sleepy means you want to sleep.* — sleepy 是困了，想睡。

*There's a difference between sleepy and asleep.* — sleepy 和 asleep 不一样。

*Asleep means you are already sleeping.* — asleep 是已经睡着。

*For example, the princess is sleepy.* — 比方说，公主困了。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### once upon a time

*Once upon a time, there was a princess.* — 从前有一位公主。

*Do you understand this sentence?* — 这句话你听懂了吗？

*Do you know what "once upon a time" means?* — 你知道 once upon a time 是什么意思吗？

*Once upon a time is how a fairy story starts.* — once upon a time 是童话的开头。

*We use that expression only at the start.* — 只用在故事最前面。

*Then the story uses was and were.* — 后面的故事用 was 和 were。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
