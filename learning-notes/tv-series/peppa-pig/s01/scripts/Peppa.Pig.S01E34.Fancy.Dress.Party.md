**Fancy Dress Party**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态**、**What are you / I'm a** 是这一集的跟读版。

**Mummy Pig -MP**

**Daddy Pig -DP**

**Peppa Pig -P**

**George Pig -G**

**Suzy Sheep -S**

**Candy Cat -C**

**Danny Dog -D**

**Rebecca Rabbit -R**

**Pedro Pony -Pony**

Peppa and George are having a fancy dress party. All their friends are invited. Peppa is dressed as a fairy princess, and George is dressed as a dinosaur.

G: Err. Dinosaur.

Here are peppa's friends. Candy cat, Suzy sheep, Danny dog, Rebecca rabbit and Pedro pony. Suzy is wearing her nurses costume.

DP: Hello Suzy.

S: Hello Mr. Pig.

DP: I'm glad the nurse is arrived. Have you just come from the hospital?

S: En, I'm not a real nurse. I just pretend.

DP: Ha, very good. My, my, what have we here?

D: Wow, I'm a pirate. Shiver me timbers.

C: Mew, I'm a witch. I can turn you into a frog.

P: Eh. I'm a clown.

DP: That's funny. What are you, Rebecca rabbit?

R: I'm a carrot.

DP: Fantastic. Come in, there are a fairy princess and a dinosaur waiting to meet you.

P: Hello, everyone.

All: Hello, peppa..

P: Hello, Suzy. I'm princess peppa. You must Bow when you speak to me.

S: Hello, your Majesty. I'm nurse Suzy. Open wide and say aha.

P: Aha.

Pony: I'm a clown.

R: Do something funny.

D: Rebecca, why are you dressed as a carrot?

R: I like carrots.

G: Dinosaur.

D: Ha. A scary dinosaur.

Peppa is reminding herself in a mirror.

P: Mirror, mirror on the wall, who is the fairest of them all? You are, peppa.

C: Hello, Peppa.

P: Hello. I am a little fairy princess.

C: I'm a witch. And I've got a magic wand.

P: I've got a magic wand too.

C: I can turn you into a frog.

P: And I'll turn you into a frog.

G: Dinosaur. (Crying…)

Oh, dear, George is scared by his own reflection.

MP: Silly George, look, it's you in the mirror.

MP: Children, it's time to decide who has the best fancy dress costume.

DP: We need a judge.

All: Me, me.

DP: As it is peppa's party, maybe she should be the judge.

P: I'm the judge. I'm the judge. Daddy, what is a judge?

DP: The judge decides who has the best costume.

P: Oh goodie.

Peppa is going to choose who has the best costume.

P: Suzy, can I see your costume please?

S: I'm nurse Suzy. I make people better.

P: Very good, Suzy. Now, Danny?

C: I am a pirate. Shiver me timbers.

P: Now, Candy?

C: I'm a witch. I can turn you into a frog.

P: I am a fairy princess. And I can turn you into a frog.

MP: Children, children.

P: Sorry mummy.

MP: Who is next?

Pony: I am a clown.

P: That's funny. And, Rebecca?

R: I'm a carrot.

P: Lovely, and my little brother George is a scary dinosaur. Everyone's costume is very good.

All: Hurray.

MP: Now you say who the winner is.

P: Oh yes, and the winner is ME.

MP: Peppa, you can’t pick yourself. You are the judge.

P: Oh, can't I?

MP: You have to pick another winner.

P: OK. The carrot wins.

R: Thank you.

Peppa loves fancy dress parties. Everyone loves fancy dress parties.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 你扮的是谁

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What have we here?** | 这是什么呀？ | **What is this outfit?** — 这一身是什么？ | 她从门后走出来 |
| **What are you?** | 你扮的是什么？ | **What are you today?** — 你今天扮的是什么？ | 她穿上了衣服或披风 |
| **Why are you dressed as a carrot?** | 你为什么打扮成胡萝卜？ | **Why are you dressed as a cat?** — 你为什么打扮成猫？ | 她的打扮很好认 |
| **What is a judge?** | 裁判是干什么的？ | **What does a judge do?** — 裁判要做什么？ | 她问一个新词 |

### 谁最好

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Can I see your costume, please?** | 我可以看看你的衣服吗？ | **Can I see your hat, please?** — 我可以看看你的帽子吗？ | 她想展示 |
| **Who is next?** | 下一个是谁？ | **Who wants a turn?** — 谁想下一个来？ | 轮着看 |
| **Who is the fairest of them all?** | 谁是最好看的？ | **Who looks the best today?** — 今天谁最好看？ | 她对着镜子玩 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *Suzy is wearing her nurse's costume.* | *You are wearing your red cape.* — 你穿着红披风。 |
| **现在完成时** | *Have you just come from the hospital?* | *Have you just come from the kitchen?* — 你刚从厨房过来吗？ |
| **be going to** | *Peppa is going to choose who has the best costume.* | *You are going to choose the best hat.* — 你来选最好看的帽子。 |
| **have to** | *You have to pick another winner.* | *You have to pick someone else.* — 你得另选一个人。 |
| **can't** | *You can't pick yourself.* | *You can't pick your own picture.* — 你不能选自己的那张。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Come in.** — 进来。
- **Open wide and say "aha".** — 张大嘴，说 aha。
- **Do something funny.** — 做个好笑的动作。

## 特色 · What are you / I'm a

这一集每个人报自己扮的是谁。问句是 **What are you?**，回答是 **I'm a …**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What are you** 你是什么 | *What are you, Rebecca?* | *What are you today?* — 你今天是什么？ |
| **I'm a** 我是海盗 | *I'm a pirate.* | *I'm a cook today.* — 我今天是厨师。 |
| **I'm a** 我是女巫 | *I'm a witch.* | *I'm a cat.* — 我是一只猫。 |
| **I'm a** 我是胡萝卜 | *I'm a carrot.* | *I'm a banana.* — 我是一根香蕉。 |
| **I'm not** 我不是真的 | *I'm not a real nurse. I just pretend.* | *I'm not a real doctor. I am just pretending.* — 我不是真医生，我在假装。 |
| **I am a** 我是小公主 | *I am a little fairy princess.* | *I am a little star.* — 我是一颗小星星。 |
| **I'm the** 我是裁判 | *I'm the judge.* | *You are the judge today.* — 今天你来当裁判。 |
