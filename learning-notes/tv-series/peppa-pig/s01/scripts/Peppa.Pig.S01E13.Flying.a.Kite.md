## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 风筝为什么不飞

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The kite won't fly if there isn't any wind.** | 没有风，风筝不会飞。 | **Is there any wind?** | 她跑了风筝还是不起来 |
| **You might get the kite stuck in one.** | 你可能会把风筝挂在树上。 | **Might it get stuck?** | 靠近树的时候 |
| **Can we jump in the puddle?** | 我们可以跳进水坑吗？ | **Can we jump in it?** | 她看见泥坑 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be going to** | *They are going to fly a kite.* | *We are going to fly a kite.* |
| **won't** | *The kite won't fly.* | *It won't fly.* |
| **一般过去时** | *You didn't run fast enough.* | *You didn't run fast enough.* |
| **have to** | *We'll just have to wait.* | *We will have to wait.* |
| **might** | *You might get the kite stuck.* | *It might get stuck.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Daddy Pig has got the kite caught in a tree.* | *It is caught in the tree.* |
| **将来进行时** | *Daddy Pig is going to be doing the washing.* | *You are going to be doing the washing.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Watch out for the trees.** — 小心那些树。
- **Stand back.** — 往后站。
- **Please be careful.** — 请小心。
