**Treasure Hunt**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态**、**look for**、**亲子讲解** 是这一集的跟读版。
**相关：** 讲解套式见 [[learning-notes/tv-series/a-day-in-the-life-of-jeff/episode-notes/jeff-e01-getting-up-notes|Jeff E01 · 讲解用语]]、[[learning-notes/tv-series/a-day-in-the-life-of-jeff/episode-notes/jeff-e02-cleaning-up-notes|Jeff E02 · 讲解用语]]。

**Daddy -----D**

**Mummy -----M**

**Peppa ------ P**

**Georg ------G**

**Grandpa ---- Gp**

**Grandma-----Gm**

Granny Pig and Grandpa Pig are making a treasure hunt for Peppa and George. Granny Pig is drawing the treasure map. Grandpa Pig is burying the treasure in a secret place in the garden.

Gm: Grandpa Pig, have you finished? Peppa and George will be here soon.

Gp: No, no need to panic.

Peppa and her family are here.

Gm: Quick, Grandpa Pig! They are here.

Gp: Oh, almost done.

Grandpa Pig just finished in time.

P: Granny Pig, we’re here.

G: Granny Pig.

Gm: Hello, my little ones.

P: Grandpa Pig.

G: Grandpa Pig.

Gp: Ohayo there, mihateeze!

Gm: Peppa, George, we’ve made you a treasure hunt.

Gp: Somewhere in the garden is the buried treasure.

P: Wow, treasure! Where is it?

Gp:Oh, you have to look for it.

Gm: Here is the treasure map for Peppa.

Gp: And George could wear my pirate hat.

Gp: Ohayo there, captain George!

P: The map is a bit difficult. Daddy, can you help?

D: Of course, Peppa. I’m very good at maps. Oh, it is a bit difficult.

Gm: Oh, Daddy Pig, you’re holding the map upside down.

D: Yes, I thought of that much.

P: It’s easy. The red cross shows where the treasure is.

D: They were in the garden of the two apple trees.

P: Oh, I don’t know.

Gm: Would you like a clue?

P: Yes, please.

Gm: The first clue is in a bottle. But where is the bottle?

P: I can see it!

Peppa has found the first clue ----- a message in the bottle.

P: Look, everyone! Here’s the bottle.

GP: Well done! Peppa. Let’s see what the message says. Oh, it’s a message from a pirate.

P: Mummy, can you read the pirate’s message?

M: En, this pirate has very bad handwriting.

GP: This pirate’s handwriting is excellent.

M: No, I can’t make it out at all.

GP: The pirate has clearly written: Follow the arrows.

P: Follow the arrows?

George has found a second clue: sticks in the shape of an arrow.

P: Look! They point to this way!

Peppa and George are following the arrows.

P: Look, George! A key!

Peppa has found the next clue: a key.

Gm: Well done, Peppa! Now you just have to find the treasure chest that has its locks.

P: But there aren’t any more clues.

Gm: Maybe you should take another look at the map. Look, Peppa. The map has two apple trees on it.

P: Here’s an apple tree. And here’s an apple tree. So the treasure must be here.

D: Let’s take a look.

D: Oh, dear! There doesn’t seem to be anything here. Hang on. There is something here.

P: Wow, treasure!

All of them: Hurray!

Gm&Gp: Well done! Well done!

P: I’ve got the key to open it.

D: Wow, I can’t believe it! Gold coins! They must be worth a fortune.

Gp: It’s better than that. They are not gold coins. They are chocolate coins.

Gm: And there’s a chocolate coin for everybody.

All of them: Huray!

Peppa and George love chocolate coins. Everyone loves chocolate coins.

P: Granny, Grandp, this is the best treasure hunt ever.

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *Granny Pig is drawing the treasure map.* | *I am drawing the map.* |
| **will** | *Peppa and George will be here soon.* | *They will be here soon.* |
| **have to** | *You have to look for it.* | *You have to look.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Peppa has found the first clue.* | *You have found the first clue.* |
| **must** | *The treasure must be here.* | *It must be here.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Follow the arrows.** — 跟着箭头走。
- **Take another look at the map.** — 再看看地图。

## 特色 · Look for

这一集在寻宝。要 **look for**，线索用完了就说 **any more**。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **somewhere** 某个地方 | *Somewhere in the garden is the buried treasure.* | *The crayon is somewhere in the bedroom.* — 蜡笔在卧室的某个地方。 |
| **look for** 去找 | *You have to look for it.* | *We have to look for the missing sock.* — 我们得去找那只不见的袜子。 |
| **where** 在哪儿 | *The red cross shows where the treasure is.* | *The picture shows where the toy is.* — 这张图标明玩具在哪儿。 |
| **first clue** 第一条线索 | *The first clue is in a bottle.* | *The first clue is in the kitchen.* — 第一条线索在厨房。 |
| **any more** 没有更多 | *But there aren't any more clues.* | *We do not need any more clues.* — 我们不需要更多线索了。 |
| **anything** 什么都没有 | *There doesn't seem to be anything here.* | *I cannot see anything in the drawer.* — 抽屉里我什么都看不见。 |

## 亲子讲解 · chest、clue、upside down

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### chest

*Do you know the word chest?* — 你知道 chest 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: chest.* — 跟我说：chest。

*Here, a chest is a box for treasure.* — 这里的 chest 是宝箱，不是胸口。

*There's a difference between a treasure chest and your chest.* — 宝箱和胸口不是一个 chest。

*For example, the chest needs a key.* — 比方说，这个箱子要钥匙。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### clue

*Do you know the word clue?* — 你知道 clue 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: clue.* — 跟我说：clue。

*A clue is a hint that helps you find the next place.* — clue 是帮你找下一处的线索。

*We say one clue is enough.* — 我们说 one clue is enough。

*For example, the note is a clue.* — 比方说，纸条就是一条线索。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### upside down

*The picture is upside down.* — 这张画上下颠倒了。

*Do you understand this sentence?* — 这句话你听懂了吗？

*Do you know what "upside down" means?* — 你知道 upside down 是什么意思吗？

*Upside down means the top is at the bottom.* — upside down 是上下颠倒。

*We say hold the map upside down.* — 我们说 hold the map upside down。

*Then you cannot read it the right way.* — 那样就没法按正确方向看。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
