## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 可以帮忙吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Are you sure?** | 你确定吗？ | **Are you sure?** | 上次翻饼翻砸了 |
| **Can we help make the pancakes?** | 我们可以帮忙做饼吗？ | **Can I help?** | 她想搅面糊 |
| **Can I stir?** | 我可以搅吗？ | **Can I stir?** | 碗在妈妈手里 |
| **Is everyone watching?** | 大家都在看吗？ | **Are you watching?** | 有人要表演翻饼 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *Today is a day for pancakes.* | *Today we make pancakes.* |
| **一般过去时** | *Last time you dropped the pancake.* | *Last time you dropped it.* |
| **be going to** | *Mummy Pig is going to flip the pancake.* | *I am going to flip it.* |
| **will** | *You will get a chance.* | *You will get a turn.* |
| **should** | *It should be a simple matter to get it down.* | *It should be simple.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Leave that to me.** — 交给我。
- **Sit at the table.** — 坐到桌边。
- **On the count of three, jump.** — 数到三就跳。
