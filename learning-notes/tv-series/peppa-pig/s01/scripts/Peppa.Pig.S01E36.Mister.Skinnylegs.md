## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *George is putting the dolls to bed.* | *You are putting your bear to bed.* — 你在让小熊睡觉。 |
| **must** | *You must wash your hands.* | *You must wash your hands before cake.* — 吃蛋糕前你必须洗手。 |
| **have to** | *If you want to play, you have to help with the tea party.* | *If you want to play, you have to help pour.* — 你要是想玩，就得帮忙倒。 |
| **be going to** | *We're all going to have tea.* | *We are all going to sit down.* — 我们都要坐下。 |
| **won't** | *Come downstairs right now, or you won't get any cake.* | *Come here now, or you won't get any cake.* — 现在过来，不然就没有蛋糕。 |
| **must have** | *I must have eaten them all.* | *I must have eaten the last biscuit.* — 最后那块饼干一定是我吃的。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Come downstairs right now.** — 马上下楼。
- **Don't worry.** — 别担心。
- **Take it away.** — 把它拿走。
- **Sit down.** — 坐下。
- **Not so close.** — 别靠这么近。

## 特色 · He's quite big, isn't he

这一集先说蜘蛛很小，看见了又说它挺大。句尾用 **isn't he**、**are you** 来确认。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **small** 很小 | *Spiders are very, very small, and they can't hurt you.* | *This bug is very small. It can't hurt you.* — 这只虫子很小，弄不疼你。 |
| **quite big** 挺大 | *He's quite big, isn't he?* | *This one is quite big, isn't it?* — 这只要算大的，对不对？ |
| **big** 大 | *Mr. Skinnylegs is big, isn't he?* | *Your toy spider is big, isn't it?* — 你的玩具蜘蛛挺大的，对不对？ |
| **are you** 你是不是 | *You're not scared, are you, Daddy?* | *You are not scared of it, are you?* — 你不怕它，对不对？ |
| **too scary** 太吓人 | *It's too scary. Take it away.* | *This picture is too scary. Turn it over.* — 这张画太吓人了，翻过去。 |
| **little** 一只小的 | *I hear you've found a little spider.* | *You found a little bug.* — 你发现了一只小虫子。 |

## 亲子讲解 · skinny legs、teapot、braver

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### skinny legs

*The spider is Mr. Skinnylegs.* — 这只蜘蛛叫瘦腿先生。

*Do you understand this sentence?* — 这句话你听懂了吗？

*Do you know what "skinny legs" means?* — 你知道 skinny legs 是什么意思吗？

*Skinny means very thin.* — skinny 是非常细。

*We call the spider Mr. Skinnylegs.* — 我们叫这只蜘蛛 Mr. Skinnylegs。

*It is a story name, not a name for a person.* — 这是故事里的称呼，不是叫人瘦腿。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### teapot

*Do you know the word teapot?* — 你知道 teapot 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: teapot.* — 跟我说：teapot。

*A teapot is the pot you pour tea from.* — teapot 是用来倒茶的壶。

*We say the tea is in the teapot.* — 我们说 tea is in the teapot。

*It is not a cup.* — 它不是杯子。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### braver

*Do you know the word braver?* — 你知道 braver 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: braver.* — 跟我说：braver。

*Braver means more brave than before.* — braver 是比以前更勇敢。

*We say you are braver now.* — 我们说 you are braver now。

*Brave is the first word. Braver compares two times.* — brave 是原来的词。braver 是拿来比。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
