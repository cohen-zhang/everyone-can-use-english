**Tooth Fairy**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态** 两节是这一集的跟读版。

P:I’m Peppa Pig, this is my little brother George, this is Mummy Pig, this is Daddy Pig, Peppa Pig!

**The Tooth Fairy**

N: Peppa and George are having their favorite food—Spaghetti D: oh.. what a lot of noise.

P: Finished!

M: Now I’ll have a bit of quiet.

P: what’s that?

D:It’s a tooth P:Where did that come from?

M: Peppa, maybe you should look in the mirror P: oh.. It’s my tooth, it’s fallen out.

M: Don’t worry, Peppa! It’s just milk tooth, they meant fallen out.

P: Will I grow new one, Mummy?

M: You will, Peppa! It also means the tooth Fairy will be paying your a visit P: Tooth Fairy?

M: yes, the Tooth Fairy, if you put your tooth under your pillow tonight, the Tooth Fairy will come, the Tooth Fairy will take the tooth, and in it’s place she will leave a shining coin P: when I grow up, I want to be a Tooth Fairy D: and what would you like to be when you grow up, George?

G: Dinosaur D: Hah… a dinosaur P: Quick, George! It’s bed time, we don’t want to miss the Tooth Fairy.

N:Before going to bed, Peppa and George brush their teeth D: Peppa, what you doing?

P: I’m brushing my tooth, so it’s nice and clean for the Tooth Fairy.

N: Peppa can’t wait to get in bed, Peppa is putting her tooth under the pillow for the Tooth Fairy M: Good night, Peppa and George!

P: Good night, Mummy! Good night, Daddy!

D: Good night, my little piggies!

P: George, I’m not going to sleep, let’s both stay awake all night, and see the Tooth Fairy P: This Tooth Fairy is very late.

N: oh, the Tooth Fairy is taking a long time to arrive P: Where is that Tooth Fairy?

N: What is that noise? Is it the Fairy?

P: George, can you hear something? Oh.. George!

N: Oh, the noises is George, he was so tired that he has fallen asleep.

P: George isn’t very good at staying awake, but I am, I’m going to stay awake to see the Tooth Fairy. I’m not going to sleep. (snoring sound) N: The Tooth Fairy has arrived, but Peppa is asleep.

Tooth Fairy: Hello, Peppa! Would you like this coin in return for your tooth? What a nice clean tooth! Thank you, Peppa! Good night!

M: Peppa, George! Wake up! It’s morning.

P: What? I wasn’t asleep.

D: Did the Tooth Fairy come?

P: No!

D: Let’s take a look under your pillow.

M: oh.. look! Peppa! the Tooth Fairy has been, and she’s left you a coin.

P: Horray!

M: You fell asleep, didn’t you?

P: Maybe I fell asleep just for a little bit, next time I will stay awake, and I will see the Tooth Fairy.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 这是谁的牙

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **Where did that come from?** | 那是从哪儿来的？ | **Where did it come from?** | 她没意识到是自己的 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在完成时** | *It's fallen out.* | *Your tooth has fallen out.* |
| **will** | *You will grow a new one.* | *A new one will grow.* |
| **will** | *The Tooth Fairy will come.* | *She will come tonight.* |
| **be going to** | *I'm going to stay awake.* | *I am going to stay awake.* |
| **过去完成 / 如此……以至于** | *He was so tired that he has fallen asleep.* | *You were so tired that you fell asleep.* |
| **现在完成时** | *The Tooth Fairy has been, and she's left you a coin.* | *She has left you a coin.* |
| **一般过去时** | *You fell asleep, didn't you?* | *You fell asleep.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Look in the mirror.** — 照照镜子。
- **Don't worry.** — 别担心。
- **Wake up.** — 醒醒。
