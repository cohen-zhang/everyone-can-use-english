## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 盒子里是什么

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Can anyone guess what's inside?** | 有人猜得出里面是什么吗？ | **Can you guess what's inside?** | 打开旧盒子之前 |
| **Can you play your violin?** | 你能拉小提琴吗？ | **Can you play it?** | 她看见一件旧乐器 |
| **Can I play the violin?** | 我可以拉小提琴吗？ | **Can I try?** | 她想上手 |
| **What other instruments are in the box?** | 盒子里还有什么乐器？ | **What else is in the box?** | 这一件太难了 |

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在完成进行时** | *They have been tidying the house.* | *We have been tidying.* |
| **used to** | *This is the violin I used to play.* | *I used to play this.* |
| **现在完成时** | *I haven't played it for a long time.* | *I haven't played it for years.* |
| **be meant to** | *It is not meant to sound like that.* | *A violin is not meant to sound like a drum.* |
| **would** | *Maybe you would do better with this tin drum.* | *You would do better with the drum.* |
| **will** | *I will play my drum.* | *I will play the drum.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Hold it like this.** — 这样拿。
- **Stick to the accordion.** — 还是拉手风琴吧。
