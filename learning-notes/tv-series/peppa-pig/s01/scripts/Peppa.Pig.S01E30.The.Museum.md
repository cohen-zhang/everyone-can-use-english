**S1-30 the museum**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态** 两节是这一集的跟读版。

### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa pig.

The Museum!

Peppa and her family are going to the museum.

Daddy, What is a museum?

It's a place full of interesting things that are very old.

Older than you?

Yes, even older than me.

Ooooo!

Really old.

There is one room full of things that belonged to kings and queens from Long ago.

I want to see the king and queen's room!

And there's another room with a real dinosaur.

Wow! Dine-Saw!

Grrr.

Hello, Mummy Pig!

Hello, Ms. Rabbit How many tickets, please?

TWo adults and two children.

Dine-Saw!

Grrrr.

And a dinosaur!

Peppa wants to see the room full of things that belong to kings and queens from Long ago.

Wow!

Dine-Saw?

But George wants to see the real dinosaur.

Don't worry, George.

We will see the dinosaur next.

### Scene · Peppa These Are All the Things（博物馆 · 段2/3）


Peppa, these are all the things that the queen had a Long time ago.

This is the queen's special chair.

It's called a throne.

It's beautiful!

This is the queen's dress.

It's so pretty!

Look, Peppa. This is the queen's golden crown.

Wow! What Lovely things!

Mummy, where is the queen's television?

They didn't have television then.

No television!

But they did have computers?

No, they didn't have computers, either.

What did they do all day?

Mummy, if I Was the queen, I Would eat as much cake as I Wanted.

Peppa imagines being a queen.

Mmmmm... delicious!

Is there anything else you Would care for, Queen Peppa?

Yes! More cake, please!

Of course.

Come on, everyone.

Coming, Daddy!

Daddy! I'm Queen Peppa!

You must bow when you speak to me.

Oh! I'm most terribly sorry, your royal highness.

And What do you do?

I'm your daddy.

### Scene · Hmmm That Must Be（博物馆 · 段3/3）


Hmmm, that must be very interesting.

Yes! It's very interesting!

This is the dinosaur room.

The dinosaur room?

George! This is the dinosaur room!

Dine-saw!

I can't see him.

Wow.

The room with the cakes in.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 博物馆是什么

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What is a museum?** | 博物馆是什么？ | **What is a museum?** | 第一次去 |
| **Older than you?** | 比你还老吗？ | **Is it older than you?** | 她听见「很古老」 |
| **How many tickets, please?** | 请问几张票？ | **How many tickets?** | 排队买票 |
| **Where is the dinosaur?** | 恐龙在哪儿？ | **Where is it?** | 进了恐龙厅却看不见 |

### 如果你是女王

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Where is the queen's television?** | 女王的电视在哪儿？ | **Did they have television?** | 她按现在的生活去想古代 |
| **Is there anything else you would care for?** | 您还想要点别的吗？ | **Would you care for more cake?** | 玩「女王点菜」 |
| **Which room is that?** | 那是哪一间？ | **Which room do you like?** | 每个人喜欢的厅不一样 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *They are going to the museum.* | *We are going to the museum.* |
| **一般过去时** | *These things belonged to kings and queens.* | *This belonged to a queen.* |
| **will** | *We will see the dinosaur next.* | *We will see it next.* |
| **if + 过去式** | *If I was the queen, I would eat cake.* | *If I were the queen, I would eat cake.* |
| **must** | *You must bow when you speak to me.* | *You must bow.* |
| **must** | *He must be very small.* | *It must be very big.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Don't worry.** — 别担心。
- **Come on, tuck in.** — 来，开动吧。
