### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig. This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa Pig.

Gardening!

### Scene · Peppa and George Are Playing at（种菜 · 段1/3）
Peppa and George are playing at Granny Pig and Grandpa Pig's house.

Grandpa! Catch!

Catch!

What's this?

Dine-Saw! Grrrrr!

A dinosaur!

Grandpa, What are you doing?

I'm planting these seeds.

Seeds? What do seeds do?

Seeds grow into plants.

I just make a little hole and put the seed in.

Then I cover it with earth and water it.

Everything in my garden grows from tiny seeds Like these.

Even the big apple tree?

Oh! Yes!

This tiny seed...

...Will grow into a little apple tree, Like this.

Oooh.

And that Little apple tree Will grow into a big apple tree Like this.

Ouch!

Mmmm !

And inside this apple are more seeds.

To make more apple trees!

Exactly!

Grandpa! Grandpa!

I want to plant a seed!

Would you Like to plant a strawberry seed?

### Scene · Yes Please（种菜 · 段2/3）


Yes, please!

This seed will grow into a Lovely strawberry plant.

First, make a little hole.

Then I put the seed in and cover it with earth.

Shall I Water it for you?

No! No! I want to water it.

Good! Now We Wait for it to grow.

Peppa and George are waiting for the seed to grow.

It's not doing anything!

You will have to be patient, Peppa.

It will take a Long time to grow.

Peppa! George!

It's time to go home.

But We're waiting for my strawberry plant to grow!

I Wanted strawberries for teas.

Don't worry, Peppa.

Next time you come, the seed will have grown into a plant.

And We will have strawberries!

Yes.

Come on, Peppa.

Bye bye, Grandpa.

Bye bye, strawberry.

Grandpa Pig Looks after Peppa's strawberry plant.

After many days, Grandpa Pig finds a tiny plant growing.

### Scene · Grandpa Pig Finds a Tiny Plant（种菜 · 段3/3）



Day by day, the plant grows bigger and bigger.

Then one day, Grandpa Pig finds something very special.

Strawberries!

Grandpa! We're back!

Peppa and George have Come to play again.

Grandpa! Grandpa!

Did my plant grow?

Yes! Look!

Oooo!

Strawberries!

Thank you, Grandpa!

Grandpa, can We plant something else?

Yes! Now, it's George's turn to choose.

Yes! You choose, George.

Choose... a carrot!

Grandpa, I think George Wants to grow a carrot.

George, Would you Like to grow a carrot?

No!

What Would you Like to grow?

George has thought of something he wants to grow.

Dine-Saw!

George wants to grow a dinosaur tree!

Silly George!

Dinosaurs don't grow on trees.

Dine-saw. Grrrrr....


### Closing · Outro（片尾）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 种子做什么

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **现在进行时** | *I'm planting these seeds.* | *I am planting a seed.* |
| **will** | *This seed will grow into a plant.* | *It will grow.* |
| **have to** | *You will have to be patient.* | *You have to be patient.* |
| **将来完成时** | *The seed will have grown into a plant.* | *It will have grown by next week.* |
| **一般过去时** | *I wanted strawberries for tea.* | *I wanted strawberries.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Make a little hole.** — 挖一个小坑。
- **Look.** — 看。

## 特色 · First, then

这一集种草莓，是按顺序说的：**First** 先挖坑，**Then** 再放种子、盖土、浇水。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First** 先 | *First, make a little hole.* | *First, wash your hands.* — 先洗手。 |
| **Then** 然后放进去 | *Then I put the seed in and cover it with earth.* | *Then drop one seed into the soil.* — 然后把一颗种子放进土里。 |
| **Then** 然后浇水 | *Then I cover it with earth and water it.* | *Then water the little hole.* — 然后给小坑浇水。 |
| **Then one day** 后来有一天 | *Then one day, Grandpa Pig finds something very special.* | *Then one day, a little plant will show.* — 然后有一天，会冒出一棵小苗。 |
| **Next time** 下次 | *Next time you come, the seed will have grown into a plant.* | *Next time you come, it will be bigger.* — 你下次来，它会长大一点。 |
