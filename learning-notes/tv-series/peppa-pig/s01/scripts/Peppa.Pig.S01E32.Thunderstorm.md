## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **will** | *It means there will be a thunderstorm.* | *There will be rain.* |
| **be going to** | *There is going to be a thunderstorm.* | *It is going to rain.* |
| **现在进行时** | *The sky is getting darker and darker.* | *It is getting dark.* |
| **一般过去时** | *I left Teddy in the garden.* | *I left my book outside.* |
| **had better** | *You'd better hurry.* | *You'd better come in.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Come inside the house.** — 进屋。
- **Don't worry.** — 别担心。
- **Don't be frightened.** — 别害怕。
- **Let's count.** — 我们一起来数。
