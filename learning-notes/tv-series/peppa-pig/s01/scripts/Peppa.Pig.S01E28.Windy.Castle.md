**S1-28 Windy Castle**

**相关：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态框架]]。下面 **亲子问句**、**时态** 两节是这一集的跟读版。

### Cold open · Intro（片头自我介绍）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

This is Mummy Pig.

And this is Daddy Pig.

Peppa pig.

Windy Castle!

### Scene · Peppa and Her Family Are（风息堡 · 段1/3）
Peppa and her family are going out for the day.

Peppa, George, today We are going to Windy Castle!

What's Windy Castle, Daddy?

It's a castle on a very high hill!

George likes casties.

Windy Castle sounds Like a boring thing for boys.

No, Peppa, you'II Love it.

There's a great view from the top of Windy Castle.

You can even see Granny and Grandpa's house!

Wow! Let's go!

I'll map-read and Mummy Pig will drive.

Are you sure, Daddy Pig?

When you map-read, We always get Lost and you get grumpy.

We will not get Lost and I Will not get grumpy.

Windy Castle, here We come!

Are We nearly there yet?

Not quite.

Oh.

Would you Like to play a game?

Yes, please. Let's play ''I Spy!''

Okay. I'll go first.

Daddy Pig has to secretly Look at something and the others have to guess What it is.

I spy with my Little eye, something colored ''red''.

Red? My dress!

That's red!

No. It's not your dress.

Hmmmm.

The car!

That's right!

Our red car.

### Scene · My Go I Spy With My（风息堡 · 段2/3）


My go. I spy with my Little eye, something ''blue'' Hmmm...

George's blue shirt!

Nope.

Is it something in the car?

No. Give up?

Yes.

The sky! The blue sky!

I Win.

Daddy Pig? Do you know where We are?

I know exactly where We are.

Although... this road doesn't Look the same as it does on the map.

Oh, Daddy Pig!

We're Lost!

We are not Lost.

So how do We get to Windy Castle from here?

Just give me a moment.

Hmmmm!

I know, We can ring Granny and Grandpa.

There's no need to ring Granny and Grandpa.

I'll get us to Windy Castle, if it takes me all day.

We haven't got all day.

Hello, Grandpa Pig speaking.

Grandpa Pig, We've got a bit Lost on the Way to Windy Castle.

Is Daddy Pig doing the map-reading?

Yes, Daddy Pig is doing the map-reading.

And he's a bit grumpy at the moment.

I am not grumpy.

This is the best route: keep on the main road until you see Windy Castle up ahead.

### Scene · Thank You Grandpa Pig（风息堡 · 段3/3）


Thank you, Grandpa Pig.

We follow the main road And look out for Windy Castle.

As I thought.

Look!

Is that a castle?

Yes! It's Windy Castle!

Come on, car!

Come on, car! You can make it!

Hurray!

Peppa and her family have arrived at Windy Castle.

Wow! It's so tall!

Let's go inside. The view from the top is fantastic!

Wow!

Look at the view!

Mummy, is that Granny and Grandpa's house?

Yes, it is!

It's so far away!

Let's take a Look through the telescope.

Can I Look first, please?

Yes, but you must Let George look next.

Wow!

The telescope makes everything Look bigger.

I can see Granny and Grandpa!

Look, George!

Grandpa Pig! Grandpa Pig!

George is Waving at Granny and Grandpa Pig.

Silly George!

Granny and Grandpa are too far away to see you Waving!

I know! We can ring them!

Hello?

Grandpa Pig! We can see you from Windy Castle!

Wave at us!

Granny and Grandpa are Waving at us!

I love Windy Castle!


### Closing · Outro（片尾）
I'm Peppa Pig.

This is my little brother, George.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 还要多久

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What's Windy Castle?** | 风息堡是什么？ | **What is that?** | 她看见一个不认识的地方 |
| **Are we nearly there yet?** | 我们快到了吗？ | **Are we nearly there yet?** | 坐车时她问第好几次 |
| **Do you know where we are?** | 你知道我们在哪儿吗？ | **Do you know where we are?** | 地图和眼前的路对不上 |
| **Would you like to play a game?** | 你想玩个游戏吗？ | **Would you like to play a game?** | 路还长，用来打发时间 |

### 在看什么

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Is that a castle?** | 那是城堡吗？ | **Is that a castle?** | 远远看见一座建筑 |
| **Can I look first, please?** | 我可以先看吗？ | **Can I look first?** | 望远镜、窗口只有一个先看 |
| **Is it something in the car?** | 是车里的东西吗？ | **Is it something in this room?** | 玩 I spy 时缩小范围 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be going to** | *Today we are going to Windy Castle.* | *Today we are going to the park.* |
| **will** | *Mummy Pig will drive.* | *I will drive.* |
| **现在完成时** | *We've got a bit lost.* | *We've got a bit lost.* 原句就能用。 |
| **一般现在时** | *Windy Castle sounds like a boring thing.* | *That sounds boring.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Keep on the main road.** — 一直走大路。
- **Look out for the castle.** — 留神找城堡。
- **Let George look next.** — 下一个让别人看。
