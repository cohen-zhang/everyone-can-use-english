Mess?

Don't break the Wall, Daddy.

Don't be silly, Peppa.

Easy as pie.

## 亲子问句 · Questions you can ask

这一集里，家长可以对 Celine 直接说的问句。先跟原句，再用「家里怎么换」换场景。

### 挂得上吗

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Are you sure you can do it?** | 你确定你能做好吗？ | **Are you sure you can hang it straight?** — 你确定能挂直吗？ | 她要自己钉或贴 |
| **Can we help put up the picture?** | 我们可以帮忙挂画吗？ | **Can I help hold the picture?** — 我可以帮忙扶着画吗？ | 她伸手要参与 |
| **Do you think Mummy will notice?** | 你觉得妈妈会发现吗？ | **Do you think anyone will notice this mark?** — 你觉得有人会看见这个印吗？ | 墙上留下痕迹 |

### 刚才在干什么

| 本集原句 | 简中 | 家里怎么换 | 用在什么时候 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What are they doing?** | 他们在干什么？ | **What are you two doing?** — 你们两个在干什么？ | 她和玩具躲起来 |
| **What have you been doing?** | 你们刚才在做什么？ | **What have you been doing in here?** — 你们在这儿做什么来着？ | 一进门就看见乱 |

## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **should** | *We should put this lovely new picture on the wall.* | *We should put this picture by the door.* — 我们应该把这幅画挂在门边。 |
| **be going to** | *Daddy Pig is going to knock the nail into the wall.* | *I am going to put one nail here.* — 我要在这里钉一颗钉子。 |
| **have to** | *Daddy Pig has to mend the wall before Mummy comes home.* | *We have to tidy this before dinner.* — 晚饭前我们得把这里收拾好。 |
| **现在完成进行时** | *What have you been doing?* | *What have you been building?* — 你刚才在搭什么？ |
| **现在完成时** | *You've broken the wall.* | *You have made a mark on the wall.* — 你在墙上留下了一个印。 |
| **had better** | *We had better clean up before Mummy comes back.* | *We had better wipe this up now.* — 我们最好现在就擦掉。 |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Leave it to me.** — 交给我。
- **Don't make a mess.** — 别弄乱。
- **Don't break the wall.** — 别把墙弄坏。
- **Stand back.** — 往后站。
- **Don't be silly.** — 别傻了。
- **Pretend nothing has happened.** — 装得什么都没发生。
- **Don't tell anyone.** — 别告诉别人。
