## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *I don't feel very well.* | *I don't feel well.* |
| **现在完成时** | *Dr. Brown Bear has come to make Peppa better.* | *The doctor has come.* |
| **will** | *The rash will clear up quickly.* | *It will clear up.* |
| **must** | *Peppa must stay in bed.* | *You must stay in bed.* |
| **一般过去时** | *Dr. Brown Bear gave me medicine that tasted really horrible.* | *The medicine tasted horrible.* |
| **be going to** | *I'm going to wear my nurse's outfit.* | *I'm going to wear my coat.* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Stick your tongue out, please.** — 请把舌头伸出来。
- **Open wide, please.** — 把嘴张大。
- **Don't worry.** — 别担心。

## 特色 · Not very and a little

这一集说不舒服，用 **not very**（不太）和 **a little**（一点点）。*a little bit* 是「一小会儿」或「好了一点点」，*a little bit more* 是「再多一会儿」。每一行先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

| 词 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **don't feel very** 觉得不太 | *I don't feel very well.* | *I don't feel very hungry.* — 我不太饿。 |
| **don't look very** 看起来不太 | *You don't look very well.* | *You don't look very sleepy.* — 你看起来不太困。 |
| **not very nice** 味道不太好 | *I'm afraid it doesn't taste very nice.* | *This soup doesn't taste very good.* — 这汤味道不太好。 |
| **a little** 一点点 | *If you like, I can give you just a little medicine.* | *I will give you just a little water.* — 我给你一点点水。 |
| **a little bit** 一小会儿 | *You must stay in bed for a little bit.* | *Rest on the sofa for a little bit.* — 在沙发上歇一小会儿。 |
| **a little bit** 回答「好一点了吗」 | *A little bit.* | *I feel a little bit better.* — 我好了一点点。 |
| **a little bit more** 再多一会儿 | *I think I should stay in bed a little bit more.* | *I should rest a little bit more.* — 我该再歇一小会儿。 |

## 亲子句式 · says that 与 Ask … if

这一集有两套家里天天用的句式。先跟本集原句，再换一句家里的话。

### says that · 转述别人的话

**says that** 是现在还这么规定。**said … and that …** 是已经说过，而且说了两件事。第二个 **that** 别省，不然听起来像后半句是自己补的。

| 句式 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **says that** 现在还这么说 | *Dr. Brown Bear says that you must stay in bed for a little bit.* | *Daddy says that you must finish your soup.* — 爸爸说你得把汤喝完。 |
| **says that** 许可 | *Dr. Brown Bear did say you could have visitors.* | *Mummy says that you can have one visitor.* — 妈妈说你可以有一个来看你的人。 |
| **said … and that** 两件事 | *He said I was not very well and that I was very brave.* | *The doctor said I was not very well and that I was very brave.* — 医生说我不太舒服，还说我很勇敢。 |
| **said … and that** 换场景 | 同上 | *Grandma said the soup was hot and that I should wait.* — 奶奶说汤很烫，还说我该等一等。 |

**said** 后面用 **was**，因为是在转述已经说过的话。对着 Celine 复述医生的话，就说 *The doctor said you were very brave.*

### Ask … if · 请别人去问

自己问，直接说 **Can I …?** 请旁边的人去问另一个人，用 **Ask + 人 + if**。本集的 **could** 是请朋友传话时的客气说法；家里直接传话，用 **can** 就够。

| 句式 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask … if** 请人去问 | *Ask my mummy if I could have some ice cream.* | *Ask Daddy if I can have some ice cream.* — 问问爸爸我能不能吃冰淇淋。 |
| **Ask … if** 再待一会儿 | 同上 | *Ask Grandma if we can stay a little bit more.* — 问问奶奶我们能不能再待一小会儿。 |
| **Can I** 自己问 | 本集没有这句 | *Can I have some ice cream?* — 我能吃冰淇淋吗？ |

## 亲子讲解 · rash、catching、fancy

家长对着 Celine 说英文。先问懂不懂，再用 *means*、*Notice that we say*、*There's a difference between* 解释。词组先问这句话听懂了吗。

### rash

*Do you know the word rash?* — 你知道 rash 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: rash.* — 跟我说：rash。

*A rash is red spots on the skin.* — rash 是皮肤上的一片红疹。

*There's a difference between one spot and a rash.* — 一个点是 spot，一片才叫 rash。

*For example, it is only a rash.* — 比方说，只是疹子。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### catching

*Do you know the word catching?* — 你知道 catching 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: catching.* — 跟我说：catching。

*Catching means other people can get it from you.* — catching 是会传染。

*We say a cold can be catching.* — 我们说 a cold can be catching。

*Isn't catching means you will not give it to others.* — isn't catching 是不会传给别人。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？

### fancy

*Do you know the word fancy?* — 你知道 fancy 这个词吗？

*Say it with me: fancy.* — 跟我说：fancy。

*Here, fancy means you want something.* — 这里的 fancy 是想要，不是花哨。

*There's a difference between I fancy an apple and a fancy dress.* — I fancy an apple 和化装服不是一个 fancy。

*For example, I fancy an apple.* — 比方说，我想吃个苹果。

*Does that make sense?* — 这样讲得通吗？
