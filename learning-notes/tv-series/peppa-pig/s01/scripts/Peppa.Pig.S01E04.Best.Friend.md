## 时态 · Tenses in this episode

只收这一集真正用到的框架。每一行：本集一句，加一句家里能换的话。更全的提问框架见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/communication-patterns/parenting-question-bank-by-category|亲子问句分类清单 · 时态]]。

| 时态 | 本集原句 | 家里可替换 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **一般现在时** | *They are best friends.* | *You are best friends.* |
| **be going to** | *I'm going to wave my magic wand.* | *I'm going to be a fairy.* |
| **have to** | *You'll have to play somewhere else.* | *You'll have to play in the other room.* |
| **一般现在时 · 感觉** | *He feels a bit lonely.* | *Do you feel lonely?* |

跟读命令句，不另算一种时态：

- **Go and play with your own toys.** — 去玩你自己的玩具。
- **Take a big breath.** — 深吸一口气。
- **Open wide.** — 把嘴张大。
