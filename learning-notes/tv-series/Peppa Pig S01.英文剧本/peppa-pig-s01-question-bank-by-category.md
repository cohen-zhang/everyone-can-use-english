## 句型模板速查（直接替换）

- **请求/许可：** `Can I ___?` / `Can we ___?` / `Could I ___, please?` / `May I ___, please?` / `Shall I ___?`
- **意愿/邀请：** `Would you like to ___?` / `Do you want to ___?` / `Will you ___?`
- **状态/确认：** `Are you ___?` / `Is everyone ___?` / `Are you sure (you ___)?`
- **信息探询：** `What's ___?` / `Where is ___?` / `Who is ___?` / `Why do(es) ___?` / `How will ___?`
- **现况追问：** `What are you doing?` / `What's going on?` / `What's wrong?`
- **选择/偏好：** `What do you like best?` / `What's your favorite ___?` / `Which ___?`
- **确认理解：** `Is that ___?` / `Do you know ___?` / `..., isn't it?` / `..., are you?`

> 小提示：Peppa 台词里最高频的是 **请求/许可（Can…?）+ 邀请（Would you like…?）+ 确认（Are you…? / Is everyone ready?）+ Wh- 探询**。先把这四类问熟，亲子日常基本够用。
