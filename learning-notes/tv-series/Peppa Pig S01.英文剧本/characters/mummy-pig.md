# Mummy Pig

**索引：** [[README|Peppa Pig S01 · Family Character Guides]]

Mummy Pig is Peppa and George's mother.  
猪妈妈是 Peppa 和 George 的妈妈。

She is calm, capable, and often the person who keeps the family moving.  
她很稳、很能干，也常常让一家人的事情顺利进行。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Mummy Pig is Peppa and George's mummy. She is calm, capable, and clear about family rules. When she works on her computer, Peppa and George may watch, but they must sit quietly and must not touch the computer. Mummy Pig also makes home feel warm: she cooks pancakes, lets the children help with simple jobs, and enjoys birthday surprises from her family. She loves going to the theater, and Granny Pig and Grandpa Pig can babysit when she and Daddy Pig go out. Mummy Pig is not loud or silly all the time, but her gentle words help the family feel safe.

猪妈妈是 Peppa 和 George 的妈妈。她冷静、能干，也会把家里的规则说清楚。她用电脑工作时，Peppa 和 George 可以看，但必须安静坐着，不能碰电脑。猪妈妈也让家里很温暖：她会做煎饼，让孩子们帮简单的忙，也喜欢家人为她准备的生日惊喜。她喜欢去剧院；当她和猪爸爸外出时，猪奶奶和猪爷爷可以帮忙看孩子。猪妈妈并不总是夸张搞笑，但她温柔清楚的话会让一家人觉得安心。
