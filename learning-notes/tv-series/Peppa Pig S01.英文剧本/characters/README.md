# Peppa Pig S01 · Family Character Guides

**索引：** [[learning-notes/tv-series/Peppa Pig S01.英文剧本/README|Peppa Pig S01 剧本说明]]

These guides introduce Peppa's family in simple parent-child English.  
这些文档用简单亲子英文介绍 Peppa 的一家人，方便边看动画边讲解、提问和对话。

Use them when you want to say more than “This is Peppa.”  
当你不只想说「这是佩奇」，而是想继续聊身份、关系、爱好、性格和好笑的情节时，可以从这里开始。

---

## How to Use `亲子`

1. Pick one character before watching an episode.  
   看一集前，先选一个人物。
2. Read the short identity lines aloud.  
   先读身份和关系句。
3. Watch one source scene, then ask two easy questions.  
   看一个相关片段，再问两个简单问题。
4. Let the child answer with a short sentence, not a full explanation.  
   让孩子用短句回答，不要求长篇解释。

Example pattern:  
示例句型：

| Parent says | Child can answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who is Peppa? — Peppa 是谁？ | She is George's big sister. — 她是 George 的姐姐。 |
| What does George love? — George 喜欢什么？ | He loves Mr. Dinosaur. — 他喜欢恐龙先生。 |
| Is Daddy Pig being silly? — 猪爸爸是不是有点好笑？ | Yes, he is. — 是的。 |
