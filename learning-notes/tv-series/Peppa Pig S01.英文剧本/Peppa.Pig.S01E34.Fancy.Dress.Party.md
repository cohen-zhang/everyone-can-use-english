**Fancy Dress Party**

**Mummy Pig -MP**

**Daddy Pig -DP**

**Peppa Pig -P**

**George Pig -G**

**Suzy Sheep -S**

**Candy Cat -C**

**Danny Dog -D**

**Rebecca Rabbit -R**

**Pedro Pony -Pony**

Peppa and George are having a fancy dress party. All their friends are invited. Peppa is dressed as a fairy princess, and George is dressed as a dinosaur.

G: Err. Dinosaur.

Here are peppa's friends. Candy cat, Suzy sheep, Danny dog, Rebecca rabbit and Pedro pony. Suzy is wearing her nurses costume.

DP: Hello Suzy.

S: Hello Mr. Pig.

DP: I'm glad the nurse is arrived. Have you just come from the hospital?

S: En, I'm not a real nurse. I just pretend.

DP: Ha, very good. My, my, what have we here?

D: Wow, I'm a pirate. Shiver me timbers.

C: Mew, I'm a witch. I can turn you into a frog.

P: Eh. I'm a clown.

DP: That's funny. What are you, Rebecca rabbit?

R: I'm a carrot.

DP: Fantastic. Come in, there are a fairy princess and a dinosaur waiting to meet you.

P: Hello, everyone.

All: Hello, peppa..

P: Hello, Suzy. I'm princess peppa. You must Bow when you speak to me.

S: Hello, your Majesty. I'm nurse Suzy. Open wide and say aha.

P: Aha.

Pony: I'm a clown.

R: Do something funny.

D: Rebecca, why are you dressed as a carrot?

R: I like carrots.

G: Dinosaur.

D: Ha. A scary dinosaur.

Peppa is reminding herself in a mirror.

P: Mirror, mirror on the wall, who is the fairest of them all? You are, peppa.

C: Hello, Peppa.

P: Hello. I am a little fairy princess.

C: I'm a witch. And I've got a magic wand.

P: I've got a magic wand too.

C: I can turn you into a frog.

P: And I'll turn you into a frog.

G: Dinosaur. (Crying…)

Oh, dear, George is scared by his own reflection.

MP: Silly George, look, it's you in the mirror.

MP: Children, it's time to decide who has the best fancy dress costume.

DP: We need a judge.

All: Me, me.

DP: As it is peppa's party, maybe she should be the judge.

P: I'm the judge. I'm the judge. Daddy, what is a judge?

DP: The judge decides who has the best costume.

P: Oh goodie.

Peppa is going to choose who has the best costume.

P: Suzy, can I see your costume please?

S: I'm nurse Suzy. I make people better.

P: Very good, Suzy. Now, Danny?

C: I am a pirate. Shiver me timbers.

P: Now, Candy?

C: I'm a witch. I can turn you into a frog.

P: I am a fairy princess. And I can turn you into a frog.

MP: Children, children.

P: Sorry mummy.

MP: Who is next?

Pony: I am a clown.

P: That's funny. And, Rebecca?

R: I'm a carrot.

P: Lovely, and my little brother George is a scary dinosaur. Everyone's costume is very good.

All: Hurray.

MP: Now you say who the winner is.

P: Oh yes, and the winner is ME.

MP: Peppa, you can’t pick yourself. You are the judge.

P: Oh, can't I?

MP: You have to pick another winner.

P: OK. The carrot wins.

R: Thank you.

Peppa loves fancy dress parties. Everyone loves fancy dress parties.
