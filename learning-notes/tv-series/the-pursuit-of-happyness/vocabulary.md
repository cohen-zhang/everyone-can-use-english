## 朗读串联记忆 · Read-aloud chain

*约 1 分钟 · 先英后对照简中*

### A. 分句场景链

- **Don't ever let somebody tell you you can't do something.** — 别让任何人告诉你你做不到。
- **You got a dream, you gotta protect it.** — 有梦想，就得护住它。
- **People can't do something themselves, they wanna tell you you can't do it.** — 自己做不到的人，才爱说你也做不到。
- **If you want something, go get it.** — 想要什么，就去拿到它。
- **This part of my life is called "Happiness."** — 我人生的这一段，叫做「幸福」。

### B. 一段串联

**Don't ever let somebody tell you you can't do something. You got a dream, you gotta protect it. People can't do something themselves, they wanna tell you you can't do it. If you want something, go get it. This part of my life is called "Happiness."**

**简中：** 别让任何人告诉你你做不到。有梦想，就得护住它。自己做不到的人，才爱说你也做不到。想要什么，就去拿到它。我人生的这一段，叫做「幸福」。
