# Cameron Tucker

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Cameron is Mitchell's partner—dramatic, warm, and often the bigger personality in the room.  
Cameron 是 Mitchell 的伴侣——戏剧化、温暖，常常是房间里最抢眼的那一个。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Cameron Tucker arrives late on the plane with snacks and an apology: *"Sorry, sorry, sorry. Daddy needed snacks."* He co-parents Lily with theatrical affection—*"Who is a good girl? Who's that?"*—and stops Mitchell's inflight rant with perfect comic timing: *"You are not giving the speech"* and *"She's got the cream puffs."* Cameron can go big (Halloween *director mode* in later episodes) and Mitchell's line *"you always just take things a little too far"* is basically his character summary. Cam's English is expressive, musical, and great for practicing pitch range, warmth, and interrupting someone gracefully.

Cameron 在飞机上迟到，抱着零食道歉：*"Sorry, sorry, sorry. Daddy needed snacks."* 他和 Mitchell 一起带 Lily，感情表达很戏剧化——*"Who is a good girl? Who's that?"*——又用完美喜剧节奏拦住 Mitchell 的机上演讲：*"You are not giving the speech"* 和 *"She's got the cream puffs."* 他能把事情搞很大（后面几集的万圣节「导演模式」），Mitchell 那句 *"you always just take things a little too far"* 几乎就是他的角色摘要。Cam 的英语富有表现力、音调起伏大，适合练情绪、温暖和「体面地打断别人」。
