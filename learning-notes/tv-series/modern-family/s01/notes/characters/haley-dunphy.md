# Haley Dunphy

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Haley is Claire and Phil's oldest daughter—a teenager testing boundaries on clothes, dating, and privacy.  
Haley 是 Claire 和 Phil 的大女儿——在穿着、约会和隐私上不断试探边界。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Haley Dunphy speaks like a typical American teen: impatient, defensive, and quick with a comeback. When parents yell from downstairs, she asks, *"Why are you guys yelling at us when we're way upstairs?"* On the skirt fight she pushes back: *"What's wrong with it?"* With siblings she escalates: *"Fight in the sun. It'll be a nice change."* Her first boyfriend Dylan triggers Claire's *"You have to scare him"* and Phil's cringe *"Phil Dunphy, yo"* performance. Haley's lines are short, reactive, and perfect for practicing casual teen intonation and pushback phrases.

Haley 说话像典型美国青少年：不耐烦、爱顶嘴、回怼很快。父母在楼下喊，她会说：*"Why are you guys yelling at us when we're way upstairs?"* 裙子争执里她反问：*"What's wrong with it?"* 和兄弟姐妹吵架时她会升级：*"Fight in the sun. It'll be a nice change."* 第一个男朋友 Dylan 让 Claire 说 *"You have to scare him"*，也让 Phil 上演尴尬的 *"Phil Dunphy, yo"*。Haley 的台词短、反应式，很适合练青少年随意语调和反驳句型。
