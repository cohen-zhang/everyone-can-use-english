# Jay Pritchett

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Jay is the family patriarch: Claire and Mitchell's father, Gloria's husband, and Manny's stepdad.  
Jay 是家族大家长：Claire 和 Mitchell 的父亲、Gloria 的丈夫、Manny 的继父。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Jay Pritchett is a blunt, old-school dad who shows love through protection more than soft words. On the soccer field he yells *"Vamos, Manny! Kick it!"* and threatens another parent with *"How about I take you out?"* When someone mistakes Gloria for his daughter, he deadpans, *"Actually, no, I'm her husband."* He is hard on Manny because he wants the boy toughened up, yet he also worries about hurting Manny's feelings. Jay's humor is dry, his temper is quick, and his lines are perfect for practicing confident, slightly aggressive American speech.

Jay 说话直、作风老派，比起甜言蜜语，他更常用「护短」表达爱。球场上他会喊 *"Vamos, Manny! Kick it!"*，也会对别人放狠话 *"How about I take you out?"* 有人把 Gloria 当成他女儿时，他面无表情地说 *"Actually, no, I'm her husband."* 他对 Manny 严厉，是想让孩子更坚强，但也怕自己伤到他。Jay 的幽默偏冷、脾气来得快，台词很适合练自信、略带攻击性的美式口语。
