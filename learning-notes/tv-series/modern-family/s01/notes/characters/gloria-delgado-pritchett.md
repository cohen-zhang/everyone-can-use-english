# Gloria Delgado-Pritchett

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Gloria is Jay's wife and Manny's mother—a warm, fiery Colombian woman with sharp comic timing.  
Gloria 是 Jay 的妻子、Manny 的母亲——热情、火爆，喜剧节奏感很强。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Gloria Delgado-Pritchett introduces herself plainly: *"Hi, I'm Gloria Pritchett. Manny's mother."* She contrasts her poor but beautiful Colombian village with Jay's city life, and lands dark humor with lines like *"Murders. Yes. The murders."* Gloria is passionate and protective—she tells Jay to stop being a *"gloomy goose"* and to ease up on Manny. She mixes warmth (*"Ay, thank you, Phil"*) with sudden fire (*"Hey, watch it"*). Her English is expressive and slightly non-native in places, which makes her lines great for rhythm, emphasis, and emotional clarity.

Gloria 自我介绍很干脆：*"Hi, I'm Gloria Pritchett. Manny's mother."* 她把哥伦比亚小村庄的贫穷与美丽和 Jay 的城市生活对照，还能用 *"Murders. Yes. The murders."* 这种黑色幽默收尾。她热烈又护短——叫 Jay 别当 *"gloomy goose"*，也别对 Manny 太苛刻。她一面温柔（*"Ay, thank you, Phil"*），一面突然发火（*"Hey, watch it"*）。她的英语富有表现力，偶尔带非母语节奏，很适合练重音、情绪和清晰度。
