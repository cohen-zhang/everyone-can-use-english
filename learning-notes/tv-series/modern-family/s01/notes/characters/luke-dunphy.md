# Luke Dunphy

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Luke is Claire and Phil's youngest son—accident-prone, literal-minded, and oddly confident.  
Luke 是 Claire 和 Phil 的小儿子——容易出事故、思维偏字面、却又莫名自信。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Luke Dunphy is the Dunphy chaos kid. His head gets stuck in the banister *again*; he explains, *"I thought I could get out this time."* Phil frees him with *"There. Be free."* Luke also shoots someone by accident: *"Luke just shot me! — I didn't mean to."* The family's running joke is on the calendar: *"Shoot Luke."* He wears too many layers, ruins gadgets, and takes Phil's lessons literally. Luke's English is simple, honest, and great for practicing plain statements, apologies, and physical-comedy reactions.

Luke 是邓菲家的「混乱担当」。他的头*又*卡进楼梯栏杆；他会说：*"I thought I could get out this time."* Phil 把他弄出来：*"There. Be free."* 他也会误伤别人：*"Luke just shot me! — I didn't mean to."* 家里的长期梗写在日程上：*"Shoot Luke."* 他穿太多层、弄坏电子设备、把 Phil 的教导听得太字面。Luke 的英语简单、诚实，适合练直白陈述、道歉和肢体喜剧反应。
