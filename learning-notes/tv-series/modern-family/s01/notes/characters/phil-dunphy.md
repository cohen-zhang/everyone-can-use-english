# Phil Dunphy

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Phil is Claire's husband—a real estate agent, enthusiastic dad, and accidental comedy engine.  
Phil 是 Claire 的丈夫——房产中介、热情型爸爸、也是行走的喜剧发动机。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Phil Dunphy opens S01E02 with a question that defines him: *"What's the key to being a great dad?"* His answer mixes sincerity and sitcom: *"Patience"* and *"Giving them the freedom to be whatever they want to be."* At home he waffles (*"That looks really cute, sweetheart"*) until Claire forces follow-through. With Haley's boyfriend he tries too hard: *"Phil Dunphy, yo"* and a stare-down lesson that ends in baby oil and back pain. Phil's vocabulary—*Buddy*, *uncool*, *Deal with this*—is peak casual American dad talk. He is cringe, loving, and very useful for practicing upbeat, slightly awkward spoken English.

Phil 在 S01E02 一开口就定调：*"What's the key to being a great dad?"* 他的答案又真诚又好笑：*"Patience"* 和 *"Giving them the freedom to be whatever they want to be."* 在家他常常和稀泥（*"That looks really cute, sweetheart"*），直到 Claire 逼他 *follow through*。面对 Haley 的男朋友，他又用力过猛：*"Phil Dunphy, yo"*，再加上一套眼神震慑教学，最后滑倒在婴儿油上。Phil 的 *Buddy*、*uncool*、*Deal with this* 是典型美式老爸口语。他尴尬、有爱，特别适合练积极又有点社死的口语。
