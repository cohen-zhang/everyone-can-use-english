# Alex Dunphy

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Alex is the middle Dunphy kid—sharp, observant, and often the family's reality check.  
Alex 是邓菲家的中间孩子——聪明、观察力强，常常是家里的「现实检验器」。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Alex Dunphy is the brainy sister who notices what others miss. She interrupts Haley's date with *"Alex, get out! Mom!"* and defends herself: *"I was just getting my book. Gosh."* In the kitchen she trades gossip with Claire about a classmate's secret pregnancy rumor—hyper-detailed, skeptical, and funny. She needles Luke (*"He needs to be checked by a specialist"*) and enforces odd logic on his wardrobe. Alex's lines are crisp, slightly sarcastic, and ideal for practicing intelligent teen speech and deadpan delivery.

Alex 是家里最机灵的那个，常看到别人忽略的细节。她打扰 Haley 约会时会喊 *"Alex, get out! Mom!"*，也会辩解：*"I was just getting my book. Gosh."* 在厨房她和 Claire 交换同学秘密怀孕的八卦——细节多、怀疑态度强、也很好笑。她会吐槽 Luke（*"He needs to be checked by a specialist"*），也会用奇怪逻辑管他的穿着。Alex 的台词干脆、略带讽刺，适合练聪明青少年的口语和冷面 delivery。
