# Manny Delgado

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Manny is Gloria's son and Jay's stepson—romantic, old-soul, and fiercely earnest.  
Manny 是 Gloria 的儿子、Jay 的继子——浪漫、少年老成、认真得让人又好笑又动容。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Manny Delgado is not a typical kid. He wants to tell Brenda Feldman *"I love her"* and picks flowers in a white silk shirt. Jay groans—*"Manny, she's 16"*—but Manny fires back: *"Oh. It's okay for you to take an older lover?"* Gloria says *"Manny's very passionate, just like his father."* On the soccer field Jay screams *"Vamos, Manny! Kick it!"* while Manny plays with dramatic pride. Manny writes songs, reads poetry, and treats love like art. His lines blend sincerity and comedy—excellent for practicing earnest, slightly formal teen speech and romantic vocabulary.

Manny 不是普通小孩。他想告诉 Brenda Feldman *"I love her"*，还会穿着白色丝绸衬衫去采花。Jay 会叹气——*"Manny, she's 16"*——但 Manny 会回敬：*"Oh. It's okay for you to take an older lover?"* Gloria 说 *"Manny's very passionate, just like his father."* 球场上 Jay 喊 *"Vamos, Manny! Kick it!"*，Manny 则带着戏剧化的骄傲踢球。Manny 写歌、读诗，把爱情当艺术。他的台词真诚又好笑，适合练认真、略正式的青少年口语和浪漫词汇。
