# Mitchell Pritchett

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Mitchell is Jay's son, Claire's younger brother, and Cameron's partner—they adopt baby Lily in the pilot.  
Mitchell 是 Jay 的儿子、Claire 的弟弟、Cameron 的伴侣——首集里他们领养了 Lily。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Mitchell Pritchett is a lawyer with a principled streak and a thin skin for being overlooked. On the plane home with Lily, he jokes that lesbian friends are *"already mean enough"* to be surrogates. When passengers ignore them, he almost launches a speech: *"Love knows no race, creed or gender"* and calls out *"you small-minded, ignorant few"*—until Cameron stops him because *"She's got the cream puffs."* Mitchell pays for everyone's headsets as damage control. He can be snobbish (*"Mitchell is a snob"*) and tense with Cameron (*"you always just take things a little too far"*), but his lines are rich for formal-meets-emotional American speech.

Mitchell 是律师，原则感强，也很在意被忽视。在带着 Lily 回家的飞机上，他开玩笑说女同性恋朋友当代孕者已经 *"already mean enough"*。乘客不理他们时，他差点发表演讲：*"Love knows no race, creed or gender"*，并点名 *"you small-minded, ignorant few"*——直到 Cameron 拦住他，因为人家说的是 *"She's got the cream puffs."* 他最后请大家耳机费赔礼。别人说他势利（*"Mitchell is a snob"*），他也常嫌 Cameron *"you always just take things a little too far"*，但他的台词很适合练「正式 + 情绪化」的美式表达。
