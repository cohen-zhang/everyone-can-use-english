# Claire Dunphy

**索引：** [[README|Modern Family S01 · Character Guides]]

Claire is Phil's wife and the mother of Haley, Alex, and Luke—organized, intense, and haunted by her own wild youth.  
Claire 是 Phil 的妻子，Haley、Alex、Luke 的妈妈——有条理、情绪强烈，也一直被自己青春期的失控经历影响着。

---

## Short Introduction（短文）

Claire Dunphy runs the Dunphy household with rules and follow-through. She tells Phil to set boundaries on Haley's skirt: *"People know you're a girl. You don't need to prove it to them."* Her parenting standard is blunt: if Haley never wakes up *"half-naked on a beach in Florida,"* Claire considers the job done. She admits she was *"out of control growing up"* and does not want her kids to repeat her mistakes. Claire can be sharp with Phil (*"you don't have to be so emotional all the time"*) and strategic with teens (*"You have to scare him"* when Dylan visits). Her lines are ideal for practicing firm, fast American family speech.

Claire 用规则和「说到做到」撑起邓菲家。她让 Phil 管 Haley 的裙子：*"People know you're a girl. You don't need to prove it to them."* 她的育儿底线很直白：只要 Haley 不会 *"half-naked on a beach in Florida"* 醒来，就算完成任务。她承认自己青春期 *"out of control growing up"*，不想孩子重蹈覆辙。她对 Phil 可以很尖（*"you don't have to be so emotional all the time"*），对青少年也很会算计（Dylan 上门时说 *"You have to scare him"*）。她的台词适合练坚定、快节奏的美式家庭口语。
