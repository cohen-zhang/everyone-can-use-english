## 本集剧情简介

首集通过纪录片式视角介绍三个家庭：Jay 与 Gloria 的再婚家庭、Claire 与 Phil 的三娃家庭、以及 Mitchell 与 Cameron 这对伴侣。全家在晚餐聚会中逐步揭开彼此的生活状态与冲突，最后以 Mitchell 和 Cameron 抱着领养回来的女婴登场，把“多元但相连”的家庭主题立住。

**English version:** The pilot introduces three connected households in a mock-documentary style: Jay and Gloria's blended family, Claire and Phil's busy home with three kids, and Mitchell and Cameron as a couple preparing for parenthood. A family dinner gradually reveals each household's tensions and personalities. The episode closes with Mitchell and Cameron bringing home their adopted baby, establishing the show's theme of diverse but deeply connected family life.

## 剧情分段台词（Plot beats）

> 按 **叙事线** 分段，非关键词生活桶。每段 8–15 句；口头禅详见下节 **俚语表**（剧情段不重复罗列）。

### A. 机场回程与清晨开场

*邓菲家叫吃早饭；楼上楼下喊话；赴宴时间。*

- **Stop the car. - Oh, where are you going?** — 停车。——你去哪儿？
- **She barely slept on the plane and she's still wide awake.** — 她在飞机上几乎没睡，现在却还精神抖擞。
- **Even Pepper pointed it out on the way home from the airport.** — 从机场回家的路上，连 Pepper 都看出来她没困。

- **Kids, breakfast!** — 孩子们，吃早饭了！
- **Why are you guys yelling at us when we're way upstairs?** — 我们在楼上老远，你们喊也没用啊。
- **And then- Oh, we gotta leave for that dinner thing at 5:00.** — 然后——哦，我们五点还得去赴宴。
- **Kids? Get down here!** — 孩子们？快下来！
- **Phil, would you get them?** — 菲尔，把他们叫下来好吗？
- **Just text me. - All right. That's not gonna happen.** — 发短信告诉我。——那不可能。

### B. Haley 短裙与 Phil「酷爸」

*Claire 要 Phil 管裙子；Phil 嘴上说可爱、实则和稀泥。*

- **Honey, do you have anything to say to your daughter about her skirt? - Sorry.** — 亲爱的，对你女儿这条裙子有什么要说的吗？——不好意思。
- **Oh, yeah. That looks really cute, sweetheart. - Thanks.** — 哦对，挺可爱的，宝贝。——谢谢。
- **And, wow! You're not wearing that outfit.** — 哇，你不能穿这套出门。
- **What's wrong with it?** — 这衣服怎么了？
- **No. It's way too short.** — 不行，太短了。
- **People know you're a girl.** — 大家都知道你是女孩子。
- **You don't need to prove it to them.** — 没必要穿成这样给他们看。
- **Let's take it down a notch.** — 小声点。

### C. Claire 的成长史与育儿底线

*Phil 旁白式坦白；Florida 底线梗；家法「射卢克」与 follow through。*

- **I just don't want my kids to make the same bad mistakes I made** — 我只是不想让孩子重蹈我当年的覆辙。
- **I was out of control growing up.** — 我青春期简直无法无天。
- **There, you know? I said it.** — 看吧，我坦白了。
- **I-If Haley never wakes up on a beach in Florida, half-naked,** — 只要海莉别半裸着在佛罗里达沙滩上醒来，
- **Our job. - Right.** — 咱俩的任务。——对。
- **I've done our job.** — 我就算完成了咱俩的任务。
- **"Shoot Luke." - Sorry, dude. It's on the calendar.** — 「射卢克。」——抱歉老弟，日程表上写着呢。
- **And now you have to follow through.** — 所以你现在得按说好的执行。
- **Redheaded dad is angry daddy. - No, I'm not.** — 红发老爹是怒火老爹。——我才不是。
- **you don't have to be so emotional all the time.That's all I'm saying.** — 我只是说，你别老那么激动。

### D. Gloria 的身世与 Jay 家

*哥伦比亚村庄黑色幽默；商场线 Jay 护 Gloria。*

- **We're very different.** — 我俩太不一样了。
- **Jay's from the city.** — Jay 来自城市。
- **I come from a small village.** — 我来自一个小村庄。
- **Very poor but very, very beautiful.** — 很穷，但非常非常美。
- **It's the number one village in all Colombia for all the** — 我们村在全哥伦比亚……数量排第一，
- **What's the word?** — 那个词怎么说？
- **Murders.** — 凶杀案。
- **Yes. The murders.** — 对，凶杀案。
- **Hi, I'm Gloria Pritchett. Manny's mother.** — 你好，我是 Gloria Pritchett，曼尼的妈妈。
- **Hey, watch it. - I wanna go to the mall where she works.** — 嘿，说话注意点。——我要去她上班的商场。

### E. Manny 球场与 Jay 护短

*Jay 场边咆哮；*take you out* 双关；误认丈夫。*

- **Vamos, Manny! Kick it! Kick it!** — 加油曼尼！踢啊！踢啊！
- **Manny, stop him!** — 曼尼，拦住他！
- **Damn it, Manny!** — 该死的，曼尼！
- **Come on, coach. You gotta take that kid out.** — 教练，你得把那孩子换下去。
- **You wanna take him out? How about I take you out?** — 你想让他下场？不如我让你「下场」？
- **He spent the first half with his hand in his pants!** — 他上半场手一直插在裤裆里！
- **Actually, no, I'm her husband.** — 其实不，我是她老公。
- **Don't be fooled by the, uh** — 别被外表骗了，呃——
- **Give me a second here.** — 给我一点时间。

### F. Mitchell & Cam 领养 Lily

*机上归国；代孕玩笑；晚宴演讲；cream puffs 双关。*

- **And, uh, we just- we decided we really wanted to have a baby.** — 我们真的很想要一个孩子。
- **Who is a good girl? Who's that?** — 谁是乖宝宝呀？是谁呀？
- **Uh, we just, uh- We just adopted her from Vietnam.** — 我们刚从越南领养了她。
- **And we're bringing her home for the first time, huh?** — 这是我们第一次带她回家，对吧？
- **She's an angel. You and your wife must be thrilled.** — 她像小天使。你们一定很激动吧。
- **So, we had initially asked one of our lesbian friends to be a surrogate, but** — 起初我们想找一位女同性恋朋友代孕，但
- **Then we figured, they're already mean enough.** — 后来想，她们平时就够刻薄了。
- **Look at that baby with those cream puffs. - Okay. Excuse me.** — 你看那个宝宝和那些泡芙。——好了，失陪。
- **This baby would have grown up in a crowded orphanage** — 这宝宝原本可能在拥挤的孤儿院长大。
- **You saw that, right? Everybody fawning over Lily,** — 你看到了吧？大家都在围着 Lily，
- **Hear this. Love knows no race, creed** — 听好了，爱不分种族、信仰
- **Or gender.** — 不分性别。
- **What? -She's got the cream puffs.** — 干嘛？——人家说的是泡芙。
- **We would like to pay for everyone's headsets.** — 各位的耳机费我们请了。

### G. Luke 卡栏杆与三娃冲突

*banister 名场面；Manny 男生来访；BB 枪；*Deal with this* / *uncool*。*

- **Luke got his head stuck in the banister again.** — Luke 又把头卡在楼梯栏杆里了。
- **Buddy. Why do you keep getting stuck like this?** — 老弟，你怎么老卡在这儿？
- **I thought I could get out this time.** — 我以为这次能拔出来。
- **I'm just gonna say it. He needs to be checked by a specialist.** — 我直说了，他得找个专家查查（智商）。
- **There. Be free.** — 好了，你自由了。
- **I'm having a friend over today. - Who?** — 今天有朋友来我家玩。——谁？
- **Ooh, a boy. You gonna kiss him?** — 哇，男生？你要亲他吗？
- **Fight in the sun. It'll be a nice change. - Haley.** — 到太阳底下打。换换口味。——海莉。
- **Deal with this. - Buddy, uncool.** — 你看着办。——老弟，这样不对。
