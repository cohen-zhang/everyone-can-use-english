## 4. 精读自检（做完词表再开片）

1. 能脱稿说出 **There are no accidents.** 与 **There is no secret ingredient.**  
2. 能解释 **present** 的双关（礼物 / 此刻）  
3. 听到 **destiny / fulfill / worthy / warrior** 能对上片中主题  
4. **绝不**开中文字幕；不懂查词典或回看本表

## 朗读串联记忆 · Read-aloud chain

*约 1 分钟 · 先英后对照简中*

### A. 分句场景链

- **There are no accidents.** — 没有意外。
- **One often meets his destiny on the road he takes to avoid it.** — 人常常在躲避命运的路上遇见命运。
- **Yesterday is history, tomorrow is a mystery, but today is a gift.** — 昨日已成历史，明日仍是谜，今天是礼物。
- **That is why it is called the present.** — 所以它才叫 present。
- **There is no secret ingredient.** — 没有什么秘密配方。
- **To make something special, you just believe it's special.** — 要让它特别，你只需相信它特别。

### B. 一段串联

**There are no accidents. One often meets his destiny on the road he takes to avoid it. Yesterday is history, tomorrow is a mystery, but today is a gift. That is why it is called the present. There is no secret ingredient. To make something special, you just believe it's special.**

**简中：** 没有意外。人常常在躲避命运的路上遇见命运。昨日已成历史，明日仍是谜，今天是礼物。所以它才叫 present。没有什么秘密配方。要让它特别，你只需相信它特别。
