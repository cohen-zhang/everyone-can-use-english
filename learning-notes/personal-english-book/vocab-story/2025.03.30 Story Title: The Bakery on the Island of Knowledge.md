# The Bakery on the Island of Knowledge

## English story

Lily visited a small **bakery** on an **island** and met an old owner who asked her to mix colors on a **palette** before he would **launch** a new recipe. A broken **hydrant** sprayed water outside, but he said pressure builds **robustness**. He taught Lily about **serotonin**, **melatonin**, and **insomnia**, and how stress affects the **digestive** system, including the **gallbladder** and **bowel**.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

莉莉在一座**岛屿**（island）上的小**面包店**（bakery）遇到一位老人。老人让她先用**调色板**（palette）配色，再一起**推出**（launch）新配方。店外坏掉的**消防栓**（hydrant）正在喷水，老人却说压力也能培养**韧性**（robustness）。随后他讲解了**血清素**（serotonin）、**褪黑素**（melatonin）与**失眠**（insomnia）的关系，以及压力如何影响**消化系统**（digestive system），包括**胆囊**（gallbladder）和**肠道**（bowel）。最后他把继续传播健康知识的任务**委托**（entrust）给莉莉。
