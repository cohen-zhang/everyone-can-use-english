The Forest Olympics began with **Elephant** Ellie lifting acorns overhead, while **Giraffe** Gregor stretched his neck to touch the highest flagpole. **Shark** Sammy zoomed through water obstacle courses, and **Rabbit** Ruby burrowed tunnels so fast competitors got lost.

**Koala** Kiki slept through early events but won the bamboo pole-climbing final. **Panda** Panpan ate bamboo while balancing on a ball, **Monkey** Mike swung through rings with ease. **Deer** Darcy outran cheetah wannabes, **Cow** Clara ensured everyone stayed hydrated with fresh milk.

**Horse** Harry sensed approaching storms, **Sheep** Sherry wove wool blankets for the winners. **Chicken** Chuck invented alarm clocks that crowed in Morse code, **Dog** Duke sniffed out hidden medals buried in the sand. **Pig** Pinkie sculpted gold trophies from mud, and the whole forest celebrated under a starry sky.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

森林奥运会拉开帷幕：**大象**（Elephant）艾莉举起橡果当杠铃，**长颈鹿**（Giraffe）格雷戈尔伸长脖子触碰最高旗帜。**鲨鱼**（Shark）萨米在水上障碍赛道飞驰，**兔子**（Rabbit）露比飞速打洞让对手迷路。

**乌龟**（Turtle）汤米在滚烫的沥青路上爬得飞快，**松鼠**（Squirrel）萨米在树枝间收集松果保持平衡。**狐狸**（Fox）菲奥娜用爪印谜题智取对手，**鹦鹉**（Parrot）波莉为外国队翻译指令。**蝴蝶**（Butterfly）贝拉沿途授粉，**蜜蜂**（Bee）巴兹穿过迷宫。

**蛇**（Snake）西迪利用热感应绕过电网，**乌鸦**（Crow）卡尔用树枝制作工具。**猫头鹰**（Owl）胡特用超强夜视裁判夜间项目，**海豚**（Dolphin）戴夫表演水中特技，**鲸鱼**（Whale）瓦利用水柱为赛场降温。

**考拉**（Koala）奇奇边睡觉边赢得竹竿攀爬赛，**熊猫**（Panda）盼盼吃竹子时平衡木上翻跟头。**猴子**（Monkey）麦克轻松荡过绳环，**鹿**（Deer）黛西瞬间加速超越猎豹选手。**奶牛**（Cow）克拉拉提供新鲜牛奶解渴，**马**（Horse）哈里感知天气变化。

**绵羊**（Sheep）雪莉织羊毛毯给获胜者，**公鸡/鸡**（Chicken）查克发明摩尔斯电码闹钟，**狗**（Dog）杜克嗅出沙地里的隐藏奖牌，**猪**（Pig）平克用泥土雕刻金牌。整片森林在星空下欢庆胜利。
