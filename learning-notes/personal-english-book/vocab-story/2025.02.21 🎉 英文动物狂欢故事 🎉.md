## English story

In the town of Zootopia, **Cat** Midnight patrolled the streets with lightning reflexes. **Hamster** Hank built a maze of tunnels to store festival supplies, while **Lizard** Lily changed colors to match the parade floats.
**Frog** Freddy won the pond jump contest, and **Swan** Serena's elegant dance mesmerized the crowd. **Rhino** Rocky pulled the flower-covered carriage through the town square, with **Hippo** Holly splashing water everywhere.
**Crocodile** Clyde潜伏在河岸，用鼻子喷出彩虹水柱。**Polar Bear** Penny dazzled everyone with ice sculptures glowing under the moonlight. **Wolf** pack howled in perfect harmony as **Eagle** Edgar soared overhead, lighting fireworks with his talons.
**Raccoon** Rudy sorted recyclables with his clever paws, while **Otter** Ollie balanced trays of treats on his tail. **Starfish** Sammy judged the sandcastle competition, and **Seahorse** Sammy steered boats through the harbor maze. **Spider** Gwen wove glowing webs for the night market, and **Lobster** Larry served **lobster** rolls with precision.
**Ant** Army delivered meals in tiny convoys, **Pigeon** Penny dropped love letters from above. **Falcon** Fiona caught runaway balloons, **Sparrow** Sam chirped hourly news updates. **Mole** Molly fixed underground pipes without breaking the pavement, **Duck** Dunkin cleaned up pond debris, **Goose** Gert guided ships safely.
As **Turkey** Tom strutted into the center, he trumpeted loudly: "This town's diversity makes us stronger!" The entire animal kingdom cheered, their unique talents creating unforgettable memories.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

在动物小镇Zootopia，猫头鹰午夜用闪电般的速度巡逻街道。仓鼠汉克建造了迷宫隧道储存节日物资，蜥蜴莉莉随着游行花车变色。
青蛙弗雷迪赢得池塘跳远比赛，天鹅塞蕾娜优雅的舞姿令观众着迷。犀牛罗基拉着缀满鲜花的花车穿过广场，河马霍莉四处泼水制造欢乐水花。
鳄鱼克莱德潜伏在河岸，用鼻子喷出彩虹水柱。北极熊潘妮用冰雕在月光下闪耀，狼群用和谐嚎叫作为天空烟花表演的配乐。
浣熊鲁迪用巧爪分类回收物，水獭奥利用尾巴平衡餐盘。海星山姆担任沙滩城堡比赛裁判，海马萨米引导船只穿过港口迷宫。蜘蛛格温编织发光的夜市横幅，龙虾拉里精准供应龙虾卷。
蚂蚁军团用微型车队运送食物，鸽子佩妮从高空投递情书。隼菲奥娜接住逃走的氢气球，麻雀萨姆每小时播报社区新闻。鼹鼠莫莉在不破坏地面的情况下修复地下管道，鸭子邓金清理池塘垃圾，鹅格特担任渡轮安全引航员。
当火鸡汤姆昂首阔步走进中心时，它高声宣布："我们社区的多样性让我们更强大！" 全体动物欢呼，它们独特的才能创造了难忘的回忆。
