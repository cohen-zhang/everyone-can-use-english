# Jungle Adventure and Brain Mysteries

## English story

On a sunny day, Alice and Ben entered a dense jungle with their **backpacks**. Soon they saw a **crocodile** near an **alligator**, and Alice whispered that both were **reptiles**. They walked deeper and found tracks of **mammals**, which made Ben dream about discovering a rare species and winning great **merits**.

When Alice hit a branch, she felt a **head rush**. Ben joked that she should protect her **cerebrum** and **cerebral cortex**. In a cave, they found strange **ganglia** and **glia**. Ben explained that in **neuroscience**, **synapses** and **axons** pass signals like a network. After leaving the cave, they had a **dispute** about which road to choose. Ben laughed and said, "Fine, your way first. **Vice versa** next time."

They later found an old hut with dusty **facilities** and a notebook full of **conceptual** ideas about **physiological** research. A wrinkled creature appeared and got **irritated**. Alice noticed possible **dysplasia** in its body. At the lowest moment, she found a stone **cue** on the ground. Following it, they escaped the jungle.

Back at school, the **principal** invited them to share their story. Ben explained that the **cerebellum** plays an **integral** role in balance and movement. During a project, their team argued again about measuring **subconscious** neural signals. A **consulting** expert recommended a mixed method. Near the end, Ben discovered **complementary** data patterns across different **dimensions**, and their report became a breakthrough.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

阳光明媚的一天，艾丽丝和本背着**背包**（backpack）走进丛林。他们先看到**鳄鱼**（crocodile）和**短吻鳄**（alligator），并提醒彼此这些都属于**爬行动物**（reptile）。继续前进后，他们发现了**哺乳动物**（mammal）的脚印，本还幻想能立下大**功绩**（merit）。

艾丽丝撞到树枝后出现**头晕**（head rush），本打趣她要保护**大脑**（cerebrum）和**大脑皮层**（cerebral cortex）。在山洞里，他们看到疑似**神经节**（ganglia）和**神经胶质**（glia）的组织。本又解释了**神经科学**（neuroscience）里**突触**（synapse）与**轴突**（axon）的作用。后来两人因路线发生**争论**（dispute），最后决定这次听艾丽丝，**反之亦然**（vice versa）留到下次。

他们在一间老屋里找到陈旧的**设施**（facility）和记满**概念性**（conceptual）内容的笔记本，内容多与**生理学的**（physiological）研究有关。途中遇到一只被激怒的生物，可能有**发育异常**（dysplasia）。就在几乎放弃时，艾丽丝发现地上的**线索**（cue），两人终于走出丛林。

回校后，**校长**（principal）安排他们分享经历。本讲到**小脑**（cerebellum）在平衡中的**关键作用**（integral）。在后续项目中，团队围绕**潜意识**（subconscious）信号测量再次争论，最后由**顾问**（consulting）专家提出融合方案。最终，他们发现了数据间的**互补关系**（complementary）和新**维度**（dimension）规律，研究取得突破。
