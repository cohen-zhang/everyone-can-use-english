# The Mysterious Adventure in the Desert

## English story

A group of friends drove a **vehicle** into the desert and worried about a **bandit** attack. Their van suddenly went into a **slump**, and they feared they might need to **mortgage** valuables. Friendly locals appeared, **endorsed** their trip, and repaired the van. Near an oasis, water **rippled** as Tom took a **peek** at old ruins. Later they joined a movie set with a **celebrity**, survived a strategy **controversy**, and had to **swerve** around rocks on the way home.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

一群朋友开着**车辆**（vehicle）进入沙漠，担心遇到**土匪**（bandit）。不久后车辆性能**下滑**（slump），他们甚至考虑是否要**抵押**（mortgage）物品修车。幸好当地人出现并**支持**（endorsed）他们的旅程。绿洲里水面起了**涟漪**（rippled），汤姆去废墟**偷看**（peek）时又发现新线索。后来他们还偶遇拍片**名人**（celebrity），解决现场**争议**（controversy），并在返程时多次**急转弯**（swerve）避险。整趟旅程充满了**意外之喜**（serendipity）。
