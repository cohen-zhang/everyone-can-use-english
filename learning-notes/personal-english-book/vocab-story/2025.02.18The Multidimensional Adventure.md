# 2025.02.18The Multidimensional Adventure

## English story

One sunny day, Alex, a curious student, met his mentor (指导者) who commended (称赞) his creative ideas. "Your project needs a multidimensional (多维的) approach!" he said. Determined, Alex grabbed his kit (配套元件) containing a leather (皮革) notebook and a lipstick (口红) for his art class.

During the peak season (旺季) at school, Alex often visited the vending machine (自动售货机) to buy snacks (小吃). One day, he noticed an auction (拍卖) flyer. The prize? A mysterious parcel (包裹) from Swiss (瑞士的) with a battery (电池)-powered makeup kit (化妆包)!

Alex checked the instruction (操作指南) online. Netizens (网民) suggested studying the distribution (分布) of bids. He submitted a clever quotation (报价) and won! The parcel had a note: "Refactor (重构) this etiquette (礼仪) guide to teach robots manners."

Excited, Alex shared his success. His mentor laughed, "You’ve mastered thwacking (重击) challenges!"

One crisp autumn morning, Alex, a young inventor with a passion for robotics, hurried to meet his mentor in the bustling university lab. "Your latest design shows promise," the professor commended, adjusting his glasses. "But to win the competition, you need a multidimensional approach—combine mechanics, art, and social intelligence!"
Alex nodded, clutching his weathered leather backpack. Inside was his trusty kit: circuit boards, a battery tester, and even a lipstick he’d repurposed as a paint marker for prototypes. As the peak season for tech fairs approached, his days blurred into nights. He often relied on the campus vending machine for snacks, fueling his marathon coding sessions.
One evening, while slurping instant noodles, a flyer caught his eye: "Mystery auction! Grand prize: a Swiss engineering marvel!" Intrigued, Alex dug deeper. The item was a sleek parcel rumored to contain a makeup kit that taught AI etiquette—perfect for his robot’s "social skills" module. But winning required strategy.
He scoured forums where netizens debated bidding tactics. "Study the distribution of past winners," one user advised. Alex crunched numbers, submitted a shrewd quotation, and held his breath. Days later, an email arrived: "Congratulations! Collect your prize at Lab 3B."
The parcel was smaller than expected. Inside lay a velvet box with a note: "Refactor this guide to teach machines courtesy." Attached was a cryptic instruction manual and a tiny, battery-powered device. As Alex tinkered, sparks flew—literally. A miswired circuit caused the gadget to sputter, nearly singeing his eyebrows.
"Need a hand?" His mentor appeared, chuckling. Together, they debugged the code, blending logic with creativity. "Sometimes," the professor mused, "you must thwack obstacles sideways to see solutions."
By dawn, the AI could bow and greet visitors politely. At the competition, judges marveled at Alex’s multidimensional bot—part engineer, part artist, part diplomat. As the crowd cheered, Alex grinned, realizing innovation wasn’t just about circuits… it was about connections.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

一个秋日的清晨，年轻的发明家Alex急匆匆地去实验室见他的导师（mentor）。“你最新的设计很有潜力，”教授称赞（commend）道，“但要赢得比赛，你需要多维（multidimensional）的方法——结合机械、艺术和社交智能！”

Alex抓了抓磨旧的皮革（leather）背包，里面装着他的工具套件（kit）：电路板、电池（battery）测试仪，甚至一支被他改造成涂色笔的口红（lipstick）。科技展旺季（peak season）临近，他日夜忙碌，靠着校园自动售货机（vending machine）的零食（snack）充饥。

某天深夜，一张传单吸引了他的注意：“神秘拍卖（auction）！大奖：瑞士（Swiss）工程奇迹！”拍品是一个据说能教AI礼仪（etiquette）的化妆包（makeup kit）——完美适配他的机器人“社交模块”。但获胜需要策略。

他潜入论坛，网民（netizens）们正分析竞标分布（distribution）规律。Alex计算整晚，提交了一份精明的报价（quotation）。三天后，他颤抖着点开邮件：“恭喜！请至3B实验室领取包裹（parcel）。”

盒中只有一张纸条：“重构（refactor）这本指南，教会机器礼貌”，附着一本晦涩的操作手册（instruction）和微型电池（battery）设备。调试时，电路冒出的火花差点烧焦他的眉毛。

“要帮忙吗？”导师（mentor）笑着出现。两人重写代码，将逻辑与创意结合。“有时候，”教授说，“你得换个角度重击（thwack）问题。”

天亮时，机器人已能鞠躬问好。比赛当日，评委们惊叹于Alex多维（multidimensional）的发明——半是工程师，半是艺术家，半是外交家。掌声中，Alex忽然明白：创新不仅是电路，更是人与人的联结。
