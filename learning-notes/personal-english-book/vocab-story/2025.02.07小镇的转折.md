# The Turning Point of the Town

## English story

In quiet Greenfield, Jack looked **astute** but used his cleverness in the wrong way. He believed he controlled his **destiny** and held **contempt** for the **sheeplike** residents. At the start of a new **semester**, he spread a **fabrication**, claiming he could get high **compensation** if people paid him first.

When the factory project began, noise and pollution made life **miserable**. The river was no longer clean, and once-**fertile** land became damaged. Residents finally **blamed** Jack, but he still **claimed** the decision was **irreversible**.

A journalist named Amy made an **intervention**. With sharp **insight**, she spoke **frankly** and gave **precise** numbers of the losses. The town entered a new **epoch**. People wrote environmental slogans with **chalk**, used **common sense**, and practiced **free will** instead of blind trust. Jack's stolen money vanished like smoke from his **cigarettes**, and the town moved forward like **amphibians** adapting to a new environment.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

格林小镇里，杰克看起来很**精明**（astute），却把聪明用在欺骗上。他自认掌控**命运**（destiny），还对过于顺从的居民充满**轻视**（contempt）。新**学期**（semester）开始后，他编造**谎言**（fabrication），说只要先交钱，就能替大家争取高额**补偿**（compensation）。

工厂开建后，噪音和污染让小镇变得**悲惨**（miserable），河流浑浊，原本**肥沃**（fertile）的土地也受损。居民醒悟后纷纷**指责**（blamed）杰克，但杰克仍**声称**（claimed）一切都**无法逆转**（irreversible）。

记者艾米展开**介入调查**（intervention）。凭借敏锐**洞察力**（insight），她以**坦率**（frank）态度公布了**精确**（precise）损失数据。风波后，小镇进入新**时代**（epoch）：人们用**粉笔**（chalk）写环保标语，靠**常识**（common sense）与**自由意志**（free will）判断信息。杰克挥霍的钱像**香烟**（cigarette）烟雾般散去，小镇则像**两栖动物**（amphibians）一样完成了环境适应。
