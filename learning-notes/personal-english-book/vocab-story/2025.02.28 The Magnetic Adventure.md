# The Magnetic Adventure

## English story

In a **rural** valley, Lily found a **magnetic** compass pointing to a cave. Inside were old **equities**, one **dividend** coin, and a map over **mud** paths. A **pathetic** squirrel begged for help because a **pirate** stole its **acorn-nut**. Lily held a **lantern**, used **perfume** **spray**, and escaped when the cave shook by **clutching** a wooden **prop** and **crawling** out.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

在**乡村**（rural）山谷里，莉莉发现一枚**磁性**（magnetic）指南针，带她进入洞穴。里面有旧**股票**（equities）和一枚**股息币**（dividend）。一只**可怜的**（pathetic）松鼠求助，说宝藏被**海盗**（pirate）偷走。莉莉提着**灯笼**（lantern），喷了**香水喷雾**（perfume spray）干扰对方。洞穴塌陷时，她**抓住**（clutched）木制**支撑杆**（prop）并**爬行**（crawled）逃生。
