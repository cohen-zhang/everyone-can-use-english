# 2.8和2.9 A Noisy and Crazy Adventure

## English story

One day, **Buddy** and his friend decided to go camping in the forest. When they set up their tent and settled down for the night, they soon realized it wasn't going to be a peaceful sleep. **Buddy**'s friend started **snoring** loudly. It was like a chainsaw cutting through the quiet night. **Buddy** tried to cover his ears, but it was no use.

Fed up, **Buddy** stormed out of the tent. He was so angry that he let out a **huff**. As he was walking around, he came across a strange sight. There was a huge tree with a hole in it, and something was **spewing** out of it. At first, he thought it was just some sap, but then he realized it was a strange liquid, like **venom**.

Suddenly, a **dude** dressed in a fancy but dirty suit emerged from behind the tree. He looked a bit **insane**. He started talking nonsense and **spitting** as he spoke. **Buddy** was shocked and a little scared. This **dude** seemed to be **violating** all the rules of normal behavior.

Just then, a strong wind blew, and the **windpipe**-like sound it made added to the eerie atmosphere. The **dude** claimed that the liquid was some kind of magical potion that could **resuscitate** the dead. **Buddy** thought he had lost his **sane** mind. But the **dude** was so persistent that **Buddy** decided to play along for a while.

The **dude** then showed **Buddy** how **flexible** he was, bending and **stooping** in all sorts of odd ways. It was as if he had no bones. But as they were "experimenting" with the liquid, it started to cause **havoc** in the forest. Small animals started running around in panic, and the trees seemed to be affected too.

**Buddy** realized they had to stop this madness. He knew he couldn't let the **dude** **wreak** more chaos. Just as he was about to stop the **dude**, a bee flew by and stung the **dude**. The **dude** started to **suffocate** because he was allergic to bee stings. **Buddy** quickly called for help and managed to save the **dude** before it was too late.

After this crazy adventure, **Buddy** learned that in the wild, you never know what strange things might happen. And he also learned to be more understanding of his friend's **snoring** because it was definitely better than dealing with an **insane** **dude** in the forest!

## 中文故事（双语对照）

一场喧闹又疯狂的冒险

一天，巴迪（Buddy）和他的朋友决定去森林露营。当他们搭好帐篷，准备晚上休息时，很快就意识到这一晚不会安宁。巴迪的朋友开始大声地**打鼾**（snoring），那声音就像电锯划破宁静的夜空。巴迪试图捂住耳朵，但毫无用处。

受够了的巴迪怒气冲冲地走出帐篷，生气地**哼**了一声（huff）。他四处走动时，看到了一个奇怪的景象。有一棵大树，上面有个洞，有东西正从里面**喷涌**而出（spewing）。一开始，他以为只是一些树液，但随后发现那是一种奇怪的液体，像**毒液**（venom）一样。

突然，一个穿着华丽但脏兮兮西装的**家伙**（dude）从树后走了出来。他看起来有点**疯狂**（insane），一边说着胡话，一边说话时还**吐口水**（spitting）。巴迪既震惊又有点害怕，这个**家伙**（dude）似乎**违反**（violating）了所有正常行为的规则。

就在这时，一阵强风吹过，那声音就像**气管**（windpipe）发出的一样，增添了诡异的气氛。这个**家伙**（dude）声称这种液体是某种神奇的药水，可以**复活**（resuscitate）死人。巴迪觉得他已经失去了**理智**（sane）。但这个**家伙**（dude）太执着了，巴迪决定暂时配合他一下。

然后，这个**家伙**（dude）向巴迪展示了他有多**灵活**（flexible），以各种奇怪的方式**弯腰**屈身（stooping），就好像他没有骨头一样。但当他们在「试验」这种液体时，它开始在森林里造成**大破坏**（havoc）。小动物们惊慌失措地四处逃窜，树木似乎也受到了影响。

巴迪意识到他们必须停止这种疯狂行为。他知道不能让这个**家伙**（dude）再**引发**（wreak）更多混乱。就在他要阻止这个**家伙**（dude）时，一只蜜蜂飞过来叮了这个**家伙**（dude）一口。这个**家伙**（dude）开始**窒息**（suffocate），因为他对蜜蜂的叮咬过敏。巴迪赶紧呼救，在一切还来得及之前救了这个**家伙**（dude）。

在这次疯狂的冒险之后，巴迪明白了在野外，你永远不知道会发生什么奇怪的事情。而且他也学会了更包容朋友的**打鼾**（snoring），因为这绝对比在森林里对付一个**疯狂**（insane）的**家伙**（dude）要好得多！
