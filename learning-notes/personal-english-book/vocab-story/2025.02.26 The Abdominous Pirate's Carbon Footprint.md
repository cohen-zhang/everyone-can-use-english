# The Abdominous Pirate's Carbon Footprint

## English story

Captain Lily sailed toward an **isle** to find a hidden **trophy**. Her team measured unusual **dioxide** levels in the air. In a wreck, they met an **abdominous** parrot guarding a **carbon** chest. Pirate Pete's **selfishness** nearly sank the trip. Lily told everyone to **slice** floating wood, escape shark **pursuit**, and survive together. The chest held a mirror, and Pete learned teamwork beats greed.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

莉莉船长航向一座**小岛**（isle）寻找传说**奖杯**（trophy），并用空气中异常**二氧化物**（dioxide）作为线索。在沉船里，他们遇到一只**大肚子**（abdominous）鹦鹉守着**碳**（carbon）宝箱。海盗皮特的**自私**（selfishness）差点害了全队。关键时刻，莉莉让大家把木头**切开**（slice）做漂浮支撑，摆脱鲨鱼**追逐**（pursuit）。最终宝箱里是一面镜子，提醒他们协作比贪婪更重要。
