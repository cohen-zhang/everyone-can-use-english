# 2025.01.30 The Mysterious Medical Mission

## English story

In a big city hospital, Dr. Lily worked in the **consulting** room. She was a **neutral** person, always calm and fair. One day, a patient came in with a **weird** problem. He said he often felt **irritated** for no reason, and sometimes even got so angry that he would **slam** doors. Dr. Lily knew she had to find out what was wrong.

She **sought** help from other doctors. There was a **collegial** atmosphere in the hospital, and everyone was willing to share their knowledge. Dr. Tom, an expert in the brain, mentioned that the **amygdala** might be related to the patient's mood swings. But they needed more information.

Dr. Lily was **assigned** to do an **endoscopic** examination on the patient. It was a difficult task. During the process, she found some signs of **dysplasia** in the patient's body. This discovery made her realize the situation was more serious than she thought.

The hospital's research team started to discuss different **scenarios** for treatment. They had to follow the strict **protocol** of medical research. Dr. Lily thought they should try a new treatment method, but some doctors were worried about its **merits** and risks. Dr. Lily listened to their opinions with **humility** and then carefully explained her idea.

As they delved deeper into the research, they faced many challenges. Dr. Lily was **exhausted** from long hours of work, but she was determined to find a **cure** for the patient. One day, while looking through the patient's records, she found a **cue** - a small detail that might be the key to solving the problem.

She **compelled** herself to stay focused. With the help of the team, they **triangulated** the data from different tests and finally came up with a treatment plan. It was a **genuine** breakthrough.

During the treatment, the patient's condition improved gradually. His **crashing** mood swings became less frequent. The doctors and nurses were all happy. Dr. Lily knew that this success was due to the **untapped** potential of the team's cooperation.

After the patient recovered, he was very grateful. In a **conversation** with Dr. Lily, he said he used to be **nuts** about getting angry, but now he felt like a new person. Dr. Lily smiled, feeling proud of her work and the power of medicine.

随着研究的深入，他们面临许多挑战。长时间的工作让莉莉医生疲惫不堪（exhausted），但她决心为病人找到治愈（cure）的方法。一天，在查看病人的病历记录时，她发现了一个线索（cue）—— 一个可能是解决问题关键的小细节。
