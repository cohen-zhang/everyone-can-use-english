2025.02.13 A Day in the City

One sunny morning, yep, I decided to go on an adventure in the city. I took a different route than usual. As I walked, I saw a huge tower in the distance. It was so tall that it seemed to tower over everything else.
On my way to the tower, I passed by a construction site. There were lots of rough materials lying around. A worker was carrying some unprocessed (from "rough", meaning not processed) wood. Suddenly, a big truck drove through a puddle, and water splashed everywhere. "Ugh!" I exclaimed. "Oops," the driver said sheepishly.
When I finally reached the tower, there were people from different ethnic groups. Some were tourists taking pictures, and some were locals. I joined a group and listened to the guide. He talked about the history of the tower and how it was built on a tight budget. They had to innovate to save money while still making it a great building.
After that, I went to a park nearby. There was a small pond. A kid was playing near it, trying to make a splash with a stick. Some creatures, like ducks and fish, were in the pond. The ducks looked so cute, but the fish were a bit shy (not in the list, just to make the story more interesting).
I then met a man who seemed to be an elite in the business world. He was well - dressed and had an air of confidence. But he was also very grasping. When someone asked him for help with a small project, he only thought about how he could benefit from it.
In a science museum I visited later, I saw a comparator. It was used to compare different objects. It made me think about how we compare things in our daily lives.
As the day ended, I was tired but happy. I had a great time learning new things and seeing new places. And I even got to use a plunge pool in a hotel on my way home. It was a perfect way to end the day.

中文故事：城市中的一天
一个阳光明媚的早晨，没错（yep），我决定去城市里探险。我选了一条和往常不一样的路线（route）。走着走着，我看到远处有一座巨大的塔（tower）。它特别高，好像高耸（tower）于其他所有东西之上。
在去塔的路上，我路过一个建筑工地。周围有很多粗糙的（rough）材料。一个工人正扛着一些未经加工的（“rough”的含义，即未加工的）木材。突然，一辆大卡车驶过一个水坑，水溅（splashed）得到处都是。“啊（ugh）！”我叫了起来。“哎哟（oops），”司机不好意思地说。
当我终于到达那座塔时，那里有来自不同种族（ethnic）的人。有些是拍照的游客，有些是当地人。我加入了一个团体，听导游讲解。他谈到了这座塔的历史，以及它是如何在预算（budget）紧张的情况下建成的。为了省钱，同时又把它建成一座很棒的建筑，他们不得不创新（innovate）。
之后，我去了附近的一个公园。公园里有一个小池塘。一个小孩在池塘边玩耍，想用一根棍子弄出水花，引人注意（make a splash）。池塘里有一些生物（creatures），比如鸭子和鱼。鸭子看起来很可爱，不过鱼有点害羞（不在单词表里，只是为了让故事更有趣）。
接着，我遇到了一个看起来像是商业界精英（elite）的男人。他穿着得体，充满自信。但他也很贪婪（grasping）。当有人请他在一个小项目上帮忙时，他只想着自己能从中得到什么好处。
后来我去了一个科学博物馆，在那里我看到了一个比较器（comparator）。它是用来比较不同物体的。这让我想到在日常生活中我们是如何比较事物的。
一天结束时，我虽然很累，但很开心。我学到了新东西，看到了新地方，过得很愉快。在回家的路上，我还在一家酒店里用了跳水池（plunge pool）。这是结束这一天的完美方式。
