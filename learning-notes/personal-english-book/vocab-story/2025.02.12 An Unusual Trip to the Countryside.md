# 2025.02.12 An Unusual Trip to the Countryside

## English story

One sunny day, Tom decided to take a trip to the countryside. When he arrived at a small village, he saw an old **mill**. The **mill** looked a bit **weakened** by the years, but it still stood there firmly.
Tom walked into the village. There was a **parish** church. Some people were chatting in front of it. He heard them talking about a strange **breed** of sheep in the village. These sheep had very soft **fur**, like the best **rubber** that could be used to make erasers.
He then met a **correspondent** who was writing a story about the village. The **correspondent** told Tom that there was a **debut** of a new local product soon. It was a special kind of **saucepan** made in the village's small factory. This **saucepan** was said to be very useful for cooking all kinds of food.
As Tom walked around, he noticed a boy **poking** at a small hole with a stick. Tom went over to see what he was doing. The boy said he was trying to find a small animal that lived in the hole. Tom **grasped** the situation and decided to help him.
Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was a car coming. The driver **braked** hard to avoid hitting a dog on the road. Tom thought it was dangerous. He remembered that in the city, there were rules to **punish** people who drove carelessly.
After a while, Tom learned that the villagers were trying to **utilize** the local resources to develop the village. They wanted to make more products to **retail** in the market. They believed they could achieve **triumph** in this way. But they also faced some problems, like **discrimination** from people in big cities who thought their products were not good enough. However, the villagers were **eligible** to succeed with their hard work. They deserved fair **compensation** for their efforts.
Tom had a great time in the village. He took many pictures with his camera, using different lenses. When he got home, he told his friends about this interesting trip, **borrowing** some **rhetoric** to make the story more vivid. He also showed them some **holdings** he brought back, like a small piece of the sheep's **fur** and a sample of the local **saucepan**. He felt as if he had been in an **orbit** of a different world during this trip.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

一次不寻常的乡村之旅
在一个阳光明媚的日子里，汤姆决定去乡村旅行。当他到达一个小村庄时，他看到了一座老磨坊。这座磨坊（mill）由于岁月的侵蚀看起来有点破旧（weakened），但它仍然稳稳地矗立在那里。
汤姆走进村庄。那里有一座教区（parish）教堂。一些人在教堂前聊天。他听到他们在谈论村里一种奇特的品种（breed）的羊。这些羊有着非常柔软的毛（fur），就像最好的能用来制作橡皮擦的橡胶（rubber）一样。
然后，他遇到了一位记者（correspondent），这位记者正在写一篇关于这个村庄的报道。记者告诉汤姆，当地很快会有一款新产品首次亮相（debut）。那是一种在村里小工厂生产的特殊炖锅（saucepan）。据说这种炖锅在烹饪各种食物时都非常好用。
汤姆四处走动时，注意到一个男孩正用一根棍子戳（poke）一个小洞。汤姆走过去看看他在做什么。男孩说他在试图找到住在洞里的小动物。汤姆理解（grasped）了情况，决定帮他一起找。
突然，他们听到一阵响声。是一辆汽车开过来了。司机紧急刹车（braked），以免撞到路上的一只狗。汤姆觉得这很危险。他记得在城市里，有规定会惩罚（punish）那些粗心驾驶的人。
过了一会儿，汤姆了解到村民们正试图利用（utilize）当地资源来发展村庄。他们想生产更多的产品在市场上零售（retail）。他们相信通过这种方式可以取得成功（triumph）。但是他们也面临一些问题，比如来自大城市的人的歧视（discrimination），那些人认为他们的产品不够好。然而，村民们凭借努力工作是有资格（eligible）获得成功的。他们的努力理应得到公平的补偿（compensation）。
汤姆在村庄里度过了愉快的时光。他用相机，换上不同的镜头（lenses）拍了很多照片。回到家后，他向朋友们讲述了这次有趣的旅行，还运用了一些修辞手法（rhetoric）让故事更加生动。他还向朋友们展示了他带回来的一些纪念品（holdings），比如一小片羊毛和当地炖锅的样品。他感觉在这次旅行中，自己仿佛进入了一个不同世界的轨道（orbit）。
