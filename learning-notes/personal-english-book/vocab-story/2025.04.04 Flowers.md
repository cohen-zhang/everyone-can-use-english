# Flowers Vocabulary Notes

## English story

In spring, **Lily** visited a flower market and made a memory game. She grouped flowers by color and season: **rose**, **tulip**, and **peony** for bright colors; **lavender** and **jasmine** for fragrance; **cherry blossom** and **daffodil** for early spring.

At another booth, she found elegant **lily**, **orchid**, **violet**, **hyacinth**, and **pansy**. The seller explained that each flower can carry a feeling: **carnation** for gratitude, **sunflower** for energy, and **chrysanthemum** for autumn calm.

**Lily** wrote short bilingual examples to review daily. She found that linking each flower to a scene helped her remember both spelling and meaning.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

春天里，莉莉逛花市做了一个记忆练习。她按颜色和季节分组：**玫瑰**（rose）、**郁金香**（tulip）、**牡丹**（peony）归为明亮色；**薰衣草**（lavender）和**茉莉**（jasmine）归为香味组；**樱花**（cherry blossom）和**水仙**（daffodil）归为早春组。

另一边她还看到了**百合**（lily）、**兰花**（orchid）、**紫罗兰**（violet）、**风信子**（hyacinth）和**三色堇**（pansy）。店主说，不同花也代表不同情感：**康乃馨**（carnation）常用于表达感谢，**向日葵**（sunflower）代表活力，**菊花**（chrysanthemum）则给人秋日的平静。

莉莉把这些词写成中英小句子每天复习，发现“场景 + 花名”的方法能更快记住拼写和含义。
