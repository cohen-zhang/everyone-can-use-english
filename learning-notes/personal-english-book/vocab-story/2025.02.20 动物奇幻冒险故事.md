# Moonlight Forest Treasure

## English story

Sophie the **quokka** found a glowing map from Benjamin the **bumblebee**. She teamed up with a **flamingo**, **toucan**, **hedgehog**, and **hummingbird** to enter a cave guarded by a **rattlesnake**. Later a **platypus**, **narwhal**, and **peacock** helped unlock the crystal compass. With support from a **beaver**, **kangaroo**, **octopus**, and **jaguar**, they finally restored the treasure.

## 中文故事（双语对照）

月光下，苏菲这只**短尾矮袋鼠**（quokka）发现了熊蜂（**bumblebee**）留下的发光地图。她和**火烈鸟**（flamingo）、**巨嘴鸟**（toucan）、**刺猬**（hedgehog）与**蜂鸟**（hummingbird）一起进入洞穴，面对**响尾蛇**（rattlesnake）守卫。随后，**鸭嘴兽**（platypus）、**独角鲸**（narwhal）和**孔雀**（peacock）帮助解开机关。最后在**海狸**（beaver）、**袋鼠**（kangaroo）、**章鱼**（octopus）和**美洲豹**（jaguar）协助下，水晶罗盘被放回原处。
