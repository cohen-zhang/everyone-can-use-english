## 1. 基本面 — Fundamentals

**基本面**问的是：这家公司**赚什么钱、能不能持续赚、财务健不健康**。

- **I start with the business model — how does this company make money?** — 我先看商业模式——公司怎么赚钱？
- **What's the core product, and who pays for it?** — 核心产品是什么？谁付钱？
- **Revenue growth matters, but quality of growth matters more.** — 收入增长重要，但增长**质量**更重要。
- **Is this a cyclical business or a steady compounder?** — 这是周期股，还是能长期滚雪球的生意？
- **They have a wide moat — brand, scale, or switching costs.** — 他们有宽护城河——品牌、规模或转换成本。
- **Margins are expanding because costs are under control.** — 利润率在扩大，因为成本控得住。
- **The balance sheet is strong — low debt and plenty of cash.** — 资产负债表很强——负债低、现金充裕。
- **Free cash flow funds buybacks and dividends without borrowing.** — 自由现金流够回购和分红，不用靠举债。
- **Management execution has been consistent for five years.** — 管理层五年执行力一直稳定。
- **I'd worry if growth relies on one customer or one region.** — 若增长靠单一客户或单一地区，我会警惕。
- **Competition is heating up — pricing power may fade.** — 竞争在加剧——定价权可能减弱。
- **This is a turnaround story — fundamentals are still messy.** — 这是困境反转故事——基本面还乱。

## 2. 财报 — Financial reports & earnings

- **Earnings season starts next week — I'll watch the calendar.** — 财报季下周开始——我会盯日历。
- **They report after the bell on Thursday.** — 他们周四盘后发财报。
- **Revenue beat estimates, but EPS missed slightly.** — 收入超预期，每股收益略低于预期。
- **Guidance for next quarter looks cautious.** — 下季度指引偏谨慎。
- **Management raised full-year guidance on the call.** — 业绩会上管理层上调了全年指引。
- **Same-store sales grew eight percent year over year.** — 同店销售同比增长 8%。
- **Gross margin improved thanks to lower input costs.** — 毛利率改善，得益于投入成本下降。
- **Operating income grew faster than revenue — good leverage.** — 营业利润增速快于收入——经营杠杆不错。
- **Net income was dragged down by a one-time charge.** — 净利润被一次性费用拖累。
- **Cash flow from operations covers capex comfortably.** — 经营现金流轻松覆盖资本开支。
- **The 10-K is dense — I skim the risk factors first.** — 年报很厚——我先扫风险因素章节。
- **Read the footnotes — accounting changes can hide weakness.** — 读脚注——会计调整可能掩盖弱点。
- **They filed the annual report with the SEC last Friday.** — 上周五已向 SEC 提交年报。

**A 股脚注：** 季报/半年报/年报常对应 **quarterly / semi-annual / annual report**；业绩说明会可说 **earnings briefing** 或 **results presentation**。

## 3. 估值 — Valuation

- **The stock trades at thirty times forward earnings.** — 这只股按远期盈利算大约 30 倍市盈率。
- **P/E looks rich compared to its own history.** — 市盈率相对自身历史偏贵。
- **On a P/B basis, it's cheaper than peers.** — 按市净率，它比同行便宜。
- **EV/EBITDA helps when debt levels differ across companies.** — 负债水平不同时，用 EV/EBITDA 更公平。
- **The market is pricing in perfect execution — that's risky.** — 市场在定价「完美执行」——这很危险。
- **I think fair value is around sixty dollars per share.** — 我认为合理估值大约每股 60 美元。
- **It's trading at a premium to the sector average.** — 相对板块均价有溢价。
- **There's a discount here if you believe the turnaround.** — 若你相信反转，这里有折价。
- **DCF is sensitive to the terminal growth assumption.** — 现金流折现对永续增长率假设很敏感。
- **Don't pay a high multiple for slowing growth.** — 增长放缓时别付高估值倍数。
- **Valuation alone doesn't tell you when to buy — timing still matters.** — 单靠估值不知道何时买——时点仍重要。
- **The rerating came after two clean quarters.** — 估值重估发生在连续两季干净财报之后。

## 4. 市场与资金 — Market & capital flow

- **Volume spiked on the breakout — real money was moving.** — 突破时放量——是真资金在动。
- **Turnover is high — traders are active in this name.** — 换手率高——这只股交易活跃。
- **Institutional ownership keeps rising quarter by quarter.** — 机构持股比例逐季上升。
- **Retail flows chased the rally last week.** — 上周散户资金追涨。
- **Northbound funds bought heavily in Hong Kong listings.** — 北向资金大举买入港股标的。（A/H 语境）
- **Short interest climbed — bears are getting louder.** — 空头兴趣上升——看空声音更大。
- **The sector is rotating from growth into value.** — 板块资金从成长轮动到价值。
- **Market breadth is weak — only a few large caps are up.** — 市场广度弱——只有几只大盘股在涨。
- **Liquidity dried up during the holiday week.** — 假期周流动性枯竭。
- **Big blocks traded after hours — someone repositioned.** — 盘后有大宗成交——有人在调仓。
- **The index is up, but my watchlist is mostly red.** — 指数涨，但自选列表大多收跌。
- **Follow the flow, but don't confuse flow with fundamentals.** — 跟资金，但别把资金流和基本面混为一谈。

## 5. 消息面 — News & catalysts

- **Earnings day is the main near-term catalyst.** — 财报日是近期主要催化剂。
- **They announced a major product launch at the conference.** — 他们在发布会上宣布了重磅新品。
- **The FDA approval could unlock a new revenue stream.** — FDA 批准可能打开新收入来源。
- **M&A rumors popped the stock — nothing confirmed yet.** — 并购传闻拉高股价——尚未证实。
- **Regulatory headlines whipsawed the sector all week.** — 监管新闻整周让板块剧烈震荡。
- **An analyst upgrade moved sentiment, not just the target price.** — 分析师上调评级提振的是情绪，不只是目标价。
- **Treat social media tips as noise until there's a filing.** — 社交媒体小道消息，没公告前当噪音。
- **The CEO resignation was a negative surprise.** — CEO 辞职是负面意外。
- **Supply chain news matters more for hardware names.** — 供应链新闻对硬件股更重要。
- **Geopolitical risk is back on the radar for exporters.** — 地缘政治风险重新进入出口企业视野。
- **Buy the rumor, sell the news — old trader wisdom.** — 谣言买、新闻卖——老交易员格言。
- **I'll wait for the 8-K before I react to the headline.** — 等 8-K 公告出来再对标题反应。

## 6. 交易与风控原则 — Trading & risk principles

（与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/investing/stock-trading-investor-essentials|投资者必备]] 互补：本页偏**研究结论如何落地成纪律**。）

- **My thesis has three bullets — I write them down before I buy.** — 投资逻辑三条——买入前写下来。
- **If the thesis breaks, I sell — no heroics.** — 逻辑破了就卖——别硬扛。
- **Position size caps my downside before the stop even hits.** — 仓位大小在止损触发前就限制了亏损。
- **I don't add to a loser just because it's cheaper.** — 不会因为更便宜就给亏损仓加仓。
- **One stock should not dominate my portfolio.** — 单只股票不应主导整个组合。
- **I scale in on pullbacks, not on vertical green candles.** — 回调分批买，不追垂直阳线。
- **Cash is a position when valuations feel stretched.** — 估值拉伸时，现金也是一种仓位。
- **I set a stop or a mental exit before entry.** — 进场前就设止损或心理离场点。
- **Revenge trading after a loss is how accounts die.** — 亏损后报复性交易是账户杀手。
- **Review winners and losers the same way — no ego.** — 赢家输家同样复盘——别带 ego。
- **Liquidity and slippage are part of the trade cost.** — 流动性与滑点是交易成本的一部分。
- **No leverage on stories I can't explain in one minute.** — 一分钟说不清的故事，不加杠杆。

## 7. 宏观环境 — Macro environment

- **The Fed meeting is the macro event everyone watches.** — 美联储会议是人人盯的宏观大事。
- **Higher rates pressure high-P/E growth stocks.** — 利率上行压制高市盈率成长股。
- **Inflation cooled, but core CPI is still sticky.** — 通胀降温，但核心 CPI 仍粘性。
- **A soft landing is the consensus — don't confuse consensus with certainty.** — 软着陆是共识——别把共识当确定。
- **Recession fears spike whenever unemployment ticks up.** — 失业率一抬头，衰退担忧就升温。
- **Fiscal stimulus boosted consumption last year.** — 财政刺激去年提振了消费。
- **Tariff talk hits exporters and supply chains fast.** — 关税言论很快打击出口链。
- **The dollar strengthened — EM earnings face a headwind.** — 美元走强——新兴市场盈利遇逆风。
- **Oil prices feed into transport and inflation expectations.** — 油价影响运输成本和通胀预期。
- **Geopolitics adds a risk premium to energy and defense.** — 地缘政治给能源和国防加风险溢价。
- **Macro doesn't pick stocks — but it sets the tide.** — 宏观不选股——但定大潮方向。
- **I keep a simple macro checklist: rates, inflation, growth, policy.** — 我用简单宏观清单：利率、通胀、增长、政策。

## 8. 串联示范 · One narrative（读研报口语）

*约 2 分钟 · 泽哥口头梳理一只标的*

**Before I buy, I read the fundamentals.** Revenue still grows, but **margins** are under pressure. **Free cash flow** is positive — the **balance sheet** is not scary. **Earnings** last quarter **beat** on revenue but **missed** on **EPS**; **guidance** was soft. At **thirty times forward P/E**, **valuation** feels full unless growth reaccelerates. **Volume** picked up after the call, but **institutional** flows look mixed. The **catalyst** is next month's product launch — until then, headlines are noise. **Macro** is tougher: **rates** stay high, and **inflation** hasn't fully normalized. So I keep the **position** small, **diversify**, and **stop out** if the **thesis** breaks. **Cash** on the sidelines beats forcing a trade.

**简中：** 买入前我先读基本面。收入还在增，但利润率承压。自由现金流为正，资产负债表不算吓人。上季财报收入超预期、每股收益略逊，指引偏软。按远期盈利约 30 倍 PE，除非增长再加速，估值偏满。业绩会后成交量上来，机构资金流向不一。催化剂是下月新品发布——此前标题多是噪音。宏观更难：利率仍高，通胀未完全正常化。所以仓位小、分散持有，逻辑破了就止损；场边现金好过硬做一笔。

## 10. 一分钟小结 — One-Minute Summary
