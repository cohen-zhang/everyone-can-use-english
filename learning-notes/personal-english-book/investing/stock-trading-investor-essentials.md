## 1. 账户与标的 — Account & symbols

- **I opened a brokerage account last month.** — 我上个月开了证券账户。
- **My broker app shows all my holdings in one place.** — 券商 App 把我所有持仓集中显示。
- **What's the ticker for that company?** — 那家公司的股票代码是什么？
- **I bought ten shares of Apple.** — 我买了十股苹果股票。
- **I added three names to my watchlist.** — 我把三只股票加进了自选列表。
- **I keep most of my money in index funds.** — 我大部分钱放在指数基金里。
- **This stock is too expensive for a full share.** — 这只股票太贵，买不起一整股。
- **I'll check the symbol before I place the order.** — 下单前我先核对代码。

---

## 2. 下单操作 — Placing orders

- **I'm going to buy the dip — but only a small slice.** — 我想逢低买一点，但只买一小笔。
- **I'll sell half my position if it hits my target.** — 到目标价我就卖出一半仓位。
- **Place a market order if you need to get in fast.** — 要快速进场就下市价单。
- **I set a limit order at forty-five dollars.** — 我在 45 美元挂了限价单。
- **My stop-loss is five percent below my entry.** — 止损设在买入价下方 5%。
- **The order filled in under a second.** — 订单不到一秒就成交了。
- **I canceled the order before the market opened.** — 开盘前我把订单撤了。
- **Good till canceled — I'll leave the bid there.** — 撤单前有效，我就把买单挂着。
- **I don't chase green candles; I wait for my price.** — 我不追阳线，我等自己的价位。

---

## 3. 报价与盘口 — Quotes & liquidity

- **The bid is forty-nine eighty; the ask is fifty.** — 买一 49.80，卖一 50.00。
- **The spread is pretty wide on this small cap.** — 这只小盘股买卖价差挺大。
- **Check the live quote before you click buy.** — 点买入前先看实时报价。
- **Volume is thin today — be careful with market orders.** — 今天成交量很薄，市价单要小心。
- **There's enough liquidity to exit without moving the price.** — 流动性够，平仓不太会冲击价格。
- **The stock gapped up at the open.** — 这只股开盘跳空高开。
- **Pre-market trading looks volatile.** — 盘前交易看起来很波动。
- **After-hours moves don't always stick at the open.** — 盘后涨跌不一定能延续到开盘。

---

## 4. 盈亏与仓位 — P/L & positions

- **I'm long this name — I'm betting it goes up.** — 我做多这只股，赌它上涨。
- **My position size is only two percent of my portfolio.** — 这笔仓位只占组合 2%。
- **My cost basis is around thirty-two dollars a share.** — 我的成本价大约每股 32 美元。
- **I'm up twelve percent on paper — unrealized gain.** — 账面浮盈 12%，还没兑现。
- **I took a realized loss and moved on.** — 我认了实亏，然后翻篇了。
- **Today's P/L is ugly, but I'm still within my plan.** — 今天盈亏难看，但我还在计划内。
- **I trimmed the winner and let the rest run.** — 我减仓了赚钱的那部分，剩下的继续拿。
- **I'm averaging down — maybe that's a mistake.** — 我在补仓摊低成本——也许这是个错。

---

## 5. 行情用语 — Market moves

- **We're in a bull market until proven otherwise.** — 在证伪之前，先当牛市看。
- **Bear markets test your patience and your cash.** — 熊市考验耐心，也考验现金。
- **The index rallied three days in a row.** — 指数连涨三天。
- **I'm waiting for a pullback, not a breakout chase.** — 我等回调，不追突破。
- **A ten percent correction is normal in stocks.** — 股票跌一成很常见，算正常调整。
- **That headline triggered a flash crash in minutes.** — 那条新闻几分钟内引发闪崩。
- **Earnings beat expectations, and the stock popped.** — 业绩超预期，股价跳涨。
- **The P/E looks rich at these levels.** — 在这个价位市盈率偏贵。

---

## 6. 风控与纪律 — Risk & discipline

- **I sized the trade so a full stop-out won't hurt me.** — 仓位小到止损全出也伤不到我。
- **I diversify across sectors — not just tech.** — 我跨行业分散，不只押科技。
- **Know your risk tolerance before you click buy.** — 点买入前先清楚自己能承受多大风险。
- **Don't chase the rally with money you need next month.** — 别拿下个月要用的钱去追涨势。
- **I keep cash on the sidelines for better entries.** — 我留现金在场边，等更好的买点。
- **No trade is worth blowing up your account.** — 没有任何一笔交易值得让你爆仓。
- **I review my rules every Sunday night.** — 我每周日晚复盘交易规则。
- **A dividend stock helps me stay patient.** — 股息股能让我更有耐心拿住。

## 8. 一分钟小结 — One-Minute Summary

- **Account** — 开户、代码、持仓、自选。 — 先搞清账户与标的。
- **Orders** — 市价、限价、止损、成交、撤单。 — 会下单比会选股更重要。
- **Quotes** — 买卖价、价差、量、流动性。 — 薄量市场别乱市价追。
- **P/L** — 成本、浮盈、实亏、仓位比例。 — 用仓位管理保护本金。
- **Market** — 牛熊、反弹、回调、突破、暴跌。 — 有词才能跟行情对话。
- **Discipline** — 分散、风险承受、不追涨、留现金。 — 纪律比灵感更赚钱。
