## What do you do?

**— What sort of work do you do?**

**—** I’m a **stock and commodity broker**. I work for a **securities company**.

---

## Personal (single / joke)

**—** Are you single?

**—** Yes, I’m single. Ladies… *‘山东狗’*（外教口误）

**—** Yeah, *‘单身狗’*.

**—** What did I say?（问摄影师）

**—** You said *‘山东狗’*…

**—** Oh, my god.

---

## How did you get into the industry?

**—** How did you get into the industry?

**—** I started to learn how to do **stock trading** by myself, during the time when I was still at **university**. Just because of **luck** I earned a lot of money.

**—** Did you invest much?

**—** I started from, like, **100,000 RMB**.

**—** How much did you earn?

**—** Like **20 times**. I picked up the right horse, and took the train — but you know, it’s also very **risky**.

**—** I know. I realise it right now. I could have lost all of it in a couple of days.

**—** I realise that if I really want to keep doing this, I have to **learn more**. I have to read books, I have to talk to more people, and I have to, you know, **improve myself**.

---

## Background & plans

I was born in **1995**; I’m just **24 years old**.

So I want to learn more things to make myself more **valuable**, and then I can **sell myself** with a better price in the future.

I want to help people **stop making** the same **wrong** things I did when I was in university.
