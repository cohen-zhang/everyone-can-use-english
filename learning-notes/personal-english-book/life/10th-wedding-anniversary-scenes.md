## Anniversary Milestones & Reflections | 周年里程碑与反思

**Spoken English**

Time flies.  
A little while ago, my wife told me— our tenth wedding anniversary is coming up.  
And I'm just... wow.

Ten years.  
That's ten times three-sixty-five days.  


时间过的真快，前段时间我妻子和我说马上就是我们的结婚十周年；我靠。10 年了，一共是 10×365 天。

You still look gorgeous.  
People still mistake you for a student.

你颜值还是那么在线，很多人误以为你还是学生。

Your students adore you.  
If I were in your class— I'd adore you too.

你的学生喜欢你。如果我是你的学生，我也会喜欢你。

I've never, never gotten tired of holding your hand,  
or kissing you.

我从来、从来没有厌倦牵你的手、吻你。

If I'm gonna pick a number— I'm pretty sure—  
for years now, I've initiated at least one kiss a day, on average.  
So that's...  
well, over three thousand, six hundred and fifty kisses.  
Ten years.

如果要说一个数字，我有信心，在过去的时间里，我至少有主动平均给你每天一个吻，那 10 年就是超过 3650 个吻！

## Daily Sweet Nothings & Romantic Scenes | 日常情话与浪漫场景

**Spoken English**

I love how your smile curves—  
how the corners of your lips go up when you see me.

我喜欢你微笑的弧度 — 当你看到我时，嘴角上扬的样子。

We argue sometimes.  
But you're always— always— the easy one to comfort.  
Get you something tasty.  
Give you a hug first.  
And things get better.

我们有时会吵架，但你总是、永远是好哄的那一个。给你买点好吃的、主动给你一个拥抱，一切都会好起来。

I love watching you focus at work during the day.  
At night I love holding you from behind.  
In the morning I love waking up to your scent.

我喜欢白天看着你专注工作的样子，喜欢晚上从背后抱着你睡觉，早上闻着你的香味。

And yeah—  
in your work clothes—  
you look perfect.  
You work so hard.  
You're amazing at what you do.

当然，当你穿上职业装也很完美，你在工作上很努力、很出色。

We've grown together.  
We've laughed together.  
We've faced challenges together.  
And today—  
I choose you again.

我们一起成长。我们一起欢笑。我们一起面对挑战。今天我再次选择你。

For our future—  
let's make the next ten years even better.  
More trips.  
More memories.  
More love.

为了我们的未来：让下一个十年更好。更多旅行。更多回忆。更多爱。

## Scenes with Our Child (不多 / Celine) | 和孩子一起的场景

Use short, repetitive English to teach gratitude and family love on this special day.

- **Mommy works hard. Daddy loves Mommy. We are lucky. Say thank you to Mommy.**
- **Today is special. It is Mommy and Daddy's anniversary. Ten years. Big number. Happy day.**
- **Look at the cake. It is sweet. Like our family. Mommy is beautiful. Daddy is happy.**
- **We can draw a picture. For Mommy. For 10 years. You draw a heart. I draw a heart. Good job.**

**Tie in animal vocabulary for fun (child-friendly short descriptions):**

**The lioness protects her family. Mommy is like the lioness. She is strong. She is brave.**

母狮保护她的家庭。妈妈就像母狮。她很强壮。她很勇敢。

**The bear hugs his cubs. Daddy gives big hugs. Like the bear.**

熊抱它的幼崽。爸爸给大大的拥抱。就像那只熊。

**The birds build a nest together. We have our home. Ten years strong.**

鸟儿一起筑巢。我们有我们的家。十年坚固。

This combines parenting English with anniversary celebration — perfect for family time.

## 启蒙句式 for Child Scenes | 孩子场景启蒙句式 (不多 / Celine)

描述时尽量用**短句、重复结构**（例如 *It is big. It lives in Africa. It has a long nose.*），孩子听得懂、跟得上，就是在对话里做英语启蒙。

**Anniversary examples with simple repetition (use during family celebration):**

**It is our anniversary. It is ten years. We are happy. Mommy is beautiful. Daddy loves Mommy.**

这是我们的周年纪念日。已经十年了。我们很幸福。妈妈很漂亮。爸爸爱妈妈。

**Mommy is strong. She works hard. She takes care of us. She is like a lioness.**

妈妈很坚强。她工作很努力。她照顾我们。她像一头母狮。

**Daddy is kind. He gives hugs. He plans surprises. He is like a big bear.**

爸爸很善良。他给拥抱。他准备惊喜。他像一头大熊。

**We are a family. We laugh together. We eat cake. It is special day.**

我们是一家人。我们一起笑。我们吃蛋糕。今天很特别。

Use these short, repeatable patterns when talking to **不多** (Celine) about the 10th anniversary. Point to photos, gifts, or animals in books to make it fun and memorable.

## Modern Family Inspired Compliments | 《摩登家庭》启发夸奖句 (S01)

**一句定调（推荐优先记）**

| English | 简中 | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| **I love you.** | 我爱你。 | Core phrase - say often |
| **Don't know how I got so lucky.** | 不知道自己怎么这么幸运。 | Perfect for 10 years reflection |
| **You are the best wife ever!** | 你是史上最棒的老婆！ | Direct anniversary compliment |
| **I could not love you any more than I do right now.** | 我现在对你的爱已经到顶了。 | Emotional peak for milestone |
| **Hey, there's my beautiful wife.** | 嘿，我美丽的老婆来了。 | Warm daily greeting |

**More Phil/Jay style expressions (adapt names to your wife):**

- **Claire, consider this a grand gesture of my love.** — 克莱尔，把这当作我对你爱的盛大表示。（Use for gift or surprise party. Replace with her name or **Honey**）
- **Buckle your seat belt, lady. You're being wooed.** — 系好安全带，女士。我在追你。（Playful for anniversary date night）
- **You're so beautiful.** / **That's ridiculous! You're beautiful.** — 你太美了。/ 胡说，你美翻了。
- **I love your smile. I love your strength. I love our life together.** — 我爱你的笑容。我爱你的坚强。我爱我们一起的生活。（Enriched repetition for 10 years）
- **Happy 10th anniversary, gorgeous. You still look as gorgeous as ever.** — 十周年快乐，美女。你还是那么光彩照人。

**使用提示**：Change character names to **Honey**, **Sweetheart**, or your wife's preferred term. These work great in private or with family toast.
