## 5. 天气预警（雷雨大风 / 暴雨）

### 中文

**【管家播报 · 天气提醒】**🌧  

📢 小管温馨提醒各位邻居：  
根据深圳市气象台 2026 年 3 月 30 日 09 时 00 分发布的信息，全市雷雨大风黄色预警已升级为**橙色**，**暴雨黄色预警**同时生效。受强对流天气影响，将出现强雷雨，局地阵风可达 10 级，可能出现小冰雹。

- ☂ 暴雨时请关好门窗，防止雨水入户造成地板、家具受损或人身风险。  
- ☂ 外出携带雨具，在安全处躲避强对流天气。  
- ☂ 及时收取阳台衣物；勿将花盆或杂物放在窗台、阳台外沿，防高空坠物。  
- ☂ 雷电时请及时关闭家电电源，防雷击损坏。  
- ☂ 建议减少外出；雨中行车减速，**积水较深勿强行涉水**，注意车距。您的安全，我们始终关心[爱心]  

如需帮助请联系专属管家或 **24 小时监控中心：0755-84622975**。

### English

**[Housekeeper · weather alert]** 🌧  

📢 A friendly reminder for all neighbors:  
Per the Shenzhen Meteorological Observatory (issued **09:00, March 30, 2026**), the citywide **orange** warning for thunderstorms and strong winds is in effect (upgraded from yellow), and the **yellow** heavy-rain warning also applies. Expect **strong thunderstorms**, local gusts up to **force 10**, and possible **small hail**.

- ☂ In heavy rain, **close windows and doors** to keep water out and protect floors, furniture, and safety.  
- ☂ Take **rain gear** when going out; seek **safe shelter** from severe convection.  
- ☂ **Bring in** laundry; do **not** place pots or clutter on outer sills or balconies (**falling-object risk**).  
- ☂ During lightning, **switch off** appliances to reduce surge damage.  
- ☂ **Limit** non-essential trips; **slow down** in rain; **do not drive through deep water**; keep a safe following distance. Your safety is our priority.[爱心]  

For help, contact your housekeeper or the **24-hour control center: 0755-84622975**.

*说明：文中预警等级、日期为示例素材；实际转发请以气象台当日通报为准。*
