- 每次复习 15–25 词：先看 `Word` 回忆中文，再读 `示例句` 跟读，最后扫 `解释（含笔记）` 补搭配。

- `No.` 保留原 PDF 编号，便于回查来源；组内按**身体动线 / 逻辑链**重排。

## 分类短文 · 串联记忆（每类一篇）

### A. 头脸五官 — 照镜子路线：从上往下点名

**English**

Zege stands at the mirror and starts at the top.
He raises one **eyebrow**, then checks each **eyelid**.
The doctor once checked his **pupil** size after a late night.
A **tear** shows up when he laughs too hard — not from sadness.
He touches his ear **lobe**, then pats his **cheek** and **chin**.
His **jaw** feels tight from coding, so he loosens it.
He trims his **mustache** and **beard**; even a cat's **whiskers** look neater than his morning face.
He sticks out his **tongue**, clears his **throat**, and remembers swollen **tonsils** from last winter.

**简中**

阿泽站在镜子前，从上往下点名。
先挑挑 **eyebrow**，再看两侧 **eyelid**。
医生加班后查过他的 **pupil**。
笑太狠也会滚出一滴 **tear**——不一定是难过。
摸摸耳 **lobe**，拍拍 **cheek** 和 **chin**。
写代码写到 **jaw** 发紧，就松开一点。
修修 **mustache** / **beard**；连猫的 **whiskers** 都比他的晨脸整齐。
