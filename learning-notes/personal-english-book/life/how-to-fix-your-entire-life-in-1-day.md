## 一句话 How it works in one day

You do not fix a life by stacking more resolutions. — 你不是靠再立几条新年决心来修好人生。

You spend **one full day** making unconscious goals visible, then you walk into tomorrow as a slightly different person. — 你用 **一整天** 把无意识目标摊开，再以稍微不同的身份走进明天。

Protocol shape — 协议形状：

| 时段 | 英文 | 简中 |
|------|------|------|
| Morning | **Psychological excavation** — anti-vision + minimum viable vision | 心理挖掘：反愿景 + 最小可行愿景 |
| Daytime | **Interrupting autopilot** — random phone reminders | 打断自动驾驶：随机闹钟提问 |
| Evening | **Synthesis** — 1-year / 1-month / daily **lenses** | 综合：一年 / 一月 / 每日透镜 |

You need a pen, paper, and an uncomfortable degree of honesty. Do not outsource the morning questions to AI. — 一支笔、一张纸、以及让人不舒服的诚实。早晨那组问题不要丢给 AI。

## 核心摘录 Key excerpts

> **If you want a specific outcome in life, you must have the lifestyle that creates that outcome long before you reach it.**
>
> 你若想要某种结果，必须先过上会**长出**那种结果的生活方式——远在你真正到达之前。

> **Trust only movement. Life happens at the level of events, not of words.** — Alfred Adler
>
> 只信任行动。人生发生在事件层面，不在口头层面。

> **The only real test of intelligence is if you get what you want out of life.** — Naval Ravikant
>
> 智力的唯一真正检验，是你能否从人生里拿到自己想要的东西。

> **Changing who you are so that your behavior naturally follows** is first-order. Changing actions while the old identity stays put is second-order — and it usually collapses.
>
> **先成为那种人，行为才会自然跟上**，这是一阶。旧身份不动、只改动作，是二阶——通常撑不过几周。

## 七个想法 Seven ideas（学习摘要）

### I — You are not that person yet（你还不是会到达那里的那种人）

Most resolutions only train **actions**. Lasting change trains **identity**, so the new behavior feels natural rather than forced. — 多数决心只练**行动**。持久改变练的是**身份**，让新行为像本能，而不是硬撑。

A bodybuilder does not “grind” to eat well; eating poorly feels like the grind. A founder does not hype themselves to lead; lying in bed past the alarm feels wrong. — 健美选手不是靠硬扛才吃得健康；吃得差才像在硬扛。创始人不是靠给自己打气才去带队；赖床反而别扭。

If you cannot wait to “enjoy life again” after the diet, you never adopted the lifestyle. The old gravity wins, and you waste **time** — the resource you cannot get back. — 若你只盼减完肥再「重新享受生活」，说明生活方式从未换过。旧引力会赢，浪费的是**时间**——唯一要不回来的资源。

When the new standard is real, habits that do not **move the needle** start to feel disgusting. You tolerate the current standard because you have not fully seen where it compounds. — 新标准一旦成真，那些**不推动指针**的习惯会开始让你恶心。你能忍受现状，是因为还没看清它会复利成什么样。

### II — Your behavior already reveals the real goal（行为已经泄露了真正目标）

All behavior is **goal-oriented**, even when the goal is unconscious. — 一切行为都是**目标导向**的，哪怕目标藏在无意识里。

Procrastination is often not “I lack discipline.” It can be a goal: protect yourself from judgment after you finish and share the work. — 拖延常常不是「我缺纪律」。它本身可能是一个目标：保护自己，免得做完、发出去之后被评判。

Staying in a **dead-end job** while saying you want risk can be a goal of safety, predictability, and an excuse not to look like a failure. — 口头想冒险、人却留在**没前途的工作**里，真正目标可能是安全、可预期，以及「看起来不像失败者」的借口。

A **goal** here is a projection into the future that acts as a **lens of perception**. Change the lens, and you start noticing different information. Surface-level goal-setting often still serves a hidden, harmful goal. — 这里的**目标**是投射到未来的**感知透镜**。换透镜，你才会看见不同信息。表层立 flag，往往仍在服务一个有害的隐性目标。

### III — Identity defends itself（身份会自卫）

Anatomy of identity（身份形成回路，摘要）：

1. You want a goal. — 你想达成一个目标。
2. You perceive reality through that lens. — 你透过该透镜看现实。
3. You notice only “important” information (**learning**). — 你只注意到「重要」信息（**学习**）。
4. You act and get feedback. — 你行动并得到反馈。
5. The behavior becomes automatic (**conditioning**). — 行为自动化（**条件反射 / 条件作用**）。
6. It becomes “I am the type of person who…” — 变成「我就是那种人……」
7. You **defend** the identity for psychological consistency. — 为了心理一致，你**捍卫**这个身份。
8. Identity shapes new goals; the cycle restarts. — 身份再塑造新目标，循环重启。

The break has to happen between 6 and 7. The loop starts in childhood: survival, then conforming to parents, then surviving at the **ideological** level — you protect and reproduce your mind the way a body protects itself. Challenge the identity, and **fight or flight** shows up as stress. Echo chambers follow. — 必须在第 6–7 步之间打断。循环从童年就开始：生存 → 迎合父母 → 在**观念**层面求生——你像保护身体一样保护并繁殖自己的心智。身份被挑战时，**战或逃**会以压力出现。回音室随后跟上。

### IV — The life you want sits at a level of mind（想要的生活对应一种心智层级）

The mind moves through stages (Maslow, ego development, Spiral Dynamics; Koe’s Human 3.0). A short 80/20 of **ego development** — 心智按可预测阶段演化。自我发展九阶（80/20）：

| Stage | 简中 | 记忆钩 |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Impulsive** | 冲动 | 感受 = 行为（幼儿生气就打人） |
| **Self-protective** | 自我保护 | 世界危险；学会撒谎、报喜不报忧 |
| **Conformist** | 遵从 | 群体规则 = 现实本身 |
| **Self-aware** | 自我觉察 | 内心与外表开始分裂 |
| **Conscientious** | 尽责 | 自建原则并自我问责 |
| **Individualist** | 个体化 | 看见原则也被语境塑造，握得更松 |
| **Strategist** | 策略 | 操作系统，同时看见自己的盲点 |
| **Construct-aware** | 建构觉察 | 框架与身份都是有用的虚构；地图不是领土 |
| **Unitive** | 一体 | 工作 / 休息 / 玩不再割裂 |

Most readers sit somewhere between 4 and 8. Moving through any stage follows a pattern; the protocol is meant to create that pattern on purpose. — 多数读者落在 4–8 阶之间。跨阶有规律；协议就是故意制造这段规律。

### V — Intelligence is steering toward what you want（智力 = 把人生舵到你想去的地方）

Success ingredients: **agency** + **opportunity** + **intelligence**. Privilege talk often hides the other two. — 成功配方：**能动性** + **机会** + **智力**。把一切说成特权，常常是在掩盖另外两味。

**Cybernetics** (Greek *kybernetikos*, “good at steering”) is the art of getting what you want: have a goal → act → sense → compare → act again. Intelligence is the system’s ability to **iterate** and persist. Quitting at the first roadblock is the mark of low intelligence in this frame. — **控制论**（希腊语「善于掌舵」）就是得到想要之物的技艺：有目标 → 行动 → 感知 → 比较 → 再行动。智力是系统**迭代**并坚持的能力。一碰到路障就停，在这个框架里就是低智力。

Ideas are **hierarchical**. You cannot go from papyrus to Google Docs **in one fell swoop**. Even an “impossible” goal is often a missing-resource problem on a long enough timescale. — 想法是**分层**的。你无法**一蹴而就**从纸草跳到 Google 文档。所谓不可能，常常只是时间尺度上还缺资源。

**Teleology**: everything serves a purpose; goals decide what counts as success or failure. Enjoying “the journey” does not work if the goal itself is wrong. The mind is an OS made of goals — often assigned: school, job, retire at 65. To get smarter: reject the known path, set higher goals, become a **deep generalist**. — **目的论**：万物皆有所为；目标决定何谓成败。目标选错，「享受过程」也享受不了。心智是由目标写成的操作系统——常常是被写入的：上学、上班、65 岁退休。要更聪明：拒绝已知路径，设更高目标，做**深度通才**。

### VI — One-day launch protocol（一天启动协议）

Successful identity flips often follow three phases: **dissonance** (fed up) → **uncertainty** (experiment or get lost) → **discovery** (years of progress in months). The day is designed to walk that arc. — 身份翻转常走三阶段：**失调**（受够了）→ **不确定**（试验或迷路）→ **发现**（几个月走完几年的进度）。这一天就是故意走完这段弧。

Koe cannot guarantee it hits everyone: the climax cannot sit on page one of your story. — 他无法保证对每个人都有效：高潮不能硬塞在你故事的第一页。

#### Part 1 · Morning — Vision & anti-vision

Spend 15–30 minutes. Write by hand. — 手写 15–30 分钟。

**Pain / anti-vision prompts（反愿景，意译练习题）：**

- What dull dissatisfaction have you learned to **tolerate**? — 你已经学会**忍受**的那种钝痛式不满是什么？
- What do you **complain** about but never change? Name three. — 你反复**抱怨**却从不改的事？列出三件。
- If someone watched your **behavior** (not your words), what would they say you actually want? — 若有人只看你的**行为**不听你的话，会认为你真正想要什么？
- What truth about your life would be **unbearable** to admit to someone you respect? — 有哪句关于现状的真话，对你敬重的人说出会**难以承受**？
- If nothing changes for **five years**, describe an average Tuesday in painful detail. Then **ten years**. Then the end of a **safe** life: what did you never let yourself feel, try, or become? — 若五年毫无改变，用令人难受的细节写一个普通周二。再写十年。再写安全版人生的终点：你从未允许自己感受、尝试或成为什么？
- Who is already living that future, 5–20 years ahead on the same **trajectory**? — 谁已经活在那条未来里，在同一**轨迹**上领先 5–20 年？
- What identity would you have to **give up**? What would it cost socially? — 你必须**放弃**哪个身份？社交上要付什么代价？
- What is the most **embarrassing** reason you have not changed? — 你还没改变的最**丢人**的理由是什么？
- If the current behavior is **self-protection**, what is it protecting — and what is that protection costing? — 若当前行为是**自我保护**，它在保护什么——这份保护又让你付出什么？

A truthful pass should leave **dis-ease** or disgust. Point that energy at a **minimum viable vision**: — 写得够真，应感到**不适**甚至厌恶。把这股能量对准**最小可行愿景**：

- Snap your fingers: a Tuesday in **three years**, same level of detail — not “realistic,” what you actually want. — 打个响指：三年后的一个周二，细节同等——不问「现实」，只问你真正想要的。
- What would you have to **believe** about yourself for that life to feel natural? Write: “I am the type of person who…” — 要让那种生活显得自然而非硬撑，你必须**相信**自己是怎样的人？写下身份句。
- What is **one thing** that person would do **this week**? — 那种人**本周**会做的**一件事**是什么？

#### Part 2 · Daytime — Break autopilot

Journaling is cute; patterns do not break unless you **force a pattern break**. Set random reminders: — 日记很可爱；不**强迫打断模式**，模式不会自己断。设随机提醒：

| 建议时刻 | 英文提问 | 简中 |
|----------|----------|------|
| 11:00 | What am I **avoiding** right now by doing this? | 我此刻做这件事，是在**回避**什么？ |
| 13:30 | If someone filmed the last two hours, what would they conclude I want? | 若有人拍下过去两小时，会认为我想要怎样的人生？ |
| 15:15 | Am I moving toward the life I **hate** or the life I want? | 我在走向自己**厌恶**的生活，还是想要的生活？ |
| 17:00 | What’s the most important thing I’m **pretending** isn’t important? | 哪件最重要的事，我在**假装**它不重要？ |
| 19:30 | What did I do today out of **identity protection** rather than genuine desire? | 今天哪些事是出于**身份保护**，而非真心想做？ |
| 21:00 | When did I feel most **alive**? When most dead? | 今天何时最有**活力**？何时最像死了一样？ |

Extra prompts on a commute or a walk: — 通勤 / 走路加问：

- What would change if I stopped needing people to see me as [old identity]? — 若我不再需要别人把我看成「旧身份」，会改变什么？
- Where am I trading **aliveness** for safety? — 我在哪里用**活力**换安全？
- What’s the **smallest** version of the person I want that I could be tomorrow? — 我想成为的那种人，明天能先当上的**最小版本**是什么？

#### Part 3 · Evening — Synthesize

- After today, what feels most true about **why you’ve been stuck**? — 今天过后，关于「你为何卡住」哪一句最真？
- Name the **actual enemy**: an internal pattern, not circumstances or other people. — 点名**真正的敌人**：内在模式，不是环境或别人。
- One sentence: the life you **refuse**. That is compressed anti-vision. — 一句话：你**拒绝**过成什么样。这是压缩的反愿景。
- One sentence: what you are **building toward** (it will evolve). Vision MVP. — 一句话：你在**朝什么建造**（它会演化）。愿景 MVP。

Treat goals as **lenses**, not finish lines. Enjoyment lives in **progress**. — 把目标当**透镜**，不当终点线。享受住在**进展**里。

| Lens | 英文 | 简中 |
|------|------|------|
| 1-year | What must be true in one year to know the **old pattern is broken**? One concrete thing. | 一年后必须成立的一件具体事，用来证明**旧模式已断**。 |
| 1-month | What must be true in one month so the year lens stays possible? | 一个月后必须怎样，一年透镜才仍可能？ |
| Daily | **Timeblock** 2–3 actions tomorrow that the future person would simply do. | 明天**时间块**里放 2–3 个「未来的你会直接去做」的动作。 |

### VII — Turn your life into a video game（把人生做成游戏）

Csikszentmihalyi: order in consciousness when attention meets a realistic goal and skill matches opportunity — **flow**. — 心流：注意力投入现实目标，技能与机会匹配时，意识里出现秩序。

Write six components on a fresh page: — 新开一页写下六件套：

| Component | Game metaphor | 简中 |
|-----------|---------------|------|
| **Anti-vision** | What’s at **stake** if you lose | 反愿景：输了或放弃会怎样 |
| **Vision** | How you **win** (until the game evolves) | 愿景：如何算赢（游戏会演化） |
| **1-year goal** | The **mission** / sole priority | 一年目标：主线任务 |
| **1-month project** | The **boss fight** — XP and loot | 一月项目：Boss 战，涨经验、拿装备 |
| **Daily levers** | The **quests** — needle-moving tasks | 每日杠杆：每日任务，推动指针 |
| **Constraints** | The **rules** — what you will not sacrifice | 约束：规则；你不愿牺牲什么 |

These sit as **concentric** circles, a **forcefield** against shiny-object distractions. Play long enough and the game becomes who you are. — 它们像**同心**圆，形成挡住亮闪闪干扰的**力场**。玩得够久，游戏就会变成你是谁。

## 阿泽可落地的一天（可选）

把协议接到现有笔记，而不是另起一套空 flag：

1. **Morning**：手写 anti-vision / vision（不要丢给 AI）。身份句可以对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/parenting-self-talk-phrases|家长自说自话]]。
2. **Daytime**：闹钟提问；通勤对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/one-minute-drill/a-weekday|工作日的一天]]。
3. **Evening**：2–3 个 timeblock；专注块对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/focus-notes|专注时间]]。
4. **Constraints**：写清不愿牺牲什么（睡眠、陪 Celine / 不多、健康），避免「愿景」变成自我剥削。
