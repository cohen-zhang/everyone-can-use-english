# 餐厅常见物品 · Dining area（深圳商品房）

**索引：** [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/README|个人英文材料书索引]]

**场景短文（约 2 分钟跟读）：** [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/shenzhen-apartment-home/整合#4-餐厅--dining-area|整合 · 餐厅篇]]（与此页相向互链）

## 本页在做什么

面向**深圳典型商品房**里「餐客一体」或**独立餐厅**：餐桌、餐椅、餐边收纳与桌面摆设的英文词，配 **IPA（英 / 美）** 和简短**介绍句**；文末补充**准备就餐 → 就餐互动 → 餐后收拾**整句练习。与客厅词汇互补；自述户型、请客摆桌或选购家具时用得上。不含门牌与隐私。客厅休闲区用语见同目录 [living-room-common-items-en.md](living-room-common-items-en.md)。

## 场景句子 · 准备就餐

- **Could you help set the table? We need bowls, chopsticks, and spoons.**  
能帮忙摆桌吗？要碗、筷子和勺。
- **Dinner’s almost ready—wash your hands and take your seats.**  
饭快好了——先去洗手，再入座。
- **I’ll lay the placemats. Could you fill the pitcher with cold water?**  
我来铺餐垫。谁能把大水壶装满凉水？（可对 **泽哥** / 家人说）
- **The rice is steaming—grab the serving spoons from the sideboard drawer.**  
米饭冒热气了——从餐边柜抽屉拿公勺。
- **Turn on the pendant light and switch the ceiling fan to low—it’s sticky tonight.**  
打开餐桌吊灯，吊扇调到低档，今晚有点闷。
- **Let’s move the pedestal fan closer to the dining area before the soup arrives.**  
汤端上来前把落地扇挪近就餐区一点。

## 场景句子 · 吃饭时家人互动

- **Pass the lazy Susan slowly—don’t spill the soup.**  
转盘慢慢转，别把汤晃出来。
- **Try one bite of the broccoli; you can dip it in the sauce.**  
西兰花尝一小口，可以蘸酱吃。
- **No phones at the table—tell us about school. How was your day, Celine?**  
吃饭别看手机啦——说说学校的事吧，Celine，今天怎么样？
- **Thank you for cooking; this fish tastes amazing.**  
谢谢你下厨，这鱼特别好吃。
- **Please chew with your mouth closed and don’t talk with your mouth full.**  
嘴巴闭上嚼东西，嘴里有食物先别说话。
- **Would you like some more rice? I can spoon it with the serving spoon.**  
还要饭吗？我用公勺帮你舀。
- **The pedestal fan is noisy on high—should we tilt it toward the wall?**  
落地扇开大了有点吵——要不要把头仰过去对着墙？

## 场景句子 · 吃完收拾

- **Let’s clear the plates and stack them carefully by the sink.**  
咱们收盘子，在水槽边小心摞好。
- **Leftovers go into food containers—we’ll pack lunch tomorrow.**  
剩菜装进保鲜盒，明天带便当。
- **Could you wipe the table with a damp cloth and shake the crumbs into the bin?**  
用湿抹布擦桌子，把屑抖进垃圾桶行吗？
- **I’ll rinse if you load the dishwasher.**  
你来放进洗碗机的话，我来冲一遍。
- **Fold the napkins and put them back in the sideboard.**  
餐巾叠好收回餐边柜。
- **Turn off the ceiling fan and roll the pedestal fan back to the corner.**  
关掉吊扇，落地扇推回角落。
- **Who wants to dry the glasses while I put away the chopsticks?**  
谁愿意擦玻璃杯？我来收筷子。

## 深圳商品房小贴士（英文一句）

- **Open-plan flats often squeeze the dining zone beside the kitchen door—measure chair clearance before you buy.**  
餐客一体户型常把餐桌压在厨房门口，买椅前先量过道尺寸。
- **Heat-proof mats matter when soup pots land straight from the hob onto wood tops.**  
热锅端上桌时隔热垫能保住实木桌面。
- **Shared serving spoons reduce chopsticks dipping into communal dishes—a polite habit worth naming in English.**  
公勺减少私筷伸进大盘菜，是值得用英文讲清楚的餐桌礼仪。
