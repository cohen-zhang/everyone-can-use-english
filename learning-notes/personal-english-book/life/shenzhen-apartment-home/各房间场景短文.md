## 1. 客厅 · Living room

*约 2 分钟 · ~265 words*

I step onto the **doormat**, swap street shoes for **slippers**, and drop my **car keys** on the **console table**. Shenzhen afternoons are brutal, so **sunglasses** and a dab of **sunscreen** stay right there for school pickup runs. **不多** is already on the gray **sofa**, hugging a **throw pillow** and begging for cartoons. I dim the **floor lamp**, grab the **remote control**, and queue Peppa Pig on the **wall-mounted TV**. The skinny **soundbar** makes dialogue clear enough for her to repeat lines.

After homework, **Celine** spreads **building blocks** on the **play mat** while I answer one last Slack ping from the **armchair**. **Central air conditioning** hums from the ceiling; I pin the **thermostat** at 26 °C so nobody fights over cold air. When cousins visit, we drag the **bean bag chair** closer and raid the **snack basket** under the **coffee table**. **Facial tissue** and **baby wipes** live on the **side table**—sticky fingers meet snacks faster that way.

Before bed, the **robot vacuum** maps around LEGO walls and docks at the **charging dock** beside the **shoe cabinet**. I run the **air purifier** on low during hazy weeks. On typhoon nights, we count thunder between flashes and remind each other: **don't worry**, the **blackout curtains** are drawn. This room is where work stress loosens, where family noise is welcome, and where **everyone loves jumping up and down in muddy puddles**—at least on TV.

**简中：** 踩 **门垫**换 **拖鞋**，**车钥匙**甩 **玄关条案**；接娃用的 **墨镜**和 **防晒**也搁那儿。 **不多** 窝灰 **沙发**抱 **抱枕**要看动画片；我调暗 **落地灯**、拿 **遥控器**在 **壁挂电视**上放佩奇， **回音壁**让人声清楚好跟读。作业后 **Celine** 在 **游戏垫**上搭 **积木**，我坐 **扶手椅**回最后一条工作消息。 **中央空调**嗡嗡响， **温控**锁 26℃。亲戚来就拖 **豆袋沙发**、翻 **茶几**下 **零食篮**； **面巾纸**和 **baby wipes** 在 **边几**，脏手先擦再吃。睡前 **扫地机器人**绕开乐高回 **充电座**；雾霾开 **净化器**。台风夜数雷声、拉 **遮光帘**、互道 **别担心**。这儿是下班松口气、家人吵闹也欢迎的地方——至少电视里大家都爱跳泥坑。

## 2. 书房 · Study room

*约 2 分钟 · ~270 words*

My **study room** has a frosted **glass door**, so **Celine** knocks twice instead of bursting in during a **video call**. Inside, the **sit-stand desk** faces the window. **阿泽** raises preset two every ninety minutes during release week—sit for **code review**, stand for demos. The **ergonomic chair** lumbar support is locked; bare feet rest on the **anti-fatigue mat** when I host a morning **stand-up**.

**Dual monitors** split IDE and docs; one USB-C **docking station** feeds Ethernet when Wi-Fi wavers behind the metal door frame. **Noise-cancelling headphones** block renovation drills downstairs. I flip **Do Not Disturb** on, close the **sliding door**, and treat the next hour as **deep work**. A **sticky note** on the monitor bezel lists tomorrow's deploy checklist.

When the school bell rings, homework help happens at the lowered desk preset—not during stand-up. **Celine** practices **reading aloud** on the **fold-out sofa bed** side while I highlight vocabulary in a **binder** on the **bookshelf**. The **whiteboard** still shows last sprint tasks until retro clears them.

After **Celine**'s bedtime, the warm **desk lamp** lights textbook corners only. I sip water from the one allowed **water bottle** and keep the chipped **mug** far from the **mechanical keyboard**. The **split AC** stays at 26 °C with the door closed; a quiet **humidifier** fights dry throats. Before guests arrive, we **tidy the study room** and feed stray papers through the **shredder**. If the Wi-Fi is slow in here, I say it out loud, then **turn Wi-Fi off and on again**—same fix as production, honestly.

**简中：** **书房** **磨砂玻璃门**—— **Celine** 开会时会 **敲两下**。 **坐站升降桌**朝窗；发版周 **阿泽** 每九十分钟升一档，坐着 **code review**、站着 demo。 **工学椅**腰托锁死，站会光脚踩 **抗疲劳垫**。 **双显示器**分屏， **扩展坞** 拉有线网； **降噪耳机**挡装修电钻。开 **勿扰**、关 **推拉门**，进入 **深度工作**。 **便利贴**写着明天发布清单。

放学 **作业辅导**用降下的桌档； **Celine** 在 **沙发床**一侧 **朗读**，我在 **书架** **活页夹**里标词汇。 **白板**上的迭代任务回顾会才擦。

娃睡后 **台灯**只照课本；桌上只留一只 **水杯**，缺口 **马克杯**离 **机械键盘**远远的。 **挂机** 26℃、 **加湿器**救干嗓。来客前 **收拾书房**、 **碎纸机**过敏感邮件。网速慢就大声说，然后 **无线关掉再开**——跟线上排障一个套路。

## 3. 厨房 · Kitchen

*约 2 分钟 · ~260 words*

Our **open kitchen** shares one sight line with the living room, so **泽哥** can stir soup and still watch **Celine** build block towers. I open the **fridge** door shelf for milk before the kettle boils over. **Fridge magnets** pin the weekly grocery list beside a school permission slip. Leftovers go into labeled **food containers**—nothing disappears behind tomorrow's produce.

**泽哥** taps one cup from the **rice dispenser**, rinses rice, and starts the **rice cooker** permanently parked on the counter. Wok nights mean the **range hood** runs high; we wash **range hood filters** monthly or stir-fry smoke wins. He splashes **cooking wine** down the hot **wok** rim, tosses veggies, and lands the pot on a cork **trivet** at the **dining table**—heat-proof mats save the wood.

**Celine** ties a cotton **apron** before standing on a **small stool** to stir pancake batter. The **gas hob** has two strong burners; the **induction cooktop** heats milk fast on humid mornings. **Instant hot water** fills my mug for tea between stand-ups. After breakfast, **plastic wrap** covers bowls headed for the **microwave oven** above the stove.

Dishes stack by the **sink**; the pull-down **faucet** rinses every corner. **Celine** helps unload Saturday groceries from the **kitchen trolley** into **upper cabinets**—**instant noodles** on the middle shelf, **hot pot** gear on top for winter weekends. I hang the damp **dishcloth** on the rail so mildew won't bloom. When the **crisper drawer** smells off, we rinse it and leave baking soda overnight. This kitchen is narrow but vertical storage saves us—filtered water, fast rice, and loud family breakfasts included.

**简中：** **开放式厨房**和客厅一条视线， **泽哥** 边搅汤边看 **Celine** 搭积木。茶快溢了先从 **冰箱门架**拿奶； **冰箱贴**压采购清单和回条。剩菜贴签 **保鲜盒**，别被明天菜挡住。

 **泽哥** 从 **米桶**量一杯米、 **电饭煲**煮饭；爆炒开满 **油烟机**，滤网月月洗。 **炒锅**边淋 **料酒**，出锅垫 **隔热垫**上桌。 **Celine** 系 **围裙**踩凳搅面糊。 **燃气灶**火力大， **电磁炉**热奶快； **管线机**一键泡茶。剩饭 **保鲜膜**松松盖住再进 **微波炉**。

碗摞 **水槽**边， **抽拉龙头**冲净。周六 **Celine** 把 **推车**上的货放进 **吊柜**——方便面中层、 **火锅**装备顶层等冬天。 **抹布**挂杆上防霉； **果蔬抽屉**有味就冲洗加小苏打。厨房窄但往上收纳能活——净水、快饭、吵嚷早餐都在这儿。

## 4. 餐厅 · Dining area

*约 2 分钟 · ~255 words*

The **dining area** catches soft evening light from the kitchen window. Our **dining table** is extendable—Spring Festival relatives pull the leaf and squeeze onto the **dining bench** side. I lay **placemats**, fill the glass **pitcher** with ice water, and switch the **pendant light** on. On sticky nights, the **ceiling fan** spins low so Grandma won't freeze under full-blast AC.

**Could you help set the table?** Bowls, **chopsticks**, and spoons for each **place setting**. **Dinner's almost ready—wash your hands and take your seats.** **Celine** spins the **lazy Susan** gently to pass greens without reaching. **No phones at the table—tell us about school. How was your day, Celine?** She talks about math drills while I spoon rice with the long **serving spoon**, not my own chopsticks.

**泽哥** bought kid-length **chopsticks** so practice meals feel easier. **Thank you for cooking; this fish tastes amazing.** We chew with mouths closed; **please don't talk with your mouth full.** The **thermal carafe** keeps morning tea hot on weekends. **Silicone placemats** catch ketchup when art projects meet dinner.

After plates empty, we **clear the plates** and stack them by the **sink**. Leftovers slide into **food containers** for tomorrow's lunch boxes. **Could you wipe the table with a damp cloth?** **Fold the napkins** back into the **sideboard**. I rinse; **Celine** loads the **dishwasher** when she wants extra screen time. **Turn off the ceiling fan** and roll the **pedestal fan** to the corner. This table is where work calendars pause, where English phrases sneak in between bites, and where shared spoons teach polite habits we can name in both languages.

**简中：** **就餐区**傍晚有柔光。 **餐桌**可加长——过年亲戚拉开，孩子坐 **条凳**。我铺 **餐垫**、 **大水壶**加冰水、开 **吊灯**；闷热夜 **吊扇**低档，姥姥不怕空调太冷。

**帮忙摆桌好吗？** 碗筷勺各一份。 **饭好了，洗手入座。** **Celine** 轻转 **转盘**递菜。 **吃饭别看手机，说说学校。** 她讲数学，我用 **公勺**舀饭。 **泽哥** 买了儿童 **筷子**。 **谢谢做饭，鱼真好吃。** 闭嘴嚼、嘴里有食别说话。周末 **保温壶**泡茶； **硅胶餐垫**接番茄酱。

收盘摞 **水槽**边，剩菜 **保鲜盒**装便当。 **湿布擦桌**， **餐巾**收回 **餐边柜**。我冲洗、娃塞 **洗碗机**。关 **吊扇**、 **落地扇**推回角。这张桌子让工作日历暂停，一口饭一句英文，公勺教餐桌礼貌。

## 5. 阳台 · Balcony

*约 2 分钟 · ~250 words*

Our **sealed balcony** faces southeast with **double-glazed sliding doors**. After the wash cycle, **泽哥** lowers the **electric clothes drying rack** with the wall **remote control**; socks grip **clothespins** on windy afternoons. The front-load **washing machine** sits on a leveling mat above the **floor drain**—rain spray still happens during typhoons, so the drain must stay clear.

**Celine** carries the **watering can** on Sunday mornings. Dwarf **sunflowers** and **basil** line the railing-side **planters**; **mint** gets its own pot so it won't choke herbs. **泽哥** dilutes **liquid fertilizer** twice a month in summer. Before storms, we haul **hanging baskets** down from ceiling hooks and wheel the **dehumidifier** out when laundry dries indoors.

At dusk, **阿泽** rocks in the **rocking chair** with a paperback while breeze slips through the **window screen**. **Celine** stands on a plastic **small stool** to reach upper pots. The cordless **vacuum cleaner** charges on the balcony outlet overnight; **mop** and **bucket** hide in the narrow **storage cabinet**.

This **utility balcony** is not a showroom—it's laundry HQ, plant lab, and five-minute reset zone. I rinse muddy shoes at the **outdoor tap** before **entryway** floors suffer. When **fabric softener** makes towels fluffy, **不多** approves. Typhoon prep means retracting the **retractable clothesline** and checking **security bars** so pots don't sail. Out here, work thoughts slow down: water, sun, socks, and basil scent beat another email refresh.

**简中：** **封阳台**朝东南， **双层玻璃推拉门**。洗完 **泽哥** 用 **遥控器**降 **电动晾衣架**；大风天 **晾衣夹**夹牢袜。 **滚筒洗衣机**垫平放 **地漏**上方，台风雨水可能溅入要保持通畅。

周日 **Celine** 拎 **洒水壶**；矮 **向日葵**、 **罗勒**排栏杆 **花盆**， **薄荷**单独一盆。夏天 **泽哥** 稀释 **液体肥**。暴风雨前摘下 **吊盆**，室内晾衣推 **除湿机**。

傍晚 **阿泽** **摇椅**上看书， **纱窗**透气。 **Celine** 踩 **小板凳**够高处的花。 **吸尘器**夜间在阳台插座充； **拖把** **水桶**收 **杂物柜**。

这 **生活阳台**不是样板间——是洗衣总部、植物实验室、五分钟发呆角。 **室外龙头**冲泥鞋护 **玄关**地面。 **柔顺剂**让毛巾松软 **不多** 点赞。台风前收 **伸缩晾衣绳**、检查 **防护栏**。在这儿工作念头慢下来：水、阳光、袜子和罗勒香比刷邮箱治愈。

## 6. 儿童房 · Kids' room

*约 2 分钟 · ~265 words*

After eight o'clock, toys migrate back to the **kids' room**. **Blackout curtains** soften six-a.m. sunlight before school days. **Celine** sleeps on a **single bed** with a firm **mattress**; fresh **fitted sheets** land every Sunday. Board games vanish into **tatami bed** **under-bed drawers** before grandparents visit.

The **wardrobe** holds school uniforms on low **clothes rails** **Celine** can reach. **Pencil**, eraser, and ruler live in the desk **pen holder**; cartoon **pillowcases** flip inside-out before laundry day. A fleece **blanket** waits on the **nightstand** for story time. We set a **timer** for twenty minutes of reading—**clock**, **timer**, **stopwatch**: she learns which is which.

Homework happens at the child-height **desk** while I sit on the **floor cushion**. **English flashcards** clip into a **binder** on the **bookshelf**. When she finishes, **building blocks** return to the **toy storage bin**—clear bins slide under the bed so LEGO colors stay sorted.

The **desk lamp** warms the page; the **split AC** remote lives in the **pen holder** so nobody hides it. A quiet **desk fan** points up so worksheets don't flutter. **Humidifier** runs on allergy nights; **air purifier** low during haze weeks. **Night light** glows soft if **不多** wakes from thunder dreams.

This room is study corner and pillow fort headquarters. We practice: **What's on your desk? There is a ruler. I like my blue blanket.** Knocking rules apply when Daddy's **video call** leaks through the wall—**quiet voice** in the hallway, please. Childhood English starts here: names of things, gentle routines, and proud whispers after she reads a page alone.

**简中：** 八点以后玩具回 **儿童房**。 **遮光帘**挡住上学早晨六点强光。 **Celine** 睡 **单人床** **床垫**偏硬，周日换 **床笠**。来客前棋盘塞进 **榻榻米** **床下抽屉**。

 **衣柜**低杆挂校服她能自己够。 **铅笔**橡皮尺在 **笔筒**；卡通 **枕套**洗衣日翻面。 **绒毯**在 **床头柜**等睡前故事。 **计时器**定阅读二十分钟——分清 **钟**、 **计时器**、 **秒表**。

作业在儿童 **书桌**，我坐 **坐垫**。 **单词卡**夹在 **书架** **活页夹**。玩完 **积木**进透明 **玩具收纳箱**滑床底。

 **台灯**暖光； **空调遥控器**放 **笔筒**防藏。 **桌面风扇**往上吹不让作业飞。过敏开 **加湿器**，雾霾 **净化器**低档。雷雨天 **小夜灯**陪 **不多**。

这是学习角也是枕头堡垒总部。练 **桌上有什么？有一把尺。我喜欢蓝毯子。** 爸爸 **视频会**时走廊要 **小声**。童年英语从这里开始：指认物品、温柔作息、独自读完一页的骄傲低语。

## 7. 主卧 · Master bedroom

*约 2 分钟 · ~275 words*

We keep laptops out of the **master bedroom** after eleven—sleep hygiene beats one more diff. **Blackout curtains** buy thirty extra minutes on Sunday. The **king-size bed** swallows two duvets; his-and-hers **pillows** differ in loft so neither neck complains. Steam from the **en-suite bathroom** stays behind a closed door so the **anti-fog mirror** outside stays clear.

**泽哥**'s **height-adjustable desk** sits in the corner: preset one for sit, preset two for stand, preset three for tutoring **Celine** on weekends. A slim **picture frame** with **family photos** rests beside the **desk lamp** without stealing monitor backlight. **Is Wi-Fi okay in here?** Guests always ask **what's the Wi-Fi password** before uploading shared albums.

Between calls, I pull a paperback from the **bookshelf**—bilingual **dictionary** within arm's reach. On quiet nights, a **yoga mat** unrolls by the window; a **throw blanket** turns five-minute meditation into something we actually finish. **Noise-cancelling headphones** separate late **deep work** from hallway chatter.

The **wardrobe** splits work shirts left, weekend tees right. Gym gear drops into the **laundry hamper** before it hits carpet. **HomePod** plays white noise when urban honks leak through **blackout curtains**. **Humidifier** fights AC dryness; **pedestal fan** stirs air when shoulders feel cold under central vents. Mosquito season means the electric repellent clicks on before we read.

This suite is marriage HQ: calendar whispers, apology hugs, and borrowed chargers on the **nightstand**. Work bleeds in sometimes—**mute the mic**, close the **en-suite** door, stand for a five-minute sync. But mostly we protect this room for rest, stretch, and the kind of learning that doesn't need a keyboard.

**简中：** 十一点后电脑不进 **主卧**，睡眠比多改一行重要。 **遮光帘**让周日多睡半小时。 **加大床**两床被子不抢；夫妻 **枕头**高低不同护脖子。 **套内卫生间**蒸汽关门，外间 **防雾镜**才清楚。

角落 **升降桌**：坐、站、周末给 **Celine** 讲题三档预设。 **全家福** **相框**在 **台灯**旁不抢屏幕光。来客总问 **这儿 Wi-Fi 行吗**、 **密码是啥**。

会间从 **书架**抽书， **词典**伸手可及。安静夜 **瑜伽垫**在窗边展开， **盖毯**五分钟冥想真能做完。 **降噪耳机**把深夜 **深度工作**和走廊人声隔开。

 **衣柜**左衬衫右休闲；健身衣进 **脏衣篓**再碰地毯。 **HomePod** 白噪音挡车流。 **加湿器**对抗空调干， **落地扇**补风。蚊季电热驱蚊先开再看书。

这是夫妻总部：小声对日程、拥抱道歉、 **床头柜**借充电器。工作偶尔渗入—— **关麦**、关 **套卫**门、站着开五分钟会。但多数时候这屋留给睡眠、拉伸和不必敲键盘的学习。

## 8. 卫生间 · Bathroom（含套卫）

*约 2 分钟 · ~260 words*

The **bathroom** follows dry-then-wet flow: basin, vanity, toilet, then shower. I wash hands at the wide **washbasin**; the pull-down **faucet** reaches every corner. **Toothbrush holders** separate adult and kid brushes; **Celine** prefers lint-free **disposable face towels** during breakout weeks. Skincare tubes hide behind **mirror cabinet** doors—**toner**, **serum**, **moisturizer** in order before lights off.

**Pass the facial tissue** when someone sneezes on mirror glass. The weighted **tissue box** stays on the shallow shelf. **Toilet paper** hangs on the wall holder; we teach **flush** and **wash hands** as one routine. A pedal **trash bin** stands where wet feet won't kick it.

Push the **shower door**—wet zone begins. **Shower gel** and **shampoo** sit on the corner shelf; **conditioner** lasts longer when hair is combed first. The **floor drain** must stay clear or suds pool during kid baths. **Exhaust fan** runs before long showers so paint doesn't peel in humid Shenzhen summers.

**泽哥** checks the **water heater** panel when pressure drops. **Hair dryer** cord loops on a hook beside the vanity. The digital **scale** slides under the skirt between weigh-ins. In the **en-suite**, steam stays contained so bedroom mirrors don't fog.

This room is quick hygiene between meetings, splash battles with **Celine**, and Sunday sheet-mask peace. We say: **We're almost out of tissues**; **dry your hands with a tissue** when guest towels spin in the washer. Small English, daily rhythm—clean hands, closed cabinet, quiet fan—and the apartment feels cared for, not just occupied.

**简中：** **卫生间**先干后湿：洗手盆、浴室柜、马桶、再淋浴。宽 **洗手盆**洗手， **抽拉龙头**冲得到角。 **牙刷架**分大人小孩； **Celine** 长痘用 **一次性洗脸巾**。护肤品在 **镜柜**门后—— **化妆水**、 **精华**、 **面霜**睡前按序涂。

打喷嚏 **递面巾纸**；带配重 **抽纸盒**放浅架。 **厕纸**挂墙； **冲水**和 **洗手**绑成一套。脚踏 **垃圾桶**放湿脚碰不到处。

推开 **淋浴门**进湿区； **沐浴露** **洗发水**放角架，先梳头 **护发素**更省。 **地漏**要通，娃洗澡泡沫才不积。长淋浴先开 **排气扇**，深圳夏天油漆才不起皮。

 **泽哥** 水压小查 **热水器**面板。 **吹风机**线挂 **镜柜**钩。 **体重秤**称完推进柜裙下。 **套卫**蒸汽关住，卧室镜子才不糊。

这是会间快速洗漱、和 **Celine** 溅水大战、周日敷面膜的角落。会说 **抽纸快用完了**、客巾还在洗就 **用纸巾擦手**。小小英文、日日节奏——手净、柜关、风扇轻——房子像被照料而不只是住人。
