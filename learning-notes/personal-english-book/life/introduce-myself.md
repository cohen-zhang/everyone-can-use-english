# 自我介绍学习笔记 Self-Introduction Notes

**索引：** [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/README|个人英文材料书索引]]

## 参考资源 Reference

- **YouTube 视频**: "Learn Easy English–Self Introduction in English" by Shane Michael Peterson
- **核心结构**: Name → Family → Education → Work Experience → Hobbies → Future Hopes/Dreams
- **学习目标**: 掌握简单清晰的自我介绍格式，便于日后实际使用

> **隐私说明：** 下文学校、公司、项目名已替换为通用表述，便于对外分享练习稿。

---

## 1) 基本介绍 Basic Introduction

> **Hi, my name is Zege. I'm from China.**
> 你好，我叫阿泽（Zege），来自中国。

> **My family and I live in a major city in southern China. It's a great place and I love it here. I'm having a great time.**
> 我和家人住在南方一座大城市。这里很棒，我很喜欢，过得很愉快。

> **I am a software development engineer. I'm also a father—I have a two-year-old daughter, Celine.**
> 我是一名软件开发工程师，也是一位父亲，有一个两岁的女儿 Celine。

> **She is a very cute girl. She is my angel, my sweet, and my sunshine.**
> 她是一个非常可爱的女孩。她是我的天使、我的甜心、我的阳光。

---

## 2) 教育背景与工作经验 Education & Work Background

> **I went to a provincial university and I graduated in 2013.**
> 我就读于一所省属大学，2013 年毕业。

> **My major was Computer Science and Technology.**
> 我的专业是计算机科学与技术。

> **In 2012, I joined a tech company in southern China through campus recruitment.**
> 2012 年，我通过校招加入了一家南方科技公司（Company A）。

> **Since then, I spent about three years doing software development work there.**
> 从那时起，我在那里做了大约三年软件开发工作。

> **In that job, I mainly worked on an HR management system for higher education (Project X).**
> 在那份工作中，我主要负责开发一套高校人力资源管理系统（Project X）。

> **I participated in all the procedures including needs analysis, prototype designing, database designing, coding and case presenting.**
> 我参与了全流程，包括需求分析、原型设计、数据库设计、编码和案例演示。

> **So I have strong professional abilities.**
> 因此我具备较强的专业能力。

### 工作品质关键词 Work Qualities

| 英文 English | 简中 Chinese |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Responsible** | 负责任的 |
| **Trustworthy** | 值得信赖的 |
| **Can be depended on** | 可以依靠的 |
| **Get things done** | 把事情做成 |

> **People who are responsible and trustworthy are important in almost any situation.**
> 在任何场合，有责任心且值得信赖的人都很重要。

## 3) 兴趣爱好 Hobbies & Free Time

> **These days, my hobbies are learning English, reading and practicing.**
> 最近，我的爱好是学英语、阅读和练习。

> **I moved to a new house recently, and I like to stay at home—you know, an "otaku".**
> 我最近搬到了新家，喜欢待在家里——你知道的，一个"宅男"。

> **I love writing (sharing useful and interesting software, personal and child growth records).**
> 我喜欢写作（分享实用有趣的软件、个人成长记录和育儿记录）。

> **They are very fascinating to me.**
> 这些对我来说非常有吸引力。

> **Other than that… that's pretty much it. I like watching movies!**
> 除此之外……差不多就这些了。我还喜欢看电影！

## 4) 未来计划 Future Plans

> **Well, in the future, I wanna continue learning English. I really love it.**
> 未来，我想继续学英语。我真的很喜欢。

> **However, I really would love to be able to live some place different!**
> 不过，我也很想能住在不同的地方！

> **I want to experience other countries and other cultures.**
> 我想体验其他国家和文化。

> **My dream is to work for an international hi-tech company.**
> 我的梦想是在一家国际高科技公司工作。

> **But am I qualified for the job? That's the problem ^_^.**
> 但我有资格胜任那份工作吗？这就是问题所在 ^_^。

## 实用表达 Useful Phrases

| 场景 Scenario | 表达 Expression | 简中 Chinese |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|
| 开场 | Hi, my name is... | 你好，我叫…… |
| 来自哪里 | I'm from... / I come from... | 我来自…… |
| 介绍工作 | I am a... / I work as a... | 我是一名…… |
| 介绍家庭 | I'm also a father/mother | 我也是一位父亲/母亲 |
| 描述爱好 | My hobbies are... | 我的爱好是…… |
| 未来愿望 | In the future, I wanna... | 未来，我想…… |
| 谦虚收尾 | But am I qualified? | 但我够格吗？ |
