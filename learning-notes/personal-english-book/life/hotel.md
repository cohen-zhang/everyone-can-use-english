## 2. 预订 — Booking `亲子`

- **I'd like to book a family room for two nights.** — 我想订一间家庭房，住两晚。  
- **Do you have a room with twin beds?** — 有双床房吗？  
- **We need a crib for our two-year-old.** — 我们需要一张婴儿床。  
- **Is breakfast included?** — 含早餐吗？  
- **Do you have connecting rooms?** — 有连通房吗？  
- **Is there a kids club or indoor playground?** — 有儿童俱乐部或室内游乐场吗？  
- **What's the age limit for the play area?** — 游乐区年龄上限是多少？  
- **Is there a kids' pool?** — 有儿童泳池吗？  
- **Can I get a non-smoking room on a high floor?** — 可以要高层无烟房吗？  
- **What's your cancellation policy?** — 取消政策是怎样的？

## 3. 到达与办理入住 — Arrival & Check-in `亲子`

- **Hi, we have a reservation under Aze.** — 你好，预订名是 Aze。  
- **We booked a family room for two adults and one child.** — 我们订了家庭房，两成人一儿童。  
- **Here's my ID and booking confirmation.** — 这是我的证件和预订确认。  
- **What time is check-out?** — 退房几点？  
- **Could we get two key cards?** — 能给两张房卡吗？  
- **Is Wi-Fi free? What's the password?** — Wi‑Fi 免费吗？密码多少？  
- **Where is the elevator to the rooms?** — 去客房的电梯在哪？  
- **Could someone help with our luggage?** — 能派人帮忙拿行李吗？  
- **Does the room have a bathtub or a shower only?** — 房间是浴缸还是只有淋浴？  
- **My daughter is tired—could we get early access if a room is ready?** — 女儿累了，若有空房能提前入住吗？

## 4. 客房与亲子需求 — In the Room `亲子`

- **The key card doesn't work. Could you reprogram it?** — 房卡打不开门，能重新写卡吗？  
- **Please bring extra towels and a kids' toothbrush.** — 请送额外毛巾和儿童牙刷。  
- **Can we get a rollaway bed / an extra blanket?** — 能加一张床／再给一床毯子吗？  
- **The A/C is too cold. Can you show me how to adjust it?** — 空调太冷了，能教我调一下吗？  
- **Is the minibar charged by use?** — 迷你吧是按消费计费吗？  
- **Please do not disturb. My daughter is napping.** — 请勿打扰，女儿在午睡。  
- **Could housekeeping come after four?** — 客房整理能四点以后再来吗？  
- **There's no hot water. Could someone check?** — 没有热水了，能派人检查吗？  
- **We need more toilet paper and tissue.** — 需要再补点卷纸和抽纸。  
- **Where are the ice machines / laundry?** — 制冰机／洗衣房在哪？  

### 对 Celine 说的短句

- **Don't jump on the bed.** — 别在床上跳。  
- **Keep your voice down in the hallway.** — 走廊里小声点。  
- **Hold my hand in the elevator.** — 电梯里牵着我的手。  
- **Don't play with the door lock.** — 别玩门锁。  
- **Put your sandals by the door.** — 拖鞋放门口。

## 5. 酒店游乐场 / Kids Club — Playground & Kids Club `亲子`

酒店常见：**outdoor playground**（户外游乐场）、**indoor soft play**（室内软体乐园）、**kids club**（托管/活动）、**ball pit**、**slide**、**swing**、**trampoline**。

### 找路与规则

- **Where is the children's play area / kids club?** — 儿童游乐区／儿童俱乐部在哪里？  
- **Is the playground indoors or outdoors?** — 游乐场在室内还是室外？  
- **Do I need to sign in / put on a wristband?** — 需要登记／戴手环吗？  
- **Is parental supervision required?** — 必须家长陪同吗？  
- **What are the opening hours?** — 开放时间是几点到几点？  
- **Is there an age range for this soft play?** — 这个软体区有年龄限制吗？  
- **Shoes off before you go in.** — 进去前先脱鞋。  
- **Socks only in the ball pit.** — 球池里只能穿袜子。  

### 游玩中口令

- **Wait your turn on the slide.** — 滑梯要排队轮流。  
- **Hold the rail. Sit down, then slide.** — 扶好扶手，坐好再滑。  
- **No running on the trampoline—one at a time.** — 蹦床别乱跑，一次一个人。  
- **Don't climb up the slide.** — 别从滑梯口往上爬。  
- **Share the swings. Two more minutes, then switch.** — 秋千要分享，再两分钟轮换。  
- **Stay where I can see you.** — 待在我看得见的地方。  
- **If another kid is waiting, let's take turns.** — 有人在等，咱们轮流玩。  
- **Wash your hands after soft play.** — 软体乐园玩完要洗手。  
- **We're leaving in five minutes. One last slide.** — 五分钟后走，再滑最后一次。  

### 与工作人员沟通

- **My daughter is two. Is this zone safe for her age?** — 我女儿两岁，这个区适合她吗？  
- **Can a parent stay inside with the child?** — 家长可以进去陪吗？  
- **Is there a kids club session this afternoon?** — 今天下午有儿童俱乐部活动吗？  
- **Do we need to book the kids club in advance?** — 儿童俱乐部要提前预约吗？  
- **She has a food allergy—please keep snacks simple.** — 她有食物过敏，零食请尽量简单。

## 6. 泳池 — Pool (with kids) `亲子`

- **Where is the kids' pool / the shallow end?** — 儿童池／浅水区在哪？  
- **Are there life jackets for toddlers?** — 有幼儿救生衣吗？  
- **The pool towel station is by the chairs.** — 泳池毛巾在躺椅那边。  
- **No diving. Walk, don't run.** — 禁止跳水，走路别跑。  
- **Put on sunscreen before we swim.** — 下水前先涂防晒。  
- **Hold the handrail on the pool steps.** — 下池台阶时扶好扶手。  
- **Time to get out—your lips look blue.** — 该上岸了，嘴都发紫了。  
- **Rinse off before you enter the pool.** — 下水前先冲一下。

## 8. 退房 — Check-out `亲子`

- **We're ready to check out. Room 1208.** — 我们要退房了，1208 房。  
- **Could you print the receipt / folio?** — 能打印账单吗？

## 9. 短对话 — Sample Dialogues

### 场景 1：前台办理入住

**阿泽:** Hi, we have a reservation under Aze—family room, two nights.  
**前台:** Welcome. May I have your ID and a card for the deposit?  
**阿泽:** Sure. Also, is there a kids club or indoor playground?  
**前台:** Yes—soft play is next to the pool, open 9 to 8. Parents must stay with kids under six.  
**阿泽:** Perfect. Two key cards, please.

**简中：** 阿泽报预订名并问儿童游乐；前台说明开放时间与陪同要求，给两张房卡。

### 场景 2：酒店游乐场

**Celine:** Dad, I want the slide!  
**阿泽:** Wait your turn. Sit down, then go. I'll watch from here.  
**Celine:** Can I jump on the trampoline?  
**阿泽:** One at a time. After that, wash your hands.  
**工作人员:** Soft play closes at eight. Five-minute warning.  
**阿泽:** Thanks. One last slide, then we go back upstairs.

**简中：** 排队滑梯、蹦床轮流、洗手；闭园前五分钟提醒，再滑一次上楼。

### 场景 3：请客房服务 / 退房前

**阿泽:** Hi, room 1208. Could you send a crib and extra towels?  
**客房:** Of course. Anything else?  
**阿泽:** A kids' toothbrush, please. And can housekeeping skip tonight—she's napping.  
*(次日)*  
**阿泽:** Checking out. Can we store the bags until three? We'll play at the playground once more.

**简中：** 要加婴儿床与毛巾、儿童牙刷，今晚勿打扰；退房后行李寄存，再去游乐场玩一会儿。

## 10. 英文短文 · 阿泽自述（带娃住酒店）

*约 2 分钟 · 描述训练 · 串联本页常用句*

**English**

When we travel with Celine, I book a **family room** first.  
I ask for **twin beds** or a **crib**, and I always check **kids amenities**.  
At the **front desk**, I say we have a **reservation** under my name.  
I get two **key cards**, ask about **Wi-Fi**, and confirm **check-out** time.

In the room, I set clear rules for her.  
No jumping on the bed. Hold hands in the elevator. Keep voices down in the hallway.  
If we need towels or a toothbrush, I call **housekeeping**.  
If she naps, I put up the **do not disturb** sign.

Our favorite part is the hotel **playground**.  
Some hotels have an outdoor play area; others have indoor **soft play** and a **ball pit**.  
I ask about **opening hours**, age limits, and whether parents must stay.  
Then I coach her: wait your turn on the **slide**, sit down, hold the rail.  
On the **trampoline**, one at a time. Share the **swings**. Stay where I can see you.

If there is a **kids' pool**, we put on **sunscreen** first.  
No running. No diving. Hold the handrail on the steps.  
At breakfast, we look for a **high chair** and mild food.  
Sometimes we order **room service** when she is too tired to go downstairs.

On the last day, we **check out**, print the **folio**, and leave bags at the **luggage room**.  
If we still have time, we take one last round at the play area.  
Travel with a kid is slower—but with clear English and calm routines, it still feels doable.

**简中：**

带 Celine 出行，我先订好**家庭房**。  
会问**双床**或**婴儿床**，并确认有没有**儿童设施**。  
到**前台**报预订名，拿两张**房卡**，问 **Wi‑Fi**，确认**退房**时间。

进房先立规矩：床上别跳、电梯牵手、走廊小声。  
要毛巾或牙刷就找**客房服务**；她午睡就挂上**请勿打扰**。

我们最期待的是酒店**游乐场**。  
有的是户外，有的是室内**软体乐园**和**球池**。  
我会先问开放时间、年龄与是否必须家长陪同。  
再给她口令：滑梯排队、坐好再滑、扶好扶手；**蹦床**一次一人；秋千要分享；待在我视线内。

若有**儿童池**，先涂**防晒**：不跑、不跳水、下台阶扶扶手。  
早餐找**儿童餐椅**和清淡食物；太累就叫**客房送餐**。

最后一天**退房**、打**账单**、行李**寄存**；若有时间，再去游乐区玩最后一轮。  
带娃旅行节奏更慢，但英文句式和流程清楚，依然走得动。

**扩展覆盖（更多短语用法）**

**English (extended):**  
Before we leave home, I confirm **early check-in**, **late check-out**, and whether **breakfast is included**.  
At arrival, we tip or thank the **bellhop** if they help with **luggage**.  
Inside, we learn hotel words fast: **minibar**, **safe**, **bathtub**, **shower gel**, **hair dryer**, and **extra pillow**.  
At the play zone we practice: **soft play**, **socks only**, **wristband**, **sign-in**, **take turns**, and **five-minute warning**.  
By the pool: **shallow end**, **life jacket**, **pool towel**, **rinse off**, and **get out now**.  
At the restaurant: **kids' menu**, **booster seat**, **less spicy**, **warm milk**, and **allergy**.  
At departure: **receipt**, **deposit release**, **luggage storage**, and **shuttle to the airport** if needed.

**简中（扩展）：**  
出门前确认能否提前入住、延迟退房、含不含早餐。  
到店若有行李员帮拿行李，道谢即可。  
房内快扫一遍词：迷你吧、保险箱、浴缸、沐浴露、吹风机、加枕。  
游乐区词组：软体、只穿袜、手环、登记、轮流、五分钟提醒。  
泳池：浅水区、救生衣、浴巾、冲身、该上岸了。  
餐厅：儿童菜单、增高椅、少辣、热牛奶、过敏。  
离店：收据、解除押金、行李寄存、必要时机场接驳车。

## 12. 一句话复习（家长自用）

**Book amenities first, set room rules second, coach playground turns third—then checkout stays calm.**  
先确认儿童设施，再立客房规矩，游乐场练轮流——退房就会从容。`亲子`
