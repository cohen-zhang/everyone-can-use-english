

早上 7 点 ，早上被手表的振动叫醒，而不是以前很吵的手机闹铃； 当然，这时候可以选择 stop/snooze ，如果 snooze 会再小睡 10 分钟； 

补充一杯水，快速刷牙洗脸后，骑电动车送孩子上学，把车停在地铁站附近； 
然后搭乘地铁，中间花费 50 分钟，在地铁上听一会或者读会英文或者只是背单词；
出地铁到公司还有一段距离，骑共享单车，如果单车都被人骑走，就只能走路了。

9 点前到达公司，然后在园区吃早餐，早餐多摄入一点蛋白质，一般 2 个鸡蛋 + 1 个鸡蛋饼； 或者一个鸡蛋+一碗粉； 

在 10:00， 我喜欢在飞书会议时站立办公；

12 点在园区吃午饭，减少碳水摄入，避免下午困； 

下午 3 点，下午茶时间，休息一下，去茶水间的饮水机接水； 或者下楼买杯咖啡，在园区休息区远眺，把身体尤其是脖子伸展一下。

18 点会多吃一点，饭后围着园区散步一圈，晚上如果有加班， 21 点离开公司， 22 点多到家；

# English Translation
I wake up at 7 a.m. to the vibration from my smartwatch, instead of the formerly noisy phone alarm. I can choose either Stop or Snooze; if I pick Snooze, I get an extra 10 minutes of light sleep.

I drink a glass of water first, then quickly brush my teeth and wash my face. Next, I ride an electric scooter to drop my child off at school and park the scooter near a subway station.
After that, I take the subway, which takes roughly 50 minutes. On the ride, I listen to audio, read English texts, or simply memorize vocabulary words.
There is still a short walk from the subway exit to my workplace. I ride a shared bike if one is available; if all shared bikes are taken, I have to walk.

I arrive at the office before 9 a.m. and have breakfast inside the office campus. I prioritize sufficient protein intake for breakfast, usually two eggs plus an egg pancake, or one egg with a bowl of rice noodles.

At 10:00, I prefer to work standing up during my Feishu meetings.

I have lunch on campus at 12 noon. I cut down on carbohydrate intake to avoid feeling sleepy in the afternoon.

3 p.m. marks my coffee break. I step away from work to refill my water bottle at the water dispenser in the pantry, or head downstairs to buy a cup of coffee. I then gaze into the distance at the campus lounge and stretch my body, especially my neck.

I eat a proper dinner at 6 p.m., followed by a walk around the whole campus. If I need to work overtime, I leave the office at 9 p.m. and get home after 10 p.m.

### Key Terminology Notes
1. **Feishu**: Retain the official Chinese product name; its overseas counterpart is Lark.
2. **Snooze/Stop**: Standard built-in alarm function terms on electronic devices.
3. **shared bike**: Common daily expression for public shared bicycles; alternative: shared bicycle.
4. **carbohydrate intake**: Standard fitness/diet terminology for碳水摄入.
5. **coffee break**: Natural workplace English for afternoon tea/rest time in corporate scenarios.
