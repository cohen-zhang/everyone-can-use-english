## 英文朗读版（英 + 简中）

*约 2 分钟 · ~300 words*

My hometown is **Huangmei County**, in **Huanggang City**, **Hubei Province**.  
It sits in **eastern Hubei**, right where **Hubei**, **Jiangxi**, and **Anhui** meet—**a three-province border county** without ever leaving home.  
The **Yangtze River** is close; on clear days the **low hills**, **wet fields**, and **long straight roads** still feel like **the heart of the Yangtze plain**.

People outside Hubei know Huangmei for **three old nicknames**: **a land of opera**, **a land of martial arts**, and **a land of Buddhism**.  
We call it **the birthplace of Huangmei Opera**—**folk tunes** from the fields that grew into one of **China's best-loved opera forms**.  
When I was a kid, **grandparents hummed those melodies** at dinner; today you still hear them on **TV**, at **temple fairs**, and in **small-town teahouses**.  
**Martial arts** run deep too; kids once **practiced forms** in **schoolyards** and **village squares**.  
And as **a Buddhist heartland**, the county keeps **temple bells** and **incense smoke** on the hills.

**On East Mountain** sits the **Fifth Patriarch Temple**—that is why we say **a land of Buddhism**.  
A famous **Zen** site where **Master Huineng** trained centuries ago.  
Pilgrims **climb** the stone steps; farmers **grow tea** and **rice** on the slopes below.

Life there felt **slower** and **more spread out** than **Shenzhen** today.  
**Markets** opened early; **bikes** and **buses** linked **town** and **village**.  
**Neighbors** knew each other by name; **New Year's visits** could last a whole afternoon.  
**Winter** brought **cold drizzle**; **summer** brought **humid heat** and **fireflies** after rain.

I left for school and work years ago, but Huangmei is still **home base** in my head.  
When someone asks where I'm from, I say **Huangmei, Huanggang, Hubei**—and if they've heard of **Huangmei Opera**, **martial arts**, or **the Fifth Patriarch Temple**, I know the name **traveled farther than I did**.

**简中：** 我的家乡在**湖北省黄冈市黄梅县**。它在**鄂东**，**鄂、赣、皖三省交界**——不出县界就有「三省通衢」的感觉。**长江**不远；晴日里**低丘、湿地、笔直的公路**，仍像**长江中游平原**的腹地。

外地人提起黄梅，常听到三个老名号：**戏曲之乡**、**武术之乡**、**佛教之乡**。这里是**黄梅戏发源地**——**田间小调**长成广为人知的地方戏；小时候**祖辈在饭桌边哼**那些调子，如今在**电视**、**庙会**、**小镇茶馆**里仍能听见。**武术**也底子厚，当年孩子们在**操场**、**村口**练套路。**佛教**更是烙在山里——

**东山五祖寺**便是「佛教之乡」最有名的一处——**禅宗**名刹，**六祖慧能**曾在此参学。香客**登**石阶；山下的农户种**茶**、种**稻**。

那里的生活节奏比今天的**深圳**慢、**散**。**早市**开得早；**自行车**和**班车**把**镇**和**村**串起来。**邻居**彼此叫得出名字；**拜年**能坐上大半天。**冬天**湿冷细雨；**夏天**闷热，雨后看**萤火虫**。

我多年前离家求学、工作，但黄梅仍是心里的**根**。别人问「你从哪来」，我说 **Huangmei, Huanggang, Hubei**——若对方听过**黄梅戏**、**武术之乡**或**五祖寺**，我就知道这名字**走得比我还远**。

## 分句跟读 · Sentence drills

- **My hometown is Huangmei County, in Huanggang City, Hubei Province.** — 我的家乡在湖北省黄冈市黄梅县。
- **It sits in eastern Hubei, where three provinces meet.** — 它在鄂东，三省交界。
- **Huangmei is known as a land of opera, martial arts, and Buddhism.** — 黄梅以戏曲之乡、武术之乡、佛教之乡闻名。
- **East Mountain holds the Fifth Patriarch Temple, a famous Zen site.** — 东山有五祖寺，是著名的禅宗圣地。
- **Life there felt slower than big-city Shenzhen today.** — 那里的生活比今天的深圳慢。
- **When someone asks where I'm from, I say Huangmei, Huanggang, Hubei.** — 别人问从哪来，我就报「湖北黄冈黄梅」。
