# APEC in Shenzhen
（适合四年级｜A2 难度｜亲子共读）
## English Text
Do you know APEC? Its full name is Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation.
It is a big family of many member economies around the Pacific Ocean.

China has hosted APEC before. We had meetings in Beijing and Shanghai. Now, it is Shenzhen’s turn! We are lucky, because we live in this great city.

Many friends will come. Let’s meet ten of them: China, the United States, Australia, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia and New Zealand.

Leaders and experts get together at APEC. They talk, share ideas and work together. They hope to make trade easier, grow new technology and help people live better lives.

Why Shenzhen? Shenzhen is young and creative. Lots of tech companies are here. It is a wonderful place for this big meeting.

We can be warm and polite to all the visitors from far away. We are proud of Shenzhen. When people work hand in hand, the world becomes a nicer place.

# APEC in Shenzhen
（成人口播稿｜CEFR B2｜移居深圳视角）
## English Text
I live in Shenzhen. There is a well-known local saying here: “Once you come, you are a Shenzhener.” Though I was not born here, this city has given me a strong sense of belonging. That is why hosting APEC feels so special to me.

APEC stands for Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation. Officially, we use the term “member economies”, because its participants include both sovereign states and regional economies. All 21 APEC member economies are: Australia, Brunei, Canada, Chile, China, Hong Kong (China), Indonesia, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea, Peru, the Philippines, Russia, Singapore, Chinese Taipei, Thailand, the United States and Viet Nam.

China has hosted APEC twice before: the 2001 meeting in Shanghai and the 2014 gathering in Beijing. This is the very first time the APEC meeting takes place here in Shenzhen.

The core mission of APEC is to advance trade liberalization, facilitate investment, and strengthen economic and technical cooperation across the Asia-Pacific. Its agenda covers many key topics we care about today: the digital economy, resilient regional supply chains, technological innovation and sustainable development.

As someone who has settled down here, I can feel Shenzhen’s energy every day. It started as a frontier of reform and opening-up, and has grown into a global hub for high-tech industries and foreign trade. Sitting at the heart of the Greater Bay Area, it connects mainland China closely with Hong Kong and Macao. The spirit of openness, innovation and collaboration is not just a slogan for us — it is the way we live and work.

This conference offers the world a great chance to get to know Shenzhen better. For me, as one of the people who chose this city, I hope this gathering will deepen dialogue among all these Pacific partners, build more consensus, and create shared opportunities for growth.

I’m truly glad to witness this moment, and I look forward to more open cooperation and mutual prosperity across the whole Asia-Pacific region.

## 中文对照译文
我住在深圳。这里有一句家喻户晓的话：“来了就是深圳人。”我并非在这里出生，但这座城市给了我很强的归属感。所以，能够迎来 APEC，对我而言意义格外特别。

APEC 的全称是亚太经济合作组织。官方使用“成员经济体”这一说法，因为参会方既包含主权国家，也包含地区经济体。APEC 的全部 21 个成员经济体为：澳大利亚、文莱、加拿大、智利、中国、中国香港、印度尼西亚、日本、韩国、马来西亚、墨西哥、新西兰、巴布亚新几内亚、秘鲁、菲律宾、俄罗斯、新加坡、中国台北、泰国、美国、越南。

中国此前已经两次承办 APEC：2001 年在上海，2014 年在北京。这是 APEC 会议第一次落地深圳。

APEC 的核心使命是推进贸易自由化、促进投资便利化，并且深化亚太区域内的经济技术合作。议程涵盖当下众多重要议题：数字经济、具备韧性的区域供应链、科技创新以及可持续发展。

作为定居于此的一员，我每天都能感受到深圳的活力。它从改革开放的前沿，一步步成长为全球科创与外贸枢纽。地处大湾区核心，它让内地与港澳紧密相连。开放、创新、协作，对我们来说不只是一句口号，而是我们日常工作与生活的底色。

这场盛会，给了世界一个更好认识深圳的窗口。对我这个选择扎根于此的人来说，我期待这次大会能够加深环太平洋伙伴之间的对话，凝聚更多共识，创造共赢的发展机遇。

能够见证这一刻，我由衷欣喜，也祝愿整个亚太地区持续开放协作、共同繁荣。
