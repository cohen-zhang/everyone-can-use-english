## 1. Big picture — 总原则

- **Weight change comes down to energy balance over time.** — 体重变化，长期来看还是看能量收支。
- **Eat mostly whole foods; cut back on ultra-processed stuff.** — 尽量吃天然食物，少碰高度加工食品。
- **Move your body most days; sleep and stress matter too.** — 多数日子要动一动；睡眠和压力也会影响体重。
- **Build habits you can keep — not a crash diet for two weeks.** — 养成能长期坚持的习惯，别搞两周就崩的极端节食。

## 2. How each food group affects weight — 各类食物与体重

### 2.1 **Carbs**（碳水）

- **Carbs are your main quick fuel — they don't automatically make you fat.** — 碳水是主要快燃料，本身不会「一吃就胖」。
- **Refined carbs and sugary drinks spike blood sugar and leave you hungry sooner.** — 精制碳水和含糖饮料容易拉高血糖，也更容易很快又饿。
- **Whole grains, beans, and starchy veggies give you fiber and steady energy.** — 全谷物、豆类、淀粉类蔬菜有纤维，能量更平稳。
- **Match carb portions to how active you are that day.** — 碳水份量最好跟当天活动量挂钩。

### 2.2 **Fats / oils**（油脂）

- **Fat is calorie-dense — a little goes a long way.** — 脂肪单位热量高，少量就很有「存在感」。
- **Cooking oil, nuts, and cheese add up fast if you don't watch portions.** — 炒菜油、坚果、奶酪不注意份量的话，热量涨得很快。
- **Some fat is essential for hormones and absorbing vitamins A, D, E, and K.** — 适量脂肪对激素和维生素 A/D/E/K 吸收是必需的。
- **Prefer olive oil, avocados, and fatty fish over deep-fried snacks.** — 橄榄油、牛油果、多脂鱼，通常比油炸零食更值得选。

### 2.3 **Sugar**（糖）

- **Added sugar is easy to overeat and doesn't fill you up.** — 添加糖很容易吃多，而且不太顶饱。
- **Watch labels for sugar in drinks, sauces, and packaged snacks.** — 饮料、酱料、包装零食里的糖，看标签才看得清。
- **Whole fruit has fiber; fruit juice behaves more like liquid sugar.** — 整颗水果有纤维；果汁更像「液体糖」。
- **Cutting sugary drinks alone often helps people lose weight without feeling deprived.** — 很多人只戒掉含糖饮料，体重就会下来，还不觉得太委屈。

### 2.4 **Protein**（蛋白质）

- **Protein keeps you full longer and helps you hold on to muscle.** — 蛋白质更顶饱，也有助于保住肌肉。
- **Spread protein across meals — don't save it all for dinner.** — 蛋白质最好三餐分摊，别全堆晚餐。
- **Good sources: eggs, fish, chicken, tofu, Greek yogurt, legumes.** — 常见好来源：蛋、鱼、鸡胸、豆腐、希腊酸奶、豆类。
- **After strength training, a protein-rich meal supports recovery.** — 力量训练后，含蛋白质的餐食有助于恢复。

### 2.5 **Vegetables**（蔬菜）

- **Vegetables add volume with few calories — they stretch a meal.** — 蔬菜体积大、热量低，能把一餐「撑开」。
- **Fiber slows digestion and helps you feel satisfied.** — 纤维放慢消化，更容易有满足感。
- **Colorful veggies bring vitamins and minerals you won't get from rice alone.** — 各色蔬菜提供光吃米饭得不到的维生素矿物质。
- **Aim to fill half your plate with vegetables at lunch and dinner.** — 午晚餐尽量让蔬菜占盘子大约一半。

## 3. Rough proportions — 大致占比（灵活框架）

没有唯一标准答案；下面是一个**日常可参考**的框架，可按活动量微调：

| Food group | Rough share of plate / daily intake | 简中 |
|------------|-------------------------------------|------|
| **Vegetables** | ~40–50% of plate volume | 蔬菜占餐盘体积约四成到五成 |
| **Protein** | ~20–30% of plate; ~1.2–1.6 g per kg body weight for active adults (adjust with a pro if needed) | 蛋白质约占餐盘两成到三成；活跃成人常见约每公斤体重 1.2–1.6 克（个体化请找专业人士） |
| **Carbs** (whole grains, starchy veg, fruit) | ~20–30% of plate; more on hard training days | 碳水约占两成到三成；大训练日可略增 |
| **Fats / oils** | Small amounts for cooking and flavor; not a full plate section | 油脂主要用于烹饪和调味，不宜占满半盘 |

**Spoken summary:**

- **Half veggies, a palm of protein, a fist of carbs — that's my default plate.** — 我的默认餐盘：半盘菜、一掌蛋白质、一拳碳水。
- **I don't weigh every gram; I use my hand as a rough guide.** — 我不天天称克数，用手掌大小当粗略参照。
- **On rest days I shrink the carb portion a bit.** — 休息日我会把碳水份量略减一点。

## 4. Eating order — 进食顺序

**先蔬菜 → 再蛋白质 → 最后碳水** 是一个简单、可执行的策略，不是魔法，但常常有用：

- **Start with vegetables or salad — you fill up on fiber first.** — 先吃蔬菜或沙拉，先用纤维垫肚子。
- **Then eat your protein while you're still hungry enough to finish it.** — 接着吃蛋白质，趁还有胃口把它吃完。
- **Save rice, bread, or noodles for last — you often need less.** — 米饭、面包、面放最后，通常就会少吃一点。
- **This order can blunt blood-sugar spikes after a meal.** — 这种顺序有助于缓和餐后血糖飙升。
- **At a buffet, I walk the veggie line before the carb station.** — 吃自助餐时，我先拿蔬菜，再去碳水区。
- **If I'm eating out, I order a side of greens to eat first.** — 外食时我会先点一份青菜开头。

**Why it works (plain English):**

- **Veggies and protein trigger fullness signals early.** — 蔬菜和蛋白质会较早触发「饱了」的信号。
- **Carbs taste great when you're already partly full — so you stop sooner.** — 半饱后再吃碳水依然好吃，但更容易早点停筷。
- **It's easier than banning rice entirely.** — 这比彻底禁米饭容易坚持得多。

## 5. **Autonomy** — 自主性（不靠 rigid 规则）

原文「自主性」在这里指：**自己判断、自己调整、长期可持续**，而不是被外部清单绑死。

- **I decide what goes on my plate — no one else's shame list.** — 盘子里放什么由我决定，不吃别人的「羞耻清单」。
- **Some days I eat more; some days I eat less — I don't panic.** — 有时多吃、有时少吃，我不因此恐慌。
- **I notice how food makes me feel — energy, sleep, mood — not just the scale.** — 我关注吃完后的精力、睡眠、情绪，不只看体重秤。
- **If a rule makes me binge later, it's not my rule anymore.** — 若某条规则让我之后暴食，那它就不再是我的规则。
- **I can say no to seconds without explaining myself.** — 我可以不加第二份，不必向任何人解释。
- **Progress is a trend over weeks, not one meal or one weigh-in.** — 进步看几周的趋势，不看某一餐或某一次称重。
- **When I travel or eat out, I adapt — I don't quit the whole plan.** — 出差或外食时我会调整，但不会因此全盘放弃。

**Autonomy vs. rigid control:**

| Mindset | Example line | 简中 |
|---------|--------------|------|
| **Autonomy** | *I'll skip dessert tonight because I'm full — not because I'm "bad" for wanting it.* | 今晚不 dessert 是因为饱了，不是因为「想吃就很坏」。 |
| **Rigid control** | *I blew it — I ate rice, so the whole day is ruined.* |  rigid 控制：「完了，吃了米饭，今天全毁了。」 |

## 6. Putting it together — 一日串联示例

- **Breakfast: eggs and spinach first; toast last if I'm still hungry.** — 早餐：先蛋和菠菜；还饿再碰 toast。
- **Lunch: big salad, then chicken; rice only if I need it.** — 午餐：大份沙拉，再吃鸡；需要才加饭。
- **Dinner: stir-fried veggies, then fish; noodles are optional, not automatic.** — 晚餐：先炒菜，再鱼；面是可选，不是默认。
- **I drink water before meals — sometimes thirst feels like hunger.** — 饭前先喝水——有时渴会被误判成饿。
- **I log weight once a week, same time, same scale — not five times a day.** — 体重我每周固定时间、同一台秤称一次，不一天称五次。

## 8. Quick review sentences — 一分钟朗读

1. **Veggies first, protein second, carbs last — that's my default.** — 先菜、再蛋白、最后碳水，这是我的默认顺序。
2. **Sugar and oil add up quietly — I watch drinks and cooking oil.** — 糖和油会在不知不觉里堆热量——我盯饮料和炒菜油。
3. **Protein keeps me full; vegetables stretch the meal.** — 蛋白质顶饱，蔬菜把餐盘撑大。
4. **I aim for balance over weeks, not perfection every meal.** — 我追求几周的整体平衡，不是每一餐都完美。
5. **My goal is a plan I still own six months from now.** — 我的目标是六个月后仍然是我自己的计划。
