# 爸爸是做什么的？What Does Daddy Do?

**索引：** [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/README|个人英文材料书索引]]

**相关（成人向 · 开发设备口语）：** [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/study/macbook-pro-m4-java-developer-work-partner.md|MacBook Pro 工作伙伴（Java）]]

## 给 不多 / Celine 的温馨解释 A Warm Explanation for Celine

---

### 开场 Opening

> **Hi, sweetie! You know how Daddy goes to work every day? Today I want to tell you what I do.**
>
> 嗨，宝贝！你知道爸爸每天都去上班吗？今天我想告诉你我是做什么的。

> **I am a software development engineer — that's a fancy way of saying "computer builder."**
>
> 我是一名软件开发工程师——这是"电脑建造者"的fancy说法。

---

### 用积木来理解 Understanding with Building Blocks

> **Imagine you have a big box of LEGO blocks. You can build houses, cars, castles — anything you want!**
>
> 想象你有一大盒乐高积木。你可以建造房子、汽车、城堡——任何你想要的东西！

> **That's what Daddy does, but with computers. I build special programs that help people do things.**
>
> 这就是爸爸做的事情，但是用电脑。我建造特别的程序来帮助人们做事情。

---

### 爸爸建造了什么？What Does Daddy Build?

> **I build systems that help universities manage teachers and students — like a big digital helper.**
>
> 我建造帮助大学管理老师和学生的系统——就像一个大型数字助手。

> **I also work on e-commerce platforms — that's where people buy things online, like when Mommy orders your toys!**
>
> 我也做电商平台——那是人们在网上买东西的地方，就像妈妈给你买玩具的时候！

---

### 爸爸的一天 Daddy's Day

#### 早上 Morning — 听需求 Listening to Ideas

> **In the morning, I talk to people who need my help. They tell me what they want the computer to do.**
>
> 早上，我和需要我帮助的人聊天。他们告诉我他们想要电脑做什么。

> **It's like when you tell me what kind of LEGO castle you want — I need to listen carefully!**
>
> 就像你告诉我你想要什么样的乐高城堡一样——我需要仔细听！

---

#### 上午 Late Morning — 画设计图 Drawing Plans

> **Then I draw pictures called UML — it's like a map that shows how everything connects.**
>
> 然后我画叫做UML的图——就像一张地图，展示所有东西如何连接。

> **Think of it like drawing a blueprint before building a real house.**
>
> 可以想象成在建造真正的房子之前先画一张设计图。

---

#### 中午 Afternoon — 写代码 Writing Instructions

> **Next, I write code — special instructions that tell the computer what to do.**
>
> 接下来，我写代码——特别的指令告诉电脑要做什么。

> **It's like writing a recipe, but for computers. I use a language called Java.**
>
> 这就像写食谱，但是给电脑的。我使用一种叫做Java的语言。

> **When I write good code, it's like giving the computer clear directions — it knows exactly what to do!**
>
> 当我写好代码，就像给电脑清晰的指引——它就知道该做什么了！

---

#### 测试时间 Testing — 找错误 Finding Mistakes

> **After writing code, I test it. That means I try to find mistakes — called bugs.**
>
> 写完代码后，我测试它。这意味着我努力找错误——叫做bugs（小虫子）。

> **It's like when you check your homework to make sure you didn't make any mistakes.**
>
> 就像你检查作业确保没有犯错一样。

> **Sometimes I find a null pointer — that means something is missing, like when you look for your teddy bear but it's not there!**
>
> 有时候我会找到null pointer——那意味着有些东西不见了，就像你找泰迪熊但它不在那里一样！

---

#### 发布时刻 Release — 让程序上线 Going Live

> **When everything works, I release the program — that means it goes live and real people can use it!**
>
> 当一切正常运行，我发布程序——这意味着它上线，真正的人们可以使用它了！

> **It's like when you finish building your LEGO castle and show it to everyone. You're proud because it works!**
>
> 就像你完成乐高城堡的建造并展示给所有人看。你很骄傲，因为它成功了！

---

### 为什么爸爸的工作很重要？Why Is Daddy's Work Important?

> **When my programs work well, lots of people can do their jobs more easily.**
>
> 当我的程序运行良好，很多人可以更轻松地完成他们的工作。

> **Teachers can manage students better. People can buy things online smoothly. It makes life easier for everyone!**
>
> 老师可以更好地管理学生。人们可以顺畅地在网上购物。这让每个人的生活更轻松！

---

### 宝贝，你也可以！You Can Do It Too, Sweetie!

> **You know what? You already think like a software engineer!**
>
> 你知道吗？你已经像软件工程师一样思考了！

> **When you solve puzzles, build with blocks, or figure out how things work — that's exactly what Daddy does!**
>
> 当你解谜、搭积木，或者弄清楚东西是怎么工作的——那正是爸爸做的事情！

> **Maybe one day you'll build amazing things with computers too. I can't wait to see what you create!**
>
> 也许有一天你也会用电脑建造令人惊叹的东西。我等不及要看你创造出什么了！

## 亲子对话练习 Parent-Child Dialogue Practice

### 场景 1：孩子问爸爸去哪里了 Scenario 1: Where Did Daddy Go?

**孩子 Child**: 爸爸去哪里了？

> **Where did Daddy go?**

**爸爸 Daddy**: 爸爸去上班写代码，就像你玩乐高积木一样！

> **Daddy went to work to write code — just like when you play with LEGO blocks!**

---

### 场景 2：解释Bug Scenario 2: Explaining Bugs

**孩子 Child**: 爸爸，什么是bug？

> **Daddy, what's a bug?**

**爸爸 Daddy**: Bug就是程序里的小错误，就像你画画时画错了线，需要擦掉重画。

> **A bug is a small mistake in the program — like when you draw a wrong line and need to erase it and draw again.**

---

### 场景 3：鼓励好奇 Scenario 3: Encouraging Curiosity

**爸爸 Daddy**: 你在搭积木吗？真棒！你知道吗，这和爸爸的工作很像。

> **Are you building with blocks? Great! You know, it's a lot like what Daddy does.**

**孩子 Child**: 真的吗？

> **Really?**

**爸爸 Daddy**: 真的！你搭积木，爸爸搭电脑程序。我们都在建造东西！

> **Yes! You build with blocks, and Daddy builds computer programs. We're both builders!**
