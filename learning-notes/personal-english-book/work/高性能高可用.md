
研发今天发现了一个ZME的问题：
问题原因：在故障恢复的场景下，框架在处理定时器消息时，触发了一个ZMI的bug，导致ZMI消息的滑窗被填满，备机rejion失败
复现方法：ZME在故障回复前，设置多个定时器任务，故障恢复后，ZME主启动成功，备机在jion的阶段，恢复之前的定时器，已到期的定时器被触发，同时由于同步主节点，新的消息，注册新的定时器。产生了并发的场景下，会产生该问题。
修复影响场景：ZME定时器相关场景，可以选择其一产生多个定时器任务。ZME撮合配置一定延迟，保证产生定时任务。需要测试持续产生定时任务场景下的，单个节点宕机，主备来回jion。多个节点宕机故障恢复。

R&D discovered an ZME issue today:
Problem cause: In the scenario of fault recovery, when the framework processes timer messages, it triggers a bug in AMI, causing the sliding window of AMI messages to be filled and the standby machine's rejion to fail.
Reproduction method: Before the ZME recovers from a fault, multiple timer tasks are set. After the fault is recovered, the ZME primary starts successfully. During the join phase of the standby machine, the previous timers are restored, and the expired timers are triggered. Meanwhile, due to synchronization with the primary node, new messages register new timers. In a concurrent scenario, this issue will occur.
Repair impact scenarios: ZME timer-related scenarios, where one can be selected to generate multiple timer tasks. ZME matching configuration has a certain delay to ensure the generation of scheduled tasks. It is necessary to test single-node downtime, master-slave switching back and forth, and fault recovery from multiple-node downtime in scenarios where scheduled tasks are continuously generated.
