# Jeff 的一天

即将到上班时间，请及时打卡呦！
It's almost time to start work, please punch in promptly!

注意休息，请及时打卡。
Please take a break and punch in/out in a timely manner.

茶水间谁的饭盒，请自己收好哦，放久了阿姨会处理掉的
Whose lunch box is in the pantry? Please put it away. If left for too long, the cleaning staff will dispose of it.

深圳职场三月第二次已消杀完毕，请大家上班后清洗一下自己的食物、水杯和水壶；并且请不要在工位的垃圾桶里放没吃完的水果、没喝完的饮料这些湿垃圾，以免有引来小飞虫。

谢谢大家！

行政小组

The second pest control in March for the Shenzhen workplace has been completed. Please clean your food, water cups, and kettles after coming to work; and please do not put wet waste such as uneaten fruits and unfinished drinks in the trash cans at your workstations to avoid attracting small flying insects.

Thank you all!

Administrative Team 

各位小伙伴们，下班的时请记得将自己工位上的小电器断电及关闭个人电脑/显示屏，确保用电安全，谢谢配合！

Dear colleagues, please remember to turn off small electrical appliances at your workstations and shut down your personal computers/displays when leaving work to ensure electrical safety. Thank you for your cooperation!

各位辛苦了，明天顺利

Everyone has worked hard, wishing you a smooth day tomorrow.
