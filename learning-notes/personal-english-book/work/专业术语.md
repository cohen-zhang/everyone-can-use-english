快速交易柜台系统运行规则（生产环境）：  
1、系统启动：  
1.1、连续交易日晚间，主系统清算完成后（时间不确定，21:00-第二天凌晨6:00都有可能）启动。  
1.2、节假日前一个交易日晚上不启动；节假日最后一个自然日，16:00之后启动，一般在16:30-21:00之间。  
1.3、启动后，上游周边系统即可连接、登录。  
2、系统关闭：交易日19:00左右关闭，周末有升级变更，周五可能提前到17:00左右关闭。  
3、业务开始：交易日08:30，HTS开始接受委托（在此之前委托报错不在交易时段）。
4、业务结束：交易日15:37系统收盘，停止接受委托（在此之后委托报错系统已收盘）。  
5、假期（包括周末），系统是关闭状态或测试状态。

Operation Rules for the Fast Trading Counter System (Production Environment):
1. System Startup:
1.1. On consecutive trading day evenings, it will be launched after the main system's clearing is completed (the time is uncertain and could be anytime between 21:00 and 6:00 the next morning).
1.2. It will not start on the evening of the trading day before a holiday; on the last natural day of a holiday, it will start after 16:00, generally between 16:30 and 21:00.
1.3 After startup, the upstream peripheral systems can connect and log in.
2. System Shutdown: The system will shut down around 19:00 on trading days. There will be upgrades and changes on weekends, and it may shut down around 17:00 on Fridays.
3. Business Commencement: At 08:30 on trading days, HTS begins to accept orders (orders submitted before this time will result in an error indicating they are outside the trading hours).
4. Business End: The system closes at 15:37 on trading days, ceasing to accept orders (orders submitted after this time will result in an error indicating that the system has closed).
5. During holidays (including weekends), the system is in a closed or test state.
