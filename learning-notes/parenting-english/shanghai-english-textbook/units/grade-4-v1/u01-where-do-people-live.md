## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where do you live? | 你住在哪里？ | Where do you live? — I live in a city. |
| Where does he / she live? | 他/她住在哪里？ | Where does she live? — She lives in the country. |
| I live in … | 我住在…… | I live in a flat in a city. |
| Where is your home? | 你家在哪里？ | Where is your home? — My home is in the country. |
| There is / are … | 某处有…… | There are many tall buildings in the city. |
| This is my … | 这是我的…… | This is my house. / This is my flat. |

## 2. 单词 — Words

| Word | 中文 | 亲子提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **city** | 城市 | busy streets, tall buildings |
| **country** | 乡村；农村 | quiet；也可说 the countryside |
| **building** | 建筑物；大楼 | tall buildings |
| **flat** | 公寓 | UK 常用；US 常说 apartment |
| **house** | 房子 | 对比 flat |
| **street** | 街道 | busy streets |
| **farm** | 农场 | in the country |
| **home** | 家 | My home is … |
| **neighbourhood** | 社区；街坊 | 待核拼写：教材或作 neighborhood |
| **bedroom** | 卧室 | 房间词 |
| **living room** | 客厅 |  |
| **kitchen** | 厨房 |  |
| **quiet** | 安静的 | The country is quiet. |
| **busy** | 忙碌的；繁忙的 | busy streets |

## 3. 词组 — Phrases

| Phrase | 中文 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a lot of** | 许多 | There are a lot of people. |
| **tall buildings** | 高楼 | Cities have tall buildings. |
| **busy streets** | 繁忙的街道 |  |
| **come with me** | 跟我来 | Come with me to the city! |
| **be good for …** | 对……有好处 | The country is good for you. |
| **in a city / in the country** | 在城市 / 在乡村 | I live in the country. |

## 4. 亲子练一句 — At home with Celine

- **Where do we live, Celine?** — 我们住在哪里，不多？ `亲子`
- **We live in a flat in Shenzhen.** — 我们住在深圳的公寓里。 `亲子`
- **Is it quiet or busy near our home?** — 家附近安静还是热闹？ `亲子`
