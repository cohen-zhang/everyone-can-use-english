## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How many … are there? | 有多少……？ | How many apples are there? — There are ten. |
| How much is / are …? | 多少钱？／多少量？ | How much is it? — It's five yuan. |
| There are … | 有……（复数） | There are twenty students. |
| I have … | 我有…… | I have three books. |
| What number is this? | 这是几？ | What number is this? — It's fifteen. |
