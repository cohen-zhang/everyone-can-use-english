## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do our grandparents do? | 祖辈做什么？ | They cook / read / walk in the park. |
| What does Grandpa / Grandma do? | 爷爷／奶奶做什么？ | What does Grandma do? — She cooks dinner. |
| He / She … every day. | 他／她每天…… | He reads the newspaper every day. |
| They like to … | 他们喜欢…… | They like to take a walk. |
