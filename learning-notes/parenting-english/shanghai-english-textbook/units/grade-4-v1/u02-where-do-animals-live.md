## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where is the …? | ……在哪里？（单数） | Where is the eagle? — It's in the tree. |
| Where are the …? | ……在哪里？（复数） | Where are the honeybees? — They're in the hive. |
| Where does the … live? | ……住在哪里？（单数动物） | Where does the owl live? — It lives in a tree hole. |
| Where do … live? | ……住在哪里？（复数） | Where do eagles live? — They live in big nests. |
| Is it in the …? / Are they in the …? | 它/它们在……吗？ | Are they in the hive? — Yes, they are. |
