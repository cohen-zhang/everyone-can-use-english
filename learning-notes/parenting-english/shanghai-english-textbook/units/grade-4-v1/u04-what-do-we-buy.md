## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do / does … buy? | ……买什么？ | What do they buy? — They buy bread. |
| What would you like? | 你想要什么？ | What would you like? — I'd like a bottle of water. |
| We need … | 我们需要…… | We need some apples. |
| How much is / are …? | 多少钱？ | How much are the carrots? — They're five yuan. |
| I'd like … | 我想要…… | I'd like a bar of chocolate. |
