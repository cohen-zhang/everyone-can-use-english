## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（My sunflower）与 **Extend**（Useful plants）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · My sunflower（日记体）

> **10th April: I put a sunflower seed in the soil. They're small black seeds.** — 4 月 10 日：我把向日葵种子放进土里。它们是小黑种子。  
> **22nd May: Now the plant has some green leaves. But I can't see the roots. They grow down.** — 5 月 22 日：现在有绿叶了。根向下长。  
> **21st July: Now my sunflower is tall with a big yellow flower. The flower is round. My family love it.** — 7 月 21 日：向日葵高了，有大黄花。花是圆的。家人很喜欢。  
> **3rd September: My sunflower has a lot of seeds. People can make oil from the seeds. Sunflowers are amazing!** — 9 月 3 日：有很多种子。人们可以用种子榨油。向日葵真奇妙！

**Think（课本问）：** What's amazing about sunflowers? — 向日葵哪里奇妙？

---

### Extend · Useful plants

> **Carrots are good to eat. They are good for our eyes. We can make carrot juice and carrot cake from them.** — 胡萝卜好吃。对眼睛好。我们可以做胡萝卜汁和蛋糕。  
> **Many people like tomatoes. Some people grow them at home. We can use tomatoes to make soup and salad.** — 许多人喜欢番茄。有人在家种。我们可以做汤和沙拉。  
> **Mint has a special taste. We can drink mint tea or eat mint candy. We also have mint ice cream.** — 薄荷味道特别。我们可以喝薄荷茶、吃薄荷糖。还有薄荷冰淇淋。

**Think（课本问）：** How do plants help us? — 植物怎样帮助我们？
