## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do you wear on a … day? | ……天你穿什么？ | What do you wear on a sunny day? — I wear a T-shirt and shorts. |
| What do we wear? | 我们穿什么？ | We wear coats when it's cold. |
| I wear … | 我穿…… | I wear a raincoat on rainy days. |
| What clothes do you have? | 你有什么衣服？ | I have a shirt, a coat and trousers. |
| I have … for the holiday trip. | 假期旅行我有…… | I have a new skirt for the holiday trip. |
