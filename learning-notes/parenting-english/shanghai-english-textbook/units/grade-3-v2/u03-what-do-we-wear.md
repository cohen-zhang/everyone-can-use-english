## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（What do students wear?）与 **Extend**（Clothes for a busy day）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · What do students wear?

> **Trousers, shorts, a skirt … What do you wear at school? Students around the world wear different clothes. Let's take a look.** — 长裤、短裤、短裙……你在学校穿什么？世界各地的学生穿不同的衣服。我们来看看。  
> **At my school, we wear a uniform. I wear a shirt and a skirt. On a sunny day, I wear a cap. — Wu Chen** — 在我的学校，我们穿校服。我穿衬衫和短裙。晴天我戴帽子。——吴晨  
> **At my school, we don't wear a uniform. On a cloudy day, I wear a T-shirt and trousers. But on a sunny day, I wear shorts. And, of course, I wear a cap too! — Gary** — 在我的学校，我们不穿校服。阴天我穿 T 恤和长裤。晴天我穿短裤。当然，我也戴帽子！—— Gary

**Think（课本问）：** What do Wu Chen and Gary wear at school? — 吴晨穿校服；Gary 穿 T 恤、长裤或短裤。

---

### Explore · Chant（What do you wear?）

> **What do you wear on a sunny day? I wear a cap and shorts. What do you wear on a rainy day? I wear a coat and boots.** — 晴天你穿什么？我戴帽子穿短裤。雨天你穿什么？我穿外套和靴子。

---

### Extend · Clothes for a busy day

> **Hi, I'm Jingjing! I live in Beijing. Friday is a busy day. I need to change clothes a lot.** — 你好，我是晶晶！我住在北京。星期五很忙。我要换很多次衣服。  
> **1 In the morning, we start school. I wear my school uniform: a shirt and a skirt. I bring my schoolbag.** — 早上我们上学。我穿校服：衬衫和短裙。我背书包。  
> **2 In the afternoon, we have PE. I put on a T-shirt and shorts. I change my shoes. I can run fast.** — 下午我们上体育课。我换上 T 恤和短裤。我换鞋。我跑得很快。  
> **3 After school, I help in the park. We pick up rubbish. We wear special T-shirts.** — 放学后我在公园帮忙。我们捡垃圾。我们穿特别的 T 恤。  
> **4 My busy day is over. It's time to go to bed. I'm tired but happy today!** — 忙碌的一天结束了。该睡觉了。我今天累但开心！

**Think（课本问）：** Why do you change clothes during the day? — 为什么一天里要换衣服？
