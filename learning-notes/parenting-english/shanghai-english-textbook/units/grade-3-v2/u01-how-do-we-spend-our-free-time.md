## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do we spend our free time? | 我们怎么度过空闲时间？ | We read / draw / play. |
| What do you do in your free time? | 空闲时你做什么？ | I watch TV. / I play football. |
| I like to … | 我喜欢…… | I like to read books. |
| Do you … in your free time? | 空闲时你……吗？ | Do you draw? — Yes, I do. |

## 4. 亲子练一句 — At home with Celine

- **How do you spend your free time, Celine?** — 不多，你空闲时间做什么？ `亲子`
- **I like to draw and read after homework.** — 作业后我喜欢画画和看书。 `亲子`
- **Let's spend free time outside this weekend.** — 这周末我们去外面玩。 `亲子`
