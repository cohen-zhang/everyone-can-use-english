## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Nini's new school）与 **Extend**（Colourful school life around the world）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Nini's new school

> **Lanlan: Welcome, Nini! My name is Lanlan. I can show you around the school. Come with me!** — 兰兰：欢迎，妮妮！我叫兰兰。我可以带你参观学校。跟我来！  
> **Lanlan: This is our classroom. Look! This is my picture. — Nini: It's very good. — Lanlan: I like painting in the art room!** — 兰兰：这是我们的教室。看！这是我的画。——妮妮：很好。——兰兰：我喜欢在美术室画画！  
> **Nini: I like playing basketball and swimming. Is there a swimming pool in our school? — Lanlan: No, there isn't. But there's a sports ground. Let's go!** — 妮妮：我喜欢打篮球和游泳。我们学校有游泳池吗？——兰兰：没有。但有运动场。我们走！  
> **Lanlan: Hi, Haohao! This is Nini. She likes playing basketball too. — Haohao: Come and play tomorrow. — Nini: Thank you! Lanlan, is there also a library? — Lanlan: Come and see!** — 兰兰：嗨，浩浩！这是妮妮。她也喜欢打篮球。——浩浩：明天来一起玩吧。——妮妮：谢谢！兰兰，还有图书馆吗？——兰兰：来看看！  
> **Lanlan: Look! There are new books in this box. — Nini: Oh! I love this story! — Lanlan: What is it about? — Nini: It's about a girl. She starts in a new school. She has lots of fun!** — 兰兰：看！这箱子里有新书。——妮妮：哦！我爱这个故事！——兰兰：讲什么？——妮妮：讲一个女孩到新学校，玩得很开心！

**Think（课本问）：** What can students do in Nini's new school? — 画画、打篮球、去运动场、去图书馆。
