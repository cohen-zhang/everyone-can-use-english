## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do we do at school? | 我们在学校做什么？ | We read, write and play. |
| What subjects do you have? | 你有什么课？ | We have Chinese, maths and English. |
| We have … class. | 我们有……课 | We have PE / art / music. |
| What do you do in class? | 课上你做什么？ | I listen to the teacher. |
| Do you like school? | 你喜欢学校吗？ | Yes, I do. / It's fun. |
