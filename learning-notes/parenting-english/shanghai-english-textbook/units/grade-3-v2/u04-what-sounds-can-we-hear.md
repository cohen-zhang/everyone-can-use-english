## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What sounds can we hear? | 我们能听到什么声音？ | We can hear birds / cars / rain. |
| What can you hear? | 你能听见什么？ | What can you hear? — I can hear a bird. |
| I can hear … | 我能听见…… | I can hear the wind. |
| Listen! What's that sound? | 听！那是什么声音？ | It's a dog. |
| Is it loud / quiet? | 大声／安静吗？ | It's loud. / It's quiet. |

## 4. 亲子练一句 — At home with Celine

- **What sounds can we hear near our home?** — 家附近我们能听到什么声音？ `亲子`
