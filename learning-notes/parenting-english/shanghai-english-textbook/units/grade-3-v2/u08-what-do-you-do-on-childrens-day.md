## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Children's Day survey）与 **Extend**（Children's Day around the world）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Children's Day survey

> **Haohao is doing a survey about Children's Day.** — 浩浩在做一份关于儿童节的调查。  
> **What do you do on Children's Day? — I go to the science museum. It's fun!** — 儿童节你做什么？——我去科技馆。很好玩！  
> **Liangliang, what do you do on Children's Day? — My family go to the zoo. I love elephants.** — 亮亮，儿童节你做什么？——我家去动物园。我爱大象。  
> **Xuanxuan, what about you? — My family go to the cinema. I like Monkey King. After the film, we go to a pizza shop. Sometimes I eat too much!** — 萱萱，你呢？——我家去电影院。我喜欢孙悟空。看完电影我们去披萨店。有时我吃太多！  
> **In our class, three classmates go to the zoo … four go to the cinema … three go to the museum … ten go out to eat!** — 我们班三个同学去动物园……四个去电影院……三个去博物馆……十个出去吃饭！

**Think（课本问）：** What do Haohao's classmates do on Children's Day? — 去动物园、电影院、博物馆或外出吃饭。

### Extend · Children's Day around the world

| # | English | 中文 |

| 2 | **Ariya is from Thailand. Children's Day is on the second Saturday in January. Ariya likes to go to the zoo! She has a great time there.** | Ariya 来自泰国。儿童节在一月第二个星期六。Ariya 喜欢去动物园！她玩得很开心。 |
