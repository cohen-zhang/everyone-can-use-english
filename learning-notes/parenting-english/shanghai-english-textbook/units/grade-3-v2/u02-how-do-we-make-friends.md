## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（At the beach）与 **Extend**（Elliot's new friend）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · At the beach

> **1 Dandan: Hi there! My name is Dandan. Your sandcastle is great! — Lanlan: Thank you. I'm Lanlan. That's my brother, Junjun. Do you like playing in the sand? — Dandan: Yes, I do. — Lanlan: Great! You can join me!** — 丹丹：嗨！我叫丹丹。你的沙堡真棒！——兰兰：谢谢。我是兰兰。那是我弟弟君君。你喜欢玩沙子吗？——丹丹：喜欢。——兰兰：太好了！你可以加入我！  
> **2 Kaikai: Hey there. I'm Kaikai. I'm Dandan's brother. — Dandan: Come here, Kaikai! Meet my new friend Lanlan and her brother Junjun. — Kaikai: Hello, Kaikai.** — 凯凯：嘿。我是凯凯。我是丹丹的哥哥。——丹丹：凯凯，过来！认识我的新朋友兰兰和她弟弟君君。——凯凯：你好。  
> **3 Do you like playing beach volleyball? — Yes, I do! Let's play together!** — 你们喜欢打沙滩排球吗？——喜欢！我们一起玩吧！  
> **4 Lanlan and Junjun have new friends. Me too. I'm so happy!** — 兰兰和君君有了新朋友。我也是。我很开心！

**Think（课本问）：** How do the children make friends? — 一起玩沙子、介绍朋友、一起打排球。

### Extend · Elliot's new friend

> **1 Elliot is sad. He sees a tortoise. Tuti: Hello, I'm Tuti. Elliot: I'm Elliot. I can't find my family. Tuti: Let's look.** — Elliot 很难过。他看见一只乌龟。Tuti：你好，我是 Tuti。Elliot：我是 Elliot。我找不到家人。Tuti：我们找找。  
> **2 Tuti: Look! Is that your family? Elliot: No! Those are zebras.** — Tuti：看！那是你的家人吗？Elliot：不！那是斑马。  
> **3 Elliot and Tuti eat, play and sleep. Tuti: Look! Is that your family? Elliot: No! Those are lions. Elliot and Tuti are afraid. They run away.** — Elliot 和 Tuti 一起吃、玩、睡。Tuti：看！那是你的家人吗？Elliot：不！那是狮子。他们害怕，跑开了。  
> **4 Tuti: Look! Elephants. Elliot: That's my family. Tuti: YES! Elliot: Thanks, Tuti. Elliot gets a new friend.** — Tuti：看！大象。Elliot：那是我的家人。Tuti：对！Elliot：谢谢，Tuti。Elliot 有了新朋友。

**Think（课本问）：** What do friends do for each other? — 朋友为彼此做什么？
