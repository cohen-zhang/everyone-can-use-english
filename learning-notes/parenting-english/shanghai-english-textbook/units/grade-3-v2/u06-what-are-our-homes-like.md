## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Our homes）与 **Extend**（Special homes around the world）原文 + 简中对照，便于和 Celine 跟读、填空复述。

### Explore · Our homes（两封邮件）

**To:** Lotte · **From:** Panpan · **Subject:** My new home

> **Dear Lotte,**  
> 亲爱的 Lotte，
>
> **I can't wait to show you my new home!** — 我迫不及待想给你看看我的新家！  
> **There's a kitchen, a dining room, a living room, a bathroom, three bedrooms and a garden.** — 有厨房、餐厅、客厅、卫生间、三间卧室和一个花园。  
> **In my bedroom, there's a desk.** — 我卧室里有一张书桌。  
> **I like reading and writing at my desk.** — 我喜欢在书桌前读书写字。  
> **There's a garden in front of my bedroom.** — 我卧室前面有一个花园。  
> **I like looking out of the window.** — 我喜欢朝窗外看。  
> **How about your home? What's in your bedroom?** — 你家呢？你卧室里有什么？  
> **Best wishes,**  
> **Panpan**

---

**To:** Panpan · **From:** Lotte · **Subject:** RE: My new home

> **Dear Panpan,**  
> 亲爱的 Panpan，
>
> **Thank you for your photos.** — 谢谢你的照片。  
> **Let me show you my home.** — 我来给你看看我的家。  
> **The rooms in my home are small.** — 我家里的房间很小。  
> **In my bedroom, there's a teddy bear on the bed.** — 我卧室里，床上有一只泰迪熊。  
> **It's my favourite toy.** — 它是我最喜欢的玩具。  
> **There's no garden.** — 没有花园。  
> **That's because my home is a boat!** — 那是因为我的家是一艘船！  
> **Do you like my home?** — 你喜欢我的家吗？  
> **Best wishes,**  
> **Lotte**

**Think（课本问）：** What are Panpan's and Lotte's homes like? — Panpan 家大、有花园；Lotte 家住船上、房间小、床上有 teddy bear。

> **There's a chair in my room.** — 我房间里有一把椅子。  
> **I sit by the window,** — 我坐在窗边，  
> **And read in the light.** — 在光线下读书。

### Extend · Special homes around the world

> **There are many kinds of homes around the world. Some of them are very special.**  
> 世界各地有很多种家。其中一些非常特别。

| # | English | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **I'm from the Arctic. My home is made of snow, but it's warm.** | 我来自北极。我的家由雪建成，但很暖和。 |
| 2 | **I'm from Mongolia. We move from place to place. We like our home!** | 我来自蒙古。我们从一个地方搬到另一个地方。我们喜欢我们的家！ |
| 3 | **I'm from the UK. My sister and I have a tree house! We have so much fun there!** | 我来自英国。我和妹妹有一间树屋！我们在那里玩得很开心！ |
| 4 | **I'm from China. My home isn't on a street. It's by the water! It's beautiful.** | 我来自中国。我的家不在街上。它在水边！很美。 |

**Think（课本问）：** Do you know any other special homes? What are they like? — 你还知道哪些特别的家？它们是什么样的？
