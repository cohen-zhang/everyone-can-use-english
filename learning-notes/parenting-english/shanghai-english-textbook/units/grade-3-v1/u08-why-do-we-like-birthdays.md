## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Taotao's birthday）与 **Extend**（A special birthday）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Taotao's birthday

> **1 Taotao is 10 years old today. Happy birthday, Taotao! Come and see your gifts!** — 涛涛今天 10 岁了。生日快乐，涛涛！来看看你的礼物！  
> **2 I have a gift for you. Do you like it? Yes. What a lovely picture! Thank you, Lele! Wow! I love this toy plane! Thank you, Mum and Dad. Let's go out!** — 我有礼物给你。你喜欢吗？喜欢。多可爱的画！谢谢你，乐乐！哇！我爱这架玩具飞机！谢谢爸爸妈妈。我们出去吧！  
> **4 The family meet Ms Li. Good afternoon, Ms Li! Good afternoon. It's nice to see you again. What's in your box, Taotao? Some books, balls and kites!** — 一家人见到李老师。下午好，李老师！下午好。很高兴又见到您。涛涛，你盒子里有什么？一些书、球和风筝！  
> **5 Taotao shares his toys. It's windy. Let's fly a kite. Yeah! I like noodles.** — 涛涛分享玩具。有风。我们放风筝吧。好！我喜欢面条。  
> **6 What a great birthday!** — 多棒的生日！

**Think（课本问）：** What does Taotao do on his birthday? — 收礼物、见老师、分享玩具、放风筝、吃面条。

---

### Explore · Chant（A great birthday）

> **This is Fred. He's my friend. Today is his birthday. I am eight. Now he is too.** — 这是 Fred。他是我朋友。今天是他生日。我八岁。现在他也八岁。  
> **We go to his house to play. We eat lots of cake. We play lots of games. We have lots of fun. What a great birthday!** — 我们去他家玩。我们吃很多蛋糕。我们玩很多游戏。我们玩得很开心。多棒的生日！

---

### Extend · A special birthday

> **Today is a very special day at Rainbow Primary School. We are ten this year. We have a special birthday party.** — 今天在彩虹小学是非常特别的一天。我们今年十岁。我们有一个特别的生日派对。  
> **Our parents write us letters. We read the letters.** — 父母给我们写信。我们读这些信。  
> **We put on a show! We sing and dance.** — 我们表演节目！我们唱歌跳舞。  
> **We have a big cake! We light the candles. We make wishes. It is great to be ten.** — 我们有大蛋糕！我们点蜡烛。我们许愿。十岁真棒。

**Think（课本问）：** Do you celebrate your birthday with your classmates at school? What do you do together? — 你在学校和同学一起过生日吗？你们一起做什么？
