## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do we eat? | 我们吃什么？ | We eat rice and vegetables. |
| What do we drink? | 我们喝什么？ | We drink milk / juice. |
| What do you like? | 你喜欢什么？ | What do you like? — I like apples. |
| I like / I don't like … | 我喜欢／不喜欢…… | I like bananas. I don't like tomatoes. |
| Here is / are … | 这儿有…… | Here are some apples. Here is a glass of milk. |

## 4. 亲子练一句 — At home with Celine

- **What do we eat for breakfast?** — 早饭我们吃什么？ `亲子`
- **I like fruit. I don't like onions.** — 我喜欢水果。我不喜欢洋葱。 `亲子`
