## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（In the park）与 **Extend**（Let's play games!）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · In the park

> **1 The children are in a park. Children: Hello, Tingting. — Tingting: Hello.** — 孩子们在公园里。孩子们：你好，婷婷。——婷婷：你好。  
> **2 Yaoyao can fly a kite! She is happy! Chenchen can ride a bike. But he is too fast. Chenchen: It's fun. Woo hoo!** — 瑶瑶会放风筝！她很开心！晨晨会骑车，但太快了。晨晨：真好玩。哇！  
> **3 Chenchen: Oh no! Huihui can run fast and help Chenchen.** — 晨晨：哦不！慧慧跑得快，帮了晨晨。  
> **4 Chenchen: Hey! Let's skip together. Children: OK! Tingting: I can't skip. But I can get some juice. Are you thirsty?** — 晨晨：嘿！我们一起跳绳吧。孩子们：好！婷婷：我不会跳。但我可以去拿果汁。你们渴吗？  
> **5 Tingting can skateboard. She brings some juice. Dangdang: Wow, Tingting! Thank you!** — 婷婷会滑滑板。她带来了果汁。当当：哇，婷婷！谢谢你！

**Think（课本问）：** How do the children have fun in the park? — 放风筝、骑车、跑步、跳绳、滑滑板、分享果汁。

---

### Explore · Chant（I can … I can't …）

> **I can't fly up in the sky, but I can fly a kite up high.** — 我不能飞上天，但我能把风筝放得很高。  
> **I can't ride a bike at all, but I can play football.** — 我根本不会骑车，但我能踢足球。  
> **I can skip and I can run. And I can jump — it's fun!** — 我会跳绳也会跑。我还会跳——真好玩！

---

### Extend · Let's play games!

> **We play games every day. We have a lot of fun.** — 我们每天都玩游戏。我们玩得很开心。  
> **Jump, jump and jump. One, two, three! We can play hopscotch. You and me!** — 跳啊跳啊跳。一、二、三！我们可以玩跳房子。你和我！  
> **Move the rope round and round. We can skip up and down.** — 绳子转啊转。我们可以上下跳绳。  
> **Two teams can play tug of war. Pull the rope more and more!** — 两队可以拔河。把绳子越拉越紧！

**Think（课本问）：** What games do you like to play? Why? — 你喜欢玩什么游戏？为什么？
