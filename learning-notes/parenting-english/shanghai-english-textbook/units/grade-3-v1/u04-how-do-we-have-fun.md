## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do we have fun? | 我们怎么玩？ | We can run and play games. |
| I / He / She can … | 我／他／她会…… | I can ride a bike. She can skip. |
| I / He / She can't … | 我／他／她不会…… | I can't skateboard yet. |
| Let's …! | 我们……吧！ | Let's play games! |
| Can you …? | 你会……吗？ | Can you fly a kite? — Yes, I can. |
