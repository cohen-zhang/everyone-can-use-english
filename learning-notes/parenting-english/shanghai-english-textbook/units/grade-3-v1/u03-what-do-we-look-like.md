## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Minmin's haircut）与 **Extend**（People look different）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Minmin's haircut

> **Look at your hair, Minmin. It's too long. Minmin, I can't see your eyes. Now sit on this chair, please!** — 看看你的头发，敏敏。太长了。敏敏，我看不见你的眼睛。请坐在这把椅子上！  
> **But I like it!** — 可我喜欢！  
> **Snip! Snip!** — 剪！剪！  
> **Look! Do you like your haircut? — I like my haircut, Mum. Oh! I have short hair. You look great! Thanks, Mum.** — 看！你喜欢你的新发型吗？——我喜欢，妈妈。哦！我留短发了。你看起来很棒！谢谢，妈妈。

**Think（课本问）：** What does Minmin look like before and after his haircut? — 剪发前头发长；剪发后短发，眼睛更清楚。

---

### Explore · Chant（Look at my face）

> **Look at my face. Look at my nose. I have a round face. I have a small nose.** — 看看我的脸。看看我的鼻子。我有圆脸。我有小鼻子。  
> **Look at my eyes. Look at my hair. I have bright eyes. I have long black hair.** — 看看我的眼睛。看看我的头发。我有明亮的眼睛。我有黑色长发。

---

### Extend · People look different

> **People around the world look different. Some people have brown eyes. Some have blue eyes. Some people have black hair. Some have brown hair.** — 世界各地的人看起来不一样。有些人是棕色眼睛，有些是蓝色眼睛。有些人是黑头发，有些是棕头发。  
> **What do you look like? Do you have long or short hair? Do you have a big or small mouth?** — 你长什么样？你是长发还是短发？你的嘴大还是小？  
> **People look different. It is okay to be different.** — 人各不同。不一样也没关系。

**Think（课本问）：** How do people around the world look different? — 眼睛、头发等外貌不同。
