## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How do you feel? | 你感觉怎么样？ | How do you feel? — I feel happy. |
| How are you? | 你好吗？ | How are you? — I'm fine. / I'm tired. |
| I feel / I'm … | 我感觉……／我…… | I feel sad. / I'm angry. |
| You look … | 你看起来…… | You look tired. How do you feel? |
| Don't be … | 别…… | Don't be angry. / Don't be sad. |

## 4. 亲子练一句 — At home with Celine

- **How do you feel after school, Celine?** — 不多，放学后感觉怎么样？ `亲子`
- **I feel happy when we play together.** — 我们一起玩的时候我很开心。 `亲子`
- **You look tired. Do you feel hungry?** — 你看起来累了。饿了吗？ `亲子`
