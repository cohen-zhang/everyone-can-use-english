## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（We like different weather）与 **Extend**（On a windy day）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · We like different weather

> **1 How's the weather today? It's rainy. I don't like the rain.** — 今天天气怎么样？下雨了。我不喜欢雨。  
> **2 It's windy today. I like the wind. I can fly my kite. Let's go to the park. Hooray! Oh, dear!** — 今天有风。我喜欢风。我可以放风筝。我们去公园吧。好耶！哦，天哪！  
> **3 It's hot today. I don't like the sun. I like the rain. Oh no, my hat and my plants! I don't like the wind.** — 今天很热。我不喜欢太阳。我喜欢雨。哦不，我的帽子和植物！我不喜欢风。  
> **4 I like the sun. I have a big harvest. I'm very happy.** — 我喜欢太阳。我大丰收了。我非常开心。

**Think（课本问）：** What weather do the children and the farmer like? Why? — 孩子与农夫喜欢的天气不同，各有原因。

---

### Explore · Chant（How's the weather?）

> **How's the weather? How's the weather today? Is it sunny? Is it sunny? Can we go out and play?** — 天气怎么样？今天天气怎么样？是晴天吗？是晴天吗？我们能出去玩吗？  
> **It's sunny. It's sunny. It's sunny today. We can go to the park. Let's go there and play.** — 是晴天。是晴天。今天是晴天。我们可以去公园。我们去那儿玩吧。

---

### Extend · On a windy day

> **Woosh! Woosh! The wind is strong. My kite goes up. The string is long.** — 呼！呼！风很大。我的风筝飞起来了。线很长。  
> **Oh no! Oh no! It's in the tree. Please, Mr Wind, don't take it from me!** — 哦不！哦不！它在树上了。风先生，请别把它从我这里带走！  
> **Woosh! Woosh! There it goes! My kite is free. The strong wind blows!** — 呼！呼！它飞走了！我的风筝自由了。强风在吹！

**Think（课本问）：** What can you do on a windy day? — 有风的日子你可以做什么？
