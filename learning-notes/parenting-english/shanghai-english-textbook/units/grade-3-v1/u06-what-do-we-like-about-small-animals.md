## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Coco's day in the park）与 **Extend**（A frog's life）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Coco's day in the park

> **1 It's a nice day today. Woof! Woof! What is it, Coco? Oh! It's a cat! Look! It has small ears. It's cute. I like its big eyes.** — 今天天气很好。汪！汪！可可，那是什么？哦！是只猫！看！它耳朵小。很可爱。我喜欢它的大眼睛。  
> **4 Look! That's a squirrel! Is this a squirrel too? Yes. They have big long tails.** — 看！那是松鼠！这也是松鼠吗？是的。它们有又长又大的尾巴。  
> **6 Oh! They're frogs! They're small. I like frogs. They eat bad bugs.** — 哦！是青蛙！它们很小。我喜欢青蛙。它们吃害虫。

**Think（课本问）：** What do the children like about the small animals in the park? — 猫眼睛大、松鼠尾巴长、青蛙吃害虫。

---

### Explore · Chant（What is it? / What are they?）

> **What is it? What is it? It is a little frog.** — 它是什么？它是什么？它是一只小青蛙。  
> **What are they? What are they? They are cute, little dogs.** — 它们是什么？它们是什么？它们是可爱的小狗。

---

### Extend · A frog's life

> **It is a small egg. It can't swim or jump.** — 它是一颗小卵。它不会游泳也不会跳。  
> **It is a baby frog. Its tail is long. It can swim.** — 它是小蝌蚪。尾巴很长。它会游泳。  
> **It is still a baby frog. It has four legs. Its tail is small and short. It can swim and jump.** — 它还是小青蛙。它有四条腿。尾巴又小又短。它会游泳也会跳。  
> **It is a frog! It has four legs. It has no tail. Its mouth is big. It can swim and jump.** — 它变成青蛙了！四条腿，没有尾巴。嘴巴很大。它会游泳也会跳。

**Think（课本问）：** Is a frog's life amazing? What do you like about a frog's life? — 青蛙的一生奇妙吗？你喜欢青蛙的哪一点？
