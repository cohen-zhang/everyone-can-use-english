## 5. 课文 — Explore & Extend

课本 **Explore**（Families）与 **Extend**（At the Mid-Autumn Festival）原文 + 简中对照。

### Explore · Families

> **My name is Linlin. This is my family: my mother, father and brother.** — 我叫琳琳。这是我的家：妈妈、爸爸和弟弟。  
> **My name is Yangyang. This is my family. She's my mother. And he's my father. Who is he? He's Pengpeng, my twin brother.** — 我叫阳阳。这是我的家。她是我妈妈。他是我爸爸。他是谁？他是鹏鹏，我的双胞胎哥哥。  
> **My name is Xiaoyu. This is my family: my father, mother, brother and sister.** — 我叫小雨。这是我的家：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和妹妹。  
> **My name is Xiaojie. My family is big. This is my family: my grandfather, grandmother, father, mother and sister.** — 我叫小杰。我家很大。这是我的家：爷爷、奶奶、爸爸、妈妈和妹妹。

**Think（课本问）：** How are the families different? — 有的家小、有的家大；成员人数不同。

---

### Explore · Chant（My family）

> **Here's a picture. Come and see. This is all my family.** — 这是一张照片。来看看。这是我的全家。  
> **Who is she? She's my mother. Who is he? He's my father. Who is he? He's my brother. And who is that? Who is she? Don't be silly — that is me!** — 她是谁？是我妈妈。他是谁？是我爸爸。他是谁？是我哥哥。那是谁？她是谁？别傻了——那是我！

---

### Extend · At the Mid-Autumn Festival

> **It is the Mid-Autumn Festival. Lanlan and her brother Junjun are at their grandparents' house.** — 今天是中秋节。兰兰和弟弟君君在爷爷奶奶家。  
> **Junjun: Look, Lanlan! — Lanlan: Wow! Junjun: Who is he? — Lanlan: He's our grandpa. Junjun: Who is she? — Lanlan: She's our grandma.** — 君君：看，兰兰！——兰兰：哇！君君：他是谁？——兰兰：他是我们爷爷。君君：她是谁？——兰兰：她是我们奶奶。  
> **Lanlan: Grandpa, is this my dad? — Grandpa: Yes. That's your father. It's our Mid-Autumn Festival photo.** — 兰兰：爷爷，这是我爸爸吗？——爷爷：是的。那是你爸爸。这是我们中秋节的照片。  
> **Dad: Hey, let's take a photo together. — Lanlan: Good idea!** — 爸爸：嘿，我们一起拍张照吧。——兰兰：好主意！

**Think（课本问）：** What do your family do together? — 你们家一起做什么？
