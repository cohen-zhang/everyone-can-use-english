## 1. 核心句型 — Key patterns

| Pattern | 中文 | 例句（可跟读） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This is my family. | 这是我的家人。 | This is my family: my mother, father and brother. |
| Who is he / she? | 他／她是谁？ | Who is he? — He is my twin brother. |
| He / She is … | 他／她是…… | She is my mother. |
| My family is big / small. | 我的家庭很大／很小。 | My family is big. |
| This is my … | 这是我的…… | This is my grandfather. |

- **This is my family. Who is she?** — 这是我的家人。她是谁？ `亲子`
- **She is Grandma. He is Grandpa.** — 她是外婆。他是外公。 `亲子`
