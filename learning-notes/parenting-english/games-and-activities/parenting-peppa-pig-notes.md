## Smartphone addiction & mental focus

智能机成瘾、注意力被切碎，是很适合在不少场合讨论的话题。

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Americans, on average, touch their phones an astounding 2,617 times a day, according to research.** | 有研究称，美国人平均每天摸手机约 2617 次，数量惊人。 |
| **Smartphones were supposed to bring people together, but they are pulling people apart.** | 智能手机本该拉近人，却在把人扯开。 |
| **I’m beginning to hate my smartphone, but I know we can’t live without one, right?** | 我开始讨厌手机了，可又离不开，对吧？ |
| **A husband was complaining to his wife that she was spending too much time on her smartphone. In the end, they got a divorce.** | 丈夫抱怨妻子总玩手机，最后两人离婚了。 |
| **Hey, just a minute! I’ve got a message on my smartphone, and it can’t wait.** | 等一下！手机上有条消息，急得很。 |

**Some suggested ideas：**

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| **Do Not Disturb — turn off app notifications on your phone.** | 勿扰：关掉 App 通知。 |
| **Put your phone on silent mode.** | 手机调成静音。 |
| **Your work and family are more important than your smartphone.** | 工作和家人比手机更重要。 |
| **Don’t just read — stop to think about it and write it down.** | 别光读，停下来想并记下来。 |
| **Spend an evening without my smartphone. You will find it very peaceful and great.** | 抽一晚不用手机，会发现很安静、很好。 |
| **Read a book with no interruption.** | 不被打断地读一本书。 |
| **Learn a new language, or grab a good novel on your phone.** | 学一门新语言，或在手机上看本好书。 |
| **Play an instrument. Or meditate. Or write without interruption for 30 minutes.** | 弹琴、冥想，或连续写 30 分钟。 |

> **“Focusing on a single complex task improves your ability to focus on other tasks,”** says Harvard professor Joe DeGutis. **“Making a habit of these activities can result in ‘attentional state training,’ where you are better able to get in a relaxed, focused state for other activities.”**
>
> 哈佛教授 Joe DeGutis：专注做一件复杂的事会提升你在其他事上的专注能力；把这些活动变成习惯，能带来「注意力状态训练」，让你更容易进入放松而专注的状态。

## Love — relationship advice（截取自流利说）

| Speaker | English | 中文 |
|--------|---------|------|
| Q | You know I’m your friend, right? | 你知道我是你朋友吧？ |
| A | Sure, I know that. What are you trying to say? | 知道啊，你想说什么？ |
| Q | I’m sorry to say this, but I think you are making a fool of yourself. | 这话难听，但我觉得你在犯傻。 |
| A | I can’t imagine life without her, even if she hurts me sometimes. I love her. I trust her. She tells me what’s important to her. She’s honest with me. She has never lied to me about anything important. | 没有她我活不下去，就算她有时伤到我。我爱她、信任她。她告诉我什么对她重要。她对我诚实，重要的事从没骗过我。 |
| Q | Are you honest with her? | 你对她诚实吗？ |
| A | Yes. We’re honest with each other; we trust each other. This is the most important thing. The day she isn’t honest with me will be the end of our relationship. And when that happens, my heart will break. | 是。我们彼此诚实、信任，这最重要。她不诚实的那天，就是我们的终点；到那时我会心碎。 |
| Q | OK, I think I understand. | 好，我懂了。 |
| A | I didn’t think you knew what was going on. I’m not as stupid as I look, my friend. Being in love with her makes my life exciting. **You jump, I jump.** | 我还以为你不知道呢。我没看起来那么傻，朋友。爱她让我的生活很刺激。**你跳，我也跳。** |

（*You jump, I jump* 出自《泰坦尼克号》式表白，易记。）
