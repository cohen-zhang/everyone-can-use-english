## 7. 用眼诊断（今天讲清楚）— Eye Check-Up

学校检查有近视风险，医院确认远视储备为负。今天把这件事说清楚，后几天只练执行。

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| **The eye doctor said you're at risk for nearsightedness.** | 眼科医生说你有近视风险。`亲子` |
| **Your hyperopia reserve is negative now.** | 你的远视储备已经是负的了。`技术` `亲子` |
| **That means we have to protect your eyes every day.** | 也就是说，每天都得护眼。`亲子` |
| **More outdoor time, less close-up screen time.** | 多户外，少近距离看屏幕。`亲子` |
| **Sit up straight—keep a fist between your eyes and the book.** | 坐直，眼睛离书一拳。`亲子` |
| **When your eyes feel tired, look far away from the balcony.** | 眼睛累了，去阳台看远处。`亲子` |
| **Or go downstairs and play for ten minutes.** | 或者下楼玩十分钟。`亲子` |
| **Eye exercises at school are not optional—do them seriously.** | 学校的爱眼操不是可选项，认真做。`亲子` |

## 跟读对话 · Daddy & Celine

- **Daddy**: Wake up, Celine. It's Monday—flag-raising day. Wear your red scarf.
- **Celine**: Five more minutes, Daddy!
- **Daddy**: No, today is mandatory. Let's check your bag—water bottle, dictionary, pens.
- **Celine**: Jump rope too—PE is the fourth period.
- **Daddy**: Good catch. Hop on the e-bike. Helmet on, hold tight.
- **Celine**: Daddy, how was your eye doctor visit?
- **Daddy**: The doctor said you're at risk for nearsightedness. Your hyperopia reserve is negative.
- **Celine**: That sounds bad.
- **Daddy**: It just means we protect your eyes every day. More outdoor time, look far away when you're tired.
- **Celine**: Like from the balcony?
- **Daddy**: Yes. Or go downstairs and play for ten minutes. Now—open the English app on the iPad.
- **Celine**: Can I do Peppa first?
- **Daddy**: Textbook first, then a few Peppa lines. Ten minutes, then balcony break.
- **Celine**: OK. Daddy, I'm home by six—right?
- **Daddy**: Right. Walk straight home. The smart lock tells us you're back.
- **Celine**: Start the washer when I'm home?
- **Daddy**: Yes. When it beeps, hang the clothes up. Don't open the door while it's running.
- **Daddy**: Did you fall asleep at nap time? About how long?
- **Celine**: Maybe half an hour. I couldn't sleep at first.
- **Daddy**: That's okay. Quiet rest still helps. Then you can find your friends.
- **Celine**: Can I eat an apple first?
- **Daddy**: No snacks or fruit before dinner. After you finish.
- **Celine**: Five more minutes after dinner?
- **Daddy**: No. Bath now. Then your chapter book. Lights out at ten.
