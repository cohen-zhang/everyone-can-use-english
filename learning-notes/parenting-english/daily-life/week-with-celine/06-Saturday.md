## 9. 睡前 — Bedtime

跳完舞先洗，别接着玩到很晚。十一点睡，澡要早点开始。

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| **You danced for three hours. Shower now.** | 跳了三小时，现在就洗。`亲子` |
| **Don't start another game. Bath first.** | 别再开一局，先洗澡。`亲子` |
| **Eleven is still bedtime. Start the shower early.** | 十一点照样睡，澡要早点开始。`亲子` |
| **Pick a chapter book—not the iPad tonight.** | 选一本章节书，今晚不带 iPad 上床。`亲子` |
| **Read one chapter, then lights out.** | 读一章，然后关灯。`亲子` |
| **Saturday bedtime is eleven.** | 周六睡觉时间十一点。`亲子` |

## 跟读对话 · Daddy & Celine

- **Daddy**: It's Saturday, Celine. Sleep in—no alarm today.
- **Celine**: Yay! Can I open the iPad?
- **Daddy**: After breakfast. Same three things—English, Peppa, math.
- **Celine**: Then can we go to the park?
- **Daddy**: Yes, your eyes need outdoor time. Two hours outside is the best eye care.
- **Celine**: And dance class?
- **Daddy**: Two to five. Pack your dance bag—tights, leotard, dance shoes.
- **Celine**: Street shoes in the tote?
- **Daddy**: Right, studio floors only. After class, find your friends or head home—be back by dinner.
- **Celine**: Mom's not back tonight?
- **Daddy**: No, she's out. We'll order takeout. No snacks or fruit before it.
- **Celine**: Can I read on the iPad in bed?
- **Daddy**: Shower first. You danced all afternoon. Then the chapter book. Lights out at eleven.
