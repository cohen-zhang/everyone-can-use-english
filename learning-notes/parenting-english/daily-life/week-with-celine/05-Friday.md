## 2. 出门检查 — Before You Leave

周五有**德育常规检查**。这不是第七节道法课。重点看指甲。

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| **Friday conduct check. Nails are the main thing.** | 周五德育常规检查，重点查指甲。`亲子` |
| **Clip your nails short. They will look.** | 把指甲剪短，他们会看。`亲子` |
| **School badge on your left chest. Check it.** | 确认衣服左胸有校徽。`亲子` |
| **Button up. Tuck your shirt into your pants.** | 扣好扣子，衣服束进裤子。`亲子` |
| **Red scarf or Young Pioneer badge. Wear one.** | 务必佩戴好红领巾或队徽。`亲子` |
| **Jump rope—PE is second period.** | 跳绳，第二节体育。`亲子` |
| **Dictionary, notebook, three pens, highlighters.** | 字典、登记本、三支笔、荧光笔。`亲子` |
| **Hand in anything that's due today.** | 今天要交的，放在最上面。`亲子` |
| **Check the homework notebook. Anything left?** | 看一下作业登记本，还有没写的吗？`亲子` |

## 7. 妈妈的快手菜 — Mom's Quick Dinner

面条是底。再炒一个菜，通常是西红柿炒鸡蛋。另外两个选项是花菜炒肉、香干炒肉。

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| **Mom's home. Noodles, plus one stir-fry.** | 妈妈回来了。面条，再加一个炒菜。`亲子` |
| **Tonight it's tomato and egg.** | 今晚是西红柿炒鸡蛋。`亲子` |
| **Or cauliflower with pork.** | 也可以花菜炒肉。`亲子` |
| **Or dried tofu with pork.** | 或者香干炒肉。`亲子` |
| **Wash your hands and set out the bowls.** | 洗手，把碗摆上。`亲子` |
| **Less talking, more eating. It's hot.** | 少说话，多吃，菜烫。`亲子` |

## 跟读对话 · Daddy & Celine

- **Daddy**: You walk today. Umbrella in the bag. Friday conduct check—clip your nails.
- **Celine**: School badge on the left?
- **Daddy**: Yes. Button up, tuck your shirt in. Red scarf or the badge. PE is second period.
- **Celine**: It feels muggy.
- **Daddy**: If it rains, slow down. Message me at the gate.
- **Celine**: After-school class, then home by six.
- **Daddy**: How was school this week?
- **Celine**: Music was fun. Math was hard.
- **Daddy**: We'll preview next week's math. Examples only. Fix the red marks.
- **Celine**: Peppa too?
- **Daddy**: Two old lines, then a new bit. Then the balcony if your eyes sting.
- **Celine**: Mom's cooking?
- **Daddy**: Noodles, plus tomato and egg. Or cauliflower with pork, or dried tofu with pork. Set out the bowls.
- **Celine**: Lights out at ten?
- **Daddy**: Bath first. Mom already called you twice. Then lights out at ten. Saturday you can sleep in.
