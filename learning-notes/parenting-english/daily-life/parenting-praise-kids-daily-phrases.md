# 夸奖、赞美小朋友 — 亲子日常英文

帮家长用英文**具体、诚恳**地夸奖 **不多 / Celine**：覆盖 **日常行为、学习、礼貌、外貌衣着、内心与品格**。句子偏口语，可直接套用或略改主语。

**索引：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/README|亲子英文材料索引]]

**相关（剧摘对照）：** [[learning-notes/tv-series/modern-family/s01/notes/parenting-praise-and-blessings|夸奖、祝福 — 《摩登家庭》S01 摘录]] — 台词原句、祝福与回应；本篇是 **跨场景日常句库**，可与该剧摘 **相向互链**、对照练习。

---

## 使用小提示

- **越具体越好**：点出「你做了什么」比只 say *Good girl* 更有分量（也利于孩子重复好行为）。
- **外表与衣着**：可以夸 **用心、整齐、配色、自理**，避免只强调「漂不漂亮」单一标准。
- **内心与品格**：把 **勇敢、诚实、善良** 说出来，帮孩子把行为和身份联系起来（*You **are** kind.* / *That **was** kind.* 可换着用）。

## 1. 日常行为 / Everyday behavior

- **You put your toys away without me asking. Nice job.** — 你没等我说就把玩具收好了，真棒。
- **I noticed you turned off the lights. Thanks for thinking of that.** — 我注意到你关灯了，想得真周到。
- **You shared your snack. That was generous.** — 你把零食分给别人，很大方。
- **You waited quietly while I was on the phone. That helped a lot.** — 我打电话时你很安静等着，帮了大忙。
- **You tried again after it didn't work the first time. I like that.** — 第一次没成你又试了一次，我很喜欢这一点。
- **You used your words instead of screaming. That's growth.** — 你用话说出来而不是大叫，这是进步。
- **Thanks for helping me carry this. You're strong and careful.** — 谢谢你帮我拿，又有力气又小心。
- **You washed your hands before dinner—great habit.** — 吃饭前洗手了，好习惯。

---

## 2. 学习与努力 / Learning & effort

- **I'm proud of how hard you worked on this.** — 你在这上面这么用功，我很骄傲。
- **You stuck with it even when it got tricky.** — 难了你也坚持下来了。
- **I can tell you practiced.** — 我听得出来你练过。
- **You asked a good question—that's how we learn.** — 你问了一个好问题，学习就是这样。
- **Your writing is easier to read than last week.** — 字比上周好认多了。
- **You explained your thinking. That was clear.** — 你把想法讲清楚了，很清楚。
- **It's okay to make mistakes; you fixed this one yourself.** — 犯错没关系，这次你自己改对了。
- **You focused for a long time today. Good concentration.** — 今天你专注了很久，专注力不错。

---

## 3. 礼貌与社交 / Manners & social

- **You said "thank you" on your own.** — 你自己说了谢谢。
- **I like how you waited your turn.** — 你会轮流、会排队，很好。
- **You used a polite tone just now. I appreciate that.** — 你刚才语气很有礼貌，我很感激。
- **You introduced yourself. That was confident.** — 你自己做了自我介绍，很自信。
- **You offered your seat. That was thoughtful.** — 你让座了，想得很周到。
- **You cleaned up your mess at the table. Good manners.** — 你把桌上弄乱的地方收好了，很有礼貌。

---

## 4. 外貌与衣着 / Looks & outfit（Healthier angles）

- **I like the colors you chose. They look happy on you.** — 我喜欢你自己配的颜色，穿起来很活泼。
- **You look ready for school—neat and comfy.** — 看起来准备好上学了，又整齐又舒服。
- **Did you brush your hair yourself? It looks tidy.** — 自己梳的头吗？很整齐。
- **You picked practical shoes for today. Smart choice.** — 今天选的鞋很实用，选得聪明。
- **Your coat is on straight—good job dressing yourself.** — 外套穿正了，自己穿得真好。
- **That drawing has so much detail. I can see the effort.** — 这幅画细节好多，看得出你很用心。（**创作**也常和「外表呈现」一起夸）

---

## 5. 内心与品格 / Heart & character

- **That was brave. You told me how you felt.** — 很勇敢，你愿意跟我说你的感受。
- **You're kind—you noticed someone needed help.** — 你很善良，注意到别人需要帮助。
- **You told the truth even when it was hard. That builds trust.** — 哪怕不容易你也说了实话，这让人信任。
- **You were patient with your little brother/sister.** — 你对弟弟/妹妹很有耐心。
- **You calmed yourself down. That takes strength.** — 你自己冷静下来了，这需要力量。
- **I love your curiosity. Keep wondering "why."** — 我很喜欢你的好奇心，继续问「为什么」。
- **You apologized sincerely. That shows maturity.** — 你道歉很诚恳，这很成熟。
- **You cheered your friend up. You're a good friend.** — 你让朋友开心起来了，你是个好朋友。

---

## 6. 简短万能句 / Short all-purpose praise

- **Good job.** — 做得好。
- **Nice work.** — 干得漂亮。
- **Well done.** — 真不错。
- **I'm proud of you.** — 我为你骄傲。
- **You should be proud of yourself.** — 你也可以为自己骄傲。
- **That's the spirit.** — 这就对了 / 这股劲儿好。
- **You nailed it.** — 你搞定了。（口语）
- **High five.** — 击个掌。

## 8. 英文短文 · 阿泽自述（夸奖的日常用法）

*约 1–2 分钟 · 描述训练 · 串联本页常用句*

**English**

I use praise every day with Celine, but I try to praise with purpose.  
I don't only say "Good job."  
I point to a real action: "You stayed focused." "You waited your turn." "You were kind to your friend."  

When she works hard, I praise effort and strategy, not only results.  
I say, "I can see you kept trying," or "You fixed it step by step."  
This helps her connect success with process.  

For manners, I notice small moments: greeting people, saying thank you, and speaking politely.  
For character, I reinforce brave, honest, helpful choices.  

I also keep praise balanced.  
I avoid comparing her with other kids.  
I don't use praise as pressure.  
I use it to build self-trust and healthy motivation.  

At night, I often end with one specific line:  
"I am proud of how you handled today."  
That sentence reminds her she is seen for who she is becoming, not only for what she scored.  
Over time, good praise becomes emotional nutrition.

**简中：**

我每天都会夸 Celine，但我尽量“有目的地夸”。  
不只说 Good job，而是点出具体动作：你很专注、你有耐心排队、你刚刚很友善。  

遇到学习任务，我优先夸努力和方法，不只夸结果：  
我看到你一直在尝试、你是一步一步改对的。  
这样她会把进步和“过程”连接起来。  

礼貌层面，我会抓小时刻：打招呼、说谢谢、语气得体。  
品格层面，就强化勇敢、诚实、愿意帮忙。  

我也会控制夸奖方式：不和别的孩子比较，不把夸奖变成压力。  
夸奖的目标是建立自我信任和内驱力。  

睡前我常用一句收尾：我为你今天处理事情的方式感到骄傲。  
这让她知道，被看见的是“正在成长的她”，不只是分数。  
长期来看，好的夸奖就是情绪营养。
