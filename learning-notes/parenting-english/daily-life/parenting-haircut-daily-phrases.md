# 理发 — Haircut (Boys & Girls)

## 1. 该理发了 — Time for a Haircut

- **Your hair is getting long.**  
  你的头发长长了。

- **Your hair is covering your eyes.**  
  你的头发挡住眼睛了。

- **I think it's time for a haircut.**  
  我觉得该理发了。

- **Do you want a haircut this weekend?**  
  这周末去理发好吗？

- **We're going to the barbershop today.**  
  我们今天去理发店。

- **Would you like to keep it long, or cut it short?**  
  你想留长，还是剪短？

- **Don't worry, a haircut doesn't hurt.**  
  别担心，理发不疼。

- **Your hair will grow back. It always does.**  
  头发会再长出来的，一直都会。

---

## 2. 到理发店 — At the Barbershop / Hair Salon

- **Let's wait for our turn.**  
  我们等着排队。

- **It's almost your turn.**  
  快轮到你了。

- **Hop up into the chair.**  
  爬上椅子坐好。

- **Sit up straight, please.**  
  请坐直。

- **The barber will put a cape on you.**  
  理发师会给你披上围布。

- **This cape keeps the hair off your clothes.**  
  这个围布能挡住碎发，不弄到衣服上。

- **Keep your hands under the cape.**  
  手放在围布里面。

- **Say hello to the barber.**  
  跟理发师问好。

---

## 3a. 沟通发型 · 男生版 — Talking About the Style (Boys)

- **He'd like it short on the sides, please.**  
  麻烦两边剪短一点。

- **Short on the sides, a little longer on top.**  
  两边短，头顶留长一点。

- **Just a trim, please—not too short.**  
  稍微修一修就好，别太短。

- **Can you tidy up the back and the neck?**  
  能把后面和脖子那里修整齐吗？

- **A buzz cut would be easy for summer.**  
  夏天剃个寸头会很方便。

- **Please leave the fringe a bit long.**  
  刘海请留长一点。

- **The clippers might tickle—it's okay.**  
  电推子可能会有点痒，没关系的。

- **Do you want it like last time?**  
  你想和上次一样吗？

---

## 3b. 沟通发型 · 女生版 — Talking About the Style (Girls)

- **Just a trim, please—keep the length.**  
  请稍微修一下就好，长度保留。

- **Could you cut off about this much?**  
  能剪掉大概这么多吗？

- **Please trim the ends—they're a bit split.**  
  请修一下发尾，有点分叉了。

- **Can you cut her bangs a little?**  
  能帮她把刘海剪短一点吗？

- **Not too short on the bangs, please.**  
  刘海别剪太短。

- **She'd like some layers.**  
  她想剪点层次。

- **Let's keep it long enough for a ponytail.**  
  长度保留到能扎马尾。

- **Cut it to about her shoulders, please.**  
  大概剪到肩膀，麻烦了。

---

## 4. 理发过程 — During the Haircut

- **Hold still, please.**  
  请别动。

- **Keep your head straight.**  
  头放正。

- **Look down a little.**  
  稍微低头。

- **Chin up, please.**  
  请抬一下下巴。

- **Turn your head this way.**  
  把头转到这边。

- **Close your eyes for a second.**  
  闭一下眼睛。

- **Almost done.**  
  快好了。

- **You're doing great. Just a little more.**  
  你做得很好，再一点点就好。

- **Try not to talk while she's cutting near your ears.**  
  剪到耳朵旁边时尽量别说话。

---

## 5. 安抚紧张的孩子 — Reassuring a Nervous Child

- **It's okay. I'm right here.**  
  没事的，我就在这里。

- **The buzzing sound is just the clippers.**  
  那个嗡嗡声只是电推子的声音。

- **It tickles a little, but it doesn't hurt.**  
  有点痒，但不疼。

- **You can hold my hand.**  
  你可以拉着我的手。

- **Take a deep breath.**  
  深呼吸。

- **Be brave—you're doing so well.**  
  勇敢一点，你做得真棒。

- **We can watch a video while you wait.**  
  等的时候我们可以看个视频。

- **When it's done, you'll look so cool.**  
  剪完你会很帅 / 很美的。

---

## 6. 洗头和吹干 — Washing and Drying

- **Time to wash off the little hairs.**  
  该把碎头发冲掉了。

- **Lean your head back.**  
  头往后仰。

- **Is the water warm enough?**  
  水温合适吗？

- **Tell me if it's too hot or too cold.**  
  太烫或太凉就告诉我。

- **Close your eyes so no water gets in.**  
  闭上眼睛，别让水进去。

- **Now they'll dry your hair.**  
  现在他们会给你吹干头发。

- **The hairdryer is a bit loud, that's all.**  
  吹风机有点吵，仅此而已。

---

## 7. 理完之后 — After the Haircut

- **All done! Take a look in the mirror.**  
  好啦！照照镜子看看。

- **Do you like your new haircut?**  
  你喜欢你的新发型吗？

- **You look so smart!**  
  你看起来真精神！

- **It feels lighter now, doesn't it?**  
  现在轻松多了，对吧？

- **Let's brush off the little hairs.**  
  我们把碎头发拍掉。

- **Your neck might feel itchy—let's wipe it.**  
  你脖子可能会痒，我们擦一擦。

- **Say thank you to the barber.**  
  跟理发师说谢谢。

- **Let's take a photo of your new look.**  
  我们给你的新造型拍张照。

## 10. 场景对话 — Sample Dialogues

### 场景 1：男孩剃寸头

**Dad:** Your hair is getting long, buddy. Let's get a haircut.  
爸爸：你头发长长了，伙计。我们去理发吧。

**Boy:** Will it hurt?  
男孩：会疼吗？

**Dad:** Not at all. The clippers just tickle a little.  
爸爸：一点都不疼。电推子只是有点痒。

**Dad (to barber):** Short on the sides, a little longer on top, please.  
爸爸（对理发师）：麻烦两边剪短，头顶留长一点。

**Boy:** It's buzzing!  
男孩：在嗡嗡响！

**Dad:** That's just the clippers. You're being so brave.  
爸爸：那只是电推子。你真勇敢。

---

### 场景 2：女孩修发尾和刘海

**Mom:** Just a trim today, Celine—we'll keep the length.  
妈妈：今天只修一下，Celine，长度保留。

**Celine:** Don't cut too much!  
Celine：别剪太多！

**Mom (to stylist):** Could you trim the ends and her bangs a little?  
妈妈（对发型师）：能修一下发尾，再把刘海剪短一点吗？

**Celine:** Not too short on the bangs.  
Celine：刘海别剪太短。

**Stylist:** All done. Take a look in the mirror.  
发型师：好啦，照照镜子看看。

**Celine:** I love it!  
Celine：我好喜欢！

---

### 场景 3：安抚怕理发的小小孩

**Dad:** It's okay. I'm right here. You can hold my hand.  
爸爸：没事的，我就在这里。你可以拉着我的手。

**Kid:** I don't like the sound.  
孩子：我不喜欢那个声音。

**Dad:** The buzzing is just the clippers. Take a deep breath.  
爸爸：那个嗡嗡声只是电推子。深呼吸。

**Dad:** Almost done. When it's finished, you'll look so cool.  
爸爸：快好了。剪完你会超帅的。

## 11. 每次理发小清单 — Quick Haircut Prompts

- **Sit up straight and hold still.**  
  坐直，别动。

- **Tell the barber how short you want it.**  
  告诉理发师你想剪多短。

- **Close your eyes when there's water or hair near your face.**  
  脸旁有水或碎发时闭上眼睛。

- **Say thank you when it's done.**  
  剪完说谢谢。

- **Brush off the loose hairs before we leave.**  
  走之前把碎头发拍掉。
