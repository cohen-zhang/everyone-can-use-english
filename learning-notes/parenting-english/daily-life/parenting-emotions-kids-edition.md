## 询问孩子的感受

### 基本问句

- **How are you feeling?**  
  你感觉怎么样？

- **How do you feel?**  
  你感觉如何？

- **Are you okay?**  
  你还好吗？

- **What's wrong?**  
  怎么了？

- **Why are you crying?**  
  你为什么哭？

- **Is something bothering you?**  
  有什么事让你不开心吗？

- **You look sad. What's the matter?**  
  你看起来很难过。发生什么事了？

### 具体情绪询问

- **Are you happy or sad?**  
  你开心还是难过？

- **Are you scared?**  
  你害怕吗？

- **Is it too hard? Are you frustrated?**  
  是不是太难了？你感到沮丧吗？

- **Are you angry at me?**  
  你在生我的气吗？

- **Are you excited about tomorrow?**  
  你对明天感到兴奋吗？

## 表达和承认情绪

### 家长回应孩子情绪

- **It's okay to feel sad.**  
  感到难过是正常的。

- **Everyone gets angry sometimes.**  
  每个人都会有时候生气。

- **It's okay to cry.**  
  哭是可以的。

- **I understand you're frustrated.**  
  我理解你很沮丧。

- **You have every right to be upset.**  
  你有权利感到不高兴。

- **Your feelings are important.**  
  你的感受很重要。

### 帮助孩子命名情绪

- **You seem a little worried.**  
  你看起来有点担心。

- **That made you angry, didn't it?**  
  那让你生气了，对吗？

- **You look disappointed.**  
  你看起来很失望。

- **Are you feeling jealous?**  
  你是不是感到嫉妒？

- **That's embarrassing, isn't it?**  
  那很尴尬，对吗？

## 安慰和鼓励

### 当孩子难过时

- **Come here. Let me give you a hug.**  
  过来。让我抱抱你。

- **Everything will be okay.**  
  一切都会好起来的。

- **I'm here for you.**  
  我在这里陪着你。

- **Do you want to talk about it?**  
  你想聊聊吗？

- **Let it all out.**  
  哭出来吧。（把情绪都释放出来）

- **It's not your fault.**  
  这不是你的错。

### 当孩子生气时

- **Take a deep breath.**  
  深呼吸。

- **Let's count to ten together.**  
  我们一起数到十。

- **Use your words, please.**  
  请用语言表达。

- **It's okay to be angry, but it's not okay to hit.**  
  生气是可以的，但打人不行。

- **Let's cool down first.**  
  我们先冷静一下。

### 当孩子害怕时

- **I'm right here.**  
  我就在这儿。

- **There's nothing to be afraid of.**  
  没什么好怕的。

- **I'll protect you.**  
  我会保护你。

- **The monster isn't real.**  
  怪兽不是真的。

- **You're safe with me.**  
  你和我在一起很安全。

## 引导孩子调节情绪

### 情绪调节策略

- **Let's take some deep breaths.**  
  我们来深呼吸。

- **Want to draw how you feel?**  
  想把你的感受画出来吗？

- **Let's go for a walk to calm down.**  
  我们去散散步冷静一下。

- **Why don't you tell me what happened?**  
  告诉我发生了什么事好吗？

- **Let's think of something happy.**  
  我们想点开心的事吧。

- **Can you try to use a calm voice?**  
  你能试着用平静的声音说话吗？

### 转移注意力

- **Look at this funny picture!**  
  看这张有趣的图片！

- **Want to play with your favorite toy?**  
  想玩你最喜欢的玩具吗？

- **Let's sing a happy song.**  
  我们来唱首开心的歌。

- **Shall we go outside and play?**  
  我们出去玩好吗？

## 情绪场景对话

### 场景 1：孩子不想上学

- **Child:** I don't want to go to school. 我不想上学。
- **Parent:** Why not? Are you worried about something? 为什么？你在担心什么吗？
- **Child:** I'm scared the teacher will be mad at me. 我怕老师会生我的气。
- **Parent:** It's okay to feel scared. Let's talk to the teacher together. 感到害怕是正常的。我们一起和老师谈谈。

### 场景 2：孩子摔倒哭了

- **Parent:** Oh no! Are you hurt? 哦不！你受伤了吗？
- **Child:** (crying) 呜哇…
- **Parent:** Come here, sweetie. Let me check. 过来，宝贝。让我看看。
- **Parent:** It's just a little scratch. You'll be okay. 只是一点小擦伤。你会没事的。
- **Child:** It hurts! 疼！
- **Parent:** I know it hurts. Let me kiss it better. 我知道疼。让我亲亲，帮你好起来。

### 场景 3：孩子因为玩具生气

- **Child:** I hate this toy! 我讨厌这个玩具！
- **Parent:** You sound very angry. What's wrong? 你听起来很生气。怎么了？
- **Child:** It won't work! 它坏了！
- **Parent:** It's frustrating when things don't work, right? 东西不好用的时候很让人沮丧，对吗？
- **Parent:** Let's try to fix it together. 我们一起试着修好吧。

### A Tiny Emotion Mission with Celine（Celine 的情绪小任务）

This morning, Celine looked [grumpy](#kw-grumpy), not [happy](#kw-happy).  
今天早上，Celine 看起来有点[脾气坏](#kw-grumpy)，不太[开心](#kw-happy)。

She said she was [sad](#kw-sad) because her toy rocket was broken.  
她说自己很[难过](#kw-sad)，因为她的玩具火箭坏了。

Then she got [angry](#kw-angry), and a little [silly](#kw-silly), kicking a pillow like a tiny dragon.  
然后她变得[生气](#kw-angry)，还有点[淘气](#kw-silly)，像小龙一样踢枕头。

When the room went quiet, she felt [scared](#kw-scared) and [worried](#kw-worried).  
房间安静下来后，她又开始[害怕](#kw-scared)、[担心](#kw-worried)。

Mom said, "Let's [calm down](#kw-calm-down) first. Breathe in, breathe out."  
妈妈说：“我们先[冷静下来](#kw-calm-down)，吸气，呼气。”

"You are [brave](#kw-brave), and we can fix it together."  
“你很[勇敢](#kw-brave)，我们可以一起修好它。”

After three deep breaths, Celine became more [patient](#kw-patient).  
做了三次深呼吸后，Celine 变得更[有耐心](#kw-patient)了。

They found one loose button, fixed the rocket, and she felt [excited](#kw-excited) again.  
她们找到了一个松掉的按钮，修好了火箭，她又重新[兴奋](#kw-excited)起来。

Mom smiled and said, "When feelings get big, we can always [cheer up](#kw-cheer-up) step by step."  
妈妈笑着说：“当情绪变大时，我们总能一步步[振作起来](#kw-cheer-up)。”

## 英文短文 · 阿泽自述（亲子情绪引导）

*约 1–2 分钟 · 描述训练 · 串联本页常用句*

**English**

In our home, I treat emotions as signals, not problems to hide.  
When Celine looks upset, I start by naming what I see:  
"You look sad." "You seem worried." "Are you feeling angry?"  

I let her talk before I teach.  
I listen, keep eye contact, and say, "It's okay to feel this way."  
Then I help her find words:  
"I feel frustrated because..." or "I feel scared when..."  

If she is too emotional, we pause first.  
We take slow breaths, drink water, or sit quietly for one minute.  
After she calms down, we solve the problem together.  
I ask, "What can we do now?" and "What will help next time?"  

I also practice positive emotions, not just difficult ones.  
When she is excited, proud, or brave, I name those feelings too.  
That helps her build emotional vocabulary in both happy and hard moments.  

My goal is not to make her "never cry."  
My goal is to help her notice feelings, express them clearly, and return to calm with support.  
That is emotional strength for life.

**简中：**

在我们家，我把情绪当信号，不当需要隐藏的问题。  
Celine 情绪上来时，我先描述观察到的状态：你看起来难过、你是不是有点担心、你现在很生气吗？  

我会先听，再教。  
看着她、接住她，并说“你有这种感受是可以的”。  
然后帮她把感觉说成句子：我现在很挫败，因为…… / 我会害怕，是因为……  

如果情绪太满，就先暂停，不急着讲道理。  
慢呼吸、喝口水、安静一分钟，等她回稳后再一起想解决办法。  
我常问：现在我们可以怎么做？下次可以怎么提前处理？  

我也会刻意标注积极情绪，不只盯着负面：兴奋、自豪、勇敢都要说出来。  
这样她在顺境和逆境里都能积累情绪词汇。  

我的目标不是让她“别哭”，而是让她会觉察、会表达、会在支持下回到平静。  
这才是长期受用的情绪能力。
