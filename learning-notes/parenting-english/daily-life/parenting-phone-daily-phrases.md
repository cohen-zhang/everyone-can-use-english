# 手机使用日常 — Phone & Daily Life

**索引：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/README|亲子英文材料索引]]


亲子与手机、平板类设备相关的日常英文表达：拿来放下、开关机与重启、屏幕手势与 App 操作、接打电话、消息、拍照、屏幕与规则、网络与充电、安全边界。`亲子`

*口语里 **cell phone**（美式）、**mobile (phone)**（英式 / 国际场合）都常见；下文默认用 **phone**，需要区分时再写。`技术`*

## 1. 拿放与注意 — Handle With Care

- **This is my work phone. Please ask before you use it.**  
这是爸爸 / 妈妈的工作机。用之前要先问。`亲子`
- **Use two hands. It’s easy to drop.**  
两只手拿。容易摔。`亲子`
- **No throwing. No food or sticky hands on the screen.**  
别扔。别在屏幕上沾食物或满手油。`亲子`
- **I’ll take the phone. It’s not safe right now. / Let’s keep it in my pocket for now.**  
我先把手机收起来。现在别玩。/ 先放我口袋里。`亲子`
- **The screen is cracked. We need a repair. We’re not buying a new one just because of that.**  
屏裂了。要修。别指望这就换新。`亲子`
- **The case / the screen protector helps a bit, but it’s not magic.**  
手机壳 / 贴膜有点用，但也不是万能的。`技术`

## 2. 开关机与重启 — Power On, Off & Restart

（**power** 常指电源相关；**power on / off** 与 **turn on / off** 在口语里常可换用。）`技术`

### 开机、唤醒、锁屏

- **Press and hold the power button / side button until the logo appears.**  
长按电源键 / 侧边键直到出现商标。`技术` `亲子`
- **The phone is booting up. Wait a second.** / **(UK) It’s starting up.**  
手机正在启动。等一下。/（英：starting up.）`技术`
- **Tap the screen or press the side button to wake it.** / **Swipe up to unlock.**  
点一下屏幕或按侧边键唤醒。/ 上滑解锁。`技术` `亲子`
- **Enter your passcode.** / **Use Face ID / Touch ID.**  
输密码。/ 用人脸 / 指纹解锁。`技术`
- **The screen went to sleep. / The screen locked itself.**  
屏幕自己息屏了。/ 自动锁屏了。`技术`

### 关机、重启

- **I’m going to turn the phone off.** / **I’ll power it down for the flight.**  
我要关机了。/ 飞机上我把手机关掉。`技术`
- **Slide to power off.** / **Drag the slider to turn off.**  
滑动关机。/ 拖滑块关机。`技术`
- **Let’s restart it. It’s acting weird.** / **(UK) It needs a reboot.**  
我们重启一下。有点怪。/（英：需要 reboot.）`技术`
- **Force restart: hold the power and volume buttons as the manual says—models differ.**  
强制重启请按说明书—**不同机型按键组合不同。**`技术` `亲子`
- **The phone froze. Nothing moves. We have to force-restart.**  
手机死机了。只好强制重启。`技术` `亲子`

## 3. 屏幕手势与导航 — Gestures & Navigation

### 点击、滑动、缩放

- **Tap once.** / **Double-tap to zoom (in some apps).**  
点一下。/ 有的界面里双击放大。`技术` `亲子`
- **Swipe left / right / up / down.** / **Swipe away the notification.**  
向左 / 右 / 上 / 下滑。/ 把通知划掉。`技术` `亲子`
- **Scroll up and down the page with your finger.**  
用手指上下滑浏览整页。`亲子`
- **Pinch to zoom in. Spread your fingers to zoom out.**  
双指捏合放大、张开缩小。`技术` `亲子`
- **Long press / press and hold the icon until it jiggles.** / **(UK) Press and hold…**  
长按图标直到图标晃动。/（英：press and hold 很常用.）`技术` `亲子`
- **Drag the app to the folder.** / **Drag the slider.**  
把 app 拖到文件夹里。/ 拖滑块。`技术`

### 返回、主页、多任务

- **Tap the back arrow / the back button.** / **(Android gesture) Swipe from the edge to go back.**  
点返回箭头 / 返回键。/（安卓手势：从边缘滑返回.）`技术`
- **Go to the home screen.** / **Press the home bar / home indicator.**  
回主屏幕。/ 按底部 Home 条 / 指示条。`技术`
- **Swipe up from the bottom and pause—open recent apps.** / **(iOS) Open the app switcher.** / **(Android) Open the recents menu.**  
从底部上滑停一下—打开最近应用。/ 应用切换器 / 最近任务。`技术`
- **Swipe up on the app card to close it.** / **Force-quit from the switcher.**  
在多任务里上滑卡片关掉 app。/ 在切换器里划掉以强制退出。`技术`
- **Pull down from the top for notifications and quick settings.** / **(Android) Open the notification shade.**  
从顶部下拉看通知与快捷开关。/（安卓：通知栏 / 状态栏.）`技术`

### 给孩子说的短句

- **One finger. Don’t tap too fast.**  
一根手指。别点太快。`亲子`
- **Slow swipe. You swiped too hard.**  
轻一点滑。你划得太猛了。`亲子`

## 4. App 操作 — Apps: Open, Switch, Update & More

### 打开、查找、退出

- **Open the app.** / **Launch it from the home screen.**  
打开 app。/ 从主屏幕点开。`技术` `亲子`
- **Search for the app.** / **Pull down on the home screen to search.**  
搜一下这个 app。/ 主屏幕下滑搜索。`技术`
- **The app is still running in the background. I’ll close it.**  
app 还在后台。我关掉。`技术`
- **The app crashed / froze. I’ll reopen it.** / **(UK) The app’s frozen.**  
app 闪退 / 卡死。我重开。/（英：冻住了.）`技术`

### 安装、更新、卸载

- **Download it from the App Store / Google Play.** / **(UK) Get it from the Play Store.**  
从应用商店下载。`技术`
- **This app needs an update. I’ll update on Wi-Fi.** / **Turn on auto-update for apps.**  
这 app 要更新。我用 Wi-Fi 更新。/ 开应用自动更新。`技术`
- **We’re low on storage. I’ll delete / uninstall apps we don’t use.** / **(iOS) Offload unused apps.**  
空间紧。我卸掉不用的 app。/（iOS：卸载省空间 offload.）`技术`
- **This is a paid app. Not today.** / **In-app purchases—ask me first.**  
付费 app。今天不买。/ 应用内购买—先问大人。`亲子` `技术`

### 账号、权限、评价

- **Log in / Sign in.** / **Log out on a shared device when you’re done.**  
登录。/ 共用设备用完要登出。`技术`
- **The app asks for camera / microphone / location permission. I’ll allow or deny.**  
app 要相机 / 麦克风 / 定位权限。我来选允许或拒绝。`技术` `亲子`
- **Don’t tap Allow on everything. Read what it’s asking.**  
别什么都点允许。看它问什么。`亲子`
- **Rate the app / leave a review—only if we really used it.**  
打分 / 写评价—真用过再评。`技术`

## 5. 来电、去电 — Calls

- **The phone is ringing. Who is it?** / **It’s ringing—should I get it?**  
手机在响。是谁？/ 在响—要我接吗？`亲子`
- **I’ll take this. It’s a work call.** / **Daddy’s on a call. Shh—quiet, please.**  
我接。工作电话。/ 爸爸在打电话。嘘—轻一点。`亲子`
- **It’s for Mom. I’ll get her. / I’ll hand her the phone.**  
找妈妈的。我去叫她。/ 我把手机递给她。`亲子`
- **I missed the call. I’ll call them back.** / **I’ll return the call later.**  
没接到。我回过去。/ 我晚点回电。
- **Sorry, wrong number.** / **You’ve got the wrong number.**  
抱歉，你打错了。/ 你打错号码了。
- **The signal is bad. I can’t hear you. Let me go near the window.**  
信号不好。听不清。我到窗边试试。`技术`
- **I’m going to put you on speaker, okay?** / **(UK) I’ll put you on hands-free for a second.**  
我开免提，好吗？/（英：开免提也常说 hands-free.）`技术`
- **I’ll text them instead. It’s faster than playing phone tag.**  
我发短信吧。比来回没接到电话还快。  
*（play phone tag＝你打过去对方不在、对方打来你又不在。）*

## 6. 消息、语音、视频 — Text & Chat

- **I got a text. / I got a message. It’s from work.** / **(UK) I’ve just had a text from…**  
收到条短信。/ 收到条消息。是单位的。/（英：I’ve just had a text…）`技术`
- **I’m in a WeChat group for Celine’s class.** / **The teacher posted in the group chat.**  
我在不多班级的群里。/ 老师发在群里了。`技术`
- **Don’t read my messages, okay? That’s private.**  
别看我的信息，好吗？这是隐私。`亲子`
- **I’ll send a voice message. It’s too long to type while walking.**  
我发条语音。走路打字太长。`技术`
- **I’ll FaceTime you when I get home. / I’ll start a video call in five minutes.**  
我到家就跟你视频。/ 五分钟后我发起视频。`技术`
- **The video is laggy. Is your Wi-Fi on?** / **Your picture froze. / The sound cut out.**  
视频卡。你 Wi-Fi 开着的吗？/ 你画面定住了。/ 声音断了一下。`技术`
- **I’ll add you to the call. / I’m in a three-way / group call.**  
我把你加进这通电话。/ 我在多人 / 群里语音里。`技术`

## 7. 屏幕、声音与“规矩” — Screen, Sound & House Rules

- **The screen is too bright. I’m dimming it.** / **Enable Dark Mode in the evening.**  
屏太亮。我调暗。/ 晚上开深色模式。`技术`
- **The font is too small. I’ll make the text bigger in Settings — Accessibility.**  
字太小。我在设置里把字调大—辅助功能。`技术`
- **I’m setting Screen Time. No games after 8 p.m. on a school day.** / **(Android) I’ll set a focus mode / app limit.**  
我设个屏幕使用时间。上学日晚上八点以后不玩游戏。/（安卓：专注模式、应用限制。）`技术`
- **Put the phone on silent / vibrate. We’re in the library. / I’m in a meeting.** / **(UK) Put it on silent / stick it on vibrate.**  
手机调静音 / 振动。在图书馆。/ 我在开会。/（英：stick it on vibrate.）`技术`
- **The notification is driving me crazy. I’ll turn off the alerts for this app.** / **(UK) The notifications are doing my head in.**  
通知烦死了。我把这个 app 的提醒关掉。/（英：口语 doing my head in.）`技术`
- **The battery is at 10 percent. I’m going to use Low Power Mode. / (UK) Low battery. I need power-saving mode.**  
剩 10% 电。我开低电量模式。/（英：低电量。）`技术`
- **No phones at the dinner table. That’s our family rule.**  
吃饭不看手机。我们家规矩。`亲子`
- **Eyes on the road, not the phone, when I’m driving.** / **(UK) Don’t use your phone in the car while I’m moving.**  
我开车时别看手机，看路。/（英：车在开就别玩。）`亲子`

## 11. 孩子自己／青少年前的边界 — Safety & Etiquette

- **We don’t share our home address, school name, or passwords with random people online.**  
别在网上随便说家庭住址、校名、密码。`亲子`
- **If something looks scary or makes you feel weird, tell a grown-up right away. Don’t click strange links.**  
网上看到怪东西、心里不舒服，马上告诉大人。别乱点链接。`亲子`
- **I’m checking the parental controls. Some apps are 12+ for a reason.** / **(UK) I’m reviewing the child safety / age limits in the app store.**  
我看看家长控制。有的 app 标 12+ 是有道理的。/（英：应用商店里年龄限制.）`技术`
- **When you have your own phone, we’ll write simple rules on paper first.** / **(UK) We’ll agree a few clear rules before you get your first mobile.**  
等你真有自己的手机，我们先在纸上立几条简单规矩。/（英：第一部手机前说好.）`亲子`

## 12. 短对话（阿泽、不多、Celine）

**场景 A：响铃、孩子递手机**

- **Celine (不多)**: Daddy, it’s beeping!  
- **阿泽 (Dad)**: That’s a call. Thank you. Don’t press anything—I’ll take it.  
- **Celine**: Okay!  
— 教孩子：响了先叫人，自己别乱按。`亲子`

**场景 B：坐车时要手机**

- **Celine**: Can I see your phone? I’m bored.  
- **Dad**: We’re in traffic, but I’m still driving. You can’t hold it; look out the window for red cars, okay?  
- **Celine**:… Okay. I see a red one!  
— 开车规矩 + 用游戏转移注意力。`亲子`

**场景 C：要下载游戏**

- **Celine**: Can I get this app? The ad looks fun!  
- **Dad**: I’ll read the reviews and check the app age rating. If it’s free, there are often in-app ads—we’ll see.  
- **Celine**: Please?  
- **Dad**: Not right now. We’ll look at it after dinner together.  
— 广告里的游戏先家长把关；提到「年龄分级」「内购广告」可顺带教词。`亲子` `技术`

**场景 D：教手势（从边缘滑返回）**

- **Celine**: It’s stuck! I can’t get out!  
- **Dad**: Don’t keep tapping. Swipe from the left edge to go back—or tap the arrow up here.  
- **Celine**: Like this?  
- **Dad**: Yes. If it still freezes, I’ll close the app from the app switcher.  
— 区分「乱点」与「边缘滑返回 / 点箭头 / 上滑多任务关 app」。`亲子` `技术`
