## 1. 进园与场地 — Arrival & Areas

- **We're at the park entrance. Let's check the map.**  
  我们到公园门口了，看一下导览图。

- **The children's play area is past the lawn, near the trees.**  
  儿童游乐区在大草坪那头、靠近树林那边。

- **Stay on the path—bikes may ride through here.**  
  走在步道上——这里可能有单车经过。

- **The restrooms are next to the visitor center.**  
  卫生间在游客中心旁边。

- **Let's find a bench in the shade.**  
  我们找张树荫下的长椅。

- **The grass is wet from last night's rain. Watch your step.**  
  草皮昨晚下过雨还湿，注意脚下。

- **Some fitness equipment is for adults only—read the sign.**  
  有些健身器材仅限成人使用——看一下告示。

## 4. 安全与轮流 — Safety & Taking Turns

- **Wait your turn at the slide.**  
  滑梯要排队，等你轮到。

- **One person on the swing—don't stand on the seat.**  
  秋千上一个人——不要站在座板上。

- **Hold on with both hands.**  
  两只手抓牢。

- **Don't push on the stairs of the play structure.**  
  别在游乐架的楼梯上推人。

- **The metal gets hot in the sun—touch it first.**  
  太阳底下金属会烫——先摸一下。

- **Stay where I can see you.**  
  待在我能看见你的地方。

- **That's for grown-ups—let's go back to the kids' area.**  
  那是大人用的——我们回儿童区吧。

## 7. 小对话 — Sample Dialogues

### 场景 1：选设施

**Dad:** Do you want the tall slide or the short one?  
爸爸：你想玩高的滑梯还是矮的？

**Celine:** The tall one!  
Celine：高的！

**Dad:** Okay—shoes off the edge, sit down, then go.  
爸爸：好——脚别伸出边沿，坐下来再滑。

### 场景 2：器械区边界

**Mom:** Please don't climb on the outdoor gym—it's not a jungle gym.  
妈妈：别爬户外健身器——那不是攀爬架。

**Celine:** But it looks fun!  
Celine：可是看起来很好玩！

**Mom:** I know. Let's race to the swings instead.  
妈妈：我知道。那我们比赛跑到秋千那儿吧。
