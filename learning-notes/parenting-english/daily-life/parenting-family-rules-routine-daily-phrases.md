# 家庭守则与作息 — Family Rules & Daily Routine

围绕亲子家庭里的固定作息、日常规矩、诚实沟通与说到做到。句子尽量短，方便不多 / Celine 跟读和家长稳定重复。`亲子`

**索引：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/README|亲子英文材料索引]]

**相关：** 吃饭规则、礼貌和坐姿可搭配 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/daily-life/parenting-table-manners-daily-phrases.md|日常礼貌与餐桌礼仪]]（本页偏“家庭总规矩”，那页偏“餐桌具体动作”）。

## 1. 亲子作息 — Daily Routine Rules

- **We wake up at seven on school days.**  
  上学日我们七点起床。`亲子`
- **Brush your teeth, wash your face, then get dressed.**  
  先刷牙洗脸，再穿衣服。`亲子`
- **Homework first, then screen time.**  
  先作业，再看屏幕。`亲子`
- **Dinner is at six thirty.**  
  晚饭是六点半。`亲子`
- **Bath time is at eight.**  
  八点洗澡。`亲子`
- **Lights out at nine.**  
  九点关灯睡觉。`亲子`

---

## 2. 家庭规矩 — House Rules

- **Use kind words at home.**  
  在家要好好说话。`亲子`
- **No hitting, no kicking, no yelling.**  
  不打人，不踢人，不大吼。`亲子`
- **Ask before taking someone else's things.**  
  拿别人东西前先问。`亲子`
- **Put things back where they belong.**  
  东西用完放回原位。`亲子`
- **Clean up your toys before bedtime.**  
  睡前把玩具收好。`亲子`
- **When we say stop, you stop right away.**  
  我们说“停”时，要马上停下。`亲子`

---

## 3. 不说谎与说到做到 — Honesty & Keeping Promises

- **In our family, we tell the truth.**  
  在我们家，要说真话。`亲子`
- **It's okay to make mistakes. It's not okay to lie.**  
  犯错可以，撒谎不可以。`亲子`
- **Tell me what really happened.**  
  告诉我到底发生了什么。`亲子`
- **Thank you for being honest.**  
  谢谢你说实话。`亲子`
- **A promise is a promise.**  
  说到就要做到。`亲子`
- **If you promise, you need to do it.**  
  你答应了，就要做到。`亲子`
- **I will do what I said.**  
  我会说到做到。`亲子`

---

## 4. 规则句型（家长稳定复读）

- **Our rule is: first ___, then ___.**  
  我们的规则是：先___，再___。`亲子`
- **Do you remember our family rule?**  
  你还记得我们的家庭规则吗？`亲子`
- **What should you do next?**  
  你下一步该做什么？`亲子`
- **Thank you for following the rule.**  
  谢谢你遵守规则。`亲子`
- **Let's try again and do it the right way.**  
  我们再来一次，用正确方式做。`亲子`

## 5. 小对话（阿泽、不多、Celine）

**场景 A：先作业再玩**

- **Dad (阿泽):** What's our rule after school?  
- **Celine (不多):** Homework first, then play.  
- **Dad:** Right. Thank you for remembering.  
— 用固定句反复强化节奏。`亲子`

**场景 B：说实话**

- **Dad:** Did you break the cup?  
- **Celine:** Yes... I did. I'm sorry.  
- **Dad:** Thank you for telling the truth. Let's clean it up together.  
— 先肯定诚实，再处理后果。`亲子`

**场景 C：说到做到**

- **Celine:** I promise I'll put my toys away.  
- **Dad:** Great. A promise is a promise.  
- **Celine:** Okay, I'll do it now.  
— 把“承诺”变成可执行动作。`亲子`
