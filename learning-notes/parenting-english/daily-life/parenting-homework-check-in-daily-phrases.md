# 家庭作业打卡 — Homework Check-In

面向**亲子作业场景**：从放学回家、作业准备、各科作业、检查订正、打卡提交到睡前整理的完整流程。学习者身份：**阿泽**；孩子：**不多 / Celine**。`亲子`

---

## 本页在做什么

**homework**：学校布置的课后作业，需按时完成并提交。  
**homework check-in**：家校平台上记录完成进度、上传照片或音视频、家长确认的 routine；口语里也可说 **daily submission**。**打卡**是本页中文里对这类操作的统称。  
下列场景覆盖：**放学回家 → 作业准备 → 分科作业 → 检查与订正 → 打卡提交 → 整理书包**，例子贴近国内小学至初中家庭常见场景。

---

## 1. 放学回家与作业启动 — After School & Getting Started

### 进门与状态切换

- **Welcome home—how was school today?** — 欢迎回家，今天学校怎么样？  
- **Snack first, then we start homework.** — 先吃点东西，然后开始作业。  
- **Wash your hands and change out of your uniform.** — 洗手，把校服换了。  
- **School mode off, homework mode on.** — 学校模式关闭，作业模式开启。  

### 准备作业环境

- **Clear the desk—only homework items allowed.** — 清理桌面，只留作业相关的东西。  
- **Where's your homework notebook?** — 你的作业记录本在哪里？  
- **Let's read today's assignments together.** — 我们一起看看今天的作业任务。  
- **Which subject do you want to start with?** — 你想从哪一科开始？  

### 作业时间与规划

- **How much homework do you have today?** — 今天作业多吗？  
- **Let's estimate—thirty minutes for math, twenty for Chinese?** — 估计一下，数学三十分钟，语文二十分钟？  
- **Set the timer for focused work.** — 设个倒计时，专心写。  
- **We'll take a break after the first subject.** — 写完第一科休息一下。  

---

## 2. 语文作业 — Chinese Language Homework

### 朗读与背诵

- **Read the text aloud twice.** — 把课文大声读两遍。  
- **Record your reading—we'll upload it for today's homework check-in.** — 录下朗读，待会儿上传到今日作业打卡。  
- **Try to recite from memory—I'll listen.** — 试着背一背，我听着。  
- **Your tone sounds natural now.** — 现在语气自然多了。  

### 写字与抄写

- **Write each new character three times.** — 每个生字写三遍。  
- **Pay attention to stroke order.** — 注意笔顺。  
- **Keep your handwriting neat—don't rush.** — 字写工整，别急。  
- **This character is off-center—try again.** — 这个字偏了，再来一个。  

### 阅读与作文

- **Read the assigned chapter quietly.** — 安静读指定章节。  
- **Summarize what you read in three sentences.** — 用三句话总结你读的内容。  
- **For the essay, brainstorm ideas first.** — 写作文先想思路。  
- **Draft first, revise later—don't aim for perfect.** — 先打草稿再改，别追求一遍完美。  

---

## 3. 数学作业 — Math Homework

### 计算练习

- **Start with the mental arithmetic sheet.** — 先从口算题开始。  
- **Show your work—don't just write answers.** — 写出步骤，别只写答案。  
- **Check your calculations with a calculator after finishing.** — 做完用计算器验算。  
- **You skipped a step here—go back.** — 这里跳了一步，回去补上。  

### 应用题与思维

- **Read the word problem carefully—what's being asked?** — 应用题仔细读，问的是什么？  
- **Underline the key numbers and question.** — 把关键数字和问题划出来。  
- **Draw a diagram if it helps.** — 可以画图帮助理解。  
- **This solution is creative—but does it make sense?** — 这个解法有创意，但合理吗？  

### 检查与订正

- **Review your answers before closing the book.** — 合上书之前检查一遍答案。  
- **Circle the ones you're unsure about.** — 把不确定的圈出来。  
- **Let's go through the mistakes together.** — 我们一起看错题。  
- **Understand why it's wrong, then correct it.** — 搞懂错在哪，再订正。

## 4. 英语作业 — English Homework

### 单词与课文

- **Copy the new words twice—English and Chinese.** — 新单词抄两遍，英文和中文。  
- **Use each word in a sentence.** — 每个单词造一个句子。  
- **Listen to the audio and repeat after it.** — 听音频，跟读。  
- **Record yourself reading the dialogue.** — 录下自己读对话。  

### 听力与口语

- **Put on your headphones for the listening exercise.** — 戴上耳机做听力。  
- **If you didn't catch it, play it again.** — 没听清就再放一遍。  
- **Practice the tongue twister slowly first.** — 绕口令先慢慢练。  
- **Your pronunciation improved—keep practicing.** — 发音进步了，继续练。

## 5. 其他科目与任务 — Other Subjects & Tasks

### 科学与社会

- **The science worksheet is due Friday.** — 科学作业单周五交。  
- **Research your topic online—save reliable sources.** — 上网查资料，保存可靠来源。  
- **For the social studies map, label all provinces.** — 社会课的地图，标注所有省份。  

### 美术与手工

- **The art project needs color pencils, not markers.** — 美术作业要彩铅，不要水彩笔。  
- **Cut along the dotted line carefully.** — 沿虚线小心剪。  
- **Glue dries clear—don't worry about the mess.** — 胶水是透明的，别担心弄脏。  

### PE homework & homework check-ins

- **Jump rope fifty times—I'll count.** — 跳绳五十下，我数着。  
- **Record a video of your sit-ups.** — 录一段仰卧起坐的视频。  
- **Upload your PE homework check-in before dinner.** — 体育作业的打卡／上传晚饭前要完成。

## 6. 检查、订正与打卡 — Review, Correction & Check-In

### 自查

- **Go through your checklist—what's done?** — 对照清单，哪些做完了？  
- **Sign your name at the top when finished.** — 做完在顶部签名。  
- **Pack everything into your schoolbag neatly.** — 把东西整齐收进书包。  

### 家长检查

- **Show me your completed work.** — 给我看看完成的作业。  
- **This page is blank—did you miss it?** — 这页空的，漏了吗？  
- **Your handwriting is especially neat today.** — 今天字写得特别工整。  
- **One mistake here—can you find it?** — 这里有个错，能找到吗？  

### 订正与理解

- **Don't erase—write the correction next to it.** — 别擦掉，在旁边写订正。  
- **Explain your thinking—why did you choose this answer?** — 说说你的想法，为什么选这个答案？  
- **Mistakes are how we learn—no shame in that.** — 错误是学习的方式，没什么好难为情的。  

### Submitting homework check-ins

- **Open the家长通 / ClassDojo app.** — 打开家长通 / ClassDojo。  
- **Take a clear photo of each page.** — 每页拍清楚照片。  
- **Make sure the lighting is good—no shadows.** — 光线要好，别留影子。  
- **Submit your homework check-in before nine p.m.** — 晚上九点前提交作业打卡。  
- **Mark as completed—green check.** — 标记完成，打绿勾。  
- **Teacher already gave feedback—let's read it.** — 老师已经反馈了，我们看看。

## 7. 作业困难与情绪 — Homework Struggles & Emotions

### 遇到难题

- **Stuck on this problem? Let's look together.** — 这题卡住了？一起看看。  
- **Break it into smaller steps.** — 拆成小步骤。  
- **It's okay to skip and come back later.** — 可以先跳过，等会儿回来做。  
- **You're not expected to know everything—ask for help.** — 不需要什么都懂，可以求助。  

### 疲惫与拖延

- **You look tired—shall we take a ten-minute break?** — 看起来累了，休息十分钟？  
- **Procrastinating makes it feel worse—start small.** — 拖着不做会更难受，先做一点点。  
- **One page done is better than zero.** — 做完一页也比零强。  
- **Homework first, then screen time—that's the deal.** — 先作业后屏幕，我们说好的。  

### 鼓励与肯定

- **I see you trying hard—that matters.** — 我看到你在努力，这很重要。  
- **You figured it out yourself—well done!** — 你自己想出来的，做得好！  
- **Progress, not perfection.** — 要进步，不是完美。  
- **We can tackle the rest tomorrow morning.** — 剩下的明天早上再做。

## 10. 场景对话 — Sample Dialogues

### 场景 1：作业太多抱怨

**Celine：** Too much homework today—I can't finish!  
**阿泽：** Let's list it out. Chinese, math, English… what else?  
**Celine：** And science worksheet.  
**阿泽：** We'll do one at a time. Start with your favorite.  
**Celine：** Math first—it's easiest.  
**阿泽：** Good choice. Twenty minutes, then a break.

### 场景 2：作业打卡拍照（check-in photo）

**阿泽：** Time to submit today's homework check-in.  
**Celine：** The lighting is bad in my room.  
**阿泽：** Move to the living room—near the window.  
**Celine：** Is this clear enough?  
**阿泽：** Perfect. Now upload and mark complete.  
**Celine：** Done! Teacher can see it now.  
**阿泽：** Great. One more subject to go.

### 场景 3：错题订正

**阿泽：** Math check—this answer looks different from the key.  
**Celine：** I got it wrong?  
**阿泽：** Walk me through your thinking.  
**Celine：** I added instead of subtracting.  
**阿泽：** Good catch. Write the correction here, and note why.  
**Celine：** "Added instead of subtracted—be careful next time."  
**阿泽：** That's how we learn.

## 一句话家长口令

**Start together, check together, celebrate together—homework is a habit built with patience, not pressure.** — 一起开始，一起检查，一起庆祝——作业是耐心养成的习惯，不是压力逼出来的任务。
