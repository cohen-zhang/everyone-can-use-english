# 日常礼貌与餐桌礼仪（含坐姿）— 亲子英文

**索引：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/README|亲子英文材料索引]]


家庭高频礼貌表达 + 吃饭场景规则，句子尽量短，方便不多 / Celine 跟读。`亲子`

---

## 1. 日常礼貌基础 — Daily Manners

- **Please.** / **Say please.**  
请。/ 说“请”。`亲子`
- **Thank you.** / **Thank you so much.**  
谢谢。/ 非常感谢。`亲子`
- **You’re welcome.** / **My pleasure.**  
不客气。/ 很乐意。`亲子`
- **Excuse me.**  
不好意思（借过、打断前）。`亲子`
- **Sorry.** / **I’m sorry.**  
对不起。`亲子`
- **May I…?** / **Can I…?**  
我可以……吗？`亲子`

### 家里常用完整句

- **May I have some water, please?**  
我可以喝点水吗？`亲子`
- **Could you pass me the spoon, please?**  
请把勺子递给我，好吗？`亲子`
- **Thank you for helping me.**  
谢谢你帮我。`亲子`
- **I’m sorry I shouted.**  
对不起，我刚刚大声了。`亲子`
- **Please wait your turn.**  
请排队 / 轮到你再来。`亲子`

## 2. 餐前礼貌 — Before the Meal

- **Wash your hands before eating.**  
吃饭前先洗手。`亲子`
- **Come to the table, please. Dinner is ready.**  
请来餐桌，晚饭好了。`亲子`
- **Wait for everyone before you start.**  
大家到齐再开始。`亲子`
- **Let’s say “Enjoy your meal.”** / **(US) Dig in.**  
说“用餐愉快”。/（美口语）开吃吧。`亲子`
- **Please bring your bowl and spoon.**  
请把你的碗和勺子拿来。`亲子`

---

## 3. 餐桌礼仪（含坐姿）— Table Manners & Posture

### 坐姿和手部

- **Sit up straight.**  
坐直。`亲子`
- **Keep your feet on the floor.**  
双脚放地上。`亲子`
- **Don’t kneel on the chair.**  
不要跪在椅子上。`亲子`
- **Don’t lean back too far.**  
别往后仰太多。`亲子`
- **Elbows off the table while eating.** / **(UK) Keep your elbows off the table.**  
吃饭时手肘别撑桌。`亲子`
- **Use your napkin on your lap.**  
餐巾放腿上用。`亲子`

### 吃东西时

- **Chew with your mouth closed.**  
闭嘴嚼。`亲子`
- **Don’t talk with food in your mouth.**  
嘴里有食物时别说话。`亲子`
- **Take small bites.**  
小口吃。`亲子`
- **Eat slowly. Don’t rush.**  
慢慢吃，不着急。`亲子`
- **Use your spoon/chopsticks gently.**  
勺子 / 筷子轻一点用。`亲子`
- **No playing with your food.**  
不要拿食物玩。`亲子`
- **Don’t zone out. Focus on your meal.**  
别发呆，专心吃饭。`亲子`
- **No daydreaming at the table.**  
吃饭时别走神 / 别光发呆想别的。`亲子`
- **You’re spacing out. Come back and take a bite.**  
你走神啦，回神吃一口。`亲子`
- **Please pay attention—you’ve barely eaten.**  
注意一下，你几乎没怎么吃。`亲子`

### 礼貌请求与拒绝

- **Could I have a little more rice, please?**  
我可以再来一点米饭吗？`亲子`
- **No, thank you. I’m full.**  
不用了，谢谢。我饱了。`亲子`
- **May I leave the table?**  
我可以离开餐桌吗？`亲子`
- **Please clear your bowl after dinner.**  
饭后请把你的碗收走。`亲子`

## 4. 中国家庭餐桌常见表达

- **Use serving chopsticks.**  
用公筷。`亲子`
- **Take only what you can finish.**  
吃多少夹多少。`亲子`
- **Finish what’s in your bowl.**  
先把碗里的吃完。`亲子`
- **Don’t stand up and walk around while eating.**  
吃饭时不要走来走去。`亲子`
- **Soup is hot—blow on it first.**  
汤很烫，先吹一吹。`亲子`

## 5. 短对话（阿泽、不多、Celine）

**场景 A：提醒坐姿**

- **Dad (阿泽)**: Celine, sit up straight and keep your feet down.  
- **Celine (不多)**: Like this?  
- **Dad**: Yes, that’s great table posture.  
— 用 *sit up straight* + *feet down* 组合提醒。`亲子`

**场景 B：礼貌加饭**

- **Celine**: May I have a little more soup, please?  
- **Dad**: Great asking. Here you go.  
- **Celine**: Thank you!  
- **Dad**: You’re welcome.  
— 一次练四句：请求、肯定、感谢、回应。`亲子`

**场景 C：结束用餐**

- **Dad**: Are you done?  
- **Celine**: Yes. May I leave the table?  
- **Dad**: Yes, after you clear your bowl.  
— 把 “离席前收碗” 说成固定规则句。`亲子`

**场景 D：别发呆、专心吃**

- **Dad (阿泽)**: Hey, you’re zoning out. Focus on your food, okay?  
- **Celine (不多)**: Okay… I’m eating.  
- **Dad**: Good. Small bites.  
— 用 *zone out* / *focus on your food* 提醒吃饭时走神。`亲子`
