# 洗澡日常 — Bath & Shower Time

**索引：** [[learning-notes/parenting-english/README|亲子英文材料索引]]

**相关：** 洗漱核心动词速查（wash, rinse, scrub, brush, wipe 等）见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/vocabulary/parenting-actions-vocab-extended-routines#8-梳洗与卫生--grooming--hygiene|动作动词 — 分类总表 §8 梳洗与卫生]]（与本页相向互链）。**再五分钟／快睡觉了** 等时间口令总入口 → [[learning-notes/parenting-english/daily-life/parenting-time-expressions-daily-phrases#4-多久再几分钟--duration--countdowns|时间表达汇总 · 多久]]。

面向**亲子洗澡场景**：从放水、调试水温、洗头洗澡、擦干到出浴室的完整流程。学习者身份：**阿泽**；孩子：**不多 / Celine**。`亲子`

---

## 1. 洗澡前准备 — Before the Bath

### 进浴室 / 准备物品

- **Time for your bath, Celine.** — Celine，该洗澡了。  
- **Let's go to the bathroom.** — 我们去浴室吧。  
- **Grab your towel and clean clothes.** — 拿好毛巾和干净衣服。  
- **Take off your dirty clothes first.** — 先把脏衣服脱掉。  
- **Put your dirty clothes in the hamper.** — 脏衣服放进洗衣篮。  

### 孩子拖沓 / 家长催促

- **Come on, Celine—bath time!** — 快点，Celine，该洗澡了！  
- **Stop playing and head to the bathroom.** — 别玩了，去浴室。  
- **I've asked you three times already.** — 我都叫你三遍了。  
- **We're running late. Hurry up, please.** — 要来不及了，快点吧。  
- **Put the toy down—we'll play after your bath.** — 玩具先放下，洗完澡再玩。  
- **No more TV. Time to get cleaned up.** — 电视关啦，该洗澡了。  
- **You can have five more minutes, then it's bath time.** — 再给你五分钟，然后必须洗澡。  
- **Almost bedtime—we need to wash up first.** — 快睡觉了，得先洗干净。  
- **I can hear you still playing in your room.** — 我听见你还在房间玩呢。  
- **Last warning—bath starts now.** — 最后一次提醒，现在洗澡。  
- **If you come now, you still get story time.** — 现在来洗，讲完故事照旧。  
- **You're stalling again. Let's move.** — 又在磨蹭了，动起来。  
- **Quick shower, then pajamas and bed.** — 快速冲一下，然后睡衣睡觉。  

### 调水温与放水

- **Let me run the water.** — 我来放水。  
- **Test the water with your hand.** — 用手试试水温。  
- **Is it too hot or too cold?** — 太烫还是太凉？  
- **Turn the knob to make it warmer.** — 旋钮往这边调热一点。  
- **The water feels just right.** — 水温正好。  
- ~~**Don't fill the tub too full.** — 浴缸别放太满。~~

## 2. 洗头 — Washing Hair

### 打湿与上洗发水

- **Lean your head back.** — 头往后仰。  
- **Close your eyes tight.** — 眼睛闭紧。  
- **I'll wet your hair first.** — 我先把你头发打湿。  
- **Here's the shampoo.** — 洗发水来了。  
- **Rub it gently into your scalp.** — 轻轻搓到头皮上。  
- **Make lots of bubbles.** — 揉出好多泡泡。  

### 冲洗

- **Rinse thoroughly.** — 冲干净。  
- **Keep your eyes closed.** — 眼睛一直闭着。  
- **Tilt your head back so soap doesn't run into your eyes.** — 头往后仰，别让水流进眼睛。  
- **Is the shampoo all out?** — 洗发水冲干净了吗？  
- **One more rinse.** — 再冲一遍。  

### 护发素（可选）

- **This is conditioner—it makes hair soft.** — 这是护发素，让头发顺顺的。  
- **Leave it in for a minute, then rinse.** — 停留一分钟再冲掉。

## 3. 洗身体 — Washing Body

### 上身

- **Wet your washcloth.** — 把 washcloth（洗澡巾）打湿。  
- **Soap up your washcloth.** — 打上沐浴露。  
- **Start with your neck.** — 从脖子开始洗。  
- **Wash behind your ears.** — 耳朵后面也要洗。  
- **Scrub your armpits.** — 腋下搓一搓。  
- **Don't forget your belly button.** — 别忘了肚脐眼。  

### 下身

- **Wash between your toes.** — 脚趾缝也要洗。  
- **Scrub your knees—they're dirty.** — 膝盖搓搓，好脏呀。  
- **Rinse all the soap off.** — 把泡泡都冲掉。  

### 隐私部位（温和版）

- **Wash your private parts quickly and gently.** — 私密处快速轻轻洗一下。  
- **Front to back, always.** — 永远是从前往后洗。  
- **That's your own body—keep it clean.** — 这是自己的身体，要保持干净。

## 4. 泡澡时光（可选）— Tub Time

- **Sit down slowly in the tub.** — 慢慢坐进浴缸。  
- **Don't splash water out.** — 水别泼出来。  
- **You can play with your rubber duck.** — 可以玩小鸭子玩具。  
- **Five more minutes, then we get out.** — 再玩五分钟就出来。  
- **The water is getting cold.** — 水开始凉了。  
- **Time to pull the plug.** — 该拔塞子放水了。  

---

## 5. 淋浴 — Shower Time

- **Stand under the showerhead.** — 站到花洒下面。  
- **Turn the handle to start the water.** — 扳把手开水。  
- **Stay still so I can rinse you.** — 站着别动，我给你冲。  
- **Turn around—let me get your back.** — 转过来，后背冲一下。  
- **The water's stopping—turn it off.** — 水停了，关掉吧。  

---

## 6. 擦干与出浴 — Drying Off

### 擦干身体

- **Step out carefully—the floor is slippery.** — 小心出来，地面滑。  
- **Wrap yourself in the big towel.** — 用大浴巾把自己包起来。  
- **Dry your hair first.** — 先把头发擦干。  
- **Pat your face dry—don't rub hard.** — 把脸按干，别用力擦。  
- **Dry between your toes.** — 脚趾缝也要擦干。  
- **Shake the water off your hands.** — 手甩一甩水。  

### 换衣服

- **Put on your clean underwear.** — 穿上干净内裤。  
- **Your pajamas are on the hook.** — 睡衣挂在钩子上。  
- **Let's get dressed in your room.** — 去房间穿衣服吧。  

---

## 7. 吹头发与护肤 — Hair & Skincare

### 吹头发

- **Sit on the stool for me to dry your hair.** — 坐小凳子上，我给你吹头发。  
- **This is the hair dryer.** — 这是吹风机。  
- **Low heat or cool air?** — 要低温还是冷风？  
- **Hold still—the noise is loud.** — 别动，声音有点响。  
- **Your hair is all dry and fluffy now.** — 头发现在又干又蓬松。  

### 护肤

- **Put on some lotion so your skin doesn't get dry.** — 涂点润肤露，皮肤不干。  
- **A little lip balm—your lips look chapped.** — 一点点唇膏，嘴唇有点干。  
- **Brush your teeth after bath.** — 洗完澡刷牙。

## 9. 安全提醒 — Safety Notes

- **Never run in the bathroom—the floor is wet.** — 浴室别跑，地面湿。  
- **Don't touch electrical things with wet hands.** — 手湿别碰电器。  
- **Call me if you need help.** — 需要帮忙就叫我。  
- **Stay seated in the tub—don't stand up suddenly.** — 浴缸里坐好，别突然站起来。  
- **Test the water before jumping in.** — 先试水再进去。

## 10. 场景对话 — Sample Dialogues

### 场景 1：不愿意洗澡

**阿泽：** Time for bath, Celine.  
**Celine：** But I'm not dirty!  
**阿泽：** You played in the park. Quick shower, then story time.  
**Celine：** Okay, but I want bubbles.  
**阿泽：** Deal—bubble bath for ten minutes.

### 场景 2：洗发水进眼睛

**Celine：** Ouch! Soap in my eyes!  
**阿泽：** Close them tight—I'll rinse right now. Tilt your head back.  
**Celine：** It stings…  
**阿泽：** Almost done. Next time keep eyes shut until I say open.  
**Celine：** Okay, Dad.

### 场景 3：洗完擦干

**阿泽：** Step out—here's your towel.  
**Celine：** I'm cold.  
**阿泽：** Wrap up quick. Dry your hair first so you don't catch cold.  
**Celine：** Can I wear my bunny pajamas?  
**阿泽：** They're on your bed. Go get dressed.

## 一句话家长口令

**Warm water, gentle scrub, dry thoroughly—three steps keep bath time calm and safe.** — 温水、轻搓、擦干——三步让洗澡时光平静又安全。

## 11. 英文短文 · 阿泽自述（洗澡全流程）

*约 1–2 分钟 · 描述训练 · 串联本页常用句*

**English**

In our home, bath time starts with preparation.  
I run warm water first and test the temperature with my hand.  
Then I call Celine in and remind her to walk slowly on the wet floor.  

We wash hair before body when she is still calm.  
I wet her hair gently, add a little shampoo, and massage lightly.  
When I rinse, I ask her to close her eyes and tilt her head back.  
If soap gets in her eyes, I rinse right away and stay calm.  

After hair, we clean arms, neck, back, knees, and feet.  
If she wants bubble time, we keep it short and safe.  
Then we switch to shower, rinse everything off, and step out carefully.  

Outside the bathroom, I wrap her in a towel and dry her thoroughly.  
Hair first, then body, so she doesn't feel cold.  
We put on pajamas, use a little moisturizer or lip balm if needed, and finish with a calm bedtime transition.  
A predictable bath routine helps her feel safe, clean, and ready for sleep.

**简中：**

在我们家，洗澡先做准备：先放温水，用手试温。  
叫 Celine 进来后先提醒地面湿，慢慢走。  

她状态好时先洗头：先打湿、少量洗发水、轻轻按摩。  
冲洗时让她闭眼、头微微后仰。  
如果进眼睛，就立刻冲干净，语气保持稳定。  

然后洗身体：手臂、脖子、后背、膝盖、脚都带到。  
要泡泡浴可以，但时间短、边界清楚。  
最后淋浴冲净，出浴室注意防滑。  

出来后先包毛巾，彻底擦干，先头发再身体，避免着凉。  
换睡衣，按需涂点润肤或润唇，再平稳进入睡前流程。  
固定的洗澡节奏会让孩子更有安全感，也更容易安静入睡。
