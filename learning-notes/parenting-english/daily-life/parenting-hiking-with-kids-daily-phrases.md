带孩子徒步时，能直接开口用的英文短句与小对话。

## 1) 出发前 Before We Start

- **Check your water bottle and hat.**  
检查一下你的水壶和帽子。
- **Wear your hiking shoes, not your slippers.**  
穿徒步鞋，不要穿拖鞋。
- **Put on sunscreen before we leave.**  
出门前先涂防晒。
- **Keep a light snack in your backpack.**  
书包里放一点轻便零食。

---

## 2) 路上提醒 On the Trail

- **Stay on the trail and walk beside me.**  
沿着步道走，走在我旁边。
- **Slow down on the downhill section.**  
下坡路段慢一点。
- **Watch your step. The rocks are loose.**  
注意脚下，石头有点松。
- **Use your hands on the steep part.**  
陡一点的地方用手辅助。
- **Take small steps and keep your balance.**  
小步走，保持平衡。

---

## 3) 安全与边界 Safety Boundaries

- **No running near the cliff edge.**  
靠近崖边不要跑。
- **Stop at the red sign and wait for me.**  
走到红色标志那儿就停下等我。
- **If you feel tired, tell me right away.**  
如果你累了，马上告诉我。
- **One by one on the narrow bridge.**  
窄桥上一个一个过。
- **Hold the rail with one hand.**  
一只手扶栏杆。

---

## 4) 鼓励孩子 Keep Them Motivated

- **You're doing great. Keep going.**  
你做得很棒，继续。
- **Let's walk to that tree and take a break.**  
我们走到那棵树再休息。
- **You climbed that hill all by yourself.**  
你自己爬上了那段坡。
- **High five! You stayed focused.**  
击掌！你很专注。

---

## 5) 休息点休整 Break Time

| English | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| **Let's sit here for five minutes.** | 我们在这里坐五分钟。 |
| **Drink some water first.** | 先喝点水。 |
| **Do you want an apple or a sandwich?** | 你想吃苹果还是三明治？ |
| **Take deep breaths and relax your legs.** | 深呼吸，放松一下腿。 |
| **We'll head back after this break.** | 这次休息完我们就往回走。 |

---

## 6) 小对话 Mini Dialogues

### 场景 A：上坡

**Dad:** Big hill ahead. Short steps, okay?  
爸爸：前面是大坡，小步走，好吗？

**Celine:** Okay. Can I use my hands too?  
Celine：好。我可以用手一起爬吗？

**Dad:** Yes, great idea. Three points of contact.  
爸爸：可以，好主意。保持三点着力。

### 场景 B：想放弃

**Celine:** I'm tired. I don't want to walk.  
Celine：我累了，不想走了。

**Mom:** I hear you. Let's rest for two minutes, then walk to that sign.  
妈妈：我知道你累了。我们先休息两分钟，然后走到那个路牌。

**Celine:** Deal.  
Celine：成交。

---

*先练“短句 + 手势”，再升级到完整对话，孩子更容易跟上。*
