## 歌手简介 · About Sara Kays

**English**

**Sara Kays** (born 24 November 1999) is an American **singer-songwriter** from **Silver Spring, Maryland**. She started writing songs at age 13 and gained attention through YouTube and TikTok with intimate, diary-like **indie pop** and **folk-pop** ballads. Her lyrics often explore anxiety, body image, and the small moments in relationships. **"When You Look At Me"** (2022), released via **Atlantic Records**, became one of her most-streamed songs; she had already released the EP *Camera Shy* (2020) and the breakout single **"Remember That Night?"**, and toured as an opening act for artists such as **Lauv** and **AJR**.

**中文**

**Sara Kays**（1999 年 11 月 24 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**，来自**马里兰州银泉市**。13 岁开始写歌，通过在 YouTube 和 TikTok 发布私密、日记感的 **独立流行** 与 **民谣流行抒情曲** 获得关注。歌词常写焦虑、身体形象、恋爱里的细碎瞬间。**"When You Look At Me"**（2022，**Atlantic Records**）是她播放量最高的作品之一；此前已有 EP *Camera Shy*（2020）与爆款单曲 **"Remember That Night?"**，并为 **Lauv**、**AJR** 等艺人担任开场嘉宾巡演。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"When You Look At Me"** was released on **18 February 2022** as a standalone single. Kays wrote it about **insecurity in a relationship**: wondering whether her partner still sees her the way he once did when he compared her to the **moon**—gentle, luminous, special. The chorus contrasts **moon** vs **sun**, asking if someone else now shines brighter in his eyes. The bridge (*I won't be spun around anymore*) shows her recognizing the pattern before walking through his door again. The song's sparse production and conversational tone make it ideal for sing-along practice; it became one of Kays's most-streamed tracks on **Spotify** and **Apple Music**, with strong traction on **TikTok**.

**中文**

**"When You Look At Me"** 于 **2022 年 2 月 18 日**以单曲形式发行。Kays 写的是恋爱里的**不安全感**：对方曾说她像**月亮**——温柔、发光、特别——可现在他看她的眼神还一样吗？副歌用 **月亮 / 太阳** 对比，问是否已有别人在他眼里更耀眼。桥段 *I won't be spun around anymore*（我不会再被耍得团团转）表明她看清模式，却仍可能重蹈覆辙。编曲克制、语气像聊天，很适合跟唱练习；在 **Spotify** 与 **Apple Music** 上成为 Kays 播放量最高的作品之一，**TikTok** 传播也很广。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *When you look at me* 重复多遍，适合 **KTV 跟唱**；先听清 moon/sun 两句再练 Verse。
- 注意 *I don't feel like you **like** me* 里两个 *like* 不同用法：第一个是「觉得」，第二个是「喜欢」。
- 想延伸练**恋爱里的自我怀疑**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 对照不安、委屈类表达。
