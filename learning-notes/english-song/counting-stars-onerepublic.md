## 歌手简介 · About OneRepublic

**English**

**OneRepublic** is an American **pop rock** band formed in **Colorado Springs** in 2002, led by vocalist and songwriter **Ryan Tedder**. Tedder is also a prolific producer and songwriter for other major artists. The band broke through with **"Apologize"** (2007) and followed with global hits including **"Counting Stars,"** **"Good Life,"** and **"Secrets."** Their sound mixes **arena rock**, **pop**, and **folk** elements with anthemic choruses. *Native* (2013), which features **"Counting Stars,"** became one of their best-selling albums; the song topped charts in multiple countries and has billions of streams.

**中文**

**OneRepublic** 是 2002 年在**科罗拉多斯普林斯**成立的美国**流行摇滚**乐队，主唱兼词曲作者 **Ryan Tedder** 为核心。Tedder 也是多位大牌艺人的制作人。乐队以 **"Apologize"**（2007）成名，随后推出 **"Counting Stars"**、**"Good Life"**、**"Secrets"** 等全球热单。风格融合**舞台摇滚**、**流行** 与 **民谣** 元素，副歌宏大。2013 年专辑 *Native* 收录 **"Counting Stars"**，为乐队最畅销作品之一；单曲多国夺冠，流媒体播放量达数十亿。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Counting Stars"** was released on **14 June 2013** as the second single from *Native*. Co-written by Ryan Tedder, it contrasts **counting dollars** with **counting stars**—chasing money versus chasing dreams and meaning. Lyrics like *Everything that kills me makes me feel alive* and *doing the wrong thing / doing the right thing* express moral tension: society says follow the safe path, but the singer feels more alive when breaking rules. The track blends acoustic verses with a stomping, hand-clap chorus influenced by **gospel** and **folk** rhythms, making it a karaoke favorite and a strong piece for practicing stress and rhythm. It reached **No. 2** on the **Billboard Hot 100**, **No. 1** in the UK, and has surpassed **3 billion** streams worldwide—one of the best-selling singles of the 2010s.

**中文**

**"Counting Stars"** 于 **2013 年 6 月 14 日**发行，为 *Native* 第二支单曲。由 Ryan Tedder 等人创作，核心对比是 **数钞票** 与 **数星星**——追钱还是追梦、追意义。*Everything that kills me makes me feel alive*（杀不死我的让我更鲜活）以及 *doing the wrong thing / doing the right thing* 呈现道德张力：社会要你走稳妥的路，歌手却在「犯错」时感到更活着。木吉他段落与拍手、跺脚式副歌结合，带 **福音** 与 **民谣** 节奏感，是 KTV 热门曲目，也适合练重音与节奏。单曲在 **Billboard Hot 100** 最高 **第 2** 位、**英国单曲榜夺冠**，全球流媒体播放量逾 **30 亿**，是 2010 年代最畅销单曲之一。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **losing sleep** | 睡不着 | *lose sleep over* = 为……失眠 |
| **counting dollars / counting stars** | 数钞票 / 数星星 | 歌眼：逐利 vs 追梦 |
| **praying hard** | 努力祈祷 | *hard* 加强程度 |
| **swinging vine** | 摆荡的藤 | 比喻人生冒险 |
| **swing across the line** | 荡过界线 | 越过安全边界 |
| **seek and ye shall find** | 寻找，就会找到 | 圣经句式；*ye* = 古语「你们」 |
| **not that old / not that bold** | 没那么老 / 没那么敢 | 自我定位的对比句 |
| **the world is sold on** | 世人全都信服 | *be sold on* = 被说服、买账 |
| **doing what we're told** | 照别人说的做 | 从众、听话 |
| **everything that kills me** | 杀不死我的（那些事） | 矛盾修辞，接 *makes me feel alive* |
| **watch it burn** | 看它烧掉 | 对金钱/物质的决绝态度 |
| **four-letter word** | 四个字母的词 | 常暗指粗口；此处指 *hope* 双关 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Lately, I've been, I've been losing sleep* 节奏鲜明，适合 **KTV** 与通勤跟唱；注意 *counting dollars* 与 *counting stars* 的对比重音。
- *Old, but I'm not that old / Young, but I'm not that bold* 是平行结构，可用来练「让步对比」句型。
- Bridge 拍手跺脚段适合练**节奏感**；想延伸「人生选择」话题，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于内心冲突的表达。
