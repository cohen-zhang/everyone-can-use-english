## 歌手简介 · About Lewis Capaldi

**English**

**Lewis Capaldi** (born 7 October 1996) is a **Scottish singer-songwriter** from Bathgate, West Lothian. He began posting cover songs on **SoundCloud** and **YouTube** as a teenager and was discovered after his track **"Bruises"** went viral in 2017. His debut album **Divinely Uninspired To A Hellish Extent** (2019) became one of the UK's best-selling records of the decade. Capaldi is known for a **powerful, raspy voice** and emotionally direct ballads about heartbreak and grief; he won the **Brit Award for Best New Artist** in 2020 and has cited **Adele** and **Travis** as influences. He took a career break in 2023 to focus on **Tourette syndrome** and mental health before returning with new music.

**中文**

**Lewis Capaldi**（1996 年 10 月 7 日生）是苏格兰**创作型歌手**，来自 **West Lothian** 的 **Bathgate**。少年时在 **SoundCloud** 与 **YouTube** 发翻唱，2017 年 **"Bruises"** 病毒传播后被发掘。首张专辑 **Divinely Uninspired To A Hellish Extent**（2019）成为英国十年最畅销唱片之一。他以**有力、沙哑的嗓音**和直白的失恋、哀悼 ballad 著称；2020 年获 **全英音乐奖「最佳新人」**；影响包括 **Adele** 与 **Travis**。2023 年因 **妥瑞症** 与心理健康暂别乐坛，之后回归发新曲。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Someone You Loved"** was released on **8 November 2018** and later became the cornerstone of Capaldi's debut album. Co-written with **TMS** (Tom Barnes, Ben Kohn, Peter Kelleher) and **Romans**, the piano ballad describes the void after losing someone who once made you feel safe—*I was getting kinda used to being someone you loved*. Lewis has said the song was inspired partly by the death of his **grandmother** and partly by a breakup; the grief is universal enough to fit both. The image *the day bleeds into nightfall* captures how time blurs when you're drowning. The track topped the **UK Singles Chart** for seven weeks and hit **No. 1** on the **Billboard Hot 100** in 2020.

**中文**

**"Someone You Loved"** 于 **2018 年 11 月 8 日**发行，后成为 Capaldi 首张专辑的核心曲目。与 **TMS**（Tom Barnes、Ben Kohn、Peter Kelleher）及 **Romans** 共同创作，这首钢琴 ballad 写失去那个曾让你感到安全的人之后的空洞——*I was getting kinda used to being someone you loved*（我差不多习惯了当你爱的那个人）。Lewis 说灵感部分来自**祖母去世**、部分来自分手；哀悼写得够 universal，两种情境都说得通。*the day bleeds into nightfall* 写溺水般的日子里白昼与黑夜糊成一片。单曲在英国榜首停留七周，2020 年登顶 **Billboard Hot 100**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **going under** | 要沉下去了；撑不住了 | 字面溺水，引申情绪崩溃 |
| **all or nothing** | 孤注一掷；非黑即白 | 形容爱得太极端、没有中间地带 |
| **drive someone crazy** | 把某人逼疯 | *got a way of* = 总会…… |
| **numb all the pain** | 麻木所有痛苦 | *numb* 作动词 |
| **the day bleeds into nightfall** | 白昼淌进夜幕 | 诗意写法：时间糊成一片 |
| **get me through it all** | 陪我熬过这一切 | *get through* = 撑过 |
| **let my guard down** | 卸下防备 | 习语；guard = 戒备 |
| **pull the rug (out from under someone)** | 抽走地毯；突然坑某人 | 让人失去支撑、摔得很惨 |
| **getting kinda used to** | 差不多习惯了 | *kinda* = kind of |
| **someone you loved** | 你爱的那个人 | 歌题核心：身份随对方消失 |
| **fall into your arms** | 跌入你的怀抱 | 浪漫固定搭配 |
| **safe in your sound** | 在你声音里安全 | *sound* 指对方的声音／存在 |
| **come back around** | 回过神来；恢复常态 | 从崩溃中缓过来 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Someone you loved* 旋律线清晰，**KTV 热门**；先练 *the day bleeds into nightfall* 的重音与元音。
- *pull the rug* 是常用习语，完整说法是 *pull the rug out from under someone*；歌里省略了后半段。
- 想积累**分手与失恋口语**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 相关句型。
