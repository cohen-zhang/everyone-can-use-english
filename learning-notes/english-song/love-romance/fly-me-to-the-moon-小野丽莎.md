## 歌手简介 · About 小野丽莎 (Lisa Ono)

**English**

**Lisa Ono** (小野丽莎, born **1962** in **São Paulo, Brazil**) is a **Brazilian-Japanese** singer celebrated for **bossa nova** and soft **vocal jazz**. She moved to Tokyo as a child and later built a career singing in Portuguese, English, and Japanese, with a light, intimate voice that suits café and late-night listening. Albums such as *Dream*, *Pretty World*, and *Cheek to Cheek* made her a familiar name across Asia for jazz-standard covers with a Rio-tinged warmth. Her take on *"Fly Me to the Moon"* sits in that tradition: unhurried, romantic, and easy to sing along with.

**中文**

**小野丽莎**（Lisa Ono，**1962** 年生于巴西**圣保罗**）是**日裔巴西**歌手，以 **bossa nova** 与柔和的**人声爵士**著称。童年迁居东京，后以葡萄牙语、英语、日语演唱，嗓音轻、近、适合咖啡馆与深夜听感。*Dream*、*Pretty World*、*Cheek to Cheek* 等专辑让她在亚洲以「带里约温度的爵士标准曲翻唱」闻名。她的 *"Fly Me to the Moon"* 正是这一路：不赶、浪漫、很好跟唱。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Fly Me to the Moon"** was written by **Bart Howard** in **1954** (originally titled *"In Other Words"*) and became one of the most recorded **jazz standards**. Lisa Ono's version appears on ***Cheek To Cheek -Jazz Standards from RIO-*** (released **4 March 2009**). The lyric is a playful love plea: take me to the moon, let me play among the stars—**in other words**, hold my hand, kiss me, be true, I love you. Jazz singers often repeat the full cycle; this note includes **one complete pass** of the classic lyric. For a punchier big-band contrast, hear [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-弗兰克辛纳特拉|Frank Sinatra with Count Basie]].

**中文**

**"Fly Me to the Moon"** 由 **Bart Howard** 于 **1954** 年创作（原名 *"In Other Words"*），是被翻唱最多的**爵士标准曲**之一。小野丽莎版本收录于 ***Cheek To Cheek -Jazz Standards from RIO-***（**2009 年 3 月 4 日**发行）。歌词是俏皮的求爱：带我去月球、让我在星星间玩耍——**换句话说**，牵我的手、吻我、对我真心、我爱你。爵士演唱常整段循环；本笔记收录**完整一轮**经典歌词。若想听更有力的大乐队对比，见 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-弗兰克辛纳特拉|Frank Sinatra 与 Count Basie 版]]。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **fly me to the moon** | 带我飞去月球 | 祈使 + 间接宾语；浪漫夸张，不是真要航天 |
| **play among the stars** | 在星星间玩耍 | *among* = 置身其间；画面感强 |
| **what spring is like** | 春天是什么样 | *what … is like* = ……是怎样的 |
| **in other words** | 换句话说 | 歌的核心转折：太空意象 → 现实亲密 |
| **hold my hand** | 牵着我的手 | 最直白的亲密请求 |
| **fill my heart with song** | 用歌声填满我的心 | *fill A with B* = 用 B 填满 A |
| **forevermore** | 从此以后；永远 | 偏文学/歌词用词；口语更常说 *forever* |
| **all I long for** | 我全部的渴望 | *long for* = 渴望、思念 |
| **worship and adore** | 崇拜又珍爱 | 程度很深的爱慕；略夸张、偏歌词 |
| **please be true** | 请对我真心 | *be true (to me)* = 忠诚、不欺骗 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 抓住 **In other words** 两次转折：前面是幻想，后面才是真正想说的话——练叙事语气很有用。
- 小野丽莎版偏 **bossa / 轻爵士**，节奏不赶；跟唱时把 *fly me / let me / fill my* 的弱读唱顺即可。
- *Jupiter and Mars / forevermore / worship and adore* 押韵整齐，适合练元音开口与句尾落点。
- 对照 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-弗兰克辛纳特拉|Sinatra + Basie 版]]：同一句歌词，一个软、一个甩，听出 **cover 风格差** 比背词更有收获。
