## 歌手简介 · About 藤井风 (Fujii Kaze)

**English**

**Fujii Kaze** (藤井风, born **1997** in **Okayama, Japan**) is a Japanese **singer-songwriter** and pianist known for blending **J-pop, R&B, soul, and jazz**. He rose to national fame with *"Nan-Nan"* and *"Kirari"*, then reached a global audience with *"Shinunoga E-Wa"* through social media. His debut album ***HELP EVER HURT NEVER*** (2020) established his warm falsetto, piano-driven grooves, and bilingual lyric sensibility. He often covers Western classics in English with a soft, intimate delivery—*"Sunny"* on the cover album ***LOVE ALL COVER ALL*** is a clear example.

**中文**

**藤井风**（Fujii Kaze，**1997** 年生于日本**冈山**）是日本**唱作人**、钢琴手，风格融合 **J-pop、R&B、soul 与爵士**。凭 *"Nan-Nan"*、*"Kirari"* 走红国内，又以 *"Shinunoga E-Wa"* 借社交媒体打入国际视野。2020 年首专 ***HELP EVER HURT NEVER*** 奠定其温暖假声、钢琴律动与双语歌词气质。他常以柔和、贴耳的唱腔翻唱英文经典——翻唱专辑 ***LOVE ALL COVER ALL*** 中的 *"Sunny"* 便是代表。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Sunny"** was written by **Bobby Hebb** and first became a hit in **1966**. Fujii Kaze's English-language cover appears on ***LOVE ALL COVER ALL*** (released **4 December 2020**). The song is a bright **soul / pop** love letter: after days of rain and pain, someone named Sunny smiles, eases the hurt, and turns the singer's world sunny again. Hebb wrote it after personal tragedy; the lyric's gratitude—*thank you for the sunshine bouquet*—reads as both romance and emotional recovery. Fujii's version keeps the classic English words but softens them with R&B phrasing and piano warmth.

**中文**

**"Sunny"** 由 **Bobby Hebb** 创作，**1966** 年成名。藤井风的英文翻唱收录于 ***LOVE ALL COVER ALL***（**2020 年 12 月 4 日**发行）。这是一首明亮的 **soul / pop** 情歌：生活曾满是雨和痛，名叫 Sunny 的人一笑，痛就轻了，日子也重新放晴。Hebb 在个人伤痛后写下此曲；歌词里的感恩——*thank you for the sunshine bouquet*——既是恋爱，也是情绪自愈。藤井风保留经典英文歌词，却用 R&B 咬字与钢琴温度把它唱得更柔、更近。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **filled with rain** | 满是雨；生活灰蒙蒙 | 隐喻情绪低落，不是字面天气预报 |
| **eased the pain** | 减轻了痛苦；把痛抚平 | *ease* = 缓和；比 *stop the pain* 更柔和 |
| **the dark days are gone** | 黑暗的日子过去了 | 常见励志/疗愈句式；*are gone* = 已结束 |
| **shines so sincere** | 笑得/照得那么真诚 | *sincere* 修饰人的真诚，不是灯光亮度 |
| **sunshine bouquet** | 阳光花束 | 把阳光比作一束花；浪漫又口语化的感谢 |
| **brought my way** | 带到我身边；送到我路上 | *come / bring someone's way* = 碰巧来到某人生活里 |
| **your all and all** | 你的全部；倾其所有 | 强调毫无保留地付出 |
| **feel ten feet tall** | 觉得自己很高大；自信爆棚 | 习语：因被爱/被肯定而自我感觉良好 |
| **from A to Z** | 从头到尾；一应俱全 | 字母首尾 = 全部细节 |
| **torn like a windblown sand** | 像风吹的沙子一样散乱 | 明喻：生活破碎、站不稳 |
| **a rock was formed** | 形成了一块岩石；站稳了 | 与 *sand* 对比：从散到稳 |
| **held my hand** | 握住我的手 | 字面牵手，也象征陪伴与支撑 |
| **gleam that flows with grace** | 带着优雅流淌的光泽 | *gleam* = 微光；写眼神/笑容的美 |
| **spark of nature's fire** | 大自然之火的火花 | 把对方比作生命力的点火点 |
| **complete desire** | 完整的渴望；全部的心意 | *complete* = 圆满、别无他求 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 每段都以 **Sunny, …** 起句，结构整齐，适合**分段跟唱**：先练第一段，再叠第二段的 *thank you for…*。
- *eased the pain / ten feet tall / from A to Z* 是口语高频块，可单独抽出来练连读。
- 藤井风的英文咬字偏软、偏 R&B，注意 *Sunny* 的鼻音收尾和 *I love you* 的轻声落点，别唱得太硬。
- 想延伸练**感恩、雨过天晴、被爱后的自信**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
