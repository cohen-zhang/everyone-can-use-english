## 歌手简介 · About Frank Sinatra

**English**

**Frank Sinatra** (1915–1998) was an American singer and actor, often called **Ol' Blue Eyes** and one of the defining voices of **20th-century popular music**. Rising from the big-band era with Tommy Dorsey, he became a solo superstar in the 1940s–50s with intimate ballads and swinging standards for Columbia and later **Capitol** and **Reprise**. His phrasing—behind the beat, conversational, emotionally precise—shaped how generations sang jazz and pop. Collaborations with arrangers like Nelson Riddle and **Quincy Jones**, and with **Count Basie's** orchestra, produced some of his most celebrated swing recordings.

**中文**

**弗兰克・辛纳特拉**（Frank Sinatra，1915–1998）是美国歌手、演员，常被称作 **Ol' Blue Eyes**，也是**二十世纪流行音乐**的标志性嗓音之一。早年随 Tommy Dorsey 大乐队出道，1940–50 年代以抒情与摇摆乐标准曲成为独唱巨星，先后签约 Columbia、**Capitol**、**Reprise**。他的乐句——略拖在拍后、像说话、情绪准——影响了几代人唱爵士与流行。与 Nelson Riddle、**Quincy Jones** 等编曲家，以及 **Count Basie** 大乐队的合作，留下多首经典摇摆乐录音。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Fly Me to the Moon"** (Bart Howard, 1954) is a cornerstone **jazz standard**. Sinatra's best-known recording is the **1964** version with **Count Basie and His Orchestra**, arranged by **Quincy Jones**, from the album ***It Might as Well Be Swing***. It later appeared on compilations such as ***Nothing But the Best (Remastered)*** (the Apple Music track in this playlist). Where many covers stay soft and dreamy, this take is **brisk swing**: brass punches, Basie's groove, and Sinatra's cool confidence turning *in other words, I love you* into a suave closer. Same lyric as [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-小野丽莎|Lisa Ono's version]]; different temperature.

**中文**

**"Fly Me to the Moon"**（Bart Howard，1954）是爵士标准曲里的硬通货。辛纳特拉最广为人知的录音是 **1964** 年与 **Count Basie 大乐队**合作、由 **Quincy Jones** 编曲的版本，原收于专辑 ***It Might as Well Be Swing***；后亦见于 ***Nothing But the Best (Remastered)*** 等精选（本播放列表的 Apple Music 曲目）。许多翻唱偏柔、偏梦，这一版却是**利落的 swing**：铜管点射、Basie 律动，加上 Sinatra 从容自信，把 *in other words, I love you* 收成一句潇洒的告白。歌词与 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-小野丽莎|小野丽莎版]] 相同，温度完全不同。

#### 重复说明

The Basie cut is short and brisk—often one full pass, or a light repeat of the closing line. Focus less on copying lyrics again, more on **swing accents** and Sinatra’s slightly behind-the-beat phrasing.

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **fly me to the moon** | 带我飞去月球 | 祈使句；浪漫夸张请求 |
| **play among the stars** | 在星星间玩耍 | 轻松、孩子气的画面 |
| **what spring is like** | 春天是什么样 | *what X is like* = X 是怎样的体验 |
| **in other words** | 换句话说 | 把夸张比喻翻译成真实亲密动作 |
| **hold my hand** | 牵着我的手 | 比「飞月球」更落地的请求 |
| **fill my heart with song** | 用歌声填满我的心 | 被爱时想歌唱的心情 |
| **forevermore** | 永远；从此以后 | 歌词书面语；日常说 *forever* |
| **all I long for** | 我全部的渴望 | *long for* = 渴望得到/见到 |
| **worship and adore** | 崇拜又珍爱 | 程度很深；偏歌词修辞 |
| **please be true** | 请对我真心 | *be true* = 忠诚、不劈腿、不敷衍 |
| **I love you** | 我爱你 | 全曲最直白的落点；Sinatra 常唱得很「轻、稳」 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 先听 **铜管与鼓的 swing**：重音常在 2、4 拍；跟唱别按流行歌的「正拍砸字」。
- Sinatra 喜欢 **behind the beat**（略晚于拍点进字），尤其 *in other words* 与 *I love you*——先听他的气口，再模仿。
- 与 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-小野丽莎|小野丽莎版]] 对照听：同一句 *Fly me to the moon*，一个像耳语，一个像举杯——练的是**风格感**，不只是音准。
- 时长约 2:27，适合通勤单曲循环；把 *in other words* 四次说清，这首歌就立住了。
