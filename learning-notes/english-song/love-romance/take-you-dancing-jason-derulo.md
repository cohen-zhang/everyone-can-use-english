## 歌手简介 · About Jason Derulo

**English**

**Jason Derulo** (born 1989 in Miramar, Florida) is an American singer, songwriter and dancer. He first wrote songs for other artists, then broke through in **2009** with *Whatcha Say*. Hits like *In My Head*, *Talk Dirty*, *Want to Want Me* and *Savage Love* made him a steady name in dance-pop, and in 2020 he became hugely popular on **TikTok** with short comedy and dance clips.

**中文**

**Jason Derulo**（1989 年生于美国佛罗里达州 Miramar）是美国歌手、词曲作者与舞者。早年为其他歌手写歌，**2009 年**凭 *Whatcha Say* 出道走红，之后 *In My Head*、*Talk Dirty*、*Want to Want Me*、*Savage Love* 等让他稳居舞曲流行圈；2020 年起又因 **TikTok** 搞笑与舞蹈短视频再度爆红。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Take You Dancing"** was released as a single in **July 2020**. It is a bright, disco-flavored dance-pop track with a catchy *da-da-da* hook. The singer flirts with someone at a party: he knows he maybe *should* run away, but his heart wants to stay, so he asks to *take you dancing*—with a playful double meaning. It spread quickly through TikTok dance videos.

**中文**

**"Take You Dancing"** 于 **2020 年 7 月**作为单曲发行，是一首明快、带迪斯科味道的舞曲流行歌，*da-da-da* 哼唱钩子非常洗脑。歌中主角在派对上撩人：理智说该*跑*，心却想*留*，于是提出 *let me take you dancing*（带你去跳舞）——语带双关。借 TikTok 舞蹈视频迅速传播。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Let me take you dancing** | 让我带你去跳舞 | *take sb + doing*：带某人去做某事；邀约句型 |
| **Run away, run away** | 逃吧，快逃 | 重复制造犹豫感；*run away* 逃开 |
| **I know that I should** | 我知道我应该（走） | 省略式：*should* 后省略 *run away* |
| **my heart wanna stay** | 我的心想留下 | *wanna* = want to；口语，语法上应为 *wants* |
| **You can see it in my eyes** | 你从我眼里就能看出来 | 常用口语：心思写在脸上 |
| **if you know what I mean** | 你懂我的意思吧 | 暗示双关的口头禅 |
| **Anything could happen** | 什么都可能发生 | *could* 表可能性 |
| **Let me see your best move** | 让我看看你最拿手的舞步 | *move* 作名词：舞步；也可指招数 |
| **You're a keeper** | 你值得留住 | 俚语：值得长期交往的人（歌中 *you's a keeper* 更口语） |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 节奏快但副歌简单，先练 *Let me take you dancing* 与 *Two-step to the bedroom* 两句。
- *wanna stay, wanna stay* 连续重复，注意 *wanna* 弱读成 /ˈwɑnə/。
- 歌词里有 *Tequila*、*Louis*、*Prada* 等品牌/酒名，属于派对歌常见炫耀元素，不必逐一记。
- 歌词暧昧（*two-step to the bedroom*），KTV 场合自行掂量。
