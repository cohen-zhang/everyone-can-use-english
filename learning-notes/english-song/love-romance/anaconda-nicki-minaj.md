## 歌手简介 · About Nicki Minaj

**English**

**Nicki Minaj** (born **Onika Tanya Maraj**, 8 December 1982, Saint James, Trinidad and Tobago; raised in Queens, New York) is a Trinidadian-American **rapper**, **singer**, and **songwriter**. She broke through with mixtapes in the late 2000s, then debuted with ***Pink Friday*** (2010). Known for rapid-fire flows, theatrical alter egos, and pop-rap hooks, she became one of the best-selling female rappers of her generation. Later albums include ***Pink Friday: Roman Reloaded*** (2012) and ***The Pinkprint*** (2014). She has scored multiple Billboard Hot 100 hits and is often credited with expanding the mainstream space for women in hip-hop.

**中文**

**Nicki Minaj**（本名 **Onika Tanya Maraj**，1982 年 12 月 8 日生于特立尼达 Saint James，成长于纽约皇后区）是特立尼达裔美国**说唱歌手**、**歌手**与**词曲作者**。2000 年代末靠混音带崭露头角，2010 年以 ***Pink Friday*** 正式出道。以高速说唱节奏流、戏剧化人设与流行说唱钩子句著称，是同代销量最高的女说唱歌手之一。后续专辑包括 ***Pink Friday: Roman Reloaded***（2012）、***The Pinkprint***（2014）。多首单曲打入 Billboard Hot 100，常被视作拓宽主流女说唱空间的关键人物。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Anaconda"** is the second single from ***The Pinkprint*** (2014), released **4 August 2014**. It was produced by **Polow da Don**, **DJ Spydr**, and **Da Internz**, and **prominently samples** Sir Mix-a-Lot's 1992 hit **"Baby Got Back"**—especially the hook *My anaconda don't want none unless you got buns, hun*. Minaj said she wanted a playful, melodic track that **celebrates curvy women** while packing dense sexual innuendo. The song peaked at **No. 2** on the Billboard Hot 100 (her highest solo peak at the time). For English learners: treat it as **slang + rhythm practice**, not as classroom-safe vocabulary.

**中文**

**"Anaconda"** 是 ***The Pinkprint***（2014）第二支单曲，2014 年 8 月 4 日发行。由 **Polow da Don**、**DJ Spydr**、**Da Internz** 制作，并**大量采样** Sir Mix-a-Lot 1992 年热曲 **"Baby Got Back"**——尤其是副歌 *My anaconda don't want none unless you got buns, hun*。Minaj 称想做一首旋律好跟、**赞美曲线身材**、同时塞满性暗示的玩笑歌。单曲最高登上 Billboard Hot 100 **第 2**（当时她个人最高）。对英语学习者：当 **slang + 节奏练习** 用，别当课堂安全词表。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **难度：** 难——语速快、俚语密、双重否定多（*don't want none*、*ain't missing no meals*）；主歌 2 性双关最密。
- **先攻副歌：** *My anaconda don't… unless you got buns, hun* 重复度高，适合练节奏与连读；再听 *Oh my gosh, look at her butt*。
- **采样语境：** 对照 Sir Mix-a-Lot *Baby Got Back*（1992）能听出钩子句从哪来；本笔记不另建该曲文件，听原曲即可。
- **与 WAP：** 同属女说唱脏词版路线。见 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/wap-cardi-b|WAP — Cardi B]]。
