## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Where Have You Been"** comes from Rihanna's sixth album ***Talk That Talk*** (2011) and was released as a single in **2012**. Its opening line *I've been everywhere, man* borrows from the classic song **"I've Been Everywhere"** by **Geoff Mack**, who is credited as a writer. Produced with **Calvin Harris**, it builds into a big EDM drop. The singer has searched *everywhere* for the right person and asks, *Where have you been all my life?* It reached the top 5 of the US Billboard Hot 100 and earned a Grammy nomination.

**中文**

**"Where Have You Been"** 收录于 Rihanna 第六张专辑 ***Talk That Talk***（2011），**2012 年**作为单曲发行。开头 *I've been everywhere, man* 借用了 **Geoff Mack** 的经典老歌 **"I've Been Everywhere"**，Geoff Mack 因此列入词曲作者。与 **Calvin Harris** 合作制作，层层推进到 EDM 高潮。主角*到处*寻找对的人，发问：*Where have you been all my life?*（我这一生你都去哪了？）打进 Billboard Hot 100 前五，并获格莱美提名。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I've been everywhere, man** | 伙计，我哪儿都找遍了 | 借用老歌名句；*man* 口头感叹 |
| **looking for someone** | 寻找某个人 | *look for* 寻找 |
| **Someone who can please me** | 能让我满意的人 | *please* 动词：取悦 |
| **Where have you been all my life?** | 我这一生你都去哪了？ | 常用撩人金句：终于遇到你 |
| **'Cause I never see you out** | 因为我从没在外面见过你 | *out* = 外出社交、夜生活 |
| **Are you hiding from me?** | 你在躲着我吗？ | *hide from* 躲避 |
| **Somewhere in the crowd** | 在人群中某处 | *crowd* 人群 |
| **You can have me all you want** | 你想怎样都行 | *all you want* 尽情 |
| **Any way, any day** | 任何方式，任何一天 | 韵律重复 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌几乎只有 *Where have you been all my life?* 一句，节奏清楚，适合**通勤跟唱**。
- 注意 *have you* 常连读成 /hævjə/，*all my life* 中 *all my* 连读。
- *I've been everywhere, man* 可以对照 Johnny Cash 版 *I've Been Everywhere*，感受同一句在乡村歌与舞曲中的不同。
