## 歌手简介 · About Rihanna

**English**

**Rihanna** (born 1988 in Barbados) is a global pop and R&B star. **JAY-Z** (Shawn Carter, born 1969 in Brooklyn) is a rapper, producer and businessman who signed Rihanna to Def Jam while he was the label's president—so his rap intro on *Umbrella* is also a kind of mentor's blessing.

**中文**

**Rihanna**（1988 年生于巴巴多斯）是全球流行与 R&B 巨星。**JAY-Z**（本名 Shawn Carter，1969 年生于纽约布鲁克林）是说唱歌手、制作人与商人，担任 Def Jam 总裁期间亲自签下 Rihanna——所以他在 *Umbrella* 开头的说唱，也带着「伯乐站台」的意味。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Umbrella"** is the lead single from Rihanna's third album ***Good Girl Gone Bad*** (**2007**). Written by **The-Dream**, **Tricky Stewart**, **Kuk Harrell** and JAY-Z, it was reportedly first offered to Britney Spears. The song uses an umbrella as a symbol of loyal friendship and love: when it rains, *you can stand under my umbrella*. It spent **seven weeks at No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and won the Grammy for **Best Rap/Sung Collaboration**. The *-ella, -ella, eh, eh, eh* hook became iconic.

**中文**

**"Umbrella"** 是 Rihanna 第三张专辑 ***Good Girl Gone Bad***（**2007**）的首支单曲，由 **The-Dream**、**Tricky Stewart**、**Kuk Harrell** 与 JAY-Z 合写，据说最初是给 Britney Spears 的。歌曲以雨伞象征忠诚的友情与爱：下雨时，*你可以站在我的伞下*。在 Billboard Hot 100 **连续 7 周冠军**，获格莱美**最佳说唱/演唱合作**奖；*-ella, -ella, eh, eh, eh* 成为经典记忆点。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You can stand under my umbrella** | 你可以站在我的伞下 | 全曲核心比喻：我来为你遮风挡雨 |
| **When the sun shine, we shine together** | 阳光灿烂时，我们一起闪耀 | 口语省略：标准应为 *shines* |
| **Told you I'll be here forever** | 说过我会永远在这里 | 省略主语 *I*；口语常见 |
| **I'll always be your friend** | 我会永远是你的朋友 | — |
| **Took an oath** | 发过誓 | *take an oath* 宣誓、立誓 |
| **I'ma stick it out to the end** | 我会坚持到底 | *I'ma* = I'm going to；*stick it out* 熬过去、坚持 |
| **we'll never be worlds apart** | 我们永远不会相隔万里 | *worlds apart* 天差地别、相距遥远 |
| **Don't be alarmed** | 别惊慌 | *alarmed* 担心、惊恐 |
| **stack chips for the rainy day** | 为雨天攒钱 | JAY-Z 说唱：*save for a rainy day* 未雨绸缪 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 开头 JAY-Z 的说唱语速快、梗多（*Dow Jones*、*Roc-A-Fella*），可以先跳过，从 Rihanna 主歌开始练。
- 副歌 *umbrella, -ella, -ella, eh, eh, eh* 只需跟节奏，重点练 *You can stand under my umbrella* 的连读。
- *When the sun shine* / *I'ma stick it out* 是口语非标准语法，听懂即可，写作时用 *shines* / *I'm going to*。
- *save for a rainy day*（未雨绸缪）是职场与理财口语高频习语，值得单独记。
