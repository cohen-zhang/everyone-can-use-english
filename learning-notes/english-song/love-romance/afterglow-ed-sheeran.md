## 歌手简介 · About Ed Sheeran

**English**

**Ed Sheeran** (born 1991 in Halifax, England, raised in Suffolk) is a British singer-songwriter famous for loop-pedal live shows and hits like *The A Team*, *Thinking Out Loud*, *Shape of You* and *Perfect*. His albums are named with math symbols: **+**, **×**, **÷**, **=**, **−**.

**中文**

**Ed Sheeran**（1991 年生于英格兰 Halifax，在 Suffolk 长大）是英国创作歌手，以 loop 效果器现场与 *The A Team*、*Thinking Out Loud*、*Shape of You*、*Perfect* 等金曲闻名；专辑用数学符号命名：**+**、**×**、**÷**、**=**、**−**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Afterglow"** was released on **21 December 2020** as a surprise holiday gift to fans during his break, and later added to ***= (Tour Edition)***. It is a quiet acoustic love song set on a winter Saturday morning: light in her hair, coffee in her hand, snow outside, Iron & Wine on the radio. The *afterglow*—the soft light after sunset—stands for love that stays warm even when things go dark.

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 吉他伴奏、语速平缓，是很好的**通勤精听**材料，画面感强的描述句可以直接借用写作。
- *colours* 为英式拼写；*hazel* /ˈheɪzəl/ 淡褐色（眼睛、发色常用）。
- *losing track of time* 在日常与工作中都常用：*I lost track of time debugging.*
- 与 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/perfect-ed-sheeran|Perfect]] 对照，体会 Ed Sheeran 用生活细节写情歌的手法。
