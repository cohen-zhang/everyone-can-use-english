## 歌手简介 · About Old Dominion

**English**

**Old Dominion** is a **country music** band formed in **Nashville** in 2007: **Matthew Ramsey** (lead vocals), **Trevor Rosen** and **Whit Sellers** (both from South Carolina), **Geoff Sprung** (bass), and **Brad Tursi** (guitar). Before the band took off, members wrote hits for other artists—Ramsey co-wrote *"Chainsaw"* for The Band Perry and *"Say You Won't Let Go"* for James Arthur. Their own breakthrough came with *"Break Up with Him"* (2015), followed by the album **Happy Endings** (2017). Old Dominion blends **contemporary country** with **pop hooks** and witty, conversational lyrics; they have won multiple **CMA** and **ACM** awards, including Vocal Group of the Year.

**中文**

**Old Dominion** 是 **2007 年**在 **Nashville** 成立的**乡村乐队**：**Matthew Ramsey**（主唱）、**Trevor Rosen** 与 **Whit Sellers**（均来自南卡）、**Geoff Sprung**（贝斯）、**Brad Tursi**（吉他）。走红前，成员曾为他人写歌——Ramsey 参与 The 乐队 Perry 的 *"Chainsaw"*、James Arthur 的 *"Say You Won't Let Go"* 等。乐队自己的突破是 2015 年 *"Break Up with Him"*，随后发行专辑 **Happy Endings**（2017）。风格融合**当代乡村**与**流行副歌**，歌词机智、口语化；曾获多项 **CMA**、**ACM** 奖，包括「年度声乐组合」。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"One Man Band"** was released on **17 June 2019** as the second single from the band's self-titled third studio album *Old Dominion* (25 October 2019)—the follow-up to *Happy Endings*. Co-written by **Matthew Ramsey**, **Trevor Rosen**, **Brad Tursi**, and frequent collaborator **Josh Osborne**, it is a mid-tempo **country-pop love song** built on a touring-musician metaphor: after years of flying solo, the singer finds someone in the same key and no longer wants to be a **one-man band**—a solo act in love. Ramsey has called it the band's **"first love song"** and noted a double meaning that can also point to his bond with his bandmates. The idea struck just before a show; they sketched the chorus beside the stage speakers, then finished it on the road. It peaked at **No. 20** on the Billboard Hot 100—their highest-charting single at the time.

**中文**

**"One Man Band"** 于 **2019 年 6 月 17 日**发行，是同名第三张录音室专辑 *Old Dominion*（2019 年 10 月 25 日）的第二支单曲——接在 *Happy Endings* 之后。由 **Matthew Ramsey**、**Trevor Rosen**、**Brad Tursi** 与常合作词人 **Josh Osborne** 合写。这是一首中速的**乡村流行情歌**，用巡演乐手的意象打比方：独自飞了太久，终于遇见同调的人，不想再当恋爱里的**一人乐队**。Ramsey 称这是乐队的**「第一首情歌」**，并说歌词也有双关，可指向与队友的羁绊。灵感在上台前突然冒出来，他们在舞台音箱旁先哼出副歌，后来在路上写完。单曲登上 Billboard Hot 100 **第 20**，当时是乐队最高排位。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **flying solo** | 单飞；独自行动 | 航空/乐队双关；口语也指一个人干 |
| **singing a harmony** | 唱和声 | 引申：有人配合、不孤单 |
| **No base / no bass** | 没有贝斯 | 歌词常写作 *base*；音乐语境实为 **bass**（低音吉他） |
| **in the same key** | 同一个调；合拍 | 音乐术语转恋爱：价值观/节奏合得来 |
| **one man band** | 一人乐队 | 核心隐喻：恋爱里不想单打独斗 |
| **a rolling stone alone** | 一个人漂泊不定 | 呼应习语 *a rolling stone gathers no moss*；此处强调孤独漂泊 |
| **putting miles on** | 把里程数往上堆 | 指车/人一路奔波 |
| **run-down van** | 破旧的面包车 | *run-down* = 年久失修 |
| **take our own show on the road** | 把咱们的演出带上路 | 巡演用语；引申：一起开启共同生活 |
| **lay down the beat** | 打下鼓点/节奏底 | 制作/乐队口语 |
| **carry the tune** | 扛住主旋律 | *carry* = 撑起、负责 |
| **trash hotel rooms** | 把酒店房间闹翻天 | 摇滚刻板印象；此处是玩笑式「一起疯」 |
| **run down your wild dreams** | 追着你的狂梦跑 | *run down* 此处偏「追赶、落实」 |
| **chase every high** | 追每一次兴奋的高峰 | *high* = 兴奋峰值，非仅毒品义 |
| **sing the blues** | 唱布鲁斯；陷入伤感 | 习语：抱怨、难过；*never learn how to…* = 永远不学那一套 |
| **our own little groove** | 咱们自己那点小节奏 | *groove* = 律动；引申为默契相处方式 |
| **the spotlight goes cold** | 聚光灯冷下来 | 名气/热闹退去 |
| **curtains come down** | 幕布落下；演出结束 | 舞台收场意象 |
| **nobody's paying, we'll still be playing** | 没人买单，咱们也还在弹 | Bridge 金句：热闹散尽仍在一起 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I don't wanna be a one man band* 重复强、旋律顺，**KTV / 跟唱**友好；注意 *wanna*、*'Cause* 的口语连读。
- 全曲用**乐队/巡演意象**讲恋爱：*harmony / same key / lay down the beat / carry the tune*——练「音乐词转生活隐喻」很合适。
- *No base* 听感接近 *bass*；跟唱时按歌词文本即可，理解时记成「没贝斯」。
- 桥段三行画面感强（灯灭、椅子叠好、没人买单仍在弹），可单独练慢速跟读，体会 *goes cold / come down / stacked / paying / playing* 的押韵与节奏。
- 想延伸练**「想一起、不想单飞」**类恋爱口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]；同乐队另一首态度歌见 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/no-such-thing-as-a-broken-heart-old-dominion|No Such Thing as a Broken Heart]]。
