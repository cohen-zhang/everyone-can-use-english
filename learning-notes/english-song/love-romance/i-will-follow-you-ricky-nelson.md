## 歌手简介 · About Ricky Nelson

**English**

**Ricky Nelson** (1940–1985), born **Eric Hilliard Nelson**, was an American **singer, musician, and actor**. He grew up on his parents' hit TV show *The Adventures of Ozzie and Harriet*, where America watched him become a **teen idol**. From the late 1950s he scored rock-and-roll hits like *Hello Mary Lou* and *Travelin' Man*, then moved into **country-rock** and **ballads** in the 1960s. He was inducted into the **Rock and Roll Hall of Fame** (1987, posthumously). Nelson's clear, relaxed vocal style made his love songs easy to sing along to—ideal for learners practising classic American pop.

**中文**

**Ricky Nelson**（1940–1985），本名 **Eric Hilliard Nelson**，是美国**歌手、乐手与演员**。他在父母主演的电视节目 *The Adventures of Ozzie and Harriet* 中长大，全美见证他成为**青少年偶像**。1950 年代末起凭 *Hello Mary Lou*、*Travelin' Man* 等摇滚热曲走红，1960 年代转向**乡村摇滚**与**抒情民谣**。身后入选**摇滚名人堂**（1987）。嗓音清澈、放松，情歌极易跟唱，适合学习者练经典美式流行。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Will Follow You"** was released in **1963** on the album *For Your Sweet Love*. Written by **Gerald Goldstein** and **Richard Gottehrer**, it is a **devotional pop ballad**: the singer vows to follow his love anywhere—no ocean too deep, no mountain too high. The repeated lines *I love you* and *You'll always be my true love* make it a favourite for **slow dances** and easy **KTV** practice. The melody is straightforward, with clear syllable stress on each phrase.

**中文**

**"I Will Follow You"** 于 **1963** 年发行，收录于 *For Your Sweet Love*。由 **Gerald Goldstein** 与 **Richard Gottehrer** 创作，是一首**深情流行民谣**：歌者发誓无论爱人去哪都跟定——海再深、山再高也拦不住。*I love you* 与 *You'll always be my true love* 反复出现，适合**慢舞**与 **KTV** 入门跟唱。旋律直白，每句重音清楚。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **follow you wherever you may go** | 无论你走到哪里我都跟 | 经典誓言句型；*may* 表「可能」 |
| **there isn't an ocean too deep** | 没有海深得能（拦住我） | 否定 + too…to… 结构变体 |
| **keep me away** | 把我隔开；阻止我靠近 | *keep sb away (from)* |
| **ever since** | 自从 | *Ever since you touched my hand* |
| **destiny** | 命运；命中注定 | *You are my destiny* 情歌高频 |
| **true love** | 真爱 | 名词；比 *real love* 更浪漫 |
| **from now until forever** | 从现在直到永远 | 正式感略强于 *forever and ever* |
| **nothing can keep you from me** | 没什么能把你从我这里夺走 | 强调执着与占有欲 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 整首歌 **词汇简单、句式重复**，适合初学者练发音与节奏；建议先跟副歌 *I love you / I'll follow*。
- 注意 *follow you* 的连读：*fol-low-you* 中间辅音轻读。
- *destiny / forever* 等词尾 **-y / -er** 可练美式非卷舌或轻卷舌（ Nelson 偏经典美式流行腔）。
- 副歌重复多遍，适合 **KTV 暖场**；也可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的告白句型。
