## 歌手简介 · About 手嶌葵 / Aoi Teshima

**English**

**手嶌葵** (**Aoi Teshima**, born 21 June 1987, Yokohama / raised in Fukuoka, Japan) is a Japanese **singer** known for a clear, gentle voice and cinematic covers. She first drew wide attention singing for Studio Ghibli–related and film projects, then released albums of film and classic songs—including ***The Rose ~I Love Cinemas~*** (2008). Her English ballads are prized for soft diction and emotional restraint: ideal listening for learners who want **slow, clear vowels** without shouting pop energy.

**中文**

**手嶌葵**（**Aoi Teshima**，1987 年 6 月 21 日生，横滨出生、福冈长大）是日本**歌手**，以清澈、温柔的声线与影视向翻唱著称。早年为吉卜力相关及电影项目献声，后发行多张电影金曲与经典翻唱专辑——包括 ***The Rose ~I Love Cinemas~***（2008）。她的英文抒情曲咬字软、情绪克制：适合想练**慢速、清晰元音**、又不想跟喊麦流行歌的学习者。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"The Rose"** was written by **Amanda McBroom** and made famous by **Bette Midler** on the 1979 film soundtrack *The Rose*. It is a quiet classic about what love *is*—and what it is *not*: some say love is a river, a razor, or a hunger; the singer answers that love is a **flower**, and you, the lonely one who thinks love has passed you by, are the **seed**. **手嶌葵**'s 2008 cover on ***The Rose ~I Love Cinemas~*** keeps the standard English lyric in a soft, cinematic arrangement. (Note: some lyric DBs mis-tag this track with *"Alfie"*; the learning text below uses the standard **"The Rose"** lyric.)

**中文**

**"The Rose"** 由 **Amanda McBroom** 创作，因 **Bette Midler** 演唱并收录于 1979 年电影 *The Rose* 原声而广为人知。这是一首安静的经典，写爱**是什么**、**不是什么**：有人说爱是河、是刀刃、是饥饿；歌者回答——爱是一朵**花**，而那个以为爱已与自己无缘的孤独者，其实是那颗**种子**。**手嶌葵** 2008 年在 ***The Rose ~I Love Cinemas~*** 中的翻唱保留标准英文歌词，编曲柔和、电影感强。（注：部分歌词库误标为 *"Alfie"*；下方学习文本采用标准 **"The Rose"** 歌词。）

And the road has been too long
And you think that love is only

Far beneath the bitter snow

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Some say… / I say…** | 有人说… / 我说… | 对比观点的经典句式 |
| **drown the tender reed** | 淹没柔弱的芦苇 | *tender* = 柔嫩、脆弱 |
| **leaves your soul to bleed** | 让灵魂流血 | *leave … to* = 使处于某种状态 |
| **an endless aching need** | 没完没了的隐痛与渴求 | *aching* 既可身体也可情感 |
| **you, its only seed** | 你是它唯一的种子 | 全曲核心隐喻 |
| **afraid of breaking** | 怕碎 / 怕受伤 | *heart … breaking* |
| **never learns to dance** | 永远学不会跳舞 | 隐喻：不敢投入生活 |
| **takes the chance** | 抓住机会；敢试一次 | = take a chance |
| **won't be taken** | 不愿被带走 / 不愿交出自己 | 此处偏「不愿被爱带走」 |
| **the lucky and the strong** | 幸运者与强者 | 对比后文「种子也能开花」 |
| **far beneath the bitter snow** | 在苦寒的雪下很深的地方 | *bitter* = 严酷的 |
| **with the sun's love** | 借着阳光的爱 | 拟人：阳光像爱 |
| **becomes the rose** | 开成玫瑰 | 结局意象；慢唱咬清 *becomes* |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 三段结构清晰：**否定隐喻 → 恐惧清单 → 冬雪下的种子**；跟唱时按段记，比硬背整首轻松。
- 语速慢、元音长，适合练 *river / razor / hunger / flower* 的清晰开口。
- *Some say… I say…* 是很好的口语辩论骨架，可迁移到日常表达观点。
- 注意 *seed / reed / need* 等长元音押韵；末句 *becomes the rose* 轻轻落地即可。
- 想延伸「爱与勇气」相关表达，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
