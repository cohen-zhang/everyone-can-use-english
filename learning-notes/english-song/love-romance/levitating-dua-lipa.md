## 歌手简介 · About Dua Lipa

**English**

**Dua Lipa** (born 22 August 1995, London) is a British–Albanian **singer and songwriter**. She broke through with her self-titled debut (2017) and *"New Rules"*, then dominated the early 2020s with ***Future Nostalgia*** (2020)—a sleek **disco-pop** album that won the **Grammy for Best Pop Vocal Album**. Known for sharp hooks, dance-floor confidence, and retro-futurist production, she has become one of the defining pop voices of her generation. Collaborations and remixes (including a popular **DaBaby** feature on *"Levitating"*) pushed her further into global chart territory.

**中文**

**Dua Lipa**（1995 年 8 月 22 日生于伦敦）是英裔阿尔巴尼亚**歌手、词曲作者**。以同名首专（2017）和 *"New Rules"* 出圈，再以 ***Future Nostalgia***（2020）统治 2020 年代初——这张利落的 **disco-pop** 专辑拿下**格莱美最佳流行演唱专辑**。以抓耳钩子句、舞池自信与复古未来感制作著称，是同代流行乐的代表声线之一。含 **DaBaby** 的 *"Levitating"* 混音版等合作进一步推高全球榜单表现。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Levitating"** is a standout from ***Future Nostalgia*** (27 March 2020), co-written by Dua Lipa with **Clarence Coffee Jr.**, **Sarah Hudson**, and **Stephen Kozmeniuk**. It is buoyant **nu-disco / dance-pop**: glitter, galaxies, and the feeling of floating when chemistry clicks—*I'm levitating*. The chorus pairs *moonlight / starlight* with *come on, dance with me*; verses invite *run away* to a *galaxy* and get *lost on Mars*. A later remix with **DaBaby** became a massive US radio hit. Perfect commute and KTV practice—clear vowels, repeating hooks, and playful space metaphors.

**中文**

**"Levitating"** 是 ***Future Nostalgia***（2020 年 3 月 27 日）的亮点曲，由 Dua Lipa 与 **Clarence Coffee Jr.**、**Sarah Hudson**、**Stephen Kozmeniuk** 合写。轻快的 **nu-disco / 舞曲流行**：亮片、星系、来电时飘起来的感觉——*I'm levitating*。副歌把 *moonlight / starlight* 和 *come on, dance with me* 绑在一起；主歌邀请一起 *run away* 去 *galaxy*、在火星上 *get lost*。后来与 **DaBaby** 的混音版成为美国电台大热。通勤与 KTV 都很友好——元音清楚、钩子句重复、太空隐喻好玩好记。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **run away with me** | 跟我私奔 / 一起逃走 | 情歌常见邀请，不一定真逃 |
| **take you for a ride** | 带你兜风 | 也可双关「耍你」；此处偏字面好玩 |
| **premonition** | 预感 | 稍正式；= feeling something will happen |
| **fell into a rhythm** | 掉进同一种节奏 | 默契、合拍 |
| **the music don't stop** | 音乐不停 | 非标准（*doesn't*）；歌词口语 |
| **a little bit of company** | 一点陪伴 | *company* = 有人陪，非公司 |
| **sugarboo** | 小甜心 | 自造亲昵称呼；*boo* = 宝贝 |
| **levitating** | 飘起来；悬浮 | 字面漂浮；此处=开心到飘 |
| **Milky Way** | 银河 | 专有名词；注意重音 |
| **renegading** | 当叛逆者 / 离经叛道 | 由 *renegade* 活用为动词 |
| **moonlight / starlight** | 月光 / 星光 | 把对方比作光 |
| **written in the stars** | 写在星星上；命中注定 | 习语：注定如此 |
| **now or never** | 机不可失；现在不做就永远不做 | 催促行动 |
| **get lost on Mars** | 在火星上迷路 | 玩乐式「一起消失」 |
| **blast off** | （火箭）点火升空 | 航天用语；此处比喻爱爆发 |
| **dance my arse off** | 跳到腿软 / 疯了似的跳 | 英式 *arse*；美式常说 *ass*；口语夸张 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I'm levitating* + *moonlight / starlight* 极适合通勤跟唱；注意 *levitating* 四音节节奏。
- *sugarboo*、*come on, dance with me* 练亲昵邀请语气，别咬太死板。
- *music don't stop*、*dance my arse off* 是口语/英式色彩，学习时认意思即可。
- 桥段的 *blast off / electric* 可当「能量词」小清单，派对英语很实用。
- 想延伸甜蜜称呼，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
