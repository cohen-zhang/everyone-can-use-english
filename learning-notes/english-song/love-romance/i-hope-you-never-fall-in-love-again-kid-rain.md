## 歌手简介 · About KiD RAiN

**English**

**KiD RAiN** (born **Dan Reynard**, 5 July 1996) is a British **singer-songwriter and producer** from **Harrogate**, North Yorkshire. He started on cornet at six, guitar at eight, and taught himself piano as a teenager before building a home studio. Signed to **Peermusic UK** in 2021, he broke through with **"Accidentally in Love"** (10 million+ streams, BBC Introducing Record of the Week). His sound blends **pop** and **indie pop** with shout-along hooks and confessional lyrics. KiD RAiN is known for inclusive storytelling—he released **"I Hope You Never Fall in Love Again"** in three versions (ex-girlfriend, ex-boyfriend, and they/them) after fans helped shape the concept.

**中文**

**KiD RAiN**（本名 **Dan Reynard**，1996 年 7 月 5 日生）是英国**创作型歌手兼制作人**，来自北约克郡 **Harrogate**。六岁学短号、八岁学吉他，少年时自学钢琴并搭建家庭录音室。2021 年签约 **Peermusic UK**；单曲 **"Accidentally in Love"** 播放量超千万，获 BBC Introducing 本周推荐。风格融合 **流行** 与 **独立流行**，副歌上口、歌词直白。他以**包容性叙事**著称——在粉丝参与下，将 **"I Hope You Never Fall in Love Again"** 推出前女友、前男友与 they/them 三个版本。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Hope You Never Fall in Love Again"** was released on **16 March 2022** as the title track of an EP. KiD RAiN calls it an **inclusive FU to an ex**: a bitter, funny pop stomper wishing your ex endless bad dates, weirdos, and friend-zone misery. The second verse skewers modern dating—**swipe right**, the **talking stage**, guys twice your age—and lands on a cathartic *one, two, Fuck you!* The song went viral on TikTok and became a sing-scream anthem for anyone who wanted to say what they couldn't in a breakup text.

**中文**

**"I Hope You Never Fall in Love Again"** 于 **2022 年 3 月 16 日**发行，为同名迷你专辑主打。KiD RAiN 称其为**对前任的包容性「去你的」**：一首又苦又好笑的流行踩点劲歌，祝前任约会不断翻车、遇见怪人、永远困在**朋友区**。第二段嘲讽当代约会——**右滑配对**、**暧昧期**、年纪大你一倍却赚得少的人——最后以 *one, two, Fuck you!* 宣泄。歌曲在 TikTok 爆红，成为许多人想对前任吼出口却说不出来的**跟唱发泄曲**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **fall in love again** | 再谈恋爱 | *I hope you never…* 整句是报复式诅咒 |
| **weirdo** | 怪人；怪胎 | 口语贬义，歌里祝对方遇人不淑 |
| **friend zone** | 朋友区 | 只想当朋友、不给恋爱机会；*make it out of* = 摆脱 |
| **swipe right** | （约会 App）右滑喜欢 | Tinder 等 App 手势；*swipe left* = 跳过 |
| **the talking stage** | 暧昧期；只聊不确认关系 | Gen Z 约会俚语 |
| **turn up late** | 迟到出现 | 英式也常说 *turn up* = 到场 |
| **mummy's estate** | 妈妈家（住宅区） | 英式 *mummy*；暗示还住在家里、不成熟 |
| **go downhill** | 走下坡路；越来越糟 | 约会或关系恶化 |
| **want to split** | 想 AA；想分开付账 | *split the bill* 的口语缩略 |
| **forget your name** | 记不住你名字 | 讽刺对方不够在乎 |
| **thinkin' bout** | 想着（about 缩略） | 口语连读：*thinkin' about* |
| **throw away** | 扔掉；糟蹋 | *everything we had that you threw away* |
| **loser** | 失败者；废柴 | 副歌变体；比 *weirdo* 更直接贬义 |
| **chose her** | 选了闺蜜 | *they chose her* = 约会对象反而看上闺蜜 |
| **fucking arsehole** | 该死的混蛋 | 英式粗口；第二段情绪爆发点 |
| **getting ready for a date** | 打扮去约会 | *spend six hours straight* 强调精心准备 |
| **nice eyes** | 眼睛好看 | 约会初期常见夸赞；歌里讽刺「表面完美」 |
| **one, two, Fuck you!** | 一、二，去你的！ | 结尾倒数式宣泄；跟唱时可单独练 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌节奏干脆，适合**跟唱**练重音：每句 *I hope you…* 落在 hope 和动词上。
- 第二段 *(It's Sarah, you fucking arsehole)* 是整首歌的**情绪爆发点**，可先单独练这句再串全曲。
- 歌里大量**当代约会俚语**（*swipe right*, *talking stage*, *friend zone*），可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的恋爱场景表达。
