## 歌手简介 · About Rascal Flatts

**English**

**Rascal Flatts** is an American **country music** trio formed in **1999** in Nashville: **Gary LeVox** (lead vocals), **Jay DeMarcus** (bass, keyboards, vocals), and **Joe Don Rooney** (guitar, vocals). Their **self-titled debut** (2000) blended country with pop-rock production and harmony-heavy choruses. They became one of the **best-selling country acts** of the 2000s with hits like *"Bless the Broken Road"*, *"What Hurts the Most"*, and *"Life is a Highway"* (from *Cars*). Known for **emotional ballads** and polished arena-country sound, the group announced their farewell tour in 2021 and disbanded after decades together.

**中文**

**Rascal Flatts** 是 **1999 年**在 Nashville 成立的美国**乡村三重奏**：**Gary LeVox**（主唱）、**Jay DeMarcus**（贝斯、键盘、和声）、**Joe Don Rooney**（吉他、和声）。**同名首张专辑**（2000）把乡村与流行摇滚制作、厚重和声副歌结合。2000 年代他们是**最畅销的乡村组合**之一，代表作有 *"Bless the Broken Road"*、*"What Hurts the Most"*、*"Life is a Highway"*（《汽车总动员》插曲）。以**抒情慢歌**和精致的舞台乡村音色著称；2021 年宣布告别巡演，数十年后解散。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"These Days"** was released in **June 2002** as the second single from their album *Melt*. Written by **Jeffrey Steele**, **Steve Robson**, and **Wayne Hector**, it tells a **chance reunion** story: the narrator bumps into an ex-girlfriend years after college, makes small talk about her new life in Vegas, and realizes he never moved on. The chorus—*Punch the clock, head for home, check the phone … that's what I'm doin' these days*—captures a lonely routine of work, hope, and dreaming of someone who left. It was Rascal Flatts' **first No. 1** on the Billboard Hot Country Songs chart and helped establish their ballad identity.

**中文**

**"These Days"** 于 **2002 年 6 月**发行，为专辑 *Melt* 第二支单曲。由 **Jeffrey Steele**、**Steve Robson**、**Wayne Hector** 创作，讲一次**偶遇**：叙述者多年后在街上碰见大学时代的旧爱，寒暄她嫁到 Vegas 的新生活，才发觉自己从未走出来。副歌 *Punch the clock, head for home, check the phone … that's what I'm doin' these days* 写的是一种孤独日常：上班、回家、查手机、睡前还想她。这是 Rascal Flatts 在 Billboard Hot Country 歌曲的**首支冠军单曲**，也奠定了他们抒情慢歌的路线。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 故事是**偶遇前任**的三段式叙事：寒暄 → 各自近况 → 道别；跟唱前先理清时间线，情感更到位。
- *Norma Jean* 指 **Shooter Jennings** 的歌（2001），不是 Marilyn Monroe 本名；歌里当作两人当年的「我们的歌」。
- 副歌 *Punch the clock … these days* 六行节奏稳定，适合**通勤跟唱**；尾声重复时可专注 *teardrops fall down like rain* 的连读。
- 想练**旧情难忘**口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里类似场景句型。
