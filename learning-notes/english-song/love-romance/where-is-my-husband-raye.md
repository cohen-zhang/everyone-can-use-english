## 歌手简介 · About RAYE

**English**

**RAYE** (born **Rachel Agatha Keen**, 1997) is a British **singer-songwriter** from **South London** (Tooting / Croydon). Raised in a musical household, she attended the BRIT School briefly and built a dual career: writing for others (credits include Beyoncé, Charli XCX, Little Mix) while releasing her own R&B-tinged pop. After a public fight to leave a major-label deal that stalled her album, she went independent and dropped *My 21st Century Blues* (2023)—a breakthrough that won her the **Mercury Prize** and a stack of BRITs. Known for sharp storytelling, gospel-soul belts, and fearless honesty, she writes and often co-produces her own hits.

**中文**

**RAYE**（本名 **Rachel Agatha Keen**，1997）是来自**南伦敦**（Tooting / Croydon）的英国**唱作人**。在音乐家庭长大，短暂就读 BRIT School，一边为别人写歌（合作过 Beyoncé、Charli XCX、Little Mix 等），一边发自己的 R&B 风流行。曾公开抗争被大厂搁置的专辑合约，独立后推出 *My 21st Century Blues*（2023）一举突破，拿下 **Mercury Prize** 与多项 BRIT。以锋利叙事、福音灵魂式高音和毫不掩饰的坦白著称，作品多自写并常参与制作。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"WHERE IS MY HUSBAND!"** (stylised in all caps) dropped **19 September 2025** as the lead single toward RAYE's second album *This Music May Contain Hope* (2026). Co-written and co-produced with frequent collaborator **Mike Sabath**, it blends **R&B, big-band brass, pop drama, and gospel** into a theatrical plea: she's ready for her husband—*where the hell is he?* Premiered live at **Glastonbury 2025** with a brass band, it became a festival earworm and later a UK chart smash. The lyric swings between comedy and ache—reviewing "applications," lonely acrobatics at 2 a.m., praying for a diamond ring—while the chorus stays shout-along simple.

**中文**

**"WHERE IS MY HUSBAND!"**（全大写）于 **2025 年 9 月 19 日**发行，是第二专 *This Music May Contain Hope*（2026）的主打单曲。与长期搭档 **Mike Sabath** 合写合制，把 **R&B、大乐队铜管、流行戏剧感与福音**搅在一起：她已经准备好遇见丈夫——*他到底在哪？* **2025 格拉斯顿伯里**首演就带铜管，成了音乐节洗脑曲，后来冲上英国榜。歌词在好笑与心酸之间晃——「审简历」、凌晨两点解裙子、求一枚钻戒——副歌却简单到能跟着喊。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **where the hell is…?** | ……到底在哪？ | *the hell* 加强语气；口语急躁/戏谑 |
| **what is taking him so long** | 他怎么这么久 | *take so long* = 花太长时间 |
| **getting down with** | 跟……玩得起劲；亲热 | 俚语；语境可偏暧昧 |
| **he should holler** | 他该喊一声/联系我 | *holler* = 大声叫；美式口语「打招呼」 |
| **testing my patience** | 考验我的耐心 | 日常抱怨高频句 |
| **that ain't me** | 不是我 | *ain't* = isn't；非标准但流行歌常见 |
| **reviewing applications** | 审简历/申请 | 此处幽默：把追求者当求职者 |
| **get my hands on him** | 逮到他 | *get one's hands on* = 抓住、弄到手 |
| **tell him off** | 训他一顿 | 英式口语；= scold |
| **I'ma** | 我要（I'm gonna） | 口语缩略；跟唱时别读成 I'm a |
| **heart yearns for** | 心渴望…… | *yearn for* 偏书面/深情 |
| **lonely acrobatics** | 孤独的杂技 | 自造意象：独自折腾的夜晚 |
| **fix up his tie** | 打好领带；收拾妥当 | *fix up* = 整理好 |
| **this where your wife is** | 你老婆在这儿呢 | 口语省略 *is*（*This is where…*） |
| **turn it up** | 把（音量/火力）开大 | 双关：爱也要加码 |
| **'bout to love ya** | 马上要爱你 | *'bout to* = about to；*ya* = you |
| **no one above ya** | 没人能排你上面 | *above* = 地位更高 |
| **I intend to rush ya** | 我打算催你一把 | *rush* = 催促 |
| **die alone** | 孤独终老 | 夸张恐惧；口语常见 |
| **hurry up here, sir** | 快点过来，先生 | 礼貌称呼 + 急切命令，反差好笑 |
| **wedding finger** | 无名指（戴婚戒的手指） | 通常指左手无名指 |
| **wave around** | 晃给别人看 | 炫耀动作 |
| **until death, I do** | 至死不渝，我愿意 | 婚礼誓词 *I do* 的戏仿 |
| **is he about it?** | 他上不上心？认不认真？ | *about it* = 投入、当真 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *where the hell is my husband?* 短、冲、好喊——**KTV / 通勤**先拿下这句；*hell* 咬清楚但不需真生气，戏谑感更对。
- 注意英式口音里 *husband*、*holler*、*tell him off* 的节奏；跟唱时抓住铜管空隙换气。
- 主歌的幽默点在于把找对象说成 *reviewing applications*——可练「一本正经说荒诞事」的语气。
- 桥段 *I would like a ring* 像许愿清单，重复多，适合练 **would like** 的礼貌请求语感。
- *I'ma / 'bout to / ain't / ya* 全是口语缩略；学意思即可，正式写作改回完整形式。
