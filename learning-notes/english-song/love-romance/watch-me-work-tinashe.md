## 歌手简介 · About Tinashe

**English**

**Tinashe** (Tinashe Jorgensen Kachingwe, born 1993 in Lexington, Kentucky, raised in Los Angeles) is an American singer, songwriter and dancer. After child acting and a girl group, she released self-produced mixtapes and broke through in **2014** with *2 On* from her debut album ***Aquarius***. Her later hit *Nasty* (2024) went viral worldwide.

**中文**

**Tinashe**（本名 Tinashe Jorgensen Kachingwe，1993 年生于美国肯塔基州 Lexington，在洛杉矶长大）是美国歌手、词曲作者与舞者。童星与女团经历之后，她自制混音带积累人气，**2014 年**凭首张专辑 ***Aquarius*** 中的 *2 On* 走红；2024 年 *Nasty* 再度全球爆红。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Watch Me Work"** was released as a single in **October 2014**, around the launch of *Aquarius*. It is a moody, slow-burning R&B track: the singer is confident she can make someone fall in love—*just sit back and watch me work*. Apple Music marks it as **explicit**.

**中文**

**"Watch Me Work"** 于 **2014 年 10 月**作为单曲发行，前后正值 *Aquarius* 问世。这是一首氛围感强、慢热的 R&B：主角自信能让对方爱上自己——*just sit back and watch me work*（你坐好，看我施展）。Apple Music 标注为 **explicit**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Just sit back and watch me work** | 你只管坐好，看我施展 | *sit back* 放松旁观；*watch me work* 看我出手 |
| **Someone I can call the one** | 我可以称为真命天子的人 | *the one* 命中注定的那个人 |
| **I'm a make you fall in love** | 我要让你爱上我 | *I'm a* = I'm gonna；*make sb do* |
| **Tell me that you want it** | 告诉我你想要 | — |
| **Only if you mean it** | 只要你是认真的 | *mean it* 是真心的；高频口语 |
| **You're taking too long** | 你太磨蹭了 | *take too long* 花太久 |
| **The night is young** | 夜还长着呢 | 习语：时间还早，还能继续玩 |
| **I'm ready now** | 我现在准备好了 | — |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 旋律慢、气声多，重点练 *watch me work, work, work* 的节奏与 /w/ 的圆唇。
- 歌词暧昧并含脏词（lrclib 版已打码），不适合亲子场合。
- *Only if you mean it* 与 *The night is young* 是日常口语里很好用的短句。
