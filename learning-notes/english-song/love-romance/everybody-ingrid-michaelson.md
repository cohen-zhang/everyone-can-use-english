## 歌手简介 · About Ingrid Michaelson

**English**

**Ingrid Michaelson** is an American **indie pop** singer-songwriter from **Staten Island, New York**, known for warm vocals, ukulele/piano-led arrangements, and lyrics that feel like diary pages. She built an audience online in the mid-2000s; *"The Way I Am"* and placements on TV shows (notably *Grey's Anatomy*) brought her mainstream attention. Albums like *Girls and Boys* (2007), *Be OK* (2008), and *Everybody* (2009) cemented her as a voice of gentle, literate pop—catchy without being glossy, emotional without melodrama. She writes most of her own material and often co-produces.

**中文**

**Ingrid Michaelson** 是来自**纽约 Staten Island** 的美国**独立流行**唱作人，以温暖嗓音、尤克里里/钢琴编曲，以及像日记一样的歌词著称。2000 年代中期靠网络积累听众；*"The Way I Am"* 与电视剧（尤其 *Grey's Anatomy*）插曲让她进入主流。*Girls and Boys*（2007）、*Be OK*（2008）、*Everybody*（2009）等专辑确立了她温和、有文采的流行路线——好听但不浮夸，动情但不煽情。作品多自写，也常参与制作。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Everybody"** is track 2 on Ingrid's album *Everybody*, released **25 August 2009** on Cabin 24. She wrote it herself; the album's stated theme is simple and universal: **everybody wants to be loved**. The song is an **indie-pop anthem** about loneliness and connection—falling down, struggling to get life right, and admitting that what the heart needs is *love, love, love*. The chorus is built for sing-alongs; the bridge lists what everybody does with love (knows / holds / feels / steals / heals). Soft, hopeful, and easy to learn—ideal commute and pronunciation practice.

**中文**

**"Everybody"** 是 Ingrid 专辑 *Everybody*（**2009 年 8 月 25 日**，Cabin 24）第 2 轨，由她本人创作。整张专辑的主题她说得很直白：**每个人都想被爱**。这首**独立流行 anthem** 写孤独与连接——又跌倒了、这世界很难做对、心里真正需要的是 *love, love, love*。副歌适合合唱；桥段罗列 everybody 对爱做的事（知道/握住/感受/偷走/疗愈）。温柔、有希望、好学——很适合通勤跟唱和发音练习。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **fallen down again** | 又跌倒了 | 字面/比喻皆可：失败、情绪低落 |
| **hard to get it right** | 很难做对；很难过好 | 口语极常用；*get it right* = 做对 |
| **fit like a glove** | 严丝合缝；刚刚好 | 习语：完全合适 |
| **what it needs is…** | 它真正需要的是…… | 强调句型；突出真正需求 |
| **everybody wants to be loved** | 每个人都想被爱 | 歌的核心；被动 *be loved* |
| **happy is the heart that…** | ……的心才是幸福的 | 倒装，偏诗意；= The heart that… is happy |
| **still feels pain** | 还能感到痛 | 痛 = 还活着、还在感受 |
| **darkness drains** | 黑暗退去/流尽 | *drain* = 排空；此处表消散 |
| **light will come again** | 光会再来 | 希望句；可背作鼓励语 |
| **swing open (up) your chest** | 敞开心胸 | *swing open* = 猛地打开 |
| **let it in** | 让它进来 | 接受情绪/爱 |
| **falls for love** | 为爱沦陷；爱上 | *fall for* = 爱上某人/某事 |
| **heals with love** | 靠爱疗愈 | *heal with* = 用……治愈 |
| **let the love begin** | 让爱开始吧 | 邀请式祈使句 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Everybody, everybody wants to…* 节奏规整、词少，**通勤跟唱**友好；注意 *everybody* 重音在第一音节。
- *wants to love* vs *wants to be loved*——主动爱与被爱，练听 **to be** 的弱读。
- 桥段一串动词（*knows / holds / falls / feels / steals / heals*）适合当**动词闪卡**跟唱。
- *fit like a glove* 是地道习语，可记进口语：*This job fits me like a glove.*
- 想延伸「孤独 / 被理解」表达，对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
