## 歌手简介 · About Lola Amour

**English**

**Lola Amour** is a **Filipino indie pop / soul band** formed in **2017** in **Las Piñas**, Metro Manila. The lineup—**Paco Guerrero** (vocals, guitar), **Miguel Giangan** (bass), **Nico Angeles** (guitar), **Kenneth Wayne Zacarias** (drums), and **Jox Salazar** (keyboards)—blends **jazz, funk, bossa nova, and bedroom pop** with bilingual lyrics (English and Tagalog). They broke through with **"Fallen"** and **"Pahinga"** during the pandemic era, building a loyal following through live streams and intimate gigs. The band name nods to the **Kinks** song *"Lola"* and the French word **amour** (love). Their sound is often described as **"sophisticated pop"**—warm grooves, clever hooks, and emotionally self-aware lyrics about crushes, hesitation, and modern romance.

**中文**

**Lola Amour** 是 **2017 年**成立于马尼拉大都会 **Las Piñas** 的菲律宾**独立流行／灵魂乐团**。阵容包括 **Paco Guerrero**（主唱、吉他）、**Miguel Giangan**（贝斯）、**Nico Angeles**（吉他）、**Kenneth Wayne Zacarias**（鼓）、**Jox Salazar**（键盘），风格融合**爵士、放克、波萨诺瓦与卧室流行**，歌词常中英／他加禄语夹杂。疫情期间凭 **"Fallen"** 与 **"Pahinga"** 走红，靠直播与小型现场积累粉丝。团名致敬 **Kinks** 的 *"Lola"* 与法语 **amour**（爱）。常被称作**「精致流行」**——温暖律动、聪明钩子句、写暗恋犹豫与现代恋爱的自省歌词。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Fallen"** was released on **25 June 2021** as part of the single **"dahan-dahan"** (Tagalog for *slowly*). The track is a **bouncy indie-pop confession** about having a crush you can't quite admit: *What if I told you that I've fallen?* repeats like a question the singer keeps swallowing. Images mix sweetness (*I'll make your breakfast every morning*) with anxiety (*Please don't say my name, help me put out this flame*). The **never mind** refrain is the emotional dodge—saying you're fine staying in the friend zone while the heart races (*My heart skips a beat when I hear you calling*). It's a relatable study in **unrequited or hidden love** and the fear of ruining what you already have.

**中文**

**"Fallen"** 于 **2021 年 6 月 25 日**发行，收录于单曲 **"dahan-dahan"**（他加禄语「慢慢地」）。是一首轻快的**独立流行坦白曲**：暗恋却说不出口——*What if I told you that I've fallen?*（要是我告诉你我沦陷了呢？）像一遍遍咽回去的问题。意象又甜又慌：*I'll make your breakfast every morning*（每天早上给你做早餐）对照 *Please don't say my name, help me put out this flame*（请别叫我的名字，帮我浇灭这团火）。**never mind** 叠句是情绪闪躲——嘴上说甘心待在朋友区，心跳却失控（*My heart skips a beat when I hear you calling*）。写**暗恋／藏起来的爱**与怕毁掉现有关系，很接地气。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **what if I told you** | 要是我告诉你…… | 试探性开场，暗恋常用 |
| **I've fallen** | 我沦陷了；我坠入了 | *fall for someone* = 爱上某人 |
| **the way you say my name** | 你念我名字的样子 | 亲密细节描写 |
| **heart skips a beat** | 心跳漏一拍 | 固定说法，紧张或心动 |
| **never mind** | 算了；没事 | 英式常连写 *nevermind*；用来收回刚说的话 |
| **don't wanna give you any trouble** | 不想给你添麻烦 | 委婉保持距离 |
| **by your side** | 在你身边 | 比 *with you* 更强调陪伴 |
| **for as long as I can hide** | 只要能藏着／瞒着 | 暗恋不敢表白 |
| **heart-shaped arrow** | 心形箭 | 丘比特意象 |
| **pick you up when you're a mess** | 你狼狈时把你接住 | *mess* = 情绪或生活一团糟 |
| **whether you like it or not** | 不管你喜不喜欢 | 强调义无反顾 |
| **put out this flame** | 浇灭这团火 | *flame* = 激情／暗恋之火 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 主歌 *What if I told you that I've fallen?* 重复多次，适合练**疑问语调**与连读；副歌感不强，整首偏叙事。
- *never mind* 在口语里常用来**撤回刚说的话**，歌里叠句强化「想说又不敢说」。
- 菲律宾独立流行，咬字清楚；可注意 *don't wanna*、*I'm OK with* 等口语缩略。
- 想积累**暗恋与暧昧口语**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 相关句型。
