## 歌手简介 · About Kodaline

**English**

**Kodaline** are an **Irish rock band** from **Dublin**, formed in 2011. The lineup centres on vocalist **Steve Garrigan**, guitarist **Mark Prendergast**, bassist **Jason Boland**, and drummer **Vinny May**. They broke internationally with the heartbreak ballad **"All I Want"** (2012), then released their debut album ***In a Perfect World*** (2013), which went to **No. 1** in Ireland and **No. 3** in the UK. Kodaline blend **indie rock**, **pop rock**, and **alternative rock**; Garrigan's lyrics often draw on personal diary-like stories—breakups, doubt, and recovery—while the band's bigger anthems push themes of **self-determination** and resilience.

**中文**

**Kodaline** 是来自**都柏林**的**爱尔兰摇滚乐队**，2011 年成立。核心成员包括主唱 **Steve Garrigan**、吉他手 **Mark Prendergast**、贝斯手 **Jason Boland**、鼓手 **Vinny May**。2012 年失恋抒情曲 **"All I Want"** 打开国际知名度；2013 年首张专辑 ***In a Perfect World*** 登顶**爱尔兰专辑榜**、**英国榜第 3**。风格融合 **独立摇滚、流行摇滚、另类摇滚**；Garrigan 的歌词常像日记——写分手、怀疑与复原；较大气的颂歌则强调**自我主宰**与韧性。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Take Control"** appears on ***In a Perfect World*** (2013). Unlike the album's breakup-centred hits, this track is an **empowerment anthem**: a repeated command to *take control of who you are* and *stay the same, don't let them change you*. The chorus admits life *comes apart*—but the answer is always to *start again* and *open up your heart*. Lines like *ain't nobody gonna save you* and *show the people what you are made of* make it useful for learners practising **informal negatives** and **imperative** speech. The production is mid-tempo rock with a sing-along hook built for crowd chants.

**中文**

**"Take Control"** 收录于 ***In a Perfect World***（2013）。与专辑里以分手为主的热曲不同，这是一首**赋能 anthem**：反复命令 *take control of who you are*、*stay the same, don't let them change you*。副歌承认生活 *comes apart*——但答案永远是 *start again*、*open up your heart*。*ain't nobody gonna save you*、*show the people what you are made of* 等句适合练**口语否定**与**祈使语气**。中速摇滚编曲，钩子句很适合齐唱。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **take control of** | 掌控；接管 | *take control of who you are* = 主宰你自己 |
| **stay the same** / **don't let them change you** | 保持本色 / 别让他们改变你 | 常见自我边界句；*let someone do* 结构 |
| **ain't nobody gonna save you** | 没人会来救你 | 三重口语：*ain't* + *nobody* + *gonna*；强调只能靠自己 |
| **the world is getting stranger** | 世界越来越陌生 | *strange* 此处偏「怪异、难以理解」 |
| **when it comes apart** | 当一切分崩离析 | *come apart* = 瓦解、散架 |
| **and when it comes apart** | 而当一切分崩离析 | 第二、三段副歌用 *And* 衔接，语气更推进 |
| **start again** | 重新开始 | 比 *restart* 更口语；歌里反复出现 |
| **open up your heart** | 敞开你的心 | *open up* 短语动词；表愿意再信任/再感受 |
| **looking in the mirror** | 照镜子 | 自省常用意象；*look in* 不用 *at* |
| **underneath the skies** | 在天空之下 | 略诗意；口语更常说 *under the sky* |
| **see a little clearer** | 看得更清楚一些 | *clearer* 比 *more clear* 更自然 |
| **what you are made of** | 你是什么料；你的底色 | 习语：考验下见真章；*Show the people what you're made of* |
| **when it comes apart, just start again** | 分崩离析时，就重新开始 | 整句可整段记忆，适合跟唱 |
| **you won't break me down** | 你压不垮我 | Bridge 三连句；表韧性、不被外界击垮 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 主句 *Take control of who you are* 重复极多，适合**跟唱**练祈使句重音：重读 **control**、**you**。
- 注意 **ain't nobody** 双重否定在口语里仍表否定；读成「没人」即可，不要按字面翻成肯定。
- *comes apart* 与 *start again* 常连读；可先分句慢读，再跟原速副歌。
- 想延伸练**态度与边界**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 self-talk / 韧性相关表达。
