## 歌手简介 · About Joshua Hyslop

**English**

**Joshua Hyslop** (born 1987 in Abbotsford, British Columbia) is a **Canadian folk singer-songwriter** signed to **Nettwerk Records**. He grew up in Saskatchewan, spent years in **Vancouver**, and later moved to **Victoria, BC**. Since his 2011 debut EP *Cold Wind*, he has built a catalogue of intimate, acoustic-driven songs—*Where the Mountain Meets the Valley* (2012), *In Deepest Blue* (2015), *Echos* (2018), *Ash & Stone* (2020), and *Westward* (2022)—that have earned hundreds of millions of streams. His writing is often compared to **Damien Rice**, **Iron & Wine**, and **The Lumineers**; lyrics tend toward quiet hope, memory, and emotional honesty rather than big pop hooks.

**中文**

**Joshua Hyslop**（1987 年生于不列颠哥伦比亚省阿伯茨福德）是加拿大**民谣创作歌手**，签约 **Nettwerk Records**。成长于萨斯喀彻温，长期活跃于**温哥华**，后迁居**维多利亚**。2011 年 EP *Cold Wind* 出道以来，以 intimate、偏 acoustic 的作品积累数亿流媒体播放——*Where the Mountain Meets the Valley*（2012）、*In Deepest Blue*（2015）、*Echos*（2018）、*Ash & Stone*（2020）、*Westward*（2022）。写法常被比作 **Damien Rice**、**Iron & Wine**、**The Lumineers**；歌词多写安静的希望、记忆与情感诚实，而非大流行 hook。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Gentle Heart"** was released on **10 April 2020** as a single ahead of the album *Ash & Stone* (September 2020). Written during the early **COVID-19** lockdowns, it is a **folk ballad** that speaks directly to a weary listener—*Oh, gentle heart, oh, simple soul*—urging them not to turn back or lose hope. The recurring bridge (*Should the dawn refuse to rise… may you find your heart next to mine*) turns private endurance into shared comfort: even in a *hopeless time*, two people can still *find hope* together. The language is simple and poetic, making it strong material for **pronunciation** and **sing-along** practice.

**中文**

**"Gentle Heart"** 于 **2020 年 4 月 10 日**以单曲形式发行，随后收入专辑 *Ash & Stone*（2020 年 9 月）。创作于 **COVID-19** 早期封控期间，是一首**民谣抒情曲**，直接向疲惫的听者说话——*Oh, gentle heart, oh, simple soul*——劝其别回头、别失希望。反复出现的桥段（*Should the dawn refuse to rise… may you find your heart next to mine*）把个人坚持变成彼此安慰：即使在 *hopeless time*，两人仍可一起 *find hope*。用词简单而有诗意，适合练**发音**与**跟唱**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **gentle heart** | 温柔的心 | 歌题核心称呼；*gentle* = 温和、不伤人 |
| **simple soul** | 单纯的灵魂 | *soul* 此处偏「内在自我」，不是宗教专指 |
| **on your own** | 独自一人 | 固定短语；比 *alone* 更强调「靠自己」 |
| **lose hope** | 失去希望 | 常见搭配；反义 *keep hope* / *find hope* |
| **follow the lights** | 追随灯火 | 比喻跟着微光找路；*guide you home* 接回家意象 |
| **should the dawn refuse to rise** | 若黎明不肯升起 | 虚拟/文学句式；*should* 表「万一、倘若」 |
| **darkness close on all sides** | 黑暗从四面八方合拢 | *on all sides* = 四面；比 *outside* 更强调包围感 |
| **may you find your heart** | 愿你找到你的心 | 祝福句型；*May you…* 较正式、诗意 |
| **next to mine** | 挨着我的（心） | *mine* = my heart；省略避免重复 |
| **hopeless time** | 毫无希望的时代/时刻 | 与 *found hope* 形成对照 |
| **plant your seeds** | 播下种子 | 比喻「种下希望、开始积累」 |
| **keep on moving on** | 继续往前走 | *keep on + -ing* = 持续做某事 |
| **down the road** | 沿着路；将来 | 字面「沿路走」；口语也可指「日后、以后」 |
| **burning low** | 渐渐燃尽 | 形容灯/火将熄；也暗示精力耗尽 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 句子短、重复多，适合**跟唱**练清晰咬字；注意 *gentle heart* 与 *simple soul* 两行之间的轻停顿。
- Joshua 的 **folk 咬字**偏柔和，*hopeless time* 末尾 *time* 常轻读；可先慢速跟读再加速。
- 歌里大量 **should / may** 虚拟与祝愿句，可用来练「若……愿你……」的英文语气。
- 想延伸练**安慰与陪伴表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里 tender reassurance 类句型。
