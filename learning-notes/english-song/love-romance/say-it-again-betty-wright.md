## 歌手简介 · About Betty Wright

**English**

**Betty Wright** (born Bessie Regina Norris, 21 December 1953 – 10 May 2020) was an American **soul and R&B singer** from Miami, Florida. She began singing in her family's gospel group at age two and released her first album at 11. Wright became a **Miami soul** pioneer in the 1970s with hits like **"Clean Up Woman"** and **"Tonight Is the Night"**, known for her powerful voice, whistle-register falsetto, and frank lyrics about love and sexuality. She won a **Grammy** for **"Where Is the Love?"** (1975) and later mentored younger artists including Gloria Estefan and Joss Stone. Her 1988 album **Mother Wit**—the first gold album by a Black female artist on her own label—relaunched her career with **"No Pain, (No Gain)"** and **"After the Pain"**.

**中文**

**Betty Wright**（本名 Bessie Regina Norris，1953 年 12 月 21 日—2020 年 5 月 10 日）是美国**灵魂乐 / R&B 歌手**，成长于佛罗里达迈阿密。两岁开始在家庭福音合唱团演唱，11 岁发行首张专辑。1970 年代以 **"Clean Up Woman"**、**"Tonight Is the Night"** 等成为 **Miami soul** 代表人物，嗓音有力、哨音假声出众，歌词直白写爱与欲望。1975 年以 **"Where Is the Love?"** 获**格莱美奖**；后来还指导 Gloria Estefan、Joss Stone 等后辈。1988 年专辑 **Mother Wit** 是首位黑人女歌手在自己厂牌拿下金唱片的专辑，以 **"No Pain, (No Gain)"**、**"After the Pain"** 重回主流视野。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Say It Again"** was released in **1988** as a single from *Mother Wit*. Over a slow, sensual groove, Wright plays a woman who has been **close friends** with a man for years—she senses he loves her (*actions speak louder than words*) but needs to **hear him say it** out loud: *Did you say you love me?* The chorus repeats *Say it again* like a plea for confirmation, turning vulnerability into a soulful demand. The song peaked at **No. 22** on the Billboard R&B chart and became one of her signature late-career ballads—perfect for practicing **direct emotional requests** in spoken English.

**中文**

**"Say It Again"** 于 **1988 年**作为单曲发行，收录于 *Mother Wit*。在缓慢、性感的律动上，Betty 扮演一位与男方**多年密友**的女性——她感觉到对方爱自己（*actions speak louder than words*），却需要他**亲口再说一遍**：*Did you say you love me?* 副歌反复 *Say it again*，像恳求确认，把脆弱变成一种灵魂乐式的直白要求。单曲在 Billboard R&B 榜最高 **第 22** 位，是她后期代表作之一，很适合练**直接表达情感需求**的口语。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **say it again** | 再说一遍 | 歌题核心；请求对方重复确认 |
| **I don't believe I heard you right** | 我不敢相信我听对了 | 固定口语；*hear right* = 听清楚、没听错 |
| **real close friends** | 非常亲密的朋友 | *real* 加强语气，口语常见 |
| **something heavy hangin on your mind** | 心里有件沉重的事 | *hangin* = hanging；*heavy* 指心事重 |
| **actions speak louder than words** | 行动胜于言语 | 经典谚语；本歌关键转折 |
| **head in a crazy spin** | 脑子乱成一团 | *spin* 像旋转一样头晕、混乱 |
| **let this love affair begin** | 让这段感情开始吧 | *love affair* 可指恋情，略带浪漫色彩 |
| **what's on your mind** | 你在想什么 | 日常关心句；比 *what are you thinking* 更常用 |
| **I'll always have the time** | 我永远有时间 | 表达「愿意倾听」的温柔承诺 |
| **come on** | 来吧；拜托 | 催促或鼓励对方开口 |
| **did you say you love me** | 你是不是说你爱我 | 副歌核心问句；把暗示变成明确确认 |
| **one more time for me** | 再为我多说一次 | *for me* 强调「我需要听到」 |
| **I need to hear you say you love me** | 我需要听你说爱我 | Outro 直白诉求；比副歌更迫切 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Say it again* 节奏舒缓，适合练**重读 + 拖长元音**：*Say it agaaain, ooh honey*。
- Verse 里 *hangin*、*somethin'* 等**口语缩略**很常见，跟唱时注意轻读。
- 想延伸练**恋爱里「要对方说清楚」**的句型，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里请求确认、表达不确定的段落。
