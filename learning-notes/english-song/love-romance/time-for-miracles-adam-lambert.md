## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Time for Miracles"** was recorded for Roland Emmerich's disaster film ***2012*** and released in **November 2009**; it plays over the film's end credits. It is a big **power ballad**: late at night, the singer can't stop missing someone, but insists *I ain't giving up on love*—maybe it's *time for miracles*. The hopeful message fits the film's end-of-the-world story.

**中文**

**"Time for Miracles"** 为 Roland Emmerich 执导的灾难片 ***2012***（《2012》）录制，**2009 年 11 月**发行，用于片尾字幕。这是一首大气的**强力抒情曲**：深夜难眠、思念不止，但主角坚持 *I ain't giving up on love*——也许是时候迎来*奇迹*了。充满希望的主题正好呼应电影的末日故事。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌高音密集，先用说话音量读熟 *I ain't giving up on love*，再上高音。
- *ain't* 在歌里很常见，写作与正式场合改用 *am not / isn't*。
- 配合电影《2012》片尾看，会更容易体会「末日里仍不放弃」的情绪。
