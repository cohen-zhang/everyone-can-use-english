## 歌手简介 · About Angelika Vee

**English**

**Angelika Vee** is an independent singer who records gentle **lullaby** and acoustic covers of well-known songs. Her album ***Sweet Lullabies*** (2020) turns classics into soft, slow bedtime versions—simple piano or guitar, breathy vocals, and lots of humming.

**中文**

**Angelika Vee** 是一位独立歌手，擅长把经典歌曲改编成轻柔的**摇篮曲**与原声版本。专辑 ***Sweet Lullabies***（2020）把老歌放慢、减配器，只留钢琴或吉他、气声演唱与大量哼唱，适合睡前播放。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"You Are My Sunshine"** is an American standard first recorded in **1939** and made famous by **Jimmie Davis** in **1940**; Davis later became Governor of Louisiana, and the song is one of Louisiana's official **state songs**. The full original is actually a sad love song about losing someone, but most people only know the sweet chorus—which is why it became a favorite **lullaby** for parents and children. Angelika Vee's version keeps just the chorus and a verse, sung slowly with humming.

**中文**

**"You Are My Sunshine"** 是美国经典老歌，**1939 年**首次录音，**1940 年**由 **Jimmie Davis** 唱红；Davis 后来当选路易斯安那州州长，这首歌也成为该州官方**州歌**之一。完整原版其实是一首失恋悲歌，但大家最熟的是温暖的副歌，于是它成了父母哄孩子的经典**摇篮曲**。Angelika Vee 版只保留副歌与一段主歌，放慢并加入哼唱。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You are my sunshine** | 你是我的阳光 | 比喻：带来快乐与温暖的人；亲子口语可直接对孩子说 |
| **my only sunshine** | 我唯一的阳光 | *only* 强调独一无二 |
| **You make me happy** | 你让我快乐 | *make sb + adj*：使某人…… |
| **when skies are gray** | 当天空灰暗时 | *gray skies* = 心情低落、日子不顺 |
| **You'll never know, dear** | 亲爱的，你永远不会知道 | *dear* 作称呼，温柔亲昵 |
| **how much I love you** | 我有多爱你 | 感叹型宾语从句：*how much* + 主谓 |
| **Please don't take my sunshine away** | 请别带走我的阳光 | *take … away* 拿走；短语动词 |
| **No one can take my sunshine away** | 谁也带不走我的阳光 | 结尾句由恳求变成笃定 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 全曲只有几句、语速极慢，适合**亲子睡前跟唱**；可以把 *you* 换成孩子名字：*Celine, you are my sunshine*。
- 注意 *sunshine* 重音在前（**SUN**-shine），*away* 重音在后（a-**WAY**）。
- *You'll never know, dear* 里 *You'll* 读作 /juːl/，与 *know* 之间几乎连在一起。
- 想了解完整版（含失恋主歌）可以另找 Johnny Cash 或 Jimmie Davis 原版对照。
