## 歌手简介 · About Bobby Caldwell

**English**

**Bobby Caldwell** (August 15, 1951 – March 14, 2023) was an American **singer-songwriter** and multi-instrumentalist, often described as a pioneer of **"blue-eyed soul"**—white artists performing R&B and soul with deep authenticity. Born in Manhattan and raised partly in **Miami**, he grew up around jazz and Latin music; his mother sold real estate to **Reginald "Reggie" Van Cleef** (aka **Sinatra**'s pianist). Caldwell broke through with his **1978 self-titled debut**; label **TK Records** initially hid his photo, fearing audiences would reject a white soul singer. **"What You Won't Do for Love"** became his signature hit and one of the most **sampled** songs in hip-hop and R&B. He continued recording jazz, soul, and standards for decades and was admired for a **smooth tenor** and elegant phrasing.

**中文**

**Bobby Caldwell**（1951 年 8 月 15 日－2023 年 3 月 14 日）是美国**创作型歌手**、多乐器演奏者，常被称为 **「蓝眼灵魂乐」**（白人演绎 R&B／灵魂乐）的先驱之一。生于曼哈顿，部分成长于**迈阿密**，从小接触爵士与拉丁音乐；母亲曾做 **Sinatra** 钢琴手 Reggie Van Cleef 的房产经纪。1978 年**同名处女专辑**出道；厂牌 **TK Records** 起初不公开他的照片，担心听众排斥白人唱灵魂乐。**"What You Won't Do for Love"** 成为代表作，也是嘻哈与 R&B 里被**采样**最多的曲目之一。此后数十年持续发行爵士、灵魂与标准曲，以**顺滑男高音**和优雅句读著称。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"What You Won't Do for Love"** was released in **1978** on Caldwell's debut album. Co-written with **Alfons Kettner**, the track blends **soft rock, jazz, and R&B** with a laid-back groove and famously warm horn arrangement. The lyric is a confession: the singer returns to someone he can't stop wanting—*Got a thing for you and I can't let go*—and marvels at how love makes people do things they'd never do otherwise. The title hook *What you won't do, do for love* plays on the double **"do"** (what you refuse vs. what you will do). The song reached **No. 9** on the **Billboard Hot 100** and has been covered and sampled by artists from **Boyz II Men** to **2Pac** (*"Do for Love"*).

**中文**

**"What You Won't Do for Love"** 于 **1978 年**收录于 Caldwell 首张同名专辑。与 **Alfons Kettner** 共同创作，融合**软摇滚、爵士与 R&B**，律动松弛，铜管温暖是标志。歌词是坦白：歌手回到放不下的人——*Got a thing for you and I can't let go*（我对你有意思，没法放手）——并感叹爱会让人做出平时绝不会做的事。歌题 *What you won't do, do for love* 里两个 **do** 形成对照（你平时不肯做的 vs. 为爱会做的）。单曲在 **Billboard Hot 100** 最高 **第 9**，后被 **Boyz II Men** 翻唱、**2Pac** 采样（*"Do for Love"*）等。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I guess you wonder** | 我想你好奇／大概你想知道 | 柔和开场，比 *You must wonder* 更客气 |
| **find a love within** | 在心里／自身找到一份爱 | 先自我疗愈再回头找对方 |
| **came back to let you know** | 回来是想告诉你 | *let someone know* = 告知 |
| **got a thing for someone** | 对某人有意思；喜欢上某人 | 口语；比 *have feelings for* 更随意 |
| **can't let go** | 没法放手 | 恋爱高频句 |
| **in a daze** | 神魂颠倒；恍惚 | *daze* = 茫然、发呆状态 |
| **go around the world for love** | 为爱走遍世界 | 夸张说法，形容执着 |
| **what you won't do for love** | 你平时不肯为爱做的事 | 歌题核心；对比「为爱什么都肯」 |
| **you don't / won't give up** | 你不放弃／你不会放弃 | 两版副歌 slight 差别，都跟唱常见 |
| **only you make me do** | 只有你让我（去做） | 强调对方独一无二的影响力 |
| **what I would not do** | 我本不会做的事 | 虚拟语气，为爱破例 |
| **I only want the best** | 我只要最好的 | 表白「你值得最好」 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌节奏松弛，适合练 **swing 感弱读**：*What you won't do, do for love* 中间第二个 *do* 常轻读。
- *Got a thing for you* 与 *can't let go* 是地道恋爱口语，比课本 *I like you very much* 自然得多。
- 爵士／灵魂标准曲，通勤听几遍熟悉旋律后，可跟唱练 **连读**：*I'm in a daze from your love*.
- 想延伸**浪漫表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 相关句型。
