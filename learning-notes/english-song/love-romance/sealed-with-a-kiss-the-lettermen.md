## 歌手简介 · About The Lettermen

**English**

**The Lettermen** are an American **vocal trio** formed in **1959** in Los Angeles: **Tony Butala**, **Bob Engemann**, and **Jim Pike** (later replaced by **Gary Pike** and others as the lineup evolved). Known for **close harmony** and polished **easy listening / pop** arrangements, they scored hits through the 1960s and 1970s with songs like *"The Way You Look Tonight"*, *"When I Fall in Love"*, and *"Goin' Out of My Head"*. Their version of **"Sealed With a Kiss"** (1968) reached **No. 7** on the Billboard Hot 100. The group has continued touring for decades with Butala as the constant member, making them one of the longest-running vocal acts in American pop.

**中文**

**The Lettermen** 是 **1959 年**在洛杉矶成立的美国**三重唱**：**Tony Butala**、**Bob Engemann**、**Jim Pike**（后由 **Gary Pike** 等接替，阵容几经更迭）。以**紧密和声**与精致的 **easy listening / 流行**编曲著称，1960–70 年代凭 *"The Way You Look Tonight"*、*"When I Fall in Love"*、*"Goin' Out of My Head"* 等打入榜单。他们翻唱的 **"Sealed With a Kiss"**（1968）在 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 7** 位。Butala 一直是核心成员，组合巡演数十年，是美国流行乐坛存续最久的声乐团体之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Sealed With a Kiss"** was written by **Peter Udell** and **Gary Geld** and first became a **No. 1** hit for **Brian Hyland** in **1962**—a summer-breakup letter set to music. **The Lettermen's** 1968 cover keeps the same story: a lover who has too much to say and not enough time to say it in person, so they **write a letter** and **seal it with a kiss**. The phrase *sealed with a kiss* evokes old-fashioned romance: closing an envelope with a lipstick mark or a kiss before sending love by mail. Light, melodic, and easy to sing, it remains a staple of **oldies** playlists and romantic pop.

**中文**

**"Sealed With a Kiss"** 由 **Peter Udell** 与 **Gary Geld** 创作，**1962 年**由 **Brian Hyland** 唱红并登顶 **Billboard Hot 100**——一首把「暑假分手信」写成歌的作品。**The Lettermen** 1968 年翻唱保留同一故事：想说的话太多、当面来不及说，于是**写信**，再**以吻封缄**寄出。*sealed with a kiss* 很有老式浪漫感：寄信前在信封上印唇印或轻轻一吻。旋律轻快、好跟唱，至今仍是 **oldies** 与情歌歌单常客。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **so much on my mind** | 心里装了好多事 | *on one's mind* = 惦记、放不下 |
| **swell** | 很棒；讨人喜欢 | 老式口语，比 *great* 更复古 |
| **head over heels** | 神魂颠倒；深深爱上 | 固定搭配 *fall head over heels (in love)* |
| **seal it with a kiss** | 以吻封缄 | 歌题；寄信/示爱时的浪漫说法 |
| **open my heart** | 敞开心扉 | 比 *tell you my feelings* 更诗意 |
| **the guy I adore** | 我爱慕的那个人 | *adore* = 深爱、倾慕 |
| **crazy about you** | 为你疯狂；超喜欢你 | 口语高频，比 *love* 更热烈 |
| **rewrite and revise** | 一改再改 | 写信前斟酌字句；*revise* = 修改润色 |
| **bring … to a close** | 结束……；收尾 | 正式一点的说法，信末常用 |
| **XOXO** | 亲亲抱抱（信末落款） | X = kiss，O = hug |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 三重唱**和声**层次丰富：跟唱时可先练主旋律，再听第二、第三声部如何衬在底下。
- *head over heels*、*crazy about you* 都是**热恋口语**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里类似句型。
- 副歌 *seal it with a kiss* 节奏清楚，适合 **KTV** 跟唱；主歌句子偏长，注意在逗号处换气。
- *swell*、*XOXO* 带一点**复古**味道，写情书或怀旧场景时很贴切。
