## 歌手简介 · About Gord Bamford

**English**

**Gord Bamford** (born **17 April 1976**, Traralgon, Victoria, Australia) is an **Australian-born, Alberta-raised** Canadian **country singer-songwriter**. After his parents' divorce, he moved with his mother to **Lacombe, Alberta**, at age five; she was a touring country singer and encouraged his music. He won a local radio talent search in 1995 and spent years building a neo-traditional country career. Bamford is one of Canada's most decorated country artists, with **26 Canadian Country Music Association (CCMA) awards**, multiple **JUNO** nominations, and two **CMA Global Country Artist of the Year** wins. His warm baritone and storytelling style blend classic country with modern production.

**中文**

**Gord Bamford**（1976 年 4 月 17 日生，澳大利亚 Traralgon）是**澳裔、阿尔伯塔长大**的加拿大**乡村创作歌手**。父母离异后，5 岁随母亲移居阿尔伯塔 **Lacombe**；母亲曾是巡回乡村歌手，鼓励他走音乐路。1995 年在电台选秀中崭露头角，此后以新传统主义（新传统）乡村风格积累口碑。他是加拿大获奖最多的乡村艺人之一，获 **26 座 CCMA 奖**、多次 **JUNO** 提名，并两夺 **CMA 全球乡村艺人奖**。嗓音温厚，擅长用叙事型歌词写日常与感情。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"When Your Lips Are So Close"** was released in **September 2013** as the lead single from *Country Junkie*, Bamford's sixth studio album. Co-written with **Byron Hill** and **Brent Baxter**, the mid-tempo country track describes the torture of standing near someone you're attracted to but not yet able to kiss—*so far away when your lips are so close*. It topped the **Billboard Canada Country** chart, went **gold**, and won **CCMA Single of the Year** (2014). The neon-bar setting and physical imagery (*drink you in*, *thirsty eyes*) make it useful for learning sensual but radio-friendly country phrasing.

**中文**

**"When Your Lips Are So Close"** 于 **2013 年 9 月**发行，是第六张专辑 *Country Junkie* 的主打单曲。由 **Byron Hill**、**Brent Baxter** 与 Bamford 共同创作，中速乡村曲写「人就在旁边、嘴唇近在咫尺却吻不到」的煎熬——*so far away when your lips are so close*。歌曲登顶 **Billboard Canada Country**，获**金唱片**，并夺 **2014 年 CCMA 年度单曲奖**。霓虹酒吧场景与身体意象（*drink you in*、*thirsty eyes*）适合学习美式乡村里「含蓄但带欲望」的口语表达。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **it got hotter in this room** | 这房间一下子更「热」了 | 双关：温度 + 气氛变暧昧 |
| **drink you in** | 用目光把你「饮」进去 | 习语：贪婪地看、欣赏 |
| **thirsty eyes** | 渴切的眼神 | *thirsty* 此处非「口渴」，而是渴望 |
| **reach right out** | 立刻伸手 | *right* 加强「马上、直接」 |
| **wrap you in my arms** | 把你搂进怀里 | 浪漫/亲密常用 |
| **never let go** | 永不松手 | 跟唱时注意 *let* 的连读 |
| **gotta taste you** | 非得吻到你 | *gotta* = got to；*taste* 此处指亲吻 |
| **drives me crazy** | 让我发疯 | 表强烈欲望或烦躁 |
| **so far away when … so close** | 近在咫尺却像很远 | 歌题核心矛盾句型 |
| **miles between** | 隔着好几英里 | 夸张：物理距离近、心理距离远 |
| **small talk** | 闲聊；寒暄 | 对比 *touch*，暗示想要更亲密 |
| **can't wait for your touch** | 等不及你的触碰 | *can't wait* + for 很常见 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *When your lips are so close* 重复多遍，适合练**连读**：*lips are so close* 常读成连贯的一串音节。
- 主歌里 **drink you in / thirsty eyes / cool smile** 是典型乡村「感官描写」，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的 desire 表达。
- *drives me crazy*、*can't wait*、*gotta* 等缩略在快语速下几乎听不清辅音，建议先慢速跟读再原速。
- 注意 **so far away** 与 **so close** 的对比重音——两句押韵对立，跟唱时把重音落在 *far* 和 *close* 上更有力。
