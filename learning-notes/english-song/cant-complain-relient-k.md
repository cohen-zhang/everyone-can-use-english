## 歌手简介 · About Relient K

**English**

**Relient K** is an American **Christian rock / pop-punk** band formed in **Canton, Ohio**, in 1998. The name puns on the car **Pontiac Reliant K** and being **reliant on God**. Lead singer **Matt Thiessen** (vocals, piano, guitar) and guitarist **Matt Hoopes** anchor a lineup that has included bassist John Warne and drummer Ethan Luck. Known for witty, self-aware lyrics and melodic hooks, the band scored mainstream hits with *"Be My Escape"* and *"Who I Am Hates Who I've Been"* before exploring a cleaner **pop sound** on *Collapsible Lung* (2013). They have been nominated for multiple **Grammy** and **GMA Dove** awards.

**中文**

**Relient K** 是 1998 年在**俄亥俄 Canton** 成立的美国**基督教摇滚 / pop-punk** 乐队。队名双关车型 **Pontiac Reliant K** 与 **reliant on God**（依靠上帝）。主唱 **Matt Thiessen**（人声、钢琴、吉他）与吉他手 **Matt Hoopes** 是核心；历任成员包括贝斯 John Warne、鼓手 Ethan Luck 等。乐队以机智、自省的歌词和旋律 hook 著称，凭 *"Be My Escape"*、*"Who I Am Hates Who I've Been"* 等打入主流，2013 年 *Collapsible Lung* 转向更干净的**流行**音色。曾获多项 **格莱美** 与 **GMA Dove** 提名。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Can't Complain"** is track 5 on *Collapsible Lung*, released **2 July 2013**. Co-written by Thiessen with **Brian Lee** and **Emily Wright**, it is an upbeat **pop anthem** about choosing gratitude when life goes sideways—speeding past a cop, stuck at a train crossing, spending Christmas Eve on an airport floor. The chorus *I can't complain / treat every day like a holiday* turns setbacks into reframes rather than denial. Thiessen said the album stripped back their usual studio tricks for a more concise, transparent sound.

**中文**

**"Can't Complain"** 收录于 2013 年 7 月 2 日发行的 *Collapsible Lung*，为第 5 轨。Thiessen 与 **Brian Lee**、**Emily Wright** 合写。这是一首轻快的**流行 anthem**，写生活在出差错时仍选择感恩——超速瞥见后视镜里的警察、在铁路道口等永不来的火车、圣诞夜睡在机场地板上。副歌 *I can't complain / treat every day like a holiday* 把挫折**重新解读**，而非否认困难。Thiessen 称这张专辑刻意减少惯用制作技巧，追求更简洁、透明的听感。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **rear view mirror** | 后视镜 | 美式拼写 *rearview* 也常见 |
| **crosswalk** | 人行横道 | 英式常说 *zebra crossing* |
| **outlook is sunny** | 看法/前景是乐观的 | *outlook* = 态度或前景，非仅天气 |
| **laying out** | 躺着晒太阳 | *lay out* 口语；注意 *laying* 非 *lying*（歌词原词） |
| **pleasures gotta come with pain** | 快乐总得伴随痛苦 | *gotta* = got to；口语缩略 |
| **won't always go my way** | 不会总顺我的意 | *go someone's way* = 按某人意向发展 |
| **life is gonna suck** | 生活会很糟 | 口语粗直；正式场合用 *be tough* |
| **can't complain** | 不能抱怨；还算过得去 | 日常答「How are you?」可用 |
| **let it go to waste** | 白白浪费 | *waste* 此处作名词 |
| **treat every day like a holiday** | 把每天当假期过 | 歌的核心态度句 |
| **the cookie's gonna crumble** | 饼干会碎；计划会泡汤 | 习语 *that's the way the cookie crumbles* |
| **don't say nothing** | 别说话（双重否定） | 非标准语法但口语极常见；= don't say anything |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I can't complain* 短、重复，**通勤跟唱**友好；注意 *complain* 重音在第二音节。
- 每段 Verse 都用 **I'll just imagine…** 做「自我安慰式重构」，可用来练假设语气。
- Bridge 的 *Don't say nothing* 是**双重否定**口语，学习时记「实际意思 = 别说话」，但别在正式写作里模仿。
- 想延伸练**积极 reframing**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于 gratitude 与 daily attitude 的表达。
