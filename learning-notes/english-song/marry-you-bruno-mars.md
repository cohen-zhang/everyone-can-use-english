## 歌手简介 · About Bruno Mars

**English**

**Bruno Mars** (born **Peter Gene Hernandez**, 8 October 1985, Honolulu, Hawaii) is an American **singer-songwriter**, **producer**, and **multi-instrumentalist**. Raised in a musical family, he moved to Los Angeles in 2003 and broke through as part of the production team **The Smeezingtons** before his solo debut ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans*** (2010). Mars blends **pop**, **R&B**, **funk**, **soul**, and **doo-wop**; he has won multiple **Grammy Awards** and is known for high-energy live shows. His lyrics often mix romance, humour, and retro flair—never taking love too seriously, but always making it feel good to sing.

**中文**

**Bruno Mars**（本名 **Peter Gene Hernandez**，1985 年 10 月 8 生于夏威夷檀香山）是美国**创作歌手**、**制作人**、**多乐器演奏者**。成长于音乐家庭，2003 年赴洛杉矶；先以制作组合 **The Smeezingtons** 崭露头角，再以个人专辑 ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans***（2010）爆红。风格融合 **流行、R&B、放克、灵魂、doo-wop**；多次获**格莱美**，现场以高能量著称。歌词常把浪漫、幽默与复古气质混在一起——不把爱说得太沉重，但永远好唱。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Marry You"** is the sixth track on ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans*** (2010), written by Mars with **Philip Lawrence** and **Ari Levine**. Mars has said the hook came from freestyling *I think I wanna marry you!* after a night out in **Las Vegas**—the feeling of having *way too many drinks*, loving everybody, and wanting to do something you'll *regret in the morning*. The song became a global **wedding and proposal staple** even though it was never released as a US single; it peaked at **No. 85** on the Billboard Hot 100 after a **Glee** cover in 2010. Musically it channels **1960s pop** and **girl-group** energy with a playful, impulsive chorus.

**中文**

**"Marry You"** 是 ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans***（2010）第六轨，由 Mars 与 **Philip Lawrence**、**Ari Levine** 共同创作。Mars 说 hook 来自**拉斯维加斯**一夜后即兴哼出 *I think I wanna marry you!*——那种*喝太多*、爱所有人、想做点*明天会后悔*的事的感觉。虽未在美国正式发单曲，却成为全球**婚礼与求婚常备曲**；2010 年 **Glee** 翻唱后登上 Billboard Hot 100 **第 85** 位。音乐上走 **60 年代流行** 与 **girl-group** 式的 playful、冲动副歌。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **something dumb to do** | 找点蠢事做 | *dumb* 此处 playful，非人身攻击；表冲动、胡闹 |
| **I think I wanna marry you** | 我想我好想娶/嫁给你 | *wanna* = want to；整句适合 KTV 跟唱 |
| **dancing juice** | 跳舞酒劲 | Bruno 在伦敦听来的说法；指喝高后的兴奋感 |
| **little chapel on the boulevard** | 林荫大道上的小教堂 | Vegas 闪婚意象；*chapel* = 小礼拜堂 |
| **trashed** | 烂醉的 | 俚语；比 *drunk* 更口语、更随意 |
| **a pocket full of cash we can blow** | 兜里现金够我们造 | *blow* = 花光、挥霍 |
| **shots of Patrón** | 几杯 Patrón 龙舌兰 | *shot* = 小杯烈酒一口闷；品牌名 |
| **it's on** | 开始了；派对走起 | 俚语：活动/气氛正式启动 |
| **If you're ready, like I'm ready** | 你若准备好了，就像我一样 | 对称句；求婚/承诺场景常用 |
| **let's just run** | 咱们就跑吧 | 表冲动私奔；*just* 加强「别想了就走」 |
| **that's cool** | 也行；没关系 | 很美的 casual 接受；*break up, that's cool* |
| **I won't blame you** | 我不怪你 | 比 *I don't blame you* 更口语 |
| **Just say "I do"** | 就说「我愿意」 | 婚礼誓词；*I do* 固定搭配 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Marry You* 节奏清楚，**KTV 首选**；先练 *Don't say no / Just say yeah* 的 call-and-response。
- Bruno 的 **doo-wop 咬字**偏复古清晰，*beautiful*、*marry* 等词适合练美式元音。
- *dancing juice*、*trashed*、*that's cool* 都是**非正式口语**；正式场合慎用 *trashed*。
- 想延伸练**恋爱与轻松承诺**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里 playful proposal 类表达。
