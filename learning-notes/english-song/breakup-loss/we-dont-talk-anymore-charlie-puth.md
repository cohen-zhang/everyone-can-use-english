## 歌手简介 · About Charlie Puth

**English**

**Charlie Puth** (born **Charles Otto Puth Jr.**, 1991, **Rumson, New Jersey**) is an American singer-songwriter and producer known for **perfect pitch**, melodic pop hooks, and writing hits for himself and others. He built an early following with YouTube covers and Vine clips, then broke through worldwide with **"See You Again"** (with Wiz Khalifa) for *Furious 7*. His debut album ***Nine Track Mind*** (2016) delivered singles like *"Marvin Gaye"*, *"One Call Away"*, and **"We Don't Talk Anymore"** featuring **Selena Gomez**—a duet that frames a breakup from both sides. Later hits include *"Attention"* and *"How Long"*.

**中文**

**Charlie Puth**（本名 **Charles Otto Puth Jr.**，1991 年生于新泽西 **Rumson**）是美国创作型歌手与制作人，以**绝对音感**、旋律感强的流行钩子句，以及为自己和他人写歌著称。早年靠 YouTube 翻唱与 Vine 短视频积累粉丝，后因与 Wiz Khalifa 合作的 **"See You Again"**（《速度与激情 7》主题曲）一举成名。首专 ***Nine Track Mind***（2016）推出 *"Marvin Gaye"*、*"One Call Away"*，以及与 **Selena Gomez** 合唱的 **"We Don't Talk Anymore"**——一首从双方视角写分手后失联的对唱。后续代表作还有 *"Attention"*、*"How Long"* 等。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"We Don't Talk Anymore"** is a **pop / tropical-pop** duet from Charlie Puth's debut album ***Nine Track Mind*** (2016), featuring **Selena Gomez**. It captures **post-breakup silence**—the ache of realizing you no longer speak the way you used to, while the other person seems to move on easily. Charlie takes the first verse; Selena answers from the other side in verse 2. The pre-chorus (*Don't wanna know…*) is the emotional core: jealousy mixed with self-protection. Released as a single in **May 2016**, it became one of the album's biggest international hits.

**中文**

**"We Don't Talk Anymore"** 是 Charlie Puth 首专 ***Nine Track Mind***（2016）中的一首**流行 / 热带流行**对唱，特邀 **Selena Gomez**。主题是**分手后的失联与沉默**——你们不再像从前那样说话，对方似乎轻易向前，自己却还卡在原地。Charlie 唱第一段；Selena 在第二段从另一侧回应。前副歌 *Don't wanna know…* 是情绪核心：嫉妒与自我保护缠在一起。2016 年 5 月作为单曲发行，成为专辑最成功的国际热单之一。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **we don't talk anymore** | 我们不再说话了；失联了 | 分手后「断联」的日常说法；比 *we broke up* 更侧重沟通中断 |
| **like we used to do** | 像我们从前那样 | *used to* = 过去习惯；副歌核心对比 |
| **what was all of it for?** | 那一切到底是为了什么？ | 事后质疑整段关系的价值；口语里很常见 |
| **found the one** | 找到了命中注定的那个人 | *the one* = 对的人；恋爱口语高频 |
| **I wish I would have known** | 真希望我早点知道 | 对过去的遗憾；口语里常混用 *wish I had known* |
| **move on** | 走出来；向前看 | 分手语境 = 放下感情；职场也可指换话题/继续推进 |
| **don't wanna know** | 我不想知道 | *wanna* = want to；自我保护式回避 |
| **holding onto you so tight** | 把你抱得那么紧 | *hold onto* = 紧紧抓住；可指身体也可指情感 |
| **I overdosed** | 我沉溺过头了 | 原义用药过量；此处比喻对爱上瘾、失控 |
| **your love was a game** | 你的爱不过是场游戏 | 指责对方不认真、玩弄感情 |
| **can't get you out of my brain** | 满脑子都是你，赶不走 | = can't stop thinking about you |
| **it's such a shame** | 真是太可惜了 | 惋惜语气；也可说 *what a shame* |
| **lying next to somebody** | 身边躺着某个人 | 暗示新恋情/同床；听歌时注意场景义 |
| **every now and then** | 偶尔；时不时 | = occasionally；比 *sometimes* 更口语 |
| **show up at your door** | 突然出现在你家门口 | *show up* = 现身、到场；不请自来的画面感 |
| **giving it to you just right** | （委婉）把你哄得刚刚好 | 原文偏露骨；口语里可指「满足得恰到好处」 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *We don't talk anymore / Like we used to do* 短、重复，**KTV 跟唱**友好；先把副歌唱稳，再练主歌的叙事句。
- 注意对唱结构：**Charlie = 被甩后还走不出来**；**Selena = 另一侧也在猜、也在怕**。同一句 *Don't wanna know*，视角一换，嫉妒对象从 *he* 变成 *she*。
- *Should've known*（= should have known）连读成 /ˈʃʊdəv/，跟唱时别把三个词咬死。
- *I overdosed* 在歌里是比喻，不是字面「吸毒」；学着用 *overdose on something* 表达「对……上瘾过头」。
- 想延伸练**分手后失联、走不出来、自我保护**的说法，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
