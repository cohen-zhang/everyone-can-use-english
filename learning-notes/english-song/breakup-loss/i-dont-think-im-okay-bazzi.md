## 歌手简介 · About Bazzi

**English**

**Bazzi** (born **Andrew Bazzi**, 28 August 1997) is an American **singer-songwriter** of **Lebanese** descent, raised in Dearborn, Michigan. He taught himself music production on **GarageBand** as a teenager and broke through with the viral hit **"Mine"** (2017), which reached the **Billboard Hot 100** top ten. Bazzi's style blends **R&B, pop, and hip-hop**; his lyrics often explore love, self-doubt, and the gap between public image and private pain. After debut album *Cosmic* (2018), he continued releasing intimate singles that prioritize honest confession over bravado.

**中文**

**Bazzi**（本名 **Andrew Bazzi**，1997 年 8 月 28 日生）是具**黎巴嫩**血统的美国**创作型歌手**，在密歇根 **Dearborn** 长大。少年时用 **GarageBand** 自学制作；2017 年凭病毒式传播的 **"Mine"** 打入 **Billboard Hot 100** 前十。风格融合 **R&B、流行与 hip-hop**；歌词常写爱情、自我怀疑，以及公众形象与私下痛苦之间的落差。首张专辑 *Cosmic*（2018）之后，他持续发行更私密、以坦白胜过虚张声势的单曲。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Don't Think I'm Okay"** was released on **10 July 2020** as a standalone single during the **COVID-19 pandemic**. Over a sparse, melancholic beat, Bazzi admits he is **not okay**—and that saying so *feels so good*. The lyrics trace disconnection: a mother who knows "it isn't me here," friends calling a number that no longer matches the person inside, and a career dream that may have **killed his self-esteem**. The hook *And that's okay* reframes vulnerability as acceptance rather than weakness. The song resonated as a mental-health anthem when many felt isolated and unable to perform normalcy.

**中文**

**"I Don't Think I'm Okay"** 于 **2020 年 7 月 10 日**发行，是 **新冠疫情** 期间的独立单曲。在稀疏、忧郁的节奏上，Bazzi 承认**自己并不好**——而且说出来*感觉真好*。歌词写层层脱节：母亲知道「现在的我不是我」，朋友打电话找的是里面那个人还在不在，事业梦想也许**杀死了自尊**。钩子句 *And that's okay* 把脆弱重新定义为接纳，而非软弱。许多人在隔离中无法「正常表演」时，这首歌成了心理健康向的代表作。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a little bit over a year** | 一年多一点 | *a little bit over* = 稍微超过 |
| **it isn't me here** | 这里的不是我 | 指「真正的我」不在场 |
| **in the clouds** | 在云端；心不在焉 | 比喻飘在空中、与现实脱节 |
| **way too late** | 太晚了 | *way* 加强语气 |
| **looking for a call** | 想打个电话 | 朋友想联络、确认你还在 |
| **the person they knew's still inside** | 他们认识的那个人还在不在 | 口语缩略 *who is* → *'s* |
| **I don't think I'm okay** | 我觉得我不太好 | 承认状态不佳；比 *I'm fine* 诚实 |
| **it feels so good to say** | 说出来感觉真好 | 承认脆弱有时是一种释放 |
| **and that's okay** | 而这样也可以 | 自我接纳；歌的核心句 |
| **killed my self-esteem** | 杀死了自尊 | 梦想／成功带来的压力 |
| **got so good at being someone else** | 太擅长扮演别人了 | 为迎合他人失去自我 |
| **in the search of your acceptance** | 在追寻你们的认可时 | *search* 此处作名词 |
| **ugly heart / ugly scars** | 丑陋的心／伤疤 | 不想示人的内心创伤 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I don't think I'm okay* 语速适中，适合练**坦白式口语**；注意 *'kay* 是 *okay* 的口语尾音。
- 主歌里 *I ain't seen*、*I don't want nobody* 是口语/双重否定，跟唱时保留原词，写作时慎用。
- 想延伸读**成人情绪表达**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里承认脆弱、自我认同相关句型。
