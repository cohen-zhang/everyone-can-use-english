## 歌手简介 · About Noah Cyrus

**English**

**Noah Lindsey Cyrus** (born 8 January 2000) is an American **singer-songwriter** from Nashville, Tennessee—the youngest daughter of Billy Ray Cyrus and younger sister of Miley Cyrus. She debuted with the Labrinth duet **"Make Me (Cry)"** (2016) and built a following through confessional, **emo-leaning pop** and collaborations with artists like XXXTentacion and PJ Harding. Her debut EP **Good Cry** (2018) addressed anxiety, depression, and a two-year breakup she had kept private. Cyrus has said songwriting is her **diary** and that she gravitates toward sad, honest material rather than uptempo hits.

**中文**

**Noah Lindsey Cyrus**（2000 年 1 月 8 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**，来自田纳西 Nashville，Billy Ray Cyrus 幼女、Miley Cyrus 的妹妹。2016 年与 Labrinth 合作 **"Make Me (Cry)"** 出道，以坦白、偏 **emo 气质** 的流行曲风及与 XXXTentacion、PJ Harding 等合作积累听众。首张迷你专辑 **Good Cry**（2018）直面焦虑、抑郁与一段长达两年、长期未公开的恋情结束。Noah 称写歌是她的**日记**，偏爱悲伤、真实的表达而非快歌。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Good Cry"** was released on **21 September 2018** as the title track of Noah Cyrus's debut EP. She wrote it during a dark period when depression, anxiety, and a failing relationship collided—**drinking the bar dry**, unable to be alone, searching **dusk to dawn** for a self that felt gone. The chorus reframes sadness as something valid: *Oh, I just need a good cry*. Noah has said the EP is not about any one tabloid romance but about **inner demons** and accepting that it's okay to feel hopeless. The track blends sparse guitar with soulful vocals in a slow **3/4 waltz**.

**中文**

**"Good Cry"** 于 **2018 年 9 月 21 日**发行，为 Noah Cyrus 首张迷你专辑同名曲。她在抑郁、焦虑与濒临破裂的感情交织的低谷期写下这首歌——**把酒吧喝干**，无法独处、**从黄昏找到天亮**也找不回原来的自己。副歌把悲伤重新定义为正当需求：*Oh, I just need a good cry*（我只是需要好好哭一场）。Noah 表示迷你专辑并非写某段八卦恋情，而是关于**内心魔鬼**与「允许自己绝望」。曲风为稀疏吉他配灵魂感嗓音的慢 **3/4 华尔兹**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **drink this place dry** | 把这地方喝干 | 习语；*dry* = 喝光库存；比 *best drop* 更常见听写 |
| **can't be alone** | 没法独处 | 情绪依赖或恐惧孤独的直白表达 |
| **dusk to dawn** | 从黄昏到天亮 | 整夜；也可作副词短语 |
| **hits like a train** | 像火车一样撞过来 | 比喻打击突然且猛烈；常接 *Reality hits…* |
| **face, God** | 天啊（看自己的脸时感叹） | 口语感叹，非宗教用语 |
| **hopeless / loveless** | 绝望的 / 缺爱的 | 副歌对仗核心情绪词 |
| **drink 'til I'm sick** | 喝到想吐 | *'til* = until；自我毁灭式宣泄 |
| **this weed don't do shit** | 抽大麻也一点用都没有 | *weed* = 大麻；粗口强调「什么办法都没用」 |
| **choke on my bad vibe** | 坏情绪堵得喘不过气 | 歌里用 *bad vibe* 非 *bed*；隐喻情绪窒息 |
| **me, myself, and I** | 只有我、我自己、和我 | 固定短语；强调孤独 |
| **puts me to shame** | 让我羞愧 | *This anxiety puts me to shame* |
| **the blues got me bad** | 忧郁缠得我够呛 | *the blues* = 忧郁；*got me bad* = 严重影响我 |
| **a good cry** | 好好哭一场 | 歌题；哭作为一种释放 |
| **all up in my head** | 全困在自己脑子里 | 口语 = 过度思虑、焦虑 |
| **kill the time** | 打发时间 | *swallowed a pill to kill the time* = 靠药片熬过时间 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Hopeless… loveless… good cry* 三行结构重复，适合**通勤跟听**练情绪词发音。
- *dusk to dawn* 与 *six days and five nights* 可对照学**时间跨度**的英文说法。
- 整首歌语速慢、3/4 华尔兹拍，适合初学者练**跟唱**；尾声反复 *Darling, I'm sorry* 可单独练情绪渐弱。
- 想延伸读情绪表达可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 **Unpleasant / Very Unpleasant** 相关句型。
