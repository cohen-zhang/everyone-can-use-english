## 歌手简介 · About OneRepublic

**English**

**OneRepublic** is an American **pop rock** band formed in **Colorado Springs, Colorado**, in **2002**. Frontman **Ryan Tedder** (vocals, piano, guitar) is also a major songwriter and producer for artists such as Beyoncé, Adele, and Taylor Swift. Core members have included guitarist **Zach Filkins**, guitarist **Drew Brown**, drummer **Eddie Fisher**, and multi-instrumentalist **Brent Kutzle**. After early buzz on MySpace, their debut album ***Dreaming Out Loud*** (2007) launched them into the mainstream; later hits include *"Stop and Stare"*, *"Good Life"*, and *"Counting Stars"*. The band is known for melodic piano-driven pop, arena-ready choruses, and Tedder's clear tenor.

**中文**

**OneRepublic** 是 2002 年在**科罗拉多州 Colorado Springs** 成立的美国**流行摇滚**乐队。主唱 **Ryan Tedder**（人声、钢琴、吉他）同时也是重量级词曲作者与制作人，曾为 Beyoncé、Adele、Taylor Swift 等写歌。核心成员包括吉他手 **Zach Filkins**、**Drew Brown**，鼓手 **Eddie Fisher**，以及多乐器手 **Brent Kutzle**。早期靠 MySpace 走红，2007 年首专 ***Dreaming Out Loud*** 打入主流；后续代表作还有 *"Stop and Stare"*、*"Good Life"*、*"Counting Stars"* 等。乐队以钢琴驱动的旋律流行、适合大合唱的副歌，以及 Tedder 清晰的男高音著称。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Apologize"** was written by **Ryan Tedder** and first released as a single around **30 April 2006**, later appearing on OneRepublic's debut album ***Dreaming Out Loud*** (2007). The song is a mid-tempo **pop ballad** about **betrayal and emotional cutoff**: the other person says "I need you," then cuts you down—and when they finally say sorry, the singer answers that **it's too late to apologize**. A **Timbaland remix** (featured on Timbaland's *Shock Value*, 2007) turned it into a global smash, topping charts in multiple countries and becoming one of the defining radio hits of the late 2000s. The original band version is piano-led and intimate; the remix adds Timbaland's signature beat while keeping Tedder's vocal and the unforgettable chorus hook.

**中文**

**"Apologize"** 由 **Ryan Tedder** 创作，约于 **2006 年 4 月 30 日**作为单曲发行，后收录于 OneRepublic 首专 ***Dreaming Out Loud***（2007）。这是一首中速**流行抒情曲**，写**背叛与情感断崖**：对方嘴上说「我需要你」，转头又把你砍倒；等对方终于道歉时，歌者只回一句——**道歉已经太迟了**。**Timbaland remix**（收录于 Timbaland 专辑 *Shock Value*，2007）让这首歌成为全球爆款，多国登顶，成为 2000 年代末电台代表作之一。乐队原版偏钢琴、更私密；remix 加上 Timbaland 标志性节奏，但保留 Tedder 人声与那句难忘的副歌 hook。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **holding on your rope** | 抓着你的绳子（不放手） | 歌词用 *on your rope*；日常更常说 *holding onto your rope*。意象：被对方吊着、悬空 |
| **ten feet off the ground** | 离地十英尺；悬在半空 | 夸张「被吊起来」的失控感；*feet* 为英制长度 |
| **can't make a sound** | 发不出声音 | 震惊、哽住；比 *can't speak* 更画面感 |
| **cut me down** | 把我砍倒；当面拆台 | 可指言语打击或突然否定；与前面 *need me* 形成反差 |
| **turn around and say** | 转身回一句 | *turn around and…* = 立刻反过来做某事 |
| **too late to apologize** | 道歉已经太晚 | 歌的核心句；关系已过临界点 |
| **take another chance** | 再给一次机会；再赌一把 | *chance* = 机会/冒险 |
| **take a fall** | 愿意摔一跤；替人背锅 | 此处偏「为你再承受一次伤害」 |
| **take a shot for you** | 为你拼一把；替你挡一枪 | *take a shot* 可指尝试，也可指「挡子弹」式牺牲 |
| **like a heart needs a beat** | 像心脏需要跳动 | 明喻：需要到「活着离不开」的程度 |
| **it's nothing new** | 早就不是新鲜事了 | 疲惫、看透；暗示伤害反复发生 |
| **loved you with a fire red** | 曾用火红的热情爱你 | *fire red* = 炽热；后接 *turning blue* 表热情冷却 |
| **turning blue** | 变成蓝色；冷却、熄灭 | 与 *fire red* 对比；也可联想 bruising / 冷感 |
| **say sorry like the angel** | 道歉时像天使一样 | 外表无辜、语气柔软；暗示表演感 |
| **I'm afraid** | 我害怕了；恐怕…… | 此处偏「我不敢再信了」，不是礼貌套话 *I'm afraid that…* |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *it's too late to apologize* 极短、极重复，**KTV / 通勤跟唱**友好；注意 *apologize* 重音在第二音节（a-**pol**-o-gize）。
- Verse 1 的 *holding on your rope / ten feet off the ground* 是整首歌的**悬空意象**：先听清画面，再跟唱节奏。
- Pre-Chorus 里 *need me → cut me down → you're sorry* 三连转折，适合练**叙事语气**与情绪递进。
- *I need you like a heart needs a beat* 是经典明喻句，可单独抽出来练节奏与连读（*like a* → /ˈlaɪkə/）。
- 想延伸练**分手、拒绝道歉、受伤后的边界感**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
