## 歌手简介 · About Lady Gaga & Bruno Mars

**English**

**Lady Gaga** (Stefani Germanotta, born 28 March 1986, New York) is an American **singer-songwriter** and actress whose career spans **dance-pop spectacle** (*The Fame*, *"Bad Romance"*) and stripped-down **jazz and ballad** work (*Joanne*, *A Star Is Born*). She has won **13 Grammy Awards**, an **Oscar** for **"Shallow"**, and is known for theatrical live shows and advocacy for mental health and LGBTQ+ rights.

**Bruno Mars** (Peter Hernandez, born 8 October 1985, Honolulu) is an American **singer-songwriter**, producer, and retro-styled **pop-funk** showman. Hits include **"Uptown Funk"**, **"Just the Way You Are"**, and **"When I Was Your Man"**. He has won **15 Grammy Awards** and is celebrated for tight harmonies, Motown-influenced arrangements, and effortless stage charisma.

Together on **"Die With a Smile"** (2024), they blend Gaga's emotional belt with Mars's smooth falsetto into a **软摇滚抒情曲** about loving fiercely because tomorrow is not promised.

**中文**

**Lady Gaga**（本名 Stefani Germanotta，1986 年 3 月 28 日生，纽约）是美国**创作型歌手**与演员，生涯横跨**舞曲流行奇观**（*The Fame*、*"Bad Romance"*）与更质朴的**爵士与 ballad**（*Joanne*、《一个明星的诞生》）。获 **13 项格莱美**、**奥斯卡**（**"Shallow"**），以戏剧化现场与心理健康、LGBTQ+ 权益倡导著称。

**Bruno Mars**（本名 Peter Hernandez，1985 年 10 月 8 日生，檀香山）是美国**创作型歌手**、制作人，复古风**流行放克**舞台高手。代表作 **"Uptown Funk"**、**"Just the Way You Are"**、**"When I Was Your Man"**。获 **15 项格莱美**，以紧密和声、Motown 影响编曲与轻松舞台魅力闻名。

两人在 **"Die With a Smile"**（2024）中将 Gaga 的情感高音与 Mars 顺滑假声融合，写成一首关于「明天不保证，所以要拼命爱」的 **软摇滚抒情曲**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Die With a Smile"** was released on **16 August 2024** as a standalone single—a surprise collaboration between two of pop's biggest stars. Co-written by Gaga, Mars, Dern, Bublé, and Ronson, it opens with a dream of goodbye, then commits to **carpe diem** romance: *Nobody's promised tomorrow / So I'ma love you every night like it's the last night*. The chorus imagines apocalyptic scenarios—the world ending, the party over—yet chooses closeness: *I'd wanna hold you just for a while and die with a smile*. The song debuted at **No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and won the **Grammy for Song of the Year** (2025). Musically it echoes 1970s **soft rock** and piano ballads while staying radio-friendly.

**中文**

**"Die With a Smile"** 于 **2024 年 8 月 16 日**以独立单曲形式发行——流行乐坛两位巨星的惊喜合作。由 Gaga、Mars、Dern、Bublé、Ronson 等共同创作，以一场告别的梦开场，随后宣誓**活在当下**的爱：*Nobody's promised tomorrow / So I'ma love you every night like it's the last night*。副歌设想世界末日、派对散场等场景，仍选择紧紧相依：*I'd wanna hold you just for a while and die with a smile*。歌曲空降 **Billboard Hot 100 冠军**，并获 **格莱美「年度歌曲」**（2025）。音乐上呼应 1970 年代 **软摇滚** 与钢琴抒情曲，同时保持电台友好。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **die with a smile** | 微笑着离去 | 歌题；指在爱里无悔、带着笑意面对终点 |
| **nobody's promised tomorrow** | 没人能保证有明天 | 珍惜当下的核心句；*promise* = 保证给予 |
| **I'ma** | I am going to | 口语缩约；*I'ma love you* = 我会爱你 |
| **like it's the last night** | 像最后一晚那样 | 把每一晚当最后一晚来爱 |
| **the world was ending** | 世界即将终结 | 虚拟语气；假设末日场景 |
| **our time on Earth was through** | 我们在世上的时光已尽 | *through* = 结束、用完 |
| **just for a while** | 就一会儿 | 不求永恒，只求此刻相拥 |
| **next to you** | 紧挨着你；在你身旁 | 副歌关键词；物理上的贴近 |
| **lost in the words** | 迷失在话语里 | 争吵到忘了初衷 |
| **what you mean to me** | 你对我意味着什么 | 比 *I love you* 更深一层 |
| **the only war worth fighting for** | 唯一值得为之奋战的那场战争 | 把爱情比作值得打的仗 |
| **since I survived** | 既然我（从梦中）活过来了 | *survive* 此处指醒来、活下来 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- Gaga 与 Mars **交替领唱**：主歌 1 偏 Gaga，主歌 2 偏 Mars——跟唱前先标出「谁唱哪句」，避免抢拍。
- *I'ma love you every night like it's the last night* 连读多：*I'ma* 轻读，重音落在 *last night*。
- 副歌 *If the world was ending* 用过去式表假设（非真实），语法上类似第二条件句的口语版。
- 钢琴与吉他编配偏 70 年代软摇滚，适合**通勤**听熟旋律后再做 KTV 跟唱；尾声的 *next to you* 重复适合练尾音收束。
