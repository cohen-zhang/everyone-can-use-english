## 歌手简介 · About Hugh Grant & Drew Barrymore

**English**

This track features **Hugh Grant** and **Drew Barrymore** in their roles from the 2007 romantic comedy **Music and Lyrics**. Grant plays **Alex Fletcher**, a washed-up 1980s pop star struggling to write a new hit; Barrymore plays **Sophie Fisher**, a quirky lyricist who helps him find his voice again—and fall in love. The **demo version** in the film is the rough, piano-led duet they sing together while co-writing; the finished single in the story is performed by Alex and **Cora Corman** (played by Haley Bennett). The song was written by **Adam Schlesinger** of Fountains of Wayne, who crafted many witty, melody-rich pop songs for film and TV. Grant and Barrymore are actors, not professional singers—the slightly imperfect, intimate demo performance is part of the film's charm.

**中文**

本曲出自 2007 年浪漫喜剧 **《K歌情人》（Music and Lyrics）**，由 **Hugh Grant** 与 **Drew Barrymore** 以片中人声演唱。**Hugh Grant** 饰演过气 1980 年代流行歌手 **Alex Fletcher**，正努力写新歌；**Drew Barrymore** 饰演古怪填词人 **Sophie Fisher**，帮他找回创作力，也擦出恋情。片中的 **demo 版**是两人边写边唱的粗糙钢琴二重唱；故事里完成版则由 Alex 与 **Cora Corman**（Haley Bennett 饰）演唱。歌曲由 Fountains of Wayne 的 **Adam Schlesinger** 创作，他常为影视写旋律抓耳的流行歌。Grant 与 Barrymore 是演员而非职业歌手——小样里略带瑕疵、亲密感的演唱正是电影魅力之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Way Back Into Love"** anchors *Music and Lyrics*: a ballad about feeling **stuck after heartbreak**—*living with a shadow overhead*, *trapped in the past*—and wanting to **find a way back into love** rather than settle for someone who only gets you through the night. The lyrics use gentle metaphors (shadow, cloud, stars that refuse to shine) and repeat the hopeful chorus: *All I wanna do is find a way back into love*. In the film's **demo scene**, Alex and Sophie sit at a piano in his apartment and **build the song line by line**—he sings a verse, she answers with hers, and the chorus grows out of that co-writing moment. That rough, intimate **demo duet** (not the later studio single with pop diva Cora Corman) marks when they truly connect as writers and as people. The soundtrack album was released **13 February 2007**; this demo version remains a fan favourite for its sincerity and sing-along melody.

**中文**

**"Way Back Into Love"** 是《K歌情人》的情感核心：一首抒情曲，写**心碎后走不出来**——*living with a shadow overhead*、*trapped in the past*——却渴望**重新坠入爱河**，而不是将就一个只能陪你过夜的人。歌词用轻柔隐喻（头顶阴影、床上方乌云、星星不肯亮）衬托副歌里反复的希望：*All I wanna do is find a way back into love*。片中 Alex 与 Sophie 在公寓**钢琴边即兴写歌**——她哼旋律、他配和弦，一句句对出来直到副歌成型；这支粗糙的 **demo 二重唱**（即本曲）标志着两人作为创作伙伴、也作为彼此的真正靠近，之后故事里才有 Cora Corman 演唱的完成版。原声带于 **2007 年 2 月 13 日**发行；小样因真挚、易跟唱而深受喜爱。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a shadow overhead** | 头顶的阴影 | 比喻挥之不去的低落情绪 |
| **a cloud above my bed** | 床上方的乌云 | 与 shadow 呼应，写失眠与压抑 |
| **trapped in the past** | 困在过去 | 固定搭配；*move on* = 走出来、放下 |
| **move on** | 走出来；继续生活 | 分手 / 创伤后常用 |
| **set aside time** | 腾出时间 | 正式又口语；*set aside* = 留出 |
| **clear a little space in my mind** | 清出心里的一点空间 | 比喻整理思绪、放下包袱 |
| **find a way back into love** | 找到重新坠入爱河的路 | 歌题核心；*way back into* = 回到……状态 |
| **get me through the night** | 陪我熬过这一夜 | 暗示短暂陪伴，非真爱 |
| **shed some light** | 照亮；点拨 | 习语；*shed light on* 的变体 |
| **open my heart (again / to you)** | （再次 / 向你）敞开心扉 | 情感重建的关键句 |
| **I need inspiration** | 我需要灵感 | 片里写歌语境；比 *motivation* 更带「被点亮」感 |
| **not just another negotiation** | 不是又一场讨价还价 | *negotiation* = 谈条件；对比真心与算计 |
| **be there for me / for you in the end** | 最终陪在我身边 / 在你身边 | 承诺长期陪伴 |
| **I can't make it through** | 我撑不下去 | 副歌核心；*without a way back into love* 为前提 |
| **the stars refuse to shine** | 星星迟迟不亮 | 比喻希望迟迟不来；*refuse to* 拟人化 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 本片小样是**钢琴 + 双人合唱**，适合练**交替领唱**：奇数主歌由 Alex（Hugh）领，偶数主歌由 Sophie（Drew）领，副歌两人合 *Oh oh oh*。
- 片中场景是 Alex 公寓里**边写边唱**——可对照歌词体会「一句他、一句她」如何拼成完整歌；与 Cora 完成版的 polished 录音形成对比。
- 大量 **I've been + -ing** 结构（*I've been living / sleeping / hiding*），可用来练「一直……」的叙事口吻。
- 副歌 *way back into love* 中 *back into* 连读明显，建议先慢速拆读再原速跟唱。
- 想对照影视语境，可留意小样是「写歌过程」而非舞台演出——语气偏私密、试探。
