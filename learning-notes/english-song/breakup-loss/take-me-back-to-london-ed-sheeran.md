## 歌手简介 · About Ed Sheeran & Stormzy

**English**

**Ed Sheeran** (born 17 February 1991, Halifax, England) is a British **singer-songwriter** who moved to **London** in 2008 and built his career from small gigs and EPs, including the grime-heavy *No. 5 Collaborations Project* (2011). By 2019 he was a global stadium act with multiple Grammys; *No. 6 Collaborations Project* brought him back to the **UK rap / grime** world he came up in.

**Stormzy** (Michael Owuo Jr., born 26 July 1993, Croydon, South London) is a British **grime MC** and one of the UK's biggest rap stars. His 2017 debut *Gang Signs & Prayer* made history as the first grime album to reach **No. 1** on the UK Albums Chart; he headlined **Glastonbury** in 2019—the first solo black British artist to do so. Known for baritone flow, social commentary, and South London pride, he represents **grime** culture on a global stage while staying rooted in home.

**中文**

**Ed Sheeran**（1991 年 2 月 17 生于英格兰 Halifax）是英国**创作型歌手**，2008 年搬至**伦敦**，从小场地演出和 EP 起家，2011 年的 *No. 5 Collaborations Project* 已大量合作 **grime** 艺人。至 2019 年已是全球体育馆巡演级别的巨星；*No. 6 Collaborations Project* 让他再次回到英国说唱/ grime 圈。

**Stormzy**（Michael Owuo Jr.，1993 年 7 月 26 生于伦敦南部 Croydon）是英国 **grime MC**，国内最具代表性的说唱明星之一。2017 年首张专辑 *Gang Signs & Prayer* 成为首张登顶英国专辑榜的 grime 专辑；2019 年**领衔 Glastonbury**——首位独自担纲 headline 的英国黑人艺人。以低沉 flow、社会议题与南伦敦认同著称，在全球舞台代表 **grime** 文化，同时强调「根在伦敦」。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Take Me Back to London"** was released on **12 July 2019** as a single from *No. 6 Collaborations Project*. Ed's hook is a **homecoming anthem**: jet planes, raves, bass-heavy nights—*take me back to London*. His verse admits the cost of fame (*grossed half a billi on the Divide tour*) and the pull of a simple pub night with mates. Stormzy's grime verses rep **South London** street culture—slang, status, and loyalty—while the closing section names **BRITs**, **Glastonbury**, and the line *there ain't no place like home*. The track topped the **UK Singles Chart** for five weeks. Notably, Stormzy turned down a planned **Jay-Z** feature because he felt the song should stay a London story.

**中文**

**"Take Me Back to London"** 于 **2019 年 7 月 12 日**发行，收录于 *No. 6 Collaborations Project*。Ed 的 hook 是一首**归乡 anthem**：飞机、锐舞派对、低音震天的夜——*take me back to London*。他的段落承认成名的代价（*Divide 巡演赚了半个 billion*），也写只想和老友去 pub 喝一杯的简单渴望。Stormzy 的 grime 段落代表**南伦敦**街头文化——俚语、地位与忠诚；结尾提到 **全英音乐奖**、**Glastonbury**，并以 *there ain't no place like home* 收束。单曲在英国单曲榜**连冠五周**。趣闻：Stormzy 曾拒绝原定参与的 **Jay-Z**，认为这首歌应保持「伦敦故事」的 intimate 气质。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- Ed 的 hook 旋律清楚，适合**跟唱**练连读：*take me back to* 常连读成 *take againme back t'*。
- Stormzy 段落是**英式 grime flow**，语速快、俚语多；建议先读「重点表达」表再回听 Verse。
- 注意 **英美式差异**：*crisps*（薯片）、*pub*、*pint* 都是典型英式生活词。
- 想延伸练**思乡与归属**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于 comfort / home 的表达；同专辑拉丁风曲目见 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/south-of-the-border-ed-sheeran|South of the Border]]。
