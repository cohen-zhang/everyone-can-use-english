## 歌手简介 · About Sarah Connor

**English**

**Sarah Connor** (born Sarah Terenzi, 13 June 1980 in Delmenhorst, Germany) is a German **pop / R&B** singer who rose to fame in the early 2000s with English-language hits across Europe. Breakthrough singles like *"From Sarah with Love"* and *"Music Is the Key"* topped the German charts; she has sold millions of records and remains one of Germany's most successful crossover pop stars. **"Just One Last Dance"** features guest vocals from **Natural**, the American boy band that included her then-husband **Marc Terenzi**. Connor later expanded into German-language material and television, but her early-2000s English ballads remain staples of European pop radio nostalgia.

**中文**

**Sarah Connor**（本名 Sarah Terenzi，1980 年 6 月 13 日生于德国 Delmenhorst）是德国**流行 / R&B** 歌手，2000 年代初以英语单曲风靡欧洲。*"From Sarah with Love"*、*"Music Is the Key"* 等打上德国榜首；销量数百万，是德国最成功的跨界流行明星之一。**"Just One Last Dance"** 特邀美国男孩团 **Natural** 合唱——团员包括她当时的丈夫 **Marc Terenzi**。Connor 后来转向德语作品与电视，但 2000 年代初的英语抒情曲仍是欧洲流行电台怀旧常客。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Just One Last Dance"** was written and produced by **Rob Tyger** and **Kay Denar** for Connor's third album ***Key to My Soul*** (17 November 2003). A **duet version featuring Natural** was released as a single on **1 March 2004** and hit **No. 1** in Germany (Gold-certified). Lyrically it is a bittersweet farewell in a **Spanish café**: one last dance before sunrise forces the lovers apart—*hold me tight and keep me warm / 'cause the night is getting cold*. The video (West Side Story–tinged) casts Connor and Terenzi as star-crossed teens. Ideal for KTV: clear chorus, romantic vocabulary, medium difficulty.

**中文**

**"Just One Last Dance"** 由 **Rob Tyger** 与 **Kay Denar** 创作制作，收录于 Connor 第三张专辑 ***Key to My Soul***（2003 年 11 月 17 日）。**与 Natural 合唱的单曲版**于 **2004 年 3 月 1 日**发行，登上德国榜 **第 1**（金唱片）。歌词写**西班牙咖啡馆**里的苦甜告别：日出前再跳最后一支舞——*hold me tight and keep me warm / 'cause the night is getting cold*（抱紧我、给我温暖，因为夜越来越冷）。MV 带 *West Side Story* 气质，Connor 与 Terenzi 饰演不被祝福的少年恋人。副歌清晰、浪漫词汇多，**中等难度**，适合 KTV。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Just one last dance / Just one more chance* 对仗整齐，**KTV 先抓这两句**再练主歌叙事。
- *'Cause* = because；*'til* = until——跟唱时注意缩略，不必改成书面全拼。
- *drowning in salty water* 是泪水意象，比直说 *I'm crying* 更有画面感，可整句背。
- 想延伸练**告别与挽留**口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
