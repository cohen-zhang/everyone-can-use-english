## 歌手简介 · About Richard Marx

**English**

**Richard Marx** (born 16 September 1963) is an American **singer-songwriter** from Chicago, Illinois. He emerged in the late 1980s with a string of **power ballads** and pop-rock hits—*Don't Mean Nothing*, *Hold On to the Nights*, *Should've Known Better*—and wrote songs for **Luther Vandross**, **NSYNC**, and others. Marx is also a prolific **producer and backing vocalist** (e.g. on Lionel Richie and Madonna sessions). He has sold over **30 million records** worldwide. In Chinese-speaking regions he is widely known as **理查德·马克斯**; *Right Here Waiting* remains one of the most recognised English love songs in Asia.

**中文**

**Richard Marx**（理查德·马克斯，1963 年 9 月 16 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**，来自伊利诺伊州芝加哥。1980 年代末以一系列**强力抒情民谣**与流行摇滚热曲走红——*Don't Mean Nothing*、*Hold On to the Nights*、*Should've Known Better*——并为 **Luther Vandross**、**NSYNC** 等艺人写歌。他也是多产的**制作人兼和声**（如参与 Lionel Richie、Madonna 录音）。全球唱片销量逾 **3000 万**。华语地区常称**理查德·马克斯**；*Right Here Waiting* 仍是亚洲最具辨识度的英文情歌之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Right Here Waiting"** was released on **29 June 1989** as the second single from Marx's album **Repeat Offender**. He wrote it for his then-girlfriend (later wife) **Cynthia Rhodes**, an actress filming overseas—he could not visit and poured his **long-distance longing** into the piano ballad. The chorus promise—*Wherever you go, whatever you do, I will be right here waiting for you*—became a global standard for separation and devotion. It reached **No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and topped charts worldwide. The song is slow, melodic, and vocabulary-friendly for learners.

**中文**

**"Right Here Waiting"** 于 **1989 年 6 月 29 日**发行，为专辑 **Repeat Offender** 第二支单曲。Marx 为当时女友（后妻）**Cynthia Rhodes** 而作——对方在国外拍戏，他无法探班，把**异地恋的思念**写进这首钢琴民谣。副歌誓言——*Wherever you go, whatever you do, I will be right here waiting for you*——成为全球分离与守候的代表句。单曲登顶 **Billboard Hot 100** 并在多国夺冠。节奏舒缓、旋律上口、用词友好，适合学习者。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **oceans apart** | 远隔重洋 | 形容异地恋距离 |
| **go insane** | 快要发疯 | 夸张表达思念之苦 |
| **on the line** | 在（电话）线上 | *hear your voice on the line* |
| **next to never** | 几乎从不；几乎无望 | = *hardly ever*，略书面 |
| **right here waiting** | 就在这里等 | 歌名核心；*right here* 强调「原地」 |
| **whatever it takes** | 无论付出什么代价 | 决心句型，口语高频 |
| **heart breaks** | 心碎 | *how my heart breaks* |
| **take for granted** | 视作理所当然 | 后悔时常用 |
| **going crazy** | 快要疯了 | 比 *insane* 口语；表思念失控 |
| **take the chance** | 赌一把；抓住机会 | 甘愿承担异地恋风险 |
| **this romance** | 这段感情 | *survive this romance* = 撑过这段恋情 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Wherever you go…* 旋律舒缓、句型整齐，适合 **KTV 与通勤** 跟唱；先练副歌再练主歌。
- Marx 的咬字清楚，*waiting / whatever / forever* 等词可练 **-ing / -er** 弱读。
- *next to never* 与 *take for granted* 是歌词外也好用的**书面口语**表达，可记入作文或邮件。
- 想延伸「等待 + 后悔」主题，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
