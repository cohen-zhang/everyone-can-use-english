## 歌手简介 · About Sam Smith

**English**

**Sam Smith** (born 19 May 1992, London) is an English **singer-songwriter** known for a soaring, soulful voice and emotionally direct lyrics about love, heartbreak, and identity. They broke through in 2012 featuring on Disclosure's **"Latch"**, then dominated 2014 with debut album **In the Lonely Hour**, which sold over 14 million copies worldwide and won **four Grammy Awards**, including Record and Song of the Year for **"Stay With Me"**. Smith's style blends **pop, soul, and R&B**; their ballads often sit in a confessional register—vulnerable, theatrical, and precise. Later hits include **"Too Good at Goodbyes"**, **"Unholy"**, and the Oscar-winning **"Writing's on the Wall"** (*Spectre*). They use **they/them** pronouns.

**中文**

**Sam Smith**（1992 年 5 月 19 日生，伦敦）是英国**创作型歌手**，以高亢、富有灵魂感的嗓音和直白写爱情、心碎与自我认同的歌词著称。2012 年因与 Disclosure 合作 **"Latch"** 崭露头角；2014 年首张专辑 **In the Lonely Hour** 全球销量逾 1400 万，获 **四项格莱美**，包括 **"Stay With Me"** 的「年度制作」与「年度歌曲」。风格融合 **流行、灵魂乐与 R&B**；抒情曲常带自白气质——脆弱、戏剧化又精准。后续代表作有 **"Too Good at Goodbyes"**、**"Unholy"** 及奥斯卡获奖曲 **"Writing's on the Wall"**（《007：幽灵党》）。Smith 使用 **they/them** 人称代词。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Like I Can"** was released on **26 May 2014** as the fourth single from *In the Lonely Hour*. Co-written by Smith with **Jimmy Napes** and **James Napier**, it is a tense **jealousy ballad**: the singer lists all the men their lover might choose—a gentleman, a preacher, a lawyer, a one-night stand—then insists none will love her **like I can**. The central metaphor *mine is the heart and the salt of the soul* claims depth others lack. The bridge admits shared flaws (*We both have demons*) but reframes them as intimacy (*I love your demons*). The song peaked at **No. 9** on the UK Singles Chart and became a fan favourite for its dramatic build and Smith's vocal runs on the repeated *can, can, can*.

**中文**

**"Like I Can"** 于 **2014 年 5 月 26 日**发行，为 *In the Lonely Hour* 第四支单曲。由 Smith 与 **Jimmy Napes**、**James Napier** 共同创作，是一首紧绷的**嫉妒 ballad**：歌手列举恋人可能选择的各类男人——绅士、牧师、律师、一夜情对象——然后坚称没人能**像我这样**爱她。核心比喻 *mine is the heart and the salt of the soul* 宣称自己才有灵魂的深度与滋味。桥段承认彼此都有心魔（*We both have demons*），却把它重述为亲密（*I love your demons*）。歌曲在英国单曲榜最高 **第 9**，因情绪层层推进及副歌 *can, can, can* 上的转音而成为歌迷最爱之一。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **like I can** | 像我（这样）能（爱） | 歌题核心；*love you like I can* = 以我能做到的方式爱你 |
| **sinner / gentleman** | 罪人 / 绅士 | 对比「坏男人」与「体面男人」 |
| **soul is damned** | 灵魂遭谴 | 宗教色彩；*damned* = 被诅咒、下地狱 |
| **witness stand** | 证人席 | 法庭用语；*on a witness stand* |
| **one-night stand** | 一夜情（对象） | 名词短语；非「站一夜」 |
| **second glance** | 再看一眼 | *give someone a second glance* = 多留意一下 |
| **trophy** | 战利品 | 此处指被炫耀、不认真的关系对象 |

| **the salt of the soul** | 灵魂的核心滋味 | 比喻最深、最本质的部分；*salt* = 精华 |
| **hold out their hands** | 伸出手（示好） | 恋人主动靠近的姿态 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *never love you like I can* 的 **can** 常唱成三连音（*can, can, can*），跟唱时把最后一个 *can* 做轻处理或转音，别每个都咬死。
- 主歌 1 的排比 *He could be…* 适合练**虚拟语气式列举**：先练四个职业/身份，再一口气接 *But he'll never…*。
- *mine is the heart and the salt of the soul* 是全歌最难记的一句隐喻——可先理解「盐 = 灵魂里最有滋味、最本质的部分」再跟唱。
- 歌曲短（约 2:48），情绪集中，适合**精研**时反复听桥段里 *demons / devils* 的双关与语气转折。
