## 歌手简介 · About Gabby Barrett

**English**

**Gabby Barrett** (born 5 March 2000) is an American **country singer-songwriter** from Munhall, Pennsylvania. She finished **third on American Idol** in 2018, married fellow Idol alum **Cade Foehner**, and became a leading voice in modern **country-pop**. Her debut album **Goldmine** (2020) produced the six-week **No. 1** hit **"I Hope"**; her second album **Chapter & Verse** (2024) reflects her life as a Christian, wife, mother, and artist. Barrett co-writes much of her material and is known for big-voiced, emotionally direct storytelling.

**中文**

**Gabby Barrett**（2000 年 3 月 5 日生）是美国**乡村创作歌手**，来自宾州 Munhall。2018 年 **《美国偶像》** 季军，后与同届选手 **Cade Foehner** 结婚，成为当代 **乡村流行** 代表之一。首张专辑 **Goldmine**（2020）诞生连冠六周的 **"I Hope"**；第二张 **Chapter & Verse**（2024）书写她作为基督徒、妻子、母亲与艺人的当下人生。Barrett 大量参与创作，以嗓音饱满、情感直白的叙事著称。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Dance Like No One's Watching"** was released on **2 February 2024** on *Chapter & Verse*. **Luke Combs** co-wrote it with Emily Weisband and James McNair and sent it to Barrett because it tells a **father-daughter** story across three life stages: a nervous seven-year-old at a school dance, a teenager leaving for college, and a grown daughter coaching her dad at her **wedding**. The chorus advice—*dance like no one's watchin', fall in if life gives you love, but always be you*—echoes through every chapter. Combs adds backing vocals on the recording.

**中文**

**"Dance Like No One's Watching"** 于 **2024 年 2 月 2 日**收录于 *Chapter & Verse*。**Luke Combs** 与 Emily Weisband、James McNair 共创后送给 Barrett，因歌写**父女**一生三幕：七岁在学校舞会紧张、少女离家上大学、成年后在**婚礼**上反过来安慰父亲。副歌忠告——*没人看时就跳舞，遇见爱就投入，但永远做自己*——贯穿每个阶段。Combs 在录音中担任和声。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **dance like no one's watching** | 没人看时就跳舞 | 歌题；引申为「别在意眼光，做自己」 |
| **holdin' my hand** | 牵着我的手 | *holdin'* = holding 口语缩略 |
| **stand on his feet** | 踩着他的脚跳 | 父女舞会经典画面 |
| **fall in** | 投入（爱情） | *if life gives you love, fall in* |
| **always be you** | 永远做自己 | 副歌核心忠告 |
| **get lost in** | 迷失于 | *it's so easy to get lost in* = 世界太大容易迷失 |
| **tape up the box** | 封好箱子 | 搬家/离家意象 |
| **shut the trunk** | 关上后备箱 | 送子女上路的常见场景 |
| **have a ball** | 玩得痛快 | 口语；*go have a ball* |
| **hold it together** | 绷住；忍住不哭 | 婚礼父亲常需要 *hold it together* |
| **two left feet** | 笨脚；不会跳舞 | 习语；形容舞步笨拙 |
| **spin me around** | 转我一圈 | 婚礼父女舞常见动作 |
| **never forget** | 永远别忘了 | 父亲送别叮嘱；接副歌 *dance like no one's watchin'* |
| **pretend it's just you and me** | 假装只有我们俩 | 父亲七岁舞会安慰；减轻社交焦虑 |
| **it's a big world** | 世界很大 | 副歌核心意象；提醒女儿人生辽阔、易迷失 |
| **hour ahead** | 早一个时区 | 离家上大学；*you're an hour ahead* |
| **let us know you got there** | 到了告诉我们 | 父亲送别时的叮嘱句型 |
| **don't drink too much** | 别喝太多 | 送别叮嘱；口语很实用 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *dance like no one's watchin'* 在童年、离家、婚礼三幕各出现一次，适合练**叙事跟唱**：注意第三段改叫 *Daddy*，且 *spin me around* 是父女舞经典动作。
- *two left feet* 是常用习语，可单独记；*hold it together* 在婚礼、葬礼等场合都很实用。
- 三幕结构（七岁舞会 → 离家上大学 → 婚礼父女舞）可用来练**同一副歌在不同人生阶段的语气变化**。
- 乡村口音中 *watchin'*、*holdin'* 的 **-ing 省略** 很自然，跟唱时可模仿。
- *always be you* 与 *fall in* 可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于自我边界与人生态度的表达。
