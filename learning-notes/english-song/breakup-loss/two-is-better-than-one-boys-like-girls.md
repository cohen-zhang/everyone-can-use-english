## 歌手简介 · About BOYS LIKE GIRLS (feat. Taylor Swift)

**English**

**Boys Like Girls** is an American **pop rock** band formed in **Boston** in 2005, led by singer-songwriter **Martin Johnson**. Their self-titled debut (2006) and second album **Love Drunk** (2009) blended **emo-pop**, **power pop**, and arena-rock hooks, with hits like **"The Great Escape"** and **"Love Drunk"**. Johnson co-wrote **"Two Is Better Than One"** with **Taylor Swift**, who had recently opened for the band on tour and was rising fast after *Fearless*. Swift sings the **female vocal and harmonies** on the studio recording—listen for her on the chorus and outro, not a separate featured verse. The band went on hiatus in the 2010s; Johnson later co-wrote hits for other artists and formed **The Night Game**. Swift, meanwhile, became one of the biggest pop songwriters of her generation.

**中文**

**Boys Like Girls** 是 2005 年在**波士顿**成立的美国**流行摇滚**乐队，核心为创作型主唱 **Martin Johnson**。首张同名专辑（2006）与第二张 **Love Drunk**（2009）融合 **emo-pop**、**强力流行** 与体育场式副歌，代表曲包括 **"The Great Escape"**、**"Love Drunk"**。Johnson 与当时刚为乐队做巡演嘉宾、凭 *Fearless* 快速走红的 **Taylor Swift** 合写 **"Two Is Better Than One"**；录音版里 Swift 负责**女声与和声**（副歌与 Outro 最明显，并非独立 featured verse）。乐队 2010 年代暂停活动；Johnson 后为他人创作热门曲，并组建 **The Night Game**。Swift 则在此后成为一代最具影响力的流行创作人之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Two Is Better Than One"** was released on **8 September 2009** as the second single from *Love Drunk*. Johnson and **Taylor Swift** wrote it together after she opened for Boys Like Girls on tour; on the studio recording Swift sings the **female vocal and harmonies** (most audible on the chorus and outro—not a separate featured verse). It is a **mid-tempo pop ballad** about falling hard for someone you did not expect—*I remember what you wore on the first day*—and realizing you **cannot live without them**, even while telling yourself there is *so much time to figure out the rest of my life*. The title hook *Two is better than one* is simple but sticky; Swift's harmonies add a tender counterpoint to Johnson's lead. The song peaked at **No. 18** on the Billboard Hot 100 and became a staple of late-2000s acoustic-pop playlists and wedding covers.

**中文**

**"Two Is Better Than One"** 于 **2009 年 9 月 8 日**发行，为 *Love Drunk* 第二支单曲。Johnson 与 **Taylor Swift** 在 Swift 为乐队做巡演嘉宾后合写此曲；录音版中 Swift 负责**女声与和声**（副歌与 Outro 尤其明显，并非独立 featured verse，但辨识度很高）。这是一首**中速流行抒情曲**，写对意外心动对象的沦陷——*I remember what you wore on the first day*——并发现自己**离不开对方**，尽管嘴上还说人生还有很多时间 *to figure out the rest of my life*。歌名钩子 *Two is better than one* 简单好记；Swift 的和声为 Johnson 主唱添了一层温柔。单曲在 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 18** 位，成为 2000 年代末原声流行歌单与婚礼翻唱常客。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **what you wore on the first day** | 你第一天穿的什么 | 回忆初见；浪漫叙事开场 |
| **this could be something** | 这可能会发展成什么 | 口语；暗示恋情可能开始 |
| **take my breath away** | 让我屏息；惊艳到我 | 经典表达强烈心动 |
| **I'm left with nothing** | 我心里什么都不剩 | 语境里指「满心都是你」 |
| **I can't live without you** | 我离不开你 | 情歌高频句；注意 *can't* 弱读 |
| **two is better than one** | 两个人在一起更好 | 歌题；谚语感，易跟唱 |
| **figure out the rest of my life** | 弄清余下的人生 | *figure out* = 想明白、搞定 |
| **got me coming undone** | 让我溃不成军 | *come undone* = 情绪 / 防线崩溃 |
| **roll your eyes** | 翻白眼 | 写熟悉感与小动作 |
| **make it hard for breathing** | 让我呼吸困难 | 夸张写心动；口语常省略 *me* |
| **drift away** | 神游；飘走 | *close my eyes and drift away* = 闭眼放空 |
| **I'm finally now believing** | 我终于开始相信了 | 从怀疑到确信的转折 |
| **when all that's said and done** | 当一切尘埃落定 | 习语；总结、下结论时用 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌旋律平缓，**通勤跟听 + 跟唱**都很友好；先熟 *Two is better than one* 再练 Pre-Chorus 长句。
- **Taylor Swift** 的和声在第二遍副歌与 Outro 最明显（*'Cause baby, two is better than one*），可先听清她的层再跟 Johnson 主唱。
- *takes my breath away*、*coming undone* 两处适合练**连读与重音**：breath / undone 可略拖长。
- **feat. Taylor Swift** 段落人声轻柔，适合练男女声交替：Johnson 主唱 Verse，Swift 和声叠在副歌 *two is better than one*。
- 想延伸 Taylor Swift 同类型抒情表达，可打开 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/you-belong-with-me-taylor-swift|You Belong With Me]] 对照 *I can't live without you* 与「暗恋 / 认定对方」叙事。
