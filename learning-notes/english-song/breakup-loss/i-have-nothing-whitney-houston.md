## 歌手简介 · About Whitney Houston

**English**

**Whitney Houston** (1963–2012) was an American **R&B / pop** singer and actress, often called **the Voice** for her power, range, and control. Born in **Newark, New Jersey**, into a gospel family (mother **Cissy Houston**; cousins include **Dionne Warwick**), she signed with Arista and exploded in the mid-1980s with hits like *"Saving All My Love for You"*, *"How Will I Know"*, and *"I Wanna Dance with Somebody"*. Her role in *The Bodyguard* (1992) and its soundtrack—led by *"I Will Always Love You"*—made her a global icon. She won multiple **Grammys**, sold over 200 million records, and set the template for modern power-ballad singing.

**中文**

**Whitney Houston**（1963–2012）是美国 **R&B / 流行**歌手与演员，因嗓音力量、音域与控制力常被称作 **the Voice**。出生于**新泽西 Newark** 的福音音乐家庭（母亲 **Cissy Houston**；表姐包括 **Dionne Warwick**），签约 Arista 后于 1980 年代中期爆红，代表作有 *"Saving All My Love for You"*、*"How Will I Know"*、*"I Wanna Dance with Somebody"* 等。1992 年电影 *The Bodyguard* 及原声带（以 *"I Will Always Love You"* 领衔）让她成为全球偶像。多次获**格莱美**，唱片销量逾两亿，几乎定义了现代力量抒情曲的唱法。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Have Nothing"** is a **power ballad** from *The Bodyguard (Original Soundtrack Album)* (**17 November 1992**); it was released as the third single on **20 February 1993**. Written by **David Foster** and **Linda Thompson** and produced by Foster, it was an original song for the film—unlike the soundtrack's big covers. In the movie Whitney's character performs it on stage; on record it became a worldwide hit (US Hot 100 #4, Adult Contemporary #1) and earned an **Academy Award** nomination for Best Original Song. The lyric is a plea not to walk away: *I have nothing if I don't have you*—vulnerability wrapped in Whitney's soaring belts.

**中文**

**"I Have Nothing"** 是 *The Bodyguard* 原声带（**1992 年 11 月 17 日**）上的**力量抒情曲**，**1993 年 2 月 20 日**作为第三支单曲发行。由 **David Foster** 与 **Linda Thompson** 创作、Foster 制作，是为电影量身写的原创（不像原声带里几首大热翻唱）。片中 Whitney 饰演的角色在台上演唱；唱片版成为全球热单（美国 Hot 100 第 4、成人当代冠军），并获**奥斯卡**最佳原创歌曲提名。歌词是挽留的恳求：*I have nothing if I don't have you*——脆弱感裹在 Whitney 冲天的高音里。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **take me for what I am** | 接受真实的我 | *for what I am* = 按我本来的样子 |
| **change all my colors** | 改掉自己的底色/本性 | 比喻：不为对方伪装 |
| **ask for too much** | 要求太多 | 口语常见；反义 *not ask for much* |
| **look further** | 再往别处找 | *I don't need to look further* = 你就够了 |
| **where you don't follow** | 你不会跟着来的地方 | 条件/地点从句，练跟唱节奏 |
| **hold it back** | 压抑住（情绪/激情） | *hold back* = 忍住、克制 |
| **can't run from myself** | 逃不开自己 | 自我无法回避 |
| **nowhere to hide** | 无处可藏 | 固定搭配 |
| **close one more door** | 再关上另一扇门 | 比喻：再失去一次机会/关系 |
| **stay in my arms if you dare** | 你敢的话就留在我怀里 | *if you dare* 带挑战语气 |
| **must I imagine you there?** | 难道我只能想象你在？ | 修辞问句，挽留感极强 |
| **don't walk away from me** | 别离我而去 | *walk away from* = 抽身离开（关系） |
| **I have nothing if I don't have you** | 没有你我就一无所有 | 歌的核心句；条件句结构 |
| **see through (to the heart)** | 看穿（到内心） | *see through* 也可指「识破」 |
| **break down my walls** | 拆掉我的心墙 | 情感防御的常用比喻 |
| **hold on to** | 紧紧抓住（记忆/人） | *hold on to a memory* |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Don't make me close one more door* 是**KTV 高光句**：先小声跟节奏，再练 Whitney 式拉长音，别一上来就喊破。
- *I have nothing, nothing, nothing* 三连重复——练**重音落点**（nothing 的第一音节）。
- 预副歌句子偏长，适合练**意群断句**：*I don't wanna have to go / where you don't follow*。
- *Don't you dare…* 比 *Don't walk away* 更硬、更急；听最终段情绪如何升级。
- 想对照更多「挽留 / 离不开你」说法，见 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
