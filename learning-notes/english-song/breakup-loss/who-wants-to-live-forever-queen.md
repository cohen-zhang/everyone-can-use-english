## 歌手简介 · About Queen

**English**

**Queen** are a British **rock band** formed in London in 1970: **Freddie Mercury** (vocals), **Brian May** (guitar), **Roger Taylor** (drums), and **John Deacon** (bass). Blending **arena rock, pop, opera, and glam**, they became one of the world's best-selling acts, with anthems like *Bohemian Rhapsody*, *We Will Rock You*, and *We Are the Champions*. Mercury's dramatic vocals and May's layered guitar harmonies defined their sound. The band scored multiple UK No. 1 albums and a legendary **Live Aid** performance (1985). After Mercury's death in 1991, Queen's catalog endured; May and Taylor continue touring with guest vocalists. *Who Wants to Live Forever* showcases May's songwriting and the band's orchestral ballad side.

**中文**

**Queen** 是 1970 年成立于伦敦的英国**摇滚乐队**：**Freddie Mercury**（主唱）、**Brian May**（吉他）、**Roger Taylor**（鼓）、**John Deacon**（贝斯）。融合**竞技场摇滚、流行、歌剧与华丽摇滚**，成为全球最畅销乐队之一，代表作有 *Bohemian Rhapsody*、*We Will Rock You*、*We Are the Champions*。Mercury 戏剧化嗓音与 May 多层和声吉他构成标志性听感。多张英国冠军专辑与 **1985 Live Aid** 传奇现场使其名留青史。Mercury 1991 年去世后，作品影响延续；May 与 Taylor 至今与客座主唱巡演。*Who Wants to Live Forever* 体现 May 的创作与乐队管弦抒情面。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Who Wants to Live Forever"** was written by **Brian May** for the **1986 fantasy film** *Highlander*, in which immortal Connor MacLeod (Christopher Lambert) must watch his mortal wife Heather age and die in his arms. May conceived the melody and much of the lyric **in a cab ride home** after viewing an early cut of that scene—drawing on his own grief over his father's death and personal losses. The **power ballad** features Mercury on lead vocals (May sings the first verse on the album), backed by an **orchestra** arranged by **Michael Kamen**. Released on *A Kind of Magic* (June 1986), it peaked at **No. 24** in the UK. The refrain questions whether eternal life is worth the pain of outliving love: *Who wants to live forever when love must die?*

**中文**

**"Who Wants to Live Forever"** 由 **Brian May** 为 **1986 年**奇幻电影 *Highlander* 创作：不死者 Connor MacLeod 必须怀抱凡人妻子 Heather 看她老去、逝去。May 在看完该场粗剪后**乘车回家途中**几乎一气呵成旋律与大部分歌词——亦融入父亲去世等个人伤痛。这首**强力抒情慢歌**以 Mercury 主唱（专辑版第一段由 May 唱），**Michael Kamen** 编排的**管弦乐**伴奏。收录于 *A Kind of Magic*（1986 年 6 月），英国榜最高 **第 24**。副歌追问：若爱终将消逝，永生又有何意义？*Who wants to live forever when love must die?*

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **there's no time for us** | 我们没有时间 | 表 mortal / immortal 的时间不对等 |
| **slips away from us** | 从指间溜走 | *slip away* = 悄然逝去 |
| **who wants to live forever** | 谁想永生 | 歌题；反问表「永生未必是福」 |
| **it's all decided for us** | 一切早已注定 | 命运感；*decide for* = 替……决定 |
| **one sweet moment set aside** | 留给我们的一个甜蜜瞬间 | *set aside* = 预留 |
| **who dares to love forever** | 谁敢爱 forever | *dare* 强调勇气与风险 |
| **when love must die** | 当爱必须消逝 | 全曲情感核心 |
| **touch my tears with your lips** | 用双唇触碰我的泪 | 诗意、亲密的动作描写 |
| **touch my world with your fingertips** | 用指尖触碰我的世界 | 与上句平行结构 |
| **forever is our today** | 永远就是我们的今天 | 把「当下」等同于「永恒」 |
| **who waits forever anyway?** | 反正谁又会永远等？ | 结尾反诘，带宿命与无奈 |
| **builds our dreams** | 构筑我们的梦想 | 与 *slips away* 形成对比 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Who wants to live forever* 音域较高、拖长——适合 **KTV 前** 先哼旋律找音，再练词。
- 歌曲由慢到情绪爆发，Orchestra 与摇滚交织；精研时可留意 **May 吉他** 与弦乐进入的时机。
- *Forever is our today* 把抽象 *forever* 拉回「此刻」，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于 loss 与 acceptance 的表达。
- 电影 *Highlander* 中此曲配 Heather 离世场景；了解背景有助于理解 *when love must die* 为何如此沉重。
