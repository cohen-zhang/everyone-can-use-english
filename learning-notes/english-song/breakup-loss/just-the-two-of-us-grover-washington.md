## 歌手简介 · About Grover Washington, Jr. & Bill Withers

**English**

**Grover Washington, Jr.** (1943–1999) was an American **jazz-funk saxophonist** from Buffalo, New York, and one of the best-selling instrumental jazz artists of his era. Known for smooth, melodic tenor and soprano sax lines, he helped define **"smooth jazz"** in the 1970s–80s. **Bill Withers** (1938–2020) was a **singer-songwriter** from Slab Fork, West Virginia, who came to music late after nine years in the **Navy**. Hits like **"Ain't No Sunshine"**, **"Lean on Me"**, and **"Lovely Day"** made him a soul-folk icon with plainspoken, universal lyrics. On **"Just the Two of Us"**, Withers sings lead on Washington's **1980** track—Washington's sax carries the instrumental hook while Withers delivers the romantic vocal.

**中文**

**Grover Washington, Jr.**（1943－1999）是美国**爵士放克萨克斯手**，来自纽约州 Buffalo，是当年最畅销的爵士器乐艺人之一。以流畅、旋律感强的次中音与高音萨克斯著称，1970–80 年代助力定义 **「平滑爵士」**。**Bill Withers**（1938－2020）是**创作型歌手**，来自西弗吉尼亚 **Slab Fork**，在海军服役九年后才入行。**"Ain't No Sunshine"**、**"Lean on Me"**、**"Lovely Day"** 等让他成为灵魂／民谣偶像，歌词朴实 universal。在 **"Just the Two of Us"** 里，Withers 为 Washington **1980** 年作品主唱——Washington 的萨克斯负责器乐主旋律，Withers 演绎浪漫人声。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Just the Two of Us"** appeared on Grover Washington Jr.'s album **Winelight** (1980), with **Bill Withers** on vocals. Co-written by Withers, **Ralph MacDonald**, and **William Salter**, it is distinct from Withers' own 1981 song of the same title on *Bill Withers' Greatest Hits*—this version is the **smooth-jazz classic** most people know from radio. The lyric celebrates **intimacy and partnership**: raindrops, morning dew, and *building castles in the sky* paint a private world where only two people matter. The refrain *We can make it if we try* is an anthem of commitment. The single reached **No. 2** on the **Billboard Hot 100** and won a **Grammy** for Best R&B Song (1981).

**中文**

**"Just the Two of Us"** 收录于 Grover Washington Jr. 专辑 **Winelight**（1980），**Bill Withers** 主唱。由 Withers、**Ralph MacDonald**、**William Salter** 共同创作；与 Withers 1981 年同名曲（收录于精选集）不是同一首——本版是电台最常见的**平滑爵士经典**。歌词歌颂**亲密与二人世界**：雨滴、晨露、*building castles in the sky*（在天上筑城堡）描绘只属于两人的私密天地。副歌 *We can make it if we try*（只要我们努力就能成）是承诺感很强的金句。单曲在 **Billboard Hot 100** 最高 **第 2**，获 **格莱美最佳 R&B 歌曲**（1981）。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **crystal raindrops** | 晶莹的雨滴 | 诗意意象；crystal = 水晶般清澈 |
| **rainbows in my mind** | 脑海里的彩虹 | 想起爱人时心里的美好画面 |
| **just the two of us** | 就我们俩 | 歌题；强调二人世界 |
| **we can make it** | 我们能成／能撑过去 | *make it* = 成功、度过难关 |
| **building castles in the sky** | 在天上筑城堡 | 习语：不切实际的梦想；歌里偏浪漫幻想 |
| **no time for tears** | 没时间流泪 | 积极向前，不沉溺悲伤 |
| **wasted water** | 白白流掉的水 | 比喻无谓的眼泪 |
| **it don't make no flowers grow** | 它长不出花 | 双重否定口语；方言／非正式语法 |
| **good things come to those who wait** | 好事留给等待的人 | 谚语变体；歌里接 *wait too late* 转折 |
| **go for all we know** | 为所知的一切全力一搏 | *go for it* = 放手去做 |
| **morning dew** | 晨露 | 清晨意象，常象征新生 |
| **want to be the one with you** | 想成为和你在一起的那个人 | 比 *be with you* 更强调「唯一」 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Just the two of us* 旋律上口，**KTV 与通勤跟唱**都合适；先熟悉 *we can make it if we try* 的节奏。
- Bill Withers 咬字清楚，适合练 **发音**；注意 *when the sun comes shining through* 的连读。
- *it don't make no flowers grow* 是非正式双重否定，理解意思即可，书面写作避免模仿。
- 爵士／R&B 经典，可感受 **smooth jazz** 律动：人声与萨克斯交替时跟唱副歌即可。
- 想积累**恋爱与承诺表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
