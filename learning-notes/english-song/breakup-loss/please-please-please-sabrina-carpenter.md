## 歌手简介 · About Sabrina Carpenter

**English**

**Sabrina Carpenter** (born 11 May 1999) is an American **singer-songwriter and actress** from Quakertown, Pennsylvania. She began on Disney Channel (*Girl Meets World*) before launching a pop career on **Island Records**. Albums like *emails i can't send* (2022) and **Short n' Sweet** (2024) established her as a sharp lyricist with a flirtatious, self-aware voice. In 2024 she became a global pop force: **"Espresso"** and **"Please Please Please"** both hit **No. 1** in the US and UK, making her the youngest female artist to hold the UK No. 1 and No. 2 simultaneously. She opened Taylor Swift's **Eras Tour** in Latin America, Australia, and Singapore.

**中文**

**Sabrina Carpenter**（1999 年 5 月 11 日生）是美国**创作型歌手兼演员**，来自宾州 Quakertown。迪士尼频道《女孩看世界》出道后，在 **Island Records** 发展流行音乐事业。专辑 *emails i can't send*（2022）与 **Short n' Sweet**（2024）以犀利、自嘲又带挑逗的歌词确立风格。2024 年成为全球流行现象：**"Espresso"** 与 **"Please Please Please"** 双双登顶美英单曲榜，成为英国史上最年轻同时占据冠亚军的女歌手。她曾担任 Taylor Swift **Eras Tour** 拉丁美洲、澳洲与新加坡场开场嘉宾。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Please Please Please"** was released on **6 June 2024** as the second single from *Short n' Sweet*, following the global smash **"Espresso"**. **Jack Antonoff** produced the track, which Carpenter wrote with Antonoff and **Amy Allen** in one day at Electric Lady Studios. It became her **first US Hot 100 No. 1** in its second week. The song is an **anxious love plea**: she knows her judgment is good, but begs a messy new boyfriend not to embarrass her in public—*Heartbreak is one thing, my ego's another*. The music video stars her then-partner **Barry Keoghan**, blurring fiction and real-life tabloid drama. The repeated *please, please, please* is both sweet and desperate.

**中文**

**"Please Please Please"** 于 **2024 年 6 月 6 日**发行，为 *Short n' Sweet* 第二支单曲，接在爆款 **"Espresso"** 之后。**Jack Antonoff** 制作，Sabrina 与 Antonoff、**Amy Allen** 在 Electric Lady Studios 一天内写完。第二周登顶 **Billboard Hot 100**，成为她首个美国冠军单曲。这是一首**焦虑恋爱恳求曲**：她自知眼光不错，却求那位「问题男友」别在公开场合让她难堪——*Heartbreak is one thing, my ego's another*（心碎是一回事，自尊是另一回事）。MV 由当时的男友 **Barry Keoghan** 出演，虚实交织。反复吟唱的 *please, please, please* 又甜又绝望。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌三声 *please* 节奏鲜明，适合练**重音与停顿**：三声渐强，*don't prove I'm right* 是全句重心。
- *Heartbreak is one thing, my ego's another* 是整首歌的**金句**，可单独背诵用于表达「可以伤心但不能丢脸」。
- 主歌 2 *I'm just kidding, but really* 是**口是心非**的经典转折，可先练这句再串副歌。
- Sabrina 在 *please* 与 *motherfucker* 之间语气切换很快，跟唱时注意**情绪反差**。
