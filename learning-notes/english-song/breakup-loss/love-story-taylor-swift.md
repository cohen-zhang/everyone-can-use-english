## 歌手简介 · About Taylor Swift

**English**

**Taylor Swift** (born 13 December 1989, West Reading, Pennsylvania) is an American **singer-songwriter** who moved from **country** to global **pop** while keeping narrative, detail-rich lyrics. She signed with Big Machine at 15, broke through with *Tim McGraw* (2006), and built the **Fearless** era (2008–2010) into one of country-pop's biggest crossover successes. After a masters-rights dispute, she began re-recording her early catalog as **"Taylor's Version"**; **Fearless (Taylor's Version)** (2021) restored her ownership of hits including **"Love Story."** Swift has won multiple **Grammy Album of the Year** awards and is known for turning personal stories into sing-along anthems.

**中文**

**Taylor Swift**（1989 年 12 月 13 日生，宾州 West Reading）是美国**创作型歌手**，从**乡村**跨到全球**流行**，歌词始终叙事、细节满满。15 岁签约 Big Machine，2006 年以 *Tim McGraw* 崭露头角；**Fearless** 时期（2008–2010）成为乡村流行最具跨圈影响力的阶段之一。因早期母带版权纠纷，她开始以 **「Taylor's Version」** 重录旧作；**Fearless (Taylor's Version)**（2021）收回了 **"Love Story"** 等热门曲版权。Swift 多次获 **格莱美年度专辑**，擅长把私人故事写成全民跟唱颂歌。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Love Story"** was Swift's **second single** from *Fearless* (2008), inspired by a real crush whose family disapproved—she reframed it as **Romeo and Juliet** with a **happy ending**. The **Taylor's Version** (2021) is a faithful re-recording with fuller production and mature vocal tone; lyrics and structure match the original. The song blends **country storytelling** with a **pop chorus** (*It's a love story, baby, just say yes*), making it a staple for **KTV**, **commute listening**, and **easy-level** English practice: past tense narrative, direct speech in quotes, and fairy-tale vocabulary (*prince, princess, balcony*).

**中文**

**"Love Story"** 是 *Fearless*（2008）的**第二支单曲**，灵感来自一段家人反对的真实暗恋——Swift 把它改写成**有 happy ending 的 Romeo and Juliet**。**Taylor's Version**（2021）为忠实重录，制作更饱满、嗓音更成熟，歌词结构与原版一致。歌曲把**乡村叙事**与**流行副歌**（*It's a love story, baby, just say yes*）结合，是 **KTV**、**通勤**与**初级**英文练习常备曲：过去时讲故事、引号内直接引语、童话词汇（*prince, princess, balcony*）。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **flashback starts** | 闪回开始 | 叙事常用；现在时写过去画面 |
| **make your way through the crowd** | 穿过人群 | *make one's way* = 设法到达 |
| **Little did I know** | 我哪里知道 | 倒装；事后才发现 |
| **throwing pebbles** | 扔小石子 | Romeo 经典桥段 |
| **Stay away from Juliet** | 离 Juliet 远点 | 家长反对恋爱的直接引语 |
| **Begging you, "Please don't go"** | 求你别走 | *beg someone to…* |
| **all there's left to do is run** | 剩下的事就是跑 | 私奔意象 |
| **sneak out** | 溜出去 | 青少年禁恋常用 |
| **we're dead if they knew** | 被发现了就完了 | 夸张口语；*dead* = 大祸临头 |
| **scarlet letter** | 《红字》/ 耻辱标记 | 文学典故；指被标签化的女孩 |
| **make it out of this mess** | 走出这团乱局 | *mess* = 麻烦局面 |
| **on the outskirts of town** | 在镇郊 | *outskirts* = 市郊边缘 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 全曲用**过去时讲故事**，适合练叙事口语：*We were both young… I close my eyes…*
- 副歌 *It's a love story, baby, just say yes* 节奏清楚，**KTV 首选**；先熟副歌再练主歌 1。
- 注意 **Romeo / Juliet / scarlet letter** 三个文化典故，读歌词时可一并记文学背景。
- *'cause we're dead if they knew* 里 **dead** 不是真死，是「完蛋了」的夸张说法。
- Taylor's Version 与 2008 原版歌词一致；可对比两版**咬字与情绪**，选更适合跟唱的一版。
