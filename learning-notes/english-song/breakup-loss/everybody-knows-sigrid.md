## 歌手简介 · About Sigrid

**English**

**Sigrid** (born **Sigrid Solbakk Raabe**, 5 September 1996, Ålesund, Norway) is a Norwegian **singer-songwriter**. Raised in Bergen, she started piano at seven and singing at thirteen; her brother **Tellef Raabe** challenged her to write an original song, leading to 2013 debut single **"Sun."** Breakthrough came with **"Don't Kill My Vibe"** (2017); she won **BBC Music Sound of 2018**—the first Norwegian artist to do so. Albums *Sucker Punch* (2019) and *How to Let Go* (2022) topped charts in Norway and reached the UK Top 5. Known for understated stage style (often T-shirt and jeans), Sigrid's **synth-pop** balances vulnerability with direct, conversational lyrics.

**中文**

**Sigrid**（本名 **Sigrid Solbakk Raabe**，1996 年 9 月 5 生于挪威奥勒松）是挪威**创作型歌手**。在 Bergen 长大，7 岁学钢琴、13 岁唱歌；哥哥 **Tellef Raabe** Challenge 她写原创，促成 2013 年首单 **"Sun."** 2017 年 **"Don't Kill My Vibe"** 打响名号；她获 **BBC Music Sound of 2018**，为首位得奖的挪威艺人。专辑 *Sucker Punch*（2019）与 *How to Let Go*（2022）在挪威夺冠、英国 Top 5。舞台风格低调（常 T 恤牛仔裤），**合成器流行**里兼顾脆弱感与直白口语歌词。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Everybody Knows"** is **Sigrid's recording** for the *Justice League* (2017) soundtrack—it plays over the film's **opening credits**, chosen by director **Joss Whedon**. This note follows **Sigrid's version** on Apple Music (not Leonard Cohen's 1988 original studio take). The words were written by **Leonard Cohen** and **Sharon Robinson**; Sigrid delivers them in a clean, pop-forward reading with pulsing synths beneath her vocal. The lyric is a dark, witty catalogue of things *everybody knows* but won't say aloud—fixed fights, leaking boats, faithless lovers—ending on *take one last look before it blows*.

**中文**

**"Everybody Knows"** 是 **Sigrid 为** *Justice League*（2017）**原声带录制的版本**——在片头字幕上播放，由导演 **Joss Whedon** 选定。本笔记以 Apple Music 上的 **Sigrid 版**为准（非 Leonard Cohen 1988 年原版录音）。词作 **Leonard Cohen** 与 **Sharon Robinson**；Sigrid 以更清晰、偏流行的演绎呈现，合成器脉冲托底。歌词像一份黑色幽默清单，罗列*人人都懂却不说*的事——内定比赛、漏水的船、不忠的情人——以 *take one last look before it blows* 收尾。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **the dice are loaded** | 骰子做了手脚 | 习语；喻系统不公、结果预定 |
| **roll with fingers crossed** | 交叉手指掷骰 | 表「碰运气、明知希望渺茫」 |
| **the fight was fixed** | 比赛内定了 | *fix* = 操纵结果 |
| **that's how it goes** | 就是这样；没办法 |  shrug 式总结；副歌核心 |
| **the boat is leaking** | 船在漏水 | 喻局势已不可挽回 |
| **give or take a night or two** | 差不多；差个一两晚 | 口语表「大约、大概」 |
| **without your clothes** | 不穿衣服 | 歌里暗指出轨； blunt 到讽刺 |
| **now or never** | 最后机会；机不可失 | 常见口号式短语 |
| **the deal is rotten** | 这笔交易烂透了 | *rotten* = 腐败、糟透 |
| **a shining artifact of the past** | 过去闪光的遗迹 | *artifact* = 人工遗物；带历史感 |
| **take one last look** | 最后看一眼 | 结尾句；带末日感 |
| **Sacred Heart** | 神圣之心 | 宗教意象；亦可能指圣心像/符号 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 本笔记跟 **Sigrid 版** Apple Music 录音；片头版与专辑版歌词一致，但 Sigrid 咬字比 Cohen 原版更清晰，适合**通勤精听**。
- 结构是 **Everybody knows that…** 排比句叠加上去，可练「人人都知道……」的英文节奏；副歌 *That's how it goes* 是记忆锚点。
- 第三段 about fidelity 与第六段 about trouble 偏**成人讽刺**，学习时注意场合；*give or take a night or two* 是常用口语缓冲。
- 想延伸练**恋爱里的 candid / cynical 表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
