## 歌手简介 · About LAUV

**English**

**LAUV** (born **Ari Staprans Leff**, 8 August 1994) is an American **singer-songwriter** from San Francisco, raised in West Orange, New Jersey. He studied at **New York University**, where he double-majored in music and **music technology**, before breaking out with the viral single **"I Like Me Better"** (2017). LAUV's early songs were written and produced in his bedroom; his sound blends **indie pop, electronic, and R&B** textures with confessional lyrics about anxiety, loneliness, and modern relationships. He has collaborated with **Troye Sivan**, **Anne-Marie**, and **BTS**, and his debut album *~how i'm feeling~* (2020) explored mental health openly.

**中文**

**LAUV**（本名 **Ari Staprans Leff**，1994 年 8 月 8 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**，生于旧金山，在新泽西 **West Orange** 长大。曾就读 **纽约大学**，双主修音乐与**音乐科技**；2017 年凭病毒单曲 **"I Like Me Better"** 出圈。早期歌曲在卧室里创作制作；风格融合 **indie-pop、电子与 R&B**，歌词坦白写焦虑、孤独与现代关系。曾与 **Troye Sivan**、**Anne-Marie**、**BTS** 合作；2020 年首张专辑 *~how i'm feeling~* 公开探讨心理健康。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"The Other"** was one of LAUV's earliest releases, first uploaded to **SoundCloud** around **2015** and later included on the compilation *I met you when I was 18. (the playlist)*. The minimalist piano ballad captures the limbo of a relationship that isn't quite broken but **doesn't feel right**—*nothing quite wrong but it don't feel right*. The central metaphor *Either your head or your heart, you set the other on fire* describes the war between logic and emotion: you sacrifice one to feed the other. Lines like *Who wrote the book on goodbye?* express how no script exists for ending things gently. The song helped establish LAUV's signature tone: soft, anxious, and painfully honest.

**中文**

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Either your head or your heart* 节奏稳定，适合**通勤精听**；注意 *it don't feel right* 是口语，正式写作常用 *doesn't*。
- Bridge 里 *caught in between what I wish and what I know* 是整首歌主题句，可用来练「wish vs know」的对照结构。
- 想延伸读**分手与情绪表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
