## 歌手简介 · About LINKIN PARK

**English**

**Linkin Park** was an American **rock band** formed in Agoura Hills, California, in 1996. The lineup—**Chester Bennington** (vocals), **Mike Shinoda** (vocals/rap), and others—blended **nu metal, alternative rock, and hip-hop**, making them one of the best-selling bands of the 2000s. Albums *Hybrid Theory* (2000) and *Meteora* (2003) defined a generation of rock radio. Bennington's soaring, anguished vocals paired with Shinoda's rapped verses gave songs like **"Numb"**, **"In the End"**, and **"Crawling"** emotional range. The band won **Grammy** and **MTV VMA** awards; Bennington's death in 2017 shocked fans worldwide. Linkin Park reunited with new vocalist **Emily Armstrong** in 2024.

**中文**

**Linkin Park** 是 1996 年成立于加州 Agoura Hills 的美国**摇滚乐队**。**Chester Bennington**（主唱）、**Mike Shinoda**（说唱/主唱）等人融合 **新金属、另类摇滚与 hip-hop**，成为 2000 年代最畅销乐队之一。*Hybrid Theory*（2000）与 *Meteora*（2003）定义了一代人的摇滚电台。Bennington 高亢、痛苦的嗓音与 Shinoda 的说唱主歌让 **"Numb"**、**"In the End"**、**"Crawling"** 等歌情绪层次丰富。乐队获 **格莱美** 与 **MTV VMA**；2017 年 Bennington 辞世震惊全球乐迷。2024 年 Linkin Park 以新主唱 **Emily Armstrong** 重组。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Numb"** was released on **25 March 2003** as the third single from *Meteora* and later paired with **"Encore"** on the *Numb/Encore* EP with Jay-Z. Bennington sings from the perspective of someone **crushed by expectations**—a parent or authority figure who wants them to be something they're not (*walking in your shoes*). The chorus **"I've become so numb, I can't feel you there"** captures emotional shutdown: awareness without feeling. The song hit **No. 11** on the Hot 100 and remains one of Linkin Park's most streamed tracks. Its theme—**pressure vs. authenticity**—resonates far beyond teenage years.

**中文**

**"Numb"** 于 **2003 年 3 月 25 日**发行，为 *Meteora* 第三支单曲，后与 Jay-Z 的 **"Encore"** 组成 *Numb/Encore* 迷你专辑。Bennington 以**被期望压垮**者的视角演唱——父母或权威要他们成为不是自己那个人（*walking in your shoes*）。副歌 **"I've become so numb, I can't feel you there"** 写情感关闭：清醒却感受不到。单曲在 Hot 100 最高 **第 11** 位，至今仍是 Linkin Park 流媒体播放最高的曲目之一。主题——**压力与真实自我的冲突**——远不止青少年时期才有共鸣。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **tired of being what you want me to be** | 厌倦了做你想让我成为的人 | 歌的开场句；*be tired of* + 动名词 |
| **walk in someone's shoes** | 穿某人的鞋；设身处地 | 习语；歌里指被迫按对方标准活 |
| **caught in the undertow** | 困在退浪里 | *undertow* = 海水退潮时的暗流；比喻被拖下水 |
| **become so numb** | 变得如此麻木 | *numb* = 失去知觉；情感上「麻了」 |
| **smothering me** | 窒息我 | *smother* = 使窒息；也可指过度保护/控制 |
| **afraid to lose control** | 害怕失控 | 常指家长/权威的心理 |
| **fallen apart** | 分崩离析 | *fall apart* = 崩溃、瓦解 |
| **be more like me and be less like you** | 更像我自己，而不是像你 | 歌的核心诉求：真实自我 / authenticity |
| **someone disappointed in you** | 曾有人对你失望 | Bridge 转折：施压迫者也曾是受害者 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I've become so numb* 音高跨度大，跟唱前先**分句练** Bennington 的真声与假声转换。
- 背景即兴 *caught in the undertow* 可轻读；主句 *every step that I take* 注意 **step that I** 的连读。
- 想延伸读**成人情绪与压力**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 **Unpleasant / Very Unpleasant** 相关句型。
