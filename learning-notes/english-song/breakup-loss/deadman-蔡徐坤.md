## 歌手简介 · About 蔡徐坤 / KUN

**English**

**蔡徐坤** (**KUN**, born 2 August 1998, Wenzhou, China) is a Chinese **singer, dancer, and songwriter**. He rose to national fame after placing first on the survival show ***Idol Producer*** (2018) and debuting with the project group **NINE PERCENT**. As a solo artist he releases under the name **KUN**, blending **Mandopop**, **R&B**, and **English-language** tracks aimed at international listeners. Known for sharp choreography and a polished visual style, he has built a large global fan base and continues to experiment with bilingual pop and hip-hop production.

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Deadman"** is an **English-language** single by **KUN**, released **28 May 2025**. It is a sleek **R&B / hip-hop–leaning pop** track about desire so intense the singer feels *dead* without the other person—*I'd give you my soul / I'm a dead man*. The hook repeats *I'm a dead man* as romantic hyperbole (not literal death): wrapped in closeness, emptied without you. Useful for adult learners practicing **spoken intimacy**, **conditional *if it was up to me***, and soft R&B phrasing. Some lines are sensual; Chinese glosses below stay natural and lightly softened.

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **adore ya** | 爱你 / 崇拜你 | *ya* = you；口语亲昵 |
| **I want you bad** | 我好想要你 | *bad* = badly；口语加强，非「坏」 |
| **Will you oblige?** | 你愿意帮忙/答应吗？ | 偏正式；此处带调情语气 |
| **bad habit** | 坏习惯 | 把「想要你」说成戒不掉的习惯 |
| **If it was up to me** | 要是我说了算 | 口语条件句；正式写作常用 *were* |
| **I'd give you my soul** | 我愿意把灵魂都给你 | 夸张奉献；*I'd* = I would |
| **I'm a dead man** | 我完了 / 像个死人 | 此处=没有你就活不成；也有「完蛋了」义 |
| **Wrapped in your body** | 缠在你怀里 | 亲密画面；跟唱注意连读 |
| **hotter than the weather** | 比天气还烫 | 双关：气温 + 暧昧气氛 |
| **cool it down** | 降降温；让冷静点 | 字面降温 / 缓和情绪 |
| **begging for rain** | 求一场雨 | 隐喻渴望；可联想「降温」 |
| **make you scream** | 让你失控地叫 | 偏露骨；中文可委婉记「让你失控」 |
| **My lady** | 我的女人 / 女士 | 亲昵称呼，非正式头衔 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I'm a dead man* 短、重复，先跟唱 hook，再补 Verse 的暧昧细节。
- 注意 *I'd give you my soul* 里 *I'd* 与 *give* 的连读；*If it was up to me* 练条件口语。
- *adore ya / for ya* 的 *ya* 是美式口语弱读，别咬成完整 *you*。
- 部分歌词偏成人向；学习时抓**夸张表白结构**即可，不必逐字模仿露骨表达。
- 想延伸练恋爱口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
