## 歌手简介 · About Green Day

**English**

**Green Day** is an American **punk rock** band formed in **Rodeo, California**, in **1987** by vocalist-guitarist **Billie Joe Armstrong** and bassist **Mike Dirnt**. Drummer **Tré Cool** joined in **1990**, locking in the classic power-trio lineup. Rising from the East Bay / 924 Gilman Street scene, they broke into the mainstream with *Dookie* (1994), then reinvented themselves with the rock-opera *American Idiot* (2004)—a post-9/11 concept album that brought punk back to stadiums and won a **Grammy** for Best Rock Album. Known for catchy hooks, political bite, and Armstrong's raw tenor, they remain one of the most influential punk bands of their generation.

**中文**

**Green Day** 是 1987 年在**加州 Rodeo** 成立的美国**朋克摇滚**乐队，由主唱兼吉他手 **Billie Joe Armstrong** 与贝斯手 **Mike Dirnt** 组建；鼓手 **Tré Cool** 于 **1990** 年加入，形成经典三人组。从东湾 / 924 Gilman Street 场景走出来，1994 年 *Dookie* 打入主流，2004 年摇滚歌剧 *American Idiot* 再造辉煌——这张后 9/11 概念专辑把朋克带回体育场，并拿下**格莱美**最佳摇滚专辑。以抓耳旋律、政治锋芒和 Armstrong 沙哑有力的嗓音著称，是同代最有影响力的朋克乐队之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Wake Me Up When September Ends"** is track 11 on *American Idiot* (released **21 September 2004**); it went out as the album's fourth single in **June 2005**. Written by **Billie Joe Armstrong**, it is a personal **grief ballad** about his father, **Andrew Armstrong**, who died of cancer in **September 1982** when Billie Joe was ten. After the funeral he locked himself in his room and told his mother to *wake him up when September ends*—a line he held for years before he was ready to write the song. The verses mark time (*seven years* / *20 years has gone so fast*); the soft acoustic opening builds into electric guitars and bells. Though it sits on a political concept album, this track stands apart as a private elegy that also resonated with listeners mourning other Septembers.

**中文**

**"Wake Me Up When September Ends"** 是 *American Idiot*（**2004 年 9 月 21 日**发行）第 11 轨，**2005 年 6 月**作为专辑第四支单曲推出。由 **Billie Joe Armstrong** 创作，是一首写给父亲的**悼亡抒情曲**：父亲 **Andrew Armstrong** 于 **1982 年 9 月**因癌症去世，当时 Billie Joe 才十岁。葬礼后他把自己锁进房间，对母亲说 *wake me up when September ends*——这句标题他藏了很多年，直到终于写得出口。主歌用 *seven years* / *20 years has gone so fast* 丈量时间；开头木吉他轻柔，再叠进电吉他与钟声。虽收在政治概念专辑里，这首却是私人挽歌，也触动了许多在「九月」里失去过什么的人。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **come and passed** | 来了又走了 | 季节/阶段结束；*pass* = 过去 |
| **the innocent can never last** | 天真总留不住 | *innocent* 作名词：天真的人/状态 |
| **wake me up when…** | ……的时候再叫醒我 | 标题句；口语可表「先别让我面对」 |
| **come to pass** | 发生；去世（委婉） | 圣经/正式语感；此处指父亲离世 |
| **has gone so fast** | 过得好快 | 时间飞逝的常用说法 |
| **here comes the rain again** | 雨又来了 | *here comes…* = ……来了；情绪再起 |
| **drenched in** | 被……浸透 | *drenched in pain* = 沉浸在痛里 |
| **becoming who we are** | 变成现在的我们 | 成长/创伤后的自我 |
| **memory rests** | 记忆暂时歇下 | *rest* = 休息，非「剩下」 |
| **never forgets what I lost** | 从未忘记我失去的 | *what I lost* 作宾语从句 |
| **ring out the bells** | 敲响钟声 | *ring out* = 鸣响；常与仪式/季节相关 |
| **when spring began** | 春天刚开始时 | 与九月形成季节对照 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 标题句 *Wake me up when September ends* 重复多、节奏稳，适合**跟唱练连读**（*wake me up* → /ˈweɪk miː ʌp/）。
- 注意 *come to pass* 与日常 *pass away* 的语感差别：歌词更书面、更沉重。
- 主歌里 *seven years* → *20 years* 是时间跳跃；听时可对照自己「某段伤痛过了多久」。
- 开头木吉他偏轻，副歌电吉他进来后音量拉大——练听**动态起伏**，别一上来就喊。
- 想延伸练**丧亲 / 记忆**表达，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
