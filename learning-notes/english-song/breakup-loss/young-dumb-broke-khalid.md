## 歌手简介 · About Khalid

**English**

**Khalid** (born **Khalid Donnel Robinson**, 11 February 1998, Fort Stewart, Georgia) is an American **singer-songwriter** who grew up moving between military bases. He began posting tracks on SoundCloud while still in high school in El Paso, Texas; his debut single **"Location"** (2016) went platinum and led to the album **American Teen** (2017)—written largely while he was still a teenager. Khalid's warm, conversational voice blends **R&B, pop, and soul** with lyrics about youth, first love, and small-town life. **"Young Dumb & Broke"** became a Gen-Z anthem; he has since won **Grammy** and **Billboard Music Awards** and collaborated with artists from Billie Eilish to Calvin Harris.

**中文**

**Khalid**（本名 **Khalid Donnel Robinson**，1998 年 2 月 11 日生，乔治亚州 Fort Stewart）是美国**创作型歌手**，童年随军人家庭辗转多个基地。在得州 El Paso 读高中时开始在 SoundCloud 发布作品；2016 年首单 **"Location"** 获白金销量，并推出专辑 **American Teen**（2017）——大部分歌曲创作于青少年时期。Khalid 温暖、对话感的嗓音融合 **R&B、流行与灵魂乐**，歌词常写青春、初恋与小城生活。**"Young Dumb & Broke"** 成为 Z 世代颂歌；此后他获 **格莱美** 与 **Billboard 音乐奖**，并与 Billie Eilish、Calvin Harris 等合作。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Young Dumb & Broke"** was released on **2 February 2017** as the third single from *American Teen*. Over a laid-back **R&B groove**, Khalid reframes being **young, dumb, and broke** not as shame but as freedom: no money for fancy dates, but plenty of **love to give**; no rush toward **commitment** when life is still wide open. The hook's playful repetition (*young, dumb, broke high school kids*) made it a **KTV and festival sing-along** staple. The song peaked at **No. 19** on the Billboard Hot 100 and was certified **6× Platinum** in the US.

**中文**

**"Young Dumb & Broke"** 于 **2017 年 2 月 2 日**发行，为 *American Teen* 第三支单曲。在慵懒的 **R&B 律动**上，Khalid 把 **年轻、犯傻、没钱** 重新定义为自由：没钱搞浪漫约会，但仍有**爱可以付出**；人生尚宽，不必急着**承诺**。副歌里俏皮的重复（*young, dumb, broke high school kids*）让它成为 **KTV 与音乐节大合唱** 常客。单曲在美国 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 19** 位，获 **6× 白金** 认证。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **young, dumb and broke** | 年轻、犯傻又没钱 | 歌题三连；*dumb* 此处偏「不成熟」而非「蠢」 |
| **still got love to give** | 仍有爱可以付出 | *have got* = have；口语常缩略 |
| **caught up in this** | 完全沉浸其中 | *be caught up in* = 深陷、无法自拔 |
| **so high at the moment** | 此刻情绪高涨 | 可指兴奋；非一定指吸毒（语境为恋爱 euphoria） |
| **What's fun about commitment?** | 承诺有什么好玩的？ | 反问表「现在还不想定下来」 |
| **have our life to live** | 有整个人生要过 | 强调青春不必急着负责 |
| **in the name of fun** | 一切只为好玩 | *in the name of* = 以……之名 |
| **leave it all in the game of love** | 把一切押在爱的游戏里 | 比喻恋爱像赌博，全情投入 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *young, dumb and broke* 节奏清楚，适合 **KTV 跟唱**；先练副歌再练主歌。
- *caught up in this* 中 **caught up** 连读为 /kɔːtʌp/，跟读时注意 **t** 与 **up** 的衔接。
- 想延伸读**年轻恋爱口语**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里关于约会、承诺的句型。
