## 歌手简介 · About Wiz Khalifa

**English**

**Wiz Khalifa** (born **Cameron Jibril Thomaz**, 8 September 1987, Minot, North Dakota) is an American **rapper, singer-songwriter, and entrepreneur** raised largely in **Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania**. After early mixtapes and the independent album *Show and Prove* (2006), he signed with **Atlantic Records** and broke through with **"Black and Yellow"** (2010)—a hometown anthem that topped the Billboard Hot 100—followed by his major-label debut *Rolling Papers* (2011). Known for melodic, laid-back **pop-rap** and the **Taylor Gang** crew, he scored further hits including *"Young, Wild & Free"* (with Snoop Dogg) and *"We Dem Boyz"*. **"See You Again"** (feat. **Charlie Puth**) became his biggest global single.

**Charlie Puth** (born 1991, New Jersey)—Berklee-trained singer-songwriter and producer—sings and co-wrote the piano chorus; the track launched him into mainstream fame ahead of *Nine Track Mind* (2016).

**中文**

**Wiz Khalifa**（本名 **Cameron Jibril Thomaz**，1987 年 9 月 8 日生于北达科他 Minot）是美国**说唱歌手、词曲作者与创业者**，主要在**宾州匹兹堡**长大。早期靠混音带与独立专辑 *Show and Prove*（2006）起家，签约 **Atlantic** 后凭 **"Black and Yellow"**（2010）——向家乡致敬的热单——登顶 Billboard Hot 100，并推出大厂首专 *Rolling Papers*（2011）。以旋律感强、松弛的 **pop-rap** 与 **Taylor Gang** 厂牌著称，另有 *"Young, Wild & Free"*（与 Snoop Dogg）、*"We Dem Boyz"* 等热单。**"See You Again"**（feat. **Charlie Puth**）成为他全球影响力最大的单曲。

**Charlie Puth**（1991 年生，新泽西）——Berklee 出身的唱作人与制作人——演唱并参与创作钢琴副歌；此曲让他一举进入主流，随后推出 *Nine Track Mind*（2016）。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"See You Again"** was released on **10 March 2015** as the lead single from the *Furious 7* soundtrack—a **tribute to Paul Walker**, who died in a car crash in 2013. Wiz Khalifa's verses honor **friendship, family, and brotherhood** (*family's all that we got*); Charlie Puth's piano chorus carries the ache of *I'll tell you all about it when I see you again*. The song closes the film as Dom and Brian part ways on a mountain road. It topped the **Billboard Hot 100 for 12 non-consecutive weeks**, hit No. 1 in the UK and many other countries, earned **Grammy** nominations (including Song of the Year), and became one of YouTube's most-viewed music videos. IFPI named it the **best-selling song of 2015** worldwide.

**中文**

**"See You Again"** 于 **2015 年 3 月 10 日**发行，为 *Furious 7* 原声带主打单曲——献给 **Paul Walker**（2013 年车祸去世）的**致敬之作**。Wiz Khalifa 的主歌写**友情、家人与兄弟情**（*family's all that we got*）；Charlie Puth 的钢琴副歌托住那句心酸的 *I'll tell you all about it when I see you again*。片尾 Dom 与 Brian 在山路分道扬镳时响起此曲。单曲在 **Billboard Hot 100 非连续登顶 12 周**，并登顶英国等多国榜单；获多项 **格莱美** 提名（含年度歌曲），MV 长期位居 YouTube 最高播放量前列。IFPI 将其评为 **2015 年全球畅销单曲**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **it's been a long day without you** | 没有你的日子真漫长 | 告别/思念开场；*long day* 可指难熬的一天，此处偏「漫长的日子」 |
| **see you again** | 再见；再见到你 | 日常告别；歌里带「来世/天堂再见」的重量 |
| **come a long way** | 走了很长的路；进步很大 | *We've come a long way from where we began* = 从起点到现在变化巨大 |
| **hit the road** | 上路；出发 | 口语；常接 *and laugh* 一类轻松画面 |
| **something told me** | 心里有个声音说；直觉告诉我 | 预感/直觉，非字面「有人告诉我」 |
| **wouldn't last** | 不会长久 | *it wouldn't last* = 好景不长 |
| **switch up** | 换个活法；调整方式 | 口语；*switch things up* |
| **see the bigger picture** | 把格局看大一点；看全局 | 职场/人生都常用 |
| **in a better place** | 在更好的地方 | 委婉说逝者「安息/在天堂」 |
| **family's all that we got** | 家人是我们仅有的一切 | *Fast & Furious* 核心主题句 |
| **by my side** | 在我身边 | 陪伴；比 *with me* 更有「并肩」感 |
| **gon' be** | 将会（口语） | = *gonna be* / *going to be* |
| **the last ride** | 最后一程 | 字面「最后一趟车」；歌里兼指人生最后一段路 |
| **go out your way** | 特地费心；特意多走一步 | 完整常说 *go out of your way* |
| **the vibe is feeling strong** | 感觉特别对；气场很强 | *vibe* = 氛围/感觉 |
| **turned to a bond** | 变成羁绊/深厚关系 | *bond* = 情感纽带 |
| **brotherhood come first** | 兄弟情放第一 | 非标准主谓一致（*comes*）；说唱口语 |
| **the line will never be crossed** | 那条线永远不会被越过 | *cross the line* = 越界 |
| **draw the line** | 划清界限；立规矩 | *when that line had to be drawn* |
| **remember me when I'm gone** | 我不在了，也请记得我 | 告别/遗言语感 |
| **let the light guide your way** | 让光为你引路 | 祝福式；Bridge 核心 |
| **lead you home** | 带你回家 | *home* 可指家、归属、或安息之地 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **难度：** 难（`level/hard`）——Wiz 的说唱主歌语速与连读密，适合精听拆句；**Charlie Puth 副歌旋律缓、音域友好，KTV 优先跟唱副歌与 Bridge**。
- 先把副歌 *It's been a long day… / when I see you again* 唱稳，再逐句跟主歌；注意 *'Bout*、*gon'*、*we been through* 等口语缩略。
- *in a better place*、*the last ride*、*remember me when I'm gone* 是**悼念/致敬**语境，日常慎用；练口语时更实用的是 *come a long way*、*hit the road*、*see the bigger picture*、*by my side*。
- 想延伸练**失去、怀念、友情告别**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]；同列表 #85 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/we-dont-talk-anymore-charlie-puth|We Don't Talk Anymore — Charlie Puth]] 可对照 Puth 的分手向副歌音色。
