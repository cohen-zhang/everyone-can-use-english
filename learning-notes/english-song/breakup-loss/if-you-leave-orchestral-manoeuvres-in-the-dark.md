## 歌手简介 · About Orchestral Manoeuvres In the Dark

**English**

**Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark** (**OMD**) is an English **electronic / synth-pop** band formed in **1978** on the **Wirral**, Merseyside, by school friends **Andy McCluskey** (vocals, bass) and **Paul Humphreys** (keyboards, vocals). Inspired by Kraftwerk and early electronic experiments, they scored UK breakthroughs with *"Electricity"*, *"Enola Gay"*, and the album *Architecture & Morality* (1981). In the mid-1980s they leaned into a more accessible pop sound; **"If You Leave"** became their biggest U.S. hit. After a late-80s split and a 1990s McCluskey-led era, the classic lineup reunited in **2006** and continues to tour and record. OMD are widely cited as pioneers of British synth-pop, with tens of millions of records sold worldwide.

**中文**

**Orchestral Manoeuvres in the Dark**（简称 **OMD**）是 1978 年在英格兰 **默西塞德 Wirral** 成立的**电子 / 合成器流行**乐队，由中学好友 **Andy McCluskey**（主唱、贝斯）与 **Paul Humphreys**（键盘、和声）创立。受 Kraftwerk 与早期电子实验启发，凭 *"Electricity"*、*"Enola Gay"* 与专辑 *Architecture & Morality*（1981）打入英国主流。1980 年代中期转向更易听的流行音色；**"If You Leave"** 成为他们在美国最大的单曲。80 年代末分家、90 年代由 McCluskey 主导后，经典阵容于 **2006** 年重聚，持续巡演与出片。OMD 常被视作英国 synth-pop 先驱之一，全球销量数千万。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"If You Leave"** was written overnight in **Los Angeles** for John Hughes's 1986 teen classic ***Pretty in Pink***. After test audiences rejected the original ending, Hughes asked OMD to replace their earlier cue *"Goddess of Love"* with a new song at **120 BPM**—matching the tempo of *"Don't You (Forget About Me)"* that the cast had danced to on set. McCluskey, Humphreys, and **Martin Cooper** wrote and demoed it in under 24 hours; it plays over the film's prom finale. Released **21 April 1986**, the **new-wave** ballad reached **No. 4** on the Billboard Hot 100 and became OMD's signature U.S. hit—bittersweet lines about one more night before going separate ways.

**中文**

**"If You Leave"** 是为 John Hughes 1986 年青春片 **《红粉佳人》（*Pretty in Pink*）** 连夜赶写的。试映观众不买账原结局后，Hughes 要求 OMD 用新歌替换原先的 *"Goddess of Love"*，并指定 **120 BPM**——与片场已拍好的 *"Don't You (Forget About Me)"* 舞步同速。McCluskey、Humphreys 与 **Martin Cooper** 在不到 24 小时内写完并做出小样；歌在片尾舞会场景贯穿响起。单曲于 **1986 年 4 月 21 日**发行，属 **new-wave** 抒情曲，登上 Billboard Hot 100 **第 4**，成为 OMD 在美国的代表作——写「再共度一夜，然后各走各的路」的苦甜告别。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **don't leave now** | 别现在就走 | 挽留时的急切请求 |
| **take my heart away** | 把我的心带走 | 情歌常用；= 带走我的感情 |
| **go our separate ways** | 各走各的路；分道扬镳 | 分手/散伙的固定说法 |
| **time on our side** | 时间站在我们这边 | *have time on one's side* = 还不急 |
| **fading fast** | 飞快消逝 | *fade* = 变淡、消失 |
| **make it last** | 让它长久一点 | *it* 指这段关系或这一刻 |
| **won't let go at any price** | 无论什么代价都不放手 | *at any price* = 不惜一切 |
| **still be friends someday** | 总有一天还会是朋友 | 分手安慰话；现实里常难兑现 |
| **don't look back** | 别回头 | 字面 +「别留恋过去」 |
| **running the other way** | 朝相反方向跑 | 你走你的，我走我的 |
| **went under the bridge** | （时间）就这么过去了 | 来自 *water under the bridge* |
| **heaven knows** | 天知道 | 口语：谁也不清楚 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I touch you once, I touch you twice* 节奏整齐，适合先抓 **chorus 跟唱**，再练 Verse 的叙事句。
- 注意 *we've gotta / you've gotta* 的口语缩读；书面可写 *got to*。
- *Seven years went under the bridge* 是习语变体，可对照 *water under the bridge*（往事已成定局）一起记。
- 电影场景是舞会告别，情绪是「挽留 + 认命」并存；可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的分手与怀念表达。
