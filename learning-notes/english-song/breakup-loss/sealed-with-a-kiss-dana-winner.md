## 歌手简介 · About Dana Winner

**English**

**Dana Winner** (stage name of **Chantal Vanlee**, born 10 February 1965) is a Belgian **singer** who performs in Dutch, French, and English. She broke through in Flanders in the early 1990s with emotional ballads and French chanson influences, then expanded across Europe with covers and original material. Known for a **clear, warm voice** and polished pop arrangements, she has sold millions of records in the Benelux and Germany. Her English albums, including **Unforgettable Too** (2004), showcase nostalgic standards and romantic mid-tempo songs aimed at adult listeners and easy listening radio.

**中文**

**Dana Winner**（本名 **Chantal Vanlee**，1965 年 2 月 10 日生）是比利时**歌手**，用荷兰语、法语和英语演唱。1990 年代初在弗拉芒以抒情民谣和法国香颂气质走红，后以翻唱与原创作品走向全欧。她以**清澈温暖的嗓音**和精致流行编曲著称，在比荷卢和德国销量可观。英语专辑 **Unforgettable Too**（2004）收录怀旧标准曲与浪漫中板歌，面向成人听众与轻音乐电台。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Sealed with a Kiss"** is a **1962 hit** written by **Peter Udell** and **Gary Geld**, first recorded by **Brian Hyland**; it became a summer-holiday classic about lovers parting for the season but promising to stay close through letters. **Dana Winner's** version on *Unforgettable Too* (2004) gives the song a smooth, adult-contemporary reading—ideal for learners because the vocabulary is simple, the story is linear, and the title phrase is a memorable idiom. The **pledge to meet in September** and **seal it with a kiss** frames temporary separation as something survivable with ritual and hope.

**中文**

**"Sealed with a Kiss"** 原是 **1962 年** **Peter Udell** 与 **Gary Geld** 创作、**Brian Hyland** 首唱的热曲；写恋人夏日暂别、靠书信维系感情，成为假期经典。 **Dana Winner** 在 *Unforgettable Too*（2004）中的版本偏顺滑成人流行，词汇简单、叙事清楚，标题短语更是易记习语。 **九月再相聚** 并以 **吻封缄** 的誓言，把短暂分离写成靠仪式与希望就能熬过去的事。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **say good-bye** | 道别 | 歌词拼作 *good-bye*；现代多写 *goodbye* |
| **sealed with a kiss** | 以吻封缄 | 习语：在信末吻一下当「封印」；亦指郑重承诺 |
| **fill the emptiness** | 填补空虚 | *emptiness* 比 *loneliness* 更偏「心里空落落」 |
| **everyday** | 每天 | 此处作副词；正式写作常分开 *every day* |
| **in the sunlight** | 在阳光里 | 诗意说法；想象对方无处不在 |
| **tenderly hold** | 温柔拥抱 | *tenderly* 副词修饰拥抱方式 |
| **you won't be there** | 你不在那儿 | 与上一句幻想形成落差 |
| **knowing the love we'll miss** | 明知我们会思念这份爱 | *knowing* 引导原因：因为知道会想念 |
| **make a pledge** | 许下诺言 | 比 *promise* 更正式、庄重 |
| **meet in September** | 九月再相聚 | 具体日期让承诺可期待 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 全曲语速适中、句型重复，适合**通勤跟读**；先练标题句 *Sealed with a kiss* 贯穿全曲。
- *good-bye / Darlin' / wanna* 注意口语缩读；副歌三句相同，可用来练**尾音收干净**。
- 可把本歌当「**书信英语**」素材：*I'll send you all my love / dreams everyday in a letter* 是完整可套用的句式。
- 更多夏日离别与情书式情话，见 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
