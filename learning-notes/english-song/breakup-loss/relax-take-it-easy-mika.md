## 歌手简介 · About MIKA

**English**

**MIKA** (born **Michael Holbrook Penniman Jr.**, 18 August 1983, Beirut, Lebanon) is a British **singer-songwriter** raised in Paris and London. After war forced his family to leave Lebanon, he studied at the **Royal College of Music** before dropping out to pursue pop. His debut single **"Relax, Take It Easy"** (2006) drew on the melody of Cutting Crew's *"(I Just) Died in Your Arms"* and was partly inspired by walking home through London after the **7 July 2005 bombings**. Breakthrough hit **"Grace Kelly"** (2007) and debut album **Life in Cartoon Motion** topped UK charts and sold over 8 million copies worldwide. MIKA's theatrical, multi-octave voice blends **glam pop, dance-pop, and disco**; he won a **Brit Award** for British Breakthrough Act in 2008.

**中文**

**MIKA**（本名 **Michael Holbrook Penniman Jr.**，1983 年 8 月 18 生于黎巴嫩贝鲁特）是在巴黎与伦敦长大的英国**创作型歌手**。因战乱举家离黎后，他曾在 **Royal College of Music** 求学，后辍学专注流行乐。首单 **"Relax, Take It Easy"**（2006）借用 Cutting Crew《*"(I Just) Died in Your Arms"*》旋律，部分灵感来自 **2005 年 7·7 伦敦爆炸** 后步行回家时的恐惧。2007 年 **"Grace Kelly"** 与首张专辑 **Life in Cartoon Motion** 登顶英国榜，全球销量逾 800 万。MIKA 以高亢、戏剧化的多八度嗓音融合 **华丽流行、舞曲流行与迪斯科**；2008 年获 **全英音乐奖「英国最佳新人」**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Relax, Take It Easy"** was MIKA's **debut single**, released in **October 2006** and later included on *Life in Cartoon Motion* (2007). Over a propulsive electropop beat, the song tells a story of emotional panic after a relationship rupture—riding a broken train, feeling alone—then pivots to reassurance: *there is nothing that we can do*, so **relax** and stay together. The recurring line *it's as if I'm playing with fire* captures the fear of loving someone who might hurt you. Note: some lyric sites append a bogus fan-written coda about a wedding; **that text is not part of the official song**, which ends on repeated *Relax* ad-libs.

**中文**

**"Relax, Take It Easy"** 是 MIKA 的**出道单曲**，**2006 年 10 月**发行，后收录于 *Life in Cartoon Motion*（2007）。在强劲的电子流行节奏上，歌写感情破裂后的恐慌——搭上一列「坏掉的车」、孤身一人——再转向安慰：*我们无能为力*，所以**放轻松**，别离开彼此。反复出现的 *it's as if I'm playing with fire* 点出「爱一个可能伤害你的人」的险境。注意：部分歌词网站误附一段关于婚礼的粉丝文字；**官方版本不含该段**，以重复的 *Relax* 口白收尾。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **take it easy** | 别紧张；放轻松 | 口语常用；比 *calm down* 更温和 |
| **the end of the line** | 线路尽头 | 地铁/火车术语；也可喻「走到尽头」 |
| **a broken train** | 一列坏掉的车 | 歌里既是字面场景，也喻关系「脱轨」 |
| **longing** | 渴望；思念 | 名词；比 *missing you* 更文学 |
| **blame it on me / you** | 怪在我/怪在你 | *blame sth on sb* = 把责任推给某人 |
| **it's as if** | 好像；仿佛 | 虚拟语气常用框架 |
| **playing with fire** | 玩火；铤而走险 | 习语；指冒险做可能自伤的事 |
| **the darkest times** | 最黑暗的时刻 | 复数强调一段艰难期 |
| **the last thing on my mind** | 我最不想做的事 | *the last thing I'd do is…* = 绝不想… |
| **in this together** | 同舟共济；一起扛 | 疫情后更常见；口语很暖 |
| **so many roads left** | 还有很多条路可走 | *road* 喻人生/感情选择 |
| **screaming for help** | 尖叫求救 | 比 *asking for help* 更绝望 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Relax, take it easy* 节奏稳定，适合**通勤跟唱**；注意 *relax* 与 *take it* 之间的连读。
- 主歌 2 的 *It's as if I'm scared* 四句结构相同，可用来练**平行句型**与重音落在 *scared / fire* 上。
- 桥段里 *Don't scream, there are so many roads left* 是全歌最「劝慰」的一句，语气从 panic 切到 calm，听时可对比前后动态。
- 想延伸练**分手/情绪口语**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 对照 fear、reassurance 相关表达。
