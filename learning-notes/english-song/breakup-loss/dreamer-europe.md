## 歌手简介 · About Europe

**English**

**Europe** is a **Swedish rock band** formed in Upplands Väsby in 1979. Led by vocalist **Joey Tempest** and guitarist **John Norum**, they rose from melodic hard rock to global fame with **"The Final Countdown"** (1986), which topped charts in 25 countries. The band's early albums blended **heavy metal** and **AOR** (album-oriented rock); later work includes acoustic and symphonic arrangements. Europe has sold tens of millions of records worldwide and remains a staple of classic-rock and arena-radio playlists. The Apple Music version here comes from ***Almost Unplugged*** (2008), a live acoustic set recorded in Stockholm with a string quartet.

**中文**

**Europe** 是 1979 年成立于瑞典的**摇滚乐队**，主唱 **Joey Tempest**、吉他手 **John Norum** 为核心。他们从旋律硬摇滚起步，1986 年凭 **"The Final Countdown"** 风靡全球，在 25 国登榜。早期专辑融合 **重金属** 与 **AOR**（专辑导向摇滚）；后期也尝试不插电与弦乐编制。全球唱片销量数千万，至今仍是经典摇滚电台常客。本播放列表版本来自 ***Almost Unplugged***（2008）——斯德哥尔摩现场不插电演出，配有弦乐四重奏。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Dreamer"** was first released on **24 February 1984** on Europe's second album ***Wings of Tomorrow***, written by Joey Tempest. The studio original is a melodic hard-rock ballad; the **2008 live version on *Almost Unplugged*** strips it to acoustic guitar, strings, and Tempest's emotive vocal—this is the recording linked in your playlist. The lyrics tell a **third-person story** about a lonely man by the riverside at night, labelled a *madman* by others but quietly fighting to understand himself and *carry on*. The repeated line *He's a dreamer* is both sympathy and anthem: dreaming as survival, not escape.

**中文**

**"Dreamer"** 首发于 **1984 年 2 月 24 日**，收录于 Europe 第二张专辑 ***Wings of Tomorrow***，由 Joey Tempest 创作。录音室版是旋律硬摇滚抒情曲；**2008 年 *Almost Unplugged* 现场版** 改为木吉他、弦乐与人声——即本播放列表所链版本。歌词以**第三人称**讲述：深夜河边的孤独男人，被旁人叫作 *madman*（疯子），却默默挣扎着想弄懂自己、**继续走下去**（*carry on*）。反复出现的 *He's a dreamer* 既是同情也是颂歌：做梦不是逃避，而是活下去的方式。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **dreamer** | 梦想家；爱做梦的人 | 歌题核心；可褒可贬，此处偏同情 |
| **down by the riverside** | 在河边 | 民谣/福音常用意象；孤独、沉思的场景 |
| **count the stars** | 数星星 | 字面动作；也象征发呆、想心事 |
| **madman** | 疯子 | 外人贴的标签；与歌者视角形成对比 |
| **act like a man** | 装成男人该有的样子 | 压抑情绪、强撑的社会期待 |
| **in vain** | 白费；徒劳 | *years were in vain* = 那些年是否值得 |
| **fightin' for his life** | 为活下去而战 | 不只指肉体；更多是心理挣扎 |
| **carry on** | 继续走下去 | 英式摇滚常用；坚持、撑下去 |
| **lonely man** | 孤独的人 | 结尾落点；重复加深怜悯感 |
| **gathering thoughts** | 搜集、聚拢思绪 | 回忆往事；偏文学化表达 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- *Almost Unplugged* 版节奏较慢，**弦乐进入**时适合练听清每个词；先听 Joey Tempest 咬字再跟唱。
- 副歌 *He's a dreamer* 重复多遍，是记忆锚点；注意 **fightin'**、**tryin'** 的 *-in'* 省音。
- 歌词用 **第三人称** 叙事（*he*），适合练「讲述他人故事」的口语结构；可改成 *I* 做个人日记练习。
- 想延伸练**孤独与压抑**的表达，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 Unpleasant / Very Unpleasant 相关句型。
