## 歌手简介 · About Aamir

**English**

**Aamir** is a **Canadian R&B** vocalist based in **Toronto**. He is best known on streaming platforms for **cover / rendition** releases that rework late-2000s and 2010s R&B and pop hits with a smoother, more intimate vocal approach. His 2017 project ***Renditions*** collects several of these takes—including **"Right Now (Na Na Na)"**—aimed at listeners who want the familiar hooks without the original's club-heavy production. He has also collaborated on updated electronic versions of the same track with producers such as ConKi and marchy. For learners, his clearer diction and shorter runtime make the cover a useful practice companion to the Akon original.

**中文**

**Aamir** 是常驻**多伦多**的**加拿大 R&B** 歌手。在流媒体上以**翻唱 / rendition** 作品见长：把 2000 年代末至 2010 年代的 R&B、流行热曲，用更柔和、更贴近的人声重新演绎。2017 年作品集 ***Renditions*** 收录多首此类翻唱，包括 **"Right Now (Na Na Na)"**——保留熟悉 hook，但弱化原版的夜店制作感。他后来也与 ConKi、marchy 等制作人合作推出电子更新版。对学习者而言，吐字更清晰、时长更短，适合作为 Akon 原唱的跟唱陪练。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Right Now (Na Na Na)"** was originally the lead single from **Akon**'s 2008 album *Freedom*—a **dance-pop / R&B** reconciliation plea built on the chant *na na*. **This playlist entry is Aamir's cover** from ***Renditions*** (released **29 March 2017**, length **2:43**): same core lyric about wanting to **"make up"** and **"link up"** after a breakup, but in a leaner arrangement that foregrounds the vocal. Use it to drill the chorus phrases first; then compare energy and slang delivery with [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/right-now-akon|Akon's original]].

**中文**

**"Right Now (Na Na Na)"** 原是 **Akon** 2008 年专辑 *Freedom* 的主打——一首以 *na na* 口号推进的 **dance-pop / R&B** 复合恳求。**本播放列表条目为 Aamir 翻唱版**，出自 ***Renditions***（**2017 年 3 月 29 日**发行，时长 **2:43**）：核心歌词仍是分手后想立刻 **"make up"**（和好）、**"link up"**（重新联系），但编曲更精简、人声更靠前。建议先用此版练熟副歌，再对照 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/right-now-akon|Akon 原唱]] 感受能量与俚语咬字差异。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- Aamir 版时长更短、人声更靠前，适合 **先抓副歌 hook**（*make up / link up right now*）再练 Verse。
- *tryna / why'd / somethings* 等为口语拼写，跟唱前对照表理解即可。
- Bridge 的 *fly / lie / dine / grind* 四句排比，可单独慢速朗读练**尾韵**。
- 对照 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/right-now-akon|Akon 原唱]]：原版更夜店、更长；本版更适合精听吐字。想延伸「想复合」口语，见 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
