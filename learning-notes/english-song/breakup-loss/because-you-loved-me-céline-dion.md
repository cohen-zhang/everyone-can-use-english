## 歌手简介 · About Céline Dion

**English**

**Céline Dion** (born 30 March 1968, Charlemagne, Quebec) is a Canadian **singer** and one of the best-selling recording artists of all time. She rose to fame in the French-speaking world as a teenager, then became a global pop icon with English-language hits in the 1990s. Her powerful, controlled **belt** and emotional ballads defined an era of adult contemporary pop. Signature songs include **"My Heart Will Go On"** (*Titanic*), **"The Power of Love"**, and **"Because You Loved Me"**. Dion has won **five Grammy Awards**, including Album of the Year for *Falling Into You* (1997), and performed a long residency in Las Vegas. She is known for precise pitch, dramatic phrasing, and songs that turn gratitude and devotion into stadium-sized choruses.

**中文**

**Céline Dion**（1968 年 3 月 30 日生，加拿大魁北克）是加拿大**歌手**，全球唱片销量最高的艺人之一。青少年时期在法语圈成名，1990 年代以英文金曲成为国际流行巨星。她富有力量、控制精准的**高音 belt** 与抒情 ballad 定义了一代成人当代流行乐。代表作包括 **"My Heart Will Go On"**（《泰坦尼克号》）、**"The Power of Love"**、**"Because You Loved Me"**。Dion 获 **五项格莱美**，包括 *Falling Into You*（1997）的「年度专辑」，并曾在拉斯维加斯长期驻场演出。她以音准精准、戏剧化句读，以及把感恩与深情唱成「体育场级副歌」著称。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Because You Loved Me"** was written by **Diane Warren** and released in **February 1996** as the theme from the romantic drama *Up Close and Personal*, starring Robert Redford and Michelle Pfeiffer. It appears on Dion's album **Falling Into You** and became her fourth US **Billboard Hot 100 No. 1**. The song is a sweeping **thank-you letter** to a partner who believed in the singer when she could not believe in herself—*You were my strength when I was weak / You were my voice when I couldn't speak*. It was nominated for an **Academy Award** and a **Golden Globe** for Best Original Song, and won the **Grammy** for Best Song Written Specifically for a Motion Picture or Television. The repeated line *I'm everything I am because you loved me* makes it a staple of weddings, Mother's Day playlists, and KTV gratitude ballads.

**中文**

**"Because You Loved Me"** 由 **Diane Warren** 创作，**1996 年 2 月**发行，为罗伯特·雷德福与米歇尔·菲佛主演的爱情片 *Up Close and Personal* 主题曲，收录于 Dion 专辑 **Falling Into You**，并成为她第四首美国 **Billboard Hot 100 冠军单曲**。这是一封宏大的**感谢信**：写给那个在她无法相信自己时仍相信她的伴侣——*You were my strength when I was weak / You were my voice when I couldn't speak*。歌曲获**奥斯卡**与**金球奖**「最佳原创歌曲」提名，并赢得**格莱美「专为影视所写最佳歌曲」**。反复出现的 *I'm everything I am because you loved me* 使它成为婚礼、母亲节歌单与 KTV「感恩向」ballad 的经典。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **stand by someone** | 支持某人；守在一边 | 比 *support* 更带「不离不弃」意味 |
| **stood by me** | 始终支持我 | *stand* 过去式；婚礼、感谢信高频 |
| **made me see** | 让我看清 | *make + 人 + 动词* = 使某人做某事 |
| **made right** | 纠正；使变对 | *wrong that you made right* = 你帮我改过来的错 |
| **held me up** | 托住我；支撑我 | 物理或情感上的「撑住」 |
| **saw me through** | 陪我熬过 | 习语；*see someone through* = 帮某人度过难关 |
| **lifted me up** | 把我托举起来 | 与 *held me up* 呼应，偏「拉高、提升」 |
| **gave me faith** | 给我信念 | *faith* 此处指信心，非仅宗教 |
| **everything I am** | 我如今的一切／我这个人 | 强调身份与成长皆源于对方 |
| **touch the sky** | 触天；达到巅峰 | 浪漫夸张；常接在 *wings / fly* 之后 |
| **out of reach** | 够不着；遥不可及 | *no star was out of reach* = 没有达不到的目标 |
| **stood tall** | 挺直腰杆；昂首挺立 | 因有人支持而恢复尊严 |
| **this much is true** | 这一点千真万确 | 承认懂得不多，但核心信念坚定 |
| **a light in the dark** | 黑暗中的光 | 比喻困境中的希望与指引 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *You were my strength when I was weak* 四句排比结构清楚，适合练**平行句型**与**重音**：每句重读 *strength / voice / eyes / best*。
- Dion 的 **belting** 在长句尾（*because you loved me*）会拉高音——跟唱前先分句换气，别一口气憋到底。
- *stood by me / saw me through / lifted me up* 三个短语可连成一组「感恩动词」，对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里表达陪伴的句型。
- KTV 难点在 Bridge 后反复副歌的情绪递进；先练 Verse 1 的排比 *For all those times…*，再进副歌更稳。
