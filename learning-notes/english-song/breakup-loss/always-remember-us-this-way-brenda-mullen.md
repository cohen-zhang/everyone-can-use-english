## 歌手简介 · About Brenda Mullen

**English**

**Brenda Mullen** is a vocalist who has released **studio cover versions** of popular songs for streaming platforms. Her recording of **"Always Remember Us This Way"** (2019) presents the song as a standalone single, with lyrics and melody identical to the original. While Mullen is not the song's writer or the film performer, her version is useful for learners who want a clear, singable take on this modern standard. For the song's full backstory—its creation for *A Star Is Born* and Lady Gaga's Ally character—see the **original Lady Gaga recording** linked above.

**中文**

**Brenda Mullen** 是一位演唱者，在流媒体平台发行过多首流行歌曲的**录音室翻唱版**。她 2019 年的 **"Always Remember Us This Way"** 以单曲形式呈现，歌词与旋律与原版一致。Mullen 并非歌曲作者，也不是电影原声演唱者；但她的版本人声清晰，适合学习者跟唱这首当代抒情标准曲。想了解歌曲完整背景——为电影 *A Star Is Born* 创作、Lady Gaga 饰演 Ally 等——请参阅上方链接的 **Lady Gaga 原唱版**笔记。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Always Remember Us This Way"** was written by **Lady Gaga**, **Natalie Hemby**, **Hillary Lindsey**, and **Lori McKenna** for the 2018 film ***A Star Is Born***, performed in the movie by Gaga as the rising singer **Ally**. It is a **piano-driven country ballad** about holding on to a relationship at its brightest—*when the sun goes down and the band won't play, I'll always remember us this way*. The soundtrack single reached the **Billboard Hot 100** and became one of the decade's most covered love songs. **This playlist entry is Brenda Mullen's cover** (released July 2019); the lyrics below match the original and are ideal for KTV and commute listening practice.

**中文**

**"Always Remember Us This Way"** 由 **Lady Gaga**、**Natalie Hemby**、**Hillary Lindsey**、**Lori McKenna** 为 2018 年电影 **《一个明星的诞生》（*A Star Is Born*）** 创作，片中由 Gaga 饰演的崭露头角歌手 **Ally** 演唱。这是一首**钢琴主导的乡村抒情曲**，写「把感情最亮的样子留在心里」——*when the sun goes down and the band won't play, I'll always remember us this way*（日头落下、乐队散场，我仍会记得我们那时的样子）。原声单曲登上 **Billboard Hot 100**，成为十年间被翻唱最多的情歌之一。**本播放列表条目为 Brenda Mullen 翻唱版**（2019 年 7 月发行）；下列歌词与原版一致，适合 KTV 与通勤跟唱练习。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **always remember us this way** | 永远记得我们那时的样子 | 歌题核心；*this way* = 此刻的美好状态 |
| **choked up** | 哽咽；激动/难过说不出话 | 情绪堵在喉咙；常接 *can't find the words* |
| **can't find the words** | 找不到话说 | 太感动或太伤心时的常用表达 |
| **when the sun goes down** | 当太阳落下 | 字面日落；也象征辉煌时刻结束 |
| **the band won't play** | 乐队不再演奏 | 派对/庆典散场；关系或人生阶段落幕 |
| **California gold** | 加州金子 | 比喻埋藏深处的珍贵情感或潜能 |
| **catch on fire** | 燃烧起来 | 被对方的目光或爱意点燃 |
| **lovers in the night** | 夜里的恋人 | 浪漫但短暂的亲密时刻 |
| **don't know how to rhyme** | 不会押韵 | 自嘲不是诗人，但仍在努力表达爱 |
| **the whole world fades** | 整个世界淡去 | 眼里只剩对方；经典情歌意象 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I'll always remember us this way* 旋律平缓、重复多，**难度易**，适合通勤跟唱和 KTV 入门。
- 注意 **choked up** 与 **find the words** 几乎固定搭配，可整句记：*I'm all choked up but I can't find the words*。
- Mullen 版人声较干净，适合先练熟再对照 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/always-remember-us-this-way-lady-gaga|Lady Gaga 原唱]] 感受电影里的情绪张力。
- 想积累**分手与怀念**句型，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的记忆与告别表达。
