## 歌手简介 · About Eminem

**English**

**Eminem** (born **Marshall Bruce Mathers III**, 17 October 1972) is an American **rapper, songwriter, and record producer** from Detroit, Michigan. He broke through with *The Slim Shady LP* (1999) and became one of the best-selling artists of all time, known for rapid-fire rhyme schemes, dark humour, and autobiographical storytelling. His alter ego **Slim Shady** voices anger and shock; as Eminem he often writes about poverty, addiction, fame, and family. He has won **15 Grammy Awards** and an **Oscar** for *Lose Yourself*. **Rihanna** (Robyn Fenty), featured on this track, is a Barbadian **pop and R&B** superstar known for global hits from *Umbrella* to *Anti*; her chorus here voices the victim's side of a destructive romance.

**中文**

**Eminem**（本名 **Marshall Bruce Mathers III**，1972 年 10 月 17 日生）是美国**说唱歌手、词曲作者与制作人**，来自密歇根州底特律。1999 年凭 *The Slim Shady LP* 走红，以极快押韵、黑色幽默与自传式叙事成为史上最畅销艺人之一。分身 **Slim Shady** 负责愤怒与挑衅；以 Eminem 名义则常写贫困、成瘾、名利与家庭。曾获 **15 座格莱美**及 *Lose Yourself* 的**奥斯卡奖**。本曲合作的 **Rihanna**（Robyn Fenty）是巴巴多斯**流行 / R&B** 巨星，从 *Umbrella* 到 *Anti* 作品无数；此处副歌唱出关系中受害一方的心声。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Love the Way You Lie"** was released on **18 June 2010** as the second single from Eminem's album **Recovery**. Skylar Grey wrote the hook; Eminem expanded it into a **duet** with Rihanna about a **toxic, on-again-off-again relationship** marked by passion, jealousy, apologies, and **domestic violence**. The title is ironic: "loving the way you lie" means staying hooked despite knowing the promises are false. The song spent **seven weeks at No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and became one of the defining hits of the 2010s. **Content warning:** lyrics describe physical abuse, threats, and explicit language.

**中文**

**"Love the Way You Lie"** 于 **2010 年 6 月 18 日**发行，为专辑 **Recovery** 第二支单曲。Skylar Grey 写了副歌旋律与核心句；Eminem 将其扩展为与 Rihanna 的**对唱**，写一段**有毒、分分合合**的关系——激情、嫉妒、道歉与**家暴**交织。歌名带讽刺：「爱你的谎言」指明知对方说谎仍离不开。单曲在 Billboard Hot 100 **连冠七周**，是 2010 年代最具代表性的热单之一。**内容提示：** 歌词涉及肢体暴力、威胁及粗口。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **love the way you lie** | 爱你撒谎的样子 | 歌名核心；ironic = 明知说谎仍离不开 |
| **windpipe** | 气管；喉咙 | 比 *throat* 更具体、更痛感的意象 |
| **huffin' paint** | 吸油漆（致幻） | 比喻成瘾、自我毁灭 |
| **resuscitate** | 使复苏；抢救 | 医学词；此处指情感上「救回来」 |
| **snap** | 失控；突然爆发 | *I snapped* = 我崩了 / 动手了 |
| **lay hands on** | 对……动手（常指暴力） | 书面感较强，比 *hit* 更正式 |
| **stoop so low** | 堕落到那种地步 | *stoop* = 弯腰，引申「降低人格」 |
| **warm fuzzy feeling** | 暖心、软乎乎的感觉 | 恋爱初期常用 |
| **spew venom** | 吐出毒液般的话 | *venom* = 毒液；形容恶语 |
| **broken record** | 卡住的唱片；老话重复 | *sound like a broken record* = 翻来覆去说 |
| **show restraint** | 克制自己 | 家暴语境里指「控制暴力冲动」 |
| **windowpane** | 窗玻璃 | 双关 *pain*（痛），说唱常用 wordplay |
| **tornado meets a volcano** | 龙卷风遇上火山 | 比喻两个激烈性格相撞 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌由 **Rihanna** 演唱，旋律清楚，适合先练副歌再攻 Eminem 的快嘴主歌。
- Eminem 的主歌 **语速极快**，建议 0.75× 跟读，注意 *gonna / 'em / 'cause* 等弱读。
- 歌里大量 **矛盾修辞**（爱痛并存、道歉与威胁连着说），可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 练「愤怒 + 愧疚」的表达。
- *windowpane* 与 *pain* 的谐音是刻意 **wordplay**；读歌词时可单独标出。
- 内容涉及家暴，**仅作语言与叙事学习**；勿在不当场合引用暴力威胁句（如最后一段）。
