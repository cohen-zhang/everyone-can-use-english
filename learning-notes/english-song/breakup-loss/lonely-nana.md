## 歌手简介 · About NANA

**English**

**Nana** (Nana Kwame Abrokwa, born 5 October 1968 in Accra, Ghana) is a **Ghanaian-German** rapper and DJ, also known as **Darkman**. *"Nana"* is a Ghanaian title of nobility, not a given first name. He moved to **Hamburg** at age ten, started as a hip-hop club DJ, and briefly rapped in the eurodance project Darkness before going solo on the **Booya Music** label. His self-titled debut album *Nana* (1997) went Gold in Germany and Switzerland; the single **"Lonely"** made him one of the defining voices of **1990s Euro-rap**. He later founded **Darkman Records** and continues to release and tour in Europe.

**中文**

**Nana**（Nana Kwame Abrokwa，1968 年 10 月 5 日生于加纳 Accra）是**加纳裔德国**说唱歌手与 DJ，亦称 **Darkman**。*"Nana"* 是加纳贵族头衔，并非本名。十岁移居**汉堡**，先在嘻哈俱乐部当 DJ，曾短暂加入欧陆舞曲组合 Darkness，后以 **Booya Music** 厂牌出道。同名首专 *Nana*（1997）在德国与瑞士获金唱片；单曲 **"Lonely"** 使他成为 **1990 年代 Euro-rap** 的代表声音之一。后创立 **Darkman Records**，仍在欧洲发行与巡演。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Lonely"** was released on **7 April 1997** as the second single from Nana's debut album *Nana*. Produced and co-written by **Bülent Aris** and **Toni Cottura**, it blends **pop-rap** verses with a sung chorus (*I am lonely… God help me to survive*). The hook is performed by **Alexandra Prince**, **Jan van der Toorn**, and **Mazaya** (not always credited as featured artists). It topped charts in **Germany** and **Switzerland**, went **Platinum** in Germany, and became one of the best-selling German rap singles of the era. Themes: lost friendship, street distrust (*snakes*), faith, and isolation. **Album version ~6:21**; radio edits are shorter. Dense slang → **level/hard**.

**中文**

**"Lonely"** 于 **1997 年 4 月 7 日**发行，为 Nana 首专 *Nana* 的第二支单曲。由 **Bülent Aris**、**Toni Cottura** 制作并合写，**pop-rap** 说唱段叠上合唱副歌（*I am lonely… God help me to survive*）。副歌由 **Alexandra Prince**、**Jan van der Toorn**、**Mazaya** 演唱（常未标客串标注）。单曲登上**德国**与**瑞士**榜首，德国获**白金**，是当时最畅销的德语区说唱单曲之一。主题：友谊失落、街头戒备（*snakes*）、信仰与孤独。**专辑版约 6:21**；电台版更短。俚语密度高 → **难度难**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I am lonely** | 我好孤独 | 副歌核心；比 *I feel lonely* 更直白强调 |
| **help me to survive** | 帮我活下去 | 求助；生存层面的绝望 |
| **count on you** | 指望你；依靠你 | *If I fall, I could count on you* |
| **with no fear** | 毫不害怕 | 信任的程度 |
| **who's fake** | 谁是假的 | 看清虚伪的人 |
| **snakes** | 阴险的人；小人 | 嘻哈俚语：背后捅刀的人 |
| **all for one, one for all** | 人人为我，我为人人 | 来自《三个火枪手》的口号 |
| **trippin' on me** | 跟我过不去；找我麻烦 | *trip on* = 刁难、针对 |
| **rescue me** | 救我 | 信仰/情感双重含义 |
| **pay the price** | 付出代价 | 为自己的行为负责 |
| **the Lord is my shepherd** | 耶和华是我的牧者 | 引自《诗篇》23；歌词常拼 *shepard* |
| **what goes up must come down** | 上去的终究会下来 | 兴衰循环的谚语 |
| **in my shoes** | 站在我的位置 | *if you were in my shoes* = 设身处地 |
| **leave me alone** | 让我一个人待着 | 拒绝打扰；与副歌 *lonely* 呼应 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌极重复、旋律简单，可先**只跟唱副歌**；主歌语速快、俚语密，属 **level/hard**。
- 流媒体歌词有 OCR/拼写噪声（*stiked*→stuck、*shepard*→shepherd、*Cheppin'*→Steppin'），以听感与上表为准。
- *snakes / diggity / on my jock* 等 90 年代嘻哈口语，精听时对照 Key Phrases，不必逐词模仿粗话场合。
- 主题是「曾经有真朋友，如今只剩孤独与信仰」；可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于孤独与 trust 的表达。
