## 歌手简介 · About Idina Menzel

**English**

**Idina Menzel** (born 30 May 1971, New York) is an American **singer** and **Broadway actress** celebrated for a powerhouse **belt** and roles that define modern musical theatre. She originated **Maureen** in *Rent* (1996) and **Elphaba** in *Wicked* (2003), earning a Tony Award for the latter. Mainstream fame exploded when she voiced **Queen Elsa** in Disney's **Frozen** (2013) and sang **"Let It Go"**, which won the **Academy Award** and **Grammy** for Best Original Song. Menzel has released several solo albums and returned to Elsa in *Frozen II* (2019). Her voice spans theatre, pop, and soundtrack work—known for emotional clarity, sustained high notes, and making Broadway technique accessible to global audiences.

**中文**

**Idina Menzel**（1971 年 5 月 30 日生，纽约）是美国**歌手**与**百老汇演员**，以极具穿透力的 **belt 高音** 和定义当代音乐剧的角色著称。她在 *Rent*（1996）饰演 **Maureen**、在 *Wicked*（2003）饰演 **Elphaba** 并凭后者获 **托尼奖**。在迪士尼 **Frozen**（《冰雪奇缘》，2013）中为 **艾莎女王** 配音并演唱 **"Let It Go"** 后，她走向全球大众市场；该曲获**奥斯卡**与**格莱美「最佳原创歌曲」**。Menzel 发行过多张个人专辑，并于 *Frozen II*（2019）再次献声艾莎。她的嗓音横跨剧场、流行与影视原声——以情感清晰、长音高音稳定，把百老汇技法带给全球听众。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Let It Go"** was written by **Kristen Anderson-Lopez** and **Robert Lopez** for Disney's **Frozen** (2013). Sung by **Idina Menzel** as Elsa flees into the mountains, it is a **transformation anthem**: Elsa has hidden her ice magic for years (*Conceal, don't feel*), but isolation becomes liberation—*Let it go, let it go / Can't hold it back anymore*. The song charts a shift from shame to self-acceptance: *No right, no wrong, no rules for me / I'm free*. It won the **Oscar** and **Grammy** for Best Original Song, topped the Billboard Hot 100 (peaking at No. 5), and became a global phenomenon—sung in dozens of languages, parodied endlessly, and embraced as a metaphor for coming out and embracing one's true self. Two versions exist: Elsa's **film version** and a **end-credits pop mix**; this note follows the film lyrics.

**中文**

**"Let It Go"** 由 **Kristen Anderson-Lopez** 与 **Robert Lopez** 为迪士尼 **Frozen**（《冰雪奇缘》，2013）创作。由 **Idina Menzel** 演唱，在艾莎逃入山中时响起，是一首**蜕变 anthem**：艾莎多年隐藏冰雪魔力（*Conceal, don't feel*），孤立却换来解放——*Let it go, let it go / Can't hold it back anymore*。歌曲记录从羞耻到自我接纳的转变：*No right, no wrong, no rules for me / I'm free*。获**奥斯卡**与**格莱美「最佳原创歌曲」**，登上 Billboard Hot 100（最高第 5），成为全球现象——被译成数十种语言、无数次恶搞，也被视为出柜与拥抱真我的隐喻。存在电影版与片尾流行混音两版；本笔记依**电影版歌词**。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Let it go* 重复极高，适合**通勤**跟唱；难点在桥段与第三段副歌的 **belting** 高音——先哼旋律找换气点，再填词。
- *Conceal, don't feel* 三词短句可练**命令式排比**；与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里压抑、释放对照阅读。
- 结尾 *The cold never bothered me anyway* 语气要轻松、带点挑衅——不是抱怨冷，而是「我根本不在乎」。
- 电影版与 Demi Lovato 片尾版歌词略有不同；本笔记为 **Idina Menzel 电影版**，KTV 选曲时认准版本。
- *I'm free* 单独一句是全歌情绪顶点，唱之前可停顿半拍再爆发。
