## 歌手简介 · About Emilia

**English**

**Emilia** (full name **Hanna Emilia Rydberg-Mitiku**, born 5 January 1978) is a **Swedish pop and soul singer** from Stockholm. Her mother is Swedish and her father is the Ethiopian singer **Teshome Mitiku**; she grew up surrounded by jazz and traditional Ethiopian music. She studied at the **Adolf Fredriks Music School** for eight years and was discovered in 1996 by **Lars Anderson** (son of ABBA manager Stig Anderson). She released her debut single **"Big Big World"** in September 1998 at age 20—it became the **fastest-selling single in Swedish history** and topped charts across Europe. She later performed as **Emilia Mitiku** and competed in Sweden's **Melodifestivalen** (2009). *Big Big World* remains her signature international hit.

**中文**

**Emilia**（全名 **Hanna Emilia Rydberg-Mitiku**，1978 年 1 月 5 日生）是瑞典**流行与灵魂乐歌手**，生于斯德哥尔摩。母亲为瑞典人，父亲为埃塞俄比亚歌手 **Teshome Mitiku**；自幼接触爵士与埃塞俄比亚传统音乐。她就读 **Adolf Fredriks Music School** 八年，1996 年被 **Lars Anderson**（ABBA 经理人 Stig Anderson 之子）发掘。1998 年 9 月以 20 岁之龄发行出道单曲 **"Big Big World"**，成为**瑞典史上销售最快的单曲**，并在欧洲多国夺冠。后亦以 **Emilia Mitiku** 活动，2009 年参加瑞典 **Melodifestivalen**。*Big Big World* 仍是她最具国际知名度的代表作。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Big Big World"** was released on **17 September 1998** as the lead single from Emilia's debut album of the same name. Co-written by Emilia and **Lasse Anderson**, it is a gentle **pop ballad** about **heartbreak and loneliness** after a breakup. The singer tells herself it shouldn't be a "big deal" if her partner leaves—but she **will miss him**. Autumn imagery—**falling leaves**, **cold weather**, **rain**—mirrors her inner sadness. The chorus line *I'm a big, big girl in a big, big world* captures a fragile attempt at **maturity and independence**. The song reached **No. 1** in eight countries (including Sweden, Germany, and Norway) and **No. 5** in the UK; it won **Grammis Song of the Year** (1998) in Sweden.

**中文**

**"Big Big World"** 于 **1998 年 9 月 17 日**发行，为 Emilia 同名**首张专辑**主打歌，由 Emilia 与 **Lasse Anderson** 共同创作。这是一首温柔的**流行抒情曲**，写分手后**心碎与孤独**：嘴上对自己说「你走了也没什么大不了」，心里却**会很想你**。**落叶**、**寒冷**、**下雨**等秋日意象与内心悲伤呼应。副歌 *I'm a big, big girl in a big, big world* 像在努力表现**长大与独立**，却掩不住脆弱。单曲在瑞典、德国、挪威等 **8 国夺冠**，英国最高 **第 5**；获瑞典 **Grammis 年度歌曲**（1998）。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a big, big girl in a big, big world** | 大世界里的「大女孩」 | 歌名与副歌核心；*big* 重复加强语气，像自我安慰 |
| **It's not a big, big thing** | 不是什么大事 | *big thing* = 大事；嘴上说「没什么」常暗示其实在意 |
| **miss you much** | 非常想你 | 口语省略 *I*；比 *I miss you very much* 更歌化 |
| **the first leaf falling** | 第一片落叶 | 秋天意象；象征关系结束、季节更替 |
| **Like the way I'm feeling inside** | 就像我内心的感觉 | *the way* 引导从句，描述状态 |
| **tears are falling from my eyes** | 泪水夺眶而出 | 与 *Outside it's now raining* 形成**内外呼应** |
| **Why did it all have to end?** | 为什么一切都要结束？ | *have to* 表「不得不」；分手后的无力感 |
| **Warm like fire** | 温暖得像火 | 明喻；对比后面的 *You're gone* |
| **when I open my eyes** | 当我睁开眼 | 常指从梦或幻想回到现实 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I do, do feel that I do, do will miss you much* 语法不「标准」，是为**节奏与重复**服务；跟唱时按原词即可。
- 整首歌词汇简单、句子短，适合**初级跟唱**与练**季节/天气**词汇（*leaf, yellow, cold, rain*）。
- 想延伸练**情歌与恋爱口语**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
