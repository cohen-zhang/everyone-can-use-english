## 歌手简介 · About Em Beihold

**English**

**Em Beihold** (born 2 January 2001) is an American **singer-songwriter** from **Canoga Park, Los Angeles**. She began writing songs at age 11 and studied at the **University of Southern California (USC)** before leaving to pursue music full-time. After years of independent releases and viral moments on TikTok, her debut single **"Numb Little Bug"** (2022) broke into the **Billboard Hot 100** and became an anthem for young adults navigating **anxiety, depression, and emotional numbness**. Beihold's voice is bright and conversational; her lyrics mix dark humor with blunt honesty about therapy, medication, and feeling disconnected from life.

**中文**

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Numb Little Bug"** was released on **28 January 2022** as Em Beihold's debut single. Over a bouncy piano-pop beat, the song lists symptoms of burnout and **emotional numbness**—not feeling anything, pills that might be "too much," friends drifting away, driving through an overwhelming L.A. The chorus asks a question millions relate to: *Do you ever get a little bit tired of life?*—not suicidal, but **not happy either**, just hanging on. The title image—a **numb little bug** that still has to survive—captures functioning while hollow inside. The track peaked at **No. 46** on the Billboard Hot 100.

**中文**

**"Numb Little Bug"** 于 **2022 年 1 月 28 日**发行，是 Em Beihold 的出道单曲。在轻快的 piano-pop 节奏上，歌罗列**情感麻木**与倦怠的症状——什么都感觉不到、药是不是下猛了、朋友渐渐失联、在庞大洛杉矶里开车。副歌问出很多人共鸣的一句：*Do you ever get a little bit tired of life?*——不是想死，但也**不算开心**，只是硬撑。歌名意象——一只**麻木的小虫**仍得活下去——写「人还在运转、里面已经空了」。单曲在 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 46** 位。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **not feel a single thing** | 什么都感觉不到 | *single* 强调「一点都没有」 |
| **out of touch** | 失去联系；渐渐疏远 | 与朋友、现实脱节都可用 |
| **bound to break** | 注定会碎／会崩 | *bound to* = 很可能会 |
| **snap … like a twig** | 像折小树枝一样折断 | 比喻精神崩溃；*psyche* = 心理 |
| **hang by a thread** | 命悬一线；勉强撑着 | 习语；thread = 细线 |
| **gotta survive** | 得活下去 | *gotta* = got to，口语 |
| **fresh out of love** | 爱刚用完；爱耗尽了 | 像 *fresh out of milk* 的结构 |
| **past repair** | 修不好了；没救了 | *beyond repair* 也常见 |
| **quick repairs to cope** | 临时应付的办法 | *cope* = 应对、撑过去 |
| **stay afloat** | 不沉下去；撑住 | 字面「浮在水面」，引申「撑住生活」 |
| **broken and broke** | 又坏又穷 | 双关：*broken* 心碎／坏了，*broke* 没钱 |
| **numb little bug** | 麻木的小虫 | 歌题意象：还在动，但已麻木 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Do you ever get a little bit tired of life?* 节奏清楚，适合**跟唱**练问句语调：句末 *life?* 自然上扬。
- 歌里大量 **Like you're …** 排比，可用来练「像……但又……」的口语结构；注意 *but you don't wanna die* 里 *wanna* 的连读。
- 想延伸读**成人情绪表达**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里疲惫、麻木相关句型。
