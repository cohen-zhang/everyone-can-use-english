## 歌手简介 · About Gotye

**English**

**Gotye** (stage name of **Wouter "Wally" De Backer**, born 21 May 1980, Bruges, Belgium; raised in Australia) is a Belgian-Australian **singer-songwriter** and **multi-instrumentalist**. He records largely alone, layering samples and live instruments. After indie success with ***Like Drawing Blood*** (2006), his third album ***Making Mirrors*** (2011) made him a global name. New Zealand singer **Kimbra** (Kimbra Lee Johnson) joins on this track's second verse and shared chorus—her reply voice is half the song's emotional punch.

**中文**

**Gotye**（艺名；本名 **Wouter "Wally" De Backer**，1980 年 5 月 21 日生于比利时布鲁日，成长于澳大利亚）是比利时裔澳大利亚**创作歌手**与**多乐器演奏者**。常独自录音，叠采样与现场乐器。***Like Drawing Blood***（2006）在独立圈站稳后，第三张专辑 ***Making Mirrors***（2011）让他走向全球。新西兰歌手 **Kimbra**（Kimbra Lee Johnson）演唱本曲第二段与部分副歌——她的「回应声」几乎撑起一半情绪冲击。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Somebody That I Used to Know"** was released **5 July 2011** as the second single from ***Making Mirrors***. Written and produced by Gotye (with a guitar sample from Luiz Bonfá's *"Seville"*), it is a **breakup anthem** told in two voices: he feels cut off and erased; she answers that he was hung up on the past. The title phrase means *a person who used to matter—and now is just someone from my past*. It topped charts worldwide, won **Record of the Year** and **Best Pop Duo/Group Performance** at the 55th Grammy Awards, and became one of the defining pop songs of the early 2010s.

**中文**

**"Somebody That I Used to Know"** 于 2011 年 7 月 5 日作为 ***Making Mirrors*** 第二支单曲发行。由 Gotye 创作制作（吉他采样自 Luiz Bonfá 的 *"Seville"*）。这是一首**分手神曲**，用双声部讲故事：他觉得被切断、被当没发生过；她回应说是他放不下过去。标题短语的自然意思是：**曾经重要、如今只是「我认识过的某个人」**。全球多国榜首，获第 55 届格莱美 **年度制作** 与 **最佳流行组合**，成为 2010 年代初标志性流行曲之一。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **now and then** | 偶尔；时不时 | 比 *sometimes* 更书面一点，口语也常用 |
| **felt so happy you could die** | 开心到可以去死 | 夸张；= 开心极了 |
| **lonely in your company** | 跟你在一起却孤独 | *in your company* = 有你陪伴时 |
| **an ache I still remember** | 我仍记得的隐痛 | *ache* 比 *pain* 更闷、更久 |
| **addicted to a certain kinda sadness** | 对某一种悲伤上瘾 | 分手后自我消耗的精准说法 |
| **resignation to the end** | 认命地等着结局 | *resignation* = 无奈接受 |
| **could not make sense** | 说不通；理不清 | 关系/局面讲不通 |
| **glad it was over** | 结束了反而松口气 | 诚实、反鸡汤 |
| **cut me off** | 把我切断；断联 | 电话/关系都可用 |
| **make out like…** | 装作……；表现得好像…… | ≠ make out（亲热）；此处 = pretend |
| **we were nothin'** | 我们什么都不是 | *nothin'* = nothing |
| **treat me like a stranger** | 把我当陌生人 | 分手后常见痛点 |
| **feels so rough** | 感觉太难受 | *rough* = 难受、不好过 |
| **stoop so low** | 做得这么下作；堕落到这地步 | 道德/手段上的「低」 |
| **somebody that I used to know** | 我曾经认识的某个人 | **自然译法**；勿译成「我过去常常认识的某人」 |
| **screwed me over** | 坑我；伤我；耍我 | 口语；偏负面对待 |
| **had me believin'** | 让我相信…… | *have sb doing* = 使某人处于某状态 |
| **readin' into every word** | 把每个字都过度解读 | *read into* = 过度引申意思 |
| **hung up on somebody** | 还纠结于某人；放不下 | *hung up on* = 无法释怀 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **难度：** 中——旋律好跟，但词密、连读多（*used to know*、*make out like*）；原 catalog 标 hard，精研时按 medium–hard 处理即可。
- **标题怎么译：** *somebody that I used to know* = **「我曾经认识的某个人」** / **「一个过去的人」**——强调关系已成过去，不是字面「我过去常常认识」。
- **双视角：** Gotye 段写「被抹掉」的委屈；Kimbra 段翻案——*you screwed me over*、*hung up on somebody*。听第二遍时专门跟 Kimbra 声线。
- **高频口语：** *cut me off*、*make out like*、*stoop so low*、*hung up on* 都可直接进日常分手/断联话题。
- **对照：** 若想练「我错了」式后悔，听 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/when-i-was-your-man-bruno-mars|When I Was Your Man]]；本曲更偏「你把我当陌生人」。
