## 歌手简介 · About Rhys Lewis

**English**

**Rhys Lewis** (born 18 March 1991) is a British **singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist** from Oxfordshire, England. He studied at the **London Centre of Contemporary Music**, signed with **Decca Records** in 2016, and scrapped most of his early demos to find an authentic sound. His debut album **Things I Chose to Remember** (2020) was recorded on **analogue tape** and blends **neo-soul, pop, blues**, and **Americana**. Lewis is known for heartbreak ballads like **"No Right to Love You"** and a soulful voice that critics compare to classic soul singers. He has also featured on Norwegian DJ **Kygo**'s track **"Hurting"**.

**中文**

**Rhys Lewis**（1991 年 3 月 18 日生）是英国**创作型歌手兼多乐器演奏者**，来自牛津郡。他就读 **London Centre of Contemporary Music**，2016 年签约 **Decca Records**，并舍弃大部分早期小样以找到真实声音。首张专辑 **Things I Chose to Remember**（2020）用**模拟磁带**录制，融合 **新灵魂、流行、蓝调** 与 **美式民谣**。他以 **"No Right to Love You"** 等心碎抒情曲和灵魂感嗓音著称。亦曾参与挪威 DJ **Kygo** 单曲 **"Hurting"**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

This version is Rhys Lewis's **cover** of **Noah Cyrus**'s **"July"**, recorded for an **Apple Music Home Session** and released **1 May 2020**. The original (2019) is a folk-infused ballad about staying in a **toxic relationship** out of fear of change—*You remind me every day I'm not enough, but I still stay*. Lewis strips the arrangement to voice and guitar, letting the confessional lyrics breathe. The repeated plea *If you want me to leave, just tell me to leave and baby I'll go* captures the paralysis of knowing you should walk away but waiting for permission. Noah wrote the song while processing a relationship that ended around **July 4th**.

**中文**

本版为 Rhys Lewis **翻唱** **Noah Cyrus** 的 **"July"**，收录于 **Apple Music Home Session**，**2020 年 5 月 1 日**发行。原曲（2019）是一首民谣气质抒情曲，写因害怕改变而困在**有毒关系**里——*You remind me every day I'm not enough, but I still stay*（你每天提醒我不够好，我却还留着）。Lewis 将编曲简化为人声与吉他，让自白式歌词更突出。反复吟唱的 *If you want me to leave, just tell me to leave and baby I'll go* 捕捉「明知该走，却等对方开口」的无力感。Noah 写歌时正处理一段在 **7 月 4 日**前后结束的关系。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- Home Session 版只有人声与吉他，适合练**清晰咬字**；副歌 *I'm not enough, but I still stay* 是全曲情感核心。
- *hold my breath / count to ten* 开头两句可单独练**冷静克制**的口语节奏，再接 *throw back beers* 的对比。
- 结尾 *Baby I'll go* 与 *I'm not enough, but I still stay* 交替重复，跟唱时注意**渐弱**，不要每遍都同样用力。
- 想了解原曲背景可对照 Noah Cyrus 2019 版；她写这首歌时正回忆一段在 **7 月 4 日**结束的关系。
