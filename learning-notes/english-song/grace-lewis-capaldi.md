## 歌手简介 · About Lewis Capaldi

**English**

**Lewis Capaldi** (born 7 October 1996) is a **Scottish singer-songwriter** from Bathgate, West Lothian. He began posting cover songs on **SoundCloud** and **YouTube** as a teenager and was discovered after his track **"Bruises"** went viral in 2017. His debut album **Divinely Uninspired To A Hellish Extent** (2019) became one of the UK's best-selling records of the decade. Capaldi is known for a **powerful, raspy voice** and emotionally direct ballads about heartbreak and devotion; he won the **Brit Award for Best New Artist** in 2020 and has cited **Adele** and **Travis** as influences. He took a career break in 2023 to focus on **Tourette syndrome** and mental health before returning with new music.

**中文**

**Lewis Capaldi**（1996 年 10 月 7 日生）是苏格兰**创作型歌手**，来自 **West Lothian** 的 **Bathgate**。少年时在 **SoundCloud** 与 **YouTube** 发翻唱，2017 年 **"Bruises"** 病毒传播后被发掘。首张专辑 **Divinely Uninspired To A Hellish Extent**（2019）成为英国十年最畅销唱片之一。他以**有力、沙哑的嗓音**和直白的失恋、深情 ballad 著称；2020 年获 **全英音乐奖「最佳新人」**；影响包括 **Adele** 与 **Travis**。2023 年因 **妥瑞症** 与心理健康暂别乐坛，之后回归发新曲。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Grace"** was released on **21 September 2018** as Capaldi's second major single, months before his debut album. Co-written with **TMS** (Tom Barnes, Ben Kohn, Peter Kelleher) and **Romans**, the track is a **gospel-tinged pop ballad** about clinging to someone who feels like salvation—*'Till I found salvation in the form of your grace*. The word **grace** works on two levels: religious **forgiveness / redemption**, and the lover as the one grace that keeps the singer from falling apart. Lines like *I'm not ready to be just another of your mistakes* and *Don't take it away* show fear of being discarded once the relationship frays. The song peaked at **No. 5** on the UK Singles Chart and helped build momentum toward **"Someone You Loved"**.

**中文**

**"Grace"** 于 **2018 年 9 月 21 日**发行，是 Capaldi 第二支重要单曲，早于首张专辑数月。与 **TMS**（Tom Barnes、Ben Kohn、Peter Kelleher）及 **Romans** 共同创作，是一首带**福音感**的流行 ballad：紧抓住那个像救赎一样的人——*'Till I found salvation in the form of your grace*（直到我在你的恩典里找到救赎）。**grace** 一语双关：宗教里的**恩典／赦免**，也指爱人本身。 *I'm not ready to be just another of your mistakes*（我还没准备好当你又一个错误）与 *Don't take it away*（别把它拿走）写怕被抛弃。单曲在英国榜最高 **第 5**，为 **"Someone You Loved"** 铺路。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **another of your mistakes** | 你又一个错误 | 把自己当成对方感情里的「失手」 |
| **drown someone out** | 盖过／淹掉（声音或念头） | 原意压过噪音；歌里指没法把对方从脑海赶走 |
| **fall victim to** | 成为……的牺牲品；栽在……里 | 正式一点的说法，歌里带被动受害感 |
| **ain't ready to stop** | 还不想停 | *ain't* = am not；口语跟唱常见 |
| **got nothing but X on my mind** | 脑子里只有 X | 固定强调句型 |
| **on the verge of** | 几乎要；濒临 | *on the verge of bleeding you out* = 几乎榨干你 |
| **bleed someone out** | 把某人榨干（到极点） | 暴力意象，形容感情耗竭 |
| **find common ground** | 找到共同点；达成共识 | 关系修复常用语 |
| **let the pieces fall out of place** | 让一切分崩离析 | *pieces* = 生活的碎片／秩序 |
| **a breath removed from** | 差一口气就…… | 离某状态极近 |
| **going to waste** | 荒废掉；白白浪费 | 指人生或感情走向毁灭 |
| **salvation in the form of** | ……形态的救赎 | 宗教词 *salvation* 用在爱情语境 |
| **grace** | 恩典；优雅；宽宥 | 歌题双关：宗教恩典 + 对方这个人 |
| **colour your comfort** | 染上色你的安逸 | *comfort* = 舒适区；靠太近会打乱对方节奏 |
| **kept undercover** | 一直被藏着 | 对方真实一面没公开 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Your grace* 重复多遍，**KTV 友好**；先练 *Don't take it away* 的元音拖长与重音。
- *drown you out* 与 *fall victim to the sound of your love* 可连读：注意 *sound of your* 的轻读。
- 与 #49 **Someone You Loved** 对照：两首都在首张专辑语境里写「离不开某人」；Grace 偏**救赎与害怕被弃**，Someone You Loved 偏**失去后的空洞**。
- 想积累**恋爱与挽回口语**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 相关句型。
