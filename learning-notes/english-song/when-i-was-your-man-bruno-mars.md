## 歌手简介 · About Bruno Mars

**English**

**Bruno Mars** (born **Peter Gene Hernandez**, 8 October 1985, Honolulu, Hawaii) is an American **singer-songwriter**, **producer**, and **multi-instrumentalist**. Raised in a musical family, he moved to Los Angeles in 2003 and broke through as part of the production team **The Smeezingtons** before his solo debut ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans*** (2010). Mars blends **pop**, **R&B**, **funk**, **soul**, and **doo-wop**; he has won multiple **Grammy Awards** and is known for high-energy live shows. His second album ***Unorthodox Jukebox*** (2012) showed a wider range—from upbeat funk to stripped-down piano ballads like this one.

**中文**

**Bruno Mars**（本名 **Peter Gene Hernandez**，1985 年 10 月 8 日生于夏威夷檀香山）是美国**创作歌手**、**制作人**、**多乐器演奏者**。成长于音乐家庭，2003 年赴洛杉矶；先以制作组合 **The Smeezingtons** 崭露头角，再以个人专辑 ***Doo-Wops & Hooligans***（2010）爆红。风格融合 **流行、R&B、放克、灵魂、doo-wop**；多次获**格莱美**，现场以高能量著称。第二张专辑 ***Unorthodox Jukebox***（2012）跨度更大——从轻快放克到本曲这种几乎只剩钢琴的后悔情歌。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"When I Was Your Man"** is from ***Unorthodox Jukebox*** (2012), released as a single on **15 January 2013**. Written by Mars with **Philip Lawrence**, **Ari Levine**, and **Andrew Wyatt**, and produced by **The Smeezingtons**, it is a sparse **piano ballad** about regret: the narrator realizes too late that he failed to show love—flowers, time, dancing—and now watches his ex happy with someone else. It topped the **Billboard Hot 100** and was nominated for **Best Pop Solo Performance** at the 56th Grammy Awards. Ideal for practicing *should've* regret patterns and clear, emotional phrasing.

**中文**

**"When I Was Your Man"** 收录于 ***Unorthodox Jukebox***（2012），2013 年 1 月 15 日作为单曲发行。由 Mars 与 **Philip Lawrence**、**Ari Levine**、**Andrew Wyatt** 合写，**The Smeezingtons** 制作。这是一首几乎只靠钢琴的**后悔情歌**：叙述者太晚才明白自己没好好爱——没送花、没陪时间、没带她跳舞——如今只能看着前任和别人幸福。单曲登顶 **Billboard Hot 100**，并获第 56 届格莱美 **Best Pop Solo Performance** 提名。特别适合练 *should've* 后悔句式与清晰、带情绪的发音。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **feels just a little bit bigger** | 感觉稍微空/大了一点 | 同一张床却显得空——分手后的空间感 |
| **tear me down** | 把我击垮；让我难受 | 口语；也可指拆毁 |
| **my heart breaks a little** | 心会轻轻碎一下 | 比 *heartbroken* 更轻、更日常 |
| **too young, too dumb to realize** | 太年轻、太傻，没明白 | 后悔叙事常用句式 |
| **should've bought you flowers** | 本该给你买花 | *should have* 缩略；后悔核心句 |
| **should've gave you all my hours** | 本该把时间都给你 | 歌词用 *gave*（非标准 *given*）；跟唱照原词 |
| **when I had the chance** | 在我还有机会的时候 | 与 *should've* 搭配极常见 |
| **all you wanted to do was dance** | 你只想跳舞 | *all … want to do is …* 句型 |
| **dancing with another man** | 和别人跳舞 | 歌的刺痛点 |
| **pride / ego / selfish ways** | 骄傲 / 自我 / 自私的那一套 | 自我检讨词串 |
| **walk out my life** | 走出我的生活；离开我 | = walk out of my life |
| **clean up the mess I made** | 收拾自己捅的娄子 | *mess* = 烂摊子 |
| **it haunts me** | 它缠着我；挥之不去 | 后悔/创伤常用 |
| **I'll be the first to say that I was wrong** | 我愿意第一个承认是我错了 | 道歉句，可直接用 |
| **much too late** | 已经太晚了 | *much too* = 远远太…… |
| **when I was your man** | 在我还是你男人的时候 | 歌名句；身份已成过去 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **难度：** 易——语速慢、咬字清楚，钢琴伴奏几乎不挡人声；适合通勤跟唱与精研发音。
- **语法焦点：** 整首歌是 *should've + V* 后悔句式的活教材；注意歌词 *Should've gave* 是口语非标准，写作请用 *should've given*。
- **情绪弧线：** Verse 写「空」与「听到名字就碎」→ Chorus 列清单式后悔 → Bridge 承认错误 → 末段把祝福转给新男友（*I hope he…*），比单纯抱怨更成熟。
- **Bruno 对照：** 同文件夹还有轻快的 [[learning-notes/english-song/marry-you-bruno-mars|Marry You]]、[[learning-notes/english-song/treasure-bruno-mars|Treasure]]，以及 [[learning-notes/english-song/catch-a-grenade-bruno-mars|Catch a Grenade]]；本曲是他最「安静」的后悔球。
- **延伸：** 分手后悔与道歉表达可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
