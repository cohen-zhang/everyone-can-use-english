## 歌手简介 · About Taylor Swift

**English**

**Taylor Swift** (born 13 December 1989) is an American **singer-songwriter** who moved from **country** to global **pop** while keeping narrative, diary-style lyrics. She grew up in Pennsylvania, signed to Big Machine at 15, and broke through with *Tim McGraw* (2006). The album **Fearless** (2008) made her a crossover star—**"Love Story"**, **"You Belong with Me"**, and **"Fifteen"** defined high-school heartbreak for a generation. Swift writes from a **best-friend / girl-next-door** perspective as often as from the spotlight; she has won multiple **Grammys**, including Album of the Year four times. Her re-recorded **Fearless (Taylor's Version)** (2021) revisited early hits with mature vocals.

**中文**

**Taylor Swift**（1989 年 12 月 13 日生）是美国**创作歌手**，从**乡村**跨入全球**流行**，始终保留叙事性、日记式歌词。宾州长大，15 岁签约 Big Machine，2006 年以 *Tim McGraw* 出道。专辑 **Fearless**（2008）让她成为跨界明星——**"Love Story"**、**"You Belong with Me"**、**"Fifteen"** 定义了一代人的青春心碎。Swift 常从**闺蜜 / 邻家女孩**视角写歌，而非只有明星视角；多次获**格莱美**，包括四次专辑年奖。2021 年重录 **Fearless (Taylor's Version)**，用更成熟的嗓音重温早期热门曲。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"You Belong with Me"** was released on **18 April 2009** as the third single from *Fearless*. Swift has said it was inspired by a real phone call she overheard—a boy arguing with his girlfriend while his **best friend** (a girl) understood him better. The music video cast Swift as the **girl next door** on the bleachers opposite the cheerleader girlfriend, cementing the song's **friend-zone anthem** image. The lyrics contrast **two types of girls**—the girlfriend in *short skirts* and *high heels* versus the narrator in *T-shirts* and *sneakers* on the **bleachers**—before the chorus pleads: *If you could see that I'm the one who understands you… You belong with me*. The song peaked at **No. 2** on the Billboard Hot 100, won a **VMA** for Best Female Video, and remains one of Swift's most recognizable early hits.

**中文**

**"You Belong with Me"** 于 **2009 年 4 月 18 日**发行，为 *Fearless* 第三支单曲。Swift 称灵感来自她偷听到的一通电话——男生与女友吵架，而另一位**女性朋友**更懂他。MV 里 Swift 饰演看台边的**邻家女孩**，与啦啦队长女友形成对比，强化了**暗恋闺蜜 anthem** 的叙事。歌词对比**两类女孩**——穿短裙、高跟鞋的女友，与穿 T 恤、球鞋、坐在**看台**上的「我」——再在副歌里直白表白：*If you could see that I'm the one who understands you… You belong with me*。单曲在 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 2** 位，获 **VMA 最佳女歌手录影带**，仍是 Swift 早期最具辨识度的热门曲之一。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *You belong with me* 句式短、重复多，**通勤跟唱**极易上手；注意 *belong with* 连读。
- Pre-Chorus 对比结构 **She… I…** 适合练排比：*she wears… I wear…*，可先朗读再跟唱。
- *'bout to cry*、*isn't this easy* 等缩略在口语中极常见，跟读时练习弱读 *'bout*、*isn't*。
- 与 [[learning-notes/english-song/two-is-better-than-one-boys-like-girls|Two Is Better Than One]] 对照，可比较 Taylor 在「认定对方」叙事上的不同角度：暗恋闺蜜 vs 突然沦陷。
