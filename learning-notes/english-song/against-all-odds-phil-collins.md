## 歌手简介 · About Phil Collins

**English**

**Phil Collins** (born **30 January 1951**, London) is an English **drummer, singer, and songwriter**, best known as drummer and later lead vocalist of **Genesis** and as a hugely successful solo artist. After Genesis's 1970s prog-rock peak, Collins pursued a solo career from *Face Value* (1981), blending **pop, soft rock, and soul**. He has sold over **150 million** records worldwide, won **eight Grammy Awards**, and scored seven US **No. 1** singles. Beyond music, he acted (including *Miami Vice*) and wrote film themes. His ballads often explore heartbreak, regret, and pleading for reconciliation—qualities central to *Against All Odds*.

**中文**

**Phil Collins**（1951 年 1 月 30 日生，伦敦）是英国**鼓手、歌手、词曲作者**，以 **Genesis** 鼓手兼后期主唱及超高销量独唱生涯著称。1970 年代 Genesis 前卫摇滚巅峰后，他从 *Face Value*（1981）起发展个人路线，融合 **流行、软摇滚与灵魂乐**。全球唱片销量逾 **1.5 亿**，获 **8 座格莱美**，7 首美国 **Billboard Hot 100 冠军单曲**。亦涉足影视（含 *Miami Vice*）及电影主题曲创作。其抒情慢歌常写心碎、悔恨与求复合——正是 *Against All Odds* 的核心气质。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)"** was written and performed by Collins for the **1984 neo-noir film** *Against All Odds* (a remake of *Out of the Past*). Director **Taylor Hackford** commissioned the theme after hearing Collins's voice; the melody grew from an unused 1981 demo called *How Can You Just Sit There*. The **power ballad** finds a man begging an ex-lover to *take a look at me now*—knowing reunion is unlikely (*against all odds*) but still worth the gamble. It reached **No. 1** on the US Hot 100 (Collins's first of seven), **No. 2** in the UK, and won the **1985 Grammy for Best Pop Vocal Performance**. It was Oscar-nominated for Best Original Song.

**中文**

**"Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now)"** 由 Collins **词曲兼演唱**，为 **1984 年**新黑色电影 *Against All Odds*（翻拍自 *Out of the Past*）创作主题曲。导演 **Taylor Hackford** 因欣赏其嗓音而邀稿；旋律源自 1981 年未收录 demo *How Can You Just Sit There*。这首**强力抒情慢歌**写一个男人求前任 *take a look at me now*——明知复合希望渺茫（*against all odds*），仍愿赌一把。登美国 Hot 100 **冠军**（Collins 七首冠军之首）、英国 **第 2**，获 **1985 格莱美最佳流行男声**，并获奥斯卡最佳原创歌曲提名。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |

| **without a trace** | 不留痕迹 | 习语：消失得无影无踪 |
| **take a look at me now** | 看看现在的我 | 歌名副标题；求对方「再看一眼」 |
| **empty space** | 空白；空荡 | 比喻失去爱人后的空虚 |
| **against the odds / against all odds** | 希望渺茫；逆势 | 副歌核心；*all* 比 *the* 语气更强 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *take a look at me now* 在整首歌中出现多次且措辞略有变化，适合练**同一句型的语调递进**（从 *So* 到 *Now* 到 *Take a good look*）。
- *against the odds* 与 *against all odds* 在歌词中交替出现——跟唱时注意 Collins 有时更偏口语的 *against the odds* 发音。
- Verse 与 Chorus 之间情绪从「质问」转为「恳求」，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 loss / longing 相关表达。
- 钢琴与鼓点分明的 power ballad 结构，适合**精研**：先听清 Verse 叙事，再抓 Chorus 里 *empty space* 与 *against all odds* 的重音。
