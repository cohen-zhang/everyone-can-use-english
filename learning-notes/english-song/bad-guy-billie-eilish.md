## 歌手简介 · About Billie Eilish

**English**

**Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell** (born 18 December 2001) is an American **singer-songwriter** from Los Angeles. She and her brother **Finneas O'Connell** began writing and recording in their childhood bedroom; Finneas produces nearly all of her music. Her debut album **When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?** (2019) made her a global star at 17. Eilish is known for **whisper-pop**, bass-heavy beats, horror-inspired visuals, and lyrics that mix vulnerability with dark humor. She became the **youngest artist born in the 21st century** to top the Billboard Hot 100, and the **first woman** to sweep the Grammy "Big Four" in one night (2020).

**中文**

**Billie Eilish**（全名 Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell，2001 年 12 月 18 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**，成长于洛杉矶。她与哥哥 **Finneas O'Connell** 在童年卧室里开始写歌录歌；Finneas 几乎包办她所有作品制作。2019 年首张专辑 **When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?** 让她在 17 岁成为全球明星。她以 **耳语流行**、重低音节拍、恐怖美学视觉，以及「脆弱 + 黑色幽默」的歌词著称。她是 **21 世纪出生的歌手里最年轻** 的 Billboard Hot 100 冠军，也是格莱美史上**首位一晚横扫「四大奖」的女性**（2020）。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"bad guy"** was released on **29 March 2019** as the fifth single from *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?*. Billie and Finneas wrote and produced it at home. The song is a **playful takedown** of "tough guy" masculinity: Billie adopts the role of the real *bad guy*—more dangerous, more in control—while mocking a boyfriend who acts hard (*So you're a tough guy / Like it really rough guy*). The bass line and whispered vocal became iconic. It reached **No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 (August 2019), ending Lil Nas X's record 19-week run with *Old Town Road*, and won **Record of the Year** and **Song of the Year** at the 62nd Grammys.

**中文**

**"bad guy"** 于 **2019 年 3 月 29 日**发行，收录于 *When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?*。Billie 与 Finneas 在家完成词曲与制作。这首歌是对「硬汉人设」的**戏谑拆解**：Billie 扮演真正的 *bad guy*——更危险、更掌控——同时嘲笑男友装狠（*So you're a tough guy / Like it really rough guy*）。低音线条加耳语唱腔极具辨识度。2019 年 8 月登顶 **Billboard Hot 100**，终结 Lil Nas X《Old Town Road》19 周连冠纪录；获第 62 届格莱美**年度制作**与**年度歌曲**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **bad guy** | 坏人；反派 | 歌题核心；Billie 反讽地抢走对方的「狠人」人设 |
| **on your tippy toes** | 踮着脚尖 | 小孩用语也常见于口语；这里指偷偷摸摸 |
| **creepin' around** | 偷偷摸摸转悠 | *creep* = 蹑手蹑脚；带点不安或鬼祟感 |
| **think you're so criminal** | 你以为自己很「犯罪」 | 讽刺对方装酷；*criminal* 此处是耍帅，不是真犯罪 |
| **mad tight** | 气炸了；气死了 | 俚语，偏非正式；*tight* 此处 = angry |
| **seduce your dad** | 勾引你爸 | 夸张挑衅句；展示 *bad type* 的无所顾忌 |
| **Duh** | 废话； obvious | 拖长音的讽刺语气词；表示「这还用说」 |
| **take control** | 掌控一切 | 歌里带反讽：对方以为在掌控，其实没有 |
| **I'll be your animal** | 我当你的野兽 | 顺从表象下的权力游戏 |
| **wearing your cologne** | 喷着你的古龙水 | 结尾反转：对方前女友害怕，也许是因为 Billie 身上有他的气味 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 低音 *dun-dun-dun* 是记忆锚点；跟唱前先用手打拍子，副歌从 *So you're a tough guy* 切入最顺。
- Billie 大量 **省音与气声**（*sleepin'*, *bein'*, *creepin'*），适合练美式口语连读，不必把每个尾音咬死。
- 歌里 **-guy 排比**（tough guy / rough guy / enough guy）节奏感强，可分段跟读再提速。
- *bad guy* 标题和歌词故意 **全小写**，是艺人风格；笔记正文保留原样便于对照音频。
