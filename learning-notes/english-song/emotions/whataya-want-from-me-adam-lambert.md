## 歌手简介 · About Adam Lambert

**English**

**Adam Lambert** (born 1982 in Indianapolis, raised in San Diego) is an American singer with a wide, theatrical vocal range. He was runner-up on ***American Idol*** season 8 (**2009**), then released ***For Your Entertainment***. Since 2011 he has toured the world as the lead vocalist of **Queen + Adam Lambert**.

**中文**

**Adam Lambert**（1982 年生于印第安纳波利斯，在圣迭戈长大）是音域宽广、极具舞台感的美国歌手。**2009 年**获 ***American Idol***（美国偶像）第 8 季亚军，随后发行 ***For Your Entertainment***；2011 年起以 **Queen + Adam Lambert** 主唱身份与皇后乐队全球巡演。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Whataya Want from Me"** comes from his debut album ***For Your Entertainment*** (2009). It was written by **P!nk**, **Max Martin** and **Shellback**—P!nk originally recorded a version herself. The singer admits he is scared and messed up, but begs his partner *just don't give up*. It earned a Grammy nomination for Best Male Pop Vocal Performance. This playlist uses the **live** version from ***Glam Nation Live*** (2011).

**中文**

**"Whataya Want from Me"** 收录于他的首张专辑 ***For Your Entertainment***（2009），由 **P!nk**、**Max Martin**、**Shellback** 合写，P!nk 本人也录过一版。主角坦白自己害怕、状态糟糕，却恳求对方 *just don't give up*（别放弃我）。获格莱美最佳男歌手流行演唱提名。本歌单用的是 ***Glam Nation Live***（2011）**现场版**。

| **slow it down** | 慢一点 | *slow down* 的及物用法 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 现场版高音多、情绪起伏大，先听录音室版熟悉旋律，再跟现场版。
- *Whataya* 连读 /ˈwʌdəjə/，是美式口语 *What do you* 的典型弱读。
- *I am working it out* / *I won't let you down* 可直接用在工作沟通里安抚对方。
