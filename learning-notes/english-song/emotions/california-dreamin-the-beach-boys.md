## 歌手简介 · About The Beach Boys

**English**

**The Beach Boys** are an American **rock / pop** band formed in **Hawthorne, California**, in **1961**, centered on the Wilson brothers—**Brian**, **Dennis**, and **Carl**—with cousin **Mike Love** and friend **Al Jardine**. They defined the early-1960s **California sound**: surf, cars, and sun-drenched harmonies. Brian Wilson's writing and studio craft later pushed them into art-pop territory (*Pet Sounds*, 1966). Decades on, their catalog still means summer to millions. This playlist track is their **1986 cover** of a folk-rock classic—not an original Beach Boys composition.

**中文**

**The Beach Boys**（海滩男孩）是 1961 年在**加州 Hawthorne** 成立的美国**摇滚 / 流行**乐队，核心是 Wilson 三兄弟——**Brian**、**Dennis**、**Carl**——加上表亲 **Mike Love** 与好友 **Al Jardine**。他们定义了 1960 年代初的**加州之声**：冲浪、汽车、阳光般的和声。Brian Wilson 的创作与录音室工艺后来把他们推向艺术流行（*Pet Sounds*，1966）。几十年后，他们的曲库对无数人仍等于夏天。本播放列表这首是他们 **1986 年的翻唱**——并非 Beach Boys 原创。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"California Dreamin'"** was written by **John and Michelle Phillips** and made famous by **The Mamas & the Papas** (single late **1965** / early **1966**)—a winter anthem of longing for warm L.A. while leaves are brown and the sky is grey. **The Beach Boys** cut their cover in **1986** for the compilation *Made in U.S.A.*, produced by **Terry Melcher**, with **Roger McGuinn** (The Byrds) on 12-string guitar; John and Michelle Phillips appear in the video. Your Apple Music link is the version on *Stranger Things: Soundtrack from the Netflix Series, Season 4*—chosen because S4 is set in **1986**, matching this cover's year (the show's subtitles sometimes miscredit the Mamas & the Papas original). Same homesick lyric; brighter, harmony-heavy Beach Boys sheen.

**中文**

**"California Dreamin'"** 由 **John 与 Michelle Phillips** 创作，因 **The Mamas & the Papas**（**1965 年末 / 1966 年初**单曲）而家喻户晓——冬天里想念温暖洛杉矶的颂歌：叶子枯黄、天空灰蒙。**The Beach Boys** 于 **1986** 年为精选辑 *Made in U.S.A.* 录下翻唱，由 **Terry Melcher** 制作，**Roger McGuinn**（The Byrds）弹十二弦吉他；MV 里还有 John 与 Michelle Phillips。你 Apple Music 上的版本来自 *Stranger Things* 第四季原声——因为剧情设定在 **1986**，与这首翻唱同年（剧集字幕有时会误标成 Mamas & the Papas 原版）。歌词仍是那份想家的冷；音色则是 Beach Boys 更亮、更厚的和声。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **all the leaves are brown** | 叶子都黄了 | 秋天/初冬意象；*brown* 非「棕色衣服」 |
| **the sky is grey** | 天空灰蒙蒙的 | 美式 *gray* / 英式 *grey* 皆可 |
| **I've been for a walk** | 我出去走了一圈 | = I've been on a walk；英式也常见 |
| **on a winter's day** | 在一个冬日 | 所有格 *winter's* 表「属于冬天的」 |
| **I'd be safe and warm** | 我会又安全又暖和 | *I'd* = I would；虚拟语气 |
| **if I was in L.A.** | 要是我在洛杉矶 | 口语常用 *was*；正式虚拟多用 *were* |
| **California dreamin'** | 梦见加州；加州梦 | *dreamin'* = dreaming；标题缩略 |
| **on such a winter's day** | 在这样的冬日里 | *such a* = 这样的 |
| **stopped into a church** | 走进一座教堂 | *stop into* ≈ 顺便进去；也见 *stopped in* |
| **along the way** | 沿途；路过时 | 字面路程 / 比喻人生路上 |
| **got down on my knees** | 跪下来 | 祷告姿势；也可表恳求 |
| **pretend to pray** | 假装祷告 | *pretend to* + 动词原形 |
| **the preacher likes the cold** | 牧师喜欢这寒冷 | 冷幽默：他巴不得你留下 |
| **I'm gonna stay** | 我会留下来 | *gonna* = going to；口语 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 词少、重复多，**跟唱友好**；重点练 *dreamin'* 词尾鼻音 /n/，别唱成 *dreaming* 的清晰 /ŋ/ 也行（歌词本就缩略）。
- *If I was in L.A.* 是口语虚拟；写作正式场合可记 *If I were in L.A.*
- 和声层（括号里的回声句）适合练**听辨主旋律 vs 伴唱**——Beach Boys 版尤其厚。
- 对比原版 Mamas & the Papas：同一套词，原版更folk、更冷；本版更亮、更「加州和声」。
- 想练「向往别处 / 冬天心情」表达，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
