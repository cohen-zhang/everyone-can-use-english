## 歌手简介 · About Janji

**English**

**Janji** is a **Finnish EDM / progressive house** producer and DJ, best known for the 2015 NCS hit **"Heroes Tonight" (feat. Johnning)**—a staple of gaming montages and workout playlists. He often collaborates with Swedish singer-songwriter **Johnning** (Jonathan Nilsson), whose clear pop vocals sit on top of big-room drops and melodic builds. Janji's tracks lean **uplifting and anthemic**: simple English hooks, festival energy, and emotional choruses that are easy to sing along to even if the production is electronic.

**中文**

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Got You (feat. Johnning)"** was released on **20 May 2016** as a single (FRSH Helsinki / Warner Music Finland). It is an **uplifting dance-pop** track about **showing up for someone in fear and loneliness**: when panic takes over, when tears take control, the singer repeats *I got you*—I'm here; I've got your back. A middle section shifts to seeking **a new life / a new beginning**, because *I need you and you need me*. The English is short and repetitive—ideal for pronunciation drills and commuting sing-alongs—while the drop carries the emotional weight.

**中文**

**"I Got You (feat. Johnning)"** 于 **2016 年 5 月 20 日**以单曲发行（FRSH Helsinki / Warner Music Finland）。这是一首**振奋型舞曲流行**，写在恐惧与孤独里**有人撑着你**：恐慌压过来、眼泪失控时，歌者反复说 *I got you*——我在；有我呢。中段转向寻找**新生活 / 新开始**，因为 *I need you and you need me*。英文短、重复多，适合练发音与通勤跟唱；情绪主要靠爆发段托住。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I got you** | 有我在；我撑着你 | 口语安慰；≈ *I've got your back*；比 *I understand* 更「在场」 |
| **fear takes it over** | 恐惧占了上风 | *take over* = 接管、压过理性 |
| **tears take control** | 眼泪失控／眼泪说了算 | 情绪主导身体；可对照 *lose control* |
| **feel all alone** | 觉得只剩自己 | *all* 加强孤独感 |
| **mind is out of control** | 脑子失控 | 焦虑、思绪乱飞时常用 |
| **torn down** | 被撕碎；整个人垮了 | 比 *sad* 更重；也有「拆毁」本义 |
| **life is on the line** | 人生／性命悬在一线 | *on the line* = 岌岌可危 |
| **by your side** | 在你身边 | 陪伴套话；情歌与友情歌都常用 |
| **a new beginning** | 新的开始 | 重启人生的固定说法 |
| **start all over** | 一切重来 | *all over* = 从头再来 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I got you* 极短、极重复，适合**通勤跟唱**；注意 *got* 的短元音，别拖成 *gaht*。
- *can't breathe* 里 **breathe**（动词）与 *breath*（名词）易混；歌词站常误写成 *breath*，跟唱按「动词」理解即可。
- 爆发段前的 *When the fear takes it over* 可当**一口气跟读**练习：连读 *takes-it-over*。
- 想延伸练「安慰别人」的口语，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]，对照 *I'm here for you* / *I've got your back*。
