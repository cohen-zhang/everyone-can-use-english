## 歌手简介 · About Black Eyed Peas

**English**

**The Black Eyed Peas** are an American hip-hop and pop group from Los Angeles, formed by **will.i.am**, **apl.de.ap** and **Taboo** in the 1990s; **Fergie** joined in 2002 and helped them cross over to pop with *Where Is the Love?*, *My Humps* and *I Gotta Feeling*. They have won six Grammy Awards.

**中文**

**The Black Eyed Peas**（黑眼豆豆）是来自洛杉矶的美国嘻哈流行组合，90 年代由 **will.i.am**、**apl.de.ap**、**Taboo** 组建；**Fergie** 2002 年加入后，凭 *Where Is the Love?*、*My Humps*、*I Gotta Feeling* 等打入主流流行，共获 6 座格莱美。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Boom Boom Pow"** is the lead single from ***The E.N.D.*** (The Energy Never Dies, **2009**), produced by will.i.am. Built on heavy electronic beats and **Auto-Tune**, it is mostly boasting about having the most futuristic sound—*I'm so 3008, you so 2000 and late*. It stayed at **No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 for **12 weeks** and won Grammys for **Best Dance Recording** and **Best Short Form Music Video**.

**中文**

**"Boom Boom Pow"** 是专辑 ***The E.N.D.***（The Energy Never Dies，**2009**）的首支单曲，由 will.i.am 制作。以厚重电子节拍与 **Auto-Tune** 修音为主，歌词多在炫耀自己的声音最前卫——*I'm so 3008, you so 2000 and late*（我已活在 3008 年，你还停在 2000 年「太晚了」）。在 Billboard Hot 100 **冠军 12 周**，获格莱美**最佳舞曲录音**与**最佳短篇音乐录影带**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I'm so 3008, you so 2000 and late** | 我超前到 3008，你落后到「2000 零晚」 | 谐音梗：*2008* ↔ *2000 and late*（too late） |
| **next level** | 更高一个层级 | 俚语 / 职场口语：*take it to the next level* |
| **They try to copy my swagger** | 他们想模仿我的派头 | *swagger* 自信张扬的气场 |
| **I'm on that next shit now** | 我已经玩下一代的东西了 | 粗口俚语，理解即可 |
| **Harder, faster, better, stronger** | 更难、更快、更好、更强 | 致敬 Daft Punk 名曲 |
| **We got the beat that bounce** | 我们有让人弹跳的节拍 | *bounce* 弹跳；舞曲高频词 |
| **Put your hands in the air** | 把手举起来 | 派对 / 演唱会经典喊话 |
| **Let the beat rock** | 让节拍燥起来 | *rock* 作动词：燥、摇滚起来 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 歌词含粗口（Apple Music 标为 explicit），不适合亲子或正式场合；学习重点放在**节奏感与俚语**。
- 大量 Auto-Tune 与说唱，**难度高**，建议只挑 *I'm so 3008 / You so 2000 and late* 与 *Let the beat rock* 练节奏。
- *next level* 在科技与职场里很常用：*This feature takes our UX to the next level.*
