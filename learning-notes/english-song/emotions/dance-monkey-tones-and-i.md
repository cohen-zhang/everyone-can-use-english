## 歌手简介 · About Tones And I

**English**

**Tones And I** is the stage name of **Toni Watson** (born 1993), an Australian **singer-songwriter** from **Mount Beauty, Victoria**. She spent years **busking** on the streets of **Byron Bay**, living out of a van and building a local following before her 2019 breakthrough. Her debut EP ***The Kids Are Coming*** and the viral single **"Dance Monkey"** turned her distinctive **high, raw vocal** into a global phenomenon—the song topped charts in more than **30 countries** and made her the first Australian act to lead the **Billboard Global 200**. Watson writes candidly about street performance, loneliness, and the pressure to entertain; her later hits include **"Never Seen the Rain"** and **"Fly Away"**.

**中文**

**Tones And I** 是 **Toni Watson**（1993 年生）的艺名，澳大利亚**创作歌手**，成长于 **Mount Beauty, Victoria**。她曾在 **Byron Bay** 街头**卖唱**多年，住在 van 里、慢慢积累本地听众，2019 年爆发。**首张 EP *The Kids Are Coming*** 与病毒单曲 **"Dance Monkey"** 把她 distinctive 的**高亢、未经修饰的嗓音**推上全球——歌曲在 **30 多国**夺冠，她也成为首位领跑 **Billboard Global 200** 的澳大利亚艺人。Watson 直白写街头表演、孤独与「必须娱乐别人」的压力；后续代表作还有 **"Never Seen the Rain"**、**"Fly Away"**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Dance Monkey"** was released on **10 May 2019** as a single from *The Kids Are Coming*. Watson has said it is **not** about a romantic crush but about her life as a **street musician**: crowds only stop when she performs, then demand *dance for me… move for me… and when you're done, I'll make you do it all again*—like **an organ-grinder's monkey** trained to repeat tricks for coins. The upbeat **indie pop** production contrasts with that bitter metaphor, which is why the hook feels both catchy and uncomfortable. The track reached **No. 1** in the UK and Australia and **No. 4** on the US Billboard Hot 100, becoming one of the defining viral hits of the late 2010s.

**中文**

**"Dance Monkey"** 于 **2019 年 5 月 10 日**发行，收录于 *The Kids Are Coming*。Watson 说这歌**不是**写暗恋，而是写她当**街头音乐人**的日子：只有她表演时路人才停下，然后要求 *dance for me… move for me… and when you're done, I'll make you do it all again*——像被训练的**街头猴**，为硬币重复把戏。轻快的 **indie pop** 编曲与这层苦涩隐喻形成反差，所以钩子句既朗朗上口又让人不太舒服。单曲在英国、澳大利亚**夺冠**，美国 Billboard Hot 100 **第 4**，是 2010 年代末最具代表性的病毒热单之一。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **see the way you shine** | 看见你发光的样子 | *shine* = 发光、出众；可指才华或魅力 |
| **stopped me dead** | 让我猛然停步 | 习语：*stop someone dead* = 使某人突然停住 |
| **while I was passing by** | 在我路过时 | *pass by* = 经过；进行时表「那一刻」 |
| **beg to see you dance** | 恳求再看你跳 | *beg* 比 *ask* 更强烈；带「乞求」意味 |
| **just one more time** | 就再一次 | 歌里反复出现；强调「还要更多表演」 |
| **dance for me / move for me** | 为我跳 / 为我动 | 命令式；歌里指观众对街头艺人的要求 |
| **I've never seen anybody do… before** | 我从没见过有人…… | 夸张赞美；也可讽刺「没见过这么会表演」 |
| **just like a monkey** | 就像一只猴子 | 歌题 *Dance Monkey* 的核心隐喻 |
| **dancin' my whole life** | 一辈子都在跳舞 | 指为生存/生计不断表演 |
| **make you do it all again** | 让你再来一遍 | *make someone do* = 迫使某人做；含 control 意味 |
| **look me in my eyes** | 看着我的眼睛 | 固定搭配 *look someone in the eye(s)* |
| **do it all again** | 全部再来一遍 | Outro 重复；强调 endless loop |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Dance for me* 节奏重复、音节多，适合**跟唱**练快速口语；先慢速分句再加速。
- Tones And I 的 **高音咬字**很 distinctive；*monkey*、*again* 等词可单独练清晰收尾。
- 歌表面像恋爱告白，实则写**街头卖唱**——理解这层隐喻后，*make you do it all again* 读起来更有力。
- 想延伸读**被消耗、 burnout**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 exhaustion / boundary 相关表达。
