## 歌手简介 · About Lola Young

**English**

**Lola Young** (born 4 January 2001) is an English **singer-songwriter** from South London. She studied at the **BRIT School**—the same performing-arts school that trained Adele and Amy Winehouse—and began writing songs at age 11. After early EPs and a 2021 cover of *Together in Electric Dreams* for the John Lewis Christmas advert, she released her second album **This Wasn't Meant for You Anyway** (2024). Her breakout single **"Messy"** went viral on TikTok, topped the UK Singles Chart, and won the **Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance** (2026). Young's music blends **pop, soul, indie rock**, and **R&B**; her lyrics often explore relationships, mental health, **ADHD**, and accepting imperfection rather than chasing an ideal image.

**中文**

**Lola Young**（2001 年 1 月 4 日生）是英国**创作型歌手**，成长于伦敦南部。她就读 **BRIT School**（与 Adele、Amy Winehouse 同校）， 11 岁起写歌。2021 年为 John Lewis 圣诞广告翻唱 *Together in Electric Dreams* 后知名度上升；2024 年发行第二张专辑 **This Wasn't Meant for You Anyway**，其中单曲 **"Messy"** 在 TikTok 爆红，登顶英国单曲榜，并获 **格莱美最佳流行独唱表演奖**（2026）。她的风格融合 **流行、灵魂、独立摇滚** 与 **节奏布鲁斯**；歌词常写亲密关系、心理健康、**ADHD**，以及「接受不完美的自己」而非迎合外界标准。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Messy"** was released on **30 May 2024** as the sixth single from *This Wasn't Meant for You Anyway*. Young has called it an **"ADHD anthem"**: a raw, angry pop-rock track about never being "enough" for someone who keeps moving the goalposts—too messy one day, too clean the next; too clever, then too dumb. The chorus flips between contradictions (*I'm too messy, and then I'm too fucking clean*) to show how unreasonable expectations feel when you just want to **be yourself**. The song peaked at **No. 1** in the UK and **No. 14** on the Billboard Hot 100.

**中文**

**"Messy"** 于 **2024 年 5 月 30 日**发行，收录于专辑 *This Wasn't Meant for You Anyway*。Lola 称其为 **「ADHD  anthem」**：一首直白、带怒气的流行摇滚，写「怎么做都不对」——今天太乱，明天又太干净；太聪明又像太蠢。副歌用一组组**矛盾**（*I'm too messy, and then I'm too fucking clean*）呈现对方不断改标准、自己却只想**做回自己**的窒息感。单曲登顶**英国单曲榜**，在美国 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 14** 位。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **impatient** | 没耐心的 | 形容词；*I'm impatient* 比 *I have no patience* 更口语 |
| **minus four degrees** | 零下四度 | 口语说温度；*minus* = 零下 |
| **shut up** | 闭嘴 | 很直接，熟人吵架用；正式场合避免 |
| **cut me some slack** | 放我一马；别那么苛刻 | 习语 `slack` 原意「松弛」 |
| **pull a Britney** | 来一段 Britney 式崩溃 | 指 Britney Spears 2007 年公开崩溃事件；网络梗 |
| **too messy / too clean** | 太乱 / 太干净 | 歌题核心矛盾：怎么做都不对 |
| **open my big mouth** | 开口说话（常带「说错话」意味） | *big mouth* = 话多、管不住嘴 |
| **that time of the month** | 生理期；月经 | 委婉说法；歌里指对方把哭泣归因于激素 |
| **get the hint** | 懂暗示 | *You're not getting the hint* = 你还没领会 |
| **pull those faces** | 摆臭脸 | 英式口语也常说 *pull a face* |
| **work my ass off** | 拼死干活 | 粗口强调程度；较温和可说 *work my butt off* |
| **get stoned** | （吸大麻后）嗨了 | 俚语；*stone* 此处指 intoxicated |
| **get high** | 嗨了；吸了 | 可指大麻或其他致幻物 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌节奏清楚，适合**跟唱**练连读：*too messy* → *and then I'm too* 之间常有轻读与连读。
- 歌里大量 **对比结构**（too X / too Y），可用来练「一边……一边……却……」的英文逻辑。
- 想延伸读**成人情绪表达**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 **Unpleasant / Very Unpleasant** 相关句型。
