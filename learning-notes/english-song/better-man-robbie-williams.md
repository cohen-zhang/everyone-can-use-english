## 歌手简介 · About Robbie Williams

**English**

**Robbie Williams** (born 13 February 1974) is an English **singer-songwriter** and entertainer from Stoke-on-Trent. He rose to fame as the youngest member of **Take That** (1990–1995), then rebuilt his solo career with sharp wit, arena pop, and confessional ballads. Albums like *Life thru a Lens*, *I've Been Expecting You*, and *Sing When You're Winning* made him one of Britain's biggest stars; **"Angels"** remains a national anthem. Williams has been open about **depression, addiction, and fame**, and his softer songs often balance swagger with vulnerability. He has won a record **18 BRIT Awards** and remains a staple of UK and European pop culture.

**中文**

**Robbie Williams**（1974 年 2 月 13 日生）是英国**创作型歌手与艺人**，来自斯托克。他作为 **Take That** 最年轻成员（1990–1995）成名，离队后以犀利幽默、体育场流行曲和袒露心声的情歌重建事业。专辑 *Life thru a Lens*、*I've Been Expecting You*、*Sing When You're Winning* 使他成为英国顶级巨星；**"Angels"** 几乎是国民情歌。Robbie 公开谈论过**抑郁、成瘾与名利**，柔情作品常在张扬与脆弱之间切换。他创下 **18 座全英音乐奖**纪录，至今仍是英伦与欧洲流行文化标志人物。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Better Man"** appears on **Sing When You're Winning** (2000), co-written by Williams and his longtime collaborator **Guy Chambers**. Unlike his cheeky uptempo hits, this is a **gospel-tinged piano ballad** about shame, loneliness, and the desire to heal. The narrator asks for comfort (*Send someone to love me*), admits he was taught to blame himself, and repeats the plea **"to be a better man"**—not perfection, but growth through pain. The middle section (*Love is all around*) offers a gentler, almost hymn-like counterpoint before the prayer returns. It became a fan favourite and a common **KTV / sing-along** choice for its clear melody and emotional arc.

**中文**

**"Better Man"** 收录于 **Sing When You're Winning**（2000），由 Williams 与老搭档 **Guy Chambers** 共同创作。不同于他那些俏皮快歌，这是一首带**福音味道**的钢琴情歌，写羞耻、孤独与渴望痊愈。歌中人求安慰（*Send someone to love me*），承认从小被教导「凡事怪自己」，并反复祈求**「成为一个更好的人」**——不是完美，而是在痛苦中成长。中段 *Love is all around* 像赞美诗般柔和，随后又回到祈祷式副歌。旋律清晰、情绪递进，是歌迷与 **KTV 合唱** 的热门曲目。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **rest in arms** | 在怀抱里歇息 | 比 *rest in someone's arms* 更诗意；情歌常用 |
| **keep me safe from harm** | 护我远离伤害 | *harm* 比 *danger* 更偏「受伤、受损」 |
| **pouring rain** | 倾盆大雨 | *pouring* 强调雨势极大 |
| **before my time** | 太早；未老先衰 | 此处指觉得人生还没走完就老了 |
| **heal the shame** | 抚平羞耻 | *heal* 不仅治身体，也治心理创伤 |
| **grow through this pain** | 在痛苦中成长 | 比 *get over* 更强调过程 |
| **go easy on my conscience** | 饶了我的良心吧 | *go easy on* = 别太苛刻 |
| **take the blame** | 承担责备；背锅 | 被动接受「都是我的错」 |
| **catch my tears** | 接住我的眼泪 | 形象化安慰语 |
| **walk me out of here** | 带我走出这里 | *walk someone out* 也可指送客出门 |
| **homeward bound** | 踏上归途；回家路上 | 固定说法；亦作歌名、书名 |
| **fallen on stony ground** | 落在顽石地上；徒劳无功 | 借圣经比喻：爱未被接纳 |
| **love is all around** | 爱无处不在 | 呼应 The Troggs 同名经典 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *to be a better man* 旋律上行、词义清楚，适合 **KTV 先练副歌** 再回头啃 Verse。
- 注意 **Lord** 多处出现：不是每句都重读，跟唱时多在弱拍轻带过。
- *Love is all around* 段可单独慢速朗读，练**元音拉长**与和声感。
- 想对照「自责 vs 自我成长」的口语，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 相关章节。
