## 歌手简介 · About Bruno Mars

**English**

**Bruno Mars** (born Peter Gene Hernandez, 8 October 1985) is an American **singer-songwriter, producer, and performer** from Honolulu, Hawaii. Raised in a musical family, he moved to Los Angeles after high school and became one of pop's most reliable hitmakers—writing and producing for other artists before breaking through with **"Just the Way You Are"** (2010). Mars blends **pop, R&B, funk, disco, and reggae**; his live shows are known for James Brown–style energy. His second album **Unorthodox Jukebox** (2012) won the **Grammy for Best Pop Vocal Album**; he later swept the major categories at the 2018 Grammys with *24K Magic*. He often revives retro grooves while keeping lyrics playful and romantic.

**中文**

**Bruno Mars**（本名 Peter Gene Hernandez，1985 年 10 月 8 日生）是美国**创作歌手、制作人、舞台表演者**，成长于夏威夷檀香山。出身音乐家庭，高中后赴洛杉矶，先为他人写歌制作，再以 **"Just the Way You Are"**（2010）爆红。风格融合 **流行、R&B、放克、迪斯科、雷鬼**；现场以 James Brown 式爆发力著称。第二张专辑 **Unorthodox Jukebox**（2012）获**格莱美最佳流行演唱专辑**；后以 *24K Magic* 在 2018 年格莱美横扫主要奖项。他常复活复古律动，歌词却保持俏皮、浪漫。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Treasure"** was released on **7 December 2012** as the fourth single from *Unorthodox Jukebox*. Built on a **disco-funk** groove inspired by French producer **Breakbot**'s *Baby I'm Yours*, the track is a flirty compliment song: Mars tells a woman she is his **treasure** and **golden star**, urges her to smile, and promises to **treasure** her if she lets him. The opening line *Baby squirrel, you's a sexy motherfucker* (radio edits soften it) sets a bold, party-ready tone. The song peaked at **No. 5** on the Billboard Hot 100 and became a staple of Mars's live set—short, catchy, and ideal for practicing **pet names** and upbeat romantic praise.

**中文**

**"Treasure"** 于 **2012 年 12 月 7 日**发行，为 *Unorthodox Jukebox* 第四支单曲。在受法国制作人 **Breakbot** 的 *Baby I'm Yours* 启发的 **迪斯科放克** 律动上，这是一首调情式赞美歌：Bruno 说她是他的 **treasure**、**golden star**，要她笑一笑，并承诺若她愿意就**珍藏**她。开场 *Baby squirrel, you's a sexy motherfucker*（电台版会弱化）定下大胆、派对感语气。单曲在 Billboard Hot 100 最高 **第 5** 位，成为 Mars 现场常备曲目——短小、上口，适合练**昵称**与轻快的浪漫夸赞。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **give me your attention** | 把你的注意力给我 | 口语直接；聚会 / 调情语境 |
| **a little something about yourself** | 关于你的一点事 | *something* 轻读，语气随意 |
| **walk around like you wanna be someone else** | 走来走去像想成为别人 | 劝对方自信、做自己 |
| **you're fine, so fine** | 你很美，非常美 | *fine* 此处 = 好看、有魅力 |
| **I'm gonna show you** | 我要让你看见 | 口语 *gonna*；承诺式表白 |
| **treasure** | 珍宝；珍藏 | 名词 + 动词双关：*let me treasure you* |
| **golden star** | 金星；耀眼的星 | 甜蜜昵称；比 *star* 更夸张 |
| **make my wish come true** | 让我愿望成真 | 童话感浪漫句 |
| **look so blue** | 看起来这么忧郁 | *blue* = 情绪低落 |
| **I wouldn't say that if it wasn't true** | 若不是真的我不会说 | 强调真诚；双重否定口语 |
| **wonderful, flawless** | 很棒、完美 | 成对夸赞；*flawless* 比 *perfect* 更夸张 |
| **when you're mine** | 当你属于我 | 占有欲轻的浪漫说法；副歌前铺垫 |
| **baby squirrel** | 小松鼠宝贝 | 开场昵称；俏皮、非字面（不是真说松鼠） |
| **sexy motherfucker** | 真他妈性感 | 开场粗口；电台版常改 *sexy thing*；正式场合避免 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 歌曲仅约 **3 分钟**，副歌重复多，适合 **KTV 热身** 或练迪斯科节奏跟拍。
- *Treasure* 作动词时重读在第一个音节 **TRE-asure**，跟唱时注意与名词用法一致。
- 开场粗口在课堂 / 正式场合避免；练口语可选电台净版或直接从 Verse 1 *Give me your attention* 切入。
- 复古放克 bass 线清楚，可一边跟唱一边练 **重音落在偶数拍** 的律动感。
