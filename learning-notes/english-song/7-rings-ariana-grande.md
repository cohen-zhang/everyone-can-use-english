## 歌手简介 · About Ariana Grande

**English**

**Ariana Grande** (born 26 June 1993) is an American **singer, songwriter, and actress** from Boca Raton, Florida. She began on Broadway and became a teen star on Nickelodeon's *Victorious* and *Sam & Cat* before launching a pop career with the 2013 album *Yours Truly*. Known for her **four-octave whistle register** and R&B-influenced pop, she has broken streaming records and won multiple **Grammy Awards**. Her 2019 album **thank u, next**—written after a period of highly public breakups—blends **trap-pop, hip-hop**, and soul; **"7 rings"** became one of its signature flex anthems about friendship, wealth, and self-reliance.

**中文**

**Ariana Grande**（1993 年 6 月 26 日生）是美国**歌手、词曲作者与演员**，成长于佛罗里达。她先登百老汇，后在 Nickelodeon 剧集 *Victorious*、*Sam & Cat* 走红，2013 年以专辑 *Yours Truly* 开启流行生涯。她以**四个八度的哨音**和 R&B 气质流行曲著称，屡破流媒体纪录并获多项**格莱美奖**。2019 年专辑 **thank u, next** 写于多段高调分手之后，融合 **trap-pop、嘻哈** 与灵魂乐；其中 **"7 rings"** 成为代表曲——写闺蜜情谊、财富与「靠自己」的态度。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"7 rings"** was released on **18 January 2019** as the second single from *thank u, next*. The melody and hook borrow from **"My Favorite Things"** (*The Sound of Music*), reworked into a **trap-pop** banger. Grande has said the song was inspired by a day shopping with friends after a breakup—she wanted to buy them matching rings. Lyrically it celebrates **retail therapy**, financial independence (*I see it, I like it, I want it, I got it*), and refusing to be defined by marriage (*Wearing a ring, but ain't gon' be no "Mrs."*). It debuted at **No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and sparked debate about cultural appropriation and wealth flexing.

**中文**

**"7 rings"** 于 **2019 年 1 月 18 日**发行，为 *thank u, next* 第二支单曲。旋律与 hook 借自音乐剧 **"My Favorite Things"**（《音乐之声》），改编成 **trap-pop** 热单。Ariana 说灵感来自分手后与闺蜜逛街、想给她们买同款戒指的一天。歌词庆祝**购物疗愈**、经济独立（*I see it, I like it, I want it, I got it*），以及拒绝被婚姻定义（*Wearing a ring, but ain't gon' be no "Mrs."*）。单曲首发即登 **Billboard Hot 100 榜首**，也引发关于文化挪用与炫富的讨论。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **breakfast at Tiffany's** | 蒂芙尼早餐 | 电影《蒂芙尼的早餐》梗；指奢华清晨小聚 |
| **get in trouble** | 惹事；闯祸 | *like getting in trouble* = 爱找刺激 |
| **turn me to a savage** | 让我变狠人 | *savage* 俚语：冷酷、不好惹 |
| **tied up with calls** | 被电话缠住 | *tied up* = 忙得脱不开身；与 *strings*（感情牵绊）双关 |
| **flossy** | 闪亮耀眼的 | 嘻哈俚语，形容珠宝、打扮很闪 |
| **my gloss is poppin'** | 唇彩亮得炸 | *poppin'* = 抢眼、很顶 |
| **Gee, thanks** | 哟，谢啦 | 带讽刺或傲娇语气，呼应 *Mean Girls* 梗 |
| **I see it, I like it, I want it, I got it** | 看见—喜欢—想要—到手 | 全曲核心 hook；练连读极佳 |
| **ain't gon' be no "Mrs."** | 不会当某某太太 | *gon'* = going to；三重否定口语 |
| **retail therapy** | 购物疗愈 | 用消费缓解情绪 |
| **red-bottoms** | 红底高跟鞋 | Christian Louboutin 标志红鞋底 |
| **put it in the bag** | 包起来（买了） | 购物时让店员结账的口语 |
| **got the juice** | 有影响力；有话语权 | 嘻哈俚语；*juice* = clout |
| **nothing but net** | 空心入网；百发百中 | 篮球术语，此处指赚钱稳准狠 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I want it, I got it* 节奏极快，适合练**连读与弱读**：*want it* / *got it* 常连成一个音块。
- 歌里大量 **ain't / gon' / woulda** 等非标准缩略，跟唱前先对照「重点表达」表理解含义。
- *My Favorite Things* 旋律熟悉后再听，更容易抓住改编的韵律；可对比原版练「同一旋律、不同语域」。
- 想延伸练**恋爱后的自我重建**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里关于边界与自我肯定的表达。
