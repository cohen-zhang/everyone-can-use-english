## 歌手简介 · About Ed Sheeran

**English**

**Ed Sheeran** (born 17 February 1991, Halifax, England) is a British **singer-songwriter** who rose from **busking** and small London gigs to global stadium tours. His acoustic-driven early work (*+* , 2011) gave way to massive pop hits on **÷** (*Divide*, 2017). Sheeran writes melody-first, often blending **folk, pop, hip-hop, and dancehall**; he has won **four Grammy Awards** and holds records for some of the **most-streamed songs** in history. Known for relatable storytelling and loop-pedal live performances, he has also written for Justin Bieber, Taylor Swift, and One Direction.

**中文**

**Ed Sheeran**（1991 年 2 月 17 生于英格兰 Halifax）是英国**创作型歌手**，从**街头卖唱**和伦敦小场地演出一路走到全球体育馆巡演。早期偏木吉他（*+*，2011），后在 **÷**（*Divide*，2017）交出多首流行巨作。他先旋律后歌词，常融合 **民谣、流行、嘻哈与 dancehall**；获 **四座格莱美**，并保有史上**流媒体播放量最高**歌曲之一的纪录。以贴近生活的叙事和 loop 踏板现场著称，也为 Justin Bieber、Taylor Swift、One Direction 等写歌。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Shape of You"** was released on **6 January 2017** as one of the dual lead singles from *÷* (alongside *"Castle on the Hill"*). Co-written with **Steve Mac** and **Johnny McDaid**, it was originally conceived as a **racy club track** for Rihanna before Sheeran kept it and swapped some lyrics. Built on a **dancehall / tropical house** groove (marimba riff), the song narrates meeting someone at a bar, dancing to **Van Morrison**, cheap dates, and physical attraction—*I'm in love with the shape of you*. It spent **12 weeks at No. 1** on the Billboard Hot 100 and became the **first song to reach 3 billion Spotify streams**. A staple of parties, gyms, and English sing-along playlists worldwide.

**中文**

**"Shape of You"** 于 **2017 年 1 月 6 日**发行，与 *"Castle on the Hill"* 同为 *÷* 双主打。与 **Steve Mac**、**Johnny McDaid** 共同创作，最初是按 **Rihanna** 的辛辣夜店曲写的，Sheeran 留下并改了部分词。曲风建立在 **dancehall / tropical house** 律动（马林巴 riff）上，讲在酒吧相识、跟着 **Van Morrison** 跳舞、节俭约会与身体吸引——*I'm in love with the shape of you*。在 Billboard Hot 100 **连冠 12 周**，并成为 Spotify 上**首支突破 30 亿次播放**的歌曲。全球派对、健身房与跟唱歌单常客。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **doing shots** | 干杯（烈酒一口闷） | 酒吧场景常用 |
| **start up a conversation** | 开始搭话 | *start up* 比 *start* 更口语 |
| **Van the Man** | 「范大叔」 | Van Morrison 昵称；点唱机放歌的梗 |
| **follow my lead** | 跟着我的节奏 / 跟我来 | 舞步或暧昧暗示 |
| **handmade for somebody like me** | 为像我这样的人量身定做 | 甜蜜夸张说法 |
| **push and pull** | 推拉；试探 | 像磁铁一样吸引又较劲 |
| **the shape of you** | 你的身形 | 歌题；偏外貌吸引 |
| **bed sheets smell like you** | 床单还留你的气息 | 亲密后的余韵 |
| **something brand new** | 全新的东西 | *brand new* 强调「崭新」 |
| **thrifty** | 节俭的 | 两人约会去吃自助餐的原因 |
| **all-you-can-eat** | 自助餐 | 美式餐厅常见 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 马林巴 riff 一响起就是识别点；跟唱时可先把 **Pre-Chorus** *follow my lead* 练熟，再进副歌。
- *Van the Man* 指 **Van Morrison**；不熟悉可先听他的 *Brown Eyed Girl*，再回来看 *put "Van the Man" on the jukebox* 的画面感。
- 副歌 *push and pull like a magnet do* 注意 **magnet do** 的口语语法（正式写作会说 *as a magnet does*）。
- 重复段落多：Pre-Chorus / Chorus / Post-Chorus 用 *(同前)* 记结构即可，跟唱时重点抓 **shape of you** 与 **your body** 两句。
- 想练**夜店搭讪与约会**口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里类似场景。
