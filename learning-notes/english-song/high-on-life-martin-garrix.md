## 歌手简介 · About Martin Garrix

**English**

**Martin Garrix** (born Martijn Garritsen, 14 May 1996) is a Dutch **DJ, record producer**, and songwriter. He broke through at 17 with the festival anthem **"Animals"** (2013) and has since headlined Tomorrowland, Ultra, and Coachella. Garrix founded the label **STMPD RCRDS** and is known for blending **big-room house**, **progressive house**, and pop crossover hooks. **Bonn** (Kristoffer Fogelmark) is a Swedish **singer-songwriter** and longtime Garrix collaborator; his warm, emotional vocals appear on tracks like *Scared to Be Lonely* and *High on Life*. Together they bridge **EDM drops** with **singer-songwriter storytelling**.

**中文**

**Martin Garrix**（本名 Martijn Garritsen，1996 年 5 月 14 日生）是荷兰 **DJ、制作人** 兼词曲作者。17 岁以音乐节神曲 **"Animals"**（2013）爆红，此后多次领衔 Tomorrowland、Ultra、Coachella。他创立厂牌 **STMPD RCRDS**，风格融合 **Big-room house**、**渐进浩室** 与流行副歌。**Bonn**（Kristoffer Fogelmark）是瑞典**创作歌手**，与 Garrix 长期合作；在 *Scared to Be Lonely*、*High on Life* 等歌里以温暖、感性的嗓音见长。两人把 **EDM drop** 和 **民谣式叙事** 结合在一起。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"High on Life"** was released on **29 July 2018** and premiered as Garrix's closing track at **Tomorrowland 2018** in Belgium—Bonn joined him on stage as fireworks lit the sky; the official video doubles as the festival aftermovie. The song is a **progressive house ballad** about swapping a numb, fast lifestyle for genuine connection: *Killed the demons of my mind / Ever since you came around*. **"High on life"** means feeling euphoric from living itself—not from drugs. The chorus pledge—*walk a million miles just to see you smile*—makes it a staple of festival singalongs and romantic playlists alike.

**中文**

**"High on Life"** 于 **2018 年 7 月 29 日**发行，在比利时 **Tomorrowland 2018** 压轴首演——Bonn 登台合唱，烟火满天；官方 MV 同时是音乐节 aftermovie。这是一首**渐进浩室抒情曲**：告别麻木的快节奏生活，迎来真正的联结（*Killed the demons of my mind / Ever since you came around*）。**high on life** 指因活着本身而亢奋——不是药物。**walk a million miles just to see you smile** 的副歌誓言，让它成为音乐节大合唱和浪漫歌单里的常客。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **high on life** | 为活着而狂喜；人生亢奋 | 不是吸毒；*get high on life* = 因生活本身而快乐 |
| **kill the demons of my mind** | 驱走心魔 | 比喻克服内心焦虑、抑郁或自我怀疑 |
| **numb out** | 麻木自己 | 用忙碌、派对等躲开情绪；*feels* = feelings 口语缩略 |
| **live a fast life** | 过得很快、很飘 | 快节奏、浅层社交的生活方式 |
| **strobe lights** | 频闪灯 | 夜店/派对灯光；比喻人际关系转瞬即逝 |
| **walk a million miles** | 走一百万英里 | 夸张表达愿意付出一切；浪漫承诺常用 |
| **'til the day I die** | 直到我死 | *'til* = until；誓言「一辈子」 |
| **through my fingers** | 从指缝溜走 | 习语：失去、抓不住 |
| **see your ghost** | 看见你的幽灵 | 人走了，影子还在；思念与后悔 |
| **cutting corners** | 抄近路；走捷径 | 字面可指找路；也指敷衍、图省事 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *High on life 'til the day we die* 旋律上口，适合**跟唱**；注意 *'til* 几乎不发音，常连读成 *til-the-day-we-die*。
- Drop 前后人声对比明显：先练 **Verse 慢速朗读**，再跟副歌，避免被电子节拍带跑。
- 歌里 **对比结构** 鲜明（*numb out* vs *something real*；*fast life* vs *high on life*），可用来练「以前……现在……」叙事。
- 想积累**恋爱承诺句型**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里的距离与忠诚表达。
