## 歌手简介 · About Akon

**English**

**Akon** (Aliaune Damala Badara Akon Thiam, born 16 April 1973) is a **Senegalese-American** singer, songwriter, and producer. Raised partly in Senegal and New Jersey, he broke through in the mid-2000s with a signature **melodic R&B** style layered over hip-hop beats. Hits like **"Locked Up"**, **"Lonely"**, and **"Smack That"** dominated airplay worldwide; he also built **Konvict Muzik** and helped launch artists such as Lady Gaga and T-Pain. Known for **auto-tune**-flavoured hooks and crossover appeal, he has sold tens of millions of records and remains a staple of 2000s club and radio playlists.

**中文**

**Akon**（Aliaune Damala Badara Akon Thiam，1973 年 4 月 16 日生）是**塞内加尔裔美国**歌手、词曲作者与制作人。成长于塞内加尔与新泽西，2000 年代中期以 **R&B 旋律** 叠嘻哈节拍的代表音色走红。 **"Locked Up"**、**"Lonely"**、**"Smack That"** 等席卷全球电台；他创立 **Konvict Muzik**，并助推 Lady Gaga、T-Pain 等艺人。作品常以 **auto-tune** 钩子与跨界流行见长，销量数千万，仍是 2000 年代夜店与电台经典之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Right Now (Na Na Na)"** was released on **23 September 2008** as the lead single from Akon's third album **Freedom**. Built on a **Euro-club / dance-pop** pulse with R&B vocals, it is essentially a **reconciliation plea** after a breakup: the singer admits mistakes, misses his ex, and wants to **"make up"** and **"link up"** immediately—hence the repetitive *na na* hook. The bridge contrasts past intimacy (*fly with me*, *dine with me*, *grind with me*) with present absence. It charted across Europe and became a **late-2000s party anthem**, though the lyrics are more regretful than celebratory.

**中文**

**"Right Now (Na Na Na)"** 于 **2008 年 9 月 23 日**发行，为 Akon 第三张专辑 **Freedom** 主打歌。在 **欧式夜店 / dance-pop** 节拍上铺 R&B 人声，本质是一首分手后的**复合恳求**：歌手承认过错、思念前任，想立刻 **"make up"**（和好）并 **"link up"**（重新联系）——故有重复的 *na na* hook。桥段对比昔日亲密（*fly with me*、*dine with me*、*grind with me*）与如今的空缺。单曲在欧洲多国上榜，成为 **2000 年代末派对热曲**，但歌词底色是懊悔而非狂欢。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **it's been so long** | 好久好久 | 复合句开头极常见 |
| **tryna** | 努力试着 | *trying to* 口语缩读 |
| **washing away** | 流失；被冲走 | 形容力气像被水冲掉 |
| **make up** | 和好；复合 | 分手语境 = 重新在一起 |
| **link up** | 联系；碰头 | 嘻哈/口语：重新接上关系 |
| **show our ass** | 出丑；丢脸 | 粗俗俚语：举止很难看 |
| **homie, lover and friend** | 哥们、爱人和朋友 | 三重身份强调亲密 |
| **the apple of my eye** | 眼中宝；挚爱 | 固定习语 |
| **fly with me** | 和我一起飞 | 可指旅行，也可指「一起潇洒」 |
| **lie with me** | 和我躺在一起 | 怀念同床共枕 |
| **dine with me** | 和我共进晚餐 | 比 *eat with me* 更浪漫正式 |
| **grind with me** | 与我厮守 / 一起打拼 | 嘻哈语境常指亲密陪伴；注意场合 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *make up / link up right now na na* 节奏固定，适合 **KTV 先抓 hook** 再练 Verse。
- *tryna / wont / somethings* 等为口语拼写，跟唱前对照表理解，不必改成书面语。
- Bridge 里 *fly / lie / dine / grind* 四句排比，可单独慢速朗读练**尾韵**。
- 想延伸练「分手后想复合」的口语，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]]。
