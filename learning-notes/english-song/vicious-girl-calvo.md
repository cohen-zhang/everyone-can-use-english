## 歌手简介 · About CALVO

**English**

**CALVO** (Christopher Frahm, born in Germany) is a **German DJ, producer, and vocalist** based in the electronic dance scene. He releases on labels including **Armada Music** and **Spinnin' Records**, working in **future house**, **deep house**, and festival-friendly EDM. Before *Vicious Girl*, he gained attention with tracks like **"Need U"** on Armada Deep. Frahm also produces under aliases such as **Davis Redfield** and has remixed for international artists. His sound pairs **warm chords and steady grooves** with vocal hooks designed for clubs and radio.

**中文**

**CALVO**（Christopher Frahm，德国）是德国 **DJ、制作人兼主唱**，活跃于电子舞曲圈。作品发行于 **Armada Music**、**Spinnin' Records** 等厂牌，风格涵盖 **future house**、**deep house** 与 festival 向 EDM。*Vicious Girl* 之前，他以 Armada Deep 上的 **"Need U"** 等曲目获得关注。Frahm 亦以 **Davis Redfield** 等别名发表作品，并为国际艺人制作 remix。其 sound 以**温暖和弦与稳定 groove** 搭配适合 club 与电台的人声 hook。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Vicious Girl"** was released on **11 December 2015** on **Armada Music**. Built on a **future house** groove with a male vocal, the lyrics tell a **love-as-danger** story: the singer loses his heart to a *vicious girl* who is *so beautiful* yet *a killer*—then insists *I know I'm not gonna die*, as if danger only makes the pull stronger. Images like *shoot me with that look* and *smoke without a fire* mix threat and allure; *I'm losing it tonight* captures surrender to someone toxic but irresistible. The track appeared on compilations including *Armada Miami 2016* and fits the mid-2010s **deep/future house** wave on festival playlists.

**中文**

**"Vicious Girl"** 于 **2015 年 12 月 11 日**由 **Armada Music** 发行。建立在 **future house** groove 与男声之上，歌词讲**爱即危险**：歌手把心交给 *vicious girl*——*so beautiful* 却 *a killer*——接着又说 *I know I'm not gonna die*，仿佛越危险越上头。*shoot me with that look*、*smoke without a fire* 等意象把威胁与诱惑搅在一起；*I'm losing it tonight* 写向有毒却无法抗拒的人投降。歌曲收录于 *Armada Miami 2016* 等合辑，属于 2010 年代中期 festival **deep/future house** 浪潮。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **vicious girl** | 狠女孩；蛇蝎女 | *vicious* = 恶毒、凶猛；歌题核心矛盾：危险 + 美丽 |
| **lost my heart to** | 把心交给了 | 经典恋爱表达；*lose one's heart to sb* |
| **she is a killer** | 她是杀手 | 比喻致命吸引力，非字面杀人 |
| **I'm not gonna die** | 我不会死 | 与 killer 对照：明知危险仍上头 |
| **sun of melted plastic** | 融化的塑料太阳 | surreal 意象：假、艳、危险的光 |
| **beauty makes me blind** | 她的美让我盲目 | 与 death 意象对照：美到失去判断 |
| **shoot me with a look** | 用眼神「开枪」 | *shoot* 比喻目光杀伤力；亦作 *that look in her beautiful eyes* |
| **smoke without a fire** | 无火之烟 | 表面无害却有隐忧；或「虚有其表的危险」 |
| **losing it** | 失控了；撑不住了 | 口语；可指情绪、理智或欲望崩溃 |
| **vicious heart girl** | 心狠的女孩 | 歌里固定搭配；*vicious* = 恶毒、凶猛 |
| **love only feels right** | 只有和她在一起才觉得对 | 浪漫化危险关系；*feels right* = 感觉才对味 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *Vicious girl* 重复多、旋律稳定，适合 **KTV / 跟唱**；先练清 *lost my heart to a* 的连读。
- 歌词短但**比喻密集**（killer、plastic sun、smoke without fire），适合当作「危险吸引力」词汇包记忆。
- 男声在 drop 前后清晰，可用来练 **losing it tonight** 的重音与 *beautiful* 尾音。
- 与 #79 [[learning-notes/english-song/aint-my-fault-zara-larsson|Ain't My Fault]] 对照：两首都是舞曲壳 + 恋爱主题，但本曲偏**危险痴迷**，#79 偏**俏皮调情**。
- 延伸恋爱口语：[[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 中 *lost my heart* 相关句型。
