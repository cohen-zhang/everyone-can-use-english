## 歌手简介 · About Zara Larsson & R3HAB

**English**

**Zara Larsson** (born 16 December 1997, Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish **pop singer**. She won *Talang* (Sweden's Got Talent) at age 10 and broke internationally with **"Lush Life"** (2015). Her debut album *So Good* (2017) cemented her as a **dance-pop** star with confident, flirty lyrics and a powerful voice. She has won multiple **MTV** and **Teen Choice** awards and remains one of Sweden's biggest pop exports.

**R3HAB** (Fadil El Ghoul, born 2 April 1986, Breda, Netherlands) is a Dutch **DJ and record producer** specialising in **EDM** and festival remixes. He has remixed tracks for Calvin Harris, Bruno Mars, and many others; his **R3hab Remix** of *Ain't My Fault* adds a harder club drop while keeping Zara's vocal hook.

**中文**

**Zara Larsson**（1997 年 12 月 16 生于瑞典 Stockholm）是瑞典**流行歌手**。10 岁赢得 *Talang*（瑞典达人），2015 年以 **"Lush Life"** 国际走红。首张专辑 *So Good*（2017）以自信、带调情感的词和强声线确立**舞曲流行**地位。获多项 **MTV** 与 **Teen Choice** 奖，是瑞典最具代表性的 pop 输出之一。

**R3HAB**（Fadil El Ghoul，1986 年 4 月 2 生于荷兰 Breda）是荷兰 **DJ 兼制作人**，擅长 **EDM** 与 festival remix。曾为 Calvin Harris、Bruno Mars 等制作混音；本曲 **R3hab Remix** 在保留 Zara 人声 hook 的同时加入更硬的 club drop。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Ain't My Fault"** was released on **2 September 2016** as the third single from Zara Larsson's album *So Good*; the **R3hab Remix** (7 October 2016) followed shortly after and became a club staple. Co-written with **MNEK** (Uzoechi Emenike) and **Markus Sepehrmanesh**, the song is a **playful disclaimer**: the singer meets someone irresistibly attractive and insists *it ain't my fault* if things escalate—*you keep turning me on*. The tone is confident and teasing rather than heartbroken; the hook repeats like a mantra. The original reached the **top 10** in several European charts; the remix extended its life on dance floors worldwide.

**中文**

**"Ain't My Fault"** 于 **2016 年 9 月 2 日**发行，为 Zara Larsson 专辑 *So Good* 第三支单曲；**R3hab Remix**（2016 年 10 月 7 日）随后推出，成为 club 常客。与 **MNEK**（Uzoechi Emenike）、**Markus Sepehrmanesh** 等共同创作，歌曲是一句**俏皮的免责声明**：歌手遇见无法抗拒的对象，坚称若事态升级 *it ain't my fault*——*you keep turning me on*。语气自信、戏谑，而非心碎；hook 像咒语一样重复。原版在欧洲多国**前十**；混音版让这首歌在全球舞池里续航更久。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **it ain't my fault** | 不怪我；不是我的错 | 歌题核心；*ain't* = 口语 *isn't* / *aren't* |
| **turn someone on** | 让某人心动/兴奋 | 常带性吸引力；*you keep turning me on* 重复强调 |
| **got me so gone** | 让我神魂颠倒 | *gone* = 失去理智、上头（俚语） |
| **not leavin' alone** | 不会一个人走 | 暗示不会空手离开、会带走对方或发生点什么 |
| **get it when it's hot** | 趁热打铁 | 习语；此处双关「趁气氛还在」 |
| **all in my business** | 插手我全部私事/生活 | *business* = 私事；也可指亲密介入 |
| **can't be responsible** | 不能（负）责任 | 俏皮甩锅：后果你自己承担 |
| **irresistible** | 无法抗拒的 | 正式词用在 pop 歌里很顺口 |
| **trip, fall, and land on your lap** | 绊倒摔在你腿上 | 画面感强；*trip* = 绊了一下 |
| **bad boy smooth** | 坏男孩式的顺滑 | 形容举止、魅力类型 |
| **look good on ya** | 我（穿/贴）在你身上好看 | *I'd look good on ya*；*on ya* = on you |
| **Missed me with that…** | 别跟我来那一套 | 俚语 = 拒绝、别烦我；*Miss me with* 很美式 |
| **what's your name, your sign** | 你叫什么、什么星座 | 老套搭讪台词；歌里表示「没时间废话」 |
| **that's for sure** | 千真万确 | 口语确认；*Yeah, that's for sure* |
| **your body is calling me** | 你的身体在呼唤我 | Remix 收尾；直白欲望表达 |
| **don't blame me** | 别怪我 | 与 *it ain't my fault* 呼应的甩锅句 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *It ain't my fault* 四句结构相同，适合**跟唱**练 *ain't* 的弱读与 *turning me on* 连读。
- 歌里是**主动、自信**的调情语气，不是分手歌；可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里 playful / flirty 句型。
- Verse 2 的 *Missed me with that* 很**美式口语**，适合记作「拒绝老套搭讪」模板。
- R3hab Remix  drop 较重，精听歌词建议先听**原声版**再听 remix；本笔记歌词两版通用。
- 同列表舞曲恋爱向可延伸：[[learning-notes/english-song/vicious-girl-calvo|Vicious Girl]]（#80）。
