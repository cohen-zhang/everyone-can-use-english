## 歌手简介 · About Lady Gaga

**English**

**Lady Gaga** (Stefani Germanotta, born 28 March 1986, New York) is an American **singer-songwriter**, actress, and cultural icon who reshaped 2000s–2010s **dance-pop**. After years of songwriting for other artists, she broke through with debut album **The Fame** (2008) and its reissue **The Fame Monster** (2009), which produced **"Poker Face"**, **"Telephone"**, and **"Bad Romance"**—one of the defining hits of the decade. Gaga's early work pairs **electro-pop** beats with provocative lyrics about fame, sexuality, and toxic desire; her image mixed high fashion, performance art, and MTV spectacle. She has won **13 Grammy Awards**, an **Oscar** for **"Shallow"** (*A Star Is Born*), and is known for championing LGBTQ+ rights and mental-health awareness. *Bad Romance* remains among the **best-selling singles of all time** and a staple of pop culture choreography.

**中文**

**Lady Gaga**（本名 Stefani Germanotta，1986 年 3 月 28 日生，纽约）是美国**创作型歌手**、演员与文化符号，重塑了 2000–2010 年代的**舞曲流行**。多年为他人写歌后，她以首张专辑 **The Fame**（2008）及再版 **The Fame Monster**（2009）爆红，诞生 **"Poker Face"**、**"Telephone"**、**"Bad Romance"**——十年最具代表性的金曲之一。Gaga 早期作品将 **electro-pop** 节拍与关于名利、情欲、病态渴望的挑衅歌词结合；形象融合高级时装、行为艺术与 MTV 奇观。获 **13 项格莱美**、**奥斯卡**（**"Shallow"**），并长期倡导 LGBTQ+ 权益与心理健康。*Bad Romance* 仍是**史上最畅销单曲之一**，也是流行文化编舞经典。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Bad Romance"** was released on **26 October 2009** as the lead single from *The Fame Monster*, co-written by Gaga and producer **RedOne**. Built on a stuttering **electro-club** beat, the song captures **obsessive, self-destructive love**: the narrator wants everything about a partner—the ugly, the disease, the drama—even knowing it will hurt. The hook *Want your bad romance* frames passion as something dangerous but irresistible. The bridge mixes English with French (*J'veux ton amour, et je veux ta revanche* = I want your love, and I want your revenge) before rejecting friendship: *I don't wanna be friends*. The music video—white bathtub, chess, burning, avant-garde fashion—became iconic. The track topped charts worldwide, sold over 12 million copies, and won a **Grammy** for Best Female Pop Vocal Performance.

**中文**

**"Bad Romance"** 于 **2009 年 10 月 26 日**发行，为 *The Fame Monster* 主打单曲，由 Gaga 与制作人 **RedOne** 共同创作。在顿挫的 **electro-club** 节拍上，歌写**执迷、自毁式的爱**：叙述者想要伴侣的一切——丑陋、病态、戏剧性——明知会受伤。Hook *Want your bad romance* 把激情框成危险却无法抗拒的东西。Bridge 混用英法文（*J'veux ton amour, et je veux ta revanche* = 我要你的爱，也要你的报复），随后拒绝只做朋友：*I don't wanna be friends*。MV——白色浴缸、国际象棋、火焰、前卫时装——成为经典。歌曲横扫全球榜单，销量逾 1200 万，获**格莱美「最佳流行女声演唱」**。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| **bad romance** | 糟糕的恋情；有毒的爱 | 歌题；指伤人却无法自拔的关系 |
| **caught in** | 陷入；被困在 | *caught in a bad romance* |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- Hook 里 *Rah, rah, ah-ah-ah / Roma, roma-ma* 无实义，跟唱时抓**节奏**即可；重音在 *Want your bad romance*。
- Verse 排比 *I want your ugly / disease / drama* 适合练「我要你的……」句型；注意 *everything as long as it's free* 的讽刺语气。
- Bridge 法文句可先记发音再记意思：*J'veux ton amour* ≈ 「热佛 东 阿木尔」。
- 全曲 **level/easy** 在于重复度高；难点在 Bridge 语速与 *move that bitch crazy* 等粗口——KTV 场合注意受众。
- 想延伸读**矛盾情欲**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里爱与恨交织的表达。
