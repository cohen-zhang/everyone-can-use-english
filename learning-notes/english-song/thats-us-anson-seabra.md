## 歌手简介 · About Anson Seabra

**English**

**Anson Seabra** (born 25 December 1996) is an American **singer-songwriter** and **pianist** from **Overland Park, Kansas**. He studied music at Kansas State University and built a career by posting heartfelt piano ballads on YouTube before breaking through on streaming platforms. His songs—often tagged **"sad boy pop"**—blend confessional lyrics with melodic hooks about love, heartbreak, and self-doubt. Hits include **"Robin Hood,"** **"Welcome to Wonderland,"** **"Keep Your Head Up Princess,"** and **"That's Us."** He has toured internationally and released albums such as *Songs I Wrote in My Bedroom* and *Feeling for My Life*.

**中文**

**Anson Seabra**（1996 年 12 月 25 日生）是美国**创作型歌手**、**钢琴手**，来自**堪萨斯州 Overland Park**。在堪萨斯州立大学学音乐，先在 YouTube 发布真挚的钢琴抒情曲，后在流媒体平台走红。作品常被称作 **「伤感流行」**，把坦白式歌词和朗朗上口的旋律结合，主题多为爱情、心碎与自我怀疑。代表作为 **"Robin Hood"**、**"Welcome to Wonderland"**、**"Keep Your Head Up Princess"**、**"That's Us"** 等；曾国际巡演，并发行 *Songs I Wrote in My Bedroom*、*Feeling for My Life* 等专辑。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"That's Us"** was released on **17 May 2019** as a single. Seabra wrote it about an **on-again, off-again relationship** that both people know is doomed—*this could never end well*—yet they keep calling, showing up at each other's doors, and stealing time they said they didn't want to give. The chorus reframes the story: *What if we're wrong? What if we're not all that we thought?* The title line **"I guess that's us"** is resigned acceptance; the final twist **"I guess that's love"** admits the pattern might be love itself, not just bad luck. Piano-driven and mid-tempo, it's a staple for fans who relate to **toxic but magnetic** chemistry. The song helped launch Seabra's career on **YouTube** and streaming, accumulating hundreds of millions of plays worldwide.

**中文**

**"That's Us"** 于 **2019 年 5 月 17 日**以单曲形式发行。Seabra 写的是一段双方都知道没结果、却断不干净的**分分合合**：*this could never end well*（这绝不会有好下场），却仍半夜打电话、出现在门口、「偷」彼此说好的独处时间。副歌反问：*万一我们错了呢？万一我们没自己想得那么好呢？* 歌名句 **"I guess that's us"** 是认命的「我们就是这样」；结尾改成 **"I guess that's love"** 则承认——也许这就是爱本身，而不只是倒霉。钢琴主导、中板节奏，深受「有毒但互相吸引」关系共鸣者喜爱；在 **YouTube** 与流媒体上助力 Seabra 成名，全球播放量达数亿。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **shoulda known** | 早该知道 | *should have* 口语缩略 |
| **get out my life** | 滚出我的生活 | 口语；也可以说 *get out of my life* |
| **steal someone's time** | 占用某人的时间 | 歌里指明知不该仍纠缠 |
| **end well** | 有好下场 | *never end well* = 注定悲剧 |
| **only human** | 只是人性使然 | 为犯错找借口时常用 |
| **never learn** | 永远学不乖 | 重复犯同样错的自嘲 |
| **take the fall** | 承担后果；扛下一切 | 原意「摔倒」，此处比喻承受 |
| **for worse or better** | 不论好坏 | 婚礼誓词 *for better or worse* 变体 |
| **what if we're wrong** | 万一我们错了呢 | 对自我认知的怀疑 |
| **make it along** | 撑下去；走得长远 | 口语，指关系能否继续 |
| **I guess that's us** | 我想这就是我们 | 认命、苦笑 |
| **show up at your door** | 出现在你门口 | 典型 on-off 剧情 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I guess that's us* 适合 **KTV**；注意最后一段改成 *that's **love*** 的情绪转折。
- *shoulda / could've / didn't* 集中出现，适合练 **过去虚拟语气** 的口语缩略。
- 想对照「明知不对还回头」的口语，可翻阅 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里关于分手、复合的表达。
