## 歌手简介 · About ChianoSky

**English**

**ChianoSky** (full name **ChianoSky Kruger**, born 1993, Mbombela, Mpumalanga, South Africa) is a South African **singer-songwriter** and model. Her real given name is ChianoSky—not a stage name; her mother combined a friend's "Chiano" with "Sky" for a sense of limitless possibility. She studied music at the **National School of the Arts**, was discovered while waitressing, and signed to **EMI**. Her debut album ***Hungry*** (2012) mixed jazzy pop, soul, and cabaret-tinged ballads; it earned **five South African Music Award (SAMA) nominations**. Hit singles included *"Sick Sick"*, *"Walking Away"*, and *"Home Sweet Home"*.

**中文**

**ChianoSky**（全名 **ChianoSky Kruger**，1993 年生于南非姆普马兰加省 Mbombela）是南非**创作歌手**与模特。ChianoSky 是本名而非艺名——母亲把朋友名里的 "Chiano" 与 "Sky" 拼在一起，寓意无限可能。她曾就读 **National School of the Arts**，打工时被发掘，签约 **EMI**。首专 ***Hungry***（2012）融合爵士流行、灵魂与带 cabaret 气质的情歌，获 **五项南非音乐奖（SAMA）提名**。热单包括 *"Sick Sick"*、*"Walking Away"*、*"Home Sweet Home"*。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Walking Away"** is a single from ChianoSky's debut album ***Hungry*** (often dated 2011–2012 on streaming; the album campaign peaked in 2012). Co-written with **Andre Scheepers**, it is a mid-tempo **pop / soul ballad** about mixed signals: *you said you love me one minute—then you're walking away*. The singer wants to feel **wanted** and even **haunted** by someone who claims love but won't show it. Official lyric video released around Valentine's 2012; it became one of her major South African radio hits alongside *"Sick Sick"*.

**中文**

**"Walking Away"** 选自 ChianoSky 首专 ***Hungry***（流媒体常标 2011；专辑推广高峰在 2012）。与 **Andre Scheepers** 合写。这是一首中速 **流行 / 灵魂情歌**，写感情里的矛盾信号：*你上一分钟说爱我——下一分钟却在走开*。歌者渴望被需要、甚至被对方「缠住」，对方却口惠而实不至。2012 年情人节前后发官方歌词视频，与 *"Sick Sick"* 同为南非电台热播曲。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **one minute… then you're walking away** | 上一分钟……下一秒你就在走开 | 对比句；写善变很管用 |
| **walking away** | 走开；抽身离开 | 歌名；关系里的「退场」 |
| **too scared to say** | 怕得说不出口 | *too… to…* 句型 |
| **you don't show it, I know it** | 你不表现，我却清楚 | 口惠而实不至 |
| **if falling is so easy** | 若爱上一个人这么容易 | *falling* = falling in love |
| **it's not supposed to be like this** | 不该是这样的 | 失望时高频句 |
| **know my place** | 知道自己的位置/分寸 | 此处偏「我想搞清我在你心里的位置」 |
| **break my space** | 闯进我的空间 | 歌词用法；日常更常说 *invade my space* |
| **I'm needed by you** | 我被你需要 | 被动语态；情感核心 |
| **haunted by you** | 被你缠住；对你念念不忘 | *haunt* 可指挥之不去的人/记忆 |
| **wanted by you** | 被你渴望；被你需要 | 与 *needed* 近，更偏「被想要」 |
| **so hard to get but so quick to throw away** | 得到很难，扔掉却很快 | 对称结构，好记 |
| **crave me** | 渴望我 | *crave* 比 *want* 更强烈 |
| **like a moth to a flame** | 像飞蛾扑火 | 习语；明知危险仍靠近 |
| **touch and go** | 浅尝辄止；碰一下就走 | 也作「情况危急」；此处偏短暂接触 |
| **not just a phase** | 不只是一阵热/一个阶段 | *phase* = 阶段性的 |
| **we cannot erase** | 我们抹不掉 | 与 *phase* 对仗：不是能随便抹掉的 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **难度：** 中——旋律好唱，注意 *haunted / wanted* 元音与副歌长音；Bridge 节奏稍密。
- **核心对立：** *You said you love me* vs *you're walking away*；*you don't show it, I know it*——练「言行不一」类口语。
- **两个 want：** *haunted by you*（被缠住）与 *wanted by you*（被渴望）是副歌双核；跟唱时把重音放在 *haunted* / *wanted* 上。
- **校正说明：** 旧稿 *your walking* → *you're walking*；*you to scared* → *you're too scared*；*feel like am needed* → *feel like I'm needed*；跟唱以本笔记为准。
- **延伸：** 断联与「曾经」可对照 [[learning-notes/english-song/somebody-that-i-used-to-know-gotye|Somebody That I Used to Know]]；情绪词可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
