## 歌手简介 · About Old Dominion

**English**

**Old Dominion** is a **country music** band formed in **Nashville** in 2007: **Matthew Ramsey** (lead vocals), **Trevor Rosen** and **Whit Sellers** (both from South Carolina), **Geoff Sprung** (bass), and **Brad Tursi** (guitar). Before the band took off, members wrote hits for other artists—Ramsey co-wrote *"Chainsaw"* for The Band Perry and *"Say You Won't Let Go"* for James Arthur. Their own breakthrough came with *"Break Up with Him"* (2015), followed by the album **Happy Endings** (2017). Old Dominion blends **contemporary country** with **pop hooks** and witty, conversational lyrics; they have won multiple **CMA** and **ACM** awards, including Vocal Group of the Year.

**中文**

**Old Dominion** 是 **2007 年**在 **Nashville** 成立的**乡村乐队**：**Matthew Ramsey**（主唱）、**Trevor Rosen** 与 **Whit Sellers**（均来自南卡）、**Geoff Sprung**（贝斯）、**Brad Tursi**（吉他）。走红前，成员曾为他人写歌——Ramsey 参与 The Band Perry 的 *"Chainsaw"*、James Arthur 的 *"Say You Won't Let Go"* 等。乐队自己的突破是 2015 年 *"Break Up with Him"*，随后发行专辑 **Happy Endings**（2017）。风格融合**当代乡村**与**流行副歌**，歌词机智、口语化；曾获多项 **CMA**、**ACM** 奖，包括「年度声乐组合」。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"No Such Thing as a Broken Heart"** was released on **10 March 2017** as the lead single from *Happy Endings*. Co-written by band members with **Shane McAnally** and **Trent Tomlinson**, it is an **uptempo country-pop anthem** about choosing to love boldly even when life shakes you—because you can't stop earthquakes or fading sunsets, so you might as well **jump off the rope swing** and love like a broken heart doesn't exist. The opening references **"Jack and Diane"** (John Mellencamp's 1982 hit about young love) and asks whether those small-town kids made it. The song peaked at **No. 3** on Billboard Hot Country Songs and crossed to pop radio.

**中文**

**"No Such Thing as a Broken Heart"** 于 **2017 年 3 月 10 日**发行，为 *Happy Endings* 主打单曲。由乐队成员与 **Shane McAnally**、**Trent Tomlinson** 共同创作，是一首**轻快节奏的乡村流行 anthem**：即使生活颠簸，仍选择大胆去爱——地震挡不住、夕阳拦不住，不如像**从绳秋千上跳下去**一样，爱得像「心碎不存在」。开头提到 **"Jack and Diane"**（John Mellencamp 1982 年写小镇青春恋爱的名曲），问那对少年后来有没有走下去。单曲在 Billboard Hot Country Songs 最高 **第 3**，并打入流行电台。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌句式 **You can't keep … from -ing** 重复三次，适合练「挡不住……」的英文逻辑；可先跟唱这一句再扩展整段。
- *Jack and Diane* 是文化梗：不熟可先听 John Mellencamp 原版，再回来听这句会更有画面。
- 最后一段把 *treat your life* 改成 *treat your **love***，留意这个**小变化**——从人生哲学落到「去爱」。
- 想延伸**挫折与振作**表达，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 对照 *chin up* 一类句型。
