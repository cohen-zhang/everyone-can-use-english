# Five Little Ducks — Classic Books with Holes

**书目：** *Five Little Ducks*（Classic Books with Holes 系列）  
**Illustrator:** Penny Ives · **Publisher:** Child's Play International  
**给 Celine 跟唱用** — **5→1→0** 倒数：每天少一只回来；鸭妈妈喊四声 *Quack*。最后一节 *Sad mother duck*，**五只一起回来**。

**绘本说明：** 洞洞页可以数还剩几只。歌词只唱走丢和回来；画面里小鸭子各忙各的，跟唱时可以指一指「少掉的那只去哪了」。

## 歌词 · Lyrics

> 依据 Child's Play / Penny Ives 版整理。本绘本唱 **Over the hill**（一座小山）。Raffi 等版本常唱 **Over the hills**。零只那节有的唱 **none of those little ducks**；结尾也有 *all five little ducks came back*。本笔记用洞洞书常见跟唱：**none of the five** · **Sad mother duck** · **all of the five**。

> 学习建议：先只看 **英文歌词** 跟唱；需要核对意思时，再往下看 **中文翻译**。
> Study tip: Sing along with **English Lyrics** first; scroll down to **Chinese Translation** only when you need meaning.

## 单复数说明 · Singular & plural in the lyrics

这首和 [[learning-notes/english-song/celine-kids/classic-books-with-holes/FiveLittleMen|Five Little Men]] 一样，是**倒数**：人数变，**ducks / duck** 跟着变。

### 1 · 数字 + ducks / duck

| 只数 | 怎么说 | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5–2 只 | **… little ducks** | Five / Four / Three / Two little **ducks** |
| 回来 4、3、2 只 | **only … ducks** | only four / three / two little **ducks** |
| 回来 1 只 | **one little duck** | only **one little duck** came back |
| 出去的最后一只 | **One little duck** | **One little duck** went out… |
| 0 只 | **none of the five** | **none** of the five little ducks |
| 五只同回 | **all of the five little ducks** | **all of the five** little ducks came back |

**跟娃练一句：** *Five **ducks**. One **duck**. Then **none**. Then five **ducks** again!*

### 2 · only / none / all：回来了几只

| 词 | 简中 | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **only** | 只有（比出去时少） | But **only** four little ducks came back. |
| **none of** | 其中一只也没有 | But **none of** the five little ducks came back. |
| **all of** | 其中全部 | And **all of** the five little ducks came back. |

### 3 · hill、day、mother duck：单数

| 词 | 简中 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **the hill** | 那座小山 | 本绘本是**一座**小山（别的版本唱 *hills*） |
| **one day** | 有一天 | 每一节讲**那一天** |
| **Mother duck** | 鸭妈妈 | 始终**一位**妈妈 |

**quack** 连说四遍是节奏，不是四只鸭子。

## 时态说明 · Tense in the lyrics

整首歌在讲**已经发生的事**（*one day*），动词是**一般过去时**——和 [[learning-notes/english-song/celine-kids/classic-books-with-holes/FiveLittleMen|Five Little Men]] 同类。

### 1 · 时间标志：one day

**Went out one day.** — 有一天出去了。听到 **one day**，后面用过去式。

### 2 · 过去式动词一览

| 原形（现在） | 过去式（歌词） | 简中 | 歌词句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **go** | **went** | 去了 | **went** out one day |
| **say** | **said** | 说了 | Mother duck **said**, "Quack…" |
| **come** | **came** | 回来了 | only four little ducks **came** back |

**不规则：** **go → went**，**say → said**（读 /sed/，不是 *sayed*），**come → came**。

### 3 · came back：回来了

**Came back** = **come back** 的过去式。每一节结尾都是「回来了几只」。

### 4 · 和《Five Little Men》对比

| | 本歌 *Five Little Ducks* | [[learning-notes/english-song/celine-kids/classic-books-with-holes/FiveLittleMen|Five Little Men]] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 倒数 | **5→0**，再 **五只一起回来** | **5→0**，再 **五人飞回来留下** |
| 时态 | **过去时** went, said, came | **过去时** flew, looked, came back |
| 单数那一节 | **One little duck** | **One little man** · **He** |
| 零 | **none** of the five | **nobody** / **no one** |

两首可以接在一起练：手指倒数，再练 *one duck / one man*。

## 倒数小练习 · Count-down drill

**前半段（走丢）：**

| 问 | 答 |
| --- | --- |
| How many ducks at the start? | **Five** little ducks. |
| One didn't come back. How many now? | **Four**! |
| …then? | **Three** → **Two** → **One** → **None**! |
| What does Mother duck say? | **Quack, quack, quack, quack.** |

**减法句型：** *Five take away one is four.* — 五减一等于四。

**后半段（回来）：**

| 问 | 答 |
| --- | --- |
| How does Mother duck feel? | **Sad.** |
| Then what happens? | **All of the five** little ducks **came back**. |
| How many now? | **Five** again! |
