## 歌手简介 · About Gallant

**English**

**Gallant** (born **Christopher Gallant**, 14 February 1991) is an American **R&B singer-songwriter** from **Columbia, Maryland**. He studied at **New York University** and broke through with the 2014 EP **Zebra** and the single **"Weight in Gold"**, earning a **Grammy nomination for Best R&B Performance**. Gallant's voice—a **high, agile tenor** with falsetto ease—has drawn comparisons to **Frank Ocean** and **Miguel**. His lyrics often explore **space, distance, and emotional repair** in relationships, blending electronic production with soulful vulnerability. Albums like **Ology** (2016) and **Neptune** (2021) cemented his reputation as a **thinking person's R&B** artist: poetic, sci-fi tinged, and unafraid of raw confession.

**中文**

**Gallant**（本名 **Christopher Gallant**，1991 年 2 月 14 日生）是美国 **R&B 创作型歌手**，来自马里兰州 **Columbia**。就读 **纽约大学**，2014 年 EP **Zebra** 与单曲 **"Weight in Gold"** 让他崭露头角，获 **格莱美最佳 R&B 表演**提名。嗓音是**高亢、灵活的男高音**，假声自如，常被与 **Frank Ocean**、**Miguel** 比较。歌词常写关系里的**距离、空间与情感修复**，电子制作与灵魂乐脆弱感并存。专辑 **Ology**（2016）、**Neptune**（2021）确立「**思考型 R&B**」形象：诗意、带科幻感、敢于坦白。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Julie."** is the opening track on Gallant's 2021 album **Neptune**, released **26 March 2021**. The song is a **pleading R&B ballad** addressed to someone named Julie who is about to leave: *Don't leave me until the morning* buys time; *Let me put that anesthetic on you* offers comfort like numbing pain. The verses admit fault—*I messed up the past*, *Can a nigga save face?*, *Hit a nerve once or twice*—while the chorus asks why she keeps **running away**. The line *No carbon-based life form could ever make a mistake* uses sci-fi wording for a human apology: we're all flawed, but love might still be worth saving. *Time can't erase* and *we can't undo* frame regret as irreversible, yet the singer keeps begging her to **stay until morning**.

**中文**

**"Julie."** 是 Gallant 2021 年专辑 **Neptune** 的开场曲，**2021 年 3 月 26 日**发行。一首**恳求式 R&B ballad**，对即将离开的 **Julie** 说话：*Don't leave me until the morning*（别走，至少待到早上）争取时间；*Let me put that anesthetic on you*（让我给你上点麻醉）像要抚平伤痛。主歌承认过错——*I messed up the past*（我把过去搞砸了）、*Can a nigga save face?*（还能挽回面子吗？）、*Hit a nerve once or twice*（戳了一两次痛处）——副歌追问为何她总**逃跑**。 *No carbon-based life form could ever make a mistake* 用科幻说法道歉：碳基生命都会犯错，但爱也许仍值得挽回。 *Time can't erase*、*we can't undo* 把遗憾写成无法撤销，歌手仍求她**待到早上**。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 歌名与叠句 **Julie** 是记忆锚点；副歌 *why do you keep running away* 旋律起伏大，适合**精研跟唱**。
- *carbon-based life form* 是 Gallant 标志性的**科幻 R&B 措辞**，理解「凡人都犯错」即可。
- 部分歌词含 **AAVE** 与粗口语境（如 *Can a nigga save face?*），学习时注意场合；正式写作避免照搬。
- *anesthetic* 医学词用在爱情语境，可记作「给你止痛／麻醉」的比喻。
- 想积累**分手挽回口语**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 相关句型。
