## 歌手简介 · About Kim Taylor

**English**

**Kim Taylor** (born 20 December 1973, Miami, Florida) is an American **independent singer-songwriter** based in **Cincinnati, Ohio**. She played flute in school bands, sang in church, and moved to Cincinnati in 1996 to study English before entering the local folk scene. Early albums *So Black, So Bright* (2002) and *I Feel Like a Fading Light* (2006) built a loyal Midwest following; she also ran the coffee shop **Pleasant Perk** for years. Taylor has toured with **Over the Rhine** and placed songs on TV series including *Justified*, *Flashpoint*, and *Hawthorne*. Her warm, acoustic **folk-pop** voice and literary lyrics explore love, faith, and self-discovery.

**中文**

**Kim Taylor**（1973 年 12 月 20 日生，佛罗里达迈阿密）是定居**辛辛那提**的美国**独立创作歌手**。童年吹长笛、在教堂唱歌；1996 年为读英文专业迁居辛辛那提，随后进入当地民谣圈。专辑 *So Black, So Bright*（2002）与 *I Feel Like a Fading Light*（2006）在 Midwest 积累口碑；她曾经营咖啡店 **Pleasant Perk** 多年。Taylor 曾为 **Over the Rhine** 巡演暖场，作品被 *Justified*、*Flashpoint*、*Hawthorne* 等剧选用。她温暖、偏 acoustic 的**民谣流行**嗓音与文学性歌词，常写爱、信念与自我发现。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"I Am You"** began as a co-write for a **transgender storyline** on the soap *Days of Our Lives* that was never used; Taylor finished it with producer **Mike Denneen** and released it as a digital single in **2008**. The song became one of her signature tracks, later appearing on *Build You Up* (2011) and the 2021 remaster on *Acoustic and Unreleased*. Over a gentle *la di da* hook, the lyrics use anchor-and-gravity metaphors to describe two people whose identities blur: *I am you, everything you do*. It is less a conventional love song than an **empathy anthem** about seeing yourself in another person.

**中文**

**"I Am You"** 最初为肥皂剧 *Days of Our Lives* 一条**跨性别角色线**合写，该剧情未采用；Taylor 与制作人 **Mike Denneen** 完成录音，**2008 年**以数字单曲发行。后成为代表作，收录于 *Build You Up*（2011），2021 年 remaster 版见于 *Acoustic and Unreleased*。在轻柔的 *la di da* 旋律上，歌词用锚与引力比喻两人身份交融：*I am you, everything you do*。它更像一首关于**共情与认同**的歌，而非典型情歌。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **tied by truth like an anchor** | 被真相像锚一样拴住 | 比喻「被诚实/真实束缚」 |
| **a bottomless sea** | 没有底的海 | 喻情感深不可测 |
| **heart's gravity** | 心的引力 | 把爱比作物理引力，很 poetic |
| **all because of love** | 全是因为爱 | 副歌前铺垫；口语可简说 *all for love* |
| **don't know who I am** | 不知道自己是谁 | 身份困惑；歌里指关系中的迷失 |
| **awaken to the changes** | 被变化唤醒 | *awaken to* = 开始意识到 |
| **feel alive inside my own skin** | 在自己的皮肤里感到活着 | 习语 *in one's skin* = 自在、真实 |
| **coming home to a place I've always been** | 回到一个我一直都在的地方 | 回家却像「本来就在」——认同的 paradox |
| **linked eternally** | 永远连在一起 | *link* 比 *connect* 更紧 |
| **what we can't undo** | 我们无法撤销的事 | *undo* = 撤回、逆转 |
| **I am you** | 我就是你 | 歌题核心；表 deep empathy，非字面 |
| **everything you do** | 你所做的一切 | 与 *anything you say* 并列，强调全面融合 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I am you* 旋律简单、重复多，适合**跟唱**练长元音：*you / do / undo*。
- Verse 1 与 Verse 2 各有一对**对比隐喻**（anchor/sea vs. skin/home），可用来练「一边……一边……」的英文排比。
- *La di da* 段不必逐字翻译，当作**哼唱练习**即可；注意 Taylor 咬字轻、气声多。
- 想延伸练**恋爱与自我认同**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里关于 *identity in love* 的表达。
