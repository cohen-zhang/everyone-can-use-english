## 歌手简介 · About Jessie J

**English**

**Jessie J** (Jessica Cornish, born 1988 in London) is an English singer and songwriter known for her powerful voice. Before fame she co-wrote *Party in the U.S.A.* for Miley Cyrus. Her debut album ***Who You Are*** (2011) produced several hits, and *Bang Bang* (2014, with Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj) became a worldwide smash. **B.o.B** (Bobby Ray Simmons Jr.) is an American rapper from Atlanta, known for *Nothin' on You* and *Airplanes*.

**中文**

**Jessie J**（本名 Jessica Cornish，1988 年生于伦敦）是英国创作歌手，以爆发力极强的嗓音著称。成名前曾为 Miley Cyrus 合写 *Party in the U.S.A.*；首张专辑 ***Who You Are***（2011）诞生多首热门单曲，2014 年与 Ariana Grande、Nicki Minaj 合作的 *Bang Bang* 更是全球大热。**B.o.B**（本名 Bobby Ray Simmons Jr.）是来自亚特兰大的美国说唱歌手，代表作 *Nothin' on You*、*Airplanes*。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Price Tag"** is the second single from Jessie J's debut album ***Who You Are***, released in **January 2011**. Over a light, reggae-tinged beat, she says that music and life are *not about the money*—*we just wanna make the world dance*. B.o.B adds a rap verse about keeping the cars and cash but leaving him *six strings and a half-stack* (a guitar and an amp). It went to **No. 1 on the UK Singles Chart** and was a top-10 hit in many countries.

**中文**

**"Price Tag"** 是 Jessie J 首张专辑 ***Who You Are*** 的第二支单曲，**2011 年 1 月**发行。在轻快、带雷鬼味的节奏上，她唱出音乐与生活*不是为了钱*——*我们只想让全世界跳起舞*。B.o.B 的说唱段说：车和钱你都留着，只要给我*六根弦和半套音箱*（一把吉他加一台功放）就够了。登上**英国单曲榜冠军**，并在多国进入前十。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *It's not about the money, money, money* 节奏明快，适合 KTV 与**口语金句积累**。
- *money* 连唱三遍，注意第一个音节重读 /ˈmʌni/。
- B.o.B 说唱段语速快，可先读歌词理解 *six strings*（吉他）、*half-stack*（功放）等乐手行话。
- *It's not about the money* 句式可迁移到工作中：*It's not about the tool, it's about the process.*
