## 歌手简介 · About the Eagles

**English**

**The Eagles** are an American **rock band** formed in Los Angeles in **1971** by **Glenn Frey**, **Don Henley**, **Bernie Leadon**, and **Randy Meisner**. They became one of the best-selling acts in history by blending **country-rock**, **soft rock**, and polished harmonies—*Take It Easy*, *Hotel California*, and *Desperado* define the sound of 1970s California. **Frey** and **Henley** wrote most of the hits and shared lead vocals; the band's studio craft (often with producer **Glyn Johns**) set a template for mainstream rock. They disbanded in 1980, reunited in 1994, and continued touring until Frey's death in 2016. Six Grammy Awards and over **150 million** records sold worldwide place them among rock's most enduring catalogs.

**中文**

**The Eagles**（老鹰乐队）是 **1971 年**在洛杉矶成立的美国**摇滚乐队**，创始成员 **Glenn Frey**、**Don Henley**、**Bernie Leadon**、**Randy Meisner**。他们以 **乡村摇滚**、**软摇滚** 与精致和声融合，成为史上最畅销的乐队之一——*Take It Easy*、*Hotel California*、*Desperado* 几乎定义了七十年代加州摇滚之声。**Frey** 与 **Henley** 写下大部分金曲并分担主唱；录音室制作（常与制作人 **Glyn Johns** 合作）为主流摇滚树立了范本。1980 年解散，1994 年重组，持续巡演至 Frey 2016 年逝世。六座格莱美、全球唱片销量逾 **1.5 亿**，是摇滚史上最长青的作品目录之一。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Desperado"** opens the Eagles' second album, ***Desperado*** (released **17 April 1973**). **Glenn Frey** and **Don Henley** co-wrote it; Henley sings lead and played piano on the track. The song began when Henley, looking over Los Angeles from a Laurel Canyon house, played a ballad inspired by **Ray Charles**' *Modern Sounds in Country and Western Music*—Frey urged him to finish it. The album is a **concept record** about Old West outlaws; *Desperado* addresses a lone gunfighter who keeps *ridin' fences* instead of settling down. In Henley's words, the band saw themselves as outlaws in rock—living outside normal rules—while the lyric doubles as advice to anyone who chooses isolation over love. It was not a major single at first but became a **signature ballad**; **Linda Ronstadt**'s 1973 cover helped cement its fame.

**中文**

**"Desperado"** 是老鹰乐队第二张专辑 ***Desperado***（**1973 年 4 月 17 日**发行）的开场曲，由 **Glenn Frey** 与 **Don Henley** 共同创作；Henley 主唱并弹奏钢琴。创作始于 Henley 在 Laurel Canyon 俯瞰洛杉矶时，受 **Ray Charles** 的 *Modern Sounds in Country and Western Music* 启发写下的抒情曲——Frey 催他写完。整张专辑是以西部亡命徒为主题的**概念专辑**；*Desperado* 对一位亡命徒说话：他一直在 *ridin' fences*（骑围栏巡逻），迟迟不肯安定。Henley 后来表示，乐队把自己看作摇滚界的亡命徒——活在常规之外——歌词也可读作对任何以自我封闭代替爱之人的劝诫。起初并非大热单曲，却成为**标志性抒情曲**；**Linda Ronstadt** 1973 年翻唱进一步推高了知名度。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 节奏舒缓、Henley 咬字清楚，适合**精研发音**；注意 *ridin'*、*pleasin'*、*rainin'* 等 **-in'** 口语缩读。
- 扑克意象（*queen of diamonds* / *queen of hearts*）不必逐字背，先理解「算计 vs 真情」的对照即可。
- 结尾 **Let somebody love you** 重复三遍，跟唱时可先练这一句，再串主歌 1 的 *come to your senses*。
- *Your prison is walking through this world all alone* 是全曲核心——可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 **Lonely**、封闭与求助表达。
- 与 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/hey-jude-the-beatles|Hey Jude]] 同为「劝人走出封闭、接纳爱」的经典抒情曲，可对照 *let her into your heart* 与 *let somebody love you*。
