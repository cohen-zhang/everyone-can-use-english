## 歌手简介 · About Coldplay

**English**

**Coldplay** is a British **rock band** formed in London in the late 1990s: **Chris Martin** (vocals, piano), **Jonny Buckland** (guitar), **Guy Berryman** (bass), and **Will Champion** (drums). By the album ***X&Y*** (2005) they were already a stadium band that still liked to start a song small — a keyboard, a plain sentence — and let the room fill in later.

**中文**

**Coldplay**（酷玩乐队）是九十年代末在伦敦成立的英国**摇滚乐队**：**Chris Martin**（主唱、钢琴）、**Jonny Buckland**（吉他）、**Guy Berryman**（贝斯）、**Will Champion**（鼓）。到专辑 ***X&Y***（2005）时，他们已经是体育场乐队，却仍习惯把一首歌从很小的地方唱起：一台键盘，一句很白的话，再让声音慢慢把房间填满。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Fix You"** is on ***X&Y***, released **6 June 2005**. All four members are credited as writers. The UK single followed on **5 September 2005**. The track runs about **4:54**. It opens on a quiet organ. The verses list ordinary collapses: you tried, you failed, you got what you wanted and still missed what you needed, you are too tired to sleep. The chorus does not hand over a plan. It offers lights, warmth, and an attempt: *I will try to fix you.* Then the band gets loud, and the words step back into a wordless sing-along.

Martin has said he wrote it for his partner after her father died. The private occasion became a public song for anyone who needs company more than advice. *Fix* here is not "solve it today." *Try to* is the part to keep.

**中文**

**"Fix You"** 出自 **2005 年 6 月 6 日**发行的 ***X&Y***。四位成员都挂名创作。英国单曲在 **2005 年 9 月 5 日**发行。时长约 **4 分 54 秒**。歌从安静的风琴开始。主歌罗列很普通的垮掉：尽力了还是不成，得到了想要的却不是需要的，累到睡不着。副歌不给方案。它给光、给一点暖，然后只承诺一件事：*I will try to fix you.* 接着乐队变响，词退开，变成没有字的跟唱。

Martin 说过，这首歌是伴侣的父亲去世后写给她的。私人的事后来变成一首公共的陪伴歌：对方需要的是有人在，不是一套建议。这里的 *fix* 不是「今天就解决」。要留下的是 *try to*。

#### 歌在说什么 · The arc

**English**

The verses are a list, not a story with a villain. Effort fails. You get the thing you chased and still feel the lack. You are exhausted and awake. Tears do not stop. Something you cannot replace is gone. Love went to waste. The questions (*Could it be worse?*) do not ask for an answer. The chorus answers with presence: lights toward home, warmth coming back into the body, and one person who will try. The last part of the record barely uses words. The comfort becomes a sound you can stand inside.

**中文**

主歌是一张清单，不是一个有反派的故事。尽力了还是失败。追到了想要的，缺的还在。人已经累了，却睡不着。泪停不下来。有些失去补不回去。爱也白费了。*Could it be worse?* 这种问句不是在等答案。副歌用「人还在」来回答：有光把你领回家，暖意回到身体里，还有一个人愿意试。录音后段几乎不再加新词。陪伴变成你可以站在里面的声音。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **try your best** | 尽你的力 | 固定搭配。后面常接 *but* |
| **don't succeed** | 没有成功 | *succeed* 成功。这里不需要把失败讲成戏剧 |
| **what you want / what you need** | 你想要的 / 你需要的 | 得到前者，仍可能缺后者 |
| **feel so tired** | 累到不行 | *so* 把程度拉满 |
| **can't sleep** | 睡不着 | 累和失眠可以同时在 |
| **stuck in reverse** | 卡在倒挡；往回退 | *stuck in* 困在。*reverse* 倒车、反向 |
| **tears come streaming down** | 泪一直往下淌 | *streaming* 不停，不是掉一滴 |
| **can't replace** | 无法替代 | *replace* 换上别的来补 |
| **goes to waste** | 白费了 | *go to waste* 被糟蹋、没有落点 |
| **Could it be worse?** | 还能更糟吗？ | 疲倦时的反问，多半不是在征求意见 |
| **lights will guide you home** | 光会把你领回家 | *guide* 带领。*home* 可以是人，也可以是安全的感觉 |
| **ignite your bones** | 把暖意点回身体里 | *ignite* 点燃。*bones* 在这里是身体，不是医学词 |
| **I will try to fix you** | 我会试着修好你 | *try to* 没保证做得到。*fix* 是陪伴，不是立刻解决问题 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 主歌几乎是在说话，别赶。副歌再把 *Lights will guide you home* 和 *I will try to fix you* 唱完整。
- *fix you* 不要听成「我来搞定你的人生」。*try to* 把这句话从承诺改成陪伴。
- *stuck in reverse* 是汽车画面：挡位还挂在倒车上，人想往前，生活却在往后退。
- 后段的 *oh* 不是没词可唱。词已经说完了，剩下的是留下来。
- 放在《重头再来》之前听。那首歌要求重新站起来；这一首允许人先慢一点缓过来。缓过来之后，用 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/here-comes-the-sun-the-beatles|Here Comes the Sun]] 回到日常，而不是立刻接一首更热血的歌。
