## 歌手简介 · About Yom

**English**

**Yom** (born **Guillaume Humery**, Paris) is a French **virtuoso clarinetist** and composer who describes himself as **"klezmer by origin."** After seven albums exploring his mother's Transylvanian Jewish heritage, he released **Songs for the Old Man** (2016)—an **Americana**-inflected project co-arranged with guitarist **Aurélien Naffrichoux**. The album reimagines the story of Yom's father, who left for the United States in the **1950s**, and dedicates the music to **nomads, hobos, refugees**, and anyone living on the road. Yom's clarinet "sings" where there are no words: klezmer meets folk, steel guitar, and roots-country textures recorded live with vintage gear.

**中文**

**Yom**（本名 **Guillaume Humery**，巴黎）是法国**单簧管大师**与作曲家，自称为 **「克莱兹默出身」**。在七张专辑书写母亲一侧特兰西瓦尼亚犹太文化之后，2016 年与吉他手 **Aurélien Naffrichoux** 共同编曲推出 **Songs for the Old Man**——一张融入 **美式根源音乐（Americana）** 的「单簧管民谣歌曲集」。专辑重写父亲 **1950 年代**赴美流亡的家族传说，也献给路上的**游牧者、流浪者、难民**与所有漂泊之人。没有歌词的地方，Yom 用单簧管「唱」；克莱兹默与民谣、钢棒吉他、根源乡村音色在复古设备下的现场录音里交汇。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"The Old Man"** (5:13) is the **fifth track** on *Songs for the Old Man*, sitting between the road-movie energy of *Eldorado 54* and the farewell mood of *Those Who Stayed Behind*. It is an **instrumental** piece—no sung lyrics—built around Yom's clarinet melody over Naffrichoux's **steel guitar** and acoustic arrangements. The title points to Yom's father and, more broadly, to elders who carry exile, distance, and memory. Listeners often describe the album as a **continuous journey** best heard start to finish; this track is a quiet, reflective midpoint on that path. Ideal for **listening comprehension of mood**, **pronunciation of album vocabulary**, and **life-attitude reflection** rather than sing-along.

**中文**

**"The Old Man"**（5:13）是 *Songs for the Old Man* 的**第五首**，夹在 *Eldorado 54* 的公路电影感与 *Those Who Stayed Behind* 的告别情绪之间。这是一首**纯器乐**——无人声歌词——以 Yom 的单簧管主旋律配合 Naffrichoux 的**钢棒吉他**与原声编配。曲名既指 Yom 的父亲，也指向承载流亡、距离与记忆的**老一辈**。许多听众把整张专辑当作**一条连续旅程**从头听到尾；本曲是途中一段沉静、内省的中间站。更适合练**情绪听力**、**专辑相关词汇发音**与**人生态度思考**，而非跟唱。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 本曲**无歌词**，精研重点在：**听情绪**（clarinet 主旋律的起伏）与**记专辑英文标题**的发音。
- 建议**整张专辑连续听**：从 *Journey Of Life* 到 *On the Endless Road*，体会 *The Old Man* 在叙事中的位置。
- 想练**英文跟唱**，可转听同名单曲 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/the-old-man-菲尔科尔特|The Old Man — 菲尔・科尔特]]；本曲则适合**通勤纯听**或**发音练专辑词汇**（exile、displaced、refugee 等）。
- Naffrichoux 的 **steel guitar** 与 clarinet 对话处，可练听辨两种音色英文怎么说：*steel guitar*, *clarinet*, *folk guitar*, *live take*。
