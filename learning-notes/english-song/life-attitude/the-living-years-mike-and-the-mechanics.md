## 歌手简介 · About Mike + The Mechanics

**English**

**Mike + The Mechanics** is a British **rock band** started in **1985** by **Mike Rutherford**, bassist and guitarist of **Genesis**, as a project beside that band. The classic lineup had two lead singers: **Paul Carrack** and **Paul Young** (of Sad Café, not the solo star of the same name). Rutherford often wrote with outside partners, especially the Scottish songwriter **B.A. Robertson**. Their second album, ***Living Years*** (1988), carried the ballad that gave the record its name.

**中文**

**Mike + The Mechanics** 是 **1985 年**由 **Genesis** 的贝斯手、吉他手 **Mike Rutherford** 另起的英国**摇滚乐队**。经典阵容有两位主唱：**Paul Carrack**，以及 Sad Café 的 **Paul Young**（不是同名的独唱歌手）。Rutherford 常和外部作者合作，尤其是苏格兰词曲作者 **B.A. Robertson**。他们的第二张专辑 ***Living Years***（1988）就以这首同名抒情歌为名。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"The Living Years"** was written by **B.A. Robertson** and **Mike Rutherford**. **Paul Carrack** sings lead. The single came out in **December 1988**, from the album ***Living Years*** (October 1988). Producers were **Christopher Neil** and Rutherford. It reached **No. 1** on the US Billboard Hot 100 on **25 March 1989**, and **No. 2** in the UK. A children's choir joins the chorus.

Both writers lost their fathers while the song was taking shape. Robertson had started the lyric before his father died; the last verse came later. The singer is a son who sees the pattern — each generation blames the one before — and only after the funeral wishes he had said the real thing while both of them were alive. *The living years* is not a common idiom. In this song it means the years when both people are still here.

**中文**

**"The Living Years"** 由 **B.A. Robertson** 与 **Mike Rutherford** 合写，**Paul Carrack** 主唱。单曲于 **1988 年 12 月**发行，出自专辑 ***Living Years***（1988 年 10 月）。制作人是 **Christopher Neil** 与 Rutherford。它在 **1989 年 3 月 25 日**登上美国 Billboard Hot 100 **第一**，英国榜 **第二**。副歌里有一段童声合唱。

两位作者都在这首歌成形期间失去了父亲。Robertson 在父亲去世前就写下了开头；最后一段是后来才补上的。歌里的儿子看见一个循环：每一代都在怪上一代。葬礼之后他才后悔，彼此都还活着时，真正的话没有说出口。*The living years* 不是现成习语。在这首歌里，它指两个人都还在的那些年。

#### 歌在说什么 · The arc

**English**

The son names a family pattern. Each generation blames the one before it, so a father's unspoken frustration lands on the child. The son feels trapped by what his father held dear, and by his father's hopes and fears. After the father dies, the son remembers how stiff their talks were, and that he was not there on the morning his father passed. A new baby makes the father's voice feel present again. The chorus is the lesson, sung with children already in the room: say it while you can still be heard. Once someone has died, admitting that you never saw eye to eye comes too late.

**中文**

儿子先点出一个家庭循环：每一代都在怪上一代，父亲没说出口的挫败会敲到孩子门上。他觉得自己被父亲珍视的东西困住，也被父亲的希望和恐惧挟着走。父亲走后，他想起两人说话时有多生硬，也想起父亲离开的那个早晨自己不在场。新生儿的哭声里，他又好像听见了父亲。副歌是这首歌要留下的那句话，而且是和孩子一起唱的：还听得见的时候就说。人走了，再承认「我们从来看法不同」，已经晚了。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **every generation** | 每一代人 | *generation* 一代人；代际话题的核心词 |
| **blame the one before** | 责怪上一代 | *the one before* = 前一个人、上一代 |
| **frustrations** | 挫败；闷着的火气 | 名词。没说出来的不满 |
| **come beating on your door** | 敲到你门上 | 上一代的情绪会找上门，不是抽象讨论 |
| **a prisoner to** | 被……困住 | *prisoner to* 后面接困住你的那套东西 |
| **a hostage to** | 被……挟持 | 比 *prisoner* 更身不由己 |
| **held so dear** | 看得这么重 | *dear* 这里是「珍贵」，不是称呼 |
| **hopes and fears** | 希望与恐惧 | 常成对出现 |
| **wish I could have told him** | 但愿当时告诉过他 | *could have* + 过去分词：过去没做成 |
| **in the living years** | 在彼此都还活着的年月 | 歌名自造的说法，不是通用习语 |
| **say it loud, say it clear** | 大声说，说清楚 | *clear* 在这里是副词用法，口语里很常见 |
| **listen as well as you hear** | 听见之外，还要听进去 | *hear* 耳朵收到；*listen* 用心听 |
| **see eye to eye** | 看法一致 | 习语。*don't see eye to eye* = 看法不同 |
| **it's too late when we die** | 人走了就晚了 | *when* 引出那条截止线 |
| **passed away** | 去世 | 比 *died* 委婉 |
| **stilted conversations** | 生硬的谈话 | *stilted* 不自然、放不开 |
| **crumpled** | 揉皱的 | 歌里用来写没寄出的、不完美的字条 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 先把副歌的 *Say it loud, say it clear* 跟稳，再回头听主歌里的 *Every generation blames the one before*。
- *hear* 和 *listen* 不要唱成一个意思。耳朵听见，不等于听进去。
- *in the living years* 重音在 *liv*-ing。它指「人都还在的时候」，不是「有生气的岁月」这种空泛说法。
- 副歌进来时有童声。下一代已经在房间里，这句话是唱给他们听的。
- 和《当你老了》连着听：一首看着还在变老的人，一首后悔没在活着的年月说完。然后才接到 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/fix-you-coldplay|Fix You]]：话赶不上的时候，先陪着。
