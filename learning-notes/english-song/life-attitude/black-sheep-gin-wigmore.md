## 歌手简介 · About Gin Wigmore

**English**

**Gin Wigmore** (born 6 June 1986) is a **New Zealand singer-songwriter** known for her raspy, blues-soaked voice and bold attitude. She won the International Songwriting Competition at 16, signed with Universal, and broke internationally after her song **"Oh My"** appeared in a Heineken commercial tied to the James Bond film *Skyfall* (2012). Her second album **Gravel & Wine** (2011) was recorded in California with producer **Butch Walker** after she travelled through the American South for inspiration. Wigmore blends **pop, soul, country rock**, and **Southern rock**; her lyrics often celebrate outsiders, sharp tongues, and refusing to fit in.

**中文**

**Gin Wigmore**（1986 年 6 月 6 日生）是新西兰**创作型歌手**，以沙哑、带蓝调质感的嗓音和张扬态度著称。16 岁获国际歌曲创作大赛冠军，签约环球；2012 年歌曲 **"Oh My"** 出现在《007：大破天幕杀机》相关喜力广告后，国际知名度大增。第二张专辑 **Gravel & Wine**（2011）由制作人 **Butch Walker** 在加州完成，此前她曾赴美南采风。风格融合 **流行、灵魂、乡村摇滚** 与 **南方摇滚**；歌词常写「局外人」、毒舌与拒绝融入。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Black Sheep"** was released on **26 September 2011** as the lead single from *Gravel & Wine*. Wigmore has said the album title came from a line in this song: *making my own road out of gravel and some wine*—gravel is rough, wine is soft; the record lives in that tension. The track is a **rebel anthem** for anyone who refuses to follow the crowd (*Everybody's doing it so why the hell should I*). A **black sheep** is the odd one out in a family or group; Gin leans into the image with wordplay on *once you go black, you never go back*. The song appeared in TV shows (*Grey's Anatomy*, *Teen Wolf*), a Guinness Black Lager ad, and the video game *Far Cry 5*.

**中文**

**"Black Sheep"** 于 **2011 年 9 月 26 日**发行，为 *Gravel & Wine* 主打单曲。专辑名正来自歌里一句 *making my own road out of gravel and some wine*——碎石粗糙，酒意柔软，整张专辑就在这层张力里。这是一首**叛逆 anthem**：别人都在做，我凭什么跟（*Everybody's doing it so why the hell should I*）。**black sheep** 指家族或群体里的「害群之马／异类」；Gin 还玩了一句 *once you go black, you never go back* 的双关。歌曲曾用于《实习医生格蕾》《少狼》等剧集、健力士黑啤广告及游戏 *Far Cry 5*。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **black sheep** | 害群之马；异类 | 家族/群体里「格格不入」的那个人；歌题核心意象 |
| **pistol for a mouth** | 嘴像手枪 | 比喻说话犀利、伤人；*my own mama gave me that* 带自嘲 |
| **gravel and wine** | 碎石与酒 | 专辑名来源；粗糙 vs 柔软的双重气质 |
| **why the hell should I** | 我凭什么（也要） | *the hell* 加强语气；表拒绝随大流 |
| **not here to please** | 不是来讨好的 | 很直接的自我边界；*Make me mad* 后接此句 |
| **paint someone in/into a corner** | 把某人逼到墙角 | 习语：让人无路可退；歌里接 *my color comes back* 反转 |
| **once you go black, you never go back** | 一旦变黑就回不去 | 常见俗语的双关玩梗；此处扣 *black sheep* |
| **sing a little flat** | 唱得有点跑调 | *flat* = 音准偏低；自嘲不是完美偶像嗓 |
| **shallow grave** | 浅坟；肤浅的归宿 | 比喻庸俗、空洞的生活方式 |
| **jealous lovers** | 吃醋的前任们 | *lover* 可指旧情人；*wish they had me back* = 想复合 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *I'm a black sheep* 节奏稳定、重复多，适合 **KTV 跟唱**；先练清 *bad woman to keep* 的重音。
- Gin 的 **r 音和咬字** 偏美式口语，注意 *hell*、*mad* 等词在快唱里常轻读。
- 歌里大量 **对比与反问**（*everybody's doing it* / *why should I*），可用来练「拒绝迎合」的口语句型。
- 想延伸练**情绪与态度表达**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 里 Unpleasant 档的边界感句型。
