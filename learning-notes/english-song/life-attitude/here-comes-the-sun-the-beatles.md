## 歌手简介 · About The Beatles

**English**

**The Beatles** were the Liverpool band of **John Lennon**, **Paul McCartney**, **George Harrison**, and **Ringo Starr**. By **1969** the group was near its end, and Harrison — often the third songwriter beside Lennon and McCartney — brought *Here Comes the Sun* and *Something* to their last recorded album, ***Abbey Road***. This note is about Harrison's song. Another Beatles song in this vault, [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/hey-jude-the-beatles|Hey Jude]], is McCartney's, and it spends much longer trying to talk someone through the pain.

**中文**

**The Beatles**（披头士）是来自利物浦的乐队，成员为 **John Lennon**、**Paul McCartney**、**George Harrison**、**Ringo Starr**。到 **1969 年**，乐队已近尾声。Harrison 常被放在 Lennon 和 McCartney 之后，却为他们最后录完的专辑 ***Abbey Road*** 写下了 *Here Comes the Sun* 和 *Something*。本篇是 Harrison 的这首。本库里另一首披头士的歌 [[learning-notes/english-song/breakup-loss/hey-jude-the-beatles|Hey Jude]] 是 McCartney 的，它花更长的时间劝一个人走过痛苦。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Here Comes the Sun"** opens side two of ***Abbey Road***, released **26 September 1969**. Harrison wrote it in early 1969 in **Eric Clapton's** garden, on a day he skipped a tense meeting at Apple, the Beatles' own company. He sings and plays acoustic guitar. **George Martin** produced the record. The song runs **3:05**. It was not a UK or US single in 1969.

There is no argument in the lyric. Three small pictures do the work: a long winter, smiles coming back to faces, ice starting to melt. The bridge mostly says *sun* until the light feels close. *It's all right* is permission, not a speech. That is why it sits after a low point more gently than a comeback anthem.

**中文**

**"Here Comes the Sun"** 打开专辑 ***Abbey Road*** 的第二面。专辑于 **1969 年 9 月 26 日**发行。Harrison 在 **1969 年初**写下它，地点是 **Eric Clapton** 的花园。那天他躲开了披头士自己的公司 Apple 里一场紧张的会。他主唱并弹木吉他，**George Martin** 制作。时长 **3 分 05 秒**。1969 年它不是英美单曲。

歌词里没有道理要讲。三幅小画面就够了：冬天很长，笑意回到脸上，冰开始化。桥段几乎只重复 *sun*，直到光显得很近。*It's all right* 是许可，不是动员。所以它适合低谷之后：比一首东山再起的热血歌更轻。

## 歌词 · Lyrics

> 学习建议：完整歌词跟录音走。下面按段写**这一段在说什么**，并只摘值得跟唱的短句。
> Study tip: Sing along with the recording for the full lyric. The notes below say what each part is doing, and keep only the short lines worth drilling.

#### 歌在说什么 · The arc

**English**

Someone is addressed as *little darling* — a partner, a friend, or just the person you want to steady. The winter has felt endless. Then the same tune notices a change: smiles are returning, not fully back; the ice is melting, not gone. *Here comes the sun* is the announcement. *It's all right* is the only conclusion. The bridge does not add a new idea. It lets the word *sun* arrive again and again, and the count of the music shifts, so the light feels closer than the verse did.

**中文**

歌里叫了一声 *little darling*。可以是爱人、朋友，或任何一个你想让对方稳住的人。冬天好像过了很久。然后同一条旋律看见变化：笑意正在回到脸上，还没完全回来；冰正在化，还没化完。*Here comes the sun* 是在报信。*It's all right* 是唯一的结论。桥段不加新道理，只让 *sun* 一遍遍到来，拍子也会变，于是光比主歌里显得更近。

#### 跟唱短句 · Lines to sing

Here comes the sun, and I say it's all right

太阳出来了，于是我说，没关系

Little darling, it's been a long, cold, lonely winter

亲爱的，这是一个漫长、寒冷、孤单的冬天

Little darling, the smiles returning to the faces

亲爱的，笑意正回到脸上

I feel that ice is slowly melting

我感觉那冰正在慢慢化

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **here comes the sun** | 太阳出来了 | *Here comes* + 名词，用来宣布某物到来 |
| **and I say** | 于是我说 | 引出自己的判断，口气很轻 |
| **it's all right** | 没关系 | 两个词：*all right*。这首歌不写成 *alright* |
| **little darling** | 亲爱的 | 称呼很轻。重音在 *dar*-ling |
| **a long, cold, lonely winter** | 漫长、寒冷、孤单的冬天 | 三个形容词叠在同一个冬天上 |
| **it feels like years** | 感觉像过了很多年 | *feel like* 主观感觉，不是实际年数 |
| **since it's been here** | 自从它上次在这里 | *it* 是太阳，也是那种好日子 |
| **the smiles returning to the faces** | 笑意回到脸上 | *returning* 正在回来，还没完全好 |
| **ice is slowly melting** | 冰在慢慢化 | *melt* 融化。*slowly* 拦住「一下子就好了」 |
| **since it's been clear** | 自从天色上次放晴 | *clear* 放晴、清楚 |
| **here it comes** | 它来了 | 桥段里 *it* 仍是太阳 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 三幅画面按顺序跟：*winter* → *smiles returning* → *ice is slowly melting*。都是「正在变」，不是「已经完全好了」。
- *it's all right* 唱成两个词。写作时如果要照这首歌，不用 *alright*。
- *been* 在 *it's been a long...* 里常会弱读，接近 *bin*。
- 桥段的 *sun, sun, sun, here it comes* 拍子会变，数拍会多出几下。不用先算拍号。听见「光近了」就够。
- 它比 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/dont-stop-believin-journey|Don't Stop Believin']] 和《重头再来》都轻。那些歌要你抓住感觉、重新来过；这一首只让你重新看见冬天过去了。听完 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/fix-you-coldplay|Fix You]] 再听它，是从陪伴走回日常小事。
