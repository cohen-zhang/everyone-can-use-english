## 歌手简介 · About Billy Joel

**English**

**Billy Joel** (born **9 May 1949**) is an American **singer-songwriter and pianist**. He grew up on Long Island and broke through in the 1970s with piano-driven pop and story songs. The album ***The Stranger*** (1977), produced with **Phil Ramone**, includes *Just the Way You Are*, *Movin' Out*, *She's Always a Woman*, and *Vienna*. Joel writes in plain American speech: ambition, class, love, and the fear of running out of time.

**中文**

**Billy Joel**（生于 **1949 年 5 月 9 日**）是美国**创作歌手与钢琴家**。他在长岛长大，七十年代以钢琴流行歌和叙事歌成名。与制作人 **Phil Ramone** 合作的专辑 ***The Stranger***（1977）收录了 *Just the Way You Are*、*Movin' Out*、*She's Always a Woman* 和 *Vienna*。他的歌词用很白的美式口语写野心、阶层、爱，以及怕时间不够的那种慌。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Vienna"** is a track on ***The Stranger*** (released **29 September 1977**). Joel wrote it after visiting his father in Vienna and noticing older people there still living full, unhurried lives. In the song, Vienna is a place that waits. The singer tells a driven young person to slow down: you can chase what you want, or you can burn out and just get old. *You can't be everything you want to be before your time.* It was an album track for years, then found a new audience on streaming, especially with listeners who feel behind.

**中文**

**"Vienna"** 出自专辑 ***The Stranger***（**1977 年 9 月 29 日**发行）。Joel 去维也纳看望父亲后写下这首歌：他看见那里的老年人仍过着完整、不慌的生活。歌里的维也纳是一座会等你的地方。歌手对一个拼命的年轻人说：慢一点。你可以去追想要的东西，也可以把自己耗尽，然后只是变老。*You can't be everything you want to be before your time.* 它当初只是专辑曲，后来在流媒体上重新被听到，尤其是那些觉得自己「落后了」的人。

| **ambitious for a juvenile** | 对一个年轻人来说野心太大 | *juvenile* 青少年；比 *young* 更点出「还早」 |
| **Where's the fire?** | 哪儿着火了？ | 口语：你急什么？ |
| **what's the hurry about?** | 急什么？ | *hurry* 匆忙 |
| **cool it off** | 让它凉下来；别那么冲 | *cool off* 冷静、降温 |
| **burn it out** | 把热情烧尽 | *burn out* 燃尽、倦怠 |
| **so many hours in a day** | 一天就这么多小时 | 时间有限，排不下所有目标 |
| **when the truth is told** | 说实话；真相摊开时 | 固定说法：把实话讲出来 |

| **halfway through** | 走到一半 | *through* 做完、过完 |
| **Vienna waits for you** | 维也纳会等你 | 金句。Vienna = 从容、不赶时间的人生节奏 |
| **you're doing fine** | 你已经做得够好了 | 安慰句，可直接用在口语里 |
| **before your time** | 时机未到；在你的时间之前 | 你不可能提前活完所有版本 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 先把副歌唱稳：*When will you realize / Vienna waits for you*。再加主歌 3 的 *You can't be everything you want to be before your time.*
- *Where's the fire?* 和 *you're doing fine* 都是能直接说出口的短句。
- *kick off* 在别的语境常是「开始」。这里顺着 *before you even get halfway through* 听，意思是还没过半就撑不住。
- 和 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/dont-stop-believin-journey|Don't Stop Believin']] 一起听：一首拦住「我的人生大概就这样了」，一首拦住「我必须现在全部完成」。
