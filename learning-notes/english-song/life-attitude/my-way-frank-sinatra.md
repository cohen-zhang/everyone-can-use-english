## 歌手简介 · About Frank Sinatra

**English**

**Frank Sinatra** (1915–1998) was an American **singer and actor**, the central voice of mid-century popular song. His phrasing—speaking a lyric as if it were a conversation—set the model for later ballad singers. By the late 1960s he was already a legend; *My Way* became the song he used to close shows, a final statement about owning one's life.

**中文**

**Frank Sinatra**（1915–1998）是美国**歌手与演员**，二十世纪中期流行歌的核心嗓音。他的咬字像把歌词说成一段谈话，后来的抒情歌手很多都从这里学。到六十年代末他已是传奇；*My Way* 成了他谢幕时常唱的一首，像对一生的最后陈述：这是我自己选的路。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"My Way"** uses a French melody, *Comme d'habitude* (1967), by **Claude François**, **Jacques Revaux**, and **Gilles Thibaut**. **Paul Anka** wrote the English lyric with Sinatra in mind. Sinatra recorded it on **30 December 1968**, with an arrangement by **Don Costa**. It appeared in **1969** on the album ***My Way***. The singer faces the final curtain, lists regrets without dwelling on them, and repeats one claim: *I did it my way.* The English is full of idioms—*state my case*, *bit off more than I could chew*, *took the blows*—which makes it a useful late-in-life vocabulary song.

**中文**

**"My Way"** 的旋律来自 1967 年法语歌 *Comme d'habitude*，作者是 **Claude François**、**Jacques Revaux**、**Gilles Thibaut**。**Paul Anka** 为 Sinatra 写了英文词。Sinatra 于 **1968 年 12 月 30 日**录制，编曲 **Don Costa**，**1969 年**收入专辑 ***My Way***。歌手面对最后的帷幕，承认有过遗憾，却不停留在遗憾上，反复说同一句：*I did it my way.* 英文里习语很密——*state my case*、*bit off more than I could chew*、*took the blows*——适合当作「回看一生」的词汇课。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **the end is near** | 终点将近 | 直说，不绕 |
| **face the final curtain** | 面对最后的帷幕 | 舞台谢幕；比喻人生终场 |
| **say it clear** | 说清楚 | 口语里更常写 *say it clearly* |
| **state my case** | 陈述我的情况 / 摆出我的道理 | *case* 论点、案情；这里是为自己这一生说话 |
| **of which I'm certain** | 对此我很确定 | *be certain of* 确信 |
| **a life that's full** | 充实的一生 | *full* 满、不空虚 |
| **each and every highway** | 每一条大路 | *each and every* 加强「无一遗漏」 |
| **much more than this** | 比这重要得多 | 走过很多路还不够，方式更重要 |
| **I did it my way** | 我按自己的方式做成了 | 全曲金句。*my way* 我的方式 |
| **Regrets, I've had a few** | 遗憾，我有过一些 | 倒装，强调 *regrets* |
| **too few to mention** | 少到不值一提 | *mention* 提起 |
| **saw it through** | 坚持到底 | *see something through* 把事情做完 |
| **without exemption** | 没有例外；没有逃避 | *exemption* 豁免 |
| **charted course** | 标好的航线 | *chart* 绘制；计划好的路线 |
| **along the byway** | 沿着小路 | *byway* 偏僻小路，相对 *highway* |
| **bit off more than I could chew** | 一口咬得太多；自不量力 | 习语：揽下超出能力的事 |
| **through it all** | 经历这一切的过程中 | 口语高频 |
| **ate it up and spit it out** | 吞下去又吐出来 | 这里是面对怀疑、再把它甩开 |
| **faced it all** | 全部直面 | *face* 面对 |
| **stood tall** | 站直；昂首挺立 | 即使挨打也不塌 |
| **had my fill** | 已经尝够、经历够了 | 与 *get my fill* 同族 |
| **my share of losing** | 我那一份失败 | *one's share of* 理应分到的那一份，这里偏「该吃的亏我吃过」 |
| **tears subside** | 泪水退去 | *subside* 平息、减退 |
| **not in a shy way** | 并不羞怯地说 | 敢认 |
| **what has he got?** | 他拥有什么？ | *got* = *has*，口语 |
| **he has naught** | 他一无所有 | *naught* 文言味的「无」 |
| **one who kneels** | 下跪的人 | 比喻说讨好的话 |
| **The record shows** | 记录说明；岁月证明 | *record* 记录、成绩 |
| **took the blows** | 挨过那些打击 | *blow* 打击、重击 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌只练四行就够开口：*I've lived a life that's full / I've traveled each and every highway / And more, much more than this / I did it my way.*
- 三则习语可以脱离歌曲用：*state my case*、*see it through*、*bite off more than you can chew*。
- *say it clear* 是歌词节奏留下的说法；平时说 *say it clearly*。
- 和 [[learning-notes/english-song/love-romance/fly-me-to-the-moon-弗兰克辛纳特拉|Fly Me to the Moon]] 对照：一首望向未来的轻快，一首回看一生的谢幕。
