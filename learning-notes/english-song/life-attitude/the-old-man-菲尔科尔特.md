## 歌手简介 · About Phil Coulter

**English**

**Phil Coulter** (born **19 February 1942**, Derry, Northern Ireland) is an Irish **musician, songwriter, and record producer**. Raised in a musical home—his father played fiddle, his mother piano—he studied at Queen's University Belfast and moved to London in the 1960s, writing hits for artists including **Van Morrison**, **Cliff Richard**, and **The Bay City Rollers**. With **Bill Martin**, he co-wrote **Eurovision** winners *Puppet on a String* (1967) and *Congratulations* (1968). Coulter's own compositions span **folk, pop, and Celtic ballads**; he also created **Ireland's Call**, the Irish rugby anthem. Instrumental albums like *Classic Tranquility* (1984) made him one of Ireland's biggest-selling artists. He has received **five Ivor Novello Awards** and a **Grammy nomination**.

**中文**

**Phil Coulter**（菲尔·科尔特，1942 年 2 月 19 日生，北爱尔兰 Derry）是爱尔兰**音乐家、词曲作者、制作人**。成长于音乐家庭——父亲拉小提琴，母亲弹钢琴——就读于贝尔法斯特女王大学，1960 年代赴伦敦，为 **Van Morrison**、**Cliff Richard**、**The Bay City Rollers** 等写歌。与 **Bill Martin** 合作写出 **欧洲歌唱大赛** 冠军曲 *Puppet on a String*（1967）与 *Congratulations*（1968）。个人创作涵盖**民谣、流行与凯尔特抒情**；亦创作爱尔兰橄榄球颂歌 **Ireland's Call**。*Classic Tranquility*（1984）等钢琴器乐专辑销量极高。获 **5 座 Ivor Novello 奖**及**格莱美提名**。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"The Old Man"** was written by Coulter in **1981**, inspired by memories of his **father**, who had recently died. The song follows a son mourning after the funeral: tears shed, last goodbyes spoken, then the ache of *feeling alone*—followed by flashbacks to walks in the Irish countryside, fishing, and lessons *not known to kings*. The chorus—*I never will forget him, for he made me what I am*—has made the ballad a staple at **Irish funerals and Father's Day** tributes. **The Fureys & Davy Arthur** popularized it in 1982; Coulter also recorded it on *Words & Music*. The melody is deceptively simple; the emotional weight lies in plain, universal language about regret (*no second chance to tell him thanks*).

**中文**

**"The Old Man"** 由 Coulter 于 **1981 年**创作，灵感来自刚逝世的**父亲**。歌写儿子在葬礼后悼念：泪已流尽、最后的告别已说完，只剩 *feeling alone* 的空虚——继而闪回爱尔兰乡间的散步、钓鱼，以及「君王都不知的」秘密与教导。副歌 *I never will forget him, for he made me what I am* 使这首抒情曲成为**爱尔兰葬礼与父亲节**常用曲目。**The Fureys & Davy Arthur** 1982 年版本最广为人知；Coulter 亦在 *Words & Music* 中自唱。旋律看似简单，力量在于关于悔恨（*no second chance to tell him thanks*）的平实、普世语言。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 歌曲节奏舒缓、咬字清楚，适合**精研发音**与跟读；注意爱尔兰英语里部分元音与美式略有不同，但以清晰辅音为主。
- 三段主歌 + 重复副歌结构规整，可先背副歌 *I never will forget him*，再练主歌 2 的田园意象（*pheasant*、*holly tree*）。
- *No second chance to tell him thanks* 是全文情感高点，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]] 中关于 grief 与 regret 的表达。
- 与 [[learning-notes/english-song/life-attitude/the-old-man-yom|The Old Man — Yom]] 同名不同曲；本曲为 Coulter 经典凯尔特民谣，Yom 版为爵士器乐向作品。
