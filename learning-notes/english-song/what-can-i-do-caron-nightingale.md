## 歌手简介 · About Caron Nightingale

**English**

**Caron Nightingale** (also credited as **Caron Lyn Nightingale**) is a **Canadian singer-songwriter** who has worked extensively in **production music** and adult-contemporary ballads. She co-wrote **"Promises Don't Come Easy"** with **Dean Landon**; her own recording became a staple of English-learning playlists in China, while **Ronan Keating**'s 1999 cover brought the song to mainstream charts worldwide. Nightingale's voice is warm and straightforward—ideal for learners who want **clear, classic pop English** without heavy slang.

**中文**

**Caron Nightingale**（亦作 **Caron Lyn Nightingale**）是加拿大**创作歌手**，长期活跃于 **production music** 与 adult-contemporary ballad 领域。她与 **Dean Landon** 共同创作 **"Promises Don't Come Easy"**；其原唱版在中国英语学习歌单里很常见，而 **Ronan Keating** 1999 年翻唱则让这首歌登上全球主流榜单。Nightingale 的嗓音温暖、吐字清楚——适合想练**经典流行英文**、又不想碰太多俚语的学习者。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Promises Don't Come Easy"** (often listed as **"What Can I Do (Promises)"** on streaming services) is a **mid-tempo country-pop ballad** about apologizing after love goes wrong. The chorus question—*What can I do to make it up to you?*—frames the whole song; the title line *Promises don't come easy* means rebuilding trust takes more than words. For **Chinese learners**, this is a **classic sing-along**: short sentences, high-frequency verbs (*know, read, walk, promise*), and emotional vocabulary (*proud, cared, feelings*) that appear in everyday conversation and KTV sets. Ronan Keating's cover is equally famous; lyrics match the original.

**中文**

**"Promises Don't Come Easy"**（流媒体常标为 **"What Can I Do (Promises)"**）是一首**中速 country-pop ballad**，写爱出问题后如何道歉。副歌问句 *What can I do to make it up to you?* 贯穿全曲；歌名 *Promises don't come easy* 意指重建信任不能光靠一句话。对**中文母语学习者**而言，这是**经典跟唱材料**：句子短、动词高频（*know, read, walk, promise*），情绪词（*proud, cared, feelings*）在口语与 KTV 里都很实用。Ronan Keating 翻唱同样有名，歌词与原曲一致。

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- **经典入门 ballad**：词汇日常、语速适中，适合通勤跟听与 KTV 副歌 (*What can I do…*)。
- 练 **should have known**（后悔）与 **make it up to you**（道歉）两个句型，可直接套用到口语。
- *walkin' / Wondrin' / gonna* 是典型口语缩略，跟唱时注意 **g** 在 -ing 里常弱读或省略。
- 想对比不同演绎，可听 **Ronan Keating** 版或华语翻唱 [[learning-notes/english-song/promises-dont-come-easy-陈曦|陈曦版]]，歌词相同、练咬字角度不同。
