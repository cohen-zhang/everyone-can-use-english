## 歌手简介 · About Westlife

**English**

**Westlife** is an **Irish pop vocal group** formed in **Sligo** in 1998. The classic lineup—**Shane Filan**, **Mark Feehily**, **Kian Egan**, **Nicky Byrne**, and (until 2004) **Brian McFadden**—was managed by **Louis Walsh** and mentored by **Boyzone**'s Ronan Keating. Westlife became one of the best-selling boy bands of all time, with **14 UK No. 1 singles** and a string of power ballads built for stadium sing-alongs. Their sound blends **pop**, **soft rock**, and **adult contemporary**; after a hiatus and reunion, they continue to tour globally. For English learners, their clear diction and big emotional hooks make them a reliable **KTV and pronunciation** choice.

**中文**

**Westlife** 是 1998 年在**斯莱戈**成立的**爱尔兰流行 vocal 组合**。经典阵容——**Shane Filan**、**Mark Feehily**、**Kian Egan**、**Nicky Byrne**，以及（至 2004 年）**Brian McFadden**——由 **Louis Walsh** 管理，**Boyzone** 的 Ronan Keating 指导。Westlife 成为史上最畅销的男团之一，拥有 **14 首英国冠军单曲**，大量 power ballad 适合全场大合唱。风格融合 **流行、软摇滚、成人当代**； hiatus 与重组后仍全球巡演。对学习者而言，咬字清楚、情绪 hook 大，是可靠的 **KTV 与发音** 素材。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"My Love"** was released on **31 October 2000** as the second single from ***Coast to Coast***, Westlife's second studio album. Written by the Swedish team **Jörgen Elofsson**, **Per Magnusson**, **David Kreuger**, and **Rami Yacoub** (who also wrote for Britney Spears and the Backstreet Boys), it is a **power ballad** about **long-distance longing**: empty rooms, fading memories, and a prayer to cross *overseas from coast to coast* just to *see you once again*. The chorus (*So I say a little prayer… where the fields are green*) became one of the group's signature anthems, reaching **No. 1** in the UK and topping charts across Europe. The **Radio Edit** trims the album version slightly for airplay.

**中文**

**"My Love"** 于 **2000 年 10 月 31 日**发行，是 Westlife 第二张专辑 ***Coast to Coast*** 的第二支单曲。由瑞典创作团队 **Jörgen Elofsson**、**Per Magnusson**、**David Kreuger**、**Rami Yacoub**（也为 Britney Spears、Backstreet Boys 写歌）创作，是一首关于**远距离思念**的 **power ballad**：空房间、褪色的回忆，以及一句祷告——要*跨越海洋、从海岸到海岸*，只为 *see you once again*。副歌（*So I say a little prayer… where the fields are green*）成为组合代表作，**英国冠军单曲**，并在欧洲多国登顶。**Radio Edit** 为电台播放略剪短专辑版。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a hole inside my heart** | 心里空了一个洞 | 常见失恋/思念意象；比 *I'm sad* 更诗意 |
| **the rooms are getting smaller** | 房间仿佛越来越小 | 孤独感：空间在心理上收缩 |
| **holding on forever** | 永远坚持（不放弃） | *hold on* = 撑住、不放手 |
| **reaching for the love that seems so far** | 伸手去够遥不可及的 love | *reach for* = 努力够向 |
| **say a little prayer** | 做一个小小祷告 | *a little* 软化语气，日常也可用 |
| **hope my dreams will take me there** | 希望梦能带我去那里 | *take someone there* = 带到某处 |
| **see you once again** | 再见到你一次 | *once again* = 再一次；比 *again* 更正式 |
| **overseas from coast to coast** | 跨越海洋，从海岸到海岸 | 强调远距离；美国常说 *coast to coast* |
| **a place I love the most** | 我最爱的那个地方 | *most* 作副词 = 最 |
| **where the fields are green** | 那里田野翠绿 | 爱尔兰/英伦田园意象，常指「家乡」 |
| **can't stop to keep myself from thinking** | 停不下来不去想 | *can't stop + -ing* = 忍不住一直…… |
| **to hold you in my arms** | 把你拥入怀中 | 经典 ballad 承诺句 |
| **to promise you my love** | 向你许诺我的爱 | *promise someone something* |
| **tell you from the heart** | 从心底告诉你 | *from the heart* = 真心实意 |
| **you're all I'm thinking of** | 你是我所思念的一切 | *all* 强调「全部心思都在你身上」 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌 *My love* 旋律高、重复多，**KTV 首选**；先练 *So I say a little prayer* 整句连读。
- Westlife 的 **pop 咬字**偏清晰，*overseas*、*once again* 适合练英式/爱尔兰元音与重音。
- 歌里大量 **wonder / hope / pray** 结构，可用来练「我想知道…… / 我希望……」的英文语气。
- 想延伸练**远距离恋爱与思念**，可打开 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 里 longing / reunion 类句型。
