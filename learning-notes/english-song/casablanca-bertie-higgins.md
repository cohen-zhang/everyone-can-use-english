## 歌手简介 · About Bertie Higgins

**English**

**Bertie Higgins** (born **1944** in **Tarpon Springs, Florida**) is an American **singer-songwriter** best known for the soft-rock hit *"Casablanca"* (1982). Before going solo he played in regional bands and worked as a drummer and songwriter; his breakthrough came with the album ***Just Another Day in Paradise***, which mixed storytelling ballads with yacht-rock polish. *"Casablanca"* became an international favorite—especially across Asia—thanks to its cinematic romance and easy melody. Higgins continued releasing albums that lean on nostalgia, travel, and heartland storytelling.

**中文**

**Bertie Higgins**（**1944** 年生于美国佛罗里达 **Tarpon Springs**）是美国**唱作人**，以 1982 年软摇滚金曲 *"Casablanca"* 最为人知。单飞前曾在地方乐队担任鼓手与写手；成名作专辑 ***Just Another Day in Paradise*** 把叙事抒情与 yacht-rock 的圆润制作结合。*"Casablanca"* 凭电影感的浪漫与好记旋律走红国际，在亚洲尤其长红。此后他仍持续发行偏怀旧、旅行与故事感的作品。

## 歌曲简介 · About the Song

**English**

**"Casablanca"** was co-written by **Bertie Higgins** and **Sonny Limbo**, released in **1982** on ***Just Another Day in Paradise***. It tells of falling in love at a **drive-in** while watching the classic film *Casablanca*: popcorn turns into champagne in memory, and the couple imagines themselves in Rick's café under Moroccan moonlight. The chorus nods to the film's famous song—*a kiss is still a kiss… as time goes by*—while the story ends in heartbreak: their love may never make the silver screen, but it hurt just as bad when she left. Soft rock, cinematic, and built for sing-along nostalgia.

**中文**

**"Casablanca"** 由 **Bertie Higgins** 与 **Sonny Limbo** 合写，**1982** 年收录于 ***Just Another Day in Paradise***。歌写的是在**汽车电影院**看经典影片《卡萨布兰卡》时坠入爱河：记忆里爆米花变成香槟，两人幻想自己置身 Rick 的咖啡馆、摩洛哥月光下。副歌向电影名曲致意——*a kiss is still a kiss… as time goes by*——故事却收在心碎：他们的爱情或许永不会登上大银幕，但看着她离开时，痛一点也不少。软摇滚、电影感、适合怀旧跟唱。

## 重点表达 · Key Phrases

| English | 简中 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **fell in love with you watching…** | 看着……时爱上了你 | *fall in love* + 现在分词，表「在做某事的过程中爱上」 |
| **drive-in show** | 汽车电影院的放映 | 美式文化：坐在车里看电影 |
| **flickering light** | 忽明忽暗的光 | *flicker* = 闪烁；写银幕/灯光氛围 |
| **became champagne and caviar** | 变成了香槟和鱼子酱 | 记忆美化：平凡零食 → 奢华浪漫 |
| **making love** | 缠绵；做爱 | 歌词里可偏浪漫亲密；语境敏感时注意场合 |
| **'neath** | 在……之下 | = *beneath*；歌词缩略，偏诗意 |
| **paddle fans** | 吊扇；（船式）叶片风扇 | 热带/旧式咖啡馆常见意象 |
| **candle lit cafe** | 烛光咖啡馆 | 常写作 *candlelit*；指向电影里 Rick's Café |
| **making magic** | 制造魔法；营造奇妙时刻 | 口语化：把平凡约会说成魔法 |
| **a kiss is still a kiss** | 吻依然是吻 | 向《Casablanca》插曲 *As Time Goes By* 致意 |
| **without your sigh** | 没有你的叹息 | 强调「有你的反应，吻才完整」 |
| **as time goes by** | 随着时光流转 | 电影名句；表时间流逝、感情加深或物是人非 |
| **broken hearts** | 破碎的心；失恋的人 | 常见情歌意象 |
| **silver screen** | 大银幕；电影业 | 指电影院银幕 / 登上电影 |
| **hurt just as bad** | 痛得一点也不少 | *just as bad* = 同样糟；口语很自然 |
| **watch you go** | 看着你离开 | 分手画面；比 *see you leave* 更盯着看的痛感 |

## 听歌提示 · Listening Notes

- 副歌四句结构固定，**KTV / 通勤跟唱**友好；先把 *as time goes by* 的节奏唱稳。
- Verse 1→2 是「现实约会 → 脑内电影」：注意 *popcorn and cokes* 如何 *became champagne and caviar*，练叙事对比。
- *'neath / Casablanca / Chevrolet* 有点绕口，慢速跟两遍再提速。
- 想延伸练**怀念、求复合、分手后的痛**，可对照 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/romantic-love-song-phrases|浪漫情歌与恋爱口语]] 与 [[learning-notes/personal-english-book/life/emotions-adult-edition|情绪 — 成人版]]。
