# Are You Sleeping? — Brother John

**儿歌：** *Are You Sleeping?*（英文版 *Frère Jacques* / *Brother John*）  
**给 Celine 跟唱用** — 一问一答式叠句；结尾模仿**晨钟** *Ding, dang, dong.*

## 歌词 · Lyrics

> 传统法文儿歌 *Frère Jacques* 的英文版。前两行重复提问；中间两行点名 **Brother John**；后两行说晨钟响；最后两行拟声。部分版本唱 **Ding, ding, dong** 或 **Ding, dong, ding**；跟 CD 或老师版保持一致即可。

> 学习建议：先只看 **英文歌词** 跟唱；需要核对意思时，再往下看 **中文翻译**。
> Study tip: Sing along with **English Lyrics** first; scroll down to **Chinese Translation** only when you need meaning.

### 英文歌词 · English Lyrics

#### 全曲 · Full song

Are you sleeping? Are you sleeping?  
Brother John, Brother John,  
Morning bells are ringing! Morning bells are ringing!  
Ding, dang, dong. Ding, dang, dong.

---

### 中文翻译 · Chinese Translation

#### 全曲 · Full song

你还在睡吗？约翰修士，晨钟在响——叮、当、咚。

## 跟唱提示 · Sing-along tips

| 句 | 动作 / 演 | 简中 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Are you sleeping? | 双手合十贴脸，做睡觉；问两遍可左右看 | 你在睡觉吗？ |
| Brother John | 指「对方」或绘本里的修士 | 约翰修士（名字） |
| Morning bells are ringing! | 双臂左右摇，像敲钟 | 晨钟在响！ |
| Ding, dang, dong | 每词敲一下手或拍手三下 | 叮—当—咚 |

**亲子玩法：** 一人扮 **Brother John** 躺着装睡，另一人唱前两句「叫醒」；换角色。和 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/games-and-activities/parenting-opposites-antonyms-game|反义词游戏]] 里的 *Wake up!* / *Go to sleep.* 配着练。

## 时态说明 · Tense in the lyrics

像在**此刻**敲门问人：你还在睡吗？钟**正在**响。

| 动词 | 形式 | 简中 | 歌词句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **sleep** | **-ing** 现在进行时 | 正在睡 | Are you **sleeping**? |
| **ring** | **-ing** 现在进行时 | 正在响 | bells are **ringing** |

**Are you sleeping?** = 你现在睡着吗？（不是 *Did you sleep?* 昨晚睡得好吗。）

## 认读小练习 · Quick drill

唱完问 Celine：

| 问 | 答 |
| --- | --- |
| Are you sleeping? | **No! I'm awake!** / **Yes…**（装睡） |
| Who are we calling? | **Brother John.** |
| What is ringing? | **Morning bells.** |
| What sound do the bells make? | **Ding, dang, dong.** |

**和作息句连一句：** *Morning bells are ringing. **Time to wake up!*** — 晨钟响了，该起床啦！（见 [[learning-notes/parenting-english/daily-life/parenting-family-rules-routine-daily-phrases|家庭规则与作息]]。）
