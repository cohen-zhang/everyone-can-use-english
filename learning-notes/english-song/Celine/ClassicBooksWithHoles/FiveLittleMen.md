# Five Little Men in a Flying Saucer — Classic Books with Holes

**书目：** *Five Little Men in a Flying Saucer*（Classic Books with Holes 系列）  
**Illustrator:** Annie Kubler · **Publisher:** Child's Play International  
**给 Celine 跟唱用** — 先 **5→1** 倒数（每人飞走喊 *Whoosh!*），最后一人唱 *So then he flew away!*；地球变干净后 **五人一起回来**，结尾 *So they decided to stay.*

**绘本说明：** Child's Play 版图画里，五个小人因地球脏乱先后飞走；结尾人类把环境收拾好，小人**乘飞碟回来并决定留下**——后半段歌词与画面一致。

## 歌词 · Lyrics

> 依据 Child's Play / Annie Kubler 版整理。倒数段为传统儿歌；**回来 · Came back** 为 Child's Play 环保结局专用。部分印次第二行唱 **Went round the world**；最后一节倒数常用 **So then he flew away!**

## 单复数说明 · Singular & plural in the lyrics

这首前半是**倒数歌**（5→1），后半**五人一起回来**；人数变，**men/man** 与 **they/he** 也要跟着换。

### 1 · 数字 + men / man：人少了，词也要换

| 人数 | 怎么说 | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5–2 人 | **… little men**（复数） | Five / Four / Three / Two little **men**… |
| 1 人 | **… little man**（单数） | One little **man**… |
| 飞走一个 | **one man** flew away | So **one man** flew away. |
| 最后一个 | **then he** flew away | So **then he** flew away! |
| 五人同回 | **five little men**（复数） | **Five** little men… **came back** |

**不规则复数：** **man → men**（不是 *mans*）。跟 Celine 比划手指时顺口带一句：*Five **men**. One **man**. Five **men** again!*

### 2 · they / he：几个人，用哪个代词

| 主语 | 代词 | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| five / four / three / two **men** | **They** | **They** looked left and right. · **They** didn't like… |
| one **man** | **He** | **He** looked left and right. · **He** didn't like… |
| five **men**（回来段） | **They** | **They** looked… · **they** really liked… · **they** decided… |

**跟娃练一句：** *Many men? **They** look. One man? **He** looks.* — 好多人用 **they**，一个人用 **he**。（*look / looks* 是现在时；歌词里讲故事用过去式 **looked**，见下节「时态说明」。）

### 3 · flying saucer、world、sight：始终单数

| 词 | 简中 | 为什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **a flying saucer** | 一艘飞碟 | 五个小人**共乘**一艘碟 |
| **the world** | 世界／地球 | 绕的是**一颗**地球 |
| **the sight** | 眼前的景象 | 他们不喜欢的是**同一片**看到的景象 |

### 4 · left and right：不是「两个右边」

**left** 和 **right** 在这里是**方向**，成对出现：向左看、向右看。不是可数名词复数，不用加 s 变复数。

## 时态说明 · Tense in the lyrics

整首歌在**讲已经发生过的事**（*one day* 那一天），动词几乎全是**一般过去时**——含倒数段与回来段。

### 1 · 时间标志：one day

**Flew round the world one day.** / **Came back to the world one day.** — 「有一天」他们绕地球飞了一圈，或**又有一天**飞回来了。

听到 **one day**，后面常接**过去式**，像在讲睡前故事：*One day, five little men **flew**…* · *One day, they **came back**…*

### 2 · 过去式动词一览

| 原形（现在） | 过去式（歌词） | 简中 | 歌词句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **fly** | **flew** | 飞走 | **Flew** round the world. · **then he flew** away |
| **come back** | **came back** | 回来 | **Came back** to the world. |
| **look** | **looked** | 看 | They **looked** left and right. |
| **like** | **liked** | 喜欢 | they **really liked** the sight |
| **decide** | **decided** | 决定 | they **decided** to stay |

**不规则：** **fly → flew**（不是 *flied*）；**come → came**。跟 Celine 对口型：唱到 *flew* 时手势往后飞，唱到 *came back* 时五指张开转回来。

### 3 · didn't like ↔ really liked：同一景象，两种说法

| 段落 | 过去时 | 简中 | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 倒数段 | **didn't like** | 不喜欢 | But they **didn't like** the sight. |
| 回来段 | **really liked** | 很喜欢 | And they **really liked** the sight. |

**跟娃练一句：** *First they **didn't like** it. Later they **really liked** it.* — 先不喜欢，后来很喜欢。（地球变干净了。）

### 4 · didn't：过去时的否定

**didn't** = **did** + **not**。

| 肯定（过去） | 否定（过去） | 歌词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| they liked the sight | they **didn't like** the sight | But they **didn't like** the sight. |
| he liked the sight | he **didn't like** the sight | But he **didn't like** the sight. |

### 5 · 和《The Wheels on the Bus》对比：讲故事 vs 正在发生

| | 本歌 *Five Little Men* | [[learning-notes/english-song/Celine/ClassicBooksWithHoles/TheWheelsOnTheBus|The Wheels on the Bus]] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 时态 | **过去时** flew, looked, didn't like, came back, liked, decided | **现在时** go, goes, say, says |
| 感觉 | 有一天发生过的事（先走、再回） | 公交车上**一直在**转、刷、响（*All day long*） |
| 例句 | They **came back** to the world. | The wheels **go** round and round. |

两首都是儿歌，但**时态不同**：一首像**回忆**，一首像**现场跟唱**。

## 倒数小练习 · Count-down drill

**前半段（飞走）：** 唱完可让 Celine 用英语答：

| 问 | 答 |
| --- | --- |
| How many men at the start? | **Five** little men. |
| One flew away. How many now? | **Four**! |
| …then? | **Three** → **Two** → **One** → **None** / zero! |
| Last man—what does he do? | **Then he flew away!** |

**减法句型：** *Five take away one is four.* — 五减一等于四。

**后半段（回来）：**

| 问 | 答 |
| --- | --- |
| They came back. How many now? | **Five** little men again! |
| Did they like it this time? | Yes! They **really liked** the sight. |
| What did they decide? | They **decided to stay**. |
